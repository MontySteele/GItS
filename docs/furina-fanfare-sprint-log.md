## DECISIONS entry

> **Fanfare demoted to read-only momentum stat; cap grammar retired.**
>
> Fanfare ceases to be a spendable currency. It is generated by activity
> flux (unchanged), **decays each turn**, and is **floored by permanent
> constellation grants**. No card spends it; `fanfare_cost` leaves the card
> grammar and the loader rejects it loudly. `raise_fanfare_cap` retires with
> the kickoff §4 uncapper clause it served. **Encore becomes Furina's only
> managed resource.**
>
> Rationale (user redundancy diagnosis, 2026-07-24, verbatim intent): *Encore
> already is her spendable resource; a second spendable resource is a
> redundant system.*
>
> **Explicit note on the no-passive-accrual law (kickoff §4):** floors are
> **static value, not accrual**. A floor does not grow with time, so stalling
> still earns nothing. The law is intact, not amended. This is stated
> explicitly because a floor superficially resembles the passive per-turn
> accrual that §4 bans forever, and the distinction is the whole reason the
> package is legal.

### Amendment to the plan's gate (2) — found before running, not after

Plan §5 gate (2) reads *"at-cap reads < 15% in every plan"*, and plan §10
Risk 4 is *"floor stacking re-pins the meter."* **Gate (2) cannot see Risk 4.**

F-A2 raises floor, cap and current *together*, so `floor < cap` holds by
construction. If floor grants stack hard enough to pin the meter, it pins at
the **floor**, `fanfare == cap` never fires, and the tripwire stays silent
through precisely the failure it was written to catch.

Therefore the registered telemetry adds an **at-floor read** alongside
at-cap, and gate (2) is read against **both** pins. This adds a metric; it
does not loosen a bar. Flagged for red-pen with the rest of §5.

### Metric reconciliation with sheet pass 4

Pass 4 measured Fanfare saturation at **turn start** (`fanfare_turn`
snapshot): 20.0 / 32.1 / 24.8% at-cap by act 3, overflow 46–48%. The sprint
plan's §1 evidence measures at **read time** ("salon reads Fanfare at cap
100% of the time"). These are not in conflict — the gap between them *is* the
pool refilling mid-turn and spilling. **Read-time is the governing metric**
for this sprint; the turn-start snapshot is retained as a secondary.

This closes the pass-4 Q1c item that was blocked on a [USER] metric ruling,
and pass-4 Q2 (`the_sea_is_my_stage` reprice) is dissolved rather than ruled,
