### [act2/act3] Crystal Sphere
    [Uncover Future] Pay 51-99 Gold. Divine 3 times.
    [Payment Plan] Gain a Debt. Divine 6 times.
    === Mechanics ===
    thumb|The Crystal Sphere minigame with the grid in the middle and the 2 size options for divining on the right
    The event first presents the player with a choice between two options. Both of them lead to the same minigame in which the player uncovers a crystal sphere for rewards. In the center of the screen is an 11x11 grid with rounded corners. The tiles start of unrevealed and have to be revealed in order to uncover the hidden rewards. Most of the rewards are larger than a single 1x1 tile and have to be uncovered ''completely'' before gaining them.
    The tiles are uncovered via the process of '''Divination'''. Depending on the option chosen at the beginning, the player can divine 3 or 6 times in total. The 2 options for divining are a '''Small Divination''' which uncovers only a single tile, and a '''Big Divination''' which reveals a 3x3 area. Both options count as 1 "use" for the total number of divinations. At the end of the minigame, the player is presented with a list of all the rewards they managed to find - these can be picked in any order or skipped entirely. The only exception to this is if uncovering the hidden curse Doubt, which is added instantly to the deck.
    Note that even though the box for the relic is viewed from an angled perspective, it still has the size of a 4x4 square, meaning it is not possible to uncover it completely with only 3 divinations.

### [all] The Future of Potions_
    [Insert Common Potion] Lose a specified Common potion. Obtain an Upgrade Cards rarity:Common Common 2 [Cards type:Attack Attack 2/Cards type:Skill Skill 2] card reward.
    [Insert Uncommon Potion] Lose a specified Uncommon potion. Obtain an Upgraded Cards rarity:Uncommon Uncommon 2 [Attack/Skill/Cards type:Power Power 2] card reward.
    [Insert Rare Potion] Lose a specified Rare potion. Obtain an Upgraded Cards rarity:Rare Rare 2 [Attack/Skill/Power] card reward.
    [Insert Event Potion] Lose a specified Event potion. Obtain an Upgraded Rare [Attack/Skill/Power] card reward.
    * The only Event potions are Foul Potion 2 and Glowwater Potion 2.
    [Insert Token Potion] Lose a specified Token potion. Obtain an Upgraded Common [Attack/Skill] card reward.
    * The only Token potion is Potion-Shaped Rock 2.
    === Mechanics ===
    * The first 3 potions in your potion slots are chosen as options to insert, prioritizing the leftmost potions. This can include multiple potions of the same rarity.
    * The rarity of the card reward depends on the rarity of the potion inserted.
    * Each option randomly specifies whether it gives an Attack, Skill, or Power card reward.

### [act3] Tinker Time
    This event lets you create a custom Mad Science card by choosing a card type and a rider effect.
    === Step 1: Choose Card Type ===
    *
    [Weapon] Create an Attack. (Deal 12 damage.)
    *
    [Protector] Create a Skill. (Gain 8 Block.)
    *
    [Gadget] Create a Power.
    Two of the three types are offered at random.
    === Step 2: Choose Rider Effect ===
    Two rider effects are offered at random from the type's pool:
    '''Attack Riders:'''
    * '''Sapping''' — Apply 2 Weak. Apply 2 Vulnerable.
    * '''Violence''' — Hits 2 additional times.
    * '''Choking''' — Whenever you play a card this turn, the enemy loses 6 HP.
    '''Skill Riders:'''
    * '''Energized''' — Gain 2 energy.
    * '''Wisdom''' — Draw 3 cards.
    * '''Chaos''' — Add a random card into your Hand. It's free to play this turn.
    '''Power Riders:'''
    * '''Expertise''' — Gain 2 Strength. Gain 2 Dexterity.
    * '''Curious''' — Powers cost 1 less.
    * '''Improvement''' — At the end of combat, Upgrade a random card.

### [act2] Welcome to Wongo's
    [Wongo's Bargain Bin] Pay 100 Gold. Obtain 1 random Common Relic.
    * Player also receives 32 Wongo Points.
    [Wongo's Featured Item] Pay 200 Gold. Obtain [Rare Relic].
    * This relic is randomly selected from the rare relics in the standard relic pool, and will be displayed by name in the event option.
    * Player also receives 8 Wongo Points.
    [Wongo's Mystery Box] Pay 300 Gold. Obtain 3 random Relics after 5 combats.
    * Player obtains Wongo's Mystery Ticket.
    * Player also receives 16 Wongo Points.
    [Leave] Downgrade]] a random card.
    ===Wongo Points===
    After any purchase, the player will receive Wongo Points based on which purchase they selected. Wongo Points persist between all of the player's runs on their profile (across all characters), and the event text will display how many total Wongo Points the player has after making a purchase, as well as how many more points are needed to reach 2000 total points.
    Once the player reaches 2000 total Wongo Points, they will receive the Wongo Customer Appreciation Badge, which does nothing.
