# S10 Enemy Atlas — Curated Reskin Gallery (Fable curation pass, 2026-08-05)

**What this is.** A single cross-family dedupe of the 16 S10 family atlases against the committed base roster (`tier05/content/act1_pool.yaml`, `act2_pool.yaml`, `act3_pool.yaml`, `docs/act2-act3-roster-research.md`, `tier0/content/encounters/`). Every row lists **candidates, not verdicts** — best apparent fit first, with the trade-off stated. The RESKIN-vs-REDESIGN line, and every actual assignment, is the **[USER]'s call per the north-star**; nothing here commits art, names, or mechanics. This pass pairs with the dispatch-1 S3 per-family dossiers where those exist — a handful of Genshin bodies cited below need existence verification against them (see flags).

**Status column.** `SHIPPED` = modeled in a tier05 pool today. `RESEARCH` = in the roster doc only (§3 Underdocks, §4 boss pools). `DROPPED` = on the plan doc's dropped-enemy re-add list (`run-model-rework-plan.md:786`) — pairable, but the body doesn't ship until its backlog op lands. Reskins of DROPPED/RESEARCH rows are pre-positioning, not current work.

**Confidence codes** (carried from the family agents, occasionally demoted where cross-family comparison weakened a claim): **S** strong · **P** plausible · **X** stretch.

**Non-goals honored:** no verdicts, no mechanical redesigns, no new pairings invented in this pass — only ordering, cuts, and flags.
