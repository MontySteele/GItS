# S10c — Snezhnaya Enemy-Family Atlas (EB-752)

Companion to `atlas.md` and `atlas-nod-krai.md`. Snezhnaya released as an
explorable region in Genshin Impact Version 7.0, "Everwinter Without Mercy,"
on August 12, 2026 — one month before this atlas was written, so treat
everything below as thin: aggregator coverage is fresher than usual, and
several factions only have a single faction-guide page rather than the deep
per-body pages `atlas.md`'s Fatui sections drew on. The repo's own knowledge
of Snezhnaya was previously nil; this is the first pass. `atlas.md`'s Fatui
elites section already covers the Fatui as Snezhnaya's home nation (Agents,
Cicin Mages, Mirror Maidens, Operatives, the Harbingers) — none of that is
repeated here. This atlas is Snezhnaya's NATIVE, non-Fatui wildlife: the
Snowland Fae, the Mystical Beasts new to the region, and the bio-alchemy
Chimeric line.

## 1. Family overview — what 7.0 actually shipped

Three native groups, all VERIFIED to exist (multiple independent sources
name each), with a caveat on internal taxonomy for the first:

- **Snowland Fae** — Genshin's umbrella name (per a Fandom category page
  title, `Category:Snowland_Fae`) for Snezhnaya's human-coexisting mythical
  race, split across (at least) two sub-lines with distinct combat
  identities:
  - **Fae of Crystalline Flesh** — ice-coded: Winged Soarer-Knight, Winged
    Halberd-Knight, Glacial Warden, Glaciomancer (game8's faction-guide
    roster), sharing an Ice Coffin defensive mechanic. A separately-named
    body, **Snegovik** (female form Snegurochka), is described by the
    Fandom wiki as "one of six common types of Snowland Fae" with the SAME
    ice-coffin-ward mechanic — whether Snegovik is a fifth member of this
    combat faction under a different lore name, or a separately-tracked
    common-tier body, is UNVERIFIED; no page reconciles the two listings.
  - **Volkodlak Brute** — a wolf/beast-humanoid sub-line, filed by the
    Fandom wiki under "Mystical Beasts" AND "Volkodlak Brute"
    simultaneously: Raider, Sharpshooter, Basher, Lobber, plus Voywolf
    Hunter, Fanged Zealot, Leomastiff Guard, and at least two more named
    bodies (Channel Contact, Long-Eared Striker) whose kits are
    UNVERIFIED — confirmed by name only.
- **Wildwood Leshy** — a small Mystical Beast family (Juvenile Tree
  Segment, Rugged Elder Leshy), game8-described as having "no special
  gimmick." VERIFIED.
- **Aberrant Chimeric Monster** — bio-alchemy beast hybrids: Armored Beast,
  Burrowbeast, Horned Bear, Winged Elephant, and the named boss Winged
  Lion. VERIFIED, and the best-documented family here — Winged Lion has a
  full fetched moveset (see below).
- NAMED BUT NOT WRITTEN UP (confirmed to exist by the 2026-09-15
  re-verification, kits unfetched): **Winged Sylph** (flying wind-spirit
  bodies tied to the Vila clan), **Cryo Wraith**, and **Unbound Remnant**
  (a self-reassembling Chimaera boss-weight body, probably belonging under
  Aberrant Chimeric Monsters). Too thin to verify at all: a second world
  boss, **Immortal Construct** (named in one aggregator summary only); the
  Fatui's own Snezhnaya-native elite rung, already in `atlas.md`.

## 2. Variants

### Fae of Crystalline Flesh (Winged Soarer-Knight / Winged Halberd-Knight / Glacial Warden / Glaciomancer)
Silhouette: game8 frames the line as armored humanoid fae — "Knight" naming
implies armored melee for the Soarer/Halberd pair, "Glaciomancer" implies a
caster; Glacial Warden's own silhouette is UNVERIFIED beyond "mythical
fae." All four are native to Snezhnaya's mountain ranges (Svetloledovka
named as one Glacial Warden location).
- behavior: The shared, verified mechanic is the **Ice Coffin**: "Fae will
  entomb themselves in an Ice Coffin when they are threatened" — a full
  damage-immunity/retreat state, not a partial shield, weakened
  specifically by the Stellar Glimmer reaction and "more easily broken with
  Electro or Anemo attacks" (Fandom, via search snippet), the break
  defeating the body in the same stroke. Narrower than "any reaction with
  Cryo": two named elements and one named reaction. Individual move lists beyond this defensive beat are UNVERIFIED — no
  per-body page was fetched. Intent vocabulary (partial): a hard
  invulnerability/retreat state entered under threat, cleared only by the
  correct reaction — the Fatui Skirmisher shield's logic taken to its
  extreme (total immunity, and the break IS the kill rather than a stun).
- Sources: game8.co archives 615536, 615558.

### Snegovik / Snegurochka (Snowland Fae, common tier)
Silhouette: humanoid, "pale white skin, glowing ice-blue eyes, hair ranging
from bluish-gray to white" (Fandom, via search snippet).
- behavior: "Snegoviks attack with their mastery of Cryo, and when they are
  threatened, they will encase themselves in an ice coffin, granting them a
  Cryo Ward that gradually restores their health" — the SAME Ice Coffin verb
  as Crystalline Flesh, but a regenerating ward rather than a
  hit-and-it's-dead state — the one behavioral difference sourced between
  the two listings. Moves beyond "Cryo mastery" are UNVERIFIED. Intent
  vocabulary: Cryo attack / threatened-state retreat-and-heal (a sustain
  shield rather than Crystalline Flesh's all-or-nothing coffin).
- Sources: WebSearch of genshin-impact.fandom.com for "Snegovik"; not
  independently fetched as a full page (402 on direct Fandom fetch).

### Volkodlak Brute line (Raider / Sharpshooter / Basher / Lobber / Voywolf Hunter / Fanged Zealot / Leomastiff Guard)
Silhouette: beast-humanoid — "possessing... humanoid bodies" with "the
strength of beasts" (Fandom, on Basher); filed under Mystical Beasts,
distinguishing them from the Fae-line bodies despite both being "Snowland
Fae" at the umbrella level. Raider/Sharpshooter are "relatively small and
agile"; Basher is "relatively large and powerfully built."
- behavior: Raider — heightened senses, evades attacks "by instinct alone,"
  a melee skirmisher hard to pin down. Sharpshooter — the same senses
  repurposed for ranged accuracy (bows/crossbows/cannons). Basher —
  brute-strength melee, favored to win on raw physicality. Lobber's kit is
  UNVERIFIED beyond the name. High-tier members "attack with Elemental DMG,
  and a few also attack with Physical DMG" — not mono-elemental. Voywolf
  Hunter, Fanged Zealot, Leomastiff Guard, Channel Contact, Long-Eared
  Striker are confirmed group members by name only. All "spawn in groups
  alongside other Volkodlak enemies" — squad-shaped like the Fatui
  Skirmishers. Intent vocabulary (verified members): melee
  evasion-skirmisher (Raider) / ranged marksman (Sharpshooter) / heavy melee
  brute (Basher) — close to the Skirmisher's Bracer/Legionnaire/Vanguard
  split.
- Sources: WebSearch for "Volkodlak Raider Sharpshooter Basher Lobber
  combat abilities moves" and "Voywolf Hunter / Fanged Zealot / Leomastiff
  Guard" (group membership only); game8.co archives 615492/615552/615554/
  615555 (per-body pages, Raider self-flagged "a work in progress").

### Wildwood Leshy (Juvenile Tree Segment / Rugged Elder Leshy)
Silhouette: tree-spirit Mystical Beasts wandering Snezhnaya's evergreen
sub-zones (Flamefeather Valley, Everfrozen Earth); "Juvenile" vs "Rugged
Elder" implies a size/growth-stage split rather than a role split.
- behavior: Explicitly "no special gimmick when it comes to defeating them"
  (game8) — a plain damage check, "go all out with whichever DPS you
  choose." No move list beyond this was found.
- Sources: game8.co archives 615622.

### Aberrant Chimeric Monster (Armored Beast / Burrowbeast / Horned Bear / Winged Elephant / Winged Lion)
Silhouette: "infusions of ordinary animals, all infused with elemental
energy" via a regional bio-alchemy experiment — mundane fauna (a bear, an
elephant, a burrowing beast) given an elemental overlay, structurally
similar to how Nod-Krai's Radiant Beast family reskins ordinary animals
rather than inventing new body plans. Winged Lion is a two-headed construct
and the family's named boss.
- behavior (Winged Lion, the one fully-sourced member): dual-element,
  Electro and Anemo, oscillating between two enhanced states.
  **Thundercall**: gains an Electro shield/ward; breaking it (preferably
  with Cryo) weakens the boss, and its thunder-blade attempt can be
  disrupted by the Stellar Glimmer reaction (not independently verified
  this pass). **Galeforce**: absorbs Anemo Orbs on the field to charge a
  big attack; destroying the orbs or triggering Stellar Glimmer stops the
  charge. Baseline: a dodgeable projectile volley, a twin-head beam, and a
  leap-then-diving-pounce. Intent vocabulary: shield/ward state (broken by
  the counter-element) → charge-up state (fed by a battlefield resource,
  interruptible by removing it) → alternating between the two → ranged
  volley / beam / leap-and-pounce. The richest, most sim-shaped moveset in
  either regional atlas outside the Fatui.
- behavior (the other four bodies): UNVERIFIED beyond family membership and
  "not considered particularly difficult... rely on elemental attacks, and
  can cause elemental reactions themselves should they coordinate attacks"
  — implying a squad-shaped normal-tier encounter close to the Fatui
  Skirmisher squad doctrine, but no per-body move list was fetched.
- Sources: game8.co archives 615559; WebSearch "Chimeric Winged Lion
  Thundercall Galeforce boss abilities" (page-title match, cross-checked
  against game8.co archives 613886).

## 3. Tier framing

- **FODDER** — Snegovik ("common tier," the most numerous fae per the
  Fandom summary); the four unverified Chimeric normals, by elimination
  since Winged Lion is the family's named boss and nothing marks the rest
  as elite.
- **ELITE** — the whole Volkodlak Brute line is explicitly tagged "Elite
  Enemies" by the Fandom wiki, every member — a family with no fodder rung
  of its own, the inverse of how the Fatui Skirmishers were the WHOLE
  family at fodder tier; the Winged Soarer-Knight/Halberd-Knight/
  Glaciomancer trio also reads elite-weight (armored-knight naming, Ice
  Coffin gate), though not explicitly tagged as such.
- **BOSS** — Chimeric Winged Lion (named, fully statted); Glacial Warden is
  UNVERIFIED elite-vs-boss weight — game8 declines to give it a tier label.
- **MECHANIZED** — none found; Snezhnaya's mechanized content (if any beyond
  the unverified "Immortal Construct" boss) was not surfaced by this pass.

## 4. Raw pairing candidates (Hive / Glory slots; mechanics FROZEN)

- `Infested Prism (Act 2 / Hive ELITE, 161 HP, A8 171; cycle Jab 15 → Radiate
  11 + 16 Block → Whirlwind 5x3 → Pulsate 8 + 20 Block + Vital Spark 2) —
  docs/current/research/act2-act3-roster-research.md §1.2` ↔ **Fae of
  Crystalline Flesh (Glaciomancer)** (plausible) — A caster alternating a
  plain hit with two big-Block beats fits a crystal/ice caster, and Vital
  Spark reads as a debuff a Glaciomancer could plausibly cast; the real Ice
  Coffin mechanic (full invulnerability, broken specifically by a Cryo
  reaction, ending the fight) has no analogue here, so this pairing borrows
  the silhouette without the family's signature mechanic.
- `Hunter Killer (Act 2 / Hive normal, 121 HP; Tenderizing Goop applies
  Tender; Bite 17; Puncture 7x3) — §1.1, Act 2 hard pool '1x Hunter Killer'`
  ↔ **Volkodlak Raider** (strong) — A lean, fast, mark-then-strike body fits
  a small, agile, evasive wolf-fae, and Raider's verified behavior —
  heightened senses, evading attacks by instinct — legibly explains a body
  hard to pin down, which is what Puncture 7x3 plays like from the
  player's side.
- `Turret Operator (Act 3 / Glory normal, 41 HP; Unload! 3x5; Loading +1
  Str) — §2.1, paired with Living Shield in the Act 3 easy pool` ↔
  **Volkodlak Sharpshooter** (strong) — A fragile backline body whose whole
  kit is a ranged multi-hit volley plus a chambering beat is precisely
  Sharpshooter's verified description. Pairs naturally with a Volkodlak
  Basher in the Living Shield slot below, reproducing the tank-plus-gunner
  shape `atlas.md` already established for this base-game pair.
- `Living Shield (Act 3 / Glory normal, 55 HP; 25 Rampart; Shield Slam 6;
  Smash 16 + Str) — §2.1` ↔ **Volkodlak Basher** (strong) — Basher is
  verified as the large, powerfully-built melee member of the line — a big
  standing block value plus two melee swings is exactly that read, and
  slotting it opposite the Sharpshooter reproduces the squad-shape the
  Volkodlak group ships in canon ("spawn in groups alongside other
  Volkodlak enemies").
- `Mecha Knight (Act 3 / Glory ELITE, 300 HP, A8 320; Charge 25 → cycle
  Flamethrower (4 Burns) → Windup +15 Block +5 Str → Heavy Cleave 35;
  Artifact 3) — §2.2` ↔ **Chimeric Winged Lion** (strong) — The
  best-evidenced pairing in this atlas. Windup (+15 Block AND +5 Str, then a
  big hit) is a clean read for Thundercall's shield-then-empowered-attack
  cycle; Charge 25 into an escalating cycle matches Galeforce's
  charge-from-a-battlefield-resource behavior; Heavy Cleave 35 is the
  dive-pounce finisher, and the twin-beam reads as Flamethrower's pollution
  beat re-elemented to Electro. Both bodies alternate between two distinct
  empowered states rather than one linear pattern.
