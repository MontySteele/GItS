# S10b — Nod-Krai Enemy-Family Atlas (EB-752)

Companion to `atlas.md`. Nod-Krai shipped as Genshin Impact's Version 6.0
"Luna I" (September 10, 2025), plus 6.x content on the same map. `atlas.md`'s
Fatui sections already cover the Fatui bodies that happen to live here —
Fatui Skirmishers (pan-national), and, under Fatui elites, the Oprichniki
(low ranks: Line Trooper/Support Trooper/Grenadier/Vanguard/Medic/Bombardier;
elemental ranks: Fireblade Shock Trooper/Hailstorm Cannoneer/Thunderblitz
Gvardiya, which game8 also names "Thunderblitz Druzhina") and the
Landcruisers/Super-Heavy Landrover (MECHANIZED tier). This atlas does not
re-describe any of that — see `atlas.md:402-478` — and spends its words on
what else Nod-Krai actually shipped: two beast/lifeform families new to the
game, one new Abyss sub-faction, and one unique construct boss.

## 1. Family overview

Nod-Krai's NATIVE (non-Fatui, exclusive-to-region) content, as verified
against game8.co faction guides and cross-checked search snippets of the
Fandom wiki (direct Fandom page fetches returned HTTP 402 this session —
see Sources):

- **Frostnight Scion** — an "Elemental Lifeforms"-adjacent family of
  amorphous elemental-crystal bodies, billed ELITE, one per element.
  VERIFIED to exist and to carry the Gloomveiled enhanced-state/immunity
  gimmick. Variant count is UNVERIFIED: game8 names seven location pages
  (Icy Crystal, Tangled Vines, Blazing Brilliance, Gleaming Charge, Gilded
  Sunshard, Surging Currents, Veiled Cloudchaser) but a direct Fandom search
  only turned up six elemental variants (Cryo/Pyro/Electro/Dendro/Hydro/
  Geo) plus a separate "Frostnight Herra" page whose relationship to the
  family — an Anemo variant, or something else — is unconfirmed.
- **Radiant Beast / Radiant Mutant** — a Mystical-Beasts group of
  moon-touched animals (Antelope, Bladehorn, Glacial Wolf, Lunapod as
  overworld bodies; Moonfly and Moongecko as named world bosses). VERIFIED
  via game8 faction guide; shared gimmick is the Radiant Cocoon debuff.
- **Shadow of the Wild Hunt** — a small Abyss-adjacent faction unique to
  Nod-Krai's "frozen wilderness," two bodies (Wilderness Hunter, Wilderness
  Exile). VERIFIED to exist and to carry a fixed combo plus a
  second-life/Grief-Stricken mechanic; individual move damage numbers are
  UNVERIFIED (no numeric statline page was fetchable).
- **Knuckle Duckle** — a unique named world boss (Aino's invention, an
  iron-clad mechanical duck), not a family with variants. VERIFIED.
- Sigurd (Local Legend, Blue Amber Lake) and Crab Tsar (Local Legend,
  resembling a Sternshield Crab) are UNVERIFIED as native families: Crab
  Tsar's own body is confirmed NOT exclusive — a pre-existing Fontaine
  "Fontemer Aberrants" body (Version 4.0) reused as a Local Legend skin —
  and Sigurd's species is unconfirmed beyond "uses Electro," so neither is
  written up below.

## 2. Variants

### Frostnight Scion (Elite elemental lifeform)
Silhouette: an amorphous, faceted elemental-crystal being, one visual per
element (an icy crystal formation for Cryo, a vine-wrapped mass for Dendro,
a molten shard cluster for Pyro, a charged lattice for Electro, a gilded
rock-shard body for Geo, a flowing water-glass form for Hydro).
- behavior: Baseline state carries genuine immunity to its own element (a
  Cryo Scion cannot be hurt by Cryo) plus "considerable" resistance to
  everything else. It periodically enters the **Gloomveiled State**: attacks
  get stronger, but the own-element immunity downgrades to mere resistance
  — the state can ONLY be broken (real damage taken) by hitting it with its
  own element while Gloomveiled, or by a Lunar Reaction at any time. Intent
  vocabulary: enhanced attack while shielded (state up) / normal attack
  while vulnerable-to-chip (state down) / a hard damage-gate on a
  specific-answer counter, the Fatui Skirmisher shield with the polarity
  reversed (immune to own element rather than weak to a counter-element).
- Sources: game8.co archives 550402, 574559/574561/574563; Fandom-domain
  WebSearch of "Frostnight Scion Gloomveiled State" (six elemental variant
  pages returned: Icy Crystal, Blazing Brilliance, Gleaming Charge, Tangled
  Vines, Surging Currents, Gilded Sunshard).

### Radiant Beast family (Radiant Antelope / Bladehorn / Glacial Wolf / Lunapod)
Silhouette: "magical beasts that have undergone unique evolutions due to
age-long immersion in the ancient moon's power" (game8); normal Teyvat
fauna (antelope, wolf, a horned/bladed quadruped, an amphibian-ish
"Lunapod") given a moonlit elemental gilding rather than a redesigned body
plan. Elements: Antelope = Electro, Bladehorn = Anemo, Lunapod = Hydro;
Glacial Wolf's element is UNVERIFIED.
- behavior: Shared gimmick, the **Radiant Cocoon**: landing enough hits
  traps the player in a cocoon status raising damage taken and lowering
  healing received; the ONLY clean answer is healing the affected character
  to full HP, which pops the cocoon and grants a counter-buff window. A
  bind-and-punish-inaction status — the opposite shape from a shield, since
  it punishes NOT acting rather than gating behind a specific attack.
  Intent vocabulary: elemental melee attack / debuff-apply (bind) / a
  build-then-release window gated on the PLAYER choosing to heal, not on
  hitting a specific element.
- Sources: game8.co archives 550406, 550530, 550534.

### Radiant Moonfly / Radiant Moongecko (named world bosses, Radiant Beast family)
Silhouette UNVERIFIED beyond naming.
- behavior: Moonfly "debuffs all characters, causing HP loss and reduced
  damage output" (ldshop) — a persistent party-wide drain-and-suppress aura
  rather than the single-target Radiant Cocoon; Moongecko's kit is
  UNVERIFIED beyond family membership. Intent vocabulary (Moonfly, partial):
  ambient party debuff (HP drain + damage suppression) managed by sustained
  healing/shielding rather than burst-answered.
- Sources: ldshop.gg summary, cross-checked against game8.co archives
  550406; no per-boss page independently fetched.

### Shadow of the Wild Hunt — Wilderness Hunter / Wilderness Exile
Silhouette UNVERIFIED beyond "abyss enemies that stalk the frozen
wilderness when the pitch-dark mist rolls in" (game8); dual-axe wielding
per the move names implies a humanoid/beast-humanoid frame.
- behavior: Wilderness Hunter runs a fixed three-beat melee combo —
  **Right-Hand Cleave → Left-Hand Claw Smash → Dual-Axe Spinning Slash** —
  escalating across the string. On being reduced to 0 HP it (like all Wild
  Hunt bodies) enters a **Grief-Stricken state** instead of dying: it gains
  additional HP and keeps fighting, a scripted second life. **Ascendant
  Gleam** is the player's tool to shortcut or finish that second phase.
  Intent vocabulary: fixed melee combo (three escalating beats) / an
  HP-based phase-transition that looks like a kill but isn't / a
  specific-tool finisher window.
- Sources: game8.co archives 550407.

### Knuckle Duckle (unique named boss, mechanized — Aino's invention)
Silhouette: "a massive, iron-clad avian brawler" — a giant scrap-metal duck
construct built by the playable character Aino, distinct from both the
Fatui Landcruiser line and the beast families above.
- behavior: Resists all forms of damage by default. Periodically enters
  **Duckstruction Mode** (heavier attacks) and **summons five Ducks/Stamping
  Devices**, each holding a fragment of the boss's own resistance. An
  Electro-Charged or Lunar-Charged reaction on a summon turns it hostile to
  the boss and weakens it; killing the summons is how the fight is actually
  won. Intent vocabulary: resist-everything baseline / enhanced-aggression
  phase / summon a wave of sub-bodies that are the real damage race /
  reaction-vulnerability on the summons that both punishes and buffs.
- Sources: game8.co archives 546862, cross-checked via WebSearch summary
  (Duckstruction Mode, Stamping Devices, team-comp note).

## 3. Tier framing

- **FODDER** — no clearly-fodder native family was found; Nod-Krai's new
  non-Fatui content reads as elite-and-up across the board (Fatui
  Skirmishers/Oprichniki low ranks remain the fodder rung here, per
  `atlas.md`).
- **ELITE** — Frostnight Scion (explicitly billed ELITE by game8); the
  Radiant Beast normals sit at a mid weight, UNVERIFIED which side of the
  normal/elite line the game draws them on; Shadow of the Wild Hunt reads
  elite-weight given the Grief-Stricken second-life mechanic.
- **BOSS** — Knuckle Duckle; Radiant Moonfly and Moongecko (named world
  bosses within the Radiant Beast family).
- **MECHANIZED** — the Fatui Landcruiser line (already in `atlas.md`);
  Knuckle Duckle is a second, DIFFERENT mechanized body (Aino's, not Fatui)
  and should not be conflated with it for pairing purposes.

## 4. Raw pairing candidates (Hive / Glory slots; mechanics FROZEN)

- `Infested Prism (Act 2 / Hive ELITE, 161 HP, A8 171; cycle Jab 15 → Radiate
  11 + 16 Block → Whirlwind 5x3 → Pulsate 8 + 20 Block + Vital Spark 2) —
  §1.2` ↔ **Frostnight Scion** (strong) — An elite that alternates a plain
  hit with two separate BIG BLOCK beats (16, then 20) is the shape of a body
  re-hardening between damage windows, and "Vital Spark: your Skills are
  Tainted" — punishing a specific card TYPE — is a clean analogue for
  "immune to a specific element": both say "your normal tool doesn't work
  here." Elite tier matches Genshin's own ELITE billing; "Prism" is already
  a crystal-coded name.
- `Aeonglass (Act 3 / Glory BOSS, 512 HP, A8 535; cycle Ebb 22 + 33 Block →
  Eye Lasers 11x2 → Increasing Intensity; Withering Presence: every 6 cards
  played adds a Wither to hand; Artifact 3) — §2.3` ↔ **Radiant Moonfly**
  (strong) — Both are ambient, ongoing-attrition bosses whose core threat is
  a ticking party-wide drain that punishes passive/greedy play, and both
  are explicitly countered by active player management (sustained healing
  for Moonfly per its own description; clearing Withers before they
  trigger for Aeonglass) rather than a single burst answer.
- `Test Subject (Act 3 / Glory BOSS, 3 phases 100/200/300 HP; Adaptable:
  revives into the next phase; P2 Painful Stabs shuffles Wounds on unblocked
  damage; P3 gains Intangible every other turn) — §2.3` ↔ **Shadow of the
  Wild Hunt (Wilderness Hunter, boss-scaled)** (strong) — Grief-Stricken is
  the same shape as Adaptable: the body appears to die, doesn't, and comes
  back changed rather than ending the fight, and Ascendant Gleam (the tool
  that shortcuts that second life) reads onto whatever finisher-punish the
  sim already models for the later phases. The escalating fixed combo
  (Right-Hand Cleave → Left-Hand Claw Smash → Dual-Axe Spinning Slash) maps
  cleanly onto a phase's repeating attack string.
- `Queen + Torch Head Amalgam (Act 3 / Glory BOSS; Queen 400 HP A8 419 +
  Amalgam 199 HP; Amalgam alive: Queen casts Burn Bright for Me (+1 Str to
  Amalgam, +20 Block self) on a loop; after Amalgam dies: Queen Enrages
  then Off with Your Head / Execution) — §2.3` ↔ **Knuckle Duckle** (strong)
  — The closest mechanical match in the set: a main boss measurably
  weaker/differently-postured while a SECONDARY body is alive, whose real
  fight is dealing with that secondary body. Knuckle Duckle's Duck/
  Stamping-Device summons are exactly that, and reacting them hostile
  (Electro-Charged/Lunar-Charged) maps onto killing the Amalgam to end its
  buff loop. Scale gap (solo world boss vs. a fixed two-body encounter) is
  the one seam a ruling should weigh.
