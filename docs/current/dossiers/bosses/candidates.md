## - [ ] VANTOM (Act 1 boss slot A)
*173 HP · silhouette: the single-target burst race — a lone body with a four-beat menu (Ink Blot 7 · Inky Lance 6x2 · Prepare +2 Str · Dismember 27) that announces its one huge swing a turn early and has no adds, no phases, and no second bar · unimplemented in sim: Wound injection, Slippery 9*

### 1. Andrius, Lupus Boreas — Mondstadt — loose

**Identity (one line):** The Dominator of Wolves, met in the open snow of Wolvendom rather than in any sealed domain — the wolf-king who has to be put down before he finishes circling.

**Flavor direction:** No door, no cutscene, no difficulty select — you simply walk too far into Wolvendom and the pack goes quiet. He paints a ring of ice on the ground and leaps into it; that is the whole opening ceremony. The fight is a lot of close, physical, snow-throwing brawling with one long-wound-up swing you can see coming from across the clearing, and after every heavy commitment he has to plant his feet and turn back toward you — the beat where he is simply standing there, breathing, is the beat the fight is really about. If you are too slow he starts calling the smaller wolves in, and the clearing stops being a duel.

**Why this boss fits the silhouette:** The dossier records Andrius's baseline as a short randomized menu of front-arc and gap-close attacks interleaved with a self-inflicted recovery beat (`dossiers.md:13`, `:44`) — the same object as the shipped four-intent rotation with a telegraphed heavy. He is the pool's one boss whose structure *rewards* burst rather than clamping it: enough damage in a single strike skips the Interlude and Phase 2 outright (`dossiers.md:31`, `:53`), which is the burst-race lesson stated in the boss's own rules. The mismatch to name: Andrius's later content is add-spam and destructible pillars (`dossiers.md:17`, `:30`), and Vantom has neither adds nor phases — a 173 HP body ends inside his Phase 1, so the mapping keeps his opening and discards the arena he later arms.

**Trade-off:** You buy the pool's cleanest burst-race argument by using only the first third of a five-beat canon fight; the Frozen Pillars and the phantom wolves that make Andrius *Andrius* have nowhere to sit.

**Claims & citations:**
- open-world Wolvendom arena, not a Trounce Domain; scales to World Level; despawns if you leave — `docs/current/dossiers/bosses/dossiers.md:12`, `:34`
- opener is Wolf Leap, telegraphed by an ice circle painted on the ground — `docs/current/dossiers/bosses/dossiers.md:13`
- baseline is a short randomized menu with a Repositioning recovery beat after hind-leg attacks (the fight's clearest free-hit window) — `docs/current/dossiers/bosses/dossiers.md:13`, `:51`
- burst-gate phase skipping: enough damage in one strike bypasses the Interlude and/or Phase 2 — `docs/current/dossiers/bosses/dossiers.md:31`, `:53`
- low-HP escalation is add frequency, not bigger hits — `docs/current/dossiers/bosses/dossiers.md:19`, `:30`
- launch weekly boss (28 September 2020), quest-gated behind Razor's Story Quest — `docs/current/dossiers/bosses/dossiers.md:9`
- shipped Vantom silhouette (173 HP, four intents, Slippery/Wound unimplemented) — `tier05/content/act1_pool.yaml:156-168`

### 2. La Signora — "The Fair Lady," Eighth of the Fatui Harbingers — Snezhnaya (fought at Tenshukaku, Inazuma) — loose

**Identity (one line):** The Fair Lady in her first, colder body: a duellist who opens with the same two moves every single time and punishes the single biggest number you can produce.

**Flavor direction:** She is waiting when you arrive, and she does not hurry. The opening is a courtesy: five shards in a tight spread, then a wheel of ice that curves — the same greeting every time, so that by the third run you know you are being greeted. Everything she touches leaves frost on the floor behind it, so the room gets smaller while nothing dramatic appears to be happening. And if you hit her too hard in one blow, she does not stagger; she detonates, and the cold arrives all at once.

**Why this boss fits the silhouette:** Signora's Phase 1 is a fixed scripted opener plus a mid-range menu (`dossiers.md:270`, `:288`), which is the shape of a short deterministic intent list, and The Frozen Void — a spear that lands and then detonates on a delay (`dossiers.md:271`) — is the announce-then-land grammar Vantom's Prepare→Dismember beat already runs. Pain for Pain, her per-instance cap that nullifies overkill and retaliates (`dossiers.md:271`, `:281`, `:303`), is the closest thing in the eleven-boss pool to Vantom's *unimplemented* Slippery 9. ⚑ That is also the mismatch, and it is a real one: Pain for Pain teaches "do not burst," and Vantom's ratified role is a burst race — the strongest canon rhyme here argues against the base's own design intent, and it rhymes with a mechanic the sim does not run.

**Trade-off:** The mapping's best argument is written on a mechanic flagged UNIMPLEMENTED (`act1_pool.yaml:159`; cf. `docs/current/dossiers/remap/reskin-gallery.md:117` — art must not promise the missing cap), and her defining Cryo→Pyro mirror-flip has no second phase to live in on a 173 HP body.

**Claims & citations:**

- The Frozen Void: impact then a 4-second delayed detonation — `docs/current/dossiers/bosses/dossiers.md:271`

### 3. The Knave (Arlecchino) — "Cinder of Two Worlds' Flames" — Fontaine (Fatui) — loose

**Identity (one line):** The Knave's opening duel — four committed swings in a plain room, each one paid for out of her own blood.

**Flavor direction:** Scattered Ruins is deliberately empty: no hazards, no summons, nothing to hide behind, just her and a scythe and a great deal of space. Every swing is slow and enormous and leaves her standing in it a moment too long, which is the only invitation you are going to get. Nothing else in the room helps you; nothing else in the room helps her either. She takes a cut out of herself to throw the big ones and takes it straight back out of you when they land.

**Why this boss fits the silhouette:** Phase 1's baseline is literally four short self-contained melee intents that resolve in one or two hits each, with the punish window sitting in the recovery after each committed swing (`dossiers.md:626`) — a four-beat table with a telegraphed heavy (Reaper Dive, `dossiers.md:626`) reads onto Vantom's rotation with nothing left over. She has zero adds and no enrage across both phases (`dossiers.md:626`, `:643`), and the cross-boss memo ranks her the most compact state space in the pool — no adds, no environmental hazards, no timers, a plain arena by design (`pattern-memo.md:136`). Mismatch: Ranged Immunity, a hard damage-nullify state whenever she is at distance and not mid-attack (`dossiers.md:626`, `:638`), is a defensive silhouette Vantom simply does not have, and her HP-cost/lifesteal economy (`dossiers.md:633`, `:639`) has no beat in the shipped list.

**Trade-off:** ⚑ Stature: she is the ninth weekly boss added (4.6) and a Harbinger of the first rank — spending the roster's heaviest Fatui name on a 173 HP first-act body, in a pool that already has Childe fixed elsewhere, risks reading the whole boss pool as a Fatui bracket.

**Claims & citations:**
- Phase 1 baseline: four short self-contained melee intents, punish window in the recovery; no enrage timer, no adds — `docs/current/dossiers/bosses/dossiers.md:626`
- Reaper Dive (rises and dives, 100% ATK) as the heavy — `docs/current/dossiers/bosses/dossiers.md:626`
- zero summons/minions across both phases — `docs/current/dossiers/bosses/dossiers.md:643`
- Ranged Immunity: outright dodge (pre-70%) then block, a nullify state rather than a resistance — `docs/current/dossiers/bosses/dossiers.md:626`, `:638`
- Bond of Life economy: 1.5% Max HP to attack, 3% refunded on connect — `docs/current/dossiers/bosses/dossiers.md:633`, `:639`
- most compact state space in the pool; no adds, no hazards, no timers, plain arena by design — `docs/current/dossiers/bosses/pattern-memo.md:136`

## - [ ] LAGAVULIN MATRIARCH (Act 1 boss slot B)
*222 HP · silhouette: the anti-turtle clock — three free setup turns behind 12 Plating, then a five-beat cycle (Slash 19 · Disembowel 9x2 · Slash 12 · +12 block · Soul Siphon +2 Str) that keeps her half of the theft, so every pass through the cycle hits harder than the last · unimplemented in sim: the Plating damage cap, Soul Siphon's player-side stat drain*

### 1. Magatsu Mitake Narukami no Mikoto — Inazuma — loose

**Identity (one line):** The Shogun's shadow-self in the Oneiric Euthymia, fought against a meter that fills whether you act or not — and faster every time she touches you.

**Flavor direction:** The dream-arena is a flat circle with nothing in it; the only furniture is the second bar under her health, which was already filling before you drew a card. She fights in two stances and tells you which one by where she blinks to. Then the bar caps, the armor comes on, and for a stretch of the fight your job is not to kill her but to get her out of it — and while it is up, the thing draining is not your health, it is your ability to do anything at all.

**Why this boss fits the silhouette:** The Ominous Destiny gauge is the pool's purest anti-turtle clock: no HP threshold exists anywhere in this fight, the meter fills passively on a schedule AND accelerates when she lands hits (`dossiers.md:343`, `:349`), so slow play and eaten damage both summon the punishing state — the same lesson the shipped cycle teaches by keeping Soul Siphon's +2 Str every pass. Baleful Shadowlord is a self-decaying shield plus a large global RES bonus (`dossiers.md:344`, `:355`), which is a Plating-shaped defensive window on the boss rather than a break puzzle. And her Energy drain — the dossier's note that she is the only Genshin boss whose signature mechanic attacks the player's resource generation rather than their HP (`dossiers.md:344`, `:356`) — is the nearest canon relative to Soul Siphon's backlogged player-side stat theft. Mismatch: she never sleeps; her opener is a distance-gated read (`dossiers.md:338`), so the base's three free setup turns have no canon beat to sit on.

**Trade-off:** ⚑ Stature: this is an Archon's manifestation, quest-gated behind Raiden's Story Quest (`dossiers.md:334`) — the biggest name any Act 1 slot could carry, and the pool's answer to Vantom becomes a god.

**Claims & citations:**
- Ominous Destiny meter: 900 cap, +20 every 2s passively, +40 when she lands a hit; no HP threshold anywhere in the fight — `docs/current/dossiers/bosses/dossiers.md:343`, `:349`
- Baleful Shadowlord: Electro shield, +210% global RES while shielded, self-drains ~8.3% per 10s and eventually expires on its own — `docs/current/dossiers/bosses/dossiers.md:344`, `:355`
- active character loses 20 Energy every 5s in that state; the only Genshin boss whose signature mechanic attacks resource generation rather than HP — `docs/current/dossiers/bosses/dossiers.md:344`, `:356`
- shield break = full party Energy refund plus a stunned, RES-reduced boss; the cycle then repeats indefinitely — `docs/current/dossiers/bosses/dossiers.md:346`
- two-stance normal state with explicit switch moves and teleport-direction tells — `docs/current/dossiers/bosses/dossiers.md:338`, `:341`, `:350`
- Version 2.5, End of the Oneiric Euthymia, gated behind Imperatrix Umbrosa Act II — `docs/current/dossiers/bosses/dossiers.md:334`

### 2. Azhdaha, Sealed Lord of Vishaps — Liyue — loose

**Identity (one line):** The sealed dragon under the Dragon-Queller tree: something that was asleep and armored until the containment broke, and is worse every time it comes back around.

**Flavor direction:** The arena is a closed cavern ringed with eroded stone steles held up by chains — Zhongli's work, and clearly old. The fight starts because the seal fails, not because anything approaches you. He is enormous and slow and made of the floor, and the first thing that happens is that nothing happens fast: rock, then more rock. Later the same swings come back wearing a different element, the same wind-up with something new bolted to the end of it, and the room fills with leftovers from the last few minutes.

**Why this boss fits the silhouette:** The dormancy is his premise, not a flourish — he is the *Sealed* Lord, and he opens the encounter by shattering a containment forcefield in a sealed arena (`dossiers.md:204`, `:208`, `:228`), which is the only canon opener in the pool that reads as three turns of a boss not yet fighting. His escalation is done by keeping every intent's skeleton and animation and bolting riders onto it while his resistances climb (`dossiers.md:211`, `:217`, `:219`, `:243`) — the same cycle coming round harder, which is what Soul Siphon's kept +2 Str does to the shipped five-beat rotation. The armor argument is real too: 70% Geo / 40% Physical from the first second plus scripted damage floors at each transition (`dossiers.md:217`, `:222`). ⚑ Mismatch, and it is the load-bearing one: Azhdaha has no enrage timer and no soft enrage of any kind (`dossiers.md:209`, `:213`; `pattern-memo.md:65`) — the clock is precisely the thing he does not have, so this mapping serves the silhouette's *body* and not its stated role.

**Trade-off:** ⚑ Collision: Azhdaha is already claimed as a candidate (P) for the Act 3 boss **Test Subject** in `docs/current/dossiers/remap/reskin-gallery.md:72`, on the argument that three phases re-element the whole kit — taking him for Act 1 spends that body and that argument.

### 3. All-Devouring Narwhal — Fontaine — loose

**Identity (one line):** The Star-Swallowing Whale: a thing that spends most of the fight under the floor, surfaces to take one bite, and swallows faster the longer you let it live.

**Flavor direction:** The seabed is solid ground that the whale treats as water. For long stretches it is simply not there — you can see the wake, you cannot reach it — and then it comes up through the floor exactly where you are standing and goes back down. Nothing about it hurries at first. Then it hurries: the gaps get shorter, the surfacings come closer together, and the last stretch of the fight is the same attacks arriving twice as often.

**Why this boss fits the silhouette:** The Fury meter is an accelerating clock rather than a damage ramp — accrual permanently doubles partway through and never reverts, so the cycle compresses instead of the numbers growing (`dossiers.md:572`, `:577`), and the cross-boss memo classes this as the fight's only enrage (`pattern-memo.md:65`). Its submerge/surface loop is a long untargetable beat followed by one telegraphed attack (`dossiers.md:549`, `:581`) — the nearest canon reading of an opening in which the base body is not yet fighting. Soul Siphon's fiction is also native here: the phase transition is the boss eating the party (`dossiers.md:560`, `:578`). Mismatch: none of the two-arena, second-HP-bar, interior-sub-boss structure (`dossiers.md:560`, `:587`) has anywhere to go on a single 222 HP body with five intents, so the mapping keeps the surface loop and drops the fight the boss is actually famous for.

**Trade-off:** ⚑ Stature and gating: a world-devouring leviathan behind the Fontaine Archon Quest finale (`dossiers.md:545`) is a large register jump for a first-act slot, and the mapping quietly discards the devour — the boss's entire identity.

**Claims & citations:**
- Fury meter: 1/sec for the first 80 seconds then 2/sec permanently, +1 per 1% Max HP lost, +30 per Eye destroyed, 120 threshold; Phase 1 is not HP-gated at all — `docs/current/dossiers/bosses/dossiers.md:559`, `:577`
- the fight accelerates rather than escalating — a compressing cycle, the closest thing to an enrage — `docs/current/dossiers/bosses/dossiers.md:572`; `docs/current/dossiers/bosses/pattern-memo.md:65`
- submerge-gated targetability: long invulnerable travel beat, one surfaced attack, re-submerge — `docs/current/dossiers/bosses/dossiers.md:549`, `:581`

### 4. Stormterror Dvalin — Mondstadt — loose

*(Added in a second drafting pass; provisional placement is high — [USER]'s curation orders. It is the only slot-B variant whose canon carries the "punishes slow play" half of the ratified role without invention.)*

**Identity (one line):** A dragon who slept five hundred years in a dead god's tower with poison in his veins, and who is worse company the longer the fight runs.

**Flavor direction:** The broken spire of Stormterror's Lair — a ring of separated stone platforms over a drop, and the only Trounce Domain in Teyvat that makes you watch a cutscene before it will even let you choose a difficulty. He wears a ward you have to get through before any damage you deal means anything; when it breaks he comes down onto the stone and lies there, and you get exactly one window before it grows back. Nothing about the room improves while you take your time. It only ever gets less floor.

**Why this boss fits the silhouette:** The ward is a non-elemental gate worth 20% of Max HP behind which his real HP is functionally untouchable (`dossiers.md:87`), which is the published relative of the 12 Plating on her opening beat — and the memo names that break-then-cash loop the single most turn-legible structure in the pool (`pattern-memo.md:103`). The anti-turtle half is his in canon rather than borrowed: his phases advance on a break count **or a wall clock and never on HP** (`dossiers.md:88`), so a slow party reaches the terminal state with more of the arena destroyed, and that terminal state is explicitly a sustain race "where undergeared parties lose — not to a burst check but to the compounding floor tick outpacing their healing" (`dossiers.md:84`). #### Register implications

Stated in the S2 idiom of `docs/current/dossiers/content/event-conversion-gallery.md:24-77` — each is a predicate a script can check over a gallery file. These extend the existing register blocks; no conflict with them was found, and the one adjacency (Fatui) is called out explicitly.

**Weekly bosses (Trounce Domain entities) — new register block.**
- A weekly-boss mapping **always** names the canonical Trounce Domain and its region in the flavor paragraph (e.g. "The Realm of Beginnings, Apep's Resort, Desert of Hadramaveth, Sumeru"); it **never** invents an arena, domain, or altar name. *Predicate:* for every `### N.` block whose base entity is in the `boss:` list of an act pool, the flavor paragraph must contain a domain string that appears in `docs/current/dossiers/bosses/dossiers.md`.
- A weekly boss **never** bargains, trades, prices, or offers a choice to the player. Trounce entities are not merchants and not event NPCs; the Liyue-mercantile and Adventurers'-Guild registers (`gallery.md:70-73`, `:59-61`) **never** apply to a boss body. *Predicate:* no bracketed `[choice]` line and no price/contract vocabulary inside a weekly-boss block.
- A weekly boss's minions **always** carry their published canonical names (Proliferating Organism variants, Nirvana Engines, Tenebrous Mimiflora, Crimson Lotus Moths); a boss that publishes add names **never** gets invented ones. *Predicate:* any minion noun inside a weekly-boss block must appear in that boss's dossier section.

**One body, one slot.**
- A Genshin weekly boss is **always** claimed by at most one base entity across the reskin/gallery corpus; the same weekly-boss name **never** appears as a candidate under two different base entities. *Predicate:* over `docs/current/dossiers/remap/reskin-gallery.md` plus the Track Q fragments, `count(distinct base-entity headings citing boss X) <= 1`. **Currently satisfied** — the only cross-file recurrence is Azhdaha, and it recurs on the *same* base entity (TEST SUBJECT), which is concordance, not a claim conflict. ⚑ [USER] to confirm that same-entity recurrence across two streams is permitted rather than requiring one stream to yield.

**Boss-pool nation spread.**
- An act's boss pool **never** draws all of its committed entries from a single nation. *Predicate:* for each `boss:` list in an act pool, `count(distinct nation over chosen mappings) >= 2`. ⚑ This is the rule the Apep + Shouki pairing would break (both Sumeru); Apep + Eroded Primal Fire (Sumeru + Natlan) and Azhdaha + Shouki (Liyue + Sumeru) satisfy it.

**Fatui — extension of the existing block, not a replacement.**

A **Harbinger** body inherits it with one addition: a Harbinger is **always** introduced by rank-and-epithet on first mention ("the Eighth of the Fatui Harbingers, the Fair Lady") and **never** by given name alone. *Predicate:* first mention of a Harbinger in a block contains an ordinal or an epithet. ⚑ Note the strain a boss body puts on "threat is never overt" — a Harbinger fought as an Act 3 boss is overt by construction; [USER] should rule whether the boss register overrides the faction register or whether Harbinger bodies are simply excluded from the Fatui block.

La Signora satisfies this from canon (domain subtitled "Memories: Duel to the Fiery Death", `dossiers.md:266`; `deceased` field confirmed at draft time). ⚑ **Dvalin does not** — Confront Stormterror is a live domain and Dvalin is a redeemed friend of Mondstadt by the end of the Prologue, so an Act 3 slot that re-fights him weekly is a fiction call, on top of the co-op ⚑ already filed against that variant.

# §Registers

Register rules, stated **lint-ably** in the S2 idiom: each is a rule a script could check,
with its predicate spelled out, so future boss drafts can be verified mechanically rather
than by taste. These are **proposed**, not ratified. Each act fragment above also carries
its own `#### Register implications` block with the full text; this section collects the
rules that are **pool-wide** or that three drafters reached independently.

## RQ2-A — Naming (the rules with the least judgement in them)

- **RQ2-A1 — A weekly-boss entity ALWAYS carries its enemy-archive name, NEVER the
  playable character's name**, where the two differ. Predicate: for any entity in a boss
  pool sourced from the weekly-boss dossiers, `entity.name ∉ playable_roster_names`; fail
  on "Raiden Shogun", "Scaramouche", "Wanderer", "Balladeer", "Arlecchino", "Tartaglia"
  as a *bare* entity name. Playable names may appear only in a parenthetical. The
  dossiers are already written this way — "Magatsu Mitake Narukami no Mikoto", "Shouki no
  Kami, the Prodigal", "The Knave". Two drafters reached this independently; the Act 2
  drafter sharpened the predicate to key on the wiki page's `{{About}}` line, which is
  the machine-checkable form: if a boss page's `{{About}}` points at a playable
  character, boss-facing text uses the enemy entity name or enemy title only.
- **RQ2-A2 — A Fatui Harbinger boss ALWAYS carries both a personal name and an archive
  title in the entity line, NEVER a bare given name.** Predicate: if
  `entity.faction == "Fatui"` and `entity.tier == "boss"`, the name string must contain a
  separator (`—`, `,`, or `"`) plus both a proper name and a title substring —
  "La Signora — The Fair Lady, Eighth of the Fatui Harbingers". Consistent with the
  gallery's existing Fatui/Northland Bank block, which already legislates naming precision
  for that faction.
- **RQ2-A3 — A weekly-boss entity line ALWAYS uses the canon arena name and NEVER invents
  a location.** Predicate: the location token must match a name attested in
  `docs/current/dossiers/bosses/dossiers.md` — Wolvendom, Beneath the Dragon-Queller, Narukami
  Island: Tenshukaku, End of the Oneiric Euthymia, Scattered Ruins, The Realm of
  Beginnings, Joururi Workshop, Stone Stele Records, Stormterror's Lair, Shadow of Another
  World. Direct mirror of the Melusine rule that Fontaine's research body is always the
  canon institute and never an invented name.
- **RQ2-A4 — A boss that publishes add names NEVER gets invented ones.** Predicate: any
  minion noun inside a weekly-boss block must appear in that boss's own dossier section
  (Proliferating Organism variants, Nirvana Engines, Tenebrous Mimiflora, Crimson Lotus
  Moths, Magatsu Electroculi).
- **RQ2-A5 — Mondstadt's Four Winds ALWAYS appear under both folk name and archive title,
  NEVER as an anonymous beast.** Predicate: if the entity matches a Four Winds member the
  string must carry both tokens ("Andrius, Lupus Boreas"; "Stormterror Dvalin") and the
  flavor block must not describe it as a nameless or mindless animal. They are titled
  protectors gone wrong.
- **RQ2-A6 — A dragon sealed or corrupted by a named power ALWAYS names that power, and
  is NEVER presented as mindless.** Predicate: flavor block contains "sealed" or
  "corrupted" ⇒ must also contain the sealer's or corruptor's name (Azhdaha ⇒ Morax and
  the Dragon-Queller; Dvalin ⇒ Durin's poison and the Anemo Archon). This is what keeps
  Track Q's dragons from collapsing into the atlas's Geovishap register.

## RQ2-B — The convergent framing rule

- **RQ2-B1 — A body the wiki marks `deceased`, or one the Archon Quest has resolved, is
  ALWAYS framed as memory, re-enactment, or record, and NEVER as a live present-tense
  encounter.** Predicate: if a candidate's infobox carries a `deceased` field, or the
  boss's domain name contains "Memories" / "Records", the flavor paragraph must contain a
  memory marker (`memory`, `memories`, `re-enactment`, `record`, `echo`, `as it was`,
  `remembered`) and must not open with a present-tense arrival verb (`arrives`, `enters`,
  `emerges`, `appears`) applied to that entity. **Why it exists:** a rotating pool
  re-draws its bosses every run, so a canonically dead body fought weekly is a fiction
  problem, not a mechanics problem. Two drafters reached this independently.
  La Signora satisfies it from canon — her domain is subtitled "Memories: Duel to the
  Fiery Death" and her infobox `deceased` field reads "Executed by Raiden Shogun in
  *Duel Before the Throne*", both confirmed at draft time.
  ⚑ **Dvalin does not satisfy it**: Confront Stormterror is a live domain and Dvalin is a
  redeemed friend of Mondstadt by the end of the Prologue, so any slot that re-fights him
  is a fiction call on top of the co-op flag already filed against that variant.
- **RQ2-B2 — A weekly boss NEVER bargains, trades, prices, or offers the player a
  choice.** Trounce entities are not merchants and not event NPCs; the Liyue-mercantile
  and Adventurers'-Guild registers never apply to a boss body. Predicate: no bracketed
  `[choice]` line and no price/contract vocabulary inside a weekly-boss block. Note this
  is the boss-side twin of Q1's RQ1-B1 and points the opposite way: an Ancient never
  *sells*, a boss never *offers*.

## RQ2-C — Pool-composition rules (the ones with teeth)

- **RQ2-C1 — An act's boss pool NEVER draws every entry from one nation.** Predicate:
  `distinct(nation(entry) for entry in act.boss_pool) >= 2`. This is the register-side
  twin of the ratified §10.0/§10.5 "check opposite shapes" rule — two bosses that oppose
  mechanically but share a homeland read as one fight in two costumes. Three live
  breaches as currently ordered, all flagged in place: Andrius + Dvalin would commit
  Act 1 entirely to Mondstadt (and they are canonically the 1.0 launch pair); Shouki +
  Apep would commit Act 2 entirely to Sumeru; Apep + Shouki would do the same to Act 3.
  ⚑ **[USER] must also decide which field the predicate reads** — faction or staging.
  La Signora and Magatsu Mitake Narukami are Snezhnayan and Inazuman by faction, but both
  fights are *staged* in Inazuma, so a staging-based predicate fails a pair that a
  faction-based predicate passes.
- **RQ2-C2 — A two-body base encounter takes ONE source.** Predicate: both halves of a
  paired base body (KAISER CRAB) come from a single Genshin entity's two forms/copies, or
  a single faction of a single nation; never two unrelated entities across the pair. The
  base bodies share `on_ally_death`, so the fiction must supply a reason the survivor
  inherits.
- **RQ2-C3 — A Genshin weekly boss is claimed by at most ONE base entity across the
  reskin/gallery corpus.** Predicate: over `docs/current/dossiers/remap/reskin-gallery.md` plus
  these fragments, `count(distinct base-entity headings citing boss X) <= 1`.
  ⚑ **Currently breached** — see assembly note 2; the eleven-boss pool is oversubscribed
  by design of the parallel drafting, and spending it is a curation act.
- **RQ2-C4 — A boss body already claimed in `docs/current/dossiers/remap/reskin-gallery.md` for a
  different base body NEVER appears here as an unflagged candidate.** Predicate: intersect
  candidate names against the reskin-gallery candidate column; any hit must carry a ⚑.

## RQ2-D — Honesty rules (inherited from the atlas, restated at boss tier)

- **RQ2-D1 — A candidate argument NEVER rests on a mechanic flagged UNIMPLEMENTED in the
  shipped YAML without saying so in the same line.** Predicate: if a variant's "why it
  fits" cites Slippery, Plating-as-cap, Wound injection, Soul Siphon's player-side drain,
  the pick-your-poison arms, Back Attack positioning, Enlarging Strike's growth, P1
  Enrage 2, P3 Intangible, Artifact 3, or Wither's upgrade-on-reapply, the trade-off line
  must name the flag. Inherited verbatim from `reskin-gallery.md:117`. Several variants
  across all three acts trip this, and each declares it.
- **RQ2-D2 — Flavor NEVER promises an unimplemented mechanic as something that happens.**
  The stricter sibling of RQ2-D1, aimed at scene text rather than argument.
- **RQ2-D3 — Akasha hardware stays rationed.** ⚑ The gallery already rations
  dead-Akasha-terminal scenes and Brain Leech's literal owns the device. The Shouki
  mapping's Neo Akasha Terminal is the flagged collision: it may be described as a fixture
  of that boss's own arena, never as another loose terminal the player finds.
- **RQ2-D4 — The Akademiya block carries the Knowledge Demon slot.** Any Knowledge Demon
  flavor using an Akademiya/darshan/Matra frame is dry and publication-obsessed, candid
  about dubious methodology without apologising, institutional disapproval offstage.
  Never slapstick, never onstage Matra enforcement.
- ⚑ **RQ2-D5 — the Fatui strain, unresolved.** The gallery's Fatui register is "silky
  contractual menace, threat never overt." A Harbinger fought as an act boss is overt by
  construction. [USER] rules whether the boss register overrides the faction register, or
  whether Harbinger bodies are simply excluded from the Fatui block.

promote adds to co-boss stature (Apep's two Wardens). This is stated up front in the
fragment rather than buried per-variant, and it is the single strongest argument for the
fork in note 4.

(100 → 200 → 300, reviving with a new moveset each bar). **With Childe fixed elsewhere,
exactly one true sequential-full-bar body remains in the whole pool** — Apep — and every
other TEST SUBJECT candidate approximates the shape with HP gates on a single bar. That
is stated per entry rather than smoothed over.

**7. Sourcing.** Every claim carries a dossier/memo line reference or a URL fetched at
