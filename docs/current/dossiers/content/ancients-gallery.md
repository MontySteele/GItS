### 1. Orobashi, the Watatsumi Omikami — Inazuma / Watatsumi Island (Sangonomiya worship) — loose

**Identity (one line):** The drowned coral serpent-god who broke pieces off his own body to give his people a place to stand, met once at the water's edge before the road begins.

**Flavor direction:** The road out starts at a tide flat under the serpent's ribs, where coral the color of a lamp still holds light after two thousand years. There is no priest here and no shrine, only the bones and the sound of water going out. The god does not appear; a length of coral warms in your hand and something enormous and patient sorts through what it has left to give — a conch that still remembers a voice, a rod, a pearl, a knot of old bone, a coin-weight of gold, a ring of stars. It gave away its body once already, in pieces, so that people could go somewhere. It is unsurprised to be asked again. Take one. The tide is going out and it will not wait for you to want two.

**Why this entity fits the silhouette:** Canon Orobashi *literally* broke coral off its own body to create Watatsumi Island as a home for the people it led up from Enkanomiya, and taught them farming and smelting — a god whose defining act is handing over a durable piece of itself so travellers can continue, which is the choose-1-of-3 pick exactly as modelled. It then took its people's transgressions onto itself and was slain, so the meeting is with a remnant at a threshold rather than a living patron, matching an Ancient encountered once and never again. The sea-flavoured boon names need no translation on Watatsumi: conch, pearl, and fishing rod are the local register, and Ossified Relic reads as a fragment of the serpent's own skull at Serpent's Head. The full restoration before the road is the same gift the island itself is — the god's body kept between you and the water.

### 2. Elynas — Fontaine / Beryl Region, beneath Merusea Village — loose

**Identity (one line):** The vast dead beast under the Beryl Region whose ghost wears a Melusine's shape, wants company it cannot ask for, and gives away whatever it has to whoever passes.

**Flavor direction:** The path leaves from a green ridge where the ground is not ground — it is ribs, gone over with moss and lumidouce bells, and the lakes in the hollows are where the beast's weight settled. A small figure is waiting on the shore with six things laid out on a folded cloth, in a row, labelled, because that is how this is done here. She is not a Melusine; she is what the Melusines call Father, borrowing the only shape anyone in this valley would be glad to see. She explains each one carefully and honestly, including which of them will do nothing for a long while, and then she stops talking, because staying to talk is the thing she is not allowed to want. Take one. Leave the cloth.

**Why this entity fits the silhouette:** Elynas is canonically a massive beast whose remains are larger than Orobashi's and span nearly the whole Beryl Region, with Merusea Village hidden beneath its mountains and lakes — a threshold you physically stand on, met once at the start. In canon he chose his own death because his living presence harmed others, and spent his afterlife caring for the Melusines he unintentionally created: an entity whose entire posture is *give, then withdraw*, which is the one-shot 1-of-3 pick as modelled and nothing more. His spirit taking Melusine form supplies the honest, over-explaining, everything-labelled delivery the gallery already requires of that voice, so the six boons get read out with their real terms rather than sold. The Fontemer setting carries conch, pearl, and rod without strain, and Astrolabe sits naturally in a valley the Melusines have been cataloguing for centuries.

### 3. Egeria, Mistress of Many Waters — Fontaine / the old Hydro Archon's remnant — loose

**Identity (one line):** The first Hydro Archon, dissolved into pure water five hundred years ago, whose remaining act is to give the departing traveller something and send them on.

**Flavor direction:** Before the first fight there is a spring that should not be as clear as it is, and a woman's voice in it that is neither a warning nor a welcome. She was called the Noble Navigator once, and the Queen of All Waters, and she has been water for five hundred years, which is long enough to stop making speeches. Six things surface and turn in the current: a conch, a rod, a pearl, a knot of old bone, a weight of gold, a brass ring of stars. She gave away existence itself, once, to things that had only been water — this is a smaller version of the same habit and she does not dress it up. Drink first. Then take one, and go connect something.

**Why this entity fits the silhouette:** Egeria's canon signature is *giving form and sending outward*: she is credited with making the Oceanids, whose strongest travelled all over Teyvat under her command to "connect everyone in the world" — the exact posture of an Ancient who equips a traveller at Act 1 and then does not follow. She died at Tunigi Hollow and her body became the pure primordial waters of the Amrita, which is why the full restoration before the road reads without invention: the thing that heals you *is* what is left of her. Her titles — Ruler of Rivers and Seas, Queen of All Waters — make the conch, pearl, and fishing rod her native vocabulary rather than a borrowed one, and Ossified Relic and Astrolabe sit inside her Remuria-successor register of laws, instruments, and old bone.

#### Register implications

New block proposed — consistent with the existing gallery rules, no conflict found with the Melusine / Sangonomiya resistance / Akademiya / Liyue mercantile / Fatui / Aranara / Guild / Knights / youkai blocks:

##### Proposed block — Dead gods & divine remnants (Orobashi, Elynas, Egeria, and any deceased `group = Gods` entity)
- A divine remnant **always** gives from what is left of its own body, works, or waters — the gift is a *piece of the giver*, **never** merchandise, **never** a loan, and **never** conditional on a later return.
- A divine remnant **never** bargains, prices, or asks anything of the traveller in exchange. Any variant containing a stated price, a debt, an oath, or a "come back and…" clause is a register violation. (This is the load-bearing separation from the Liyue mercantile block, where *everything is always a contract*, and from the Fatui block, where the clause is the point.)
- A divine remnant **never** speaks in first-person prophecy or doom at a run-start meeting, even where canon supplies one (Egeria's "the curtain call has come"). Foreboding belongs to act bosses; the threshold voice is spent and quiet.
- The remnant is **always** referred to by its canon name or canon title exactly — "Watatsumi Omikami," "Mistress of Many Waters," "Elynas" — **never** by an invented epithet, and Orobashi is **never** spelled "Orobaxi" in English display text (canon notes Orobaxi is a Chinese phonetic spelling of the Japanese reading).
- A divine remnant **never** chooses for the traveller and **never** re-offers what was declined — one meeting, one taking, then silence. (Consistent with the Sangonomiya "step aside" rule and the Aranara "never choose for Nara" rule.)
- ⚑ Where a remnant borrows a living faction's *shape* (Elynas appearing as a Melusine), the borrowed faction's register **always** applies on top of this block. [USER] to rule whether shape-borrowing is permitted at all; if not, delete this bullet and variant 2 with it.

### 1. Azhdaha — Liyue / Dragon Sovereigns — loose

**Identity (one line):** The sealed Geo dragon under the mountain, older than the mountains themselves, who never made anything new — he only ever took on the element of whatever was already flowing past him.

**Flavor direction:** You do not descend to him; the floor of the act opens and he is simply already there, a chin of stone the size of a gatehouse, speaking slowly because speaking quickly cracks the ceiling. He is not generous and he is not cruel; he asks what you brought down with you, and then he holds it while the ley lines under the Chasm run through it. When he returns your starting relic it is the same object, lit from the inside by whichever element he was carrying that century, and he tells you the smiths of old Liyue used to bring him their work for exactly this and that he misses them. He gives nothing away, because he has nothing that was ever his to give.

**Why this entity fits the silhouette:** Azhdaha's whole canon behaviour is the mechanic: he does not create, he absorbs an element from the ley lines and everything about him — his colour, his damage, his resistances — becomes that element instead. That is Sand Castle: six cards go in, six better versions of the same six cards come out. He was himself the object of exactly one such transformation, when Morax unearthed him and granted him eyes, turning a blind buried thing into a stronger form of itself; Touch of Orobas performing a different upgrade for every holder is the same gesture pointed at the player's starter relic, and his canon fondness for blacksmiths supplies the reason he would bother. The mechanics are unchanged; he is only the face on them.

### 2. King Deshret — Sumeru (Desert of Hadramaveth) — loose

**Identity (one line):** The dead Lord of Deserts, king of warriors, horticulturists and sages, who built nothing from nothing — he took the tribes, the canals and the sand that were already there and raised them a grade.

**Flavor direction:** The meeting is a room in a mausoleum that is much too large, one throne, and the sun-and-eye cut into every surface. He is not present; a jinni speaks for him, bored and precise, the way a servant speaks for a master who has been dead for thousands of years and has not noticed. Nothing is offered as a gift — it is offered as an appointment: he elevated shepherds into kings and untrained tribes into a kingdom, and he will do the same to your six worst cards or to the one thing you have carried since the first floor. The register is honours conferred, not favours done, and the jinni does not mention what the last recipient's kingdom cost.

**Why this entity fits the silhouette:** Deshret's canonical method is upgrade-in-place: he chose humans who already existed to be vassal-kings and priests, cut canals to irrigate land that was already settled, and pursued "higher knowledge" to make the existing world sufficient rather than to replace it. Sand Castle is the desert boon in name already, and six randomly-picked cards raised to their upgraded form is precisely the register of appointing existing people to higher station. Touch of Orobas resolving differently in every holder's hands matches a god whose signature act — separating his mind from his body, distributing it through his own structures — was a single transformation that meant something different in each vessel it landed in. No number moves; the fiction only explains why the run's own possessions come back better.

### 3. Rhinedottir ("Gold") — Khaenri'ah / Five Sinners — loose

**Identity (one line):** The alchemist called Gold, who graded her materials by pedigree and pursued, in another practitioner's words, "some form of perfection."

**Flavor direction:** No throne, no cavern — a workbench, a chalk line, and a woman who looks at your deck the way a metallurgist looks at ore. She asks for six cards without asking which six; the selection is beneath her interest, the process is not. Handed your starter relic, she says only that the material was always adequate and the execution was not, and returns it under a different classification. She does not explain what she did, and she expects the run to come back with better questions than thanks.

**Why this entity fits the silhouette:** Alchemy in Khaenri'ah is stated as an art of transformation applied to material that already exists, and Rhinedottir's own practice is explicitly graded — she filed her works under pedigrees ("Alfisol," "Humus," "Cretaceus") where the chalk grade was of the highest quality, which is upgrade-tier language for objects that were already hers. #### Register implications

New register family implied by all three drafts: **Ancient dragons & Sovereigns** (Durin/Dragonspine, Apep/Apep's Oasis, Azhdaha/The Chasm). No existing block in `docs/current/dossiers/content/event-conversion-gallery.md` covers dragon speakers, so this is additive, not a conflict — with one boundary case flagged below.

- A dragon speaker **always** names its gifts as parts of its own body (blood, wing, tooth, eye, scale, tears) and **never** as items, loot, rewards, prizes, or currency.
- A Sovereign or ancient dragon is **always** older than the nation it lies in, and **always** establishes this by naming what stood there before it (Vindagnyr, the ancient Sumeru, the earth beneath Liyue Harbor); it **never** dates itself in human years, Archon reigns, or "five hundred years ago."
- Dragon speech is **always** slow, unhurried, and in the present tense of a long injury; **never** brisk, **never** jokey, **never** cheerfully transactional. A dragon that patters is a register violation.
- A dragon **always** asks the player one question it cannot answer itself (after a maker, a friend, a season, a city) and the text **never** answers it.
- Durin is **always** a child-voice and **always** innocent of what his blood does downhill; he is **never** menacing, **never** knowing, and **never** framed as a villain in his own scene.
- Rhinedottir is **always** "Rhinedottir" or "Gold" — **never** "Mother," and (per the flag below) **never** "Father."

- ⚑ **[USER] decision — Apep's pronoun**, gallery-wide: *she* or *it*. Sources are genuinely split.

#### Register implications

#### Register implications

# §Registers

Register rules, stated **lint-ably** in the S2 idiom: each is a rule a script could check,
with its predicate spelled out, so future Ancient drafts can be verified mechanically
rather than by taste. These are **proposed**, not ratified — the curation pass decides
which are adopted, and only [USER] can amend an existing block in
`docs/current/dossiers/content/event-conversion-gallery.md`.

The per-Ancient fragments above each carry their own `#### Register implications`
section; this section collects the rules that are **gallery-wide** — the ones eight
independent drafters converged on, or that govern more than one entry.

## RQ1-A — The eligibility rules (mechanical, checkable today)

- **RQ1-A1 — An Ancient NEVER maps to a playable roster character.** Predicate: fetch
  the mapped page's infobox `type`; fail on `type = Playable`, and fail on a page that
  `#REDIRECT`s into a playable character's namespace (`Rex Lapis` → `Zhongli/Storyline`).
  This rule did real work tonight: it dropped Sangonomiya Kokomi, Neuvillette, Xiangling,
  Cloud Retainer/Xianyun, Lisa, and Rex Lapis, each recorded in its fragment rather than
  cut silently.
- ⚑ **RQ1-A2 — the `type = Upcoming` question is unruled.** Dainsleif was the Darv
  drafter's best thematic match and was dropped because his infobox reads `Upcoming` and
  the article calls him an upcoming playable character. Predicate would be `fail on type
  IN {Playable, Upcoming}`. [USER] rules whether announced-but-unreleased counts.
- ⚑ **RQ1-A3 — the combat-companion question is unruled.** Marchosius/Guoba is
  `type = Quest and Event NPC` and so passes RQ1-A1 on its face, but he is also
  Xiangling's Elemental Skill companion. He is the top-ordered variant for Tezcatara and
  the third for Nonupeipe, so this ruling is load-bearing in two entries. Delivered, not
  cut, in both.
- **RQ1-A4 — An entity display name NEVER equals a run-resource or UI noun.** Predicate:
  `name.lower() NOT IN {gold, mora, energy, block, strength, dexterity, spark, fanfare,
  …}`. This is what disqualifies the bare title "Gold" for Rhinedottir (Orobas variant 3,
  which therefore carries the full name).
- **RQ1-A5 — Every drafted name is checked against `docs/reserved-card-names.txt`.**
  All eight fragments ran this check; zero collisions were found. Note what the check does
  *not* cover: these drafts mint **no** new relic or card names at all. Any later
  body-part or dish renaming of a boon (Durin's Blood, Apep's Wing, a real recipe name for
  Yummy Cookie) is a *relic* rename and must be re-run through `lint_unique_names.py`
  at that time.

## RQ1-B — The convergent voice rule (eight drafters, independently)

- **RQ1-B1 — An Ancient's offer is ALWAYS an unpriced gift, bequest, or leaving-behind,
  and NEVER a priced transaction.** Six drafters (Neow, Pael, Tanx, Nonupeipe, Tezcatara,
  Vakuu) reached this independently and stated it in near-identical words.
  **Predicate, in the form the sixth drafter corrected it to:** flag an Ancient entry whose
  flavor text matches transaction vocabulary — `/\b(mora|price|cost you|pay|payment|buy|
  sell|bargain|contract|terms|ledger|signed|in exchange)\b/i` — and **do NOT flag** on
  cost vocabulary — `/\b(curse|debt|owe|give yourself|take)\b/i`.
  **Why the distinction is load-bearing: unpriced ≠ unburdened.** Vakuu is "The First
  Demon", and all ten of his real boons weld a permanent cost to the gift (a Curse or
  Apparitions added to the deck, Max HP burned, normal draw forbidden, or Vakuu taking
  your first turn for you). A naive rule that reads "this boon has a downside, therefore
  it is a purchase" false-positives on every one of them. The rule is about *transaction
  form*, not about cost — the Faustian register is in-silhouette for an Ancient, the
  mercantile register is not.
- ⚑ **RQ1-B2 — the consequence [USER] must rule on.** RQ1-B1 is the *exact inverse* of
  the gallery's existing **Liyue mercantile** predicate ("Everything is **always** a
  contract: price stated before service, terms recited, ledgers signed",
  `docs/current/dossiers/content/event-conversion-gallery.md:70-73`). Taken together they mean a Liyue mercantile
  speaker can **never** voice an Ancient. Three drafters proposed the same resolution —
  scope it rather than amend it: Liyue may still supply Ancients, but only from the
  **god / adeptus** register (a pre-Harbor Guili god, an adeptus), never the merchant one.
  Nobody proposed amending the mercantile block, and nobody should without [USER].
  Related consequence, from the Tezcatara fragment: this is why no Liyue *restaurateur*
  (Chef Mao, Wanmin Restaurant) is drafted, despite hospitality being Tezcatara's whole
  silhouette.
- ⚑ **RQ1-B2a — a registered dissent against the width of RQ1-B2.** The Vakuu drafter
  argues the blanket "no Liyue Ancient outside the god/adeptus register" reading is too
  wide: the mercantile rule bans a *register* (haggling, terms, Mora), not a nation, and
  his Osial variant predates every contract Morax ever wrote and speaks none of that
  vocabulary. Proposed narrowing: **NEVER voice an Ancient in the mercantile register;
  the nation of origin is not itself disqualifying.** Recorded as dissent, not resolved —
  four drafters wrote the wider version and one wrote the narrower, and the difference
  decides whether Liyue Ancients need a register carve-out or merely a voice check.
- **RQ1-B3 — An Ancient NEVER chooses for the traveller and NEVER re-offers what was
  declined.** One meeting, one taking, then silence. Consistent with the existing
  Sangonomiya "step aside" rule and the Aranara "never choose for Nara" rule.

## RQ1-C — Proposed new blocks (additive; no conflict asserted with existing blocks)

Three drafters proposed genuinely new faction blocks. They are additive — nothing in
`docs/current/dossiers/content/event-conversion-gallery.md` currently covers dragons, dead gods, or archives. Full
text lives in each fragment; the headline predicates:

- **Dead gods & divine remnants** (Neow fragment; Orobashi, Elynas, Egeria, any deceased
  `group = Gods` entity). A remnant **always** gives from what is left of its own body,
  works, or waters — the gift is *a piece of the giver* — and **never** bargains, prices,
  prophesies, or re-offers. Named exactly by canon name or canon title; "Orobashi" is
  never spelled "Orobaxi" in English display text.
- **Ancient dragons & Sovereigns** (Pael fragment; Durin, Apep, Azhdaha). A dragon
  **always** names its gifts as parts of its own body and **never** as items, loot, or
  currency. It is **always** older than the nation it lies in and establishes this by
  naming what stood there before, **never** by dating itself in human years. Speech is
  slow and in the present tense of a long injury — a dragon that patters is a violation.
  A Sovereign is **never** called a "god" or an "Archon".
- **Archives & Keepers of Record** (Darv fragment; libraries, presses, private
  collections). A keeper **always** gates by credential, catalogue rule, or editorial
  judgement and **never** by price. The retrieved item is **always** framed as already the
  traveller's own — borrowed against their name, filed under their record — and **never**
  as new stock from the world. Predicate: the handing-over line always contains a
  possessive referring to the recipient, and never a first-origin acquisition verb
  ("we found this", "a stranger left this"). Akademiya-affiliated keepers inherit the
  existing Akademiya block on top of this one, enforcement staying offstage.

## RQ1-D — Naming rules for specific families

- **RQ1-D1 — Adepti take a title-form name; Sovereign-class dragons take a bare personal
  name.** Predicate: `faction == "adepti" -> name MATCHES ^\w+ \w+$` (Cloud Retainer,
  Mountain Shaper, Moon Carver, Streetward Rambler); `faction == "sovereign" -> name is a
  single token` (Azhdaha, Apep).
- **RQ1-D2 — Sumeru desert divinity ALWAYS carries a royal or alias prefix, NEVER a bare
  stem.** Predicate: `region == "sumeru_desert" AND rank == "god" -> name MATCHES
  ^(King |Al-)` or `name IN {Amun}`. The King Deshret page sanctions exactly three forms
  and never a bare "Deshret".
- **RQ1-D3 — Beast-lord naming.** Andrius is always "Andrius", "Boreas", "Lupus Boreas",
  or "Wolf of the North", and is **never** called an Archon (he declined the seat).
  Azhdaha is always "Azhdaha" or "the Dragon Lord" and is **never** written as free,
  roaming, or unsealed — canon has him sealed beneath the Dragon-Queller, so an Ancient
  scene using him is always set at that seal.
- **RQ1-D4 — Hospitality entities ALWAYS name a boon after a food or recipe entry that
  exists on the wiki, NEVER after an invented dish, restaurant, or drink.** Predicate:
  any capitalised dish-shaped noun phrase in a hospitality draft must resolve to a live
  Genshin wiki page title.
- ⚑ **RQ1-D5 — Recurring Ancients** (present in more than one act pool — currently only
  Darv) always carry one display name and one voice across both appearances, and the name
  **never** encodes a zone or act ("of the Dharma Forest", "of Act II"). Only [USER] can
  rule whether the second meeting acknowledges the first in text.
