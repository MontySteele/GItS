# Per-Faction Register Rules (the Furina lore-grammar standard)

Same faction = same register, gallery-wide. Rules are stated lint-ably: a draft that trips an ALWAYS/NEVER gets cut or demoted.

## Melusines (Fontaine — Merusea Village, Meropide, Institute, Court)
- Melusine speakers **always** speak earnestly, politely, and at length — they over-explain procedure, reasons, and risks ("explains at length, with diagrams," "with great seriousness"), **never** tersely and **never** with sarcasm.
- Melusines **always** keep and mention a written record — a ledger, tally, report, label, or sign — somewhere in the scene.
- Melusines **always** warn honestly about a downside before the player chooses; they **never** conceal a cost, **never** sell, and are **never** menacing — a bright-voiced Melusine delivering body horror is a register violation (this rule cut *The Fitting Room Off Vasari Passage*; its own economics strain cut *The Merusea Salvage Fair*).
- Melusines may be sad, apologetic, or tearful about outcomes; they are **never** sinister or complicit in harm.
- Address is plain — **never** French honorifics ("monsieur," "madame"). (Demoted *Grandmother Coralie of Merusea Village*.)
- Costume details are diminutive (oversized hats, coats, aprons; small voices) — standard and encouraged.
- Spelling is **always** "Merusea Village," **never** "Merusee." (Demoted *Two Gifts from the Fontemer*.)
- The Fontaine research body is **always** the canon "Fontaine Research Institute (of Kinetic Energy Engineering)" — **never** an invented institute name. (Cut *Specimen 14, Flooded Annex*; demoted *The Clogged Pump of Elynas* for the invented "Fontinalis" core.)

## Sangonomiya resistance (Inazuma — Watatsumi, Yashiori)
- Speakers are **always** terse, logistical, and unsentimental; sentences count rations, tides, supplies, and dead — **never** jokey.
- Kokomi is **always** "Her Excellency" or "the Divine Priestess," **never** named.
- Resistance NPCs **never** order the player or choose for them — they state the cost and step aside ("she refuses to choose for you," "she only counts what leaves the plank").
- Grief is **always** carried in inventory terms (a soldier's sash, a ledger that does not balance), **never** stated directly.
- Time limits and clocks are **always** in-world (tide-drums, patrol rotations, bells), **never** abstract turns.

## Akademiya (Sumeru — darshans, researchers, Matra)
- Voice is **always** dry and publication-obsessed; the humor source is ethics filings, defunded theses, and peer review — **never** slapstick.
- Researchers are **always** candid about dubious methodology and **never** apologize for it ("her volunteers, she admits, have all been trees").
- Institutional disapproval (Sages, Matra, safety filings) is **always** the offstage antagonist, **never** onstage enforcement.
- Dead-Akasha-hardware scenes are rationed gallery-wide: Brain Leech's literal owns the device; further rogue-terminal scenes are redundancy candidates (cut *Akasha Echo ohcE ahsakA*, demoted *The Pruning Garden*).

## Forest Rangers & Aranara (Sumeru wilds)
- Rangers **always** warn via signage, markers, and counted observations; they are laconic and **never** intervene in the player's choice.
- Aranara **always** call humans "Nara" and themselves "Aranara," in third person; quoted Aranara speech **never** uses direct second-person address, and Aranara **never** choose for Nara. (Demoted *Nilotpala Dreamseed* for "the dream is yours.")

## Hilichurls (Guild-framed trade scenes)
- Hilichurls **always** speak only quoted hilichurlian ("Mosi mita," "Valo"), **never** translated in full; a Guild scout or notice supplies the human-side reading.
- Trade scenes are **always** non-hostile and lightly comic; hilichurls are **never** menacing at a stall. One hilichurl swap-stall per gallery region — duplicates get cut (cut *The Offering Log at Stormbearer Point*).

## Adventurers' Guild (all branches)
- Voice is **always** commission-board bureaucracy — Katheryne phrasing, ledgers, delisted commissions, wry understatement.
- The Guild **never** forbids; it disclaims ("survey only," "no takers," "neither hostile nor safe").

## Knights of Favonius (Mondstadt)
- Every Knight scene **always** cites a regulation, then bends it or grumbles ("Regulations say… Courtesy says…"); diction is drill and inventory.

## Chinju Forest youkai / tanuki (Inazuma)
- Register is **always** courtesy-bound trickster: etiquette is the law, insult is the danger, violence is not.
- Youkai **never** explain themselves; consequences are folkloric and discovered after (wake at dawn changed).

## Liyue mercantile (Qixing, Feiyun, diviners, Wangsheng)
- Everything is **always** a contract: price stated before service, terms recited, ledgers signed, payment per-descent.
- Wangsheng scenes keep the rites solemn **always**; Hu Tao may be brisk, but the dead are **never** mocked.

## Fatui / Northland Bank
- Voice is **always** silky contractual menace — politeness plus one small clause ("the smile is included at no additional charge"); threat is **never** overt.
- The org is **always** the "Marechaussee Phantom" when Fontaine's Melusine force is meant — **never** "Marechaussee Hunters," which is an artifact-set name. (Cut *The Tanglewood of Erinnyes*.)

# Appendix B — Drafters' mapping-resistance notes (raw, pre-curation)

- **Amalgamator**: Almost none — "two mediocre copies fuse into one superior copy" is a craft/alchemy idiom Teyvat already speaks fluently, so the only real friction is nominal: the harvest says "Strike (Ironclad)" and "Defend (Ironclad)", which our roster (Klee/Furina/Kokomi) does not have, so each label has to point at whatever basics the mod's starting deck actually ships rather than name Ironclad. Secondary caution, not a mapping problem: per tier05/content/events.yaml:315 the Ultimate Strike/Ultimate Defend numbers are unpublished, so no variant may quote a value in flavor text.
- **Aroma of Chaos**: Almost none — the base event carries no HP, gold, or escalation, just two parallel deck operations, so the whole conversion is naming and flavor. The only friction is making "Transform" (a random same-rarity swap the player does not choose) read as genuine loss of control rather than a second kind of curated upgrade; each variant handles it by putting the outcome in someone else's hands (the smoke, the cask, the pot).
- **Battleworn Dummy**: Almost no resistance — training dummies, sparring constructs, and drill grounds all exist plainly in Teyvat, so the escalating-HP-for-escalating-reward structure maps directly. The only friction is the abstract 3-turn limit, which needs an in-fiction clock (bell peals, a water-clock, tide-drums) rather than a natural Teyvat object.
- **Brain Leech**: Brain Leech maps almost frictionlessly — a knowledge-parasite that trades body for lore has an exact Teyvat analogue in the Akasha, so the only real strain is vocabulary: this mod deleted the base colorless pool (design principles §4.7), so "Colorless 2 card reward" reads in-fiction as a two-card companion reward, and the flavor must not promise a companion pick the shipped tier05 entry doesn't yet deliver (it approximates with `card_reward: 3` from the character pool, flagged in events.yaml).
- **Bugslayer**: Almost none — Bugslayer is a pure two-option card offer with no HP, gold, or escalation to preserve, so the only thing conversion can break is card identity. The single real friction is that "Bugslayer" implies literal vermin, which the two loose variants drop entirely; the parenthetical base names (Exterminate / Squash) are doing all the work of keeping a 4-hit AoE sweep distinguishable from a single heavy Vulnerable-applying blow.
- **Byrdonis Nest**: Almost no resistance — an unattended egg and a choice between eating it or pocketing it maps to Teyvat directly. The only friction is that Teyvat has no canonical roc-sized bird, so each variant has to borrow the egg's parent from real regional fauna (Falcon of the West, bathysmal vishaps, unnamed reef fauna) rather than name a Byrdonis analogue outright.
- **Colorful Philosophers**: The event's payload is literally "cards from the other playable characters' pools," and those pools (Ironclad, Silent, Defect, Necrobinder, Regent) are out-of-world identities with no Teyvat referent, so each variant has to gesture at "another traveller's discipline" in the label while the outcome text names the base character verbatim. The relic interactions (Prismatic Gem, Dingy Rug, Driftwood, Pael's Wing, Wing Charm, the Eggs) are engine-level and stay entirely unflavored — nothing in the scene text touches them.
- **Colossal Flower**: Almost no resistance: Teyvat ships the literal object (Whopperflowers drop nectar, Genshin's own item), and the gold ladder reads as a mora commission in every nation. The only prose hazard is the exclusivity of the rungs — payouts are alternatives, not a running total (35/75/135 at 5/6/7 HP), so each intro must state that taking payment ends the descent or players will read the escalation as cumulative harvesting.
- **Crystal Sphere**: Very little resistance: fortune-telling for a fee and lending-at-interest are both natively Teyvat (Liyue's diviners and Northland Bank, Dori's contracts), so the two options convert almost one-to-one. The only awkward seam is that "Divine 3/6 times" is a wrapper around a spatial 11x11 uncover-the-box minigame whose reward geometry (the 4x4 relic, unobtainable at 3 divinations) lives in art and grid math, not in text — flavor can gesture at it but cannot carry it.
- **Dense Vegetation**: The only friction is "Wrigglers" — Teyvat has no clean one-to-one worm-swarm enemy, so each variant renames them with the base term kept in parentheses rather than substituting a real bestiary entry that would imply different stats. "Heal HP (rest site amount)" is an abstract STS quantity with no in-fiction unit, so it is quoted verbatim in every variant rather than dressed up as a specific food or Statue of the Seven heal.
- **Doll Room**: The base event is almost pure UI — a shelf of relics with an escalating HP price for more choice — and its "Doll Relic" family is a StS2-specific relic subpool with no Teyvat naming hook, so each variant has to gesture at the family generically rather than rename its members. The HP cost also has no in-world cause in the base text, so every conversion must invent a toll (backlash, bad air, blood-seal) to justify why looking longer hurts.
- **Field of Man-Sized Holes**: The base event's uncanny body-horror register (a landscape that has pre-measured your corpse-shaped niche) has no native Teyvat idiom — Teyvat's eeriness runs toward ley-line contamination, Tatarigami, and Withering rather than anatomical mimicry, so all three variants soften the tone slightly to stay lore-plausible. "Normality" as a named curse also lacks a direct Teyvat vocabulary word; the nearest analogues are Fontaine's social conformity and Akademiya orthodoxy, which is why only the Fontaine variant renames it (base name kept in parentheses).
- **Grave of the Forgotten**: Very little resistance: "Forgotten Soul" is already spirit-flavored, so Liyue's lingering-souls-and-funeral-rites idiom maps almost one-to-one, and Decay reads cleanly as withering (Sumeru) or primordial-seawater dissolution (Fontaine). The only awkward seam is the option asymmetry — one branch is a free relic and the other costs a permanent curse — which has no obvious in-world justification, so each variant has to imply that naming the dead is the costly act rather than the merciful one.
- **Hungry for Mushrooms**: Almost no resistance: Sumeru's rainforest fungi are a one-to-one analogue, and the two-relic structure needs no reshaping. The only awkward beat is Big Mushroom's "draw 2 fewer cards," which has no natural Teyvat flavor beyond generic sluggishness, so each variant leans on heaviness/ballast imagery rather than naming the drawback in-fiction.
- **Infested Automaton**: The base event is mechanically featherweight — two forced draws from disjoint slices of your own pool, no HP, gold, or curse — so nothing fought the conversion except flavor: Teyvat needs a pest that plausibly nests inside working machinery, which narrows the honest options to Sumeru fungi, Fontemer aberrants, or Crystalflies drawn to leaking elemental power. The one thing flavor cannot express is the real rule that each option locks itself when its slice is empty; no scene text makes "you own no Power cards" feel like a reason the automaton won't be studied.
- **Jungle Maze Adventure**: Almost no resistance: the base is a bare risk/reward pair (more gold at an HP cost vs. a safe smaller cut), which maps cleanly onto any Teyvat "take the commission solo or split it" framing. The only friction is that Sumeru's rainforest is Teyvat's only true jungle, so non-Sumeru variants must loosen "jungle maze" to forest/marsh terrain, and Gold must be re-flavored as Mora while the 135-165 / 35-65 / 18 HP values stay untouched.
- **Luminous Choir**: Only Sumeru has a true one-to-one analogue — Withering/Marana gives a fungal mind-parasite curse and Fungi give the singing flesh — so the Fontaine and Inazuma variants must reframe "Spore Mind" as a song that eats memory and must supply a body to reach into (the Primordial Sea's chorus, Orobashi's coral remains) rather than mapping fungus directly. "Offer Tribute" is the easy half: Mora tithes at shrines, growths, and prison brokers are all native to Teyvat.
- **Morphic Grove**: Barely resists — a shape-shifting wood is one of the easiest base concepts to site in Teyvat. The only friction is moral: Genshin's register treats joining a communal circle as the virtuous choice, while the base mechanics punish "Group" (all Mora gone, cards randomized) and reward "Loner" with +5 Max HP, so each variant has to frame the group as a trickster or dream-bargain rather than a kindness.
