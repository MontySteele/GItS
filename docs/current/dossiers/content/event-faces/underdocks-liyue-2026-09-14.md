### The Tide-Warmed Pools of Yaoguang Shoal — Liyue / diviners — loose — DRAFTED

### Six Contracts to a Better You — Liyue / Feiyun Commerce Guild pamphleteer — literal — DRAFTED

A Feiyun pamphleteer has set up a folding stall on the Harbor steps selling
a slim, badly bound book that promises to sharpen, steady, or quicken
whatever you already carry — no refunds, terms printed small on the back
cover. "Read whichever chapter applies to your kit," she says, sliding it
across, "there's nothing in here for a hand with nothing in it." If none of

back and waves you on.

- **Read the Back** — Choose an Attack to Enchant with Sharp 2.
- **Read a Random Passage** — Choose a Skill to Enchant with Nimble 2.
- **Read the Entire Book** — Choose a Power to Enchant with Swift 2.
- **Move On** — Nothing happens.

Each reading option is available only while you hold a legal card for its
enchantment; Move On is offered only when none of the three has one.
