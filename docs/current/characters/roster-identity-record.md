# Roster-wide identity — record

**A record of what LAW used to pin, not law.** Two roster-wide statements
pinned character identity as a statline. The machinery review's change 6 moved
character identity out of LAW on 2026-09-01, and these two had no single
character's brief to go to, so they are recorded here, verbatim, with their old
`LAW.md` line numbers.

They are not law. A character's identity is now the brief's, revised by a
sentence. What each character's brief says about its own statline governs that
character.

---

**`LAW.md:22-23`, Pillar 3 of the Pillars list:**

> 3. **Character identity = statline asymmetry** — declared, not measured-gated
>    (see Design governance).

**`LAW.md:284-288`, the Roster section (the statline half of the bullet; the
co-op half stayed in LAW):**

> - **Identity = statline asymmetry:** every character scores 4–5 on exactly two of
>   seven axes and ≤2 on at least one; the weakness is load-bearing. This is
>   **declared design intent, not a measured acceptance gate** — the measured axis
>   numbers stay reportable-only (see Design governance). (principles Pillars 3–4)

---

**What stayed in LAW, and is unaffected:** the seven-axis numbers are
reportable, not load-bearing (all seven permanently CLOSED as reportable-only,
never a gate, target or justification to move a value); every playable
character ships to the §3 roster template, which still asks for a declared
7-axis statline before card design; and every character clears solo, co-op
amplified and never required.

**Why this moved.** The review's finding, §6 item 3: a statline in LAW produces
"more damage, less block" by construction, and changing it was a LAW amendment,
so nobody proposed it, so kits were designed inside the box.
