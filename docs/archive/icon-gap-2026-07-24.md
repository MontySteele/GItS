# Power-icon gap — 2026-07-24

Sweep of every `PowerModel` the mod ships against `KleePowerIcons.PathFor`
and against what is actually on disk. Opened by animation sprint 2 Track E:
the badge diet cut the strip to one badge, which made it worth asking what
the *rest* of the icon surface looks like, and the answer is worse than the
badge strip was.

Scope note: this is the POWER icon register only. Two adjacent surfaces were
checked and are **clean**, so they are not listed below:

- **Relics** — both relics (`PoundingSurprise`, `EtherealSpotlightRelic`)
  override `PackedIconPath`/`BigIconPath` and both PNGs exist.
- **Card art** — all 78 Furina card portraits resolve. The apparent 5-card
  gap is 2 roster helper classes and 3 guest cards whose art lives under
  `cards/companions/`.

Klee's own kit is also complete: every one of her 13 powers plus the 4
companion summons and the 4 live auras has a dedicated icon on disk.
(`aura_anemo.png` and `aura_geo.png` exist but are dead weight — Anemo and Geo
never leave an aura, per `ElementalApplication.LeavesAura`. Dendro has no
`AuraPower` subclass at all, so no `aura_dendro.png` is wanted.)

## C. Two weak picks and one open question

**The two `weak` marks in §A** (Friendly Visit, Study Buddy) have no good
source. They are generic companion-economy powers with no Genshin analogue,
so the shortlist falls back to Klee's two unused constellations (`Exquisite
Compound`, `Sparkly Explosion`), which are Klee-register but not semantically
right. Same for Standing Ovation and Ovation Trickle in §B, which are down to
Furina's character item/icon rather than a sigil. These four are flagged for
a re-hunt, not presented as good.

**Open question, for the D5/E2 sitting.** The proposal above gives all ten
Spotlight powers a *distinct* icon, on the sprint-1 reading that legibility
failures came from things being indistinct. The opposite case is real: ten
near-identical Furina constellation sigils at badge size may read *worse*
than one clear shared "Spotlight" mark plus the power's own counter, because
the family relationship is the thing a player needs to see first. Collapsing
to a family mark is a one-line change in `KleePowerIcons` (keep the
`SpotlightPower` base case, delete the per-subclass cases). Recorded as a
choice, not decided.

## Sourcing

All candidates come from the established channel — the Genshin Impact Fandom
wiki via `tools/art_hunt.py`, fetched by `tools/art_fetch.py`, ledgered into
`art/SOURCES.tsv` as Tier F. Titles above were enumerated from the live
File: namespace rather than guessed, per art_hunt's docstring.

Every talent/constellation PNG named above is **unclaimed** — the only Furina
sources already spoken for are the `Salon Solitaire … Preview.gif` files,
which cards use in the `vfx` register. No `art_lint` L1 source collision.

Furina icons land at `ImageGen/images/furina/powers/`, which `build_pck.ps1`
already copies to `furina/powers/` (the directory simply did not exist).
Shared/companion icons land at `ImageGen/images/powers/` → `klee/powers/`.
Note the `Copy-FurinaFallback` list covers `ui/` and `model/` only, so no
Furina power icon was ever silently filled from Klee's — the borrowing in §B
is entirely in the C# register, which is why it is fixable there.

### Two structural defects found on the way, both fixed

Neither was the icon gap; both were found because this work touched the art
pipeline, and both had already caused damage.

**1. Two producers, one out-path.** Sprint 2's B4 moved Furina's six still
surfaces onto `tools/gen_furina_stills.py` but left the five old `plan.tsv`
rows live. Both halves of that bit: `art_fetch` rewrote those files'
`SOURCES.tsv` provenance back to `Furina Profile.png`, so the ledger claimed
an origin the shipped bytes never had — and it was **committed in that state**
— and the next `art_process` run would have silently overwritten the
re-centred art with the old off-centre crops, undoing the [USER] verdict that
motivated B4. Caught only because a fetch happened to run. Fixed: the five
rows are retired with a note, provenance restored, and **art_lint L11** now
fails on any plan row whose out-path a generator owns. L11 also self-checks
its own curated list, so an entry that stops being true is a failure rather
than a guard that silently protects nothing.

**2. `plan.tsv` was read as cp1252.** `art_fetch.read_plan` opened the file
with no explicit encoding. On Windows that decoded the em dash in
`Constellation Hear Me — Let Us Raise the Chalice of Love!.png` to `â€"`, sent
the mangled title to the wiki API, and reported it `MISSING on wiki` — which
reads as "you guessed a title that does not exist" when the title was exactly
right. Any non-ASCII wiki title has always failed this way on Windows. Fixed
with an explicit `encoding="utf-8"` on all three plan/SOURCES opens.

### And one new boot check

**KleeSelfCheck R13** reflects over every concrete `PowerModel` in the
assembly and fails if it has no icon mapping, or names a path absent from the
merged pck. Both halves of this gap were invisible to every existing check: a
missing icon does not throw, it just draws the wrong thing. R13 is why the
`SpotlightPower` base case could be deleted safely — a future subclass added
without art now fails at boot instead of silently inheriting a sibling's
sigil, which is the failure mode this whole sweep was cleaning up. Powers that
legitimately need no icon are listed in `KleePowerIcons.IconExempt` with a
reason, rather than being silently tolerated.
