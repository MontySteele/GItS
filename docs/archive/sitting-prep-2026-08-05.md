## 1. S2 event-conversion gallery

**What this is.** S2 drafted 2–3 Teyvat register variants for each of 47 base
StS2 events, mechanics frozen to `docs/sts2-events-harvest.txt`; a single Fable
curation pass then selected and ordered them without rewriting any draft text.
141 variants were drafted, **130 kept, 11 cut**, 4 demoted. The artifact is
`review/event-gallery/gallery.md` (167 KB). Per-faction register rules are
stated lint-ably at gallery lines 24–76, and the redesign wishlist at the tail
is explicitly *inventory, not endorsement*.

**What a yes/no does.** Ticking an event's box accepts its **top-ordered
variant** as the shipping conversion for that event (survivors are ordered
best-first; the #1 line is the one to read if you read only one). Leaving it
unticked leaves the event unconverted — nothing ships from this gallery
without a tick. A **⚑ FLAG** is a separate, blocking question that a tick does
not answer: those twenty are listed verbatim after the checklist and each needs
its own call before the event can be built, whichever variant wins.

**Not decided here.** Cuts and demotions were curation, not ruling; no draft
text was rewritten; and the mechanics themselves are frozen — a tick converts
flavour, never numbers.
