### #10 — does Crescendo's damage reset between combats? **Yes.**

Answered by play, and it is the one question §3 said no static check could
settle. Bought Crescendo from the Act 1 merchant for 73g and carried it through
two combats.

- **Combat 3, first play, Fanfare 0/70** — dealt **exactly 6**; enemy 38 → 32.
  That is the printed base with no scaling on top.
- **Combat 3, later in the same combat** — hovering it in hand printed **`Deal
  9 damage`** in green, after one prior play and with Fanfare on the bar. The
  growth is real and it is visible on the card.
  (`EVIDENCE_crescendo_grown_combat3.png`)
- **Combat 4, a new fight** — the same card, read from the discard-pile viewer,
  printed **`Deal 6 damage` in white**, unmodified.
  (`EVIDENCE_crescendo_reset_combat4.png`)

So the `BaseValue +=` does **not** persist onto the master-deck instance: it is
combat-scoped, which is what the sim already models. The discard viewer is
showing live per-instance values rather than printed defaults — the Aria of
Recompense sitting beside it in the same shot read `Gain 0 Block` where it had
read `Gain 2 Block` in hand — so the white 6 is a live read, not a template.

**Loose thread, noted not ruled:** that `Gain 0 Block` on Aria of Recompense is
odd on its face — a card whose printed Block is 2, showing 0 while Fanfare was
5/70. It is a different card from the twelve and outside this gate, but someone
should look at whether Fanfare scaling can round a printed value *down* to zero.

### Why 3–7 could not be observed, and the lever that would fix it

**There is no way to put a chosen card into a deck.** The five checks each need
a specific uncommon Power in hand. In a Standard run you only get what rewards
and shops offer; across four combats and one merchant this run saw **two** of
the twelve (Poised Riposte 72g and Crescendo 73g, both in the same shop) and
could afford one. The P1.5 bridge does not help — the vendored STS2MCP exposes
`play_card`, `end_turn`, `select_card_reward`, seed and speed, but **no
add-card/give verb exists anywhere in it**, and no screenshot verb either.

The lever, found while routing: **Singleplayer → Custom** offers **Draft**
("choose 10 card rewards to replace your starting deck"), **Sealed Deck**
("choose 10 out of 30"), and **Specialized** ("start with 5 copies of a single
card"). Any of those turns 3–7 from luck into a short targeted session, and
Specialized in particular would put five copies of one power in the opener.
The cost is stated on that screen: *"You cannot unlock Epochs and Achievements,
or reach new Ascensions in Custom Mode."* So it buys the behaviour checks at
the price of the run not being A0-legitimate — a trade for whoever picks this
up, not one to make here.

## 5. Where the sheets live, and why they are not committed

Contact sheets are **local artifacts**, per `.gitignore`:
`art/contact_sheet.html` and `art/contact_sheet_*.html` are ignored alongside
`art/raw/` and `art/candidates/`. Principles §9 governs: Tier F material never
ships and never gets distributed, the repo is public, and an HTML page that
inlines nineteen candidate crops distributes exactly what that rule refuses.
Only the ledgers (`art/plan.tsv`, `art/SOURCES.tsv`,
`docs/art-claimed-sources.tsv`), the tools, and this page are tracked.

So: **this page is the commit, and `art/contact_sheet_g12.html` is the thing
you open.** Regenerate it with the command in §1 — it needs `art/candidates/`
present, which lives in the primary checkout (the Track I worktree reaches it
through a directory junction, itself gitignored).

`art/SOURCES.tsv` has no row for any of the nineteen, and that is correct
rather than a gap: it keys on the *out* path of picks that have been fetched,
and all nineteen were already in the local raw pool when Take a Bow shortlisted
them, so no row had to move. **Do not run `tools/art_fetch.py` to check this.**
It takes no arguments; asking it for `--help` runs a full fetch and rewrote
188/169 lines of `SOURCES.tsv` the last time someone tried, across Klee, Raiden
and Itto rows unrelated to the gate.
