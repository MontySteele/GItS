Not a taste finding — a mechanical one, and it was not visible from the sprint
log. Each candidate below was promoted to rank 1 in a scratch copy of
`plan.tsv` and `art_lint.py` was run against it; the plan was restored after
each. These are the lint's own words.

| if you pick | lint says |
|---|---|
| `standing_room_only` **r3** | `L1 standing_room_only: effective source 'Opera Epiclese.png' already used by grand_gala` |
| `warmup_act` **r4** | `L7 held_breath: source_group 'furina_pool' sibling of warmup_act reuses 'Fragrant Fantasy Furina 3.png' with an IDENTICAL crop ('cover_autocrop', 'cover@0.06') — siblings must differ by crop` |
| `crowd_work` **r2** | `L1 crowd_work: effective source '2024 7 5 amusement park art.png' already used by universal_revelry` |
| `audience_participation` **r4** | `L9 ... 'Furina Introduction Banner' family — carries a large burnt-in FURINA wordmark ... the text cannot be cropped out` |
| `tempo_change` **r3** | `L9 ... 'New Year's Advice from Teyvat' family — quote card ... Not an illustration.` |

The first one is the one that matters. **`standing_room_only` r3 is the
candidate the overturn recommends, and it is the one the lint refuses.**
`grand_gala` holds `Opera Epiclese.png` at rank 1 with a *blank* source_group,
so the reuse reads as cross-family rather than as a legal Furina-pool crop
sibling. If the overturn is upheld, the choice is a ruling, not a click:
re-pick `grand_gala` (its r2 is `Item Furina Banquet.png`), or put both rows in
the `furina_pool` group and give them genuinely different crops (r3 is already
`cover / y0.30`; `grand_gala` is `cover_autocrop / cover@0.06`, so L7 would be
satisfiable), or allowlist the pair in `PENDING_RED_PEN`. All three are
[USER]'s.

The two L9 rows are cheaper: both banned families are banned for burnt-in
text, both bans predate this sprint, and Take a Bow added them anyway — which
means **three of the seven candidates that sprint added are unpickable**
(`warmup_act` r4, `tempo_change` r3, `audience_participation` r4). The
practical effect is that `audience_participation`'s shortlist is really four
deep, not five, and `tempo_change`'s is two.

r1 of every row lints clean today, as do `warmup_act` r3, `crowd_work` r3/r4,
`tempo_change` r2 and `audience_participation` r5.

## 5. Where the sheets live, and why they are not committed

Contact sheets are **local artifacts**, per `.gitignore`:
`art/contact_sheet.html` and `art/contact_sheet_*.html` are ignored alongside
`art/raw/` and `art/candidates/`. Principles §9 governs: Tier F material never
ships and never gets distributed, the repo is public, and an HTML page that
inlines nineteen candidate crops distributes exactly what that rule refuses.
Only the ledgers (`art/plan.tsv`, `art/SOURCES.tsv`,
`docs/art-claimed-sources.tsv`), the tools, and this page are tracked.

So: **this page is the commit, and `art/contact_sheet_g12.html` is the thing
you open.** Regenerate it with the command in §1 — it needs `art/candidates/`
present, which lives in the primary checkout (the Track I worktree reaches it
through a directory junction, itself gitignored).

`art/SOURCES.tsv` has no row for any of the nineteen, and that is correct
rather than a gap: it keys on the *out* path of picks that have been fetched,
and all nineteen were already in the local raw pool when Take a Bow shortlisted
them, so no row had to move. **Do not run `tools/art_fetch.py` to check this.**
It takes no arguments; asking it for `--help` runs a full fetch and rewrote
188/169 lines of `SOURCES.tsv` the last time someone tried, across Klee, Raiden
and Itto rows unrelated to the gate.
