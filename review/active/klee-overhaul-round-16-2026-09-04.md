`0.2.2614+proto` (rows on `docs/prototype-surface.yaml`), the build with

Seed `21F5XMRQ0VQU`, Ascension 1. Five fights, five won, the four-Gardener

## 2. What the round found

**The ordering decisions are real and good.** Which of two hits carries
Melt's 1.75x; which Set off harvests the Mine a prior Set off spawned;
whether Vulnerable lands before the pile: "I computed different totals for
different orders every turn and it decided kills." The inline "Reaction
preview: Melt" tag "did the whole teaching job that the four-sentence
Elemental Reaction keyword box could not." Fourth round in a row to call
ordering the puzzle, on a build where the badge now prints the queue.

**Skittish does not fire on a Bomb, and nothing says so.** The Gardener's
"first time hit each turn, gains 6 Block" never triggered on a Set off,
which is why Klee beats that elite; the seat planned two turns around a
tax it was not paying and learned the rule by autopsy from a 26-HP body
dying to 30 points of Bomb. The Set off tip says "Block stops them" and
"no Attack trigger fires", which point opposite ways to a reader who does
not know Skittish is an on-hit trigger (`EB-490`).

**The last turn of every fight is automatic.** Once a pile exceeds the
target's HP, Ka-pow! is free and Retained, so the cash-out has no
alternative; twice the fight ended with the hand doing nothing while Mines
resolved it on the enemy's turn. "Charming twice, likely corrosive by the
tenth time." Round 14 called the cash-out fine because the decision was
made the turn before; this seat weighs the repetition. A reading for the
pool pass beside round 15's Retain-density one: the two are the same
question from both ends.

**Turn one, generic again.** Bang Bang! in hand printing `CANNOT BE
PLAYED: you have 1 Spark, and this costs 2` and no Set off card, so the
first Klee-shaped decision waited on Ka-pow! on turn 2. The starter's one
guaranteed detonator is drawn on turn one half the time.

**Defend, a fifth round.** "The one card outside every system the kit
has," never worth playing. The seat's lowest-confidence pick was passing
The Big One for Noelle.
