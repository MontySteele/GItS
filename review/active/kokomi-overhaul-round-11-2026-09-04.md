# Kokomi round eleven: the Plan reads both ways, and the fixes read true

Written 2026-09-04, the afternoon after R254 ruled starter density STANDS.
Two blind Opus seats played the Plan kit on `0.2.2476+proto`, the first
build with the six round-10 fixes live (`EB-402` to `EB-407`). Neither run
reached the act-1 boss; both spent the budget at floor 10 of 16 with an
elite in front of them. Records:
`review/qa/kokomi-round-11-2026-09-04/opus-act1.md` (run 1) and
`opus-run2-act1.md` (run 2). Prototype stage, Guardrail-7. No pick: the
one design question the round raises is the one R254 just answered, and
the round adds a second seat's reading to it rather than a new fact.

## 1. The runs in one paragraph

Run 1 (seed `7BEKR9871311`, Ascension 2, 119 of 120 actions): six fights
won including the Phantasmal Gardener elite, 44 of 80 at the stop, no
refusals. Run 2 (seed unrecorded: the embark's seed read-back crossed
lanes, `EB-210`'s known shape, while the Klee seat held lane 1; the seat
played the run the embark had started): five fights won, the Byrdonis
elite abandoned at 30 of 80 on the budget, 126 actions accepted against
120, the overrun declared in the record. Neither seat drew Tide Chart;
neither saw a reaction in six fights.

## 2. What the round found

**The fixes read true where they were met.** A bare Strike against a sole
enemy resolved to it with the target printed (`EB-402`, run 1's tool
note). The reaction gloss for Encore and the Deploy title rule were not
on this kit's screens; the carry-out log was, and run 1 called it "the
kit's best asset": Ambush hit 18 because a same-turn planned Exposed Flank
resolved first, three relic pings printed at 1, 3 and 1 off Slippery and
Vulnerable, and an enemy came out Frozen through a chain the seat had not
planned and could reconstruct from the screen.

**The Plan is the decision, and it reads both ways.** Run 1 found three
turns that "felt genuinely good" and all were the Plan: a defensive turn
that cost nothing because next turn's 12 was already bought, an enemy's
damage-free turn that made the delay free and three cards worth 31
instead of 13, and Exposed Flank planned before Ambush so the Vulnerable
multiplied it (12 to 18). Run 2: a planned War Council on four Stunned
Wrigglers, 28 for one energy off a card that does 8 played. Turn one of
fight one presented a decision in both runs.

The other reading is the same seat's: "the Plan choice collapses once
you own two Ambushes. By fight 4 I was planning every Plan card I drew
without thinking; 12 versus 5 and 10 Block versus 5 are not close enough
to be decisions," and "turn 1 of every fight is spent, not played, six
times out of six." Run 2 counted the Plan firing on "roughly 8 of 20
turns" and the other twelve as Strike, Strike, Defend. Round 9 read dead
turns with no Plan card; round 10 read the Plan as the kit; round 11
reads both in one run. §4 takes the default on the now-line ratio.

**Strike Dummy, a second time.** Slack Water went 4 to 7 under it; Oath,
Deep Current+ and Ambush+ did not move; the rule the seat inferred is
"single-target Attacks", not "cards containing Strike" (`EB-409`, open,
now with two runs' arithmetic).

**The panel's aura sentence is false with the Casket on.** "A Plan that
blocks, draws or applies a debuff leaves no aura," and Slack Water's
debuff Plan left Hydro on three bodies, the relic's ping laying it
(`EB-433`).

**Three page defects from run 1.** A Block Plan's carry-out prints a bare
number in the damage slot (`EB-426`); enemy copy numbers re-count as bodies
die, and the seat nearly aimed at the wrong Inklet (`EB-427`); the reaction
glossary fills about 40% of every battle screen for a deck that could not
fire one in four fights (`EB-428`, which prints only the reachable rows;
the Klee and Furina seats of the same day said the same).

**What a seat wants and the kit refuses.** Both seats named Defend the
dead card (four of fifteen, "strictly outclassed by Read the Field+"), the
Plan's timing against Block unprinted (`EB-411`), and Twig Slime's "give
you 1 Status card" that never appeared with no deck view to check it
(unverifiable; not a row).

## 3. What the round did not test

Tide Chart, Ripple's now-line under pressure, the act-1 boss, act 2. The
crossed embark on run 2 cost the seed and nothing else; the run's screens
were its own. Nothing here is a strength reading.

  sim decides the number when two seats say the same thing; the re-ask
  trigger is a second record calling the Plan automatic.

  re-ask: "8 of 20 turns" is round 9's finding said again, with the same
  answer waiting on Tide Chart being drawn.
- **`EB-426`, `EB-427`, `EB-428`, `EB-433` minted**; `EB-409`, `EB-411`
  cited.
