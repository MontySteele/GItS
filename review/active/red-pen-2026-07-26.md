**1 — `rain_of_roses`.** Interim, not final. The rare-Encore space is crowded
and the census confirms it: **five rares grant Encore** — `reginas_mercy` 10,
`let_the_people_rejoice` 6, `unheard_confession` 6, `rain_of_roses` 5,
`grand_gala` 4. **Pool-sweep direction recorded:** drop the Encore rider
entirely and lean into the card's unique half — hydro aura on ALL enemies, the
mass-reaction / co-op-team identity the playtest actually used it for — with the
freed budget going to aura persistence or an aura-payoff rider.

**3 — `controlled_demolition`.** The count-raising option was rejected because
raising the base also raises the ceiling and pulls on the detonation splash proc
cap. **Ecosystem anchor on the record:** StS2's 3-energy *"10(14) to a random
enemy X times"* lands around 30–42; this card at X=3 is 28 (40 upgraded) plus
the Spark setup the bombs feed. Post-edit demolition arm: **4.7%** at 600 runs —
unchanged, which is the expected result for one uncommon and is within noise.

**4 — `nicole_celestial_gift` redesign.** The card becomes **"Gain 1 Strength
and 4 Block each turn"**, cost 2, upgrade `{cost: -1}`. Rationale: 2-cost powers
must clear a high bar (anchors cited — Silent's damage-to-Weak at 1, Ironclad's
+4-Strength-per-turn at 3, Defect's double-first-card at 3), and scaling
Strength is what earns the slot. **This is a power-semantics change, not a
delta** — a per-turn Strength ratchet plus per-turn Block — so it touches the
sheet, the sim power, the generator, the C# and the upgrade entry. Supersedes
the interim `{buff: +2}` currently on the sheet.

**5 — Explosive Frags.** Doubling **rejected**: *"way too good."* Redesign
ratified: **"Gain 3 additional Sparks at the start of combat."** Measured this
session (act-2 acquisition, generous case):

| arm | before | after | delta |
|---|---|---|---|
| spark | 5.5% | 7.8% | +2.3 |
| demolition | 4.7% | 11.8% | **+7.1** (CIs disjoint) |
| reaction | 9.3% | 14.3% | **+5.0** (CIs disjoint) |

Ruled **strong for the slot but appropriate to it**: the comparison class is
act-2 ancients (Sand Castle upgrades 6 cards), and the measured value is further
diluted by real acquisition odds. **RATIFIED at 3** with those deltas in hand —
windfall-appropriate for the ancient class.

**R2 follow-up rulings ([USER], 2026-07-26), taken during implementation.**
Two things "both effects simultaneously" left underdetermined:

- **(a) The Fanfare/Companion split.** Center Stage generates Fanfare on
  Furina's cards and gives no numeric boost; Guest Cast multiplies Companions
  and explicitly generates *no* Fanfare — the second clause is a deliberate
  denial of the first. **RULED: reading 1** — Furina's cards generate Fanfare
  **and** Companions are 50% stronger; the Guest Cast no-Fanfare denial is
  simply dropped while upgraded. Each half keeps its own card class: the
  multiplier still applies only to Companions, the Fanfare only to her own
  cards. The upgrade removes the *exclusivity*, not the targeting.
- **(b) The selector card.** **RULED: stop adding it.** With both modes always
  on it has nothing to choose.

**FUNNEL CONTRACT NOTE (§3), filed before landing.** Ruling (b) touches a
contracted point — *"Spotlight is a designation event — a single designation
funnel"* — so it takes a cross-session note by the contract's own rule. The
mirror is in `docs/animation-sprint-2-log.md`.

The funnel is **not** removed, moved or renamed: `SpotlightSystem.Designate`
stays the single funnel, keeps its signature and keeps its
`SpawnSpotlightShine` call. What changes is that a Furina holding the upgraded
relic **never fires it again** — no selector, no designation event — so the
Spotlight beam goes quiet for that run from the moment Orobas is taken. Nothing
breaks, which is precisely what binding to funnels rather than values buys; the
note exists so a quiet funnel is diagnosable as a design outcome instead of
investigated as a defect.

### `controlled_demolition` is not "unchanged". It is +2.3pt.

Part 1 of this record states: *"Post-edit demolition arm: 4.7% (unchanged at
600 — one uncommon, within noise)."* Measured in-repo, the ratified edit
(`bomb_damage` 5 → 7, count back to `X+1`) moves demolition **4.7% → 7.0%**,
which is a ~50% relative increase and the largest single-card movement in this
whole ratification.

The likely explanation is the discrepancy already recorded above: the session's
"post-edit" baseline was taken in a workspace whose sheet state cannot be
reconstructed, and the repo it was quoted against still held the *rejected*
option. Whatever the cause, **the number on the record understates the change by
its entire magnitude**, and the card should be re-read at red-pen with 7.0% in
hand rather than 4.7%.

It also moves the arms it was not aimed at: spark −0.8 and reaction +1.5. That
is correct rather than surprising — `controlled_demolition` sits in Klee's
shared pool, so every arm drafts it — but it means "one uncommon, within noise"
was the wrong frame twice over.

`star_of_the_show` +3 → +5 shows up as spotlight **2.3% → 3.8%**: real, and
still nowhere near a fix for the archetype, exactly as G-D2 predicted.

`rain_of_roses` cost 2 → 1 left salon's winrate at 18.5% (act-1 62.2% → 63.2%,
deck 23.4 → 23.6). The repricing changed what the deck holds without changing
whether it wins.
