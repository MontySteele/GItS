9. FINDINGS THE GRADE RAISED — recorded, not acted on
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(F-1) THE VOCABULARY MAKES "LESS RANDOM" READ AS "LESS CONNECTED", AND THIS
      IS THE INSTRUMENT'S FINDING ABOUT ITSELF.
      `random_enemies` sits in BOTH `RANDOM_TARGETS` (→ `random_placement`)
      and `MULTI_TARGETS` (→ an `enemy_count` shared read). So a row that
      sprays across every enemy is credited with reading the board's enemy
      count. Door (a)'s CONCENTRATION form, `target: enemy`, is in NEITHER
      set — so converting a spray to a chosen single target deletes a shared
      hook in the same stroke that it deletes the randomness.
      Four Klee rows lost `enemy_count` exactly this way — `all_my_treasures`,
      `bomb_voyage`, `bombs_away`, `controlled_demolition` — and two of them
      had it as their ONLY shared hook. That is the entire mechanism behind
      K1's failing half.
      THE FOUR DISTRIBUTION ROWS DID NOT LOSE IT: `mine_toss`,
      `jumpy_dumpty_mk2`, `cluster_charge` and `sparkly_explosion` moved to
      `all_enemies`, which IS in `MULTI_TARGETS`, and kept the read. So the
      miss is specific to the concentration half — the same half that carried
      the whole of the `A2_scaling` move R204 ruled on, for the same
      underlying reason (tier0 resolves `target: enemy` as lowest-HP, so a
      chosen target is modelled as automatic focus fire).
      THE INSTRUMENT IS FROZEN AT v3 FOR THIS READ AND WAS NOT EDITED. Under
      §2.3(4) a vocabulary repair means re-running BOTH sides under a NEW
      `VOCAB_VERSION`; doing it mid-read, inside the batch whose cards moved,
      is the exact failure the freeze exists to prevent. Whether v4 should
      separate "reads how many enemies there are" from "sprays at random" is
      a question for someone else's window, on someone else's authority.

(F-2) FOUR OF THE NINE GRADED CLAUSES NAME MECHANISMS PHASE 1 NEVER TOUCHED.
      K4 (Spark competing use), F2 (Encore spending, Salon queue control),
      Ko1 and Ko2 (Kokomi anything) are graded MISS on evidence that is, in
      every case, "the input file did not change". §2.4 does not phase-scope
      its clauses — it is one prediction set for the whole richness pass —
      while §2.3(5) asks for a re-run "after each coordinated phase". The two
      sections do not agree about what a phase-boundary read is grading, and
      this read is where that shows. It is recorded as a property of the
      registration, NOT used to soften a grade: the clauses are graded as
      written, and four of them are misses at this checkpoint.

hard. Live play remains the final check.
