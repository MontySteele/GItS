# Kokomi review, round 5 (2026-10-14)

Five blind Sonnet seats, whole runs at A0, build 0.2.5414, the first round on
the pool overhaul (`kokomi-pool-overhaul-2026-10-13.md`). Drafted by a Fable
reviewer from the five seat records, then checked and edited by Claude. The
records are in the session scratchpad (`seats/archive/kokomi-*`); this page
cites them as runs 1 to 5.

| Run | End |

Rounds 2 and 4 were also 1 in 5 (`kokomi-review-r4-2026-10-13.md`), so the
gain is depth: four of five runs reached an act-2 boss and two reached the
last fight.

## What works

- **The Tactician core is the fun, in the seats' words.** Every "most wanted"
  turn is a Plan written the turn before: Second Wave + Oath+ for four
  12-damage hits (run 1), Masterstroke under Nereid's Ascension for 84 and
  later 116 (run 4), Princess of Watatsumi+ twice for 100 to ALL to finish the
  Queen (run 3), Wake + Change of Plans for three Stings in one turn (run 5).
- **Tide Wall is the one defence the seats trust:** "22 vs a visible 13
  attack" (run 5).
- **Electro on Hydro (Poison) works** whenever a Companion brings the Electro
  (runs 2, 3, 4).
- Stings read as free value, not a chore (runs 3, 5).

## What does not

- **The overhaul's own cards barely reached a deck.** Of the fourteen rows it
  changed or added, the final decks hold three (Kelp Wall, Turning Tide+, Read
  the Field). The win came from cards that predate it. The records cannot say
  whether the rest were offered and passed, so the offer census runs first
  (A9) before any of their numbers move.
- **Plans wasted into Block the enemy is about to gain**, in three runs of
  five, two of them after reading the warning. The page shows the enemy's
  Block now, not the Block its Defensive intent adds before the Plan lands.
- **"Strength is read when the Plan is written" is invisible.** Tender cut a
  planned Oath+ (run 1); a Potion's Strength showed only as "63 vs 42" after
  the fact (run 3). The page line "Shrink or Weak on you never cuts it" reads
  as "nothing on me cuts it."
- **Dead or unclear cards:** Sea Glass Harvest's Plan line ("never used",
  run 3); Watchful Shoal names Wards to a deck that has none (run 3); Kelp
  Wall's Plan number is never shown growing ("did 5 for a 12-Block turn, I
  never understood the number", run 4). Kurage Canopy was one seat's "never
  again" but two r4 lanes ran it as their Ward engine: kept.
- **Display:** Energy "3/3" read as spent, not left (run 4, two lost turns);
  a carry-out receipt under Hard To Kill printed the number asked for, not
  landed (run 2); Ripple Basin's conditional Block counted in the incoming
  total on attack turns (run 2); the Vaporize preview printing 31 for a 21
  hit (runs 1, 2; the card-tip half is fixed by #1273, the seat-page half is
  A5).
- **Turn 1 has no kit decision in all five runs.** With two Plan cards in a
  ten-card starter, 22 percent of opening hands hold none. That is a starter
  question, pick 1.

## The census (A10, built on #1275)

All fourteen overhaul rows were offered: **57 offers, 5 taken (9%)**,
against 26% for her other rows (77 of 294). Eleven were never taken: Feint 0
of 9, Coordinated Tide 0 of 4, All Streams Flow to the Sea 0 of 5. Kelp Wall
(3 of 11) is the only one taken in more than one run. Table: session
scratchpad `kokomi-r5-census.md`.

So the overhaul's cards were seen and passed. Reading their faces, two shapes
lose at the draft:
- **Reaction payoffs with no reaction to pay off.** Five of the fourteen
  (Coordinated Tide, Brine Spray, Grand Strategist, All Streams, Jellyfish
  Current) need a second element; alone she brings only Hydro, so they wait
  on a Companion.
- **Plan-count payoffs whose now-line is a Strike.** Feint and Turning Tide
  print a Strike-sized number now and pay only on a turn with several Plans
  landing; seats wrote one or two.

**The numbers pass (built with the A-list; readings unchanged).** Each
now-line rises to a base-game rate for its slot; the Plan or condition stays
the upside. Numbers are now → after (upgrade unchanged unless named).
