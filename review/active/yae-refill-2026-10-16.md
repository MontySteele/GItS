slots the dedupe cut (`review/active/yae-dedupe-2026-10-16.md`, ruled: [USER]:
"Agreed on the Yae paper picks!"), and are built in the same change as the
cuts. Pool back to 78 at 20 / 38 / 20.

**Brief** (dedupe paper, sec.3). Round 7: damage 0.78 of base in acts 1 and
2, HP loss 1.93 of base in act 1, and Sakura strike a random enemy. So the
Commons lean on early damage and Block that costs something, at least two
cards aim her Sakura, and none of the eleven is half of an attack/Block
pair.

## Readings for the builder

- "Newest" and "oldest" Sakura are the existing readings (Fox Fire Draft,
  Fallen Petals). A wither always plays its Last Bloom.
- Falling Blossom's Last Bloom hits the card's target, one hit, whatever the
  Sakura's kind (a Foxfire's included), as an aimed strike already is.
- Hunting Gaze aims every Sakura strike that would pick a random enemy (turn
  end, Tenko, Last Bloom, "a Sakura strikes"); a strike already aimed, or a
  Foxfire's hit on ALL, is unchanged. "Attacked" means hit by one of her
  Attack cards. With no living target it falls back to random. Copies do
  not stack.
- Inscribed Thunder counts distinct Annotations on the played card,
  Published ones included.
- Publisher's Deadline checks Edits made this turn (any card, any
  Annotation), as Bestseller counts them this combat.
- Sesshou Fang and Kitsune Barrage read the board when played.
