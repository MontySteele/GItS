## 1. Who he is
- Title: Grand Master of the Knights of Favonius; Knight of Boreas ("Knight of the North Wind"); protector of Mondstadt (GB, B).
- 5-star Anemo, dual Claymores; released in Version 6.4 on 2026-02-25 (B, GB, ActuGaming). Birthday Feb 17; constellation Canis Major (B), the wolf-and-dog hook. VAs: EN Joseph May, JP Sugita Tomokazu, CN Hao Xianghai, KR Park Sang-hoon (B).
- Personality (B): free-spirited, humorous, easy with people, talkative (Rosaria, DS); serious and dedicated at work; a fatherly nagger; will give up his own power to protect his people; not troubled by a glimpse of his own fate. Leaves desk work to Jean (DS, Beebom; IcyV trailer joke about dodging paperwork).
- Appearance (B): light golden medium hair, blue-purple eyes, many scars, black leather armor, green/black/gold cloak with white fur collar. Pre-release, only "very big, tall man" was canon (DS). Fans expected an old veteran; the model reads younger (Beebom).
- Origin (B): ordinary Mondstadter who wanted to be a hero from bards' tales. Failed his first knight exam, ambushed Valentine (then Grand Master) to argue, was told to travel first, came back as Knight of the North Wind, later Grand Master.
- Dish: "Exhilaration" (Northern Smoked Chicken) (B).

## 2. The expedition
- Pre-release canon (DS): left about half a year before the Traveler arrived; purpose "fight the Abyss Order" (daily commission "Whispers in the Wind"); Jean acting; Jean and Barbara's father also went.
- Version 6.x (SK, B, search summaries): Varka led about four-fifths (80%) of the Knights to Nod-Krai (Nordkale) to eradicate the Abyss. Jean's birthday letter reportedly places him and some of the force in Nod-Krai (SK). A letter to Venti reportedly said the Abyss Wild Hunt was about to attack Andrius, "a lingering soul" (Moegirl via search summary, unverified).
- What happened (B only, verify): he glimpsed his fate in the Hydromancy Plate (victory over a dragon, a coming calamity); was beaten by the Huntmoon, then helped Aether and others trap it by ambush; helped evacuate Nod-Krai and stop Dottore's Ascension Experiment with Nico, Arlecchino, Aether; defeated the Abyssal Dvalin in the "Return of the Dragon Crisis" story inside the Magic Bubble; was swallowed by Andrius in the Ley Lines, fought his way out, then led the force home "in triumph". A detachment under his direct oversight stayed to watch Nod-Krai (search summary).
- Return: v6.4 is Mondstadt-focused with no new Archon quest (ActuGaming). Whether the return is shown on screen is unverified.

## 3. Relationships (B unless noted)
- Jean: trusted Acting Grand Master, he mentored her; she calls him a legend. Kaeya likes his carefree way; Diluc dislikes it (DS) and "disapproves of his methods" (B). Crepus: old friend lost in battle.
- Eula: he promoted her despite the Lawrence rumors; never went easy in their spar, she still resents it.
- Rosaria: adoptive daughter, brought to Mondstadt in hope of an ordinary life. Razor: adoptive son raised by wolves; Varka taught him to fight and set Lisa to teach him (DS: Razor's lines, EN vague).
- Barbatos/Venti: respects his taste in wine; Barbatos once saved him and introduced him to the Hexenzirkel, with whom he made a covenant. Valentine: old Grand Master. Simon Page: companion. Ilock: Inspector General who betrayed Mondstadt.
- Andrius: sometimes sparred with Varka so Varka could pass his predecessors (B). The Knight of Boreas title and the Wolf's Gravestone are tied to Andrius (DS, [SPEC] on how).
- Fatui: Childe's lines call Varka extremely strong (DS). Nod-Krai: Arlecchino, Nico, Dottore (B).
- Not found: Klee, Albedo, Amber, Noelle details. Do not invent them.
- Four Winds: Dvalin (Dragon of the East), Andrius (Wolf of the North), Vennessa (Falcon of the West), and the Lion of the South, a human, the Knights' Grand Master (search summary of Sportskeeda/Touchtapplay; unverified). Barbatos is the Archon, not one of the four. Decarabian and Andrius were the old gods; Vennessa was a slave who helped overthrow the Lawrences and founded the Knights.
- Azure Fang's Oath: stack name in his A4 passive (GB). Boreas = the North Wind, Andrius's domain; the fang is the wolf.

## 4. Kit names (GB; effects paraphrased)
- Normal: Favonius Bladework: Dancing Radiance. Skill: Windbound Execution (Hold cooldown shortened by Mondstadt party members). Burst: Northwind Avatar. Special in Sturm und Drang mode: Four Winds' Ascension; also Azure Devour.
- Passives: Dawn Wind's March (A1; Anemo bonus with elemental allies), Wind's Vanguard (A4; Swirls give Azure Fang's Oath, max 4), Homebound Wind's Paean (Mondstadt party members).
- Constellations: GB lists the names as song lyrics; C2 is "When Dawn Breaks, Our Journey Shall Take Flight". Gists: C1 extra Ascension use; C2 800% Anemo strike; C3 skill +3; C4 team Anemo/element bonus; C5 burst +3; C6 more Devour uses, Oath adds crit damage. Get the other names in game.
- Outfit "Oath to the North Wind": "A knight's conviction is more steadfast than any armor."

## 5. Story hooks (invention built on the above)
- Values: loyalty to the rank and file, freedom, the Knights as a family, "a knight needs a reason of his own" (Valentine). Fears: losing more people (Crepus, Ilock), the calamity he glimpsed. Jokes: paperwork, Jean's desk, wine, his own late return.
- Act 1: a letter from the expedition, breezy about the Abyss, apologizing to Jean for the paperwork. Act 2: the Wolf of the North, a spar or being swallowed, the Oath. Act 3: the way home, a promise kept to Jean.
- Ancient greetings: a hero from a bard's tale who is slightly late; fatherly teasing; offers wine.
- Victory: the force came home, and that is the point. Defeat: the glimpsed fate was right, but the Knights carry on under Jean.

## 6. Fandom (not canon)
- "Daddy Varka": Rosaria and Razor treat him as a father (Roonby, Beebom). Most-buff-hero jokes (Beebom). "Farka" pronunciation in JP/CN/KR (DS). Dodging-paperwork trailer joke (IcyV). Launch complaints: too young, modest damage (Pocket Tactics via search).

## Plan vs canon
The planned arc (a letter from the expedition, the Wolf of the North, then a return to Mondstadt) fits what I could find. Letters from the expedition are canon, Andrius is tied to the Knight of Boreas title, and the returning-in-triumph ending matches the Baidu summary, which has Andrius swallow him in the Ley Lines before the return. Adjustments: Andrius is reportedly a lingering soul in Nod-Krai at that point, so the act-2 beat should be a spar or an escape rather than a conquest. Varka reportedly leaves a detachment in Nod-Krai, so the return should be most of the force. The repo dossier (docs/current/dossiers/bosses/dossiers.md, "Andrius, Lupus Boreas") covers the weekly boss and fits as the act-2 boss. The kit paper (review/active/varka-paper-kit-2026-09-28.md) already uses Boreas's Fang and Four Winds' Ascension, consistent with the names above. I could not confirm "Four Winds' Ascension" as anything beyond a kit name. Verify the Baidu story section in game before relying on it. The brief's "return to Mondstadt is our invention" note holds: the return is only reported, not seen.
