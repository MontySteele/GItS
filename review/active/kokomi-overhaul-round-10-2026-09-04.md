# Kokomi round ten: the first build after the audit, and the jellyfish that stood empty

Written 2026-09-04, the same night as R253. Two blind Opus seats played the
Plan kit on `0.2.2446+proto`, the first build that carries the tempo shelf as
the audit left it (Tide Chart and Ripple; Held Tide and Tidal Rhythm
withdrawn, `review/records/card-audit-2026-09-04.md`). Neither run reached
the act-1 boss, and in both the reason was the harness's action budget, not
a death. Records: `review/qa/kokomi-round-10-2026-09-04/opus-act1.md` (run
1) and `opus-run2-act1.md` (run 2). Prototype stage, Guardrail-7: what a
seat reports is a floor on legibility and a list of decisions, never a
measurement.

## 1. The runs in one paragraph

Run 1 (seed `1BPLAQEG976D`, Ascension 2, 65 of 70 actions): three fights
won, 75 to 45 HP across them, floor 6 with an Elite next and four actions
left; the seat bought Ripple and a Feint, took Nutritious Oyster at Neow.
Run 2 (seed `KLZ15ED1UWS2`, Ascension 2, 120 of 120 actions): five fights
won including the Phantasmal Gardener elite, stopped mid-fight against
Living Fog at 42 of 80 HP on floor 11 of 16; the seat declined Sango
Isshin at 154 gold, the Treatise, The Moon and Nereid's Ascension as
"correct declines at the HP and gold I actually had". Neither seat drew
Tide Chart; run 1 owned Ripple. Both runs opened at Ascension 2, which the
embark tool sets and no round before this one reported; the control run
was Ascension 0. §4 takes a default on it.

## 2. What the round found

**The Plan is the kit, and its best moment survived a second seat.** Run 1
found the same thing round 9 did and said it better: under Shrink the
faces re-printed honestly (Strike 4, Feint 4) and the Plan line kept its
10, because the printed rule says your Weak and Strength do not reach a
carry-out; "planning Feint for 10 while face-up Feint read 4 is the
sharpest decision of the act." The same seat turned a plan down against an
enemy about to Block, because a planned hit lands into a fresh wall, and
called the inversion "the best thing I can say about it." Run 2 found the
Oath's ALL-scaling Plan half against four Gardeners "a clean, legible
reason to choose." Turn one of fight one presented a decision in both runs
(answer (e) in both records).

**The jellyfish stood empty most turns.** Run 2 wrote a Plan on 4 of about
20 turns and read "Nothing is planned. The morning is empty." about
sixteen times; run 1 counted "roughly half of every turn was Strikes and
Defends." The starter carries two Plan cards in ten (Kurage's Oath, Slack
Water), the drafted Attacks the seats were offered mostly carried none, and
the pool's Plan-density payoffs (Sango Isshin, the Treatise, The Moon,
Nereid's Ascension) were priced past what either seat could afford by
floor 8. This is the third round to say it (round 9 §2, round 4d before
it); the tempo shelf was built to answer it and neither seat drew its
reader. §5 is the pick.

**Ripple never asked a question.** Run 1: "Planning it costs the same zero
and returns strictly more in both currencies. I planned it every time and
cannot construct the board where I would not." The audit read the wait as
its price (C2, FOLLOWS); one seat on one run says the wait does not bite.
§4 records the default: the row stands until a round in which a seat needed
the 2 Block now, and the packet that reports that round re-asks.

**The Plan lands into standing Block, and nothing says so.** Run 2 fight 4:
a Plating 8 Sewer Clam ate a whole Plan because the carry-out happens at
the start of the turn, before the player can strip Block. The tip says
"before you draw"; it does not say what that costs against Plating.
`EB-411`.

**Defects, nine rows.** Run 1 convicted the bridge: a bare play of Slack
Water with no target was answered ok and did nothing, while an ALL card
resolved (`EB-402`); the seat lost the card and the energy and saw success.
Gorou's banner printed "for 2 turns" above a Dexterity gloss that says "It
does not decay" (`EB-403`). Run 2: Strike Dummy paid on Slack Water and not
on Deep Current, neither titled Strike (`EB-409`); the page never names a
reaction when one fires, Electro-Charged printed as Poison 4 and Vaporize
printed nothing (`EB-410`); the same Strike under the same buff printed 6 on
one observe and 11 on another (`EB-408`). The Furina seat of the same night
added four more (`EB-404` to `EB-407`, the Furina round-4 packet). Six of the
nine are on an Opus fix branch beneath this packet.

**The reaction table again.** Both seats read the nine-line reaction gloss
five or six times looking for the thing they were meant to do with it, and
the starter cannot produce a reaction. The Klee round-10 seats said the same
of the Pyro side. It is one question for the pool pass, not a row: when a
deck owns one element, the page prints the table anyway.

## 3. What the round did not test

Tide Chart was never drawn, so the shelf's reader is unread. No seat met
the act-1 boss; both budgets ran out first, at 65 and 120 actions, because
a Kokomi turn spends more actions than a Klee turn (a Plan is two commands,
and a debuffer's turn is ordered). The Kurage memory (`C.KURAGE_MEMORY`) was
not part of this round. Nothing here is a strength reading: the control
and the kit clears of the sitting are the strength evidence, and round ten
adds nothing to them.

## 5. The pick

**Pick 1 (A): Plan density in the starter.** Two Plan cards in ten leaves
the jellyfish empty on most early turns, three rounds running, and the
pool's answer only works when drawn. The brief's §4 fixes the starter at
four ids and §2 rule 2 says the basics carry no Plan line, so any change
here is a rule change and [USER] plays the next build of it.

1. **STANDS (default).** The starter keeps two Plan cards; the pool's
   Commons carry the density (Feint, Ripple, Tide Chart, the six now-line
   cards of R250). Re-ask when a round is read in which Tide Chart was
   drawn. Costs nothing and settles nothing.
2. **Kurage's Oath becomes two copies**, replacing one Defend (four ids
   unchanged, ten cards). Fight one shows the Plan twice as often; the
   deck loses 5 Block it rarely wanted (both seats named Defend the card
   they never wanted to play).
3. **A fifth starter id with a Plan line** at Common scale (a Strike-shaped
   Attack with Feint's own numbers, 6 now / Plan 10), replacing one Strike.
   Adds an id to §4 and a card the pool already sells.

Default is 1 because the shelf built for this question has not been read
yet; 2 is the smallest change that answers the seats and the one this
author would take if the next Kokomi round says the same thing with Tide
Chart in the deck.
