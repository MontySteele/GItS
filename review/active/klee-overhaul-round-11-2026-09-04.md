# Klee round eleven: the loan against the clock, and the Spark that arrives unnamed

Written 2026-09-04. Two blind Opus seats played the Bomb kit on
`0.2.2476+proto`, the first build with Countdown in the pool (the pool pass's
energy-priced plain detonator, `review/records/card-audit-2026-09-04.md` §5)
and the shelf at R253's three rows. Neither run reached the act-1 boss and
neither drew Countdown. Records:
`review/qa/klee-round-11-2026-09-04/opus-act1.md` (run 1) and
`opus-run2-act1.md` (run 2). Prototype stage, Guardrail-7. No pick: what the
round found is nine rows and one design question the pool pass already
holds.

## 1. The runs in one paragraph

Run 1 (seed `N4VANPNUBCTU`, Ascension 0, 80 actions): four fights won and
a fifth abandoned at 13 of 62 on the budget, floor 5, fights four and five
costing 36 of 49 HP lost. Run 2 (seed `NJFKYHTXZ2WQ`, Ascension 0, 110
actions): five fights won and the Phantasmal Gardener elite abandoned at
16 of 62 on the budget, floor 10. Both opened at Ascension 0 where every
seat of the day before opened at 2; the embark tool's ascension is now on
every record's Identity line. Neither seat drew Countdown, Explosive Spark
is on no surface, and both decks were half Strikes and Defends at the stop.

## 2. What the round found

**The loan against the clock is the kit, and both seats priced it on the
nose.** "Every turn the badge says Bomb N, N grows by 4 per charge per
turn, and goes off only when Set off, so holding Ka-pow! is a loan against
the enemy's clock" (run 2). Run 2 predicted Bomb 33 and Bomb 21 before the
turn came and held the detonator two turns for 40 on a 0-cost card; run 1
left an enemy on exactly Mine-lethal HP twice and once held the Retain
detonator so the Mine paid for free. Turn one of fight one presented that
decision in both runs. Round 10's read holds on a third and fourth seat.

**The Spark ledger has a line nothing prints.** Run 1: Spark 1 to 2 in
fight three, round one, with no Bomb going off and Diona's "next time a
Bomb goes off this turn" buff still unspent to prove it. Run 2: twice
more, once after spending a Spark on Tinder Toss and still ending the turn
up one. Both seats budget Spark-priced cards blind while this holds.
`EB-418` is on an Opus branch beneath this packet; its answer is one of a
Mine on the enemy's turn credited late, a jump counted as a detonation, a
relic path, or a grant no rule names.

**A random-target Set off cannot be aimed, and its face does not say so.**
Rapid Fire and Tinder Toss, three plays into four Gardeners, set off
nothing; the hits landed on unbombed bodies, and Rapid Fire's 3-damage
chunks were the size Skittish eats (`EB-431`). Run 2 called Rapid Fire the
card never worth playing and Tinder Toss worth it only against one enemy.

**One charge reacts, in placement order, and nothing prints it.** Run 2's
fight five: the Melt went to a Bomb 5 placed before a Bomb 8, and the seat
reconstructed the rule by arithmetic (`EB-432`). The same seat's best
ordering decision (Diona then Ka-pow!, 33 raw into 41) was made against a
rule it had to infer. Reactions fire silently on the page (`EB-410`, open);
"the kit narrates the bad case and stays silent on the good one," the one
printed Melt being Diona's preview warning a reaction would be wasted.

**The Mine badge says Bomb.** The one property the Mine trick turns on, that
it goes off before the hit lands, is on Jumpy Dumpty's face and not on the
badge that names the charge (`EB-417`, on the same fix branch).

**Diona's rider is a printed trap, by design.** "If a Bomb goes off this
turn, gain 5 Block" is dead unless a detonator is in the same hand, and the
seat spent the energy before finding out. That is the brief's §6 as
written, a defence conditional on the decision just made; the buff it
leaves reads more clearly than the card ("the next time one of your Bombs
goes off this turn"), and the face could carry the buff's own words. Not a
row: the shelf's rows already print this shape and the pool pass weighs
them together.

**The reaction table, a fourth time.** "Nine keyword paragraphs print on
every screen for a mono-Pyro character; I triggered exactly one, Melt, and
only because a Cryo companion happened to be offered" (run 1). `EB-428`,
minted from Kokomi round 11 the same afternoon, prints only the rows the
deck can reach.

other two kits' at 120 (§4). Nothing here is a strength reading.
