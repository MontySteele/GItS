# Furina: archetypes and the pool to about 40

Paper, 2026-10-05. **RULED 2026-10-05, all three picks at their defaults** ([USER]: "Agreed on all furina picks"). Main session design. It comes from the seat round
(`review/records/furina-tab-round-2026-10-05.md`) and [USER]'s ask: "If it's
promising, we can start thinking archetypes and expand the card pool to 40ish
ahead of my first run." Card rows go in `docs/prototype-surface.yaml`.

## 1. Is it promising?

Yes, at the core.
- **Taking a hit against Blocking it is a real choice.** It shows on the
  enemy's intent, and it decided turns in both runs.
- **Banking Fanfare can lose the run.** Lane 2 lost the Kaiser Crab
  holding 113 Fanfare.

The weakness is narrowness. Both runs got Universal Revelry from Arcane
Scroll, and both played one deck: Revelry, take hits, Bravura. The paper's
three archetypes (`furina-research-proposal-2026-10-05.md` sec.4) did not
show up:

| Archetype | Cards now | What the seats did |
|---|---|---|
| Ousia (Drain for frontload) | 7, plus the starter | Drained every time; the Drain line bit only at the act-2 bosses |
| Pneuma (Repay) | 7 | Repay changed one turn per lane. Singer, Endless Waltz and Clorinde were never played |
| The Crowd (bank and cash in) | 11, plus the starter | All four acts |

The slice is 2 Basic, 14 Common, 9 Uncommon and 4 Rare. A base pool is 20
Common, 38 Uncommon and 27 Rare (`game_ref/*.json`). The base pools are
heavy at Uncommon and Rare, and Furina is light there. So the new cards go
in at Uncommon and Rare, where build-arounds belong. No Commons are added.

## 2. The fix that comes first: Revelry stops doubling hits

Sec.15 changed Revelry to read only Drain and Repay, because doubling hits
made Blocking "work against her own plan". Sec.17 put hits back to fix one
sim number: the act-2 boss win rate for a finale deck. The seats then played
the loop sec.15 warned about:
- Bravura 86 on a 19-HP enemy;
- 252 into Rocket, 134 more than the kill needed;
- 261 into Crusher, on a turn after skipping Block on purpose.

**New text:** "Whenever you Drain or Repay, gain that much additional
Fanfare." [cost 1]
- It also gives Pneuma a Rare payoff, which it lacks today.
- Lynette keeps her once-a-turn hit line, so the Crowd still has one
  hit-reader.

## 4. Drain as a reflex (K3)

No seat declined a Drain for taste in four acts ("HP returns after
combat"). The paper's pick 6 named the first lever: the Singer (Salon
Solitaire) rests on any turn you Drained. It would take 2 Repay off a
Drain turn, which is too small to change a Drain 3 that buys 5 damage.

What the seats also showed is that the line did bite, at both act-2
bosses. There Drain stopped being free and the run turned on it. That is
the paper's design, with Drain a yes early and a decision near the line.
Lyney and A Five-Century Act make the line a resource to build around. I'd
leave the reflex alone until your run.
