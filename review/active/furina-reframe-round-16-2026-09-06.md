Salon's rules be reachable without building for it") has its answer in
the draft, not the rule. Closed as a design question; the log is the
row.

**The Spotlight's line is false, both lanes, and the truth is better.**
Lane 1: "Aria and Hearts Swelling grant Encore without performing, and I
broke the rule on turn 1 of the run." Lane 2: the free arrival
performance never charges Encore and Chevalmarin grants 3, "I lit it
