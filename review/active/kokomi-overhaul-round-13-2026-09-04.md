## 2. What the round found

**The Plan is a decision axis, and one card proved it.** War Council was
planned in fight 1 (nothing pressing), played face-up in fight 2 (Weak on
three attackers now), planned in fight 5 (racing beat mitigating), and in
the boss both at once through Change of Plans. "One card generating four
different correct answers in nine fights." Turn one of fight one presented
a real choice off printed faces alone (plan or play, with Slack Water able
to supply the Weak either way).

**Ordering inside a turn is the best part.** The Effigy's Slow (10% more
per card played), Rosaria's Vulnerable landing after her own hit, and the
Casket's tick wanting Vulnerable up: elite turn 2 was 31 damage from 3
energy on ordering alone, every input printed. The boss-variant preview
("Bosses cannot be Frozen. Hydro plus Cryo is consumed and applies 2
Vulnerable instead") and the Plan lines repricing under Vulnerable were
called the best-written lines in the kit.

**A targeted potion with no target vanished.** Weak Potion used untargeted
in the boss: consumed, no Weak on any enemy, no intent moved, no refusal.
A bridge defect (`EB-452`).

**A reaction can leave no trace.** On the Cubex Construct the
Electro-Charged fired, Artifact ate the Poison, and the Casket re-laid the
Hydro the reaction consumed; the board looked untouched. `EB-410` widened
with the Artifact half.

**The carry-out panel omitted a Plan and mis-stated a number.** Two Plans
written, one printed; "7, the 7 is damage" beside a 9-HP loss, the Casket's
2 unattributed (`EB-453`).

**Change of Plans, dead three times before good once.** It needs a written
Plan and its face never says so; when it fired (boss turn 2, 2 energy for
21 damage and Weak on three) it was excellent. It gets The Big One's
refusal form (`EB-455`).

**Small legibility.** Jean's Gale Blade carries no element tag in its title
while every Hydro, Electro, Cryo and Pyro card does (`EB-454`); the Byrdonis
Egg option printed no card face (`EB-448` widened); Slimed entered the deck
from intents the seat watched and was first named at a Smith screen
(`EB-447` widened). Two one-point arithmetic gaps in opposite directions
(fight 3 printed 10 dealt 9; elite turn 2 printed 13 dealt 14) are the
fold's rounding (`EB-438`), cited. Rainbow Ring and Slow-versus-potions are
base-game text; not rows.

**The deck is two cards deep, a fourth round.** Strike and Defend were 8 of
22 cards and a third of turns had no rejected alternative; a Defend was
played into a non-attacking enemy three times for want of a sink. R254
ruled starter density STANDS; the re-ask trigger (a round with Tide Chart
drawn) is unmet for the seventh time, which is the pool pass's question
(a second copy, or a Common that reads the memory), not this packet's.
