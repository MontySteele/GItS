# Furina round ten: two Encore on turn one, a relic that reads as permanent, and a bridge that stopped

Written 2026-09-05. One blind Opus seat played the reframe arm on
`0.2.2614+proto` (rows on `docs/prototype-surface.yaml`), the first build
with R258's opening (Furina starts each combat with 2 Encore, `EB-479`),
R257's Tide Chart and the round-15 rows. Record:
`review/qa/furina-reframe-round-10-2026-09-04/opus-act1.md`. Prototype
stage, Guardrail-7. No pick.

## 1. The run in one paragraph

Seed `DNC6JEGXSCYM`, Ascension 2. Four fights, four won, including the
four-Gardener elite for 15 HP; floor 12, 66 of 84 HP. **Not a budget
stop:** at 98 of 120 actions, after `go "Unknown (path 1)"` from the
Treasure floor, nine consecutive observes timed out on the bridge's
singleplayer endpoint while its root still answered; the seat stopped as
the brief says and the lane was torn down hung (`EB-489`).

## 2. What the round found

**The opening.** "Opening: HP 62/78, Encore 2, 3 energy." Turn one of
fight one was still a generic Vulnerable-then-attack-then-Block turn: the
hand held no Deploy and no Aria, so the 2 Encore had nothing to buy until
turn 2, when the Spotlight was lit off it. R258 removes the refusal; it
does not put the stage in the opening hand, which is the pool question
below. The seat's first real kit decision was turn 2, as in rounds 5 to 8,
but for a different reason.

**The Spotlight relic reads as permanent.** "It does nothing once your
Companion cards are lit" was read as a one-time purchase; fight 2 opened
with Chevreuse printing 7 again. Per combat, said nowhere (`EB-485`).

**Backstroke's Block obeys neither the Spotlight nor an upgrade.** Damage
10, 15 lit, 18 upgraded; Block 6 throughout, while Lynette's Block moved
5, 7, 10 on the same screens. A second op outside the fold, `EB-463`'s
family (`EB-486`).

**Encore and Fanfare vanish at 0.** The header prints the row only when
the pool holds something, so a load-bearing number was inferred from a
missing line twice (`EB-487`). Grand Salon was the run's first reward with
no Salon tip on the screen, and the seat could not price it (`EB-488`).

**The Salon was furniture for three fights.** With one Deploy in the deck,
most Companion plays printed `EB-477`'s new line, "No member on stage:
performs nobody", and the kit's headline mechanic spent most of the run as
text explaining why nothing happened. The two turns it was live were the
best of the run. Round 9 read the same. The pool pass's question, now
asked twice: Deploy density, or a starter that puts a member on stage.

**What read true.** The Encore rule ("after Block it absorbs damage") seen
and confirmed in fight 1; the elite killed on a computed line; Skittish's
ordering learned by spending a card, a base-game power and not a row.
