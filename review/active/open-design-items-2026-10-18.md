# Three open design items: Kokomi's leftovers, Yae's Tenko cards, the Plan tip (2026-10-18)

The user, 2026-10-18: "a design pass on those three open items". These are the three kit follow-ups listed that
afternoon:
1. Kokomi's leftovers: Nereid's Ascension, Exhaust and Grand Design.
2. Yae's Tenko cards.
3. The Plan tip.

Each item ends in a pick with a default.

## 1. Kokomi's leftovers

**Nereid's Ascension.** "At the start of your turn, your first Plan is carried out twice." The v2 paper kept it
and named a Plan-line version to try next: "Plan: This turn, your Attacks deal double damage", with a no-stacking
clause (`BACKLOG.md`). The last two rounds argue for keeping the card as it is:
- Seats named it among their favourite turns in v2 (`kokomi-v2-round-2026-10-18.md`).
- In v3, seat 2 planned Kelp Wall under it for 20 and the kill next turn (`kokomi-v3-round-2026-10-18.md`).

The Plan-line version would be a second damage doubler beside Second Wave, and a stacking rule would have to be
written for it. **I'd close the item and keep the card.**

**Exhaust: "salvage a failed Plan".** The v2 paper held it (pick 2 (a)). GPT's caution still stands: a Plan that
fails because tomorrow's intent changed needs an affordable way to adapt. The flip covers part of that: it changes
which line fires, though not when it fires (GPT's review of this paper, 2026-10-18). Nothing in two rounds showed a
seat stuck with a dead Plan it couldn't flip. An Exhaust card would also be her only one, with no payoffs. **I'd
hold it,** not close it: no Kokomi Exhaust card now, and the space stays open if a round shows dead Plans.

**Nereid as Kokomi herself.** GPT's review also noted that Nereid is a good planning engine, but that doesn't settle
whether her signature Burst should feel like Kokomi taking command. That is a question about character, not
balance. It is noted here for the lore and Burst pass and changes nothing in this playtest.

**Grand Design** (Rare, 1 Energy, Exhaust): "Plan up to 3 cards from your Hand. They cost 0." Seats 1 and 6 found it
"cannot be played", or planning one card, whenever the hand held Strikes and Defends. Neither has a Plan line, so
the Bake-Kurage can't take them (`ArmKeywordTips`, BACKLOG). It was "excellent when the hand had Wrath, Wake and
Steady Tide". A Rare that bends the rules should always do its one thing. The fix is to let it take any card:

> "Plan up to 3 cards from your Hand, even ones with no Plan line. They cost 0."

A card with no Plan line is played as itself when it lands, at the front enemy. A planned Strike deals 6 next turn,
and a planned Defend gives 5 Block that turn. This keeps the card's decision, what to move to tomorrow, and removes
the dead hands. The starter is unchanged.

**GPT's objection holds, and it changes my default.** "Any card" also admits Powers and Companions. A planned Power
under Nereid ("your first Plan is carried out twice") would be played twice. Every planned card would also count for
Tideturn, Feint and the other Plan counters. That reaches back toward the generic value engine this cleanup was
meant to remove. A narrower version rescues the same dead hands without that reach:

> "Plan up to 3 cards from your Hand, Basic cards included. They cost 0." (61 characters)

Only Strikes and Defends gain a new door, and both are cards nobody wants doubled. GPT preferred "Draw 2" first: it
keeps Plan cards valuable to draft. It still leaves a basics-only hand dead, which is the case the seats reported.

## 2. Yae's Tenko cards (parked since 2026-10-13)

The idea is Tenko cards that carry their own scaling: +1 a strike per Tenko (paper pick 1 (b)), or a bonus when a
strike reacts (pick 1 (c)). Yae's last round put her damage above base in every act: 1.21, 1.15 and 1.45
(`yae-round-11-2026-10-18.md`). More scaling on the Tenko payoff would push that further, and her open problem is
act-1 HP, not damage. **I'd keep it parked** until a round shows Tenko decks falling behind.

Yae's Sakura display is not a design item. It was ruled on 2026-10-15 (`yae-pool-to-78`, pick 3 (a)), so it is
built now, without a pick.

## 3. The Plan tip

Today: "Instead of the line above, play it on the Bake-Kurage. Plans happen next turn in order and read enemies
then; Block lasts that turn." The flip instruction ("click to flip") left it in v2, to make room for the timing
clause. The flip instruction now lives on the Bake-Kurage's own text, "Click a waiting Plan to flip it.", where the
flip is done. Your earlier ruling had put it in this tip.

The timing clause earned its place again this round:
- seat 6's Plans hit the Tunneler's Block for 21;
- another seat said "the page never says plainly" that Plans resolve after the enemy attacks.

GPT's review asked for the full order (pay now, enemies act, you draw, Plans resolve) and the flip together. Both fit
in the 135-character tip ceiling, at exactly 135:

> "Play it on the Bake-Kurage instead. After enemies act and you draw, Plans happen in order; Block lasts that turn.
> Click one to flip it."

This replaces "read enemies then" with the order itself. The flip line also stays on the Bake-Kurage.
