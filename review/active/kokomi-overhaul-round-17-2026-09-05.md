# Kokomi round seventeen: the Plan is the kit, the doubler is its spine, and three of its payoffs cannot be seen working

Written 2026-09-05, under the Prototype loop (`operations/stage-gate.md`,
*The loop inside Prototype*). Two blind Opus seats on `0.2.2674+proto`
(main `6e873961`). Records: `review/qa/kokomi-round-17-2026-09-05/opus-arm-act1.md`
(lane 1, assembled) and `opus-natural-act1.md` (lane 2, natural).
Prototype stage, Guardrail-7. Graded against the amended seat brief.

## 1. The hypothesis

"With Plans on the Attacks she is offered, the jellyfish is written to on
most turns, and a doubler that never spends the morning (Nereid's Power)
reads as a payoff rather than a tax; Tide Chart, drawn, pays the morning a
second time."

**Assembled deck** (lane 1, seed `H8532FBJ9RT3`, Ascension 2): Tide Chart,
Nereid's Ascension, The Moon Overlooks the Waters, Battle Plan, Stolen
Chapter and Well Laid added to the starter. 118 of 120 actions; **died on
floor 8 to the Byrdonis elite**, four fights won first. **Natural run**
(lane 2, seed `FNT3MWHUPCJT`, Ascension 3): 120 of 120, floor 12, four
fights won including the Terror Eel elite, stopped mid-fight 5 at 77 of 80.

## 2. What the round found

**The hypothesis, read.** *Nereid's Ascension as a Power is the deck's
spine:* played in four of five fights, "the card the deck is built around",
and never once read as a tax; round 14's reading is answered. *Plan-line
density paid on the natural lane:* Flank, Ambush, Riptide and Ripple were
all drafted, the seat wrote a Plan on most turns, and answered "now" three
times and "Plan" five, "and could justify each in a sentence". *Tide Chart
did not pay visibly:* played three times, "I could never confirm it did
anything", one card drawn with two carry-outs pending and no line anywhere
(`EB-503`). **Verdict: supported on the doubler and the density; the third
clause is unreadable rather than false**, and the fix is narration before
any number.

**Turn one of fight one presented a decision on both lanes.** Both seats
said so unprompted: "how much of turn 1 do I spend on turn 2 was readable
off the faces before I played anything", and the game answered the moment
they committed. The seventh-round "two cards deep" reading is still there
on the natural lane (three of five opening hands all Strike and Defend,
"the kit's decisions live in about eight cards"), but the kit's first
question now arrives on turn one when a kit card is in hand.

**Three payoffs the seats could not see working.** Tide Chart, silent
(`EB-503`). Well Laid, whose face prints the live total under a clause that
reads as pending, and which counts written Plans rather than carry-outs so a
doubled morning paid +3 (`EB-501`). Nereid's Power and The Moon: the
now-copy is single and "every Plan twice" admits no exception on its face
(`EB-500`; the rule stands, the face names the morning). Each is a legibility
row, and each is on a card this round was run to read.

**Reactions fire where nobody can see them.** The natural seat met four
reactions and one was announced, as a preview on a hand card; the three
that fired from a carry-out, the relic and a companion tick produced an aura
vanishing, a "Poison 4" with no cause, and about 4 Block "from nowhere"
(`EB-410`, widened). The preview line itself was called "the single most
useful thing on the combat screen" by both seats, and the reaction
paragraph's warning about the relic re-laying the aura "predicted in advance
the exact confusion I was about to have". The display debt the sweep packet
puts first is this.

**Companions and access.** Assembled seat: four offers, took Kujou Sara on
floor 1 ("the only second element on offer"), reactions fired three times in
five fights, "never on a turn I chose", one Electro source in twenty cards.
Natural seat: eight offers, took Gorou and bought Shinobu at the shop for 74
gold ("the single largest decision of the run"), passed Sayu and Sucrose
(Swirl with nothing to spread), passed Thoma and Dahlia on gold. Companions
played per fight: 0 / 1 / 1 / 1 / 0 and 0 / 1 / 2 / 1 / 0. The Commander
question (R234) reads the same: one Companion a fight is the rate the reward
slot supports, and a Companion-count card needs two.

**What read true.** Shrink repriced the Attack faces and left the Plan lines
alone, so "play it or plan it stopped being the same card" (a decision the
enemy's debuff created); Vanguard was the natural seat's happiest draw (a
free Vulnerable that fires the Casket, whose Hydro hit sets off the waiting
reaction); Battle Plan under The Moon "refunds its own cost, draws 2, and
still stands as a Plan", the assembled seat's best card; Flank's set aim
hit both attackers for 16 each on a doubled morning.

**Defects, and the death.** The assembled seat died largely to a refusal:
`play "Riptide" on "Byrdonis"` was refused with no working forms listed, and
the bare form was found four rounds later (`EB-499`). A planned Weak did not
print on the enemy for two turns while the intent fell by a quarter
(`EB-502`). The Hexerei glossary printed Klee's rule on a Kokomi run
(`EB-504`). Vulnerable's body text still reads "from Attacks" while the
glossary and the numbers say every hit (`EB-481` reopened; the build fixed
the tips and not the power's line). Not rows: Stolen Chapter's Plan line
"never once looked right" (a doubled draw-4 against 3 energy); three
arithmetic outcomes the natural seat could not reconcile, two in its
favour, one of them the unlabelled Crystallize (`EB-410`); Trash Heap's
unnamed card (`EB-448`).

## 3. What the round did not test

The act-1 boss on either lane. Pincer, Feigned Retreat, Riptide's Plan
line, the redesigned Tide Chart counting a doubled morning. Nothing here is
a strength reading; one death on an elite at Ascension 2 after a
budget-shaped refusal is not a strength reading either.
