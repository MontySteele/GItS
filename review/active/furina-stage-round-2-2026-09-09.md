## 2. The stage is printed, and the reads changed

Every seat saw the stage line on every combat screen ("lead: Usher 3", the
reserve behind it) and the event lines beneath it. The questions round one
could not answer now have answers.

**Turn one is a decision about the bar.** All three seats chose their first
turn around the Usher's 3: raise him first so the Spend leaves a shield
standing (natural), or spend him to exactly 0 for the 13 and the 4-Block bow
and take the empty stage (Preserve, who called it "right about the
arithmetic and wrong about the consequence": turn two was five skills and
no stage). That is the wager the brief describes, and it is now visible.

**A second performer is wanted for a reason no seat expected.** Two seats
found that the reserve performs: "Crabaletta performed from the back seat.
It moved 11" was more damage than any card in the hand. The reserve is a
second damage source, not a spare tyre, and nothing printed says so. The
natural seat wanted no second performer, and gave the reason the brief's
§8 predicted: Raise loads the back while Ousia Surge reads the lead, so a
second body unhooks the only pump from the only payoff unless Scene Change
is in the deck. Both reads are right, and both are legibility: the act
belongs in the glossary (`EB-744`), and the pump-to-payoff bridge is the
Ovation plan's own card.

**The three read as three at the exit and one at the table.** All three seats
said the same sentence in their own words: the bows are three (Block,
element, damage) and the seats are one, because every card speaks in seats
and the acts are not documented. The Preserve seat protected Crabaletta on
purpose, "worth the most alive and dead", which is the first time a seat
protected a performer rather than a seat.

## 3. Spend, read with the bar visible

**The card spends for you.** Four of six seats across two rounds said so, in
the words "no verb to decline" and "the card decided": the rider fires
whenever the stage is occupied, so the wager §4 of the brief describes,
choosing to spend or to keep, never happens at play time. The Preserve seat
lost Crabaletta to a Tidal Flourish it had wanted to play unspent. This is
the round's design finding and it is applied in §5.

**The over-sized Spend split the seats 3:3.** A play (round one natural;
round two Expend, "by the fourth fight, once the bow is the point"; round
two Preserve, "a real price"), or a loophole (round one Expend and Preserve;
round two natural, who summoned a 1-bar body purely so Curtain Rise could
kill it for 13 and a bow, "a 1-energy summon converted into +6 damage and
+4 Block with no downside"). The sim on 100 single fights: the Expend deck
fires 68 percent of its Spends at bars of 1 or 2, keeps 3 HP less per fight
than Preserve, and its cast absorbs 27 percent of what gets through Block
against Preserve's 59; neither deck dominates. The reads split and the
sim does not decide it, so it is the round's one pick (§5).
