# Kokomi: her story events (2026-10-16)

*Rewards repriced 2026-10-19 by the story rewards pass (`story-rewards-pass-2026-10-19.md`, ruled all (a)); the rows below carry the new effects.*

**Why.** Kokomi is the one character with no events of her own. In co-op she
gets the "A Quiet Landing" placeholder every act (co-op record, 2026-10-16).
This follows the ruled chains standard
(`review/active/klee-varka-story-chains-2026-10-14.md`):
- eight events;
- at most one per act;
- the act 1 event chooses act 2's;
- a home thread when an act has no event;
- every price comes from its scene;
- anything outside the Spire is a memory;
- some events offer only bad choices.

**Second draft, after GPT's review** (relayed by [USER], 2026-10-16). It
keeps the eight events and three threads, and makes these revisions:
- **Endgame is earned.** Every route through The Other Commander shows the
  same habit: it always takes a piece it is offered. Endgame uses that
  habit.
- **No closing morals.** Each result shows what she does and stops.
- **More of her pleasure and warmth.** She enjoys strategy, her faith is
  affection as well as duty, and the quiet room has more than one feeling
  in it.
- **Her faith.** The private prayer is hers, with no curse. Public Address
  now comes only from a promise of a public rite.
- **The finale gets the lore right.** Bake-Kurage are her own water-made
  summons, so the tide is water she shapes.
- **Gorou's report:** a scout carries the letter and waits for her answer.
  The meal is Bird Egg Sushi, her stated favourite.

**Where she stands.** She is the Divine Priestess of Watatsumi, and the
strategist who led its resistance. She loves strategy for its own sake:
reading it, arguing with it, playing it out. Being in charge drains her, and
she guards her time alone. She is growing into a priestess who is sure of
what she can do. Her people reach her as Gorou's scouts, and the sea as
water she can shape.

## The events

**Act 1. The Sand Table** (a price, with free ways out).
*A low table on the landing holds a sand map of the Spire, floor by floor,
with a carved piece for everything that lives here. One piece is a little
jellyfish. It is standing exactly where Kokomi is standing.*

| Option | Effect | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Play it out until the candles go out. | Lose 7 HP. Upgrade 2 random cards. | She plays the next three floors four different ways and loses twice. By the end the candles are stubs, and she is smiling. |
| Move her piece up one floor. | Remove a card. | She moves it gently, as if it might be watching. Something in her pack feels lighter. |
| Pocket the enemy's general. | Gain a random potion. | It is a very small piece, and nobody said she couldn't. |

**Act 1. Coral Light** (a memory; a price, with a free way out).
*Pale coral glows in a crack in the stone, the color of the reefs off
Watatsumi. On afternoons the shrine let her keep for herself, she used to
swim out to coral like this and stay until someone came looking.*

| Option | Effect | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Reach in for a piece. | Lose 10 HP. Gain a random relic. | The coral cuts her hand. She wraps it and keeps the piece anyway. |
| Kneel at the water and pray. | Lose 6 HP. Upgrade 2 random cards. | The water is cold to the knee. She kneels anyway and prays for everyone still at sea. |
| Leave a pearl for it. | Lose 50 Gold. Remove a card. Locked under 50 Gold. | She leaves her best pearl beside it, a thank-you to the sea. |
| Leave the coral undisturbed. | Nothing. | She watches it glow for a while, then climbs on. |

**Act 1. A Room with No One in It** (all free).
*Behind a door with nothing written on it is a small room: one cushion, one
lamp, a stack of unread books. Kokomi closes the door behind her. For the
first time since the Spire began, nobody needs her to decide anything.*

| Option | Effect | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Take an hour to herself. | Heal 15 HP. | She does nothing at all, carefully, for a whole hour. |
| Read the top book. | Choose 1 of 3 random Uncommon Powers to add to your deck. | A treatise on naval supply. On page forty the author is wrong about tides, and she fills three margins explaining why. |
| Plan a holiday. | Gain a random potion. | Three days off, one beach, and two fallback plans in case of rain. |

**Act 2. The Other Commander** (follows The Sand Table; a price, with a
free way out). Each route shows the same habit, which Endgame uses. It opens
with a line from her act 1 choice:
- *played:* "She knows this floor. She has already lost on it twice, in sand."
- *moved:* "Her piece is already here. She did not move it this far."
- *pocketed:* "The general in her pocket has gone warm."

*Across another sand table sits a figure in gray with no face, its pieces in
a patient line. It opens a hand: your move.*

| Option | Effect | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Play the full game. | Lose 12 HP. Gain a random relic. | It takes every piece she offers, even the bad ones. She wins by one piece, hours later. |
| Offer a draw. | Transform a card. | It agrees only once she offers it a piece to keep. It cannot leave a piece on the table. |
| Concede and study its opening. | Add **Doubt** to your deck. Choose 1 of 3 random Rare cards to add to your deck. | She plays its first ten moves against herself until she sees it: it takes every piece left in reach. |

**Act 2. The Serpent's Bones** (follows Coral Light; every option priced).
It opens with a line from her act 1 choice:
- *reached:* "Her palm still stings where the coral cut it."
- *prayed:* "The sea prayer is still in her mouth."
- *paid:* "She has one pearl left. She is glad she kept it."

*A great curved bone is set into the wall, long as a boat, faintly purple.
Every child on Watatsumi knows that shape: Orobashi, who brought his people up
to the surface and later died fighting the Shogun. His remains have poisoned people before.*

| Option | Effect | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Lay a hand on it, through her sleeve. | Lose 8 Max HP. Gain a random relic. | She wraps her hand first and keeps the touch short; she knows what his remains did to Inazuma. For a moment she can feel the island he gave her people. The cold stays in her fingers. |
| Gather the offerings at its foot. | Gain 100 Gold. Add **Doubt** to your deck. | Someone has been leaving Mora here for a very long time. She gathers it to take back to Watatsumi, where it can do some good. The bone whispers to her all the way up. |
| Promise him a rite at home. | Remove a card. Add **Public Address** to your deck. | A proper one, on Watatsumi, with the whole shrine there. She means every word, and she is already dreading the speech. |

**Act 2. A Report from Gorou** (her home event; all free). It follows A Room
with No One in It, or no act 1 event. When it follows the room, it opens:
*"The quiet room did her good."*

*A Watatsumi scout is waiting on the stair, soaked to the knee, with a
satchel and a wrapped box. "From the General, Your Excellency. He said to
wait for your answer." The letter reads: "Drills on schedule. Fishing boats
out. Bourou Village sends sushi. Requesting permission to send
reinforcements. —Gorou. P.S. Please eat properly."*

| Option | Effect | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Send for reinforcements. | Choose 1 of 3 random Uncommon Companions to add to your deck. | The scout runs down the stairs with her orders. An hour later there are boots on the stairs below, and a familiar voice already calling drill. |
| Write back with new orders. | Upgrade 2 random cards. | Three pages of orders, and a fourth telling him to rest too. The scout salutes and goes. |
| Eat the sushi while it is fresh. | Heal 20 HP. | Bird egg sushi, her favorite. Gorou remembered. She eats every piece and sends the box back with a thank-you. |

**Act 3. Endgame** (follows The Other Commander; a price, with a free way
out). It opens with a line from her act 2 choice:
- *played:* "She beat it once, by a single piece."
- *drew:* "Their last game ended in a draw."
- *conceded:* "She knows its opening by heart now."

*The faceless commander waits at the top of the stairs without its board. It
has one piece left. So does she. The step between them is split down the
middle, like the fold in a game board.*

| Option | Effect | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Offer it her last piece. | Lose 18 HP. Gain a random Rare relic. | She sets her piece on the split step. It takes the piece, as it always does, and the stair folds shut over it like a closing board. The jolt throws her hard against the wall. |
| Pay its stake and trade pieces. | Lose 100 Gold. Transform 2 cards. Locked under 100 Gold. | It will not trade for nothing. She counts out its stake in Mora, and they trade their last pieces. |
| Bow and walk past. | Nothing. | A commander's bow, one to another. She takes the stairs, and it does not follow. |

**Act 3. The Tide Comes In** (the finale of the other two threads; a price,
with free ways out). It opens with a line from the act 2 event:
- *Serpent's Bones:* "The water smells of salt and incense, like the shrine on festival days."
- *Gorou's report, reinforcements:* "Somewhere below, she can hear Gorou's soldiers singing."
- *otherwise:* "The water moves when she does."

*Water rises up the stairs: real sea water, salt and cold. Kokomi feels the
same pull she uses to shape her Bake-Kurage, and the water answers her.*

| Option | Effect | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Shape a whole shoal. | Lose 6 HP. Choose 1 of 3 random upgraded Uncommon Powers to add to your deck. | A dozen Bake-Kurage, more than she has ever held at once. The whole stairwell turns blue with their light. |
| Ride the tide up. | Heal 20 HP. | She lets the water carry her a floor and a half, eyes closed, like floating off the reef at home. |
| Send the water home to the sea. | Remove a card. | She sends it back down toward the sea, and thinks of home until it's gone. |

## What I would check before building

- **Lore:**
  - Orobashi's remains poisoned Inazuma (canon).
  - Bake-Kurage are made from water (her talent text).
  - Bird Egg Sushi is her stated favourite (voice-overs).
  - Her childhood swimming is ours, framed as a memory.
- **Every reward card exists in her pool:** Treatise, Kurage Canopy and the
  four Gorou cards.
- **No forced lethal choice.** Every event has an option that costs no HP
  and needs no Gold (Coral Light gained "Leave the coral undisturbed" in the
  third pass), though a player can still choose an HP price at low HP.
