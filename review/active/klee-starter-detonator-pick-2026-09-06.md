# Klee's starter, after R261: the detonator, and the loop that pays for itself

Written 2026-09-06 for [USER]. Two pages, one pick. Every claim is
checkable against the named record.

## 1. What changed and what the seats saw

R261 (2026-09-05) put Innate on Jumpy Dumpty alone. Three natural blind
seats have played that starter since (`review/qa/klee-round-21-2026-09-06/opus-natural-act1.md`,
`review/qa/klee-round-22-2026-09-06/opus-natural-b-act1.md`,
`.../opus-natural-a2-act1.md`), on Ascension 0 and 1, all three acts
finished with every fight won. All three say the same two things.

**The placer is now a ritual, and the first turn plays itself.** Fourteen
of fifteen fights across the three lanes opened with Jumpy Dumpty played
first; the seats call it "not a choice, it is a ritual" and "it plays
itself". The kit's first real decision arrives on turn two, when Ka-pow!
or a Bomb size makes cash-or-cook a question. Turn one is live only when
the enemy supplies armour or the seat's HP supplies pressure. That is what
R261 bought: the dead first turn of nine rounds is gone, and a fixed
opening replaced it.

**The detonator is now the wait.** The starter has one Set off, Ka-pow!
(0 energy, Retain). Across the three lanes: "a Bomb 16 sat on the elite's
board for a turn with no decision to make, only a card to wait for"; three
turns with a Bomb up and nothing to cash it; "five turns across six fights
had a live Bomb on the board and nothing in hand that could set it off;
those turns are 100 percent filler. The draft can fix it, I took three
detonators by floor 5 and the rate fell, but the starting deck cannot."

**The Spark loop pays for itself and nothing else.** Sparks come from
Bombs going off (the relic), so a Spark-priced detonator is dead until a
detonation has already happened: "CANNOT BE PLAYED: you have 1 Spark, and
this costs 2" with a Bomb 12 on the board. Once the loop is running the
bank overflows: seats ended fights holding 3, 4, 5 and spent almost
nothing. `EB-577` (the first two rewards each carry a Spark-priced row,
built) put sinks in the seats' hands and did not change the reading.
Seven rounds now read the bank as inert or circular.

**A fourth reading, from the other edge (added the same morning).** An
assembled lane on the fixer-S build
(`review/qa/klee-round-23-2026-09-06/opus-arm-act1.md`, Ascension 1) was
given six Spark-priced kit cards and no other detonator: "the Spark loop
deadlocks; Sparks need detonations, detonations need Spark cards, nothing
printed warns of it, and that ended the run rather than damage" (the
act-1 elite, a Bomb 12 live with Fireworks Show the only detonator in hand
and priced out, 0 Sparks, 0 Block). The same lane: Energy and Sparks idled
on different turns, and the deck had no unconditional Block because its
Block cards read the Bomb. That is the pick's option 4 (the opening Spark)
and option 3 (an Energy-priced detonator in the starter) seen from the
failure state, and it moves my recommendation: 3 over 1, without waiting
for the run, because the deadlock is the starter's and not the draft's.

## 2. Why this is your pick and not a row

The starter is R242's ("basics are supposed to be bad") and R261's (Innate
on Jumpy Dumpty alone, with "avoid putting too many actually good cards
in the starting deck" standing). Every option below touches one of those
two rulings or the relic's economy, which is a design direction a brief
cannot settle. The loop has read it three times on natural lanes and
proposes nothing on its own.

## 3. The pick

**PICK 1 (A): the starter's second half.**

1. **Hold. (default)** R261 stands as ruled; the draft is the fix (all
   three seats drafted a second detonator by floor 5 and the dead turns
   fell). Read again after your own act-1 run on this build, which is
   still due. Cost: nothing; the dead-Bomb turn stays the starter's
   recurring failure state in act 1.
2. **Ka-pow! gains Innate too.** GPT's original "both Innate" (the
   design review of 2026-09-05, `review/records/klee-turn-one-design-review-2026-09-05.md`).
   Every fight opens with the placer and the detonator; turn one becomes
   cook-or-cash immediately (Bomb 8 now for 4 + 8, or hold). Cost: the
   starter's two kit cards are both guaranteed, which is the "too many
   good cards" line you drew; Ka-pow! is a 0-cost Retain card and its
   guarantee is worth more than its face.
3. **A second basic detonator replaces one Strike.** A plain basic ("Set
   off. Deal 3." at 1 energy, no Retain, not Innate) in the ten, so two of
   fourteen draws can cash a Bomb instead of one. Cost: one Strike fewer
   (the seats never wanted Strikes); a new basic row to author and audit.
4. **The relic's Spark is not the only Spark: she starts each combat with
   2.** One number on the relic (1 to 2). A 2-Spark detonator is playable
   before any detonation; the bank still overflows late. Cost: the
   circularity is eased, not removed, and the late-fight overflow the
   seats read as noise gets one Spark worse.

Options 2 and 3 answer the detonator; option 4 answers the loop; option 1
answers neither and costs nothing. My recommendation is 3: it keeps both kit cards where R261 put them and
adds a card that is bad on purpose, and the deadlock the assembled lane
hit is the starter's own (a Bomb, one priced-out detonator, no Energy
exit). Option 1 remains the default until you rule.
