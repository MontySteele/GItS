## 1. Who she is
- Titles: Divine Priestess of Watatsumi Island (GB, KQM, GR, FX); Pearl of Wisdom (FX, Search). Trailer title "Ruler of the Armies" (Mein-MMO via search).
- 5-star Hydro Catalyst, support healer (GB, FX). Constellation Dracaena Somnolenta, "Sleeping Dragoness" (FX, GRant). Birthday February 22 (FX, Search).
- VAs: EN Risa Mei, JP Suzuko Mimori, CN Guiniang, KR Yeo Yun-mi (SK).
- Signature dish: A Stunning Stratagem (FX). Favorite food: Bird Egg Sushi (GR). Hobby: reading strategy books in a quiet place for a whole day (GR).
- Signature weapon: Everlasting Moonglow, 5-star HP catalyst (SK).
- Namecard: not found.
- Release: story quest and banner "Drifting Luminescence" on 2021-09-21 (quest page via search).

## 2. Exact names
- Normal Attack: The Shape of Water (GG, GB, KQM, FX). Skill: Kurage's Oath. Burst: Nereid's Ascension (all sources).
- Passives: Princess of Watatsumi (swim stamina), Flawless Strategy (+25% Healing Bonus, no crits), A1 Tamakushi Casket, A4 Song of Pearls (GB, KQM, FX).
- A1 spelling: GB, KQM and a TCG card page say "Tamakushi Casket". GG and FX say "Tamanooya's Casket". Use Tamakushi.
- Constellations (GG, GB, KQM, FX agree): C1 At Water's Edge; C2 The Clouds Like Waves Rippling; C3 The Moon, A Ship O'er the Seas; C4 The Moon Overlooks the Waters; C5 All Streams Flow to the Sea; C6 Sango Isshin.
- Outfit: Sparkling Coralbone (GB).

## 3. Story
- Watatsumi: the people once lived under the sea in Enkanomiya until the white serpent god Orobashi (spelled "Orobaxi" on some pages) brought them to the surface (GR, FX). The Raiden Shogun killed Orobashi; its resentment became the Tatarigami; its protective will passes down the Sangonomiya bloodline (PA, GR, FX).
- Rise: she became Divine Priestess young; many doubted her; fairness, strategy and care won trust (PA, FX).
- Resistance: she led the islanders against the Vision Hunt Decree, then turned to governance and economics in peace (FX). Gorou is the resistance general, reporting to her (Search). The Traveler persuades or defeats the Raiden Shogun, who ends the decree (Search; which is unclear). Siege of Tenshukaku detail is Search only.
- Strategy books: read military treatises voraciously as a child, hence good at predicting outcomes; wrote five volumes of operational directives (PA, Search). Her strategy "weighs big picture and details" and aims to win at least cost (FX).
- Personality: introverted, drained by crowds; keeps a private "Energy System" mood notebook; retreats to read and admits only close friends (PA, FX). Motto: "as the ruler is, so shall the people be" (FX).
- Bake-Kurage: the water-made jellyfish her skill summons; it heals allies and applies Wet (FX, PA). Her A1 refreshes it when she bursts (KQM).
- Story quest: Dracaena Somnolenta Chapter, Act I (GRant). Plot (GRant): before peace talks with the Shogunate, the Sangonomiya army plans a Victory Feast; Gorou asks the Traveler to burn a Fatui ship; in Borou Village guards accuse a merchant of dealing with the Fatui, Kokomi investigates and clears him; she slips away to a secret cave hideout; at the feast Kouzen explains some soldiers oppose peace over fallen comrades, and a rumor says the Tenryou Commission works with the Fatui; at the talks fighting breaks out, Kujou Sara and Kokomi calm it and the talks succeed; Kokomi forms a secret group for soldiers who want to keep fighting; it ends at her hideout with her reflecting. Needs Story Key and AR 40 (Search).
- War with the Shogunate: only as above; battle detail is thin in fetched sources.

## 4. Relationships
- Gorou (general, calls her "Her Excellency", Search). Kouzen, a soldier (GRant). Kujou Sara (peace negotiator; Kokomi gains the upper hand by exposing Sara's troops' Fatui dealings, per Search commentary). Raiden Shogun (killed Orobashi; opponent, later ended the decree). Orobashi (ancestral god). The Traveler and Paimon (allies). Fatui (rumor and enemy). Tenryou Commission.

## 5. Places
- Watatsumi Island; Enkanomiya; Sangonomiya Shrine; Borou Village; a Fatui camp southwest of the island; her secret cave hideout near a purple tree (GRant); Nazuchi Beach, where Gorou meets the Traveler (Search); Yashiori Island, Orobashi's remains (PA).
