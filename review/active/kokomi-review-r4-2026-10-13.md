**Did Canopy work?** Yes, as the Ward engine in a Plan deck, three times. Lane 1 (F14, Spiny Toad): "Gorou Plan + Kurage Canopy+ so every Plan gave me a free upgraded Ward"; its Insatiable fight made about 20 Wards and its Living Shield fight 13, and Canopy+ is one of its three happiest-to-draw cards. Lane 5 (F8, Waterfall Giant): three Plans written, "next turn I held 4 Wards. Four Wards (16) + Block 3 + Defend (5) = 24 Block, then Tidal Rebuke for 24 AoE"; the same four-Ward morning at Aeonglass (F21 T3). Lane 3 played it late (F12, F15) for 3 and 1 Wards: real but small. The upgrade (Wards arrive upgraded) is what lane 1 valued.

**Did any Ward source go unused?** Not a drafted one. Wards made but not played were lost to the fight ending (lane 5: six fights at 1 / 0, each a Drill Ward on the lethal turn), to a full hand (lane 1 F16: 4 Wards and 5 Stings to the discard pile, now printed by the #1218 receipt) or to Ethereal (lane 1 F8). The one dead half is the same as round 3's: lane 4 drafted Watchful Shoal twice and its only Ward source was one Bubble Ward, 13 Wards in 15 fights and none in act 1. That is the draft, not the sheet: a Ward source is two Commons and one Uncommon Power, and lane 4 took Shoal payoffs without one.

**Stings** split by archetype this round rather than tracking depth. The jellyfish decks made the most (lane 4: Watchful Shoal x2, Barrage+, Kurage's Wake, "Stings at 18 each", F13; lane 1: Shoal Call+, Endless Shoal; lane 2: Jellyfish Drift x3 and Shoal Call, "a Sting made was almost always played the same turn"). The winning deck was a hybrid: Drift Stings and Itto x2 for Block-as-damage, Masterstroke and Princess+ as the Plans, and a debuff stack (At Water's Edge, Sea's Reproach, Drowning Pressure, Lisa). The brief's two archetypes, Companions and jellyfish, both showed up as whole decks, plus the Tactician as a third, which is the pool working.

**Act-1 boss exits** averaged 38 of 80 against round 3's 49 and round 1's 22; the two low ones are Vantom's Slippery 8 (lane 2, 17) and Kin Priest's Frail (lane 3, 26), both named by the seats as the boss's rule, not a chip problem. Within one round's noise.

## 2. Why runs die

**The Insatiable is where Kokomi is a notch under base.** All five lanes drew it as the act-2 boss. Three won, all narrowly: lane 1 at 27 of 80 (Poison 24 from Fischl Oz on her Hydro did the closing), lane 2 at 20 of 80 (Frantic Escape x7, Masterstroke through Grand Design), lane 5 at 6 of 90 (Tide Wall+ Plan "saved me twice", a Gambler's Brew for Princess+). Two lost: lane 3 at 48 left ("the Insatiable asks for ~330 damage over about 8 turns while the deck can only reliably do ~30-45 per turn"), lane 4 at 167 left ("Sandpit + Frantic Escape forced 6 turns of defence"; its own reading: turn 1 spent on Barrage+/Wake/Orders instead of Block). Across rounds 2 to 4 that is four Kokomi deaths to this boss (r2 lane 2, r3 lane 4, r4 lanes 3 and 4) against four wins, every win under 30 HP. The base five's baseline all passed act 2.

What the boss asks is a race with a clock, and the kit is a setup kit: a Plan lands a turn late, the Escape tax takes the Energy that writes Plans, and the two losing decks had linear damage (one Companion reaction a turn in lane 3, Stings that need two Powers out in lane 4). The three winning decks each had a multiplier or a dot: Nereid's Ascension doubling Riptide+ for 8 cards (lane 1), Masterstroke 42 (lane 2), Opening Gambit then Princess+ (lane 5). That is the parked Nereid's Ascension burst question (STATE.md, "Possible change, parked"), and this paper does not redesign it; the new fact is stated once in pick 2.

**Act 3 is the base's wall, and the kit can climb it.** Lane 1 met Test Subject form 2 (200 HP, 10x3 then 10x4, 10x5, 10x6): "my hand drew no Block on the first form-2 turn; HP 55 to 34 to 6. I blocked 55 once, then the next turn offered 18 against 60." A deck with every basic removed (Pael's Tooth, Pandora's Box) and Block at 4 to 8 a card cannot reach 60; neither can the base five's Commons, which is why the baseline died in act 3 too. Lane 2 met the same boss and won it with a deck built the other way: it arrived at 80 (the act-3 Ancient), carried seven Block cards plus Itto x2 (Block and damage on one card), killed form 2 from 98 with a Masterstroke Plan that landed 63 at the start of its turn, and spent form 3's Intangible turns blocking and the open turns on Masterstroke 54, Itto x2 and Rebuke 64 (F22). Lane 5 met Aeonglass at 52 of 90 (the rest before it healed only to 55), fed Wither every six cards, played Nereid's Ascension on the one turn with room and never got a Plan morning out of it, and died at 316 left. The r3 audit found nine blocking Commons at the base rate; nothing this round moves it, so no generic Block goes in.

**Seat error, per lane.** Lane 2 (the win) made the fewest: Pearl Current into Entomancer's Hive for five Dazed (F12, its own "mistake") and an Oath+ Plan wasted into Vantom's Slippery (F8 T1). Lane 1: mis-numbered Stings after plays and lost two (F9), left Tamakushi's Wrath (Ethereal) in hand on a setup turn (F16), played Moon's Reflection with an empty Exhaust pile (F17). Lane 3: kept three Strikes and three Defends through 33 floors, carried a Decay curse, played Orders from the Shrine at 0 Energy twice (F12, F16), and its NEVER AGAIN is a 2-cost Power it drafted into a deck with no second element in hand half the time. Lane 4: Coral Crash and Masterstroke on the same 45-HP Bowlbug (F12), the boss's first turn on setup. Lane 5: boss reward taken "without reading" (F8), every Block Plan spent before the turn that needed one (F21 T8). None of these is the kit; two of them (Moon's Reflection, Sting numbering) are defects the kit let happen and are in section 4.

## 5. Changes

### A. Build now (no pick needed)

1. **Moon's Reflection (U, 1 [0], Exhaust).** Unplayable while the Exhaust Pile is empty, reason line: "Your Exhaust Pile is empty." Face unchanged.
2. **Plan tip.** Add after "Instead of the line above": "Your Strength is counted when you write the Plan. A planned Attack counts as an Attack played."
3. **Opening Gambit and Second Wave.** Their now-line damage no longer spends Vigor or a Companion's next-Attack rider; they are Skills. Numbers unchanged.
4. **Bennett, Passion Overload and Kujou Sara, Crowfeather Cover (rider powers).** Add to each power's tip: "An Attack carries one element. If two riders are up, the newer one's lands." (The build confirms which rider wins in `ElementFor` and words it to match.)
5. **Harness.** Card handles stay stable within a turn when a play adds a copy (lane 1 F9); the difference line under a damage number names only the terms that moved it (lane 2 F10); verify the #1223 revive line on the next deploy (lane 2 F22); a define entry for boss powers such as Sandpit (lane 2 F15).
6. **Brief, rule 7.** The Casket line reads as STATE.md has it: at the start of her turn while a Plan is waiting, after the Block clear and before Plans resolve.
7. **Probe's known set.** Canopy plus a planned Ward (section 3).

Not changed, with the reason: Dawn Tide, Orders from the Shrine, Tamakushi's Wrath, Pearl Current, Coral Crash (section 3); Masterstroke (section 4, by design); Bubble Ward 6 [9] and Canopy (one round old, working); Tide Wall, Change of Plans, Tideturn (working as printed); Slack Water and Kurage's Oath (starter); the Sting and Ward tokens; no Block number anywhere (the r3 audit stands).
