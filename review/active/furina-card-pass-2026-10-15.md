# Furina: Ousia / Pneuma, and her relic and potion set (2026-10-15)

Two rulings from the kit census (`review/active/kit-census-2026-10-15.md`):
- Pick 1 (a), [USER]: "Agreed on all of the above!" Claude completes Furina's
  relics and potions to the base shape.
- Ousia / Pneuma ([USER]: "Makes sense - I like it!"): each face does one half
  of her kit, so Flip stops being a standalone trick.

Furina is at Prototype, so the cards, relics and potions here are Claude's and
carry no pick.

## 1. Ousia / Pneuma

| | Today | New |
|---|---|---|
| Ousia | Deal 8 [11] damage. | **Drain 2. Deal 10 [13] damage.** |
| Pneuma | Gain 7 [10] Block. | **Repay 2. Gain 8 [11] Block.** |

It still starts each combat on Ousia and Flips after each play. Ousia pays
Fanfare through the Drain, and Pneuma gives the HP back on the next play.

## 2. Relics

She has Salon Solitaire (starter), Opera Glasses (Common), Grand Theater
Program (Rare) and The Curtain Never Falls (Ancient). The base shape needs two
Uncommons, two more Rares and a Shop relic.

| Rarity | Name | Text | Lane | Sized against |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Uncommon | Opera Ticket Stub | At the start of each combat, Summon a random guest. | Guests | Symbiotic Virus (Channel a Dark at combat start) |
| Uncommon | Fontaine Teardrop | Whenever you Repay more than you have drained, gain the rest as Block. | Drain / Repay | Orichalcum-size with Salon Solitaire (1 a turn); larger with Repay cards |
| Rare | Claque's Ledger | Whenever a guest acts, gain 1 Fanfare. | Guests to Fanfare | Grand Theater Program (1 Fanfare a turn) |
| Rare | Bouquet of Roses | Whenever you Spend 10 or more at once, gain [E]. | Fanfare | Ivory Tile (a big card refunds Energy) |
| Shop | Epiclese Season Pass | You have 1 more guest seat. | Guests | Runic Capacitor (more Orb slots) |

Readings a builder needs:
- **Opera Ticket Stub:** the same random guest Ensemble Cast summons, after
  the opening draw.
- **Fontaine Teardrop:** any Repay (cards, guests, Salon Solitaire). The
  printed "Gain 1 Block for any HP it could not Repay" clauses on Hymn of Many
  Waters and Grand Entrance still pay too; the two stack.
- **Claque's Ledger:** every guest act, including Encore!, Tutti!, Final Bow,
  Showstopper and Bring the House Down. A guest that acts twice gains 2.
- **Bouquet of Roses:** a single Spend of 10 or more Fanfare, spend-all cards
  included. Once per Spend.
- **Epiclese Season Pass:** 3 seats become 4; with Ensemble Cast, 4 become 5.

Tear of the Lone Regina cut 2026-10-15: four Rares would be one over the base
shape, and it overlapped Lyney's line and A Five-Century Act.

## 4. What changes for her pools

Her relic offer already leaves out the Silent borrow, and her potion pool
already holds only her own. Both now hold the full set: seven relics and three
potions. Her starter and Ancient are unchanged.
