## 1. Who she is
- Name Furina (full "Furina de Fontaine", WP). In-game title (GDB): "Endless Solo of Solitude". Description line (GDB): "The absolute focus of the stage of judgment, until the final applause sounds."
- Roles: apparent Hydro Archon and ruler of Fontaine, "God of Justice" (WP). Public alias Focalors, though the game later separates the two (WP).
- 5-star Hydro Sword user, Court of Fontaine, Fontaine, version 4.2 (Nov 2023), sub-stat CRIT Rate (GDB). Walks on water while her Salon is out (GDB).
- Constellation: Animula Choragi (GDB). Birthday October 13 (GDB, Fanlore).
- VAs (GDB): EN Amber Lee Connors; CN Qian Chen; JP Inori Minase; KR Kim Ha-young (WP: Kim Ha-yeong).
- Height about 165 cm (WP). Theme song "La vaguelette", sung in French by Cecilia Cara (WP).
- Namecard: "Furina: Banquet", a Friendship 10 reward; its text muses about eating a cake a day and whether they stay fresh, ending that friends will come to share them (GDB).
- Signature weapon: Splendor of Tranquil Waters, 5-star sword, passive "Dawn and Dusk by the Lake"; "A scepter around which swirls pure water", once the highest authority over the seas (GDB; WP names it as signature).
- Special dish: "Pour la Justice", made from the cake recipe "La Lettre a Focalors" (sold by Arouet at Cafe Lutece, 5,000 Mora); party Max HP +30% (GL). Pre-release leaks said "Proustis"; superseded. Verify accents in game.
- Personality in game text: vain, theatrical, tired of the act (GDB greeting: "Fontaine's most beloved star, Furina"). Hobbies: the "Queen's Crown" series, surfing, shopping sprees; trouble: choosing a sauce for her macaroni (GDB). Favourite food: "desserts are like operas", both need careful planning to be a hit; dislikes most "creative" dishes (GDB).

## 2. Exact names
- Normal attack: **Soloist's Solicitation**. Up to 4 strikes; charged attack is a solo dance (Physical) that switches her Arkhe alignment; attacks periodically summon a Spiritbreath Thorn or Surging Blade by alignment (GDB, GBd).
- Elemental skill: **Salon Solitaire**. Ousia: summons Salon Members, who deal Hydro DMG and can boost themselves by consuming HP from nearby characters. Pneuma: summons the **Singer of Many Waters**, who heals nearby characters (GDB). One search summary says "Singers of the Streams"; unreliable, use "Singer of Many Waters".
- Salon Members (GBd text): "Mademoiselle Crabaletta", "Surintendante Chevalmarin", "Gentilhomme Usher"; plus a singer who visits on gloomy Wednesdays (the Singer). Line: "A fixed roster of regulars frequent Furina's Salon Solitaire." Verify the mapping of names to creatures in game.
- Elemental burst: **Let the People Rejoice**. Foam stage, AoE Hydro DMG, "Universal Revelry" on party members; HP changes grant Fanfare, raising party DMG and Incoming Healing Bonus (GDB). Burst line (GBd): "Cheer! Give praise! Be awed! Raise your glasses!", with a quip that those not of age may have grape juice.
- Passives (GBd, GDB): **Endless Waltz** (A1: when overflow healing from someone other than Furina reaches the active character, she heals nearby party members over several seconds); **Unheard Confession** (A4: her Max HP boosts Salon Members' DMG or shortens the Singer's healing interval, capped); **The Sea Is My Stage** (utility: -30% cooldown on Xenochromatic Fontemer Aberrant abilities).
- Constellations (GDB and GBd agree), names are lines from an opera:
  - C1 "Love Is a Rebellious Bird That None Can Tame" (burst gives 150 Fanfare, limit +100)
  - C2 "A Woman Adapts Like Duckweed in Water" (more Fanfare during burst; overflow raises Max HP)
  - C3 "My Secret Is Hidden Within Me, No One Will Know My Name" (burst +3)
  - C4 "They Know Not Life, Who Dwelt in the Netherworld Not!" (Energy when Salon hits or Singer heals)
  - C5 "His Name I Now Know, It Is...!" (skill +3)
  - C6 "Hear Me — Let Us Raise the Chalice of Love!" (Center of Attention: attacks become Hydro with a Max HP bonus; heals in Ousia, extra damage and HP cost in Pneuma)
- Outfit "Coronated Prima Donna" (GBd): "the star of the show is meant to shine on stage".
- Design note (WP): streaked hair and two eye colours reflect Ousia and Pneuma; French and Christian costume influences, Little Mermaid and musical references.

## 3. The story (needs in-game verification; quest names from G8/GW)
- The 500 years: Furina is the human half of the true Hydro Archon Focalors. About five centuries before the game, Focalors split divinity from humanity to dodge a prophecy of a great flood, and Furina was made to pose as the Archon (WP, SK1, WPF). Furina has no divine power, was cursed to stay young while Focalors lived, and spent centuries isolated and hiding her fear (WP). Origin of the prophecy: Egeria, the first Hydro Archon, made Fontaine's oceanids human, which the Heavenly Principles deemed a sin (WP, WOT slabs 1-2).
- The prophecy (wording not confirmed): WPF paraphrase: Fontaine floods, its people revert to oceanids, the Hydro Archon "would weep on her throne". WOT (machine-translated) gives the carving as "All people will dissolve into water, and only Hydro Archon will remain silently crying on his throne." The pronoun "his" is likely translation noise. Four slabs give the true history: oceanids made human; Celestia worshipped; the Archon fell into the sea; the Primordial Sea entered Meropide (WOT).
- Focalors: Egeria's successor; deceived Celestia by using Furina's performance to carry out the prophecy safely; was one with the Oratrice for centuries (WOT, WPF). The Traveler meets her inside Furina's mind (G8, WOT).
- Archon Quest Chapter IV (Fontaine): Act IV "Cataclysm's Quickening"; Act V "Masquerade of the Guilty" (GW, PGP). Act V subquests (G8): "Deluge of Wrathful Waters", "Meeting is Also Parting", "Hunters, Prophets", "Apocalypse", "The Opera of Noirceur and Blancheur", "Finale". Released with 4.2, 2023-11-08 (G8). Next quest: "Bedtime Story" (WOT).
- "Apocalypse": Furina dodges questions about the prophecy; Neuvillette decides the only way to the truth is a trial at the opera house; the Traveler refutes her with evidence; the last prophecy slate is viewed (PGP, G8). Example clue: "The meeting with The Knave" (PGP).
- The Oratrice Mecanique d'Analyse Cardinale (the judging machine): gives Neuvillette the verdict, a death sentence for "the Hydro Archon" (PGP, WOT). When Furina is declared guilty, Focalors appears to Neuvillette and admits she forced Furina to masquerade as the Archon for five centuries (WP, PGP). The Oratrice sentenced the Hydro Archon, not Furina (WOT).
- "The Opera of Noirceur and Blancheur": the Traveler enters Furina's consciousness, turns off spotlights, watches her performance, speaks with Focalors, enters the Primordial Sea with Neuvillette and defeats the All-Devouring Narwhal; Skirk appears (G8, PGP).
- Resolution: Focalors gives her full authority to Neuvillette and dies (WP, WPF). Neuvillette, power restored, seals the Primordial Sea and absolves the people, who are spared reverting to oceanids (WOT; a fan review via search, unverified). Furina's curse breaks and she lives as an ordinary mortal (WP, WPF). "Finale" ends with Charlotte, the Fortress of Meropide, Romaritime Harbor, Lyney and others, and a last talk with Neuvillette (G8).
- After: Furina withdraws from public affairs and Neuvillette governs (WPF); she leaves the opera house for an apartment (fan review via search summary; unverified). Voice lines (GDB): "her story has ended", the next act is "OUR story"; Vision line says she has "finally started to act as myself"; she says she has "hung up" her acting career (Lyney line); mentions "my crimes of deceiving the people" (Neuvillette line).
- Her story quest: "Animula Choragi Chapter: Act I 'The Little Oceanid'"; needs AR 40, Act V done, one Story Key (GW, PGP). Act I quests: "To Yesterday", "Whither Should The Water Flow?", "This Life, Just Like A Light Trickle Of Song". Plot (GW): ask the adventurer guild about Furina; find Pauleau in Poisson and accompany Furina as she unwinds; recover the former troupe director's diary from Palais Mermonia, find Neuvillette, help with rehearsal, see Dulphy and Lauwick. So she is rehearsing with a theatre troupe. Later acts not found.
- Reception (WP): critics compare her arc to "Omelas".

## 4. Relationships (GDB voice lines, Friendship 4+ unless noted)
- Neuvillette. His line: Furina's "performance" has ended; praises her duty and resolve despite self-doubt and "madness"; would personally applaud if she returned to the stage. Another: a Fontaine law forbids naming pets after Furina. Hers: he once tapped his cane to quiet an unruly opera crowd; she is grateful for his years of work and says they "no longer work together".
- Clorinde. Her line (unlocks after "Masquerade of the Guilty"): used to stand behind Furina at tea parties sword ready; now sits at the table sharing tea and treats; jokes about maintaining her physique. Furina: quiet but protective, trusted, to be invited to a tea party. So yes, a former bodyguard, in Clorinde's own words.
- Charlotte. Furina has a captivating star presence; a journalist assigned to her quit because she made him retake photos (one pose took 127 tries). Furina: Charlotte keeps requesting an interview; Furina is not ready to be understood yet. No acting reviews found.
- Navia. Saw Furina in a charity opera when young; thought she looked more at home on stage than as an Archon giving speeches. Furina praises Navia's painted macarons, is a little jealous; says Navia is becoming like her father.
- Lyney / Lynette / Freminet. Lyney: very unpredictable, treats talking as performance, Fontaine admires the drama. Lynette: behaviour always an act, hard to read. Freminet: saw her few times; her silences intrigue him; eyes like the deep ocean, hides a secret and fears letting anyone close. Furina: attended Lyney and Lynette's shows; they are less popular than her.
- Wriothesley. Pleaded guilty immediately and she left his trial uninterested; he keeps sending her high-quality tea. Furina: his tea was "exquisite".
- Chevreuse. Furina once ordered the Special Patrol to give a ceremonial musketeer performance; dropped because she never found a song.
- Arlecchino. Asked Lyney to deliver cakes for Furina's theatre troupe at teatime, made by the children as the troupe's "audience". Furina has forgotten "The Knave"; remembering gives nightmares. Cakes and a troupe are in-game text.
- Sigewinne: no Furina line (GDB). Escoffier: GDB record empty; a search summary says Escoffier's voice line has her making a lily sugar-glazed opera cake for Furina, who liked it (unverified).
- Focalors: divine half; Egeria: first Hydro Archon (WP).

## 5. Places
- Court of Fontaine: capital and largest city, with the opera house where trials are held (WPF).
- Opera Epiclese: east side of the Court of Fontaine, with the Fountain of Lucine in front of it (SK2). Written "Epicles" in WOT. The trial and the Act V opera sequence happen here.
- Palais Mermonia: Fontaine's administrative authority; cannot interfere in Meropide without due process (search summary). Furina's quest has her recover the old troupe director's diary from it (GW).
- Fortress of Meropide: underwater prison run by Wriothesley (search summary); Finale returns there (G8). In the prophecy history the Primordial Sea enters it (WOT).
- Fountain of Lucine: a Hydro offering point; offer Hydro Sigils for Mora, Intertwined Fate and the like (SK2). A gameplay fountain; no lore function found.
- Cafes: Cafe Lutece, centre of the Court of Fontaine, sells the "La Lettre a Focalors" recipe (GL). No source ties Furina to ordering there. "Cafe Lombardi" found nowhere. She hosts "tea parties" (GDB 89001).
- Cake and desserts: namecard "Furina: Banquet", line "Where's my cake? What? I just had cake for breakfast?", "desserts are like operas" (all GDB); special dish built on a cake. Poisson (village), Hotel Debord and The Steambird appear in Act V steps (G8).

## 6. Things to verify for our story
(a) Cafe she orders cake from, and its name: UNCERTAIN, leaning no. Cafe Lutece exists and sells the recipe for her cake-based dish (GL), but nothing says she orders there. "Cafe Lombardi" has no support. Safe wording: "a cafe in the Court of Fontaine", or Lutece as our own invention.
(b) Neuvillette writes to her after the trial: UNCERTAIN, no source. Closest: she says they "no longer work together"; he would applaud her return (GDB). Treat as invention.
(c) Throne of blue glass / her seat at the Opera Epiclese: UNCERTAIN, no source. The prophecy has the Archon on a "throne" (WPF, WOT) and she lived in the opera house (unverified). Colour and material not found.
(d) Prophecy wording (people dissolve, Archon weeps alone on her throne): PARTLY YES. The idea is in WPF, WOT and SK1; "dissolve" and "crying on his throne" are in WOT (machine-translated). Exact English wording needs in-game verification.
(e) Charlotte reviewing Furina's acting in The Steambird: NO / unverified. The Steambird is a place in Act V steps (G8); Charlotte's only Furina line is about photoshoots and interviews (GDB). No acting reviews found.
(f) Exact names: YES for all three. "Soloist's Solicitation" is her normal attack; "Endless Waltz" is her A1 passive; "Unheard Confession" is her A4 passive (GDB, GBd).
