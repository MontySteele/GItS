## 2. What the round found

**Arcane Scroll gave no card.** Neow's "obtain a random Rare Card" arrived
as a relic reading the same, and the deck stayed the 11 starters, confirmed
by fight 1's pile counts. The grant finds nothing in the arm's pool, or is
filtered (`EB-459`).

**Attack numbers on dual intents never landed, four for four.** Living Fog
"Attack 8 + CardDebuff" and "Attack 5 + Summon", the Merc's "6x2 + Debuff",
the Eel's "3x3 + Empower": zero damage each time, and the Klee seat of the
same round saw the Spinner do the same. The seat blocked around numbers
that were never coming (`EB-461`).

**A [Hydro] card whose own hit is not Hydro.** Kurage's Oath's title tag
says Hydro; a rider four lines down says its now-line applies no aura and
only the carry-out is a Hydro hit. The seat built a turn on the tag; Deep
Current reacted off the same Electro aura where the Oath's now-line did
not (`EB-462`).

**Electro-Charged, again.** Prints as Poison and its stack climbed 3, 10,
13 on the Eel while the glossary says it decays (`EB-357` widened). A
fight-4 sequence that had reacted in fight 2 produced a flat 7 with no line
saying whether the aura had lapsed; an expired aura prints nothing, which
reads the same as a reaction that failed (`EB-410`, cited).

**The Companion tip describes Furina's stage** on a run with no stage
(`EB-460`). The Light Door never named the two cards it upgraded
(`EB-448`'s open half). The map's deck panel went stale after floor 7 and
said so, which is `EB-447`'s disclosed limit.

**What read true.** The Bake-Kurage block numbered its two Plans and the
buff bar said "carries out 2 Plans, in order", answering the seat's turn-one
question; the carry-out line named the Casket's 2 inside the beat
(`EB-453` read as fixed); Ravenous plus Poison's tick timing made fight 2's
"do not kill it" the best turn of the round; Slack Water plus the Casket
beat a raw Strike twice. The seat called Tamakushi Casket "quietly the
best-designed thing here."

**The deck is two cards deep, a fifth round.** Plans on 5 of 16 turns;
Nereid's Ascension "reads like the kit's payoff and behaves like a tax"
with two Plan cards to double; three kill turns had no alternative. R254
ruled starter density STANDS; Tide Chart's eighth non-draw leaves the
re-ask trigger unmet. The Ascension reading is new and goes to the pool
pass with it: a doubler needs Plan density the starter does not have.
