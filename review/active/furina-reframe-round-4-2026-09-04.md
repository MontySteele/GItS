# Furina round four: the riders at the arm's scale, read by two seats who never reached them

Written 2026-09-04, the same night as R253. Two blind Opus seats played the
reframe arm on `0.2.2446+proto`, the first build with the four rider copies
at 6, 6, 8 and 10 Fanfare (round 2 pick 1, taken at its default and passed
by the charter audit, `review/records/card-audit-2026-09-04.md`) and with
Encore absorption printed (pick 2). Neither run reached the act-1 boss:
run 1 spent its 70 actions by floor 4, run 2 spent 113 of 120 at the
floor-8 elite and was losing it. Records:
`review/qa/furina-reframe-round-4-2026-09-04/opus-act1.md` and
`opus-run2-act1.md`. Prototype stage, Guardrail-7.

## 1. The runs in one paragraph

Run 1 (seed `2JDS8K04D5UU`, Ascension 2): three fights won, stopped
mid-fight against three Inklets at 31 of 78 HP, 44 gold, no potions. Run 2
(seed `64QWZUYCY2UL`, Ascension 2): five fights won, then four Phantasmal
Gardeners with Skittish 6 each took 30 HP for 27 dealt over three rounds;
the seat stopped at 14 of 78 with 25 damage declared against it. Fanfare
reached 6 in run 1 and crossed 10 once in run 2; no rider copy was drawn
in either run, and run 2 held exactly one card that read Fanfare
(Crescendo). Both runs opened at Ascension 2, which the embark tool sets;
the seat brief now prints the ascension on every record.

## 2. What the round found

**Four decisions the seats called real, and the queue is the fourth.** Run
2: Aria of Recompense against a Block card, because Encore is a buffer,
the Spotlight's price and the members' fuel at once; which member stands
at the front, since "a Companion card you play performs the Usher" and
"performs Crabaletta" convert the same hand between Block and damage and
the queue cannot be reordered, only Evoked through; 16 damage or 25 at the
cost of standing at 17 HP; and the order Aria, Spotlight, Companions,
because the Spotlight is free in energy and costs 2 of the Encore the
members spend. The elite taught "stack hits on one body, Skittish taxes
the first hit per target", which the seat called good design in a fight
it lost. Turn one of fight one presented a decision in both runs.

**The riders were never reached, and Fanfare was decorative below them.**
Run 1: "Fanfare did nothing. It climbed to 6, decayed 20% a turn, and its
own text says cards read it and none spends it." Run 2 crossed 10 once
and "never noticed the difference." The four copies are the readers this
was built to feed and neither seat drew one; the round cannot say whether
6 is the right bar, only that a deck with no reader watches the number.
§5 is the pick.

**Encore is spent before it is defined.** The word is on the Neow screen
and the opening hand; its gloss appears once the player holds some
(`EB-407`, both runs). Run 2 also named the three jobs "gain 5 Encore"
never states on the card.

**The Ethereal Spotlight paid nothing on its second copy.** Run 1: refused
at 0 Encore on round 1 with "CANNOT BE PLAYED: you have no Encore, and
this costs 2", accepted at the same 0 Encore on round 2, Exhausted, and
nothing moved (`EB-406`). The relic hands a copy every turn and every copy
after the first is dead in hand while the Spotlight holds.

**Two members did not take the buff they were owed.** Companions
generated in combat by An Invitation performed at their base numbers under
an active Spotlight while deck and reward Companions took it (`EB-413`,
measured twice against controls). Endless Waltz's own +3 landed after the
two members it deploys had performed (`EB-412`).

**The page lied by title.** The Deploy gloss was raised on a screen because
"Freminet — Pers, Deploy!" carries the word in its name; the card's body is
"Deal 6 damage" and the seat read it as a broken card six plays running
(`EB-404`). Salon Début does not say its member performs at once, which the
gloss says and the log shows (`EB-414`). A member's performance names no
target, and Crabaletta left a Hydro aura on a body the seat had not picked
(`EB-405`). The Burst gloss still prints under the arm, off the thirteen
faces `EB-369` already names, and quotes a meter the arm retired; one read
saw it at 5, which §4 notes as a question for that row's build.

**The elite is a hard counter, and the pool offered one answer.** Run 2's
Gardeners tax every first hit and the kit is many small single-target
hits; the only ALL card offered in eight floors was one Undercurrent. Not a
row: it is the pool-pass question for this arm, beside the reaction-table
question the Kokomi and Klee seats raised.
