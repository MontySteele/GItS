## 2. What the round found

**Enemy Block is never printed.** A card printing "Deal 6" on a 5-HP
Nibbit left it at 4 and nothing on the page showed the Block that ate the
hit, "the only unpredictable outcome of the run" (`EB-474`).

**Three undefined terms gated decisions.** "If you moved the Spotlight this
turn" gates two cards and is defined nowhere; "Guest Stars" and "Burst
Energy" print on cards with no glossary entry; Take Your Bow was offered
at a reward with no keyword, and the seat declined a 0-cost card it could
not read (`EB-475`; Burst is `EB-449`).

**A performance takes Vulnerable and does not Shatter.** Crabaletta's 6
became exactly 9 under Vulnerable, and Frozen survived two performances,
so the seat could not say whether a performance is an Attack. The mod's
rule (`EB-343`: non-Attack damage takes Vulnerable, fires no Attack
trigger) is consistent; the tip must say it (`EB-476`).

**An empty stage halves every Companion card, silently.** Two turns lost
including the elite's turn 1; round 8 waited two fights to learn the same.
The face prints its performance clause and the play performs nobody; a
live "performs nobody" line, Ethereal Spotlight's refusal form, is the fix
(`EB-477`).

**What read true.** Turn order is the kit's real decision and it is
legible: Slow ticked 10 to 50 on the enemy block as cards were played, the
Reaction preview named the consequence before commitment, and intents
moved live when Weak landed. Encore's shield-or-fuel tension was called
genuine; freezing an attacker instead of buying Block "reframes a whole
card type". The elite fell on turn 3 to a pre-computed line.

**Pool readings.** One dead turn with a member idle and no legal way to
make her act; Casting Call rejected on three turns as unplayable with one
Deploy in the deck; Stage Presence played once in six fights. Carried to
the pool pass: the Companion-card density the stage needs, and a second
Deploy shape.

**Turn one, the first real one.** Salon Début plus Regal Bearing plus Stage
Presence against Stage Presence ×2 plus Soloist's, both at zero damage
taken, separated by whether to set up the engine for 2 more damage; Weak
moved the printed 12 to 9 on screen. "A genuine, readable, non-obvious
turn-one choice." The caveat is round 5's pick from the other side: the
deploy's performance was dry at three-quarters because Encore starts at 0,
"so the kit's opening move is, by construction, its own weakest version."
