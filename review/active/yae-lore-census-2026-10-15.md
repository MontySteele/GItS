## 1. Who she is
- Guuji (chief priestess) of the Grand Narukami Shrine, Inazuma; co-founder and editor-in-chief of Yae Publishing House, a light-novel publisher (WP, FX, B). Descendant of the kitsune line (WP, FX); of the Hakushin clan (TG).
- 5-star Electro catalyst (FX, WP). Playable v2.5, 2022-02-16 (GG, WP). Birthday June 27 (B, FX). Constellation: Divina Vulpes (B).
- VAs: EN Ratana (Ratana Therakulsathit), JP Ayane Sakura, CN Du Mingya, KR Moon Yoo-jeong (WP, search). B gives a different Korean name; WP and search agree on Moon Yoo-jeong.
- Aliases: Guuji Yae, Miss Fox, Lady Guuji, Editor-in-Chief, "Exquisite Tofu" (B). Fond of fried tofu and light novels (WP). Personality: playful, witty, cunning; others find her intimidating (FX, TG).
- Dish: Fukuuchi Udon (GamerGuides via search; one source). Namecard: Kitsune Dreaming (SK via search; one source). Signature weapon: Kagura's Verity, 5-star catalyst (WP, search).

## 3. Story
- Kitsune Saiguu: Yae's predecessor as Guuji, worshipped as a kitsune goddess, of the Hakushin clan (TG). Yae was young when Saiguu died and rarely speaks of her (TG). A fan wiki says Saiguu raised Yae at the shrine and lured her into the Guuji role with fried tofu (MG; weak). FX says Saiguu organized a youkai gathering where young Miko pestered the storyteller Urakusai. Saiguu was corrupted in the Cataclysm, chose to become one with the earth, and her memories later gave rise to Kazari (TG).
- Ei and Makoto: Ei handed Yae her Gnosis when she cut ties with Celestia (TG, B). Yae is Ei's familiar (B); close to Makoto as a young fox (B, TG). B says Miko taught Ei how to house her consciousness in an object. WP: she disagrees with Ei's pursuit of "eternity" and trains the Traveler to oppose her. Ei says Yae was once "lured by fried tofu" (B).
- Archon Quest: she sends the Traveler into Ei's mind to change her will; recovers the Gnosis from the Fatui (B). One of two protagonists of Chapter II (fan wiki via search).
- Sacred Sakura: she managed the Sacred Sakura Cleansing Ritual and guided the Traveler through it (B). The tanuki handed over the Thunder Sakura in the old war (TG).
- Fire-Soothing Festival: FX lists Kujou Takayuki, the Tenryou Commissioner, as "rebuffed over the Fire-Soothing Festival funds." No source says Yae borrowed or spent a publishing budget. See 6(d).
- Light novel house: Yae Publishing House runs an annual novel contest (traveler.gg quest guide via search); story quest "Friendship in Writing" has Yae aiming to top the charts, displacing "A Firsthand Guide to Summoning Spirits" (GG via search). Web event "Tales of the Youkai" (Dec 2022) had players write books for the house (Siliconera via search). FX: she tells an editor a book should not be "not free enough." B: she organized the Irodori Festival, a light-novel cultural event. No named in-lore authors or novels beyond these were found.
- Tanuki Ioroi: a bake-danuki, protector of Chinju Forest, friend (TG) or rival (FF) of Saiguu; Saiguu persuaded the Shogun to spare him (TG); she hid him in a hide-and-seek during the Cataclysm (TG); he was later sealed by Kamuna Harunosuke (FF). He appears in the world quest "Tanuki-Bayashi in the Forest" (FF). His tie is to Saiguu, not Yae. Kitsune and tanuki fought about 1,600 years before peace (TG, Toki Alley Tales).
- Tengu: Saiguu raced the Chieftain of the Yougou Tengu and won, suspecting he went easy (TG). Kamuna Harunosuke is one of the Yougou Three (FF).
- Omikuji at the shrine: not found in any source read.
- Other quests (B): "The Immovable God and the Eternal Euthymia" (Tsuchikado), "The Splendorous Sky That Day" (Irodori Festival), Test of Courage (Hanabusa Chizuru), Mitsukawa Flower Festival (Ei and Chiyo).

## 4. Relationships
- Ei / Makoto: see 3. Sangonomiya Kokomi: friend and fellow shrine priestess who have not met in person; Kokomi: "I believe she is an excellent shrine maiden." (B).
- Kujou Sara: Yae reportedly teases her loyalty to the Shogunate and warned her of the Kanjou Commission's Fatui ties (MG; weak). Kujou Takayuki: see Fire-Soothing Festival (FX).
- Itto: recalls "I won that fox ramen eating contest!" (B). Ayaka calls her festival work "elegant and fruitful" (B).
- Kamisato Ayato: Yae calls him a "Yashiro rascal" in leaked lines (progameguides via search). Sayu, Thoma, Gorou, Yoimiya appear in appraisals (B). Kano Nana: shrine maiden and novel fan whom Yae teases (FX). Urakusai: youkai storyteller she argued with (FX).
- Mizuki: her voicelines call Yae a childhood companion; they transformed into humans to roam town (Sportskeeda via search).
- Traveler: trained and aided (WP, B).

## 5. Places
- Narukami Island; Grand Narukami Shrine (modeled on Fushimi Inari, WP); Yae Publishing House (exact location not read; Hanamizaka and Inazuma City quests per GG); Inazuma City; Chinju Forest (Ioroi, Sacred Sakura).

## 6. Verify for our cards
- (a) Ioroi is a tanuki who pranks or rivals Yae: NO as stated. Ioroi is a tanuki (FF, TG) tied to Saiguu, as friend or rival. No source ties him to Yae. Tanuki in general tease humans for fun (GameWith via search). A prank or rivalry with Yae is [SPEC].
- (b) Saiguu taught/raised Yae: LIKELY YES. Predecessor Guuji, close to young Yae (TG); "raised" is MG only (weak). Say "mentor" or "predecessor."
- (c) "Eternal Promise" for Ei and Yae: NO such phrase or quest found. Nearby real titles: "Omnipresence Over Mortals", "The Immovable God and the Eternal Euthymia" (B). The Ei/Yae bond itself is canon.
- (d) Yae "borrowed" Fire-Soothing Festival budget: UNCERTAIN, leaning no. Only FX: Takayuki rebuffed over the festival funds (direction unclear). A budget joke is [SPEC].
- (e) Spellings: "Daisesshou" YES (C6 name); "Kagura's Verity" YES; "Narukami" YES ("Narakumi" is a typo in TG).
- (f) "Reborn as Guuji Yae" contest: UNCERTAIN. One search summary called it an in-game Yae Publishing House writing event, but no source with the exact title was found and a repeat search failed to confirm it. Real events: the annual novel contest, Tales of the Youkai.
- (g) Quotes: "Alright, amuse me" NOT FOUND; "Not the sharpest tengu in the forest" NOT FOUND; "So much for eternity" NOT FOUND. The voice-over page was unreadable, so treat all three as unverified and do not attribute them to Yae as canon.
