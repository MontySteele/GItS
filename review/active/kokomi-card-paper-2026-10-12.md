# Kokomi card paper: Plans and Ethereal (2026-10-12)

**Why.** The direction is ruled (`review/active/kokomi-design-pass-2026-10-11.md`, all five picks (a), 2026-10-12). This paper says what each of her 78 cards becomes. [USER], on Companions: "her Personal Companions can be given specific Plan lines with the permitted better effect rates, with the flexibility to play normal Companions the next turn at the regular rate if you want to."

**Sources:** the census (session scratchpad, `kokomi-archetype-census.md`, takes over 2026-10-08 to 2026-10-11); the Inazuma Companion rows on `docs/prototype-surface.yaml`.

## 1. The rules this builds

1. **The Casket goes.** The starter relic keeps its name and art: "At the end of your turn, if a Plan is waiting, gain 3 Block." Open the Casket goes.
2. **No card with a Plan line costs 0.** Dusk is retired.
3. **Ethereal** is the base game's keyword. Writing an Ethereal card as a Plan keeps it.
4. **Jellyfish cards.** Two generated cards, both Ethereal, 1 Energy, Exhausting when played or carried out:
   - **Sting:** "Deal 7 Hydro damage. Or plan: Deal 5 Hydro damage to ALL enemies." [10 / 7]
   - **Ward:** "Gain 7 Block. Or plan: Apply 1 Weak to ALL enemies." [10 / 2]
5. **Companions as Plans.** Any Companion card can be written as a Plan. It is carried out at its printed rate, and the Bake-Kurage applies Hydro to its target first. A Companion that hits an enemy reacts with that Hydro; one that only buffs or blocks carries the Hydro alone. Generated Companions Exhaust when played or carried out.
6. **Personal Companions:** Gorou's four cards are offered to Kokomi only (pick 3) and carry their own Plan lines (sec.3).

The Common Inazuma Companions that hit an enemy are Sayu (Fuuin Dash), Heizou, Shinobu (Thundergrust) and Gorou (Inuzaka): Swirl, Electro-Charged and Crystallize. Vaporize comes from the Uncommons Thoma (Crimson Ooyoroi) and Yoimiya; Freeze only from Ayaka (Rare).

## 4. The counts the build is checked against

- **Counters:** cards read Plans waiting, a trigger, or (Sango Isshin, ruled 2026-10-11) Plans carried out this combat. Nothing else.
- **Block cards:** 16 gain Block directly, plus the two Ward generators (the census counted 15).
- **Draw:** 11 cards (17 today by the same count). **Energy:** War Council's Plan only (was 7).
- **Generators:** Commander 5 (Orders from the Shrine, Watatsumi Muster, Commander's Eye, Sangonomiya's Orders, Call the Generals); Kurage 7 (Jellyfish Drift, Bubble Ward, Watatsumi Drill, Kurage's Wake, Shoal Call, Kurage Bloom, Kurage Barrage); bridges 2 (Watatsumi Resistance, Endless Shoal). Each makes at most 2 cards.
- **Her own Ethereal cards:** 5 (Tidal Surge, Riptide Guard, Spearhead, Steady Tide, Tamakushi's Wrath), plus every generated card.
