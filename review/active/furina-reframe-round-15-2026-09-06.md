# Furina round fifteen: R260 read, the stage is never empty, and one hit rule came apart

Written 2026-09-06, under the Prototype loop. Two blind Opus seats on
`0.2.2800+proto` (round-21 branch with fixers O and P in: R260's stage,
the free arrival performance, the Evoke line, the Spotlight window line,
the Fanfare floor clause). Records:
`review/qa/furina-reframe-round-15-2026-09-06/opus-arm-act1.md` (lane 1,
assembled) and `opus-natural-act1.md` (lane 2, natural), both at
Ascension 2. Prototype stage, Guardrail-7. [USER]'s act-1 run on R260 is
still due; nothing here stands in for it.

## 1. The hypothesis

"With Crabaletta on stage from combat start, a turn-one Companion card
performs on every lane; the Spotlight window is named as a decision
before its first refusal once the tip carries it; Second Course finds an
Encore pool on more than one turn in five when a generator is in the
deck; a natural deck reaches the Evoke."

**Assembled deck** (lane 1, seed `YUKSD243P7VG`): Second Course, Curtain
Cue, Curtain Rises, Guest List added; Aria of Recompense upgraded at the
first rest site as asked. 119 of 120, five fights won including the Phrog
Parasite elite, the sixth stopped at its second turn, 43 of 78; the first
three fights cost no HP. **Natural run** (lane 2, seed `R5T1UT84786A`):
120 of 120, six of six including the four-Gardener elite, 51 of 78,
eleven HP lost, nine of it on the two turns Encore was zeroed.

## 2. What the round found

**R260, first reading: supported on every fight of both lanes.** Twelve
fights opened with Crabaletta on stage and her arrival performance
already spent ("6 damage and Fanfare 2 before my first screen"); every
Companion card played on turn one performed her (Charlotte, Chevreuse,
Lynette, Noelle, Vanguard's Valor on lane 2; fights 5 and 6 on lane 1).
Lane 2: "no turn had an empty stage; the member never left." The fifth
reading of the empty stage is closed by the rule. One fight in twelve did
not print the arrival line (`EB-585`).

**Second Course, with a generator beside it: playable, and best onto a
full stage.** Lane 1 with Curtain Cue and Aria+: refused at 0 Encore "with
a clean message", at 1 or 2 Encore it deployed plus two performances, and
onto a full stage it Evoked for 14. The round-14 reading holds in the
other direction: the price was never the constraint and the generator
was. Nothing moves.

**The Evoke: reached on the assembled lane, never on the natural one.**
Lane 1 saw it (Second Course and Curtain Rises onto a full stage) and read
two faults: it delivered its full 14 at 0 Encore beside three performances
printed dry at three-quarters, on a tip whose "the card's Encore price
pays for it" fails on Curtain Rises, which has no Encore price (`EB-587`,
D default: the Evoke pays like a performance); and the log prints it after
the joining member's performance while the card says the front member
Evokes first (`EB-582`). Lane 2, six fights, "I never saw an Evoke":
nothing in the natural draft deployed onto a full stage, and a member
deployed in one fight does not persist to the next, so with one member
granted at the start the stage still wants two Deploys in one fight. The
second reading of the same fact (round 14 was the first): the Evoke is
reachable by a deck built for it and not by a natural act-1 draft. Held;
whether a third of the Salon's rules should be reachable in act 1 without
building for it is a design question, and it is written down here for
the next Furina pass rather than answered by a row.

**The Spotlight, both lanes.** Lane 1 refused the new line's advice on
turn one of fight one: the starter holds two Companion cards, the
Spotlight costs the whole opening Encore, "the correct first move is to
refuse the screen's own advice" (`EB-586`, the line goes neutral). Lane 2
paid it in most fights and zeroed Encore twice, which is where nine of
its eleven HP went. The decision is real on both lanes and the line
should not lean.

**One hit rule came apart, and one did not.** Lane 2: Weak cut a member
performance 6 to 4 twice, against the Salon text's "a performance is not
an Attack and not a hit: Vulnerable moves it", which names no Weak
(`EB-588`, D default: the text stands; built by fixer R, a performance
now takes the unpowered door on the dealer's side too, so her Strength
goes with the Weak). The same lane's "Skittish 6 never fired" was read by
fixer R as not a defect: the witness, a 12-HP body dying to a 13-damage
card hit, is a killing hit, and Skittish grants Block to nobody dead; the
card-attack door did not move between the two builds, and it is pinned
three ways (`EB-592`). The seat's reading was honest and wrong, which is
the instrument working.

**The face folds four modifiers and not the fifth.** Lane 2: Chevreuse
printed 7, 10, 10 and delivered 11, 15, 22; the Spotlight, Weak and
Passion Overload fold into the face and the previewed Vaporize never does,
"right about four modifiers and silent about the biggest one" (`EB-589`,
`EB-559`'s shape). Lane 1 read the fold the other way, in its favour:
"Freminet base 6, 9 Spotlighted, 11 with Sharp, 7 under Shrink", every one
reprinted on the face. Lane 2's (c) 9, the seat's own wrong read that a
fetched Companion dodged the Spotlight because "a lit number and an unlit
number are indistinguishable on sight", is round 14's Dexterity reading
again (`EB-566`, not a defect): the folded face is legible and unlabelled.

**Encore as one pool.** Lane 1: "the kit's best idea, and it makes every
Block card in the deck dead"; Regal Bearing and Stage Presence filler in
five of six fights. Lane 2 the mirror: the two turns it zeroed Encore for
the Spotlight cost nine HP. The starter's Block suite read the same in
round 14; the starter is as ruled and it is not moved.

**Legibility.** Courtroom Drama's Vulnerable amplified the hit that
applied it, "50 to 19 from one 1-cost card" (`EB-591`, the clause order
read against the engine). "Move the Spotlight", a verb on two faces that
no card the seat saw performs (`EB-590`). Guest Cast's duration against
the Spotlight's (`EB-584`). Hexerei printed bare on Sucrose's and Razor's
faces with no definition (`EB-583`). Electro-Charged applied as a debuff
named Poison (`EB-410`, cited). Both copies of Chevreuse printing a
one-shot buff only one could claim; "Upgrade 2 random cards" naming
neither; an enchant screen omitting an enchanted card: recorded, the last
two the game's. Praised: enemy Strength exact on every intent; faces
reprinting resolved damage "every time"; the refusal texts.
