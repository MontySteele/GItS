30%, so planning won by 5 damage a card"; under Vulnerable "the played

printed "Plan: Deal 10" under the target's Vulnerable, the seat paid for

mechanic sold on committing a turn early, the committed number moving is
the sharpest contradiction in the kit." Both lanes then read the two

the target's Vulnerable moves the Plan line only, "undocumented". Rule 3

line folds hers and nothing of the target's, and the own line folds the
target's now (`EB-599`, D default; `EB-598` is the own line's missing
