# Klee and Varka: story chains, 2026-10-14

*Rewards repriced 2026-10-19 by the story rewards pass (`story-rewards-pass-2026-10-19.md`, ruled all (a)); the rows below carry the new effects.*

**Why.** [USER], 2026-10-14: "as our characters hit release-readiness
(currently meaning Klee and Varka), we should try to give multiple unique events
... that expand on their lore and characterization in a direct way." And:
"Rather than one event per act, we're probably looking at multiple possible
events per act, with no more than one firing per act - which one you do get in
act 1 (if any) determines which you get in act 2, etc, with a branch for if you
managed to skip an act. The events should be balanced around existing ones - so
some are overall bad, some are overall good, and anything strong should be
counterbalanced with a price." On framing: "the characters are all currently
in the Spire, so any event that clearly happens outside of it needs to be
framed as a recollection or memory of some kind."

**Second draft, ruled 2026-10-14.** On the first draft [USER] said: "all of
these read to me as all having 3 good options... you need to also sometimes
have a choice of two bad things." A GPT review then found continuity gaps
(Dodoco, Alice's finale, the Rearguard, Second Satchel) and prices that did
not come from their scenes. Claude agreed with all of it except the framing of
The First Exam, and proposed this rewrite. [USER]: "Makes sense to me - agreed
on your story rewrite ideas!" That includes keeping Alice offscreen (first
draft pick 2, now option b).

## 1. The shape

- **Act 1** offers one of three events, chosen at random. Each starts a
  thread.
- **Act 2** follows the thread. If no act-1 event fired, act 2 offers the
  **home thread** (Jean's letter for Klee, the Wolf for Varka).
- **Act 3** closes the thread. If act 2 was skipped, act 3 offers the home
  finale.
- **The run saves the event and the option chosen** in each act. Later events
  open with a line that matches what you did.
- **Each character keeps the existing three events** and gains five, so 8
  each. Every option gets a short result line in the character's voice.
- **Every price comes from the scene.** A chest that is "a bit lively" costs
  HP; leaving Mora for the next adventurer costs Gold; carrying paperwork home
  adds Overdue Reports.

Where the ratio and the story pulled apart, the story won: a reunion with
Dodoco should not be a choice of two bad things. The ratio is a check, not
the balance. A story curse (Unplayable, gone after a few combats) is a light
price; Doubt and Max HP are heavy ones and are used sparingly.

option priced" to "a price, with a free way out".

## 4. The build

- **New effects:** random relic, random Rare relic, potions, transform, "remove
  every X", and a removal of Doubt on reunion. The existing effect types cover
  the rest.
- **Routing:** the run saves the event and the option chosen in each act; act
  2 and act 3 read both to pick the event and its opening line.
- **Empty and unaffordable cases** follow the base game, which locks an option
  it cannot pay (Luminous Choir):
  - A Gold price above your Gold: locked.
  - "Remove every Curse" with no Curse: locked.
  - Remove or Transform with no eligible card: locked.
  - "Upgrade N random cards that place a Bomb" with fewer than N: upgrades
    those there are; locked with none.
  - "Remove every Overdue Reports" or Doubt with none: that part does nothing;
    the rest of the option still pays.
- **Co-op**: ruled again 2026-10-16, see "Co-op (ruled 2026-10-16)" below.
- **Cards named here that already exist:** Dodoco Tag, Alice's Recipe, Alice's
  Introduction Magic, Fish Blasting, Four Winds' Ascension, and Jean:
  Dandelion Breeze. **Doubt** is the base game's curse and stays until removed.

## Co-op (ruled 2026-10-16)

Until now, in co-op every player in an event room got a copy of the same
event: only its owner had options, the others saw the page and Proceed, and a
second story character's event waited for the act's next event room.

[USER]: "Is it possible to instead give them separate events, appropriate to
their character? And for characters who have no events, they get a
placeholder like "heal 10 or gain 50 gold"".

- **One story room per act for the whole table.** The act's story room (since
  2026-10-19 its 1st, 2nd or 3rd event room, each with chance 1/3, from one
  roll on the run's seed: `review/active/story-rewards-pass-2026-10-19.md`,
  item 2), where any player's story would claim it, is a story room for every
  player at once.
- **Each player gets their own event** in it: their character's event for the
  act, routed from their own saved story by the same rules as solo, with
  their own options, and the effects apply to them only. Each player's chain
  records only their own choice.
- **A placeholder for everyone else:** a player whose character has no story,
  whose story has no event this act, or whose story already fired this act
  gets **A Quiet Landing**: "The stairs are quiet here, and nothing on this
  landing is waiting for you." Options: Rest. (Heal 10 HP.) or Search the
  landing. (Gain 50 Gold.)
- **Solo is unchanged.**
- **How it is built** (`klee-mod/KleeCode/Story/StoryEvents.cs`,
  `StoryCoopRoomEvent`): the game gives every player a copy of one room
  event, so the room is one model whose copy shows each player their own
  event or the placeholder. Both clients route every copy from shared state
  (the seed, the act, each player's run history), so they agree. Unseen in
  game.

## Lore check (2026-10-19)

A canon check of Varka's chain (sources: Baidu Baike, Hoyodex and summaries of
the Fandom profile; the in-game text was not read verbatim) found three lines
wrong and fixed them in `Story/VarkaStory.cs`:

  spirit. The scene is now Andrius.
- **"Five years"** stays: [USER], 2026-10-19: "the 'five years' is canonically how long
  the expedition lasted".
- **Diluc:** canon has him disapprove of Varka's methods, so the warm "your
  usual table" welcome is now drier.
- **Valentine:** a libation implies he died, which no source says, so it is
  now a toast.

The First Exam and A Song on the Wind fit his timeline as written: both come
before he was a knight. The BACKLOG fallback ("move both to his Knight of
Boreas years") is dropped.
