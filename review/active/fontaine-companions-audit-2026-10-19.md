## What is there

**16 cards, 8 characters** (8 Common / 4 Uncommon / 4 Rare). Mondstadt has 42
cards over 23 characters, Inazuma 38 over 17 (34 shared plus Gorou's 4 in
Kokomi's kit). Fontaine is under half of either.

- The four 4-stars (Chevreuse, Lynette, Charlotte, Freminet) have **three
  cards each** and hold all 8 Commons: one over the two-a-character target.
- The four 5-stars (Navia, Clorinde, Neuvillette, Arlecchino) have **one Rare
  each** and no Uncommon: one under.
- **Missing 5-stars:** Wriothesley, Lyney, Sigewinne, Escoffier (each appears
  only as one of Furina's guest-star cards) and Emilie (nowhere).

**Weak for their rarity** (nearest base card in brackets):
- Bogglecat Box, 4 Block draw 2 (Backflip: 5 and 2), a Common that cycles.
- Freezing Point, 7 and draw 1 (Pommel Strike: 9 and 1), a Common that cycles.
- Shattering Pressure (U), 5 twice: Twin Strike's Common numbers. Taken 5 of
  216 offers.
- Astonishing Shift (U), Swirl and 8 to ALL: a Common number.
- First-Person Shutter, exactly Dodge and Roll.
- Interdiction Fire and Pers, Deploy!: two plain 8-damage attacks.

**Dead outside the right deck:** Navia's Cannon Fire Support (Block on a
reaction; the lowest Rare take, 3%), Enigmatic Feint (needs an aura),
Vanguard's Valor's bonus (needs a reaction).

**Missing effects:** no Energy, no Weak or Strength loss, no Retain or Exhaust
synergy, no Block Power, no Uncommon-or-better draw. Cryo carries six cards;
Hydro, the nation's element, one (Neuvillette); Geo none (Navia is Geo and
applies nothing).

**What works:** Neuvillette (taken 29%), Ring of Bursting Grenades (24%),
Clorinde (13%).

## Picks

1. **Size.** (a, default) Grow to about 26 cards: the four 4-stars trimmed to
   two each (a Common and an Uncommon), the four 5-stars given a second card
   (Uncommon or Rare), and the five missing 5-stars added at two each. (b)
   Keep the eight characters and only fix their cards (about 16).
2. **Furina's guest stars.** Seven companion characters are also guest-star
   cards in Furina's own pool, and the five new 5-stars would make that up to
   eleven. (a, default) Allow it: a guest star is that character performing
   for Furina, a different card with a different job, and the companion art
   uses a different picture. (b) A character is a guest star or a companion,
   never both: the seven overlapping companions move out, Wriothesley, Lyney,
   Sigewinne and Escoffier stay Furina's, and only Emilie is added.
3. **Element.** Today Cryo carries six cards and Hydro one. (a, default)
   Even it out: the reworked and new cards spread across the elements the
   characters use (Hydro for Neuvillette and Sigewinne, Pyro for Lyney, and so
   on), so the pool is not a Cryo pool by accident. (b) Leave the spread as
   it falls.

After the picks, the card-by-card paper follows, like Inazuma's.
