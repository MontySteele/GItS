## Changes Claude builds (no pick)

**Cards.** Yae is at Prototype, so these are Claude's. Both move onto the Edit
count that made Bestseller and Print Run work.
1. **Serialization** (U Power, 1): "Whenever you Edit a card, gain 3 [4]
   Block." (was: whenever you play an Annotated card, gain 3). An Edit deck
   makes several a turn; a 2-card Edit gives 6.
2. **Manuscript Pile** (U, 1): "Draw 2 [3] cards. Edit a card in your hand: Red
   Ink." (was: each Sakura lasts 1 more turn, which no seat valued). It becomes
   the Uncommon draw-and-Edit.

The Seven Glamours, Second Printing and Royalties stay: under five offers a
round, and The Seven Glamours' kill condition is your ruling.

**Text and page.**
3. **Meditations of a Yako, nothing to Publish.** Lane 5 still could not tell
   why no Publish came after two Elites, though the relic now says "an
   Annotation you made that fight". When a fight leaves nothing to Publish,
   the relic flashes with "Nothing to Publish" on the reward screen.
4. **Reprint stacks.** A card with a Published Reprint, Proofread again, plays
   three times (lane 1); the hover says each Reprint adds one play.
5. **Which enemy a Sakura strikes.** Three seats could not tell. The hover and
   page state the existing rule in one sentence.
6. **Incoming and Kitsune Block.** The page's Incoming line leaves out the
   Block a Kitsune Sakura will add at end of turn; the page adds it.
7. **The page one tick behind** after a boss's last end-of-turn hit (lane 2 was
   shown Aeonglass alive after the fight had ended). Harness fix.

**Not changed.** Reborn as Guuji Yae offers neither Reprint nor Royalties, on
purpose (a whole hand doubled or refunded); lane 4 guessed otherwise but the
chooser lists what it offers. Terror Eel's Shriek and Winged Boots are base
game.
