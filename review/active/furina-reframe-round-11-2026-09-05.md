# Furina reframe round eleven: the stage is full by turn two, and empty on turn one by construction

Written 2026-09-05, under the Prototype loop (`operations/stage-gate.md`,
*The loop inside Prototype*). Two blind Opus seats on `0.2.2674+proto`
(main `6e873961`), the four pool-pass Commons in. Records:
`review/qa/furina-reframe-round-11-2026-09-05/opus-arm-act1.md` (lane 1,
assembled) and `opus-natural-act1.md` (lane 2, natural). Prototype stage,
Guardrail-7. Graded against the amended seat brief.

## 1. The hypothesis

"With a second Deploy shape and a kit-owned perform in the offer, the stage
is occupied by fight two of a run and performs on most turns; Second
Course's real price (five Encore) reads on the face plus the member tip."

**Assembled deck** (lane 1, seed `MYR365RDCU9L`, Ascension 2): Curtain
Rises, Second Course, Rolling Tide and Guest List added to the starter. 120
of 120 actions, floor 7, five of five fights won including the Bygone
Effigy elite for zero HP lost, 53 of 78 at the stop. **Natural run** (lane
2, seed `JDZWL895Q9NB`, Ascension 2): 120 of 120, floor 7, five of five
including the Skulking Colony elite, 39 of 78.

## 2. What the round found

**The hypothesis, read.** The stage was occupied and performing from turn
two on in every fight on both lanes: Companion cards played per fight ran
1 / 3 / 4 / 3 / 6 / 4 on the assembled lane and 3 / 4 / 3 / 10 / 4 on the
natural one, against round 10's "furniture for three fights". Curtain
Rises was "the card that fixes the kit's worst turn", reached for on every
draw. Second Course landed twice at its real price, 10 to 5 Encore both
times, performances 6 and 7 then 6 and 6, "the biggest single burst the
kit showed me", and was locked out three times at the opening Encore of 2:
the price reads, and it is strictly an after-Aria card. **Verdict:
supported from turn two.** What it did not touch is turn one.

**Turn one is empty by construction.** The stage starts unlit every fight
(the relic says so), so on turn one every Companion card prints "performs
nobody". Assembled lane: the stage was empty for all of turn one in five of
six fights, and in one there was no legal play but Block. Natural lane: six
of twenty-two turns empty, all at rounds one and two, and zero in the two
fights where Salon Début was in the opening hand. Both seats answered (e)
"no": "exactly 12 Block against exactly 12 damage reads less like a choice
than like a solution"; "the kit's first real decision arrived on turn 2".
The reading is not Deploy density any more, which the pass answered; it is
that the one starter deploy is a one-of in a sixteen-card deck. This is a
demonstrated structural fault by the stage-gate's own test (the starter
does not present the central choice) and it is the pick (§5).

**Encore is the kit's decision, and both seats said so.** One pool pays the
Spotlight, every performance, Second Course, and absorbs damage after
Block, so "light the Spotlight on turn one" is a real bet and "almost every
turn a small budget problem". The elite on the assembled lane was won by
ordering alone: Weak, then Block, then Freminet, so Frozen and Weak stacked
and a 23-damage hit landed for nothing. The natural lane's Crabaletta-Hydro
then Diona-Cryo then Frozen then Shatter kill "paid off exactly as the
printed previews said it would".

**A real engine defect, found by the natural seat.** Under the arm, a
reaction amplifier is lost when another multiplier is already on the hit:
Chevreuse printed 7, previewed Vaporize 1.5x, dealt 8 (base times Spotlight
times Weak, no 1.5x); a dry Crabaletta on Hydro dealt 4, no 1.5x. In fight
1, with neither Spotlight nor Weak up, Melt and Vaporize composed correctly
(`EB-511`). A Deploy performed the wrong member: Salon Début fielded
Crabaletta and the Usher performed first, spending the last Encore
(`EB-508`).

**What the pool pass displaced.** Rolling Tide: never played, drawn in four
of six fights and declined every time, "2 energy for 4 damage per body, the
worst rate in the deck", its rider carried by Lynette and Bennett for 1 and
0; the four-Wriggler board it looks built for did not want it either. Guest
List: "fine and slightly dull", An Invitation with 3 Block stapled on.
Curtain Rises, from the natural lane: a "draft trap with no warning",
because it reads as an Attack and fields a Block member into the rotation,
so on a stacked stage the first Companion card each turn reaches the damage
member and the rest give 3 Block; the seat could not kill a 4-HP enemy with
three Companion cards in hand. That is a rotation-order finding wearing a
card's name, and it goes in §4.

**Legibility, seven rows.** Second Course refuses with "not enough energy"
at 3 of 3 energy when the shortfall is Encore, while Ethereal Spotlight
prints the right string (`EB-505`). The front member is unreadable after a
rotation (`EB-506`). Shipped Fanfare feeders still print under the arm
(Rapturous Applause "Fanfare +8" beside a tooltip that says only a
performance makes Fanfare; two meters appeared once, unexplained)
(`EB-507`). The Spotlight relic keeps dealing an unplayable copy every turn
once lit, five to seven dead draws a fight (`EB-509`). Several observe
screens printed the hand and the enemy list twice (`EB-510`). "Burst +5" on
Gentilhomme Usher at two rewards with no meter in the run (`EB-449`
reopened; the build blanked five faces by name and not the sheet). Fischl's
face printed Klee's Hexerei and Oz rules on a Furina run (`EB-504`
widened). Not rows: a Swirl the seat could not audit and the Spotlight's
reach into Gorou's delayed hit (`EB-410`, `EB-498` families); a Weak
rounding that cost 1 Encore on an 8-into-8.

**Companions.** The assembled seat took a Companion at every reward, "and I
never once felt the pull of the other three options"; the natural seat
played 24 in five fights. For this kit the acquisition question runs the
other way from Klee's and Kokomi's: the slot delivers, and the deck fills
with Companions because nothing else competes.

## 5. The pick

**Pick 1 (A): the stage on turn one.** R254 kept the starter at two kit
cards and said "they can be stronger". Both lanes read turn one as empty
by construction, and the natural lane's count is the fact: zero empty
turns in the fights where Salon Début was in the opening hand, six of
twenty-two otherwise.

1. **Salon Début gains Innate** [default]: the starter's own deploy is in
   every opening hand; no relic change, no new card, one keyword on the
   card the seats already reach for; "stronger", as R254 allowed.
2. The starting relic fields Mademoiselle Crabaletta at combat start: the
   stage is never unlit, and Salon Début becomes a second body; a relic
   change under the stage-gate's structural-fault clause.
3. Curtain Rises replaces one Soloist's Solicitation in the starter: two
   deploys in ten, a second shape from fight one.
4. Leave it: the first deploy is the fight's first decision.
