# Kokomi round twenty-one: Nereid's doubles the morning from turn one, every number reconciles, and the coordinator granted the wrong Oath

Written 2026-09-06, under the Prototype loop. Two blind Opus seats on
`0.2.2800+proto` (round-21 branch with fixers O and P in; The Moon
Overlooks the Waters withdrawn, `EB-570`). Records:
`review/qa/kokomi-round-21-2026-09-06/opus-arm-act1.md` (lane 1,
assembled, Ascension 2) and `opus-natural-act1.md` (lane 2, natural,
Ascension 3). Prototype stage, Guardrail-7.

## 1. The hypothesis

"An assembled Tactician deck that reaches Nereid's Ascension names the
morning it doubles as the payoff and can say what it held for it; a
natural deck that plans on most turns by fight four drafts a reader row
unprompted."

**Assembled deck** (lane 1, seed `8H3UGZKB0SNZ`): Nereid's Ascension,
Battle Plan, Tide Wall, Well Laid, Read the Field, and, by the
coordinator's error, the Kurage Memory arm's "Kurage's Oath" power instead
of the overhaul's Oath (§2, `EB-581`). 120 of 120, five fights won and the
sixth stopped with the last enemy at 1 HP under lethal Poison, 38 of 80.
**Natural run** (lane 2, seed `BG35Z1K5Z8LZ`): 120 of 120, five of five
and a sixth in progress, 59 of 87.

## 2. What the round found

**Nereid's Ascension, first clause: supported, with a hand that had no
attack.** Lane 1 played it on turn one of fight one and again in fight
three. The first doubled morning got Battle Plan only, "my opening hand
held no attack, so the only thing worth doubling was draw and energy": it
paid 2 Energy and 4 cards, and the seat named what it held for it (Defend,
Defend, Tide Wall). Turn one of fight one, in the seat's words: "Defend,
Defend, Tide Wall, Nereid's Ascension, Battle Plan, not one attack. Spend 3
energy on 15 Block and deal nothing, or spend it on a power plus a Plan and
eat 7. Reading 'carries out every Plan twice' next to 'Gain 1 Energy and
draw 2 cards' and working out that those multiply is the moment the
character taught me what it is." Ambush ("Deal 5. Plan: Deal 12") was the
happiest draw, "a 2.4x multiplier for one turn of patience, immune to
Weak, doubled again by Nereid's".

**The readers, second clause: not supported, and the reason is now
precise.** Lane 2 wrote Plans on nine of eighteen turns (two on one turn,
one written and cashed the same turn through Change of Plans, four turns
with no Plan card in hand), was offered five readers (Tide Chart, Treatise
twice, Tide Wall twice, Song of Pearls), took Treatise once for the draw
and declined the rest, "each because they are dead on exactly the hands
that are already dead." Lane 1, with Well Laid in the deck from the start,
read it 2, 0, 0, 0, 0, 2, 0, 3 across eight draws and named why: "you
cannot hold a card for a morning; the hand discards at end of turn, so you
can only build a big morning and hope" ((c) 8). Tide Wall paid 6, 6 and 9,
every one reconciling. The shelf's reading across rounds 18 to 21 is
settled: a reader pays when it is drawn on the morning after a planned
turn, which the draw decides, and a natural seat prices that as a dead
card. Held; the shelf is the second-half shelf the round-20 packet called
it, and no row moves on a fourth reading of the same fact.

**Every number reconciled, both lanes.** Lane 2: "18 = 6+6+6, 20 = Riptide
13 + Oath 7, 8 = Strike 6 + Casket 2, 6 = 4 x 1.5 with Vaporize, the
blocked Oath saying 'lost 2 HP, and 5 more absorbed by Block'. I want that
on the record because it is unusual." Lane 1's one "inert power" was the
coordinator's wrong grant (below), not the kit.

**The decision, both lanes.** Lane 1: "under Weak my attack faces shrink
but Plan lines don't, so writing becomes strictly correct; the mirror is a
telegraphed enemy Defend, which makes playing now correct instead."
Lane 2 tested Shrink the same way (the carry-out ignored it, 7 landed
while Strikes were cut 6 to 4) and called it "the kit's best moment". The
carry-out's immunity to the player's damage debuffs is the kit's lever and
the keyword names only Weak (`EB-579`).

**The coordinator's error, disclosed.** The assembled grant used
`proto_kurages_oath_memory`, the Kurage Memory base kit's power ("Whenever
the Bake-Kurage plays a card from its memory, gain 3 Block"), not the
overhaul's `proto_kk_kurages_oath` ("Deal 3 to ALL. Plan: Deal 7"). Under
the overhaul no memory pulse exists, so the power paid nothing on three
mornings and "cost me a real decision" (a Defend passed for it, 5 HP). The
two rows share a title and sat in hand as "(1)" and "(2)" by draw order.
That the old row is grantable and inert under the arm is `EB-581`; the
round's Oath reading is void and lane 2's natural Oath ("Oath 7", a Plan
the seat answered four ways in round 20) stands.

**Sharp against the Plan line.** Lane 2: Sharp 2 raised Riptide's face 9
to 11 and left its Plan at 13, "silently reversed the right play on my
best card", visible as two numbers and stated nowhere. Rule 3 says her
Strength and Dexterity count; an enchantment is the card's own, and both
lines are the card's. §5 folds it (`EB-580`).

**Legibility.** The jellyfish advertised every turn and refused by
Strike and Defend, "I learned what a Plan card is on round 3, by refusal"
(`EB-578`). Nereid's Ascension absent from the map's deck list, five
Status cards and two transformed Strikes never named (`EB-447`, cited;
the deck list is the last combat deck). Shinobu into Hydro printed Poison
8 once and Poison 4 once from the same preview ((c) 4, recorded; the relic
re-applying the aura inside the beat is the glossary's own warning, and
the seat recognised it live). Artifact eating Slack Water's Weak with no
line (`EB-495`'s table, cited). `skip` refused on the reward list whose
next screen says "You may skip this" ((c) 9, recorded). Praised: the
carry-out log ("clause number, rider and total kept separate") a third
time; the Elemental Reaction block's paragraph on the relic and the aura,
"a paragraph that earned its length"; Well Laid rewriting its own number
in hand, "the card that taught me what a morning was".

## 3. What the round did not test

The act-1 boss on either lane. The overhaul's Kurage's Oath in an
assembled deck (the grant was wrong). A reader drawn on a planned
morning by design rather than chance. Nothing here is a strength reading.

   with a colliding title, is the round's one structural fault, and it
   was the coordinator's to find.
2. **`EB-578`, `EB-579`:** the jellyfish's first two turns and the
   carry-out's immunities, both taught by refusal or by test.
3. **The reader shelf: held** on its fourth reading; the fact is settled
   and a card that needs the draw's cooperation is not moved by a round.
