## 2. What the round found

**The morning is trustworthy now.** The assembled seat: "every prediction I
made about a Plan carry-out was met", listing Pincer 9, Riptide 13, Tide
Chart 1 card, Battle Plan twice into Energy 5 and 4 cards, Oath 7 to each
of three, four Pincer carry-outs 19 through 5 Block. Tide Chart's draw is
narrated (`EB-503` read as fixed). The one exception is the relic's
accounting (`EB-518`): the carry-out block listed three Casket strikes
where the HP needed four, the fourth fired off a Frozen the Plan's own hit

**The payoffs, read one by one.** Pincer "the best" of the five, played in
four of five fights and bought again before the seat knew it was a plant.
Riptide good in both modes. Nereid's Ascension "won fight 3 alone" and was
held unplayed three times: "a card for when you are already ahead", at 2
energy on a turn under 25 HP. Tide Chart played once, held three times:
"with one Plan it is a worse Battle Plan, with none it does nothing". Well
Laid "dead weight at base 2", good only on a morning that already carried
something out. **Verdict: the hypothesis holds on narration and on the
doubler; Tide Chart and Well Laid read as what they are, cards that pay
only when the morning is already full, and the seats want them less than
Pincer.** That is a reading about the shelf, not a defect: two of the three
Plan-count payoffs (Tide Wall, Tide Chart, Well Laid) are drafted only by a
deck that already plans on most turns, which is Witches' Circle's shape.

**Plans written per turn.** Assembled lane: a Plan written on 9 of 17
logged turns, above half, with the doubler on the table from fight 3.
Natural lane: 7 of 20, the jellyfish "empty" on most turns of a 16-card
deck that was half Strike and Defend; three of five opening hands "five
vanilla cards" and turn one of fight one "had no decision at all". The
assembled seat's turn one had "exactly one decision, Pincer 6 now or 9
planned, and four cards that played themselves". The starter reading is
now the same on both kits: the kit's question arrives on turn one only when
a kit card is in the opening hand, and the R254 starter puts one there a
third of the time. Round 17's Klee pick is the same shape; Kokomi's is not
re-asked here because R254 and R257 were ruled on this exact reading, and
the packet records the eighth round of it instead.

on the board resolve". The assembled seat's best turn was fight 4 turn 3 at
24 HP against 24 printed: War Council, Pincer, Oath ordered so the Shrinker
