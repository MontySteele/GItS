# Furina reframe round thirteen: the stage is draw-gated on one card, and the price of the second performance

Written 2026-09-05, under the Prototype loop. Two blind Opus seats on
`0.2.2729+proto` (round-18 head `a280c4f3`: Rolling Tide at 1 energy,
Curtain Rises fielding Chevalmarin, the Furina rows of fixer L). Records:
`review/qa/furina-reframe-round-13-2026-09-05/opus-arm-act1.md` (lane 2,
assembled) and `opus-natural-act1.md` (lane 1, natural). Prototype stage,
Guardrail-7. The round-11 pick (the stage on turn one, PR #398) is
unruled.

## 1. The hypothesis

"Rolling Tide at 1 energy is a staple, and Curtain Rises fielding
Chevalmarin keeps the damage engine at the front; the stage performs on
most turns from turn two, with Second Course's price read correctly."

**Assembled deck** (lane 2, seed `7NH3TSGH9LLR`, Ascension 2): Rolling
Tide, Curtain Rises, Second Course, Guest List added. 119 of 120, five of
five including the elite, 34 of 78. **Natural run** (lane 1, seed
`XM9GBWLVXG04`, Ascension 2): 118 of 120, five of five including the
Bygone Effigy elite for zero HP, 56 of 78, six HP lost all run.

## 2. What the round found

**Rolling Tide at 1 energy is still the weakest card in the deck.** Held in
three of six hands: "4 damage into one body; zero against Plating 8;
actively harmful against four Skittish bodies", good only as mop-up. Three
rounds, four seats, one reading, and the price was not the reason.
**Withdrawn from the arm's offer** (`EB-552`, a D default): the shipped
Undercurrent returns at its seam. The kit's own perform verb is not the
missing piece; a Companion card is the trigger and the seats have plenty.

**Curtain Rises with Chevalmarin: "good", for the 6 damage.** "The member
it fields is nearly irrelevant"; played every time drawn. The natural seat
declined it at a reward "because Chevalmarin's 2-damage performance would
permanently dilute the front-member rotation from 6 to 4; adding a body to
the stage can make the stage worse, with no warning, a good trap." That is
the third reading that the deploy choice is a rotation choice, and this
time the seat called it a decision rather than a defect. The adjustment
stands.

**Second Course at 3 was never playable, a third round.** Four draws, four
refusals, "contributed nothing". The printed price plus the drain is 5
against an opening of 2, and no Aria arrived first. The smallest
intervention is the printed number: **1 Encore** (full value 3 with the
drain), read at the door and FOLLOWS on C2 and C6 (record §5.8), built
under `EB-552`.

**The stage is draw-gated on one card, and that is the floor.** Natural
lane: no member for five turns across five fights, "on those turns every
Companion card prints performs nobody and the deck degrades to vanilla
6-damage attacks with no decisions at all"; the card it was happiest to
draw was Salon Début, "the enabler, not a payoff". Assembled lane: every
fight opened on an empty stage, one turn each in four fights and two in
the fifth. The fourth round of this reading; it is the round-11 pick.

**The ceiling, and the two reads the rules gave away.** Elite turn two on
the natural lane: 40 damage from three performances, two Slow-ordered
Attacks and a Shatter, the elite at zero HP lost. Performances dodge Shrink
("a performance is not an Attack": Crabaletta hit for the full 6 while
Soloist's face rewrote to 4) and Companion cards dodge Tangled's Attack
tax; "both times the enemy's counter-play correctly pushed me off cards and
onto the salon; this is the best thing in the kit". The assembled seat
found the same rule against Skittish and called it "the most useful thing
I learned and effectively invisible" (`EB-548`: the performance tip owes
the sentence, `EB-538`'s twin).

**Fanfare was decorative for an act, both lanes.** Peaked at 7 and 17
against readers at 10 and 3; Aria+ is Innate, so it is played on turn one
when Fanfare is always 0 and its Fanfare half "was structurally dead every
fight" (`EB-550`: the upgrade drops Innate, a D default). No Evoke occurred
in ten fights across the two lanes.

**Legibility and defects.** A full-strength Crabaletta into Pyro consumed
the aura and moved HP by 6, not 9, while the same card dry into the same
aura did multiply (`EB-546`, unreconciled after `EB-511`). "No reaction is
reachable, Pyro is the only element" printed with a Hydro member's deploy
in hand (`EB-547`). Two companion names each label two cards ("Kaeya,
Frostgnaw" as a reward card and as a fetched Common with different bodies;
"Dahlia, Sacramental Shower" as an Attack and a Skill) (`EB-549`). The
Smith preview omits keyword deltas (Aria+'s Innate chosen unseen)
(`EB-551`). Kujou Sara's Electro ate an aura set up for another reaction
with no line (`EB-410`, cited). Guest List "filler; its fetch mattered more
than its 3 Block".

**Companions.** Assembled: five offered, three taken, plays 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 /
14. Natural: five offered, three taken, plays 3 / 3 / 4 / 7 / 7; both
Neuvillette offers declined.
