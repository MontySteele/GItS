`0.2.2696+proto` (round-17 head `2394745c`, fixers I and J in). Records:
`review/qa/furina-reframe-round-12-2026-09-05/opus-arm-act1.md` (lane 1,

## 1. The hypothesis

"Rolling Tide at its printed price is never the right card; a seat granted
it names the turn on which it would have played it at 1 energy, or names
none." The assembled seat was asked to log every hand holding it.

**Assembled deck** (lane 1, seed `HVRX9CKJWT9L`, Ascension 2): Rolling Tide,
Curtain Rises, Second Course, Guest List added. 114 of 120, floor 9, four

## 2. What the round found

**Rolling Tide: the price is the finding.** Six hands, played twice, held
four; "filler at 2 energy against single targets; at 1 energy I'd have
played it in 4 of 6 and it would be a staple." The natural seat, offered it
at a reward, "did not want it at 2 energy, less than Chevreuse for the same
cost; would take it at 1." With round 11's four declines that is three
seats and one answer. **Verdict: confirmed, and the seats named the price.**
The adjustment (1 energy) is read at §4 and built under `EB-530`.

**Curtain Rises fields the wrong member.** Held four times on the assembled
lane "because the Usher it fields is a Block member who then holds the
front"; on the natural lane "the card I never wanted to play, because it
puts the Usher at the front and converts the damage engine into a block
engine", with the Usher himself read as "below a basic Defend" (3 Block a
trigger, 2 dry). Round 11's natural seat said the same. Three seats; the
smallest change is one word on the face, Chevalmarin for the Usher, read
at §4 and built under `EB-530`. The Usher's own number is a member
constant and is not moved here.

**The empty first turn, a third round.** Assembled lane: 4 of 19 turns
with no member all turn and 9 that began empty; natural lane: 10 of 15
turns began empty and every fight's turn one did. Second Course, priced at
3, "is unplayable on turn 1 of every fight" at the opening Encore of 2,
"which leaves only two reliable Deploys". The natural seat drafted a third
stage-filler "specifically to fix this". The reading is the round-11 pick's
and is not re-asked; it is recorded as the third round of it.

**Encore is the decision, a third round.** "One pool pays card costs, pays
for member performances, and absorbs damage after Block, with no
regeneration until Aria is drawn. Turn one of every fight asks Spotlight or
performances, and I answered it both ways with visible consequences."
Sequencing Aria (wants Fanfare 3, which only performances make) against
deploys (want Encore) is the recurring second choice. Both seats' biggest
turns were a Melt or Vaporize off an aura placed a turn earlier, read off
the preview line.

**Second Course, at its real price, twice more:** 10 to 5 for 6 and 6; 4 to
0 for 6 and 4 dry. The arithmetic the packet corrected on 2026-09-05 is
what the seats see.

**Legibility.** Burst still printed on companion faces under the arm
(Bennett, Barbara "Burst +5", no meter anywhere; `EB-524`: the round-11
build blanked Furina's rows and the companion sheets are a second source).
The Final Verdict and Take Your Bow unreadable in the shop ("baseline falls

preview and no reason (`EB-529`). Slow's own card not counting itself,
unsaid (`EB-525`). Encore arithmetic the natural seat could not reconcile

**Companions.** Assembled: five offered, four taken, plays 3/4/2/4/6.
Natural: two stage-fillers all act and a third drafted for the stage. The
deck fills with Companions on this kit because nothing else competes, as
