**Why.** [USER], 2026-10-15: Furina's next items include "the event and
dialogue sweep to be brought up to par with Klee and Varka", and "go ahead and
do these items while I do the Furina run next". The standard is the ruled

events per character, one at most per act, the act-1 event choosing act 2's,
a home thread when an act is skipped, every price from its scene, outside the

**What she has today:** a one-line select description, six loc rows, the
Architect finale, and two pair lines filed in Klee's and Varka's tables. No
events, no Ancient lines, no combat lines (`StoryRegistry.All` lists only Klee
and Varka).

**Where she stands in the story.** After the trial. She was the human who
played the Hydro Archon for five hundred years so the prophecy could be
broken; the verdict ended the act, and she lives as Furina now, an actress
with friends. The events are about the habits that outlived the role: the
stage, the waiting, the people who wrote to her afterwards. Only a Palais
courier appears in person. Everyone else reaches her as letters, a newspaper
and things the Spire makes that look like home.
