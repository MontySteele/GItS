## 1. Why the runs die in act 2

**The fights are long, and the chip adds up.** Every run left act 1 with a quarter of its HP: the act-1 boss was won at 24, 33, 14, 14 and 23 of 80 (packet, table row "Act 1 boss"). The Giant took nine turns (B F9), Soul Fysh twelve (D k4b F9, "fair in text and brutal over 12 turns"). Then act 2 asks for the same again against bigger numbers: C "The cause was HP drift across the act (Hopper 47, Bowlbugs 34, then a 134-HP wall...). No decision remained"; E k5b's HP line 48, 31, 31, 26, 8, 3; A "The loss was the HP I carried in: 38/80 after the elite". This is the same finding as your 2026-09-29 run ("a lot of chip hits over time adding up... things didn't die fast"), one act later because the level pass and the Plan rate fixed the act-1 numbers.

**Why the fights are long: nothing compounds.** The five final decks hold, between them, these of her Powers: Treatise, Watatsumi's Grace, Tidal Riposte, Coordinated Tide (B2), Nereid's Ascension and At Water's Edge (C), Tidal Riposte (D2), Sangonomiya's Orders and At Water's Edge (E). Not one Strength card (Resolve of Watatsumi was offered to B twice and skipped; General's War Banner is in no record), not one of the five jellyfish Powers. The drafts went to the big numbers: Tideturn 4 of 4 offers, Tide Wall 2 of 2, Second Wave 2 of 2, Coral Crash 4 of 7, Masterstroke 3 (packet sec.5). So turn eight of a boss fight looks like turn two: Oath planned, a Strike, a Defend. The one run that drafted an engine (C, Nereid's Ascension) called it "the best decision in the kit" and it carried four fights; C still died, to Frail and a 134-HP wall, with no second engine.

**Block arriving a turn late is real but secondary.** A: "Planned Block was often huge, but one turn late." Tide Wall paid 4 on a Defend turn and 29 on an attack turn (A F10) because its Plan reads the intent at carry-out and the screen never says so. The deaths themselves were not short of Block cards at the Common rate: she has nine Commons that Block now at 5 to 10 for 1 Energy (Oath, Bulwark, Shell, Kelp Wall, Screen, Riptide Guard, Bubble Ward, Breakwater, War Banner), in line with the base five. The final turns were short of Block for seat reasons: B2 built Masterstroke x2 plus five Coral Crashes "with no reliable Block draw"; D2 took Disintegration over Mind Rot and then Sloth; A raced and took 24 unblocked (F10); E2 "relying on AWE's trigger". So no generic Block goes in. The Frail deaths (A, C) are a display defect, not a number: the card still said "Gain 5 Block" while it gave 3 (sec.4 item 15).

**Seat skill versus kit.** Seat: the four deck-building and curse errors above, and three seats writing Plans into Block they had been warned about (C F4, C F14, B F8). Kit: five different seats with five different decks all exited act 1 at a quarter HP, and no record found a drafted card that made a fight shorter as it went on.

**Which archetypes exist in play.** The **Plan half** exists and carries alone. The **Companion half** exists as a reaction engine: Fischl on a Hydro aura (A), Razor (B), Sara and Rosaria (C, D: "enemy 39 to 6 in a turn"), Thundergrust (E). It mostly ran by playing a Companion onto an aura her own Attack left; the "plan a Companion, Hydro first" rule was used once (E F2) and reacted with Hydro, as you asked. The **jellyfish half does not exist in play.** No Sting token was made in any run ("The Stings never appeared", D k4b; the word Sting in the records is Brine Sting, a Plan card). Jellyfish Drift was skipped 3 times, Kurage's Wake 3, Endless Shoal 2, Shoal Call 2, Canopy 1; Watatsumi Drill was taken once and its Ward "never wanted" (C) or written as a free Weak ALL "always right" (D). Watchful Shoal, Kurage Bloom, Barrage, The Shape of Water and All Streams are in no record at all. At the draft, a generator reads as a 6-damage Strike that hands you a 4-damage card, and the cards that make that good are Uncommon Powers no seat held. Your run saw the same ("take the big number and ignore the weird cards").

## 2. What works and is kept

- **Companion reactions off her Hydro.** Every run had one and named it a best moment (A "the poison did a lot of work later"; D F3 Rosaria Frozen plus Superconduct). The rule set from 2026-10-12 is doing the job you asked of it.
- **Coral Crash at 1 [0].** Still the best turn in three runs (A F11 42 damage, C F11 23 for 23 with no damage taken, A boss T1 81) and now priced; keep.
- **Nereid's Ascension.** "The best decision in the kit" (C); it makes the queue's order the puzzle, as the brief §5 intends.
- **Masterstroke as a Plan-only Retain card.** 42 for 3 next turn is the ruled 1.3 rate on a 3-cost Rare (Bludgeon 32); E F8 "162 to 118" on an Empower turn is the card doing its job.
- **Tide Wall, Second Wave, Opening Gambit, Tideturn, Kelp Wall.** The order riders produced the only turns the seats called decisions (D F5 "I wrote Gambit first", A F12 T6 planning Tide Wall over 4 Block, "that decision was right").
- **Plans after the draw, Casket at start of turn.** No record found the timing wrong once learned (E k5: "took me a while").

## 3. The strong turns, against the loop rule

A loop is a problem only if trivial to assemble and execute. None of these repeats; each is one turn, bought with cards in hand and the now-lines forgone.

- **Second Wave + Tide Wall, 58 Block vs a 23 hit (A F9).** Two Uncommons, 2 Energy, written in order, once. The bonus is uncapped and doubles, so against a 40 intent it reads 92; overkill wastes it. Keep both. Not a loop.
- **Second Wave + Wrath, 36 to ALL (A F12 T8).** A Rare plus an Uncommon, 4 Energy over two turns, killed three segments that Reattach would otherwise bring back. Keep.
- **Nereid + Masterstroke, 108 (B F15).** Two Rares, 5 Energy, the Power once per combat; the boss still won at 24/80. Keep.
- **Gambit + Oath, 27 to each rat (D F5).** Vulnerable ALL times double on a 1-of starter, 2 Energy. Keep.
- **For the probe's known set:** Gambit, then Second Wave, then Wrath: 18 x 2 x 2 x 1.5 = 108 to ALL for 4 Energy next turn, three cards.

### B. Picks

**The jellyfish half** is the one thing numbers alone may not fix: five runs, zero Stings. The Common generators are the Silent's rates on purpose (jellyfish paper sec.3), and the Silent's work because Blade Dance is Common and the Shivs are in every draft; the swap above gives her that. Whether that is enough is pick 1. The Casket option is in there because "Casket's 3 Block is a quiet thing I stopped noticing" (D) and "automatic" (E) is new evidence on a relic you ruled on 2026-10-12; its cost is a guard clause.

**The turn-one Oath Plan** was called automatic by three seats (A "almost never a reason not to"; B "stopped being a decision"; E "the Plan always won"). The brief §4 says that turn is the whole kit. My read is that a teaching play on turn one is fine (Ironclad's Bash is the same), and the decisions the seats did have came from drafted Plan cards. Pick 2 only because it is a starter.

**The Ward** was ruled on 2026-10-12 at "0: Gain 4 Block. Plan: Apply 1 Weak to ALL enemies." New fact: "Ward as a 0-cost Weak Plan is always right" (D), "the Plan was free and Weak was worth more than 4" (C). A token whose one choice is dead is the Dusk problem in a smaller coat.
