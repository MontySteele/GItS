# Furina round fourteen: the withdrawn card was not missed, Second Course waits on Encore not price, and Dexterity does nothing

Written 2026-09-05, under the Prototype loop. Two blind Opus seats on
`0.2.2753+proto` (round-19 head `a2eb3675`, fixer M in: Second Course at 1
Encore, Rolling Tide withdrawn, Aria+ without Innate). Records:
`review/qa/furina-reframe-round-14-2026-09-05/opus-arm-act1.md` (lane 1,
assembled) and `opus-natural-act1.md` (lane 2, natural). Both at Ascension
2. Neither lane played on R260's build (the stage starting with Crabaletta,
`EB-553`, built after these seats embarked). Prototype stage, Guardrail-7.

## 1. The hypothesis

"With Second Course at 1 Encore it is played on turn one when drawn and its
two performances read at the price printed; without Rolling Tide the
assembled deck loses nothing; Aria+ without Innate pays its Fanfare half in
most fights."

**Assembled deck** (lane 1, seed `NMVNY3R77JN7`): Curtain Rises, Second
Course, Guest List added. 111 of 120, four of four fights including the
four-Gardener elite, 47 of 78 at the stop with an elite the only node ahead.
**Natural run** (lane 2, seed `ENQWGY0E7UVL`): 112 of 120, six of six
including the Phrog Parasite elite, 63 of 78, fifteen HP lost across the
act, fourteen of it in one turn.

## 2. What the round found

**Rolling Tide: withdrawn and not missed.** Neither seat named a gap where
it had been; the assembled deck's damage came from Curtain Rises (played
seven times, "the deck's only reliable enabler") and the Companion cards.
Supported; the withdrawal stands.

**Second Course: the price was never the constraint, Encore is.** Five
appearances on lane 1, playable once: four refusals of "you have no Encore,
and this costs 1", all on mid-fight turns where the pool sat at 0. The one
play, elite turn 8 at 9 Encore into a full stage, Evoked the front member,
deployed Crabaletta, and the two performances killed the last 14-HP
Gardener outright: "when it can be played, it is the best card in the
deck." The hypothesis's first clause is not supported, and the reason is
the kit's own economy: Encore starts at 2, every performance drains 1, and
the pool is 0 on most turns unless Aria or Curtain Cue refills it. The card
is a mid-fight payoff that needs a generator beside it, and at 1 Encore its
price is right for that job. Nothing moves; it is read again on R260's
build, where a member is on stage from the first draw (§4).

**Aria+ without Innate pays.** Lane 2 drafted Aria+ naturally and called it
the runner-up happiest draw, "watching a dry 4 become a 10"; lane 1's
unupgraded Aria doubled on every play (3 Fanfare on turn two of fight one,
4 on the elite's turn three, 12 on its turn six) and is "the only card that
turns the fight back on." Supported.

**The empty stage, a fifth reading, and R260's answer.** Lane 2: four of
eighteen combat turns with no member, every one because Salon Début was the
deck's only Deploy, "so whether the stage exists on turn 1 is a draw, not a
decision"; on fight one turn one the seat did not know which card deploys.
Lane 1, fight four turn one: three Companion cards played into an empty
stage, "three dead performance lines". The Evoke was unreachable for lane 2
all act, one Deploy never filling three slots. R260 (this afternoon) puts
Crabaletta on stage at combat start; round 15 reads whether the turn-one
Companion card performs, and whether the Evoke is reached by a natural deck.

**Dexterity does nothing under the arm.** Lane 1 measured it four ways:
Charlotte 5 plus Guest List 4 gave Block 9, Lynette 8 gave 8, Stage
Presence 7 gave 7, all with Dexterity 1 on the state from Oddly Smooth
Stone. It also cost a draft: the seat refused Gorou's War Banner on it.
`EB-390` read Dexterity lifting Dig In and Barbara under Klee, so the miss
is the Furina arm's Block path (`EB-566`).

**The Spotlight window is the kit's first decision and it is unprinted.**
Both seats: Ethereal Spotlight costs 2 Encore, the fight opens with 2, and
one performance locks it out for the combat. Lane 1 lost it in fight one
("by my second card the Spotlight was locked out"), then played it first in
every fight after; lane 2 derived the ordering rule the same way and then
answered it differently across fights for printed reasons: paid with
Companion cards in hand, held when performances were the best damage, paid
instantly into an empty stage. That is the decision the reframe wanted, and
the screen teaches it by refusal one action too late (`EB-567`).

**The Salon against Skittish, both directions.** "A performance is not a
hit" was the whole answer to the four-Gardener elite: the members' hits
bypassed the 6 Block, the cards' hits did not, and the seat spread one card
hit per body on purpose. Matinée Performance's per-member rider is deleted
by the same trait, "the exact shape Skittish deletes". The rule earned its
keep; the interaction is the game's, not a defect.

**Random targeting as a decision.** Both seats built turns around it: lane
1 moved Rosaria off a body because Chevalmarin's roll had put Hydro on the
other, and aimed Gorou at the healthy Gardener so its performance might
roll onto the 1-HP one (it did: "two bodies in one card"); lane 2 twice
killed down to one body with aimed cards so the performances "could only
land where I wanted". "The most interesting thing the Salon does and it is
entirely emergent."

**Legibility.** The Evoke fired twice and printed no line; the seat read it
off Encore +3 and Hydro on all (`EB-564`). Rapturous Applause's face says
"Fanfare +8" and the sheet's op is a floor of 8; two meters surfaced and the
seat called the card "much better than printed" (`EB-568`; `EB-507` cited).
Amber's Explosive Puppet is outside the Spotlight fold, 8 before and after
while every other Companion card rewrote (`EB-565`; `EB-463`'s shape).
Crystallize (Gorou 9 Block for 4 printed) and Superconduct (Vulnerable 1)
printed nothing while Frozen and Swirl had named previews (`EB-410`,
cited). A played Power vanished from the map's deck list (`EB-447`, cited).
The Salon keyword "is 90 words long and arrives all at once", accurate in
every clause and a wall; recorded. Praised: the Spotlight rewriting the
printed number on the face ("saved me from arithmetic all round"), the
empty-stage rider on Companion cards, the refusal texts.

**The starter's Block suite.** Lane 2's one automatic turn in eighteen was
four Block cards against a board of Buff intents; Regal Bearing's Weak is
dead whenever the spare energy exists to play it, and the seat would cut
it. Stage Presence and Soloist's Solicitation are vanilla on both lanes.
That is the starter as ruled (basics are supposed to be bad); noted, not
moved.
