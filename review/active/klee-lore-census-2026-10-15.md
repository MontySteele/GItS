## 1. Who she is
- Titles: Spark Knight (G8, B, FX); Fleeing Sunlight, also "Fleeing Sun" (GBd, FX, B); Red Cake Witch (outfit) and Crimson Knight of Windrise (legend) (B); rumored "Red Knight of the Stormbearer Mountains" (G8, FX). She calls the Traveler "Honorary Knight" (B).
- 5-star Pyro Catalyst, Mondstadt, Knights of Favonius (G8, GG). Constellation Trifolium (G8, FX, B). Birthday July 27 (G8, FX, B).
- VAs: EN Poonam Basu (MM; B spells it "Panam Basu"), JP Kuno Misaki (MM, B). B also lists Hua Ling and Fang Lianzhi without languages; Hua Ling is probably CN [SPEC]. Korean unresolved.
- Dish: Fish-Flavored Toast, a Fisherman's Toast variant, party DEF (FX, PG). Origin: Jean had her make it as an apology to fishermen (PG, B).
- Namecard: not found on any fetched page. One low-quality site says it is a max-friendship reward (unverified).
- Signature weapon: no 5-star one. Dodoco Tales is the 4-star catalyst built for her, from the Midsummer Island Adventure event (Prima). An event weapon, not a gacha signature.

## 2. Exact names
- Normal Attack: Kaboom! (GG, FX; GBd mislabels it as the skill).
- Skill: Jumpy Dumpty (GG, FX, GBd). Burst: Sparks 'n' Splash (GG, FX, GBd, B).
- Passives: Pounding Surprise (A1), Sparkling Burst (A4), All Of My Treasures! (utility) (GG, GBd, FX).
- Constellations (GG, GBd, FX agree except C1 spelling): C1 Chained Reactions (FX: "Chained Reaction"), C2 Explosive Frags, C3 Exquisite Compound, C4 Sparkly Explosion, C5 Nova Burst, C6 Blazing Delight.
- B gives different constellation labels (Chain Reaction, Shrapnel, Klee's Special Blend, Detonator, Bombardment Star, All-Out Attack). They look like older or alternate names; use the GG/GBd/FX set.
- Outfits: Shooting Spark (default), Blossoming Starlight (GBd); Wonder Flower Star Candle (B).

## 3. Story
- Alice: Klee's mother, famed adventurer, author of the Teyvat Travel Guide; father Kleiner (G8, B, FX). Alice taught her gunpowder, fuses and firework design (B), left her with the Knights and gave her Dodoco (G8). Both changed Mondstadt's landscape with explosives, Alice on purpose, Klee by mistake (G8). Alice entrusted a box to Klee before leaving (B). Parents entrusted Klee to the Knights "three years earlier" (B).
- Dodoco: doll handmade by Alice, Klee's first friend, tied to her backpack with a four-leaf clover charm; Klee named it (B, FX). Her Dodoco-themed bombs are Jumpy Dumpty (SKd).
- Knights, Jean, confinement: Klee lives in the Knights' care; Jean (Acting Grand Master) promised Alice to look after her and sends her to solitary confinement after explosions (G8, B). Kaeya co-wrote the Favonius Survival Rulebook and covers for her (G8, B). Description: "a regular at the Knights of Favonius's confinement room" (GBd).
- Albedo: caretaker and big-brother figure, asked to treat her as family, cleans up her messes. His "Experiment in Progress" sign means come back later (G8, B). Said to have come to Mondstadt with a recommendation letter to Alice (SKd, unverified).
- Stormbearer Mountains: she helped Jean fight a hilichurl invasion with too many charges, blowing chunks off the mountains, hence the Red Knight rumor (G8, FX).
- Vision: received after a failed bomb experiment blew up her workshop (B).
- Fish blasting: she bombs lakes; Starfell Lake is the usual spot (FX, SKd). Event "Dodoco's Bomb-Tastic Adventure" (Oct 6 to 16, 2023) was a fish-blasting minigame (Siliconera via search).
- Starsnatch Cliff: only FX names it, as where Alice taught Klee to bring down structures with charges. B and G8 do not mention it. Unverified.
- Story quest: Klee's Trifolium quest, Act I (GGd titles: "Who is Mondstadt's Strongest Fighter?", "The Mystery of the Girl in Red's Treasure", "A Very Volatile Treasure"; B calls it "The Real Treasure"). Plot from B only: the Traveler helps recover a "treasure" that is a bomb, leading to a fight with an Abyss Mage; the box hidden at Windrise held Jumpy Dumpty and a diary.
- Other events (B only): publishing the Dodoco story in Inazuma with Albedo and Yae Miko ("Let's Go, Dodoco!" per Moegirl via search); a Golden Apple Archipelago adventure where the "Dodoking" is Alice, and Klee gives an island to Fischl; Mirage of the Sea in Sumeru; helping Razor find his parents and make wine at a festival.
- "Klee's Secret Base" and "the Adventurers": not found in any source. Treat as unconfirmed.

## 4. Relationships
- Family: Alice (mother), Kleiner (father), Albedo (older-brother figure), Durin ("Big Brother Durin") (G8, B).
- Caretakers: Jean ("a little bit scary"), Kaeya, Albedo (G8).
- Friends: Razor (best buddy, fixes her disasters), Amber (the best; Baron Bunny), Bennett (finds treasure with her), Diona (bombs and wine), Sucrose (offered a safer Jumpy Dumpty), Prune, Mona, Noelle (both ended up confined), Lisa (sneaks treats, refused her workshop), Lohen (crayons for snacks), Yoimiya (fellow explosion-banned friend) (G8, B, SKd).
- Diluc: "weird grown-up" who never smiles (G8). Varka: praises her escape skills (G8; the newest Knights lore).
- Fischl: received an island from Klee (B).

## 5. Places
- Mondstadt; Knights of Favonius HQ and solitary confinement room (G8, B); Starfell Lake and Starfell Valley (B, FX); Windrise (B); Whispering Woods (B); Stormbearer Mountains (G8); Starsnatch Cliff (FX only); Golden Apple Archipelago (B); Inazuma, Yae Publishing House (B); Gandharva Ville and Mirage of the Sea in Sumeru (B).

## 6. Name checks
- "Kaboom!": YES, the Normal Attack (GG, FX). GBd lists it as the skill, which disagrees with GG and FX.
- "Pounding Surprise": YES, A1 passive (GG, GBd, FX).
- "Sparkly Explosion": YES, C4 (GG, GBd, FX).
- "Nova Burst": YES, C5 (GG, GBd, FX).
- "Dodoco Tales": YES, but it is her 4-star event catalyst (Prima), not an ability, constellation or 5-star signature.
- "Jumpy Dumpty": YES, her Elemental Skill (GG, FX, GBd).
- "Sparks 'n' Splash": YES, her Elemental Burst (GG, FX, GBd, B).
