# Kokomi round 30: the fixed screen read as built; what a seat still reconstructs is a relic's unnamed hit, a folded number's base, and turn order; Slack Water's Dusk half went unwritten in both lanes

Written 2026-09-08, afternoon. Two blind Opus seats, natural draft, on
`0.2.3053+proto` (main `eb1ed6b5`), the first build with the twelve screen
rows of PR #455 and the lane watchdog (`EB-691`). Records:
`review/qa/kokomi-round-30-2026-09-08/opus-lane1-natural.md` (232 acts, act
1 cleared, floor 17 of act 2 on the budget) and `opus-lane2-natural.md`
(112 acts, died at 2 HP on the Bygone Effigy). Prototype stage, Guardrail 7.
The watchdog did not trip; neither lane needed it.

## 1. The hypothesis

"With FRONT marked, replaced bodies named, Dusk carry-outs under their own
heading, every reaction named in its beat and the one-use rider noted, a
seat reads each of those from the screen the first time it matters and
names no turn where it had to reconstruct one of them from a number."

Met on four of the five lines and unobserved on the fifth (no Dusk Plan was
written in either lane). What the seats did reconstruct is a different list,
§2.

## 2. The reads

**1. FRONT and where a Plan lands.** Lane 1 wrote Ambush while the front
body was about to die that turn, knowing from the panel that the target is
resolved at carry-out, and the Plan landed on the next body as the log
named: "I would not have risked that write without the line." Lane 2 fought
one body at a time and calls the rule "a whole class of doubt removed".

**2. New bodies.** Lane 1: the Gremlin Merc's split printed B and C, "two
fresh bodies rather than the Merc under a new name, so I did not waste a
turn assuming a heal"; the carry-out rows' "lost 10 HP, and died" gave the
overkill too.

**3. Dusk.** Not observed. Both seats held Slack Water on every turn it
came and played it face-up each time; lane 1 three times, lane 2 four,
same reason: the played Weak lands in the same enemy turn and the card also
deals 4. Lane 2 says the Dusk line "closed a decision rather than opening
one". No seat drafted Breakwater. The Dusk half is a crowd half, and both
lanes were single-body fights nearly throughout.

**4. Reactions named.** Live and used: "Vaporize on Sewer Clam, off Feint"
drove a fight-4 ordering (lane 1); "Swirl on Bygone Effigy, off Heizou" and
"Nothing reacted this turn" let lane 2 be sure the Casket's Hydro was not
reacting. One attribution wrong: a Vaporize "off Bake-Kurage" in a fight
with no Plan written, which was the Casket's answering strike (`EB-697`).

**5. The rider note.** Unobserved; neither seat drew a next-Attack rider.
**6. Tide Wall** paid 3 times 2 with two Plans held, counting itself, and
lane 2's count matched "exactly" (the round-26 watch item closes).
**7. Breakwater** unobserved again (never drafted in four lanes since pass
five).

**What the seats still reconstructed.** The Casket's 2 on every debuff
played from hand (named only on the Plan path, `EB-695`); a folded face's
base under Weak (`EB-700`); every Slow-multiplied number (folded into no
face, added to `EB-689`); whether end-of-turn effects resolve before the
enemies act (`EB-701`); Heizou's face under Shrink and its per-Swirl count
(`EB-696`); a 7-damage remainder lane 1 never sourced.

## 3. What this says

The twelve rows did what they were minted for, and the next reconstruction
list is shorter and different: one relic hit unnamed on one path, one base
number, one ordering sentence, and companion faces (Heizou, Ayaka, Sara)
whose text and effect disagree. The kit read is unchanged from rounds 26 to
29: write-or-play on every Plan card, "a Plan lands in whatever Block the
enemy is standing in" as the sentence that decides the most turns (lane 1
wrote no Plan against the boss and won on it), and a hand with no Plan card
has no decision in it (three of lane 1's eleven opening turns). That last
is the pool-density question round 26 raised and no pass has yet answered;
it is the next design question, not a screen one.
