6. WHAT THIS READ DOES NOT SAY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE §3 CONSTRAINT STANDS AND IS NOT DISCHARGED BY A CLEAN GRADE. No causal
role-versus-tag claim may be read out of a tier-0.5 number taken after Window
1. `is_on_plan_payoff` is literally `role == "payoff" and archetype in
card.archetypes`, and both fields are read again by the adaptive scorer, so a
drafted-world number after W1 cannot separate the role effect from the tag
effect. The attribution graded above holds for the CONNECTIVITY instrument
only. If such a claim ever matters, the honest repair is to split the window
and re-run, not to reason backwards from a mixed number.

NO THRESHOLD IS DERIVED AND NONE MAY BE. The canon pools sit at 100.0%
shared-hook share on both sides and the mod pools at 66.7-93.0%; whether that
gap is a defect or a design difference is the question §2.5 refuses to answer
from a static read, and this file does not answer it either.

NO STANDING BASELINE IS TAKEN OR OWED HERE. Under R207 a standing table is
published at a meaningful product milestone or when a pending decision needs
one, and no pending decision names one at this landing. The registered read
for this window is this file.

REGISTERED BLIND SPOTS ARE UNCHANGED and still apply (packet §2.6), plus the
Phase-2 read's G-1: a sheets-only instrument cannot see an activation window.
It cannot see a `role` conversion either -- which is this window's whole
finding about the instrument and NOT a finding about the window. W1's
sixteen role conversions are real edits with real drafting consequences; the
connectivity report is simply not the instrument that sees them, and the
per-card diff above is the proof rather than the excuse.
