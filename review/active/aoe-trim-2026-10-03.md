# AoE trim, Durin split, and the companion Powers

Status: OPEN, picks 3 to 6. Main session design, on [USER]'s direction
(2026-10-03).

## 1. Why

[USER]'s friend "felt like he saw a lot more aoe in our designs than usual."
The census (this session's scratchpad, `aoe-census.md`: every card in each
pool classified, base cards read from the game's code) bears it out.

| | Direct AoE damage | All AoE (adds random multi-hits, AoE debuffs, AoE engines) |
|---|---|---|
| Base five | 3 to 7 | 6 to 10 (Ironclad 9, Silent 10, Defect 6, Necrobinder 8, Regent 10) |
| Klee | 8 | 17 |
| Kokomi | 14 (5 only inside a Plan) | 19 |
| Furina | 7 | 11 |
| Varka | 6 | 12 |

Elemental reactions (Overloaded, Swirl spread) add AoE the census does not
count, so the real gap is wider.

[USER]: "it's fine for one of Kokomi's strengths to be 'you get AoE but at a
delay' so I am fine with some excess there, but the others don't really
justify it." On Klee: "I don't think she needs a nerf right now, so we'll need
to think of what to do with the design space to balance the loss of AoE."

**The bar:** Klee, Furina and Varka at 10 AoE cards or fewer and 7 direct or
fewer. Kokomi unchanged. A starter card is never changed (Jumpy Dumpty,
Windbound Execution stay and count).

## 5. Durin, split in two

[USER]: "one for his skill and one for his burst ... I think the two-in-one
concept is neat." Today's card, a Rare Power at 2 that deals 10 to ALL and
then picks a form, is the one Power in any pool that is a full card plus a
power (`power-onplay-census.md`: base 12 of 98 Powers do something on play,
ours 1 of 77 plus Durin). Names from his character sheet.

- **Durin: Binary Form** (U Attack, 1): "Choose one, then draw 1 card.
  [gold]White[/gold]: Deal 6 [8] Pyro damage to ALL enemies.
  [gold]Dark[/gold]: Deal 4 [5] Pyro damage to an enemy 3 times." Pommel
  Strike (C, 9 and draw 1) is the yardstick.
- **Durin: Principle of Purity** (R Power, 2): "At the start of your turn,
  deal 4 [6] Pyro damage to a random enemy. Choose one for the combat.
  [gold]White[/gold]: Enemies take 50% [75%] more damage from Elemental
  Reactions. [gold]Dark[/gold]: Your Pyro damage deals 4 [6] more."
  - White is the team form ([USER]: "Agreed on White form"). Today's code
    boosts only the holder's reactions; it becomes every player's, as the
    text already says.
  - Dark was "Pyro Attacks that react deal 8 more", which loses to White on
    any hit above 16 to 24 and is near dead for Klee, whose Pyro is Bombs.
    [USER]: "3 extra damage might not be enough for a Rare power at 2
    energy. The analogy I see is Silent's shiv power giving +4 (+6) as a 1
    cost Uncommon" (Accuracy). It takes Accuracy's number; the 1 Energy more
    pays for the turn-start hit and the White option.

The Mondstadt pool grows by one row.

## 6. Companion Powers and timed Skills

Six companion Skills last 2 or 3 turns (Lightning Rose, Melody Loop, Herald
of Frost, Signature Mix, Crimson Ooyoroi, Aurous Blaze). [USER]: "Agreed that
the companion skills can stay." Diona's Signature Mix stays as is (Exhaust;
[USER] takes it every solo run).

Yoimiya's Aurous Blaze ("For 2 turns, whenever the enemy takes damage from a
non-Attack card, deal 6 Pyro damage to ALL enemies"; [USER]: "I don't think
I've ever drafted Yoimiya's") becomes: "Deal 6 Pyro damage. For 2 turns,
whenever you play a Skill, deal 3 Pyro damage to that enemy." It does
something on the turn it is played and leaves the AoE count.
