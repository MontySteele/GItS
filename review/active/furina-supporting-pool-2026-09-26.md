# Furina: the supporting pool, 49 to 78

**What this is.** The second half of the Stage's pool expansion, which the Guest Cast paper (`furina-guest-batch-2026-09-25.md`) promised: about 30 cards across its seven families. [USER]: "we need to make sure that the supporting pool has enough depth that the overall effect is not just 'do 5 damage of x element.'" Their run's verdict was "the core concept is sound", so this batch adds depth rather than changing rules.

**Where the pool is** (census, 2026-09-26): 49 draftable cards. That is 37 Stage rows and 12 shipped cards that survive under the arm: An Invitation, Command Performance, Commanding Gaze, Courtroom Drama, Crashing Waves, Duet, The Guest List, Quick Change, Singer of Many Waters, Undercurrent, Stage Combat and The Witness Stand.

The rarity target is Klee's shape at 78 (24 / 36 / 18). Most of the batch's weight goes to Rares and Attacks, because those are what the pool lacks. Numbers are sized with the balance review's yardsticks: an act is about 0.6 Energy, and a Fanfare about 2 to 3 damage.

## Design notes

- **Nothing here is "a guest deals 5 of element X".** Each new card moves Fanfare, moves a seat, bends the fade, cashes a seat, or reads a reaction. The two new guests each have a job the eight lack:
  - **Lyney** rotates the stage by himself.
  - **Escoffier** is a star who feeds the cast instead of hitting one enemy.
- **Showstopper** is the first card that cashes the shield. It is a Rare because it bends the idea that the front is only a shield.
- **Two Rares bend a core rule** (see "Rares bend, never remove"):
  - **Sold Out** adds a fourth seat. Full House's "all three seats" becomes "every seat", and the middle seat becomes two middle seats, both of which fade.
  - **Eternal Applause** turns the fade off for a deck that pays 2 Energy for it.
- **The Solo path** grows from Improvised Number and Between Acts into a real archetype: no performers, and Furina carries the fight herself. It is the Stage's opposite, and the kit-checklist asks every kit for one off-axis path.
- **Watch items the batch touches:**
  - Season Tickets and Tide of Applause add Fanfare every turn, which the Rapt Audience lesson says to size low. Both give 2, the same as the fixed Rapt Audience.
  - Regina of All Waters stacks with Full House. That is a Rare combo, and the first seat round should look for it.
- **Art.** Lyney and Escoffier need card art, a badge and a stage body, built the same way as the other eight guests. The other 27 go through the usual art pass.
