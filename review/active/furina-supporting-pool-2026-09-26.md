# Furina: the supporting pool, 49 to 78

**What this is.** The second half of the Stage's pool expansion, which the Guest Cast paper (`furina-guest-batch-2026-09-25.md`) promised: about 30 cards across its seven families. [USER]: "we need to make sure that the supporting pool has enough depth that the overall effect is not just 'do 5 damage of x element.'" Their run's verdict was "the core concept is sound", so this batch adds depth rather than changing rules.

**Where the pool is** (census, 2026-09-26): 49 draftable cards. That is 37 Stage rows and 12 shipped cards that survive under the arm: An Invitation, Command Performance, Commanding Gaze, Courtroom Drama, Crashing Waves, Duet, The Guest List, Quick Change, Singer of Many Waters, Undercurrent, Stage Combat and The Witness Stand.

The rarity target is Klee's shape at 78 (24 / 36 / 18). Most of the batch's weight goes to Rares and Attacks, because those are what the pool lacks. Numbers are sized with the balance review's yardsticks: an act is about 0.6 Energy, and a Fanfare about 2 to 3 damage.

## The pre-build sweep (2026-09-26)

[USER] asked, before the build, for "a pre-emptive deduping and balance sweep to look for any outliers", reading the 29 as if built beside the 49. What it found and changed:

**Duplicates, replaced with a new job.**

| Cut | Why | Replaced by |
|---|---|---|
| Farewell Tour (back Bows, 2 damage per Fanfare) | Bravura already spends the back seat for 3 per point, and a performer emptied by a Spend Bows (rule 7). Farewell Tour was Bravura at a worse rate. | **Counterclaim**: an Attack that pays off letting the shield take hits, the Stage's own defence. |
| Reprise (back acts twice) | Bis! with the seat changed. | **Star Billing**: the guest deck had no payoff of its own. |
| One More Bow (front Bows without leaving, draw 1) | One act for 1 Energy, beside Bis! (two) and Grand Finale (all). | **Intermission**: the Bow cash-out that pays in cards. Final Bow pays Block and Bravura pays damage. |
| Lavish Production (Spend up to 6, AoE) | The fourth AoE cash-out after Tidal Flourish, Let the People Rejoice and Showstopper, and it needed a new variable-Spend control. | **Da Capo**: the Bow deck (Thunderous Applause, A Five-Century Act) had no Attack that grows with it. |
| Lingering Note (0: back +3, no fade this turn) | 3 Fanfare for 0 Energy beat Warm Reception (1 Energy: 3 and draw 1), and Held Applause already skips the fade for the turn. | **Stage Whisper**: moves Fanfare from the bank to the shield without moving bodies. |

**Outliers, resized.**

- **Regina of All Waters** doubled every act, both damage and Block, all the time. That made Arkhe Alignment (choose one, each turn) strictly worse, and with Full House it quadrupled the stage. It is now the Hydro engine for family 6: all enemies are soaked each turn, so every guest's elemental act reacts. It watches for Tide of Applause and Courtroom Drama.
- **Eternal Applause** turned the fade off. "Rares bend, never remove": it now raises the fade's threshold from 5 to 10. A bank deck can hold twice as much, but it still pays to hold more.
- **Escoffier** fed 4 Fanfare to a full stage while paying 3, and dealt 5 to ALL, which is more than Neuvillette at the same cost. Her damage drops to 3 Cryo to ALL, so her job is feeding and a Cryo tag.
- **Showstopper**: at 3 per point from the one seat that never fades, a 15-Fanfare front made 45 damage to ALL. It now pays 2 per point, Let the People Rejoice's rate, and still leaves Furina unshielded.
- **Grand Deluge** gave each performer 2 per reaction. With three soaked enemies and a full stage, that was 18 Fanfare. It now gives 2 each, once, if any reaction triggers.
- **Grand Finale** at 2 Energy was a worse Tutti! unless you had Bow readers. At 1 (upg 0) it is the Bow deck's once-a-fight burst.
- **Oratrice's Verdict** was blank in any one-enemy fight. It now draws 1.
- **Lyney's** swap read "swap with your front performer", which means nothing when he is the front. It now swaps the front and back performers, whichever seat he is in.
- **Undertow** fed the back, like Cheered On. It now feeds the front, so the Common feeds the bank and the Uncommon feeds the shield.
- **Intermission** would have been a 0-cost loop with two Thunderous Applause and A Five-Century Act (each Bow draws, the performer returns). It costs 1.

**Kept, and watched in the seat rounds.**

- **Season Tickets** (2 a turn), **Tide of Applause** and **Regina of All Waters** together. That is the reaction printer.
- **Sold Out** with **Full House**. "All three seats" becomes every seat, so Full House needs four.
- **Revolving Stage** with **A Rapt Audience**. The rotation keeps a fresh shield in front.
- **Gala Premiere** on a full stage makes three Bows, one per summon (rule 3).

## Design notes

- **Nothing here is "a guest deals 5 of element X".** Each new card moves Fanfare, moves a seat, bends the fade, cashes a seat, or reads a reaction. The two new guests each have a job the eight lack:
  - **Lyney** rotates the stage by himself.
  - **Escoffier** is a star who feeds the cast instead of hitting one enemy.
- **Showstopper** is the first card that cashes the shield. It is a Rare because it bends the idea that the front is only a shield.
- **Two Rares bend a core rule** (see "Rares bend, never remove"):
  - **Sold Out** adds a fourth seat. Full House's "all three seats" becomes "every seat", and the middle seat becomes two middle seats, both of which fade.
  - **Eternal Applause** raises the fade's threshold.
- **The Solo path** grows from Improvised Number and Between Acts into a real archetype: no performers, and Furina carries the fight herself. It is the Stage's opposite, and the kit-checklist asks every kit for one off-axis path.
- **Art.** Lyney and Escoffier need card art, a badge and a stage body, built the same way as the other eight guests. The other 27 go through the usual art pass.
