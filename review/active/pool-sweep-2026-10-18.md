# Pool sweep after the 2026-10-18 revisions

Asked for after the co-op night: "a general sweep over the card pools looking
for any bad picks or undertuned options after the recent revisions." Two
census packets (Yae; Kokomi and both companion pools) compared each card with
its nearest base card (decompiled numbers), the seat records (Yae rounds 9 to
11, Kokomi v3) and the uploaded runs (only 3 runs in 14 days, so pick counts
are anecdotes). This page records what changes and why. Yae and Kokomi are
Prototype kits, so their numbers are Claude's call; the companions also show
up in Klee, Varka and Furina runs, so those are picks.

## 1. Yae (built)

| Card | Was | Now | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Forbidden Art: Daisesshou (R, 0) | 9 [12] to ALL per Sakura that withers | 3 [4] to ALL per **Level** the withered Sakura had | [USER]: withering the shrine "resets all progress you've made towards leveling them up." It now cashes the Levels in. Three Level-1 Sakura: 9 to ALL for 0; three at Level 3: 27. |
| Eternity (R power) | 3 [2] Energy, no longer wither | 2 [1]; no longer wither, and Levels count twice | [USER]: 'may need a rethink to be better as a Rare level payoff.' Fights end before timers matter, so the no-wither half alone did nothing; doubling Levels makes it the Level deck's Rare payoff, and no-wither keeps the Levels it grows. |
| Scattered Blossoms (U power, 1) | draw 1 per wither | draw 2 per wither | Withers are rare; named dead in R10 and R11. Pairs with Fallen Petals and Daisesshou. |
| Fallen Petals (U, 0) | +1 Energy [and draw 1] | +1 Energy and draw 1 [2] | It withers the oldest Sakura, the one with the most Levels; net zero Energy for that was a pass (2/2). Draw 1 for a 0-cost card keeps hand size level (Prepared's rule). |
| Petal Storm (U, 1) | 3 [4] to ALL per Sakura | 4 [5] | 0 to 3 damage below a full shrine; Thunderclap is 4 to ALL. |
| Kitsune Veil (U, 1) | 5 [8] Block | 8 [11] | Only two cards plant a Kitsune Sakura, so the floor must be a fair Block card. |
| Kitsune's Shelter (U, 1) | 7 [10] | 9 [12] | Same: Pommel Strike is 9. |

Watched, unchanged: Offertory Box (+3 Energy at a full shrine, Exhausts, a
Rare), Sakura Grove (stronger since Levels), Hide Behind the Shrine.

## 3. Companions (picks 1, 2, 4, 5 ruled (a), 2026-10-18; pick 3 open)

These cards are offered to every kit, Balance kits included.

1. **Jean, Lion's Fang** (Mondstadt R power, 2 [1]): "if none of your Bombs
   went off last turn, gain 8 Block and draw 1 card." Only Klee has Bombs,
   so for everyone else it is 8 Block and a card every turn.
   (a, default) 6 Block and draw 1. (b) leave it.
   **Ruled (a), 2026-10-18.**
2. **Nicole, Revelation** (Mondstadt R power, 2): 5 Block each turn, and 2
   Strength if you ended last turn with Block. A Block deck makes that Demon
   Form (3 Energy, 3 Strength) for 2 Energy with Block on top.
   (a, default) 1 Strength. (b) leave it.
   **Ruled (a), 2026-10-18.**
3. **Raiden, Baleful Omen** (Inazuma U, 1): ALL enemies lose 2 [3] Strength
   this turn. Piercing Wail, a Common, is 6.
   (a, default) 4 [6]. (b) leave it.
4. **Thoma, Crimson Ooyoroi** (Inazuma U power, 1): keep up to 6 [9] Block.
   Kokomi's own Rare keeps 10 [15]; a Colorless Uncommon should sit a bit
   above a kit card, not under.
   (a, default) 8 [12]. (b) leave it.
   **Ruled (a), 2026-10-18.**
5. **Heizou, Windmuster Kick** (Inazuma U, 2): 12 [16], Swirl, draw 1.
   Predator is 15 and 2 cards.
   (a, default) 14 [18]. (b) leave it.
   **Ruled (a), 2026-10-18.**

Noted, no change proposed: Gorou's War Banner and Itto's Ushi give
repeatable Strength (watch in Kokomi seats); Albedo's Dust of Purification
is a Bomb card that is worse than Tidecleanse for anyone but Klee (Klee's
pick to make, not a bug).
