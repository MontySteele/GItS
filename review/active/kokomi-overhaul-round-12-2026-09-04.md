intent was Defend; the queued Kurage's Oath+ carried out at the start of
turn two into that Block, moved HP 35 to 35, laid its Hydro aura, and the

`EB-411`'s unprinted timing rule seen from the log side: the card you are
told to buy a turn ahead is the card a Defend intent deletes, and neither
the tip nor the log says so.

**The fold is inconsistent on her faces too.** Under Weak 1 on one screen,
Strike printed 4 and dealt 4; Undertow printed 7 and dealt 5, and the seat
chose the turn's plays off the stale number. Vigor 8, "your next Attack",
printed onto Kurage's Oath+, a Skill, as "Deal 11 to ALL" (`EB-441`). The
Furina seat of the same afternoon found the same shape on three faces
(`EB-438`); the fold has more than one door.

**The Plan tip's aim sentence did not land in fifteen readings.** The
`EB-380` sentence ("front non-Minion, or ALL, Minions too") sits at the
135-character ceiling and the seat could not parse it, while the Bake-Kurage
panel and the Reaction preview line were "the clearest text in the kit"
(`EB-442`: the rule goes on the panel in two sentences, where there is no
ceiling).

**The deck is two cards deep.** "Roughly a third of my turns had no Plan
card in hand and were pure Strike and Defend with no rejected
alternative." Round 11 counted the same; R254 ruled starter density STANDS
and this run does not reopen it (§4). Where the engine fired it was good:
the Smoggy hand in fight five, where The Moon's Power slipped a one-Skill
cap, was the best turn of the act.
