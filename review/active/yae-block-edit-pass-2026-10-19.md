# Yae: Block like Defect's Frost, and the Edit count

[USER], 2026-10-19: "let's do a pass over Yae. I do think that we should
model her block after Defect - how accessible are the frost orbs ... vs
Yae's Kitsune Sakura? ... My hunch is that we just give far fewer Sakura
compared to Defect's Frost orb generation. I also suspect that we're printing
too many edit cards that give too many edits, compared to cards to consume
those edits."

## 1. What the numbers say

**The gap is real and it is Block per turn, not fight length.** Seat round
2026-10-19 (`review/records/kokomi-yae-round-2026-10-19.md`) against the base
five since 2026-10-05: Yae's normal fights last 3 turns, the same as base, but
she gains 1.7 Block a turn in act 1 (base 2.5) and 3.7 in act 2 (base 6.0),
1.8 against elites (base 4.6). She loses 12% of her HP a normal act-1 fight
(base 8.8%). On 48% of the turns she opened with a Block card in hand and an
attack coming, she gained no Block at all (Kokomi 9%).

**Her flat Block cards are not the problem.** Median Block per Energy is 7.5
(10.5 upgraded) on her 8 fixed-Block cards against Defect's 5.5 (7.5) on 11.
Both kits play the same Defend.

**The hunch is right: Kitsune is far rarer than Frost.**

| | Defect: Frost | Yae: Kitsune Sakura |
|---|---|---|
| Sure makers | 6 of 80 (Coolheaded C, Cold Snap C, Chill U, Glacier U, Ice Lance R, Rainbow R) | 2 of 78 (Shrine Maiden's Guard C, Guardian of Narukami R); 2 more only on a condition |
| Block a turn, one at base | 2 + Focus | 2 + Level |
| Three standing, base | 6 | 9 (Level 1) |
| Slots | 3, shared with Lightning | 3, shared with Thunder and Foxfire |

Defect's engine makes damage AND Block; Yae's makes damage, because every
generic "Plant a Sakura" plants Thunder. A Kitsune is worth about 12 Block over
its life at Level 1; a Frost orb held and evoked, about 13. The unit is fine;
the access is a third of Defect's.

**The Edit count is balanced overall but skewed at Common.** 11 drafted
producers against 12 consumers, but Commons are 4 producers to 1 consumer, and
6 of the 7 Annotations pay for themselves (Reprint replays, Footnote draws,
Royalties gives Energy), so Editing needs no payoff card. The act-3 strength
is real: act-3 damage 1.45 of base, fights 3.05 turns against 4.79 (round 11),
mostly Reprint on Mikage Thunder. The loop risk is Footnote or Royalties on a
**0-cost** card that a recycler brings back. No seat has run one yet.

## 2. Changes (built)

Kitsune to Defect's access: five sure makers, three at Common or Uncommon.

| Card | Was | Now | Frost parallel |
|---|---|---|---|
| Twin Sakura (C, 1) | Plant 2 Sakura [+4 Block] | Plant a Sakura and a Kitsune Sakura [+4 Block] | Coolheaded |
| Cliffhanger (C, 1) → **Kitsune Lantern** | 8 damage, Edit a card: Deadline Extension | Deal 7 [10] Electro damage. Plant a Kitsune Sakura. | Cold Snap |
| Sakura Canopy (U, 1) | 4 [5] Block for each Sakura | **2 Energy:** Gain 6 [9] Block. Plant 2 Kitsune Sakura. | Glacier |
| Shrine Maiden's Guard (C, 1) | 6 [9] Block, plant a Kitsune | unchanged | |
| Guardian of Narukami (R, 2) | 14 Block, Kitsune at Level 3 | unchanged | Ice Lance |

Cliffhanger's Deadline Extension (Retain) was the one Annotation that needs a
payoff card, so it is the Common producer that goes; Commons become 3
producers to 1 consumer.

Footnote and Royalties stay as printed ([USER], 2026-10-19: "I don't want
to block Footnote or Royalties from printing on [0] cost cards - Royalties is a
rare payoff, and Footnote still requires you expend a card to apply the
footnote. We may want to make footnote a 1-use effect like Reprint if it's
proving too strong."). If Footnote proves too strong, the fallback is a
once-a-combat Footnote like Reprint.
