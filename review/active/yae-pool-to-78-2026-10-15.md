## 1. What the pool lacks

Her pool is 43 cards (`docs/prototype-surface.yaml`, `character: yae`): 15
Common, 18 Uncommon, 10 Rare; 12 Attacks, 23 Skills, 8 Powers. The target is
78 at 20 / 38 / 20, so 35 more: 5 Common, 20 Uncommon, 10 Rare.

**The engine is on Skills.** Five of her six cards that plant a Sakura are
Skills (only Foxfire Sweep is an Attack), and eleven of her thirteen Edits are
Skills (only Margin Note and Bestseller are Attacks). Three enemies punish
that: the one-Skill-a-turn enemy (your first run), Test Subject's Enrage (+2
Strength per Skill; it beat two seats), and Infested Prism (every Skill costs
HP). Her Attacks are 28% of the pool; Kokomi's are 40% and Varka's 35%.

**Energy.** Proofread+ at 0 was every seat's happiest draw, and you said "if
only I could get 3 more upgrades". Two of the new cards give Energy; one Power
makes an Annotated card free once a turn.

**What the batch does not add.** You named two worries. It adds two Edits
(both single Edits on Attacks) and no card that Publishes or pays per Edit.
That does not mean neither loop grows: new Energy, draw and cost cuts
(Offertory Box, Editorial Priority, Late Night Reading) feed the existing Edit
loops, and Editorial Priority with a Published card is a real economy engine.
They are prototyped and watched, not excluded; balance waits for the full pool.

**Lore** (checked against a sourced census, scratch
`lore-census/yae-lore-census-2026-10-15.md`, second-hand sources only).
Daisesshou, Kagura's Verity, Narukami and the six constellation names are
hers, spelled as in game. The Kitsune Saiguu was the Guuji before her and her
mentor. Spiritfox is from her title, Spiritfox Sin-Eater. Eternity is Ei's
ideal. An offertory box stands at every shrine. Two first-draft readings were
wrong and are gone: Ioroi is the Saiguu's tanuki friend, not Yae's prankster,
and no source has Yae "borrowing" a festival budget.
**Stellar Conduct** is your parked nod (proposal pick 3): any reaction, not
only Superconduct, so it does not need a Cryo partner to be live.

**Foxfire and targeted strikes.** Sacred Spark, Narukami Judgment, Saiguu's
Lesson and Fox's Verdict make a Sakura strike a chosen enemy. A Foxfire
Sakura doing so hits that one enemy once; only its own end-of-turn act hits
ALL. Fox's Verdict therefore gives each striking Sakura one hit on each Weak
enemy, never a sweep per enemy.

**To watch on the full pool:** The Shrine's Sacred Shade (automatic Block may
weaken the choice to keep a Kitsune slot) and Editorial Priority (above).

## Picks

1. **The batch** (sec.2): the engine also on Attacks, 35 cards to 78.
   - **(a) Default:** as written.
   - (b) As written, but you name cards to change or drop.
   - (c) Fewer Attacks: keep her a Skill kit and accept the bad matchups.
2. **A Sakura keeps its Annotations.** Today an Edit replaces a card's
   unpublished Annotation; Evergreen Charm alone kept a Sakura's Red Ink, which
   is the sentence you asked about.
   - **(a) Default:** on a Sakura (not on cards), distinct Annotations coexist:
     Red Ink, Fox Fire and Deadline Extension can all sit on one Sakura. Exact
     readings:
     - A second copy of one it already has does nothing (no second +3).
     - An Edit that changes nothing is not an Edit: it does not count for
       Bestseller, Print Run or Serialization. This is the guard against
       "spam Edits to win".
     - Annotations survive a Tenko refresh and a same-kind refresh, and go when
       that Sakura withers or is replaced.
     - Evergreen Charm loses its sentence.
   - (b) Keep "replaces" everywhere, Evergreen Charm included.
3. **The Sakura display.** Today the shrine is read from tooltips.
   - **(a) Default:** a row of slots beside Yae, one per shrine slot (3, or 5
     with Sakura Channeling); empty slots shown, so a full shrine is plain.
     Each Sakura: its kind by colour and icon (strike, Kitsune, Foxfire), its
     Level as the big number, its turns left as pips (a number past three, an
     infinity mark under Eternity), a small mark per Annotation. The oldest is
     outlined: it acts first and is the one a full-shrine plant replaces.
     Hovering a planting card previews it: "refreshes this Sakura" or
     "replaces this Sakura". Hover on a slot gives the one-line rules.
   - (b) One compact panel (like the Orb row) with Level and turns as text.
   - (c) Mock up both as screenshots first, then choose.
4. **What follows.**
   - **(a) Default:** build, then five seats on the full pool, then your run.
     Balance (edit loops, Publish per elite) is judged on that full pool.
   - (b) Your run first, then seats.
