## 1. What the pool lacks

Her pool is 43 cards (`docs/prototype-surface.yaml`, `character: yae`): 15
Common, 18 Uncommon, 10 Rare; 12 Attacks, 23 Skills, 8 Powers. The target is
78 at 20 / 38 / 20, so 35 more: 5 Common, 20 Uncommon, 10 Rare.

**The engine is on Skills.** Five of her six cards that plant a Sakura are
Skills (only Foxfire Sweep is an Attack), and eleven of her thirteen Edits are
Skills (only Margin Note and Bestseller are Attacks). Three enemies punish
that: the one-Skill-a-turn enemy (your first run), Test Subject's Enrage (+2
Strength per Skill; it beat two seats), and Infested Prism (every Skill costs
HP). Her Attacks are 28% of the pool; Kokomi's are 40% and Varka's 35%.

**Energy.** Proofread+ at 0 was every seat's happiest draw, and you said "if
only I could get 3 more upgrades". Two of the new cards give Energy; one Power
makes an Annotated card free once a turn.

**What the batch does not add.** You named two worries. It adds two Edits
(both single Edits on Attacks) and no card that Publishes or pays per Edit, so
neither loop grows while the pool fills; balance waits for the full pool.
