Both runs ended at the act-1 boss. The same build's Varka and Furina seats

## What played well

- **Planned turns were the best turns in both runs.** Seat 1: a planned
  Kurage's Oath+ (10 to 12 to ALL) with Flank hitting every enemy that
  intended to attack took the three-Raider fight. Seat 2: the Terror Eel fight,
  timing a 49-damage turn around its stun threshold.
- **Kurage's Oath+ was the best card,** because its AoE scales with Strength.

## What did not

- **The two halves read as both.** Both seats planned Kurage's Oath expecting
  its Block too and took hits at 0 Block; seat 2 hit it again on Ambush (no
  Vulnerable) and Tidal Screen (no Block). The face prints the Plan line under
  the now-line with nothing saying "or".
- **The delay walks into hidden turns.** Soul Fysh is Intangible the turn
  after it Empowers; the Plans written that turn landed for 1 and 1 (Nip 9,
  Ambush+ 19). Nothing on screen warned. A head start that the next turn can
  void costs more than it buys.
- **Defence ran out.** Seat 1 had three Defends and Kurage's Oath for Block
  and was at 10 HP by floor 15; the rewards rarely offered Block. Seat 2 lost
  to Beckon statuses (eight in the draw pile, a final hand of four) plus
  Vulnerable, with no way to exhaust statuses or heal.
- **The Casket is one cash-out.** Open the Casket exhausts; seat 2 reached 10
  at the boss and the count was dead weight after round 4. Seat 1's never
  passed 4.
- **Skill taxes shut her off.** Smoggy ("1 Skill per turn") nearly ended seat
  2's deck, since nearly every Plan card is a Skill.

## What to change

1. **The face says "or".** The Plan line reads "Or plan:" (or an equivalent
   the starter can carry), on every Plan card. A text change to every Plan
   card, the starter included: [USER]'s pick.
2. **A Kokomi paper on the delay itself,** not numbers: Plans that buy what
   only waiting can buy (they read the next turn's intents, draw or survivors
   when they resolve), so a hidden turn is information, not a loss. The
   intent-reading cards (Tide Wall, Flank) are the ones seats praise.
3. **The Casket:** watch whether Open the Casket should come back (Retain
   instead of Exhaust, or a second card) once the delay paper lands.
4. **Defence and status answers** for her pool, read alongside the delay
   paper rather than as a Block patch.
