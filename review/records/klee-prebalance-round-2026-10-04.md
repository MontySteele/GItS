## What played well

- Growing Bombs and cashing them past a stun line or onto a sleeping boss.
  Lane 1 dealt about 100 in one turn to the Matriarch (Boom Badge, Blasting
  Powder). Lane 2 Stunned the Terror Eel with Chain Fuse, Countdown and the
  Retained Ka-pow!.
- Spending Sparks against Energy (Boom Badge, Stoke the Fuse, Bottomless
  Bag, Quick Fuse) read as one small bank with real claims on it.
- An exact lethal at 3 HP: Boom Badge, then Stoke the Fuse on a Mine, then
  Sizzle doubled to 38 (lane 1, Louse Progenitor).
- Jumpy Dumpty Mk.III setting up Tinder Toss or Quick Fuse: the Louse
  Progenitor went from 117 to 43 in one turn (lane 2).

## What did not

- **Set off was read as global** by three of the four seats. It sets off the
  targeted enemy's Bombs only, so Bombs on another enemy stayed up (lane 1:
  Ka-pow! on one Slug, and Sizzle leaving a Bomb 24 standing). Fixed: the
  face now says "Set off the enemy." (PR after this round).
- **The Mine looked broken twice and was not.** The game log shows one Slug
  was Gooping (a Debuff, no attack) and the Eel's Mine had already been set
  off by the seat's own Sizzle. Fixed in the tip: "Any Set off spends it
  too."
- **Thinner defence in act 2**, the same as w19: Defend 5 and Breastplate 8
  against attacks of 14 to 24. That is a balance question for the Balance
  stage, not a broken card.
- NEVER AGAIN: Bottomless Bag (it spent the only Spark and stranded Tinder
  Toss), Up in Smoke! (a Dazed for a Weak), and Clumsy (an event's curse).
  None of these is a card defect.

## What to change

- Done this round: the Set off face and the Mine tip.
- Backlog: Treasure Map gives no warning when no Set off card is in the
  discard pile; the play log's "taken while it resolved; a reaction never
  hits you" line, where the 5 was Thorns, reads as a contradiction.
- **Nothing critical.** Klee moves to Balance, as [USER] ruled.
