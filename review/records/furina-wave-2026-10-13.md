## Where her damage comes from

**The Spend is the payoff, and it is big.** Let the People Rejoice at 54
Fanfare dealt 81 to ALL; Bravura+ at 36 dealt 108; Rising Applause+ landed 61
to 68 in three lanes. Three seats independently named taking hits on purpose
to bank Fanfare as the kit's best idea ("an unusual feeling", seat 1). That is
the brief's "swings her HP violently but gets rewarded", working as written.

**Both styles play.** Prima-donna decks (seats 2, 3, 4, 4b) won on Spend
turns; guest decks (seat 5) won on setup turns (Lyney, Tutti!, Wriothesley's
Cryo on every Drain); the winner mixed them and leaned on reaction poison.

**The Drain floor is not the problem.** Drain read as free early (it returns
after combat) and as a real trade at 20 to 30 HP, with "Not enough HP"
blocking burst at 6 HP. That is the intended tension. Raising the buffer from
1/4 would make the early game freer, not fix anything seen here. No change.

**What loses runs is Block, not damage.** Five of six deaths came on a hand
with no Block card. Only the winner drafted Block that scales (The Show Must
Go On, Hold the Stage); the others leaned on Gentilhomme Usher alone, and Hold
the Stage was skipped twice. Before prescribing more Block, a census of her
Block against the base five's goes into the pool review.

## What did not

- **Turn 1 of fight 1 was no decision in all six runs.** Rising Applause at 0
  Fanfare reads as "a worse Defend", and nothing says lost HP becomes Fanfare
  until the page prints a number on turn 2.
- **Drain buffer and Drain line confused four seats:** two numbers for one
  rule. Lyney's Drain was refused at the line with no message.
- **Cards that did nothing without their condition:** Fountain of Lucine
  (never again: repay with nothing drained), Soothing Waters ("Repays 0"),
  Tutti! with no guest, Rising Applause at 0. Standing Ovation was called
  strictly worse than Rejoice or Bravura+; Packed House "never won a
  decision".
- **Never taken when offered:** Encore!, Lynette (four offers), Leading Lady;
  Charlotte was skipped eight times and taken twice. Bis! appears in no record.
