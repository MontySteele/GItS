# Seat round: Varka's new starter (2026-10-03)

The starter change (#868: Four Winds' Ascension 2 cost, Retain, 10 [13];
Windbound Execution 0 cost, one target, 4 [6]) and the Oath-source line
(#867, #869) are a rule change and new text. Build 0.2.4306. Two Sonnet
seats, one act per seat with a handoff, ascension 0, on the same two seeds
as the rebalance round (`varka-rebalance-round-2026-10-03.md`), so the two
rounds compare run for run. Raw records are gitignored, in this session's
scratchpad under `v2-lane1` and `v2-lane2`.

## What played well

- **The starter turn works.** Fight 1 turn 1 on lane 1: Lisa, then the free
  Windbound Swirl, then Four Winds' Ascension, with the retained Ascension
  making a kill the next turn. Windbound was no seat's NEVER AGAIN, against
  four of four last round.
- **Retain made Ascension a decision.** Lane 1, act 2: "the Four Winds
  hold-or-fire call: fire now for 20 or wait for 70 but lose a turn of
  damage." Lane 2 held it a turn for more Oath. Hits of 69, 73 and 74 at
  Oath 20 to 33.
- **The Oath line is read.** Lane 2's act 1 seat quoted "+1 Cryo Oath" and the
  Fang's Ascension from the page; no seat said Oath came from nowhere.
- Block to a printed number (Rock's Imbalanced, the Crab's intents),
  sequencing aura refreshes between Swirls, and Thundering Verdict as an X
  finisher were the real decisions in every act.

## What did not

- **Turns with no Knight.** "Three Strikes and no Knight in hand, no Oath, no
  Four Winds" (lane 1, fight 3 turn 1). Windbound as a free 4 is fine there,
  but the start still turns on drawing the Knight.
- **Windbound fades by act 3** ("4 damage never changed a turn"), as a
  starter card should; nobody wanted it removed.
- NEVER AGAIN, one each: Crosswind (rarely Swirls), Jean — Wind Companion
  (needs a fresh aura that is usually spent), Oath of the Knights (1-2 Block
  early), Static Field (dead under Fiddle), Dawn Wind's March (2 cost, 3
  Block per Oath gain), and the Clumsy curse.
- Act 3 HP: both runs reached the boss low (11 and single digits), the kit
  having no healing; the Ancients' full heals carried both.

## Next

Ascension a timing decision, and both seeds went further. No pick comes
out of this round. Card notes for Varka's next pass: Crosswind, Jean —
Wind Companion and Dawn Wind's March were each the weakest card of a seat.
