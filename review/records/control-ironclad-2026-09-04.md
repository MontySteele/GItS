# The control: one base Ironclad through the same seats and harness

Written 2026-09-04. R250 pick 4 asked for one base-game run by the same
blind Opus seats, on the same lane and harness that produced the three
overnight clears, before those clears are read as the kits' strength. This
is that run. It is a Prototype-stage read under Guardrail-7: a floor on what
the harness can do, not a measurement, and one run is a direction, not a
rate.

## 1. The run in one paragraph

Claude Opus, lane 1, base Ironclad, **Ascension 0**, seed `MYYWMW9VDSV0`,
173 actions (`review/qa/control-ironclad-2026-09-04/opus-act1.md`). Two
rests to full, the four-Gardener Elite won at 51 of 80, then the Waterfall
Giant: the seat killed it on turn 8 at 12 HP and died to its Steam Eruption
death blow for 33 against 10 Block. The seat saw the clock from turn 2 and
lost to it anyway: "I could see it coming for five turns." The run ended on
floor 17 and never reached the act-2 map.

clears are the kits', and the two facts behind them stand as stated in
R250: pools of 30 to 33 rows hand the seat its engine every fight, and the
numbers on top of that are hot (the Splash's 232-damage turn, Electro-Charged
stacking to 15 and ticking first). A clear on a 30-row pool is consistency
as much as strength, and a control that dies does not separate the two;
widening the pools does.

## 3. What the control also read

The seat's decisions were the base game's own and it named them: the Corpse
Slugs' Ravenous trade, Skittish 6 as a tax on breadth, the Steam Eruption
clock, and ordering within a turn (a 67-damage turn was "entirely a
sequencing result"). About a third of its turns had no decision, clustered
at the ends of fights. That is the base-game floor the kit reads compare
against, and it is close to what the kit seats reported.

The page gaps it hit are all rowed already: bare names for potions, relics
and curses at the point of decision and the gold total never printed
outside the shop (`EB-323`, `EB-350`); the `999999999` HP sentinel
(`EB-332`); enemies renumbered between calls (`EB-319`'s family). The one
thing it called a hard defect, Glowwater Potion printing "Draw 10 cards" and
drawing 4, is the base game's ten-card hand limit and is not rowed.
