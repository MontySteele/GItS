## What played well

- **The Reprint choice** was every seat's main decision: which card Proofread
  or Galley Proof doubles (Evergreen Charm+ for 20 Block, Kitsune Guile for
  60, Twin Sakura for four Sakura on turn 1, Yakan Offering for two a turn).
  A Published Reprint stacks with the per-combat one.
- **Fill the shrine, then burst:** Fox Fire Draft+, Kitsune Guile (Tenko) and
  Thunderbolt Tithe+ in one turn (lane 4); Eternity plus Sakura Grove took a
  Foxfire to Level 10 (lane 1).
- **Red Ink on a planting card** (Margin Note+ on Yakan Evocation, on Shrine
  Maiden's Guard) carries to the Sakura it plants.
- Real puzzles: blocking Bowlbug Rock fully to stun it; Sakura plus a Strike
  to push the Terror Eel under 70 and cancel a 22 hit.

## What did not

- **Sakura strike a random enemy,** so against a boss with minions about two
  thirds of their damage went to the minions (lane 2, Kin Priest).
- **Bosses that scale** (Kin Priest, Insatiable, Knowledge Demon, Queen)
  outran a Defend-based Block; no seat had healing.
- **Unprinted rules the seats had to discover:** Red Ink does not stack on
  one card (three Edits wasted on one Defend); Replanting's wither also
  strikes; a same-kind plant on a full shrine merges its Red Ink onto the
  oldest; Proofread again refreshes a used Reprint.
- **A full shrine punishes Kitsune planters:** Shrine Maiden's Guard+ withers
  a Sakura you wanted ("NEVER AGAIN", lane 4).
- Kitsune Rite and Offertory Box do nothing with no Sakura out.
