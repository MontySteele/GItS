# Klee round twenty-one: R261 read, the placer is now the ritual and the detonator is the wait

Written 2026-09-06, under the Prototype loop. Two blind Opus seats on
`0.2.2789+proto` (round-21 branch with fixer O in: R261's Innate Jumpy
Dumpty, R260's stage, the round-20 rows). Records:
`review/qa/klee-round-21-2026-09-06/opus-arm-act1.md` (lane 1, assembled,
Ascension 0) and `opus-natural-act1.md` (lane 2, natural, Ascension 1).
Prototype stage, Guardrail-7. [USER]'s act-1 run on this rule change is
still due; nothing here stands in for it.

## 1. The hypothesis

"With Jumpy Dumpty Innate, turn one of fight one presents the cook-or-cash
question without the seat naming a dead turn; on a hallway held with one
bombed body, a random Set off prefers it; Careful Arrangement on a Mine
reads its Mine clause."

**Assembled deck** (lane 1, seed `LY7FUHR0J6UE`): Tinder Toss, Rapid Fire,
Mine Toss, Careful Arrangement, Powder Charge, Fwoosh! added. 120 of 120,
five fights won and a sixth in progress against Living Fog, 28 of 62; two
fights cost 5 and 0 HP. **Natural run** (lane 2, seed `812HKEALVT9P`): 117
of 120, five of five including the Skulking Colony elite, 51 of 62.

## 2. What the round found

**R261, first reading: the placer is in every opening hand, and the
placement is a ritual.** Eleven fights across both lanes opened with Jumpy
Dumpty and it was the first play in all eleven. Lane 1 calls the first turn
"yes, and a good one": Jumpy, Careful Arrangement and the Mine's
"goes off before its enemy's hit" read together built a Mine 11 on an 8-HP
body and let it do the killing, "a first turn that rewards reading three
keywords together". Lane 2 calls the same turn "one and a half": Jumpy is
"not a choice, it is a ritual", the rest of the hand was Strikes, and the
kit's first decision arrived on turn two with Ka-pow!. The two readings
agree on the fact and split on the deck: with a placer guaranteed, turn
one's quality is supplied by the board (three bodies made "detonate an 8
at its smallest to spread Mines" a real call) and by whether the draw
holds a second kit card. The dead turn named on nine rounds running is
gone; what replaced it is a fixed opening and a variable second card.

**The asymmetry the natural seat named.** "The deck ships with one
detonator and the whole kit is downstream of it": Ka-pow! was the only Set
off in fourteen cards, a Bomb 16 sat on the elite's board for a turn with
"no decision to make, only a card to wait for", and four rewards passed
before a detonator worth the slot was offered. "The guaranteed half is the
boring half." R261 put Innate on the placer alone at [USER]'s call, with
"avoid too many actually good cards in the starting deck" standing; this
is the first reading of that trade and it is recorded, not moved (§4).

**The random Set off, read at last.** Lane 1 held the hallways the round
asked for. Rapid Fire: the first iteration took the bombed body, the
remaining three split freely. Tinder Toss: the first took the bombed body,
the second hit the other, which Jumpy's rider had just Mined, "so both
picks were Bombed". The rule steers the first iteration only, and the
kit's own rider fights it: a multi-hit random Set off sprays Mines with
its first hit and eats them with its second, "spray the bank onto one body
and detonate randomly quietly undo each other, and no face mentions it".
Defensible as built; recorded for the next Rapid Fire read.

**Careful Arrangement on a Mine, exactly as printed.** Mine 3 + Bomb 3 +
Bomb 5 merged to Mine 11, "Mine-ness preserved, other body stripped". The
same seat: "best card in the deck" when the board holds charges, decided
three times three different ways by the target's Block or Thorns; and "a
blank the game charges you for" on an empty board (`EB-575`). Jumpy's
Mine-on-ALL rider survived two merges and two turns of growth, "a large
part of the kit's ceiling, completely undiscoverable" (`EB-573`).

**The decision, both lanes, the same words.** "Detonate now, or let it
grow", priced off the enemy's Block, Plating, and, on the elite, Hardened
Shell's live counter (20 HP loss a turn): lane 2 calls that fight "the best
thing that happened to this kit in five fights", the kit converted "from
stack one enormous number into manage a damage rate, without a single
surprise". Leaving a body at 1 HP for a Mine to kill before it swings
(both lanes); kill order against Ravenous; Grounded against detonation
("emptying the field to deal damage switches off the card paying for your
defence"), the round-18 adjustment read as a tension rather than a trap.

**Sparks, a sixth reading.** Lane 2 ended fights holding 3, 4 and 5; "for
the first entire fight the counter was decorative"; Dig In was the deck's
one reader and the happiest draw. Lane 1 spent Sparks on Grounded, Bang
Bang! and Sugar Rush (the last "CANNOT BE PLAYED" in three of five hands
at 2 Sparks). Six rounds say the same sentence; §5 applies an offer rule.

**Legibility.** Kaeya's face names a Grounded that no longer exists,
"two printed texts that cannot both be true" (`EB-576`). "Kills move it on"
on the Mine's own line reads as a promise about that Mine (`EB-574`).
Strength applies to Oz and nothing says a companion's hit is her Attack.
Ka-pow! printing 3 under Weak and 4 after is right and briefly reads as a
change. Arcane Scroll's card entered the deck unnamed (`EB-447`'s deck list,
cited). Praised: the Reaction preview appearing only when a target wears a
reactable aura, "it teaches the reaction system exactly when the teaching
is actionable"; Hardened Shell's counter; the enemy panel's numbers.

**Ka-pow!.** Lane 1's happiest draw ("the reason 'don't detonate yet' is a
safe decision rather than a gamble"); lane 2: "less of a decision than it
looks", 0 cost and Retain delete the cost of holding and most of the cost
of playing. Both true; the card is the starter's one detonator and reads
as such.
