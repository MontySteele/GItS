# Furina and Varka wave 2, 2026-10-12

**What ran.** Eleven whole-run Sonnet seats at A0 on build 0.2.5032 (`main` at 1971eb2c): six Furina and five Varka, three of the Varka forced to start on Pyro. The build added two changes:
- **Furina Spend paper** (#1133): higher Spend caps, Packed House as a held-Fanfare Block card, Rising Applause+ with Retain.
- **Varka Pyro paper** (#1132): Ember Cleave as Setup Strike; Stoke the Flames gives Pyro Oath and +5 to the next Pyro Attack.

The seat records and the analysis scripts (`fv2/`) are in the session scratchpad. The comparison is the morning wave (`furina-varka-wave-2026-10-12.md`).

## Results

|---|---|---|---|---|

The morning wave was Furina 0 of 4 and Varka 1 of 6. Both final-boss losses on the Varka Pyro starts were close: one left the boss at 1 HP, the other died on 3 HP.

## Varka: the Pyro pass

- **Pyro is drafted now.** His own Pyro cards were taken 22 of 61 offers (36%), against about 10% of 94 before.
  - Stoke the Flames 3 of 10, Ember Cleave 1 of 4, Ashen Oath 2 of 3, Kindled Edge 2 of 12.
  - In the two Pyro runs that took them, Stoke was played 13 and 11 times and Ember Cleave 12 times.
- **Pyro carries damage in Pyro starts:** 27%, 29% and 35% of it, beside Anemo's 24 to 39%. The half-and-half deck holds: Anemo plus Swirl is still 39 to 67% in every run.
- **Strength:** the winning run peaked at a median 2 Strength a fight (max 6). The others stayed at 0 to 4.
- **Melt and Vaporize did not rise:** 0.0 to 0.67 a fight in Pyro starts, against 0.5 to 0.7 before. Overloaded rose to 0.8 to 1.1 a fight.
  - Ember Cleave's Strength helps every hit, not only a Melt, so this check line was the wrong one for that card.
  - Stoke's +5 waits for a Pyro hit, and seats usually played it on turns without a Cryo or Hydro aura.
- **Oath, the first read** (your switch-versus-mono question):
  - The peak in one element is a median 3.5 to 5 a fight; the top fights reached 10 to 27.
  - The highest-switching run (2.4 switches a fight) peaked no higher than the others (median 4.5, max 17).
  - In five runs there is no sign that switching and banking Oath stack into a runaway. The runs that hit 25 and 27 were mono runs: one stayed on Hydro, the other on Pyro.
- **Seats said:**
  - The best turns were Ashen Oath, then Stoke, then Blazing Charge, then Charged Lunge, which stunned an elite and set up a Four Winds kill.
  - Wildfire Oath with Converging Winds and Fiery Rain won the run.
  - One seat called Stoke "mostly filler", and another played it with no Energy left for an Attack.

## Furina: the Spend paper

- **The caps fix did little to the bank.** A third of her Fanfare is still unspent: 31%, against 29% in the morning wave.
  - Plays that hit the cap with Fanfare left over fell: Spirited Aria 8 of 19, against 11 of 17 before.
  - Turns starting on 15 or more Fanfare with no Spend card in hand are 11% of turns (44 of 407), against 13% before.
  - So the bank is left behind by hands with no outlet and by holding, not by the caps.
- **Packed House** was taken 2 of 10 (1 of 14 before). Hold the Stage 2 of 3.
- **Normal-fight damage a turn by act:** 18.8, 26.3 and 33.7. The morning wave read 18.7, 32.8 and 63.8; the base five read 19.0, 30.2 and 38.6. Act 3 is 8 fights from 3 runs.
- **The swing is there:** elites and bosses cost her 36 to 58% of her HP inside the fight. She uses 52 to 63% of the Drain buffer against them, so the floor still does not bind most fights.
- **How she died:** two of the five deaths came with a big bank and no Block in hand.
  - Lane 3c had 59 Fanfare and 10 HP against 21 incoming.
  - Lane 2's boss fight: "I could not both block and spend."
  - Lane 3b's NEVER AGAIN was Regina of All Waters (HP every turn).
  - A seat's own words: Fanfare "pays out as damage but not as survival."
- **Guest acts are still 0 to 4% of her damage.** The one exception was Bring the House Down, the biggest turns of the act-3 run.

## What played well

- **Varka:** Aura, Swirl, then Four Winds' Ascension makes two-turn plans from fight 2 in every run. Rippling Guard and the Knight chains (Mika, Rippling Guard, Barbara) worked.
- **Furina:**
  - Drain or Repay first, then a Spend: two exact lethals from it.
  - Taking a hit on purpose for Lynette's doubling.
  - Bravura's many hits get round Hard To Kill.
  - Rising Applause+ (Retain) held for a 43 to 74 hit.

## What did not

- **Opening turns:** both kits' first turn of fight 1 is a starter hand with no decision (Klee and Kokomi show the same).
- **Varka:**
  - Baron Bunny is a Knight, so it switched one seat off Hydro. It hid 21 Hydro Oath and took its Swirl Block away; that seat named it as what lost the run.
  - Shifting Gale and Stormward Stance were each a seat's NEVER AGAIN.
- **Text that misled:**
  - Bis! ("Whenever you Spend all your Fanfare, keep half") did not fire when Spirited Aria emptied the bank. "Spend up to" is not "Spend all"; the card says so, but players read it the other way.
  - Stoke's bonus did not apply to Amber: Precise Shot, which is a Skill. That is correct, but it surprised the seat.
  - The Sturm und Drang and Vigor bonus preview shows on every Attack, though it pays one.
  - Enemy Block is not shown, so two kill counts came up 1 short.
