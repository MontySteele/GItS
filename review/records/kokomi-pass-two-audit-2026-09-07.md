## 2. What the rows are for, in one line each

Three make the order written matter (a rider on the next Plan; a draw that
counts what follows). Three make the queue something to spend (take one
back; cash the lot; re-aim it). Two land at dusk, before the enemy's hit,
for less than the dawn line pays after it. The cap is not a card and not
the rule: it is on in one lane of the next round so its reads can be
compared against three lanes without it.
