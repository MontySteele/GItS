## What played well

- **Klee, stacking then cashing in.**
  - Chain Fuse, Boom Badge, then Ka-pow! killed the act-1 boss (204 HP) in one turn.
  - Big Badda Boom on a three-turn stack took Louse Progenitor from 127 to 5.
  - Laying an element first and then the Bomb (Violet Arc, then Sizzle) is a puzzle the seats could read, because the board prints "Overloaded".
- **Kokomi's element order.** Hydro then Cryo freezes the enemy, and the next hit shatters it. Clorinde on an element she reacts with hits for 30 to 40. Nereid's Ascension doubled a Shoal+ Plan for 66. Every best turn came from these, not from choosing a line.
- **Enemy rules made good puzzles for both kits:** Skulking Colony's damage cap, Corpse Slug's kill order, and Bowlbug Rock's Block threshold.

## What did not

- **Klee's status package went untested.** One seat passed on It Wasn't Me!, and neither drew a loader. The status pile now also carries her defence (see What to change), so the next Klee round tests both.
- **Klee bleeds HP between fights.** Each stacking turn cost 15 to 25 HP. Both seats died in act 2 after long stretches without Block cards. The defence census (2026-10-01) found her pool had no Weak, no Strength loss and no card giving 10 or more Block outright.
- **Sparks felt scarce to both Klee seats** ("Boom Badge sat uncastable for most of the act"). [USER]'s run went the other way, because setting Bombs off quickly earns Sparks constantly. Treated as a watch item.
- **Kokomi's line chooser was a confirm button.** Across about 69 screens there were 2 flips: one a mistake, one sound but unlucky. The Plan line nearly always shows the bigger number.
- **Kokomi loses boss fights on length.** On the same boss from the same HP, she took less damage per turn than Ironclad and dealt about half his damage (§2.8 of the four-kit review). Open the Casket exhausts, so the Casket's count sat at 10 to 13, unused, in the fights she lost.
- **Readability, for the fix batch:**
  - The play log says "Put Bomb 1" while the board shows Bomb 8 or Mine 7.
  - Set off cards don't say they hit only the target's Bombs.
  - The Mine badge reads "deals 4 (sizes 3)".
  - The enchanted Defend's name changes in the log.
  - The Plan chooser shows the number before Strength (28 shown, 54 landed).
  - Opening Gambit's double fails silently when no damage Plan follows.
  - Frozen gives no warning that the next Attack shatters it.
  - Night Vigil's +6 can't be traced to a card.
  - Steam Eruption's "at the end of your next turn" was misread.
  - The Battleworn Dummy's "Setting 3" doesn't say it's a timer.
