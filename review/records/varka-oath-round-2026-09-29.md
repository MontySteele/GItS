## What played well

- **Turn one had a decision** on lane 1: Amber before Windbound Execution set
  up a 26-damage Swirl turn.
- **The current element read as a real cost.** Both seats noticed a Knight
  (Lisa, Razor) switching them away from the Oath they had built; lane 2
  added that "the kit does not warn you about this well". This is the ruled intent:
  "Oath cards are strong, but you need to think carefully before spreading
  your deckbuilding thin without a Switch card."
- **Reactions carried the big turns:** Superconduct and Melt into Vulnerable
  before Northwind Avatar or Diluc gave 43 to 60-plus hits and killed two
  bosses; Lisa on Pyro gave Overloaded, on Hydro Electro-Charged; Ayaka's
  Soumetsu left fresh auras on a three-enemy elite for Storm Surge.
- **Printed numbers matched outcomes** on the Block cards (Barbara, then
  Wall of Gales, printed and gave 27).

## What did not

- **Block is still thin on the way into act 2.** Lane 1's deck leaned on its
  Defends; lane 2's handoff said the same. The act-1 Knights' 8 Block helps,
  the pool's cheap Block does not yet.
- **Windbound Execution** was lane 2's act-3 NEVER AGAIN: "5 damage to all
  never beat any other card in my hand". Starter card, so any change is
  [USER]'s pick.
- **Stormward Stance** was lane 1's weakest card: played in three fights,
  active in one.
- **Hands of Strikes and Defends had no decision** (lane 2, act 1: fight 5
