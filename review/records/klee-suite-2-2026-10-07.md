## Picks

1. **The read.** Suite 2 did not test the scaling fix; free draft held it in
   2 of 5 runs for 7 fights. Act 2 is still the gap (damage 0.82, HP lost
   1.52). **Default: agree.**
2. **Make the scaling cards findable.** The test arm above is option (a).
   - **Default (a):** Boom Badge and Klee's Secret Base move from Uncommon to
     Common, text unchanged; the pool goes from 24 / 33 / 21 to 26 / 31 / 21.
     Commons fill most reward slots, so each of the two would be offered
     noticeably more often; it is the smallest change that lets a suite see
     the fix. The pinned pool count (`PoolCountTests.cs`) moves with it.
     **Catch:** Secret Base is a Power, and Klee's pool keeps the base game's
     rule of no Common Powers (`PoolCellCoverageTests`). A Common Secret Base
     breaks that rule; the alternative is Boom Badge to Common alone.
   - (b) Leave the rarity and put the run-long growth on a card Klee already
     drafts (a Set off Common), in a short paper first.
   - (c) Leave the cards and run more seeds until each lands in enough runs
     to read: about 4 runs for every 1 that holds it, so roughly 15 runs.
3. **What comes next if (a) is ruled.** **Default:** `klee-common-next`
   becomes `klee-next`, and the next round aims at act-3 damage (0.77), the
   one gap left. The promotion suite (two fresh seeds, read against the pooled
   base five, scaling paper sec.5) waits for a build expected to pass act 3.
