- **The growth works when it is drafted.** Lane 1's turn 1 was usually Shoal Call+, four Stings and a payoff, from fight 2 on. The best example: Slow on Bygone Effigy, four Stings and Shivs, 127 → 59 in one turn.
- **Lane 2 is the other half of her kit, unchanged.** Its Plans did 42% of its damage, and it died for the same reason the older rounds did: about 25 damage a turn against a 321-HP boss.
- **The Sting's Plan line was never used**, in 116 plays. A 0-cost Sting played now always beat writing it. The Plan line is what makes Stings hers and not Shivs, so this is the open design question.
- **Endless Shoal and All Streams Flow to the Sea were never offered.** Their known-loop check did not come up.

## What played well

- Plan or play now on Kurage's Oath, Undertide Lance and Tidal Surge.
- Change of Plans to carry out a Plan the turn it is written.
- Kill order and Weak timing: Living Shield before the Turret, and the Bowlbug Rock stunned by Casket Block from a Plan.
- "Planned hits ignore my own Weak" carried the Queen fight.

## What did not

- **Text that misled:**
  - Tide Wall's Plan reads the intent when it is carried out (8 instead of 25 against Mecha Knight).
  - A Plan's Block arrives at the start of the next turn, so it can't cover the enemy turn right after.
  - Albedo - Solar Isotoma does nothing without an aura, and says nothing.
  - Whether Strength adds to a Plan is unclear.
- **Dead cards:**
  - Kelp Wall (NEVER AGAIN) and Steady Tide never beat a Defend.
  - Lane 2 found Resolve of Watatsumi, Breakwater and Evening Watch dead, but Resolve was one of lane 1's best cards.
- **Bridge:** card names renumbered mid-turn ("Sting (3)" vanished) and caused refusals.
- **Opening turns:** fight 1 turn 1 had no Plan card in lane 2's hand.
