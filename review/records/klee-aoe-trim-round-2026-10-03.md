# Seat round: Klee after the AoE trim (w19, 2026-10-03)

The AoE trim (`review/ruled/aoe-trim-2026-10-03.md`, #862) is a new card
batch: seven Klee cards trade AoE for Block or single-target damage, Durin
splits in two, Yoimiya's card is reworked. [USER]: "I don't want to take
away the central challenge of Klee ... but if she's losing aoe as a bomb
source, she needs some survivability to make up for it."

Build 0.2.4286. Two Sonnet seats, one act per seat with a handoff, ascension
0, on the w15 to w18 seeds. Raw records are gitignored, in this session's
scratchpad under `w19-lane1` and `w19-lane2`.

## The trimmed cards in play

- **Bombs Away!** was drafted on lane 1 as "a bomb source with block" and
  played in most act-1 fights; its Block was small (6 with one Bombed
  enemy). Lane 2's act-2 seat called a Bombs Away turn under Tender its
  nothing turn (7 damage, 6 Block).
- **Team Effort** carried lane 2's act 1: it took Terror Eel to exactly 69
  under its Shriek 70 line. In act 2, with no Companion played, the same
  deck called it never worth playing.
- **Windblume Fireworks** (still AoE) was lane 1's happiest draw in act 2.
  Jumpy Dumpty's Mines on ALL were lane 2's "multi-target payoff".
- Damage Report, Red Knight, Mine Toss, Mine All Mine!, Coven Errand, both
  Durin cards and Yoimiya were not drafted by either seat.

## What played well

- Growing Bombs and cashing them across a stun line or a boss: Big Badda
  Boom turned 73 stored damage into 168 to end the act-1 boss (lane 2);
  Blasting Powder into Ka-pow! with Overloaded took the boss 222 to 138
  (lane 1).
- Spending Sparks against Energy (Boom-Boom Strike, Dig In, Quick Fuse) read
  as "a real trade, the best Block source I had" (lane 2, act 2).
- Fully blocking Bowlbug (Rock) to stun it, as printed.

## What did not

- Act-2 attrition: Frail, Tender (Hunter Killer: each card played costs 1
  Strength and 1 Dexterity) and Thorns (Spiny Toad, twice now) beat a hand
  without Block. Snecko Eye's random costs (lane 1's NEVER AGAIN) put
  Defend at 3 at 9 HP.
- NEVER AGAIN also named Once More! (two Sparks to replay a card "rarely
  worth it") and two non-Klee cards, Entrench and Decay.
- Readability, already in BACKLOG: Melt printing "nothing to amplify" beside
  an amplified hit; "Put Bomb 1" in the play log. New: Jumpy Dumpty prints
  "Mine 3 on ALL enemies" yet refuses to play without a named target (it
  aims the first Bomb, which the face does not say).

## Next

the one w18 raised: whether act 2 is a Klee problem or an act-2 problem for
any thin-Block deck, which is a Balance measurement.
