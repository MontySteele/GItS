# Co-op set: three multiplayer-only cards per character (design is final)

Ruling: [USER], 2026-09-25, "Co-op interaction, pick 3a": Claude designs a small
co-op set per character. The trigger was [USER]'s friend, after the first co-op
run: "there wasn't a ton of direct multiplayer support for the two characters to
interact, since we removed Furina's ability to transfer bonuses to other players,
and Klee's bombs can't be triggered by others." And [USER]: "Ways for allies to
help Klee by triggering bombs? Maybe make it a multiplayer card?"

Base-game model: every base character has exactly two cards the game marks
`CardMultiplayerConstraint.MultiplayerOnly`, one Uncommon and one Rare (Ironclad:
Demonic Shield and Tank; Silent: Flanking and Sneaky; see
`game_ref/ironclad-cards.yaml` and `game_ref/silent-cards.yaml`). These cards use
the same constraint, so a single-player run never offers them. There are three
per character: two Uncommons and one Rare. They sit OUTSIDE the 78-card count ("78
standard cards plus Ancients plus multiplayer cards").

"Another player" means a card that TARGETS an ally: use the base game's own
ally target type, the one Lift and Believe In You use. "Each other player" means
no target is chosen.

| id | name | cost | type | rarity | face | upgrade |

| proto_ko_pass_the_match | Pass the Match | 0 | skill | uncommon | Choose another player. This turn, their next Attack [gold]Sets off[/gold] your [gold]Bombs[/gold] on each enemy it hits. Draw 1 card. | draw +1 |
| proto_ko_hide_here | Hide Here! | 1 | skill | uncommon | Another player gains [gold]Block[/gold] equal to your largest [gold]Bomb[/gold], up to 12. | cap +4 |
| proto_ko_knights_of_favonius | Knights of Favonius | 2 | power | rare | Whenever another player plays an Attack, it [gold]Sets off[/gold] your [gold]Bombs[/gold] on each enemy it hits. | cost −1 |

- The Bombs are Klee's, so the explosions, Sparks, jumps, Mine readers and the
  Alice multiplier all belong to Klee, exactly as if she had set them off. The
  ally's card is NOT a "Set off card" for Klee's readers (Once More!, Boom Badge,
  Grounded and the rest). The Set off happens after the Attack's hits resolve.
- Hide Here! reads the largest Bomb on ANY enemy (the same read as Careful Now)
  and does not remove it.

## Furina (arm pool: FurinaStageRoster)

| id | name | cost | type | rarity | face | upgrade |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| proto_fs_guest_of_honor | Guest of Honor | 1 | skill | uncommon | Until your next turn, hits on another player land on their [gold]Block[/gold], then your [gold]front performer[/gold]'s [gold]Fanfare[/gold], then them. | cost −1 |
| proto_fs_share_the_spotlight | Share the Spotlight | 1 | skill | uncommon | Your [gold]back performer[/gold] gives all its [gold]Fanfare[/gold] to another player as [gold]Block[/gold], then [gold]Bow[/gold]s. | cost −1 |
| proto_fs_people_of_fontaine | The People of Fontaine | 1 | power | rare | Whenever another player plays an Attack, your [gold]back performer[/gold] gains 1 [gold]Fanfare[/gold]. | gains +1 |

- Guest of Honor's face was shortened on 2026-09-25 to fit the 120-character
  ceiling; it still targets the ally, and "another player" names the target, as
  on Lift.
- 2026-09-25, the Furina text pass (`review/records/furina-text-pass-2026-09-25.md`):
  the three faces above follow it. "lead performer" is "front performer", `Raise`
  is retired (faces say "gains N Fanfare"), and `Bow` is a verb. No number or
  behaviour moved.
- Guest of Honor uses the Stage's own absorption rule (rule 6, `FurinaStage.AbsorbHit`)
  for the ally. A front performer emptied by a hit on the ally leaves WITHOUT a
  Bow, as any hit does, and the next performer steps forward. A Rapt Audience
  fires on those hits too, because an enemy hit the front performer.
- Share the Spotlight on an empty stage does nothing, and is still playable. The
  Bow is a real Bow: its effect fires, Thunderous Applause fires, and Five-Century
  Act returns the performer. The Fanfare leaves the bar exactly, so this counts as
  an exact emptying.
- The People of Fontaine gives its Fanfare to the back performer, and on an
  empty stage a random performer arrives holding it (the back performer tip says
  so).

  damage" is implemented, and mirror it for the other players.

## Common requirements

- Mark every card MultiplayerOnly and offer it in the arm pool only in co-op. Keep
  it out of any single-player offer, the lint pool counts, and the 78/39/29 counts;
  make the parity lints know the multiplayer tier.
- Tips: every keyword on the faces gets its existing tip. Keep the seat glossary in
  step where the prototype-tip parity requires it.
- Tests: each card on both sides where the sim can express it. The sim may not
  model co-op; if it doesn't, test in C# only and say so. Cover a single-player
  guard: the card is not offered.
- Art: each new id needs a portrait. Use the art pipeline exactly as PR #657 did,
  with distinct crops, missing 0, and art_lint and --edges clean. Pick scenes that
  show two or more characters together where the stock allows: Klee with Jean,
  Amber, or the Knights; Furina with Neuvillette or the Salon; Kokomi with Gorou or
  her soldiers.
