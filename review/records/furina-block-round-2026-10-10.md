**Verdict.**
- **Wins:** 2 of 4, the best Furina round yet. Across both rounds on the quarter line she has 3 wins in 8. The base five's baseline was 0 in 5 (`base-five-baseline-2026-10-05.md`).
- **The control** died to the Queen on floor 48 in all three rounds. The comparison holds.
- **TYXJ's act-1 death was the seat's own play:** "I raced damage on turns 1 and 2 instead of building block." Seed-to-seat swings are still large. TYXJ won one round and died in act 1 the next.
- **She now looks at or above a base character on these seeds.** The next question is whether she has overshot. [USER]'s pre-trim co-op run called her "very OP".

**Were the Block cards drafted?**
- Velvet Curtain went into 3 of 4 decks.
- The Masquerade went into 2. In one it gave "Masquerade+Freminet+Deluge+Usher+ = 32 block".
- Private Box and The Show Must Go On were never taken. The Show Must Go On is Rare, and Private Box needs guests on stage.
- So the gain is mostly Velvet Curtain and The Masquerade. One seat called Velvet Curtain "never anything but block", which is its job.

## What played well

- **Drain, then Spend:** the clearest decision. "Play the Drain card first so the Spend-all card hits harder." Rising Applause hit for 58. Standing Ovation for exactly 20 killed two Cubex Constructs with 44 incoming.
- **Hold the Stage's Spend 6 for 16–20 Block:** fully blocked a 15 and stunned the Bowlbug.
- **Drain then Repay as a Fanfare loop:** "Drain 3 into Hymn" was the loop that worked.
- **One-action wipes:** Bravura with Surging Waters, then Grand Deluge, killed both Kaiser Crab arms in one action.

## What did not

- **Fanfare piles up with nothing to spend it on.** This was in every Furina record of both rounds:
  - 81 Fanfare with Rising Applause in the draw pile.
  - "four Dazed and one Usher with 27 Fanfare".
  - Whether a turn has a decision depends on whether a Spend-all card is in hand.
- **Fanfare from damage taken is not explained on the screen:** "The fight 1 screen said 'Fanfare 19' with no hint that being hit was the source."
- **Draining past the line is still learned by losing HP.** The counter (#1012) was built after this round's build was installed. Lyney's "never past your line" did not say his Drain does nothing once she is under the line.
- **High Stakes:** NEVER AGAIN. "It needs a narrow HP window I never wanted to build."
- **Statuses** (Dazed, Infection) clog her hands, and nothing in the kit removes them.
- **Possible defect: Bravura and Let the People Rejoice previews (164 and 152) ignored Weak 2.** The real total was about 25% lower. If confirmed, the Spend-all previews overstate damage while she is Weak.
