# Seat round: Klee's three defence cards forced into the deck (w13, 2026-10-02)

Build 0.2.4208. It carries the Kitchen Alchemy rework (#830, "ALL enemies
lose 1 [2] Strength. Exhaust every status in your hand; they lose 1 more for
each."), on top of the build in `casket-and-klee-defence-round-2026-10-02.md`.

There were two Sonnet seats, one act per seat with a handoff, at ascension 0.
They used the w12 Klee seeds, so each run has a before and after. Each lane
embarked with `--arm` for Up in Smoke!, Behind Jean's Desk and Kitchen
Alchemy, which puts all three in the starting deck. A dev grant makes the
deck one the generators never produced, so these runs read the cards, not
the kit's win rate. The raw records are gitignored and sit in session
scratchpad `3c650424`, folders `w13-lane1` and `w13-lane2`. Nobody wrote this
record overnight. It was written on 2026-10-02 from the seats' own records.
