**The question:** after the feed pass (#762: five 0-cost Plan-only Commons,
the now-and-Plan cards moved to Uncommon), is energy still what stops her?

"some Plan cards need to go to 0 cost". Two blind Sonnet seats, one act each
with a handoff note, ascension 0. Seat A played 0.2.4022, seat B 0.2.4026

Seat A went further than any Kokomi seat before it. The two cleanup-pass seats
died at act-2 bosses.

## What played well

- **Energy is no longer the wall.** Seat A was short on one turn in about half
  its fights in acts 1 and 2, and in act 3 "never energy-starved, I was
  CARD-starved or BLOCK-starved". Seat B was short in 3 of 7 fights, each time
  on "one more Defend". The 0-cost Plans (Nip, Bubble Ward, Current Read) were
  played as free Plans every time they were drawn.
- **Plan now or later was a real choice** on most turns in both records. Both
  seats named the doubling cards as their best turns: Second Wave with Kurage's
  Oath (14 to all, Casket +3), Nereid's Ascension with War Council, and
  Depths' Judgment at a Casket count of 9 to 13 (60 or more damage).
- **Casket timing became a decision** once understood. Seat A in act 3 opened
  the Casket before writing Plans so the Plans carried the Strength, on three
  separate turns.

## What did not

- **Block is still the gap.** Seat B went 80 to 28 HP in five normal fights and
  had no Block card in hand on the elite's killing turn. Seat A's act-3
  nothing turn was "no Block in hand ... and I took 17". Both earlier Kokomi
  rounds this week (the cleanup-pass seats, round 3) also died short of Block.
- **The Casket reads as reusable.** Open the Casket exhausts, so the Strength
  pays once a fight; the relic's text does not say so. Seat B opened it at 2
  and later left a count of 4 unused; Seat A was surprised the count survives
  the opening.
- **Cards whose two lines are exclusive read as doing both.** Shell of
  Sanctuary ("Draw 1 card. Dusk Plan: Gain 9 Block") played normally only
  draws; Current Read+ played normally does nothing; Kurage's Oath planned
  gives no Block now. Both seats tripped on this.
- **Weak cards named:** Pearl Diver (both acts of seat A: "too small to
  matter"), The Clouds Like Waves Rippling (seat B: "2 energy for 2 Block a
  turn"), The Moon's Mend (heals nothing when a fight starts at low HP).
