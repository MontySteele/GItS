## What played well

- **The round-3 fixes worked.** No seat reported a Vulnerable mismatch in the Plan previews. A seat quoted the Coordinated Tide line word for word. Tide Wall's Plan paid 19 to 32 Block. Kelp Wall was taken 28% of the time, and it is no longer anyone's NEVER AGAIN.
- **Plans carry her damage again.** They dealt 27, 33 and 19% of it by act. Shared Companions fell to about 8%. Masterstroke was taken 5 of 7 times.
- **The Plan decisions stay the core of her play:** Second Wave or Opening Gambit before a damage Plan, Change of Plans to fire one at once, Steady Tide into Tidal Rebuke. Plans also get past Thorns and the Owl's Soar.

## What did not

- **The act-2 boss wall.** Her own damage against those bosses is 45% of base. The Knowledge Demon's curses and The Insatiable's Sandpit clock both punish a slow engine.
- **The growth test went unrun:** Watchful Shoal was taken 1 of 11 times and played 3 times. Her engine Powers were taken 4 of 49 offers across the round. The Sonnet seats do not draft them, so a forced test is running (below).
- **The jellyfish decks lack a big hit.** Both act-1 boss deaths were jellyfish decks against bosses that cut damage (Slippery; the Matriarch's Strength and Dexterity drain).
- **Treatise draws, but silently.** Telemetry shows about 0.8 more draws a turn after it is played; four seats said it never drew.
- **Weak behaves two ways.** A Plan from her own card ignores her Weak, but a written Companion plays as a card next turn, so her Weak cuts it. The page says Weak never cuts a Plan.
