## Picks

1. **Act 3 is a turn-one damage gap, not a Block gap.**
   - The evidence: act-3 damage a turn is 0.70 of the base five's, while her Block is 1.5 times
     theirs and her HP lost is 1.36 times theirs.
   - From turn two on she is level with them. On turn one she deals 22.6 against their 53.2.
   - **Default:** the main session writes a short act-3 scaling paper once tonight's control runs
     finish. It changes no number before you rule it.
   - Alternative: run suite 6 on this build first, to see whether suite 5's gains replicate.
2. **Spark-priced Block.** Three opening Sparks turned Dig In from a 4-fight card into a 30-fight
   card, against the fast-and-fragile direction.
   - **Default:** trim Dig In to 6 [9] Block (from 8 [11]) in the same change as pick 1's damage
     lever, not alone, so a Block cut is not shipped without the damage that replaces it.
   - Alternative: trim it now.
   - Alternative: leave it.

   Blast Shield is yours and is not touched.
3. **The grading baseline.** Grade suite 5 and later suites against the five counted base runs plus
   tonight's base control on the current page, by run instance. **Default: agree.**
