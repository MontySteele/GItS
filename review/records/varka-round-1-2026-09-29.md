## What played well

- **Absorb or Swirl becomes a decision once one Wind is held.** Every seat
  said the first Wind was a chore ("Muster, then Strike" every turn), and
  that the choice turned real when an aura could either give a new Wind or
  Swirl for one already held. Run 2: "a real decision (Swirl for 2+3 dmg and
  a draw vs absorbing with Strike for a new Wind)". Boreas Unbound, which pays
  energy only on a true Absorb, kept it a choice even with all four Winds.
- **Four Winds made Swirl matter more, not less** (run 3, act 3): "every Swirl
  paid for itself in block and energy, so the question was how many Swirls
  fit". Cryo Wind's Weak was its best defence.
- **Spent auras are a second board.** A spent aura cannot be Absorbed or
  Swirled but still reacts to another element. Lisa's Overloaded on spent
  copies, and Rosaria's Frozen on a spent Hydro, were among the best plays in
  three records. Run 3 called it "my most valuable discovery".
- **Cards wanted:** Venti (Barbara on all, then a Swirl chain that killed four
  minions), Lisa: Violet Arc, Knights' Muster+, Four Winds' Ascension at 2 or
  more Winds, and Favonius Cut.

## What did not

- **The Fang Absorbs its own paint.** An Attack that applies an element to a
  bare enemy (Charlotte, Rosaria, Kaeya) has that aura Absorbed by the Fang
  in the same play. Runs 3 and 4 both reported it. It contradicts sec.9.6
  ("a Knight never Absorbs its own paint") and removes the choice from those
  cards.
- **The page hides what the Fang did.** "What reacted this turn" never names
  an Absorb, only the Winds panel changes. With the Fang ready, the hand's
  preview promised a Melt 7 that landed as a 4-damage Absorb. Nothing on the
  page says spent auras still react, and Swirl's text does not say its copies
  arrive spent (only the enemy line does). The Words line "no reaction is
  reachable" printed with Knights' Muster in hand.
- **The relic fires only off basic Strikes early.** Knights are Skills, so in
  a starter-heavy deck the Fang's only trigger is a Strike (run 2).
- **Block is short.** Run 1 reached its elite at 20/80, and run 3 its act-1
  elite with "almost no block". Run 4 died to an elite that punishes Skills,
  and run 3 died at 5 HP needing 43 Block. Eye of the Storm was named "never again" twice (4 Block per
  Wind; "Gains 0 Block" until Winds).
