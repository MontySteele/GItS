**Verdict: the Block cut landed, and the loop still plays well. Pyro Exhaust did not land.** One run cleared act 2, and the other died to the act-2 boss. The rebalance round (2026-10-03) reached the final boss twice, so this round is harder, as the cut intended.

## What played well
- **Choosing the current element when you cash in is the decision.** Lane 4's best turn: Pulsating Witch+ made Electro current, then Azure Devour cashed 15 Electro Oath into a Vulnerable boss for about 92.
- **Four Winds' Ascension at 1 Energy** finished or decided fights on both lanes (48, 37, and the boss from 114 to 17).
- **Knights' Roll Call+ was the best card on lane 3 in both acts.** It fetches any of 13 Knights for 0 Energy.
- **Shatter works when the deck stacks debuffs.** Lane 4 took Shatter+ and hit for 19, about 24, and 42 at the boss.
- **Block is no longer a wall.**
  - Lane 4 took no damage in 1 of 8 act-2 fights; the co-op run took none in 7 of its last 10.
  - Lane 3 had no damage-free fight in act 2.
  - The shortfalls came from bad hands and boss debuffs, not the deck: Mind Rot's 4-card hands, Strength and Dexterity -4, Disintegration.

## What did not
- **Pyro Exhaust was offered to three of the four seats and paid nothing.**
  - Stoke the Flames was played once, on lane 4 in act 1, while on Electro. It gave 2 Pyro Oath that nothing read and burned a Defend. Lane 4 later paid 100 gold to remove it ("never again").
  - Ember Cleave and Pyre Oath were passed in shops.
  - Lane 3 played Pyro in act 2, but nothing in its deck exhausted, so it skipped Pyre Oath.

  Seats drift into Pyro mid-run. The Exhaust cards cannot start that drift: off-element they are blank.
- **No seat ever built Cryo.**
  - Lane 3 passed Absolute Zero and Shatter because its Cryo Oath sat at 0 to 1.
  - Deep Freeze was never offered in four acts. That is luck at Uncommon, but it is still untested.
- **Never-again cards:**
  - Crosscurrent, on lane 3 in both acts: "pays twice" does not double the Swirl's 2 damage.
  - Cycle of Seasons, on lane 4 in act 2: "4 damage on an element change, rarely fired."

## Screen and text problems
1. **"Shatter" is both a Frozen keyword and a card title.** Lane 4 called it confusing. The card gets a new name.
2. **Tempest Charge's "If it Swirls, draw 1 card" was never seen to draw.** Lane 4's act 1, fight 6, turn 1 Swirled a Cryo aura with it. Needs a check.
3. The Oath readout sits next to a current element it does not describe (lane 4).
4. Artifact silently ate Frozen, Weak and Electro-Charged's Poison on both lanes. Artifact is base-game, but our reaction text should say "blocked by Artifact".
5. Bottled Gale with no aura on the board does nothing, and nothing warns you.
6. Diluc printed 9 against the 6 on the card (lane 3). The seat never learned why.

## What to change
1. **Stoke the Flames becomes Pyro's on-ramp:** "Exhaust a card. Gain 2 [3] Pyro Oath. Pyro becomes your current element." This is a card fix, not a rule change. It gives the drift a first step, and Unwavering Banner still guards a mono deck.
2. **Rename the card Shatter to Icebreaker.** Keep its text.
3. **Check Tempest Charge's draw, and fix it if it is broken.** Then work through screen items 3 to 6 as legibility work.
4. **Leave Block alone.** The two Rare Oath-to-Block engines, which the combo pass named as next, did not show up as a problem.
5. **Cryo needs a run that drafts it** before any number moves. If Deep Freeze still never shows, consider Common rarity for one Cryo payoff.
