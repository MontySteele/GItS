       control does its registered job: Q-A's prediction is stated over all
       nine arms against each one's own floor, so an empirical floor has to
       exist for each of the nine.

       `n` cell says "9 arms = 5,400 runs; 7,800 with controls", and 7,800
       implies FOUR control arms — two for C1 and two unnamed. §6.5 never says

THE `T3` EXCLUSION LINE OVER-STATES ITS HOLE, AND MIS-NAMES ITS CAUSE.
Every one of the 122 ids printed on the `external-source on-plan payoffs` lines
below carries an `@` — they are ENCHANTED reward-pool cards (`<id>@<enchant>`),
not cards from outside the pool. `base_id` normalizes only the upgrade suffix,
which is exactly what §6.5 prescribes ("explicit upgrade normalization"), so an
enchanted pool card does not reconstruct and falls into the excluded set; the
set it falls into is then labelled "entered the deck from outside the reward
pool", which it did not. GENUINELY external on-plan payoffs at this read: ZERO,
in all nine arms.
  T3's VERDICT IS ROBUST TO THIS. Re-running the comparison with the
  enchantment suffix stripped as well puts all 122 back into the compared set
  and yields ZERO membership disagreements — an enchantment moves neither
  `role` nor `archetypes`, which is the whole of the registered predicate. So
  `T3` does not fire under either normalization, and the finding is about the
  exclusion line's accuracy, not about the tripwire's answer.
