## What played well

- **Chilling Penalty on Furina** (GPT's first test): "strong, never out of
  place". It fires on every Drain (each Drain card became two hits) and on
  every hit past Block (a 7-hit elite attack took that elite from 75 to 47).
  Its Cryo aura fed her Hydro reactions.
- **Lynette's Enigmatic Feint** was the companion both seats valued most
  (Varka: "exactly 24 Block against 24 incoming at 8 HP").
- **Charlotte's First-Person Shutter+** was Furina's best defensive 1-cost;
  **Chevreuse's Ring of Bursting Grenades** won two Varka fights; **Navia's
  Cannon Fire Support+** was "strong in long fights".
- **Emilie's Aromatic Explication** worked as the specialist it was meant to
  be: it ate Enthralled, Injury, Decay and Toxic for Energy.

## What did not

- **The delete-test risk did not show:** no companion Power carried either
  run. Varka's was carried by his own cards; Rebound Hydrotherapy was never
  offered to him.
- **HP-cost cards were passed** (Lyney, Wriothesley's Gracious Rebuke, while
  Varka was low), and Freminet's Retain ramp "too slow". One seat each: not
  yet a verdict. Gracious Rebuke was never played, so GPT's third risk is
  untested.

## What changes

- Nothing on the cards yet. Chilling Penalty stays as written: strong on
  the kit it was aimed at, in a run Furina's seats often win anyway. Watch
  its per-turn count in the next Furina round before capping it.
