# Seat round: Varka after the rebalance (2026-10-03)

The Varka rebalance (`review/active/varka-rebalance-2026-10-03.md`, #863) is
a rule change and a new card batch: element cards read their own Oath
whether or not it is current, four borrowing payoffs (Kindled Edge, Storm
Battery, Frost Ward, Rippling Guard), Hydro's scaling Block, four distinct
starter Knights, and the AoE trim's two Varka cards.

Build 0.2.4295. Two Sonnet seats, one act per seat with a handoff,
ascension 0, on the Klee seeds. Raw records are gitignored, in this
session's scratchpad under `v1-lane1` and `v1-lane2`.

## What played well

- **Act-3 scaling works.** Four Winds' Ascension at Oath 12 hit for 73 to
  swing the Kaiser Crab (lane 2's most-wanted turn); Electro Oath reached
  22; Thundering Verdict hit 25 to 73, and on a Cryo aura for 45.
- **Borrowing.** Kindled Edge off an Electro aura was "22 for 1 energy" and
  lane 1's happiest draw; its decks paired Kaeya, Lisa and Amber cards with
  it. Barbara: Gleeful Songs+ was named among the happiest draws in act 2.
- Sequencing an aura before a hit of another element, Swirl timing, and
  full Block to stun Bowlbug (Rock) were the real decisions in every act.
- Setting up during Lagavulin Matriarch's sleep worked on both lanes.

## What did not

- **Windbound Execution, NEVER AGAIN in all four Varka records** ("4
  damage that does nothing without an aura"). It is a starter card, so any
  change is [USER]'s pick.
- **Oath is invisible at its source.** Both act-1 seats could not tell
  which play granted Oath ("I never saw the line that grants it, so Four
  Winds was an uncontrolled number"). Oathsworn Strike "nearly always read
  Deals 6" because Knights switch the current element, which is the rule
  for generic cards, but nothing on screen says so.
- Kaeya then Kindled Edge for Melt "was the same turn repeatedly" (lane 1,
  act 2). Worth watching as the sim's dominance check could not see it.
- Storm Battery, Frost Ward, Rippling Guard, Whisper of Water and Absolute
  Zero were not drafted by either seat.
- Act 3: no rest reachable after the Ancient, and the kit has no healing;
  22, 9, then 2 HP across four fights.
