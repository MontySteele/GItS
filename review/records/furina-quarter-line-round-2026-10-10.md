**Verdict.** This is the best Furina round so far:
- **One win in four:** TYXJ, a seed that died on floor 40 before.
- **One seed jumped a whole act:** JF391 went from floor 24 to the last phase of the act-3 boss.
- **One seed matched:** 0J42, same floor, same boss.
- **One seed went backwards:** WDETA reached act 3 or won on both earlier lines.
- **The control matched its pool-75 result,** so this round's seats were not unusually strong.

Four runs cannot settle balance. The direction is right, though: she no longer reliably dies an act early, and three of the four Furina runs reached at least the act-2 boss.

## What played well

- **Fanfare set up, then cashed out, was the real decision:** "set up now or defend now". The standouts:
  - Standing Ovation+ at 41 Fanfare killed three enemies at exactly 36 incoming.
  - Let the People Rejoice+ for 112–147 to ALL.
  - Bravura at 48 Fanfare for 102.
- **Reactions changed turns:** Hydro then Pyro (Vaporize), and Hydro onto Cryo (Frozen).
- **Guests finished enemies at end of turn.** Lyney and Freminet did this in the win.
- **Max HP stacking paid off,** as [USER] predicted: JF391 ended at 123 max HP, so its line had 30 HP of room.

## What did not

- **The cost of a Drain past the line is still learned by losing HP.** It came up in every Furina record:
  - "Drain is a real HP cost once you are below the Drain line, and I only learned that by watching HP in act 3."
  - WDETA spent the whole act-1 boss fight below its line and won at 10/78.
  - The card's "Past your Drain line" tag shows, but nothing totals what has been lost.
- **Fanfare overflows with nothing to spend it on.** Lane 4 sat at 26–44 Fanfare, and lane 2 of the last round had 44 Fanfare in a hand of Dazed. Whether a turn is a decision "depends on whether Bravura is in hand".
- **Block was light at every death:**
  - 0J42 died to the Kaiser Crab's 30-damage laser with hands short on Block.
  - JF391 had 20 Block against 36.
  - WDETA died with no Block in hand.
  - The Block cards (#1005) are not in this build.
- **Statuses clog her deck.** Infection, Toxic, Slimed and Dazed are never removed; "nothing in the kit removes" them.
- **Spirited Aria's chooser** appears only at 5 or more Fanfare. Below that, a `choose` is refused because the card resolves on its own.

**Possible defects to check, not confirmed:**
- **Frozen did not halve the Queen's 35 incoming.** It did work on four other enemies, so it may be a base-game boss rule.
- **Chains of Binding's text** says the first 3 drawn cards are Bound, but play suggested one per turn.
- **Book of Five Rings** may not count shop purchases toward its 5.
- **Bridge:**
  - A second `end turn` was not held, which cost a turn.
  - The numbering of reward offers shifts after a skip.
