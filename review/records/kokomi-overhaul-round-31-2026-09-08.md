# Kokomi round 31: every opening hand held a Plan line; the basic Strike was written on 17 turns and played on 23, each switch decided by a printed telegraph, lethal or Block; the no-decision turn is now a lethal or a hand of Defends

Written 2026-09-08, evening. Two blind Opus seats, natural draft, on
`0.2.3061+proto` (main `1ba44a7d`), the first build with `EB-703` (pool pass
six: Kokomi's own basic Strike, "Deal 6 damage. Plan: Deal 8 damage.",
face-up applying nothing, the written hit the jellyfish's Hydro). Records:
`review/qa/kokomi-round-31-2026-09-08/opus-lane1-natural.md` (137 acts, died
on the Phrog Parasite elite) and `opus-lane2-natural.md` (231 acts, Vantom
killed, floor 19 of act 2 on the budget). Prototype stage, Guardrail 7.

## 1. The hypothesis

"With a Plan line on every Strike, a seat names no opening hand without a
Plan line and fewer turns with no decision than round 30 lane 1's three of
eleven; the Strike's halves are chosen by intent and Block, not written by
default; a written Strike leaves Hydro and a played one does not, and a
seat says so."

Met on the first three; the Hydro distinction was not remarked on by either
seat, which is the right outcome for a rule meant to be felt and not read.

## 2. The reads

**1. Opening hands.** Fifteen of fifteen held a Plan line; lane 1 never
fewer than three, lane 2 twice exactly one (a basic Strike, written "purely
to arm Sango Isshin next turn"). Lane 2: "the thinnest Plan hands were the
ones that made the turn-1 decision most interesting, because a single Plan
slot forces a choice about what to arm." Lane 1: "the kit never gave me a
'nothing to do on turn one' hand."

**2. No-decision turns.** Lane 1 nine of 27, five of them lethal turns set
up by an earlier write, one the elite's summon turn with no legal play,
one a lost fight. Lane 2 six of 39, five of them a Plan landing, and "the
honest count of turns that were dead because the kit gave me nothing is
one: boss turn 9", a hand of Tide Wall, Ripple, two Defends and two Wounds.
Round 30's three of eleven opening turns is now zero of fifteen.

**3. The Strike's halves.** Lane 1 wrote 4, played 12; lane 2 wrote 13,
played 11. Written when the target was neither blocking nor about to die
and nothing incoming punished the delay, or under Shrink (planned hits
ignore it), or under Second Wave (8 doubled); played on lethal, on an
expiring Vulnerable, against a telegraphed Defend ("play now, before the
Block exists"), against Slippery (instances, not size), against an escape
clock. Lane 2: decided by a printed telegraph in 20 of 24 and by lethal in
4, "not once by an enemy's standing Block", because no enemy in that run
stood in Block when a Strike half was chosen. Lane 1: "every one of those
switches was decided by a rule the screen printed, not by feel."

**4. Wholesale writes.** Lane 2 wrote two or three Strikes on a free turn
four times, each named as such; lane 1 once (three, for an exact kill).
The rest were single writes beside another Plan. The cap question does not
reopen.

**5. Hydro.** Unremarked. Lane 2's Electro-Charged came off Shinobu's ring
and Hydro auras that the seats attributed to their other cards.

**6. The screen.** Lane 2's reaction log said "Nothing reacted" through six
Electro-Charged (`EB-710`); the "no reaction reachable" line stayed beside a
printed reaction (`EB-707`); the (S)/(M) size letters read as Minion
markers (`EB-708`); Tide Wall under Second Wave left the doubled count
unstated (`EB-709`). Lane 1: intent numbers fold Weak on some screens and
not others (`EB-706`), the confirm note is missing on the potion chooser
(`EB-704`), and the enemy list doubled again on multi-body screens
(`EB-705`).

## 3. What this says

The Plan-less hand is answered: with a Plan line on the basics, the empty
turn is a lethal, a summon with no legal play, or a hand of Defends and
status cards. Both seats now name Defend as the last card that cannot be
pointed at the jellyfish and the reason a hand goes dead, and lane 1's run
was lost to a five-card hand with no Block in it against 21 incoming: the
kit's defence sits in three cards, two of which pay only when the Casket
fires. That is the next design question, and it is the same card from
both sides: Defend is unwritable, and Block is thin. A Defend that is worth
more while the jellyfish holds a Plan ("Gain 5 Block. If the Bake-Kurage is
holding a Plan, gain 2 more.") keeps one card unwritable, ties the floor to
the engine, and does not obsolete Breakwater's Dusk; it is Fable's to draft
as the next one-page note, not a row yet.
