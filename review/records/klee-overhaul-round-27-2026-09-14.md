four seats named "set off now or let it grow" as the recurring choice and
all four answered (e) yes: Jumpy Dumpty Innate plus a Retained 0-cost
Ka-pow! makes turn one of fight one a fork with a printed reason on each
side. Lane 2: "the kit's core decision is genuinely good and it recurs";
the Cook lane: "the strongest thing I can say about this kit's legibility."
Lane 2 also named four enemies (Thorns, Ravenous, Hardened Shell, Shriek)
that each asked a different shape of the same kit. Two seats reached floor
12 and 14 with five fights won; the Cook lane cleared six.

**2. Grounded's condition is a fork, not a switch.** The Cook lane's turn
one was "Grounded against Ka-pow!, and it is a genuinely sharp one because
the two cards are written to hate each other"; it paid on quiet turns and
was knowingly forfeited on a Set off turn (fight 4 turn 1). Read (b) is
answered: the repair made the card a choice.

**3. The Splash pairing runs itself once it is up.** Fight 6 of the Cook
lane: "feeding bombs to Sparks 'n' Splash rather than cashing them", and its
NOTHING TURN is fight 6 turn 2, "two Defends and a power I did not need,
while the engine ran itself." Fight 5 turn 3 was the same deck's best turn
(Splash beating a damage cap, Return to Sender paying a 9 back as a Bomb).
So the pairing is both: the brief's Rare engine when it is assembled, and
dead hands after. One lane, one read; the C1 watch stands and nothing
moves.

**4. Return to Sender's cap read as printed.** The Cook lane doubted the
face and it held: "took a 9 to the face and printed a Bomb 12 on the
attacker" (8 absorbed, grown once). Its happiest draw.

**5. Tinder Toss was never declined, in three plays across two seats.**
Lane 2 played it in fight 3 turn 3 (its MOST WANTED TURN, a double kill on
Sparks alone) and fight 5 turn 4 into a damage cap; the Codex seat played
it in fight 3 the turn it arrived. Each was a multi-enemy or capped board
where it was right. Three plays that were each right is not "always played
the moment it is affordable"; it is too few to say. One more natural read
before any price moves (D default: the price stays).
