# Varka seat round: the open Oath (2026-10-01)

Build 0.2.4076: the co-op playtest fixes (#775 to #780), the open Oath
(#778), the Power cost sweep (#780) and the Swirl refresh (#781). Two Sonnet
seats, one act per seat with a handoff note, ascension 0, game-rolled seeds.
Raw records are gitignored (session scratchpad `vk6-lane1/`, `vk6-lane2/`).

## Results

| Lane | Act 1 | Act 2 | Act 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Kin beaten, 13/80 | died floor 28 to the Entomancer elite (52/145 left), entered at 47/80 on a forced path | - |
| 2 | Lagavulin Matriarch beaten, 41/87 | The Insatiable beaten (the act-2 seat ran out of actions mid-fight; the act-3 seat finished it) | **Aeonglass beaten, run won**, 21/87 |

The last round (2026-09-29, the closed Oath) also went one win, one loss,
and its loss was the same Entomancer elite in act 2.

## What played well

- **Ordering decides the turn.** Every seat named the same choice: an
  applier first, then the Anemo card, then the finisher (lane 1, act 1:
  Kaeya, Mika, then a free Gale Sweep Swirling three bodies into twelve hits).
- **Oath as a multi-turn plan.** Lane 2 banked Oath and cashed it: 95 in one
  turn on the act-1 boss (Oath 11), 84 printed on Azure Devour at Oath 14,
  and in act 3 Azure Devour+ (139) plus two Oathsworn Strikes (46 each) took
  Aeonglass from 256 to 25.
- **The open Oath read naturally.** Lane 1 reported "any card with an
  element seemed to set the current element", which is the rule as now built;
  no seat found it confusing in play.
- **The reaction preview and "spent" hints** made aura order readable.

## What did not

- **Change of Guard was a NEVER AGAIN twice** (lane 1, acts 1 and 2):
  "one card slot for 2 to 5 block and a switch I never needed".
- **Favonian Standard** was lane 2's act-3 NEVER AGAIN ("needs a Knight in
  hand, dead draw"); Stormward Stance+ sat dead until a boss's last turn,
  because its gate counts the current element's Oath, not the total.
- **The rule text lags the rule.** The Oath panel still says "Play a Knight
  to set it", and the Swirl tip ("The aura and new copies stay spent; old
  ones refresh.") was misread by two seats.
- **Jean: Wind Companion reads as a Knight.** The Knight tip says "a
  Companion titled with a colon"; Jean has the colon but is not a Knight, so
  Grand Master's Order+ fizzled on it.
- **Losing an element is silent.** Amber: Baron Bunny (a Pyro Knight)
  switched lane 1 to Pyro at 0 Oath and switched off Oath of the Knights+
  and Stormward Stance+ with no warning.
- **Turns with no aura source have no decision** (both lanes, most fights'
  first turn): Strike, Defend, done.
- **Elite matchup:** the Entomancer adds Dazed per Attack hit, and Varka's
  multi-hit cards fed it; by turn 7 the hand was four Dazed.

## What to change

1. Change of Guard: cost 0, no Exhaust: switch to an element you have Oath
   in, draw 1 card (2 upgraded). A cheap way back to the element you built,
   which is what Baron Bunny's silent switch shows he needs.
2. The Oath panel and Knight tip say the open rule; Jean's card takes the
   em dash the other companions use, so the colon means Knight.
3. Swirl tip: "Anemo meets a fresh aura: deal 2 damage to ALL enemies and
   copy it, spent, onto the others. Enemies already wearing it are
   refreshed."
4. Watch, no change yet: Azure Devour's ceiling (139), Favonian Standard,
   Stormward Stance's gate, the Entomancer matchup.

Smaller, for the backlog: an enemy's intent kept showing 21 while it was
