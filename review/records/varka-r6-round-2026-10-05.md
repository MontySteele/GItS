## What played well
- **Four Winds' Ascension carried every run**, and was named the happiest
  draw in all five Varka seats: 64 in one turn at Hydro Oath 18 (lane 1),
  and 74 at Electro Oath 15 or more (lane 2).
- **Swirl spread was the best turn of the round.** Gale Sweep+ on three
  auras took a Decimillipede elite from 44/35/28 to 6/17/5 and killed it.
  Wall of Gales+ and Crosswind+ took four Scrolls of Biting to 1 HP through
  44 incoming.
- **Choosing which element is current when Ascension lands** was a real
  decision on both lanes (Kaeya and Change of Guard switching into Cryo or
  Electro).
- **The enemy section decided plays on every lane.** It covered the
  Lagavulin "Awakens upon losing HP" wake with an unblockable Swirl, the
  Queen's Amalgam, and Hard to Kill and Curl Up. "Incoming this turn ...
  you would be at" set the Block on exact-Block turns.

## What did not
- **Cycle of Seasons was NEVER AGAIN for the second round running** (lane 2,
  act 2: "4 damage per element change was the weakest card"). The combo
  round's lane 4 said the same.
- **The Knowledge Demon is Varka's wall on lane 1's seed.** Mind Rot, Sloth
  (3 cards a turn) and Waste Away left 18 Block against a 23 hit at 4 HP.
- **Strike and Defend were never-again cards in three seats.** Those are
  starter cards, so they stay.

## Screen and text problems
1. **Kaeya — Frostgnaw** printed 12 against a written 8, and its own
   Superconduct's Vulnerable did not multiply the hit, although the tip says
   it "applies before this hit" (lane 2, act 1, Ceremonial Beast). Needs a
   trace.
2. **Draws from effects are invisible.** Eye of Stormterror's draw on Swirl
   (lane 1) and Static Field's draw (lane 2, act 3) left no line, so the
   seats could not tell whether either fired. The same gap made last round's
   seat think Tempest Charge never drew.
3. **Four Winds' Ascension's Oath hit** leaves a Hydro aura but raises no
   Oath, while Barbara's hit does (lane 1, twice). Whether that is the rule
   or not, nothing printed says so.
4. Boreas's Fang's Four Winds' Ascension comes back every combat, and the
   seat read that as the card joining the deck.

## What to change
1. **Cycle of Seasons: 4 [6] becomes 7 [10]** to a random enemy. Both
   rounds called it a 1-cost Power that does almost nothing. 7 is a Strike
   each time the element changes, and it stays a single target (the AoE
   trim). The prediction for the next round: no NEVER AGAIN.
2. **Pyro and Cryo: test whether the decks work once built, before changing
   how often they are drafted.** The next Varka round uses seeds whose
   starter Knight is Pyro or Cryo. If those decks play well, the problem is
   the on-ramp, and the fix is drafting (for example, a Common that makes
   the element current). If they do not, the payoffs need work.
3. Backlog: items 1 to 3 above.
