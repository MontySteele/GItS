## What played well

- **The burst turn.** Seat 2 spent the Matriarch's three sleeping turns
  stacking the back performer, then played Bravura at 19 Fanfare for 81 and
  woke the boss Stunned (222 to 132). Bravura and Critics' Darling are the
  real payoffs.
- **The Guest Cast with reactions.** Hydro from Chevalmarin or Neuvillette,
  then Electro from Clorinde, made Poison, which stacked to 18 on the act-2
  boss. Tutti! and Grand Finale made the biggest turns. Casting Agent+ gave a
  free upgraded Guest Star every fight.
- **Thunderous Applause+ on a thin stage** was seat 1's act-3 engine: every
  hit Bowed a performer, which drew a card and refilled the back, for 8+ acts
  per enemy turn.
- The preview lines (reaction preview, "what the acts will deal") carried
  the best decisions.

## What did not

- **Defence decides the losses.** Every near-death and the act-3 death came
  on turns with no Block card in hand (seat 1 at 7 HP after act 2; 60
  incoming at 26 HP in the boss's phase 2).
- **Spend's silent failures,** both seats:
  - A Spend drained Neuvillette below his own act's price, so his act did
    not fire.
  - Spend cards played at their base numbers on empty-stage turns.

  The rules pass (back-first Spend from the whole stage) answers the second
  and part of the first.
- **Enemy rules that shut her off.** Artifact ate her Weak and her Poison.
  Test Subject's Enrage gains Strength per Skill, and her deck is mostly
  Skills (34 of 78 cards since the rules pass, more in a drafted deck).
- **Statuses** (10 Burns from an elite, Toxic, Beckon) clogged later hands;
  Burns land on the front performer.
