**What it says.**
1. **The scaling read is right.** In act 1 Klee deals the same damage a turn
   as a base character. From act 1 to act 3 the base five's damage grows
   2.5 times (19.5 to 49.0) and Klee's only 1.75 times (20.1 to 35.1). The
   gap opens in act 3, not act 2, on these paired seeds. The paper's act-2
   figure of about half came from unpaired fights across many seeds.
2. **The HP gap is not a Block gap in ordinary fights.** Klee gains as much
   Block a turn as the base five, or more, yet loses 1.3 to 1.8 times their
   HP. Her act-2 normal fights last 4 turns against their 3 (median): Bomb
   damage lands when it is set off, so enemies live a turn longer and attack
   once more. The seats felt this as "Block is the weak spot" (four seats
   said so); the numbers point at the extra enemy turns.
3. **Where the damage comes from.** Bombs and Mines are Klee's largest
   source in every act (act 1: Bombs 1,257 and Mines 463 damage, against Strike's 717). Big Badda Boom is
   the one card whose damage per play grows with the run (20.9, 34.8, 36.9).
   Ka-pow!'s own hit is 3.5 to 9 a play: it is a trigger, not a damage card.
   Strike was played 125 times in act 1, the most of any card.

**Instrument gap.** A fight the player dies in writes no telemetry line: the
two act-1 boss deaths (lanes 3 and 4) are missing, so Klee's boss rows count
only the fights she won. Added to `BACKLOG.md`.

## What the seats said

- **Played well:** holding Ka-pow! until a Bomb grew to exact lethal (every
  lane); Big Badda Boom as the payoff (a 290-damage kill on the act-2 boss,
  lane 1); Fireworks Finale on four Exoskeletons; Diona's Cryo Block into a
  Melt Ka-pow!; Durin Dark+ making Mines lethal before the enemy acts.
- **NEVER AGAIN:** Klee's Secret Base (its "no Bomb of yours" condition was
  almost never true), Biiig Hug (twice: the Soot clogs hands), Cover Your
  Ears!, Sparkling Burst (never worth playing, two seats).
- **Recurring failures:** reaching a boss or forced elite at a third of HP or
  less; Spark-cost cards dead at the start of a fight (Sparks only come from
  Bombs going off); turns with no set-off card in hand.

## Screen and text problems

1. "Incoming this turn" ignores Mines that go off before the enemy attacks,
   and the Block Look Out! gives from them (lane 5, twice).
2. "Bomb: deals 111 with Melt" reads as if the whole pile gets 1.75 times;
   only the oldest Bomb does (lane 2).
3. Big Badda Boom's "damage equal to what your Bombs dealt" counts only damage
   past Block; the card does not say so (lane 1).
4. Chained Reactions' +3 did not apply when Ka-pow! set off two Bombs at once
   (lane 1). Needs a trace.
5. The Spark line disappears at 0 Sparks; Snecko Oil's re-cost adds Spark
   costs its text does not mention (lane 1).
6. Wait For It... printed "Nothing ... landed" on turns it paid out (lane 2).
7. Weak shows different Pyro numbers on the card and the board (Fish Blasting
   printed 8, dealt 6; lane 4).

Items 1 to 7 go to `BACKLOG.md` as one line.
