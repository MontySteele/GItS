matter, with Spend cards strong enough to answer it?

Earlier seat runs on the pre-fade builds: two wins, one death in act 1, and
(after the 2026-09-29 audit pass) one loss at the act 2 boss.

## What played well

- **The fade is seen and read correctly.** Both seats quoted the page ("The
  applause fades: Clorinde 6 → 5") and learned the steps (0 below 4, 1 at 4-7,
  2 at 8-11, 3 at 12-15) without help.
- **Spend-now play.** Seat B cashed Fanfare the turn it was made (Rising
  Applause then Bravura), and Seat A stopped feeding a performer already at 8
  or more because a quarter would come off. Paying Curtain Rise's Spend 3 from
  a 3-Fanfare Usher, which empties the shield, was a real choice every turn.
- **The Spend cards carried.** Bravura with a healthy back performer, Grand
  Entrance+ at 7 Fanfare and Chevreuse's Ring were the cards both seats most
  wanted. Wriothesley's Bow landed 17 to 43 Cryo after Stage Whisper or
  stacked Block.

## What did not

- **The fade almost never decided a play.** Seat B, act 3: "never a decision I
  noticed, only a number"; it changed play twice in six fights. Enemy hits on
  the front performer took far more Fanfare than the fade in every record.
- **The front performer's regain cancels it.** A lone front performer at 4-7
  loses 1 and regains 1. The upgraded starter relic, Curtain Never Falls,
  regains 2, which cancels the fade on the front up to 11 Fanfare. The fade
  only bites at the back and above 8.
- **Deaths came from bosses that strip the stage.** Knowledge Demon's
  Disintegration hit the front performer every turn (Seat A picked it over
  Mind Rot), and Test Subject's third form hit for 30 to 47 through
  Intangible turns. Neither was a fade death.
- **Weak cards named:** Regal Bearing (twice), Commanding Gaze and Tidal
  Flourish's plain side (5 to ALL).

2. **Curtain Never Falls:** its +2 regain makes the front fade-proof in most
   fights. Decide with the fade: lower it to +1 plus something else, or leave

3. **Display, to BACKLOG:** a reviving boss vanishes mid-turn and the acts
