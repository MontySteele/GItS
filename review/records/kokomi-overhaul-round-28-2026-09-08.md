# Kokomi round 28: both granted faces reached the hand; Scout Ahead counts and pays exactly and is always written last; Read the Field's look wants energy left and its Block is a boss card

Written 2026-09-08, morning. Two blind Opus seats on `0.2.3035+proto` (main
`1a4f87cf`), both decks granted Read the Field and Scout Ahead, otherwise
natural. Records: `review/qa/kokomi-round-28-2026-09-08/opus-lane1-granted.md`
and `opus-lane2-granted.md`. Prototype stage, Guardrail 7. Round 27's two
unseen faces are the subject; pass five (`EB-685`) waited for this read.

## 1. The hypothesis

"With both faces in the deck from floor 1, a seat plays Read the Field with
energy to spare and names why it took the card it took; writes Scout Ahead
at least once alongside another Plan and its count (itself included)
matches the cards drawn; and says which of the two halves of each it
preferred and why."

**Lane 1**: 240 of 240, both act-1 elites cleared, the Ceremonial Beast at
136 of 252 on the budget. **Lane 2**: 210 acts, died at the Waterfall
Giant's Death Blow (45), the second seat in two rounds to lose a run to
that boss's hidden phase number.

## 2. The reads

**1. Read the Field, face-up.** Four plays across the two seats. Two were
the first card of fight 1 with two energy left, and both seats took Scout
Ahead over a Strike, for the Plan line; neither played the take that turn.
Lane 2's second play at the boss is the card working: three energy left,
took Slack Water for the Weak, played it, took 0 that round. Lane 1's second
play misfired: one card in the draw pile, no selection screen, a Defend put
in hand and the energy charged with nothing printed (`EB-686`). Lane 1
never wrote its Plan and says why every time: 10 Block at the start of the
next turn is a turn late for the hit on the screen, and Breakwater+'s Dusk
"exists specifically to fix the flaw Read the Field has". Lane 2 wrote it
twice at the boss, where the telegraph runs a turn ahead and 10 beats a
Defend's 5 at the same cost: "Read the Field's own Plan was the best block
card in the deck." One seat never wanted it; the other used both halves
well. Kept as built.

**2. Scout Ahead.** Written three times (lane 1) and twice (lane 2); every
count matched the draw and the carry-out row printed it: 2, 2, 2 and 2, 3.
Both seats write it last, after the damage and the energy Plans, and lane 2
says the reason in the card's terms: "it is a payoff card that wants two
other Plans already on the jellyfish"; cut on four of the six turns it
competed for energy. Neither seat tested writing it first, and lane 2
believes first would have drawn 1, which the build says is not so (the
count is the whole morning). The face should say "this one included, in any
order" so the belief cannot form; folded into `EB-685`.

**3. Breakwater.** Lane 1 drafted the upgrade and valued the Dusk clause
as the kit's late-Block fix; two Dusk carry-outs at the boss. No count was
reported against the clause. Pass five's phase change stands.

**4. Preference.** Read the Field: lane 1 neither half, lane 2 the Plan at
the boss and the look with energy to spare. Scout Ahead: both seats the
written half, last, when two Plans are already there; the face-up draw on
turn 1 of a fight or ahead of a hand-upgrading potion.

**5. Elsewhere.** Both seats: the write-or-play question carried nearly
every turn and the order of Plans within a turn is "the subtlest good thing
in the kit" (Vanguard then Ambush 18, the reverse 12; Oath, Ripple, Scout
Ahead last for three cards). Ripple's Plan (an energy and 4 Block for 0) was
lane 2's best card; Sango Isshin lane 1's. Opening Gambit's doubling clause
sat dead again with only Block or draw Plans beside it (`EB-687`, its third
round). Defend and Strike are filler that cannot be pointed at the pet.

## 3. What this says

Both faces work as printed and neither is automatic: the look is a first-
card play and a boss-turn tool, the scout is a third Plan. Nothing here
reopens pass four. The round's defects are on the screen: a silent take, a
face that does not carry its own condition, a panel sentence that describes
only half its rule, and two folding inconsistencies. Pass five builds now.

## 4. Where the screen and the outcome disagreed

- **Read the Field with one card in the pile** printed nothing and picked
  for the seat. `EB-686`.
- **Opening Gambit** doubles nothing when the next Plan deals no damage,
  and the face does not say so. `EB-687`.
- **A Plan written under Shrink paid full** (lane 1, Oath 7), as Riptide
  did under Weak in round 27, while a Block Plan pays Frail (`EB-659`). The
  panel says "a debuff on you afterwards"; the rule is that the jellyfish's
  hits are its own and its Block is yours. `EB-688`.
- **The enemy's Vulnerable folded into Undertow and Gorou and not into
  Strike and Ambush** on one screen, while the player's Weak folded into
  every card. `EB-689`.
- **`play "Riptide" on "A"`** refused with no hint that the card aims
  itself. `EB-690`.
- Not ours: a one-point Weak-and-Vulnerable rounding gap; the Waterfall
  Giant's phase whose HP "is not a number this turn"; two-part intents; the
  reactions box (`EB-675` already).
