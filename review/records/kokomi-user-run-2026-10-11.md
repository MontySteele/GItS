# Kokomi, [USER]'s run (2026-10-11): a win that felt clunky, three card fixes

Build played: `0.2.4969` (deployed the evening of 2026-10-11). Solo. Kokomi
is at Prototype.

## What [USER] said

- "I won the Kokomi run, but honestly... it felt clunky. Not difficult, but
  clunky. The early game was slow and the late game was Plan Spam."
- "I think a cleaner version of the same concept would work, but there's
  something messy in the execution that's hard to pin down."
- ""Duck Plans" - it makes sense to have some cards give +1 Charge on the
  Casket and act the same turn, but they feel kinda silly." Then, correcting
  the typo: "Dusk plan*".
- "Tidecleanse as a cycling card at Common is too good  Likewise Change of
  Plans is insanely good with the upgrade removing Exhaust  - it's an auto
  pick"
- "Sango Isshin should probably be "plans this combat" rather than "plans
  this turn""
- "Overall I had insane draw and energy production which let me spam out Plan
  cards in the endgame. Grand Design definitely carried acts 1 and 2, but Act
  3 was still easy when it wasn't up."

## What follows

1. **Tidecleanse loses its draw.** The 2026-10-11 card pass added "Draw 1
   card."; it comes off. Now: "Apply 1 Weak. Plan: Exhaust up to 2 [3]
   statuses or curses in your hand."
2. **Sango Isshin counts this combat.** Now: "Deal 8 [10] Hydro damage to
   ALL enemies, plus 2 [3] for each Plan carried out this combat." It was 6
   [8] for each Plan carried out this turn.
3. **Change of Plans' upgrade keeps Exhaust.** The upgrade used to take
   Exhaust off; it now draws 3 instead of 2. Cost stays 0.
4. **Dusk Plans and the rest of the clunkiness go to a Kokomi clean-up
   paper**, which the main session is writing.
