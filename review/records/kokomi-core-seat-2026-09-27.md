# Kokomi core pass: one short seat, 2026-09-27

One blind Claude seat played act 1 on build 0.2.3938 (#726), as Kokomi at A0, on lane 1. It stopped at the 150-action cap. By then it had won six fights and was in round 5 of the four-Gardener elite, with one Gardener at 1 HP. Its HP went 80 → 35, back to 59 after a rest, and ended at 39. The raw record is gitignored in `review/qa/seats-2026-09-27/kokomi-core-lane1.md`.

The pass (`review/ruled/kokomi-core-pass-2026-09-27.md`) asked one question: does a turn with no incoming attack now ask a question?

## What played well

- **Now or plan was a live question on most turns, turn 1 of fight 1 included.**
- **The rewritten cards were used both ways, for reasons the board gave.**
  - Ambush was played now for 2 Vulnerable (and the Casket's hit) to set up Slack Water and the Oath Plan. It was written when 12 damage the next morning would finish an enemy on its own.
  - Ripple was played now on a no-attack turn ("With no attack incoming, damage now beat energy later"). It was written on turn 1 for next turn's Energy.
  - A quiet turn did produce a choice. That is what the pass was for.
- **Tide Wall's Plan was the kit working.** Written a turn ahead, it gave Block equal to the printed intent every time (8, 12, 8, 13), which freed the next hand for attacks.
- **Ordering puzzles came from reactions.**
  - Well Laid before Shinobu gave two Electro-Charged reactions.
  - The Casket re-applied Hydro between Shinobu's two hits.
- **The best turn came from an enemy's rule.** Fossil Stalker's Suck gains Strength on unblocked damage, so the seat blocked everything now instead of writing Plans.

## What did not

- **Treatise never drew a card.** The seat played it only as a last card with spare Energy, and each time the fight ended soon after. Its reason was: "it pays for playing Plan cards now, which the kit never wants". That is one seat's read, and the card never had a turn to prove otherwise. It goes to [USER]'s run as a watch item.
- **One quiet turn was still "plan everything"** (fight 6 turn 1: Ambush and Tide Wall both written). That was on a turn when Ripple, Well Laid and Shinobu had already been played now, so the turn was not empty.
- **Two gaps in the printed text:**
  - The Neow bundle's Plan gloss leaves out "instead of playing it now", so a reader can think the card does both.
  - The Casket did not answer a debuff that came from a reaction off its own hit. It did answer a debuff from a reaction off a card. That looks like a no-recursion rule that nothing prints.
- **Two things the seat could not tell were intended:**
  - Skittish never triggered on a Planned hit. Planned hits are not hits (earlier rounds), so this is intended.
  - Shinobu's "+5 additional damage" landed as a separate hit. That is companion text, not Kokomi's.
- **Not seen:** Song of Pearls, Second Wave, Feigned Retreat, Cleansing Wave and Chain of Command were not drafted. The empty-queue payoff is unread.
