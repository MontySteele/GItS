# Kokomi: the forced growth test, 2026-10-12

**What ran.** Four Sonnet Kokomi seats at A0 on 0.2.5016, each starting with Watchful Shoal in the deck (`embark --arm proto_kk_watchful_shoal`). Watchful Shoal was rewritten for round 3 (#1139) as "Your Stings deal 3 [5] additional damage. Your Wards gain 3 [5] additional Block." In round 3 the seats took it once in 11 offers, so this test put it in every deck to read what it does once it is played.

**Result.**
- **All four seats died:**
  - Lane 4: the act-1 boss (Lagavulin Matriarch).
  - Lane 2: an act-2 elite (Infested Prism), entered at 21 HP on a forced path.
  - Lane 1: the act-2 boss (Knowledge Demon), which it left at 7 HP.
  - Lane 3: the act-2 boss (The Insatiable), which it left at 56 of 321.
- **Across 53 fights,** Watchful Shoal was played 22 times. Only 28 Stings and 11 Wards were played in the whole test, about 0.5 Stings a fight. Stings made 5% of her damage.
- **The seats' own words:** lane 3's NEVER AGAIN was Watchful Shoal: "it buffs Stings and Wards and the deck had neither for most of the run". Lane 1 called it automatic. Lanes 2 and 4 won with Poison (Electro on Hydro) and never relied on it.

**Reading.** Like the Silent's Accuracy, it pays only in a deck dense with tokens, and her Sting supply is too thin for it to be her general late growth. It can stay as a jellyfish build-around, but it does not answer the late-growth question. That question goes to [USER] (`QUEUE.md`) after his own run.
