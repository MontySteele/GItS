# Kokomi round 27: two of the four rebuilt faces never appeared, Breakwater's clause paid zero four times because it counts the wrong turn, and Night Watch lost every draft it was in

Written 2026-09-08, morning. Two blind Opus seats, natural draft, on
`0.2.3035+proto` (main `057aefc4`), the first build with `EB-679` (pool pass
four: Night Watch, Breakwater, Scout Ahead, Read the Field). Records:
`review/qa/kokomi-round-27-2026-09-08/opus-lane1-natural.md` and
`opus-lane2-natural.md`. Prototype stage, Guardrail 7. The seat briefs named
the interpreter by absolute path after round 26's alias hang; neither seat
lost a call to it.

## 1. The hypothesis

"A seat that drafts any of the four rebuilt faces states what it does in its
own words, counts Breakwater's and Scout Ahead's Plan-count against the
number paid and they agree, chooses Read the Field's take for a reason it
can name, and picks between Night Watch and Slack Water by the number of
bodies at least once."

**Lane 1**: 233 of 240 acts, the Kin killed (Lizard Tail fired on a
deliberate lethal), floor 21 of act 2 on the budget. **Lane 2**: 202 acts,
died at the Waterfall Giant: poison killed it at the start of its turn and
its Death Blow phase (33) fired anyway, 14 HP behind 11 Block.

## 2. The reads, face by face

| face | lane 1 | lane 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Night Watch | offered twice, taken neither time | never offered |
| Breakwater | bought; played twice, paid 5 both times (count 0) | drafted; played twice, paid 5 both times (count 0) |
| Scout Ahead | taken upgraded; played once face-up at 0 for 1 card; Plan never written | never offered |
| Read the Field | never offered | drafted; played twice, both with no energy left to play the take |

**Breakwater.** Four plays across two seats, four zero counts, four payouts
of 5, each count checked against the printed number and agreeing. The
clause counts the morning's carry-outs, and a written Plan carries out the
next morning, so the bonus exists only on a turn after one spent writing,
when the seat wants its energy on the payoff. Lane 1: "in nine fights I never
once reached a board where Breakwater beat a Defend." Lane 2: "a card that is
only good in a deck I never got." The count is right and the phase is wrong.

**Night Watch.** Lane 1 turned it down twice, blind, with the reasoning the
card was built to invite: "its whole output against a single body is 1 Weak
plus the Casket's 2, strictly worse than Slack Water, which does that and 4
damage and can still be written; against a crowd it is better, but it
competes for the same write as Riptide's 17 to ALL." The choice between it
and Slack Water is settled at the draft, against it, before a turn can ask.

**Read the Field.** Lane 2 played it twice with its last energy and both
times the take (Riptide; Defend+) sat unplayable: "the filter is only worth
its energy if you can still pay for what it finds." An ordering the seat
learned and then repeated under pressure at the boss. The card is not wrong;
the seat says the face could warn. No row: playing a dig last is the
player's.

**Scout Ahead.** One face-up play at cost 0; the Plan was never written by
either seat, and lane 1 states why in the same words as Breakwater: the
clause reads off the current turn's carry-outs.

**Elsewhere.** Both seats: the write-or-play decision is real on every Plan
card (lane 1's turn 1 of fight 1 asked it with everything printed); Riptide
is the card that pays the kit's debuffs; Defend is a non-decision; the
Casket's answering Hydro re-reacts with a fresh Electro aura, so Slack Water
then Thundergrust pays Poison 8 and the reverse order 4 (lane 2 calls it the
deepest decision in the run and derived it from the reactions paragraph);
Nereid's doubled Ambush printed as two rows of 12; lane 2's Kusarigama made
three Strikes the best turn and crowded the pet out.

## 3. What this says

Pass four's read was that the safe-turn competition should be worth
something; two of its four faces never reached a hand, and the two that did
were built on a count that cannot pay on the turn a natural deck offers it.
The fix is a phase, not a rate: Breakwater should count the Plans the
Bake-Kurage is holding at Dusk, so the wall rises on the turn the engine is
written. Night Watch's job (Weak to ALL before the swing) is better held by
Slack Water's own Plan half moved to Dusk, and Night Watch retires. Read the
Field and Scout Ahead have no round yet: round 28 grants them.
