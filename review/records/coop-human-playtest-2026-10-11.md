# Human co-op playtest: Varka + Furina, 2026-10-11

**What ran.** Build 0.2.4925+next (`klee-next` c49eed2e), two humans in co-op, on Varka and Furina. The notes are the players', quoted. The checks and the follow-ups are Claude's.

## Varka

- "Solid! But reliant on powers for Block."
- [USER]: "Varka's still in a weird situation on Block - it was hard until I was able to power spam."
- "The Order Answers - random Knights are often commons, right? This seems bad."
- "Vow of the Blade is weak."

## Furina

- "Rising Applause doesn't say that it does Hydro damage." This is a text bug; it is fixed with no pick.
- "She was 'fine', but seemed to not have great damage scaling at act 3. Might need more Fanfare generation to keep up. 'Not a real kill option.'"
- "'Spend All' are kinda redundant with each other."
- "Seems kinda 'slow' - not enough draw / energy, at least in this run."
- "Not enough things to do besides the Spend / Drain / Repay loop."
- [USER]: "Furina's core loop is fine, but it didn't have enough 'oomph' - carried in act 1 and 2 when Varka wasn't online yet, fell behind in act 3 where Varka was strong."
- [USER]: "we should do a diversity pass on the build to allow for multiple unique playstyles besides the one core loop."

Against the 2026-10-09 co-op (`coop-human-playtest-2026-10-09.md`), where Furina "dominated act 3", the curve has flipped. Since then, the Spend cards were capped ("Spend up to X", 2026-10-10) and Standing Ovation became "Spend half" (2026-10-11).

## What follows

1. **Furina diversity pass.** A census of her pool by loop, with her draw, energy and act-3 scaling, then a paper of second playstyles and a fix for act-3 "oomph", with picks.
2. **Varka Block paper.** Card Block against power Block, compared with the base five, plus The Order Answers' Knight pool and Vow of the Blade, with picks.
3. **Hygiene:** element names on Furina's damage text.
