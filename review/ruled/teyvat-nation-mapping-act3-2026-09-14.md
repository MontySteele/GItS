# Act 3 (Glory) nation mapping — all six candidates ranked

Written per `teyvat-run-frame-2026-09-14.md` §3.2/§7.1. Scope: ACT 3 (Glory)
only — act 2 (the Hive) is a separate agent's table. Every enemy slot is
scored **only** where its best-fit body belongs to one of the six §7.1
nation-exclusive families (Inazuma: Rifthounds/Kairagi/Nobushi/Ochimusha/
Tengu/Bathysmal Vishaps/Enkanomiya; Sumeru: Eremites/Fungi/Shroom/
Aranara-adjacent/Consecrated Beasts/Withering; Fontaine: Meks/Fontemer
Aberrants/Breacher Primus/Tainted Hydro Phantasms/Clockwork Meka; Natlan:
Saurians/Tepetlisaur/Qucusaurus/Koholasaurus/Yumkasaur/Wayob/Sacred Beasts;
Snezhnaya: Fatui exclusive ranks — Oprichniki/Operatives/Harbinger-tier;
Abyss: Heralds/Lectors/Black Serpent Knights/Shadowy Husks/Rifthounds
shared with Inazuma). Pan-national families (hilichurls, slimes, Abyss
Mages, Ruin machines, Treasure Hoarders, Fatui Skirmishers, whopperflowers,
specters, cicins) score zero and are listed separately. Events, the
Ancient, and the boss score by which nation the top/secondary drafted
variant is set in, per the event-conversion and ancients galleries.

## 1. Result

Ranked by total scoring slots: **Fontaine (24) > Sumeru (23) > Inazuma (17)
> Abyss (8) > Snezhnaya (4) > Natlan (3).** Fontaine and Sumeru pull apart
from the rest almost entirely on the event layer — Fontaine's Melusine
register alone supplies a kept variant (top or secondary) for **16 of the
17 Glory-reachable events**, and Sumeru's Akademiya/Forest-Ranger/Aranara
registers cover 9 — while Natlan and Snezhnaya have **no event register in
the gallery at all** (47 drafted events, zero set in either nation). On
enemies, Sumeru's Eremite/Consecrated-Beast/Fungus families and Fontaine's
Mek/Breacher families both clear most of the twelve Glory enemy slots;
Inazuma trails on Rifthound/Kairagi/Black-Serpent coverage; the Abyss
scores respectably on enemies and owns the single strongest boss argument
in either gallery (Aeonglass ↔ Abyss Lector), but carries zero events;
Snezhnaya and Natlan each clear only a handful of slots and neither has a
faction voice drafted for this zone at all. **Proposed pair: Fontaine +
Sumeru. Single-nation fallback: Fontaine.**

## 4. Ranking, one line each

1. **Fontaine (24).** Owns the event layer near-outright (Melusine register
   covers 16/17 rows) and the Mek/Breacher family clears 5 of 12 enemy
   slots plus both boss slots (Iniquitous Baptist, Prototype Cal. Breguet).
2. **Sumeru (23).** Widest enemy coverage of any nation (9/12 — Eremite and
   Consecrated-Beast families are everywhere in Glory's ritual-caster and
   armored-devotee rows) and the only nation with a genuine Ancient
   showing (Nonupeipe, Vakuu, Darv all have a Sumeru variant).
3. **Inazuma (17).** Strong on events (10, mostly Sangonomiya-resistance
   secondary variants) and on Kairagi/Black-Serpent/Rifthound enemy rows,
   but scores zero on both boss slots and only one Ancient sub-slot.
4. **Abyss (8).** Thin on breadth but owns the single strongest citation in
   either boss gallery — Aeonglass ↔ Abyss Lector: Fathomless Flames, "the
   strongest single boss argument in the atlas" — and clears 7/12 enemy
   slots via Heralds/Lectors/shared Rifthounds; zero events sink it below
   Inazuma.
5. **Snezhnaya (4).** Only Fatui *exclusive-rank* bodies score (Oprichniki,
   Operatives, one flagged-mismatched Harbinger reading); the gallery
   never drafted a Snezhnaya-set event or Ancient for this zone at all.
6. **Natlan (3).** Weakest showing — one enemy row (Frog Knight via
   Tatankasaurus), a shared claim on Scrolls of Biting, and one uncontended
   boss reading (Lord of Eroded Primal Fire); no event, no Ancient.

## 6. Flags

- **No nation fits Tanx exclusively.** Every drafted Tanx variant is
  Liyue (Azhdaha) or Mondstadt (Andrius) or a pan-national hilichurl
  ("lawa," itself register-blocked pending a [USER] carve-out per
  `ancients-gallery.md:573`) — none is one of the six act-3 candidates.
  Recorded and parked, not proposed a fix.
- **Queen (the third Glory boss) has no candidate in any nation.**
  `reskin-gallery.md` records "zero claims across all 16 families" and
  the boss ships dropped in `act3_pool.yaml:12`. Parked, not re-opened.
- **Axebot is a single-nation slot.** Only Fontaine (Assault Specialist
  Mek, S) clears the exclusivity bar; every other candidate on the row is
  a pan-national Fatui/Treasure-Hoarder/Ruin body. Not a "no nation fits"
  case — recorded because it is the one enemy row where the pair choice
  has no second option at all.
- **No slot's only fit needs a mechanic change.** Every scored candidate
  above reads onto the *shipped* intent list; several rows argue partly
  from mechanics the sim already flags UNIMPLEMENTED (Frog Knight's
  Plating 15, Globe Head's Galvanic 6, Mecha Knight/Aeonglass's Artifact
  3) — those are pre-existing sim gaps orthogonal to the nation pick, not
  caused by it, and are not proposals to change anything.
- **Gang coherence, not per-body fit, governs the two multi-body rows.**
  Living Shield + Turret Operator and Punch Construct + 2 Cubex are each
  claimed by 2-4 different nations across their component bodies (e.g.
  Fontaine's Mek pair vs. Abyss's Herald+Lector vs. Sumeru's Eremite pair
  on the shield-and-turret row); a real pick must take one nation for the
  whole encounter, which the totals above already reflect by crediting a
  nation only where it can plausibly cover every body in the row.
- **Snezhnaya's best boss reading is under protest in its own source.**
  La Signora at Test Subject carries the boss gallery's own ⚑ for
  Harbinger stature and the mandatory deceased/memory framing
  (`candidates.md:488-507`) — recorded as Snezhnaya's strongest available
  slot, not endorsed as clean.
