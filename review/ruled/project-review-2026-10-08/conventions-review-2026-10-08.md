# Cross-kit card text and tooltip conventions (review, 2026-10-08, revised after fact-check)

Read-only review of the four kits and the companion cards against `docs/current/text-conventions.md` and the base game's templates. Sheet: `docs/prototype-surface.yaml` on `origin/klee-next` (commit 063e1b40; 375 rows: ko 84, kk 85, vk 84, mc 42, fs 36, mi 25, mf 19). Tips: `klee-mod/KleeCode/Cards/Prototype/ArmKeywordTips.cs`, `BaseKeywordTips.cs`, `KleeMod.cs`, `pck-src/klee/localization/eng/card_keywords.json`. Every number below was computed this session (scripts in `scratchpad/fanout/rev-conv/check1.py` to `check3.py`) or read at the file:line named. No game, lane or port was touched.

**Summary.** The conventions page is sound and the gate is green (`tools/lint_text_conventions.py`: "509 prototype-arm strings meet the ceilings and spellings"). The gate's card-face scope is Klee, Kokomi, the Mondstadt and Inazuma companions and Furina (`IN_SCOPE`, line 76); Varka's 84 faces and the 19 Fontaine companions are outside it, though Varka's power badges and tips are inside. That is where most of the drift sits: 10 Varka rows print an element plain against rule 6, and one Varka row carries the only parenthesis in the sheet. Between kits the same idea has two to five spellings: bonus damage ("additional" / noun-less "N more" on 14 faces across four kits / "plus N for each" / "instead"), repeating effects (four forms), first-time triggers (two), Swirl's object (five), co-op targets (five). No face is over the 120 ceiling except the carried exception. Medians against the base's 47: Furina 41 (her 2026-09-25 pass worked), Varka 61, Klee 62.5, Kokomi 64, companions 73 to 75. Of the 37 tips, 26 are measured by the gate and 15 of those are over the 90 target; 11 more are measured as zero characters because their text is a constant or a method call, and 5 of those 11 are also over 90. Three coverage gaps are real: the Frozen preview is 211 characters against a 135 ceiling and no lint reads it; the retired shipped Bomb tip is still injected; `Elemental Reaction` is golded 13 times with no tip. **Everything here is hygiene. The two picks in the first draft are withdrawn** (sec. 8): one re-asked a ruled design, the other rested on a claim the code contradicts.

## 7. Where a lint could stop the drift

What none of the lints reads, against the regex table at `lint_text_conventions.py:202-235`:

1. **Scope.** `IN_SCOPE = r"^proto_(ko|kk|mc|mi|fr|fs)_"` (line 76). Add `vk` and `mf`; drop the dead `fr`. Cost of entry: one finding, `proto_vk_pathfinders_mark`'s parenthesis: "Apply your current element to an enemy. If you have none, a random element."
2. **Noun-less "more".** `more-damage` matches only `N more damage|Block` (line 214). Widened to `\b\d+ more\b` it fires on 14 faces and 4 to 5 power badges (16 strings on main's rendered C#, 18 on klee-next's). Sec. 2 fixes 9 and keeps 5 as the scaled shape, so the rule needs a negative lookahead for ` for each` and a short allow list (`kurage_swarm`, `oath_unto_death`), or it stays narrow and the 9 are fixed by hand. Prefer the hand fix plus the lookahead.
3. **Element and pile gold.** Extend `bare-keyword` with the six elements and `Draw Pile|Discard Pile`. 15 rows fire (sec. 3).
