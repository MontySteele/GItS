## 2. What the round found

**Block now or Plan later is the kit's tension, and this seat kept choosing
later until it nearly died.** Your own first answer to the rules gate was
this trade, and the seat's sentences are the same trade made the other
way, turn after turn: "accepting the 8-damage 2x4 hit" to bank a Battle
Plan (turn 58); "I'll take 12 damage and go to 4 HP, but next turn I only
need 2 damage to finish it" (turn 99); "No Defend in hand, so I'll take the
10 damage", twice running (turns 95 and 96). That chain took the run from
49 HP to 7 across two fights, with no rest site on the path. The finding
is not that the seat played badly; it is that the trade is legible enough
to be made deliberately, and the kit's act-1 payoff for making it is a
10-damage morning. That is the number the acts-2/3 design has to beat, and
it needs the three-act read to price it.

**The morning carry-out line reached the page.** `EB-317` asked that a seat
record quote the carry-out line; 93 of the 121 pages carried one
("Bake-Kurage: Kurage's Oath (proto)+, 10", "Bake-Kurage: Battle Plan").
The seat priced its mornings off the planned number every time it wrote
one (turns 15, 36, 47, 73), so the page half of that row is met. The
casket's line is not on the page: six Slack Water plays, six Weak
applications, and no "Bake-Kurage: Tamakushi Casket" on any page, because
`EB-316`'s beat was built on screen only. Both screen halves are your
eyes-on at the fix build.

**One page rendered an event with no options.** "Doors of Light and Dark"
printed its title and nothing else on first render (turn 24); the seat
guessed, was refused, and the retry screen printed the two doors. That is
`EB-263`'s family (a live chest offering nothing to choose, a spent rest
site with no options); that row is full, so the instance lives here.

**Every map screen offered one node.** Seven map moves, seven screens each
with exactly one reachable node, and the Klee seats on lane 2 reported the
same on both of their map screens after the hand-off. Either the real map
is that linear from those nodes or the page drops siblings; `EB-325` is the
non-blind check, cheap, at the fix build.

**Smaller.** "(proto)" prints on four of the seat's card titles (Kurage's
Oath, Slack Water, Undertow, Song of Pearls); the seat never tripped on it,
but it is the same leak as `EB-322`. The Clouds Like Waves Rippling was
