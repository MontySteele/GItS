## 1. Result

Ranked all six: **Sumeru, Inazuma, Fontaine, Natlan, the Abyss, Snezhnaya.**
Sumeru and Inazuma are the proposed pair; Sumeru is the single-nation
fallback if §4.1 of the run-frame packet finds a zone can't be dressed two
ways. The split is stark and mostly explained by one fact: the 47-event
conversion gallery only ever drafted register blocks for three factions —
Melusines (Fontaine), Sangonomiya resistance (Inazuma), Akademiya/Aranara
(Sumeru) — so every Hive event's kept variants live in one of those three
nations, and Natlan/Snezhnaya/the Abyss own zero events between them.
Sumeru also fields the Hive's best-supported Ancient (Orobas, Pael and Darv
all read Sumeru) and its literal-match boss (Knowledge Demon ↔ Shouki no
Kami, the enemy-entity name literally *is* "Everlasting Lord of Arcane
Wisdom" — `dossiers.md:288`). Inazuma is close behind on enemy-exclusive
coverage (four S-tier Rifthound/Bathysmal-Vishap slots) and wins on event
*breadth* (18 of 25 events touch Inazuma, more than any other nation, mostly
Sangonomiya-resistance voiced). Fontaine has real Meks/Fontemer/Breacher
Primus coverage and a strong event bench but no Ancient or boss candidate in
the Hive pool. Natlan is the surprise of the bottom half: it has the single
richest run of enemy-exclusive bodies of any nation (Saurians, Yumkasaur,
Wayob cover six slots — more than Sumeru or Inazuma individually) but zero
events, so under the §7.1 "nation is carried by events... not by common
enemies" design view it cannot outrank the top three. The Abyss and
Snezhnaya are both enemy-and-boss-only nations with no Ancient and no
events; the Abyss edges Snezhnaya on enemy-slot count (4 vs 2), Snezhnaya
edges back with the single strongest boss pick in the whole exercise (Kaiser
Crab ↔ La Signora, a Harbinger, top of the boss gallery's own allocation).

## 4. Ranking, one line each

1. **Sumeru** — only nation with real weight in all four columns: five enemy slots, the literal boss match, three of the Hive/act-2 Ancient's four best picks, and top billing on eight events.
2. **Inazuma** — matches Sumeru's enemy-exclusive strength slot-for-slot (four clean S-tier Rifthound/Vishap rows) and wins outright on event breadth (18 of 25 touch Inazuma), but has no top-pick Ancient or boss here.
3. **Fontaine** — a real Meks/Fontemer/Breacher-Primus bench and the single strongest Slippery Bridge pick, but the Hive's Ancient and boss pools have no Fontaine candidate at all.
4. **Natlan** — the richest enemy-exclusive coverage of any nation (six slots, including the only Saurian/Wayob claims), plus a literal Ancient win, but zero events under the current gallery — decisive under §7.1's "events carry nation" view.
5. **The Abyss** — four solid S-tier enemy shares (mostly Rifthound-shared with Inazuma) and two boss alternates, no Ancient, no events.
6. **Snezhnaya** — thinnest enemy coverage (two slots) but the single best boss argument in the whole exercise (La Signora, a Harbinger, as the boss gallery's own top allocation for Kaiser Crab) — a lot riding on one very strong pick.

## 6. Flags

**No nation fits exclusively:**
- **The Obscura + Parafright** (hard pool) — every candidate (Hydro Samachurl top pick, Fatui Pyro Agent, Geochanter Bracer) is pan-national or an unlisted rank; the Wail all-enemies-buff discriminator has no nation-exclusive body (`reskin-gallery.md:49`).
- **Decimillipede** (elite) — the gallery's own flag stands: only claim is a self-declared stretch (3× Hilichurl Guard, pan-national), and Reattach has no analogue in any of the 16 surveyed families, let alone a nation-exclusive one (`reskin-gallery.md:50`, flags §1).

**Only fit needs a mechanic change (recorded and parked, not proposed):**
- **Kaiser Crab ↔ La Signora** — every weekly-boss candidate converts her canonical *sequential* two-form fight into the base encounter's *simultaneous* two-body fight; the boss gallery states this outright as the strain shared by all three candidates on this slot and does not propose fixing it (`candidates.md:354`).
- **Knowledge Demon ↔ Shouki no Kami** — the enemy-entity name is a non-playable weekly-boss entry, but the underlying model (Wanderer) is a playable roster character; the boss gallery flags this as a curation-only decision, not a mechanics one, and it collides with Act 2's own contention (Guardian of Apep's Oasis is claimed by both this slot and Kaiser Crab's slot-A alt) (`candidates.md:305-307`, `326-327`).

**Other things worth carrying into the pick:** several Hive events' single best-ranked variant sits outside the six act-2/3 candidate nations (Colossal Flower, Potion Courier, Relic Trader, Room Full of Cheese, The Future of Potions?, The Lantern Key, This or That?, Welcome to Wongo's all rank a Mondstadt or Liyue variant first) — this is because the event gallery was drafted before a nation constraint existed (`event-conversion-gallery.md:1`), not evidence against Sumeru/Inazuma; their in-scope alternates are still solidly loose-to-literal and are what's recorded above as "top-in-scope." Several of these events also carry pre-existing `[USER] decision needed` flags of their own (Colorful Philosophers, Doll Room, Ranwid, Relic Trader, Welcome to Wongo's) that are unrelated to nation and are not re-litigated here.
