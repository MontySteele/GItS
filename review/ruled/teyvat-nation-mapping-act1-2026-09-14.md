# Act 1 nation mapping — Overgrowth / Underdocks vs Mondstadt / Liyue

Written per `review/ruled/teyvat-run-frame-2026-09-14.md` §3.1/§7.1. Scoring
rule (§7.1): a slot scores for a nation only where its best-fit body is
**nation-exclusive** (Liyue: Geovishaps, Millelith, Liyue-only Treasure
Hoarder variants; Mondstadt: Dvalin-adjacent, Stormterror-era, Knights of
Favonius, Andrius/wolves). Pan-national families (hilichurls, slimes, Abyss
Mages, Ruin machines, Treasure Hoarders, Fatui, whopperflowers, specters, and
— by the same logic, not being exclusive to either nation — Rifthounds,
Eremites, Fungi, Saurians, Meks) score **zero** for both and are named in a
"pan-national" column instead. Events, the Ancient and the boss score by
their **top-kept variant's** setting; a non-top variant elsewhere is noted
but not counted.

## 1. Result

**The default stands.** Overgrowth's own top-fit content (Vantom → Andrius,
Ceremonial Beast → Golden Wolflord, plus the top-kept event variants of Room
Full of Cheese and The Future of Potions?) scores 4 for Mondstadt and 0 for
Liyue; nothing in Overgrowth scores for Liyue at all. Underdocks' one real
asset is Lagavulin Matriarch, whose top atlas body (Primo Geovishap, S) is
Liyue-exclusive — and she is canonically an *Underdocks* boss per
`act2-act3-roster-research.md` §4, even though the shipped `act1_pool.yaml`
imported her into the pool we call "Overgrowth." Read by true zone of origin
rather than by current YAML location, Liyue's only strong signal already
sits on the Underdocks side. No slot fights the default; the one dissent is
weak (§5). Overgrowth = Mondstadt, Underdocks = Liyue is confirmed.

## 4. Totals

Scoring only top-kept-variant / top-atlas-candidate hits, by **current YAML
location**:

- Overgrowth = Mondstadt, Underdocks = Liyue (default): Overgrowth side
  scores **Mondstadt 4** (Vantom/Andrius, Ceremonial Beast/Wolflord, Room
  Full of Cheese, The Future of Potions?), **Liyue 0**. Underdocks side
  scores **Liyue 1** (Lagavulin/Primo Geovishap — her one real body), even
  though she currently ships inside the Overgrowth-named pool.
- The reverse (Overgrowth = Liyue, Underdocks = Mondstadt) scores
  **Liyue 0** on the Overgrowth side (nothing there is Liyue-exclusive) and
  **Mondstadt 0** on the Underdocks side (nothing there is Mondstadt-exclusive
  at all — nine of eleven Underdocks rows are pan-national or have zero
  claimed candidates). The reverse assignment is strictly worse: it leaves
  Overgrowth's Mondstadt-exclusive content (Andrius, Golden Wolflord, two
  literal Mondstadt-top events) homeless and gives Underdocks no
  compensating Mondstadt signal at all.
- Read by **canonical zone of origin** instead of current YAML location
  (Lagavulin counted as Underdocks, per §1): default = Mondstadt 4 /
  Liyue 1, reverse = Mondstadt 0 / Liyue 0 (same conclusion, larger margin).

## 5. Slots that fight the default

None score for the reverse assignment. The only friction is weak, not a
fight: **Mawler**'s eighth-ranked, "P" candidate is Geovishap Hatchling
(Liyue-exclusive) sitting inside the Overgrowth pool — but it is explicitly
the gallery's weakest-ranked option on that row (Rockfond Rifthound, S, wins
it outright), so it argues for nothing beyond "don't cite Mawler as a clean
Mondstadt row."

Underdocks section (lines 75-84): the nine normal/elite rows (Sludge
