# Doctrine read: Klee stage one of the pool consolidation (R271), four changed faces, against the kit charter

Each numbered arm below is ONE changed or new card, built on branch
`klee-stage-one-2026-09-14` (EB-749) and read here BEFORE it reaches a
tester. For each, in order, give FOLLOWS or REQUIRES_MODIFICATION and name
the clause (C1 to C6) you ruled against, and quote the line and the
arithmetic (energy, Sparks, Block, damage, cards, turns) that breaches it,
in one sentence. A verdict with no clause named is not a verdict. Read
adversarially: the author is a different model family and the owner wants
the row that should not reach a tester named. Do not soften. Name clauses,
not remedies: a number, a face or a rewritten row you volunteer is
DISCARDED.

Why this read exists. Round 26 and the owner's consolidation (R271,
2026-09-14) cut Fwoosh! and Fireworks Show, redesigned Powder Charge as
Booby Trap, repaired Grounded (its old condition "if you have a Bomb on the
field" was nearly always true once Jumpy Dumpty is Innate, so it paid every
turn for nothing), capped Return to Sender, and gave Tinder Toss the slot
Fireworks Show held. These four are what changed.

## The charter

- **C1 (owner, 2026-09-04).** "I don't want us to end up gaming the metric
  of 'can an agent beat the game' by printing overly strong cards." A row
  that removes a losing line the kit is meant to keep, instead of pricing
  it, is out of charter.
- **C2 (LAW D3, binding prices).** A benefit carries a binding price. There
  must be a real turn in which the card is worse than a plain card of its
  cost. A Spark price counts as a price only if the bank it draws on is one
  the deck can run short of: read it against the income figures below.
- **C3 (LAW D2, player-controlled leverage).** The card's value is decided
  by a choice the player makes (which enemy; spend now or bank; which turn;
  which hit to block into), not by a number that rises while you watch.
- **C4 (Klee brief section 3 rule 7).** Nothing fires by itself: no
  start-of-turn detonation, no automatic free attack, no "at 3 Sparks".
  Every explosion is a Set off card the player played, a Mine answering an
  attack on her, or her Rare.
- **C5 (Klee brief section 9, as reframed by R270).** Sparks that only buy
  more Attacks are the named failure. A Spark-priced row must be one the
  player sometimes declines: it competes with the other sinks for the same
  bank, and there is a turn on which holding the Sparks is right.
- **C6 (not strictly better).** A row may not be a strictly better version
  of a pool row or a base-game card at the same or lower rarity and cost.
  Base-game reference points: Defend (1: 5 Block), Shrug It Off (1: 8
  Block, draw 1), Cleave (Common, 1: 8 to ALL), Whirlwind (Uncommon, X: 5
  to ALL per Energy), Metallicize (Uncommon Power, 1: 3 Block at end of
  turn), Regent's Cloak of Stars (Common, 0 Energy, 1 Star: 7 Block),
  Regent's Reflect (Uncommon, 1 Energy, 3 Stars: Block, blocked damage
  reflected this turn).

## The engine (as it stands on this branch, 2026-09-14)

A Bomb is a numbered charge on an enemy; every Bomb grows by 4 at the start
of Klee's turn and never goes off on its own. Only a card that says Set off
makes Bombs go off, each a Pyro hit for its own size. A Mine is a Bomb that
also goes off when its enemy attacks her, before the hit. Spark: each Bomb
that goes off gives 1 Spark; she starts every combat with 1; every Hexerei
companion card played gives 1; no cap; lost after combat. A Spark price
spends Sparks instead of Energy. Energy 3, HP 62. Starter: four Strike,
four Defend, Jumpy Dumpty (1 Energy, Innate: Bomb 8; when it goes off, a
Mine 3 on ALL enemies), Ka-pow! (0 Energy, Retain: Set off, 4 damage).
Relic: Pounding Surprise (the Spark-per-explosion rule).

Income, measured on the seats: a natural lane ends fights at 3 to 5 Sparks;
a lane built to spend ran 1 to 8; the bank is 1 on turn one by rule and the
cheapest existing sinks cost 1 (Set off cards, Dig In) or 2 (Blast Shield,
Return to Sender, Bottomless Bag).

Pool rows these are read against (pasted from the sheet):
- Pop! (Common Skill, 0 Energy: Place a Bomb 5).
- Mine Toss (Common Skill, 1 Energy: Place a Mine 4 on ALL enemies).
- Jumpy Dumpty (basic, above).
- Pocket Match (Common Attack, 0 Energy, 1 Spark, Retain: Set off, 5 damage).
- Quick Fuse (Common Skill, 0 Energy, 1 Spark: Bombs on the enemy grow 3,
  Set off).
- Rapid Fire (Uncommon Attack, 2 Energy: 4 times, Set off a random enemy
  and deal 3 to it).
- Flame Dance (Uncommon Attack, 1 Energy: Set off each enemy with a
  non-Pyro aura, 5 damage to ALL).
- Bombs Away! (Common Attack, 1 Energy: 3 to ALL, Bomb 2 on ALL).
- Dig In (Common Skill, 0 Energy, 1 Spark: 8 Block).
- Dodoco Cover (Common Skill, 1 Energy: Bomb 4, 5 Block).
- Run Away! (Common Skill, 0 Energy: 3 Block, 7 if a Bomb went off this
  turn).
- Careful Now (Uncommon Skill, 1 Energy: Block equal to your largest Bomb,
  up to 10).
- Explosives Workshop (Uncommon Power, 1 Energy: Bombs grow by 1 more each
  turn).
- Witches' Circle (Uncommon Power, 1 Energy: whenever you play a Hexerei
  card, Bomb 3 on a random enemy).
- Sparks 'n' Splash (Rare Power, 2 Energy: at end of turn, Pyro damage to a
  random enemy equal to its largest Bomb, without consuming it).

## The arms

1. **Booby Trap** -- Common Skill, 0 Energy, 1 Spark. Place a Mine 5 on a
   chosen enemy. Upgrade Mine 8. Replaces Powder Charge (Uncommon, 0
   Energy, 1 Spark: Bomb 6). The only single-target Mine on the pool; the
   decision is which enemy is about to swing. Compare Pop! (0 Energy, no
   Spark: Bomb 5) and Mine Toss (1 Energy: Mine 4 on ALL). Read C3 for
   whether choosing the enemy is a real choice on a one-enemy board, C5
   for whether 1 Spark is ever held rather than spent on this, and C6
   against Pop! at the same rarity and Energy.
2. **Tinder Toss** -- Common Attack, 0 Energy, 1 Spark. Set off ALL
   enemies, then deal 3 damage to ALL enemies. Upgrade 5 damage. Replaces
   its own old body (twice: Set off a random enemy and 4) and Fireworks
   Show (Uncommon, 2 Sparks: Set off ALL). Compare Cleave, Bombs Away!,
   Flame Dance, Rapid Fire. Read C6 against Fireworks Show's price (this
   is Set off ALL plus damage for 1 Spark less), C5 for whether a 1-Spark
   Set off ALL is ever declined on a multi-enemy board, and C2 for the
   single-enemy turn on which it is a 3-damage Ka-pow! that costs a Spark.
3. **Grounded** -- Uncommon Power, 1 Energy. At the start of your turn, if
   you played no Set off card last turn, gain 4 Block and 1 Spark. Upgrade
   6 Block. Two rulings are part of the card: a Mine going off because its
   enemy attacked is not a Set off card played, so a Mine deck keeps
   Grounded on; Sparks 'n' Splash is not a Set off card, so a deck holding
   both is paid every turn for not cashing by hand. Compare Metallicize
   (3 Block a turn, no condition) and Explosives Workshop at the same
   rarity and cost. Read C4 (the Block and Spark arrive by themselves; is
   the condition a choice the player makes each turn, or a switch that is
   simply on in a Cook deck), C3 for whether "cook or cash" is now priced
   by this card, and C1 against the Splash pairing: a deck that never plays
   a Set off card and gets 4 Block, 1 Spark and a free Splash hit every
   turn.
4. **Return to Sender** -- Uncommon Skill, 1 Energy, 2 Sparks. Gain 8
   Block. This turn, damage this Block absorbs is placed on the attacker as
   a Bomb. Upgrade 11 Block. Face unchanged; the rule is now that the
   conversion is capped at the Block the card granted, as one allowance
   spent across every hit that turn (blocking a 12 with it puts a Bomb 8 on
   the attacker, and two hits of 6 and 6 put 8, not 12). Round 26 read the
   uncapped version absorbing 18 of an elite's hits into a Bomb 32. Compare
   Dodoco Cover (1 Energy: Bomb 4 and 5 Block) and Regent's Reflect. Read
   C6 against Dodoco Cover at the same Energy (3 more Block and up to 4
   more Bomb for 2 Sparks), and C2 for whether 2 Sparks binds on the turn
   an elite attacks.

Four verdicts, each with its clause and its breaching line, then two
sentences: after these four, does the pool still hold a Common that is a
strictly better Pop!, and is there a turn on which a Cook deck holding
Grounded ever chooses to play a Set off card. Under 600 words.
