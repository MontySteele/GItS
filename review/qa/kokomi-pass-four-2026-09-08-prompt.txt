# Doctrine read: Kokomi pool pass four, the round-26 dead faces, against the kit charters

Each numbered arm below is ONE change. For each, in order, give FOLLOWS or
REQUIRES_MODIFICATION and name the clause (C1 to C7) you ruled against, and
quote the line and the arithmetic (energy, Block, damage, cards, turns) that
breaches it, in one sentence. A verdict with no clause named is not a
verdict. Read adversarially: the author is a different model family and the
owner wants the row that should not reach a tester named. Do not soften.
Name clauses, not remedies: a number, a face or a rewritten row you volunteer
is DISCARDED.

Why this read exists. Pool pass four (EB-679, 2026-09-08) rebuilt four rows
that round 26's two seats read as not worth a slot. It was built and played
(rounds 27 and 28) WITHOUT this read; the owner ruled on 2026-09-08 (R267)
that it goes to the door now, after the fact. One of its four changes, Scout
Ahead, has since been REVERSED by the same ruling, so arm 3 below is the
reversed row as it stands today, not the pass's version. Night Watch (arm 1)
was later retired by pass five, so arm 1 is read for the record only.

## The charter

- **C1 (owner, 2026-09-04).** "I don't want us to end up gaming the metric
  of 'can an agent beat the game' by printing overly strong cards." A row
  that removes a losing line the kit is meant to keep, instead of pricing
  it, is out of charter.
- **C2 (LAW D3, binding prices).** A benefit carries a binding price. There
  must be a real turn in which the card is worse than a plain card of its
  cost. Repeatable energy or draw at Uncommon or below is priced by
  something past the card's own cost.
- **C3 (LAW D2, player-controlled leverage).** The card's value is decided
  by a choice the player makes (play now or write; which order; which
  half), not by a number that rises while you watch.
- **C5 (Kokomi brief section 2 rule 4).** Nothing fires by itself; every
  effect is a card the player played and paid for.
- **C6 (not strictly better).** A row may not be a strictly better version
  of a pool row or a base-game card at the same or lower rarity and cost.
  Reference points: Skim (Uncommon, 1: draw 3), Seeing Red, Blur (Uncommon,
  1: 5 Block kept), Flame Barrier, Iron Wave, Shrug It Off, Pommel Strike,
  Whirlwind, Bludgeon, Sword Boomerang, Echo Form (Rare, 3).
- **C7 (Kokomi identity Law 4).** In her pool a Common never increases deck
  size.

## The engine (as it stands today, 2026-09-08)

The Bake-Kurage sits on her side all combat. A card with a Plan line may be
played ON it: cost paid now, the card to the discard, the Plan written to a
queue. At the START of her next turn (after the enemy acts, after her draw)
every queued Plan is carried out in the order written. A Dusk Plan is
carried out at the END of this turn, before enemies act. No cap (R266).
Riders: Opening Gambit (the next Plan carried out with it deals double),
Second Wave (the next is carried out twice). Readers: Well Laid (2 damage
+3 per Plan carried out this turn), Sango Isshin (Rare: if a Plan was
carried out this turn, a quarter of Max HP to ALL), Treatise (draw 1 once a
turn on a carry-out), Song of Pearls (3 Block once a turn on a carry-out),
Feint (Common, 1: 5 damage, 10 if a Plan was carried out this turn; Plan
10). Nereid's Ascension (Rare Power, 2): the FIRST Plan carried out each
turn is carried out twice. Change of Plans (Exhaust: carry out your first
Plan now); Second Thoughts (cancel your last Plan, its card and cost
return). Starter: four Strike, four Defend, Kurage's Oath (3 to ALL; Plan
7 to ALL), Slack Water (4 and 1 Weak; Plan 1 Weak to ALL, morning). Energy
3, HP 80.

## The arms

1. **Night Watch (rework; since retired by pass five)** -- Common Skill, 1.
   Dusk Plan: Apply 1 Weak to ALL enemies. Upgrade 2 Weak. No face-up half:
   it can only be written, and lands before enemies act this turn. Was:
   Dusk Plan: gain 4 Block and apply 1 Weak. Compare Slack Water's written
   half (1 Weak to ALL, the next morning, on a starter Attack with a
   face-up half), and a base-game 1-cost multi-Weak.
2. **Breakwater (rework)** -- Common Skill, 1. Dusk Plan: Gain 5 Block, plus
   3 for each Plan carried out this turn. Upgrade base 7. No face-up half.
   Was: Dusk Plan: gain 6 Block. After a three-Plan morning it blocks 14 for
   1 Energy at end of turn; after an empty morning, 5. Compare Defend (5),
   Shrug It Off (8 and draw), Blur; and read C3 against a number that scales
   with a morning already written.
3. **Scout Ahead (as it stands after R267: the position clause)** -- Common
   Skill, 1. Draw 1 card. Plan: Draw 1 card for each LATER Plan carried out
   with this one. Upgrade: cost 0. Written first of three it draws 2 the
   next morning; written last it draws 0; alone it draws 0. The face-up half
   is a 1-Energy cantrip. Compare Skim (Uncommon, 1: draw 3) and read C2
   against a Common whose written half can draw 2 or more for 1 Energy
   when the player orders the queue well, and C3 for whether the ordering
   is the player's choice or a free bonus.
4. **Read the Field (rework)** -- Common Skill, 1. Look at the top 3 cards
   of your draw pile; put one into your hand and the rest on the bottom.
   Plan: Gain 10 Block. Upgrade look at 4 / Plan 12. Was: 5 Block and look
   at 2, bottom one; Plan 10. The face-up half is now a tutor-lite that
   draws 1 chosen card of 3 for 1 Energy and no Block. Compare a 1-Energy
   draw-1 Common, Skim, and read C7 (deck size is unchanged) and C6.

Four verdicts, each with its clause and its breaching line, then two
sentences: does any of arms 2 to 4 make a safe turn's Energy compete with
writing, or does it pay the writer more for writing more. Under 600 words.
