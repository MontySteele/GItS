## Fight 1 — Shrinker Beetle, 40 HP

**Turn 1** (Energy 3, Spark 1). Played Jumpy Dumpty (Bomb 8 on the beetle), then
Strike. Rejected: Defend, because the intent line read *"Strategic
(DebuffStrong) — This enemy intends to apply a Debuff to you"* and Block does
nothing against a debuff; and rejected Dig In, whose 1 Spark is my entire bank
and buys Block on a turn with no incoming damage. So the turn's decision was
"hold the Spark", and it was a real one. I ended with 1 Energy unspent because
the only remaining card was that Defend.

**Turn 2.** Shrink landed: *"While Shrinker Beetle is alive, you deal 30% less
damage with every hit you land."* Strike's face changed from `Deal 6 damage` to
`Deal 4 damage` on the same screen — the best legibility moment of the round.
Played Chained Reactions, Defend, Strike. Rejected holding Chained Reactions: I
had no Set off card in hand and none had appeared, so the power was doing
nothing yet, but a 1-cost power is cheapest on a turn where the alternative is a
4-damage Strike. Took 2 (13-damage intent vs 5 Block? no — the intent that turn
was 7, and 5 Block ate 5 of it).

**Turn 3.** Drew Once More! (3 Sparks) and Blast Shield (2 Sparks), both
printing `CANNOT BE PLAYED: you have 1 Spark`. Played Defend + Strike + Strike
into a 13-damage intent, took 8. No alternative was rejected because none
existed: two of my five cards were unaffordable, one was the Defend and two were
the Strikes. **A dead turn.**

**Turn 4.** Drew Ka-pow! (0, Retain, Set off, deal 2). The beetle sat at 22 HP
under a Bomb that had grown 8 → 12 → 16 → 20. Played Ka-pow!; the fight ended
on the spot.

**Judged across the fight:** one real decision (turn 1, hold the Spark), one
plan that paid off (the Bomb grew for four turns and then killed in one card),
and two turns in the middle that were pure filler because the hand was Spark
cards I could not pay for. The kill also disagreed with its own screen: the
beetle was at 22 HP, the Bomb printed *"Set off here deals 20 Pyro damage"*,
Ka-pow! printed `Deal 2`, and Shrink printed *"30% less damage with every hit
you land, a Skill's damage too"*. 20 + 2 = 22 exactly if Shrink did **not**
apply, and 15 if it did — which would not have killed. Either the Bomb hit is
outside "every hit you land", or something I could not see finished it. The
screen did not let me tell.

Bank at the end of fight 1: **1 Spark**. It was never above 1 at any point in
the fight.

## Fight 2 — Leaf Slime (S) 15, Twig Slime (M) 28, Twig Slime (S) 10

**Turn 1** (Spark 1). Jumpy Dumpty put Bomb 8 on the M, then Fwoosh! (1 Spark,
Set off, 6) blew it: 14 damage, and Jumpy Dumpty's rider dropped Mine 3 on all
three. Rejected: holding the Spark for Blast Shield (2) or Once More! (3), which
I could not reach this turn anyway; and rejected bombing the 10-HP body, because
a Bomb on the fattest enemy is worth more and the Mines were going to spread
regardless. Also rejected Fwoosh! *before* Jumpy Dumpty, which would have wasted
the Set off entirely. **Spending the Spark was free:** the bank read 1 before
and 1 after — spend 1, Pounding Surprise refunds 1 when the Bomb goes off.
Then Strike on the small Twig; 1 Energy left over with nothing playable.

**Turn 2.** The small Twig's own Mine had gone off before its hit (10 → 4 →
Strike → 1) and paid me a Spark. Played Ka-pow! on the M (Mine 7 + 4 = 11),
Strike finished it, then two Defends. Rejected: Dig In (1 Spark, 8 Block) in
favour of two Defends, because the Energy was going to be wasted otherwise and
the Sparks were not — that is exactly the trade the kit is built around, and it
was the best turn of the fight. Took 0.

**Turn 3.** Fischl — Nightrider on the Leaf Slime, which was wearing a Pyro Aura
my own Fwoosh! had left: *"Reaction preview: Overloaded — Pyro meets Electro: 6
damage to ALL enemies"*. 7 + 6 killed the Leaf and the 1-HP Twig together.
Rejected: Strike + Defend, which does not clear the board. The decision was made
two turns earlier, when Fwoosh! left the aura.

Bank at the end of fight 2: **4 Sparks, all wasted** — the fight ended with them
in hand and *"Gone after combat"*.

Bank at the end of fight 3: **1 Spark**.

## The kit, after 6 fights

**(a) Which decisions felt like real choices.**

Four, and three of them were made before the turn they paid off.

1. *On the turn, fight 3 turn 2:* Nibbit telegraphed Attack **and** Defend, and
   Bomb's own text says Block stops it. Grow-or-cash, decided by a line on the
   enemy's intent. This is the kit at its best — the Bomb is a timer I control
   and the enemy can threaten.
2. *Earlier in the fight, fight 5 turn 2 and the elite turn 5:* Fischl to apply
   an aura, **then** the Set off card, so the Bomb's own Pyro hit reacts and
   Perfect Timing replays. Two cards in one order, both readable off their
   faces. The elite version of it was set up two turns earlier by Return to
   Sender making the Bomb.
3. *Every turn, the whole round:* spend Sparks or bank them. This is the kit's
   real dial and it is genuinely tense, because Sparks are *"gone after
   combat"* — a banked Spark at the last turn is a burned Spark, and I burned 4
   twice.
4. *At the draft, three times:* every card reward after the first offered three
   Spark sinks and one card that made Sparks or Block on Energy. Fischl,
   Grounded and Noelle were all "fix the income", and each of them visibly
   changed the fights after it. That is a real draft decision, but it is the
   same decision every time.

**(b) What felt automatic, and what never seemed worth playing.**

Ka-pow! is automatic — 0 cost, Retain, Set off; there is never a reason not to
play it, and it ended two fights by itself. Strike and Defend are automatic
filler and were most of what I played in fights 1 and 3.

**Sparkling Burst never got played once.** 3 Sparks for 1–2 Energy, on a deck
whose expensive cards cost Sparks and not Energy: the bank it drains is the same
bank the Energy is supposed to buy things with. I rejected it three separate
times and never regretted it.

**Once More! is close behind.** 3 Sparks to return a Set off card, but every Set
off card I owned was either free (Ka-pow!), 1 Energy (Perfect Timing) or itself
Spark-priced (Fwoosh!). I played it once, and only because the returned card was
the Energy-priced one; with Fwoosh! as the last Set off card played, the card is
a trap that spends 3 Sparks to hand you a card costing a 4th.

**(c) What I could not understand, or that contradicted its printed text.**

- **Shrink and Bomb damage (fight 1).** Shrink says *"30% less damage with every
  hit you land, a Skill's damage too"* and visibly rewrote Strike from 6 to 4 on
  its own face. The Bomb's `Set off here deals 20 Pyro damage` never moved, and
  the arithmetic of the kill only works if the Bomb was **not** reduced. Either
  the Bomb hit is not "a hit you land", or the Bomb line is a pre-Shrink number.
  Nothing on screen says which.
- **The reaction log printed empty on a turn a reaction demonstrably fired**
  (fight 5, turn 2). *"What reacted this turn"* was blank, and the enemy showed
  `Pyro Aura 2` after I had hit it with Electro — yet 44 damage only adds up
  with Overloaded's 6 **and** Perfect Timing's replay, and the replay's own
  condition is *"if a Bomb triggered an Elemental Reaction this turn"*. The
  glossary warns about exactly this ("no screen ever shows it gone and the
  reaction looks as though it did not happen"), which means the warning is doing
  the job the log should be doing.
- **Grounded's paid-line under-reports with two copies** (fight 4, turn 3):
  `paid 8 Block and 1 Spark` while the bank moved by more than 1. Whatever the
  truth is, that line is not it.
- **Two copies of Grounded, one buff row.** I could not tell from any screen
  whether the second copy stacked, and the buff row's number (`Grounded 8`) is
  Block, not stacks, which made it harder rather than easier.
- **A refusal that was the screen's own fault.** The rare-card chooser printed
  *"Say `confirm` after `choose` to take it, and until you do this chooser stays
  open"* directly above *"Confirm is not available."* I said `confirm` and was
  told *"there is nothing waiting to be confirmed"* — the `choose` had already
  taken it. One wasted command, and the same both-ways instruction appeared
  again on the Power Potion chooser.

**(d) The card I never wanted to play, and the one I was happiest to draw.**

Never wanted: **Sparkling Burst**. Happiest to draw: **Perfect Timing** — it is
the one card whose text rewards something I set up, and the turn it doubled was
the turn the kit made sense. **Grounded** is the honourable mention: it is the
card that turned five dead Spark cards into a hand.

**(e) Did the first turn of the first fight already present a decision?**

Yes, but a thin one and not the one the kit is about. The beetle's intent was
*DebuffStrong*, which made Defend and Dig In both worthless and left "play Jumpy
Dumpty and a Strike" as the only sensible line — the decision was *whether to
spend the one Spark on Dig In*, and the intent line answered it for me. The
first turn that presented the kit's actual decision was fight 2, turn 1: bomb
first or set off first, on which body.

**(f) Block turns and the bank.**

*Turns where I needed Block and did not get enough — which of the three was
missing:*

| turn | needed | got | missing |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 T3 (13 incoming) | 13 | 5 | **the Spark** — Blast Shield (2) and Once More! (3) both printed `CANNOT BE PLAYED: you have 1 Spark`; the only Block card I could pay for was one Defend |
| F3 T1 (12 incoming) | 12 | 0 | **the Spark** — no Defend in hand at all; Blast Shield 2 and Sparkling Burst 3 against a bank of 1 |
| F4 T1 (12 incoming) | 12 | 0 | **the Spark** — Return to Sender was the only Block in hand and wanted 2 |
| Elite T1 (17 incoming) | 17 | 10 | **the card** — I had the Energy and the Spark; Noelle's 6 + the Mine's 4 was simply all the Block in the hand |
| Elite T2 (13 incoming) | 13 | 5 | **the card** — one Defend and two attacks; Sparks were 3 but nothing in hand ate them |
| Elite T3 (~14 incoming) | 14 | 10 | **the Energy** — Dodoco Cover 5 + Defend 5 + Grounded used all three, and a third Block card would have needed a fourth Energy |

Three of six were the Spark, and all three were **opening turns**, where the
bank is 1 by definition and no Bomb has gone off yet. That is the shape of the
problem: the Spark economy cannot pay on turn 1, and half the deck is priced in
it. It never once was the Energy until the elite, by which point Grounded and
Dodoco Cover had fixed the income and Energy became the binding constraint
instead — which is the healthier failure.

*Turns where a Spark-priced card changed what I could do:*

- **F2 T1** — Fwoosh! (1 Spark) turned a placed Bomb into 14 damage plus Mines
  on all three bodies. Net Spark cost: **zero**, because Pounding Surprise
  refunded it.
- **F2 T2** — Dig In was *available* and I chose Defends instead; the choice
  itself was the change.
- **F3 T2** — Fwoosh! again, this time against a telegraphed enemy Block. It was
  the difference between 18 damage and a Bomb eaten by Block.
- **F3 T2** — Return to Sender (1 Energy + 2 Sparks) turned surplus bank into 13
  Block on the turn the fight was already won; pure burn-avoidance.
- **F4 T2** — Fwoosh! set off Bomb 12 and the resulting Mine killed the attacker
  before its hit. Dig In (1 Spark) covered the rest.
- **F4 T3** — Blast Shield twice, 4 Sparks for 12 Block, returning to hand both
  times. The only turn where the bank being *large* mattered.
- **F5 T3** — Once More! (3 Sparks) bought a second Perfect Timing.
- **Elite T4** — Return to Sender + Dig In (3 Sparks between them) made 20 Block
  against 18 incoming, absorbed all of it, and converted it into **Bomb 32**,
  which killed the elite the following turn. This is the single best thing the
  Spark economy did all round, and it is the card that reads worst on its face.

*Turns where I held Sparks instead of spending them, and why:*

- **F1, every turn.** Not a choice — the bank was 1 and the cheapest thing in
  hand after turn 1 was 2.
- **F2 T2** — held 3 (spent none) because the Energy was going to be wasted on
  Defends anyway and Sparks are the scarcer of the two.
- **F2 T3 and F4 T4** — held 4 and 4 through the killing turn, because the
  killing card was free. Both banks then **evaporated**: *"Gone after combat"*.
  8 Sparks burned across the round for want of anything worth buying on a turn
  that ended a fight.
- **Elite T2** — held 3 rather than play Once More!, because it would have
  returned Fwoosh! and left me 1 Spark short of playing it.

*Bank size at the end of each fight:* F1 **1**, F2 **4** (burned), F3 **1**,
F4 **4** (burned), F5 **2**, elite **not printed** — the fight ended on Perfect
Timing, which spends none.

The honest summary: with six Spark sinks and one Spark source, the bank was a
**wall for the first three fights and a surplus for the last three**, and it
crossed over the moment I drafted Grounded. There is no middle. A card that
prices itself at 3 Sparks is unplayable before that crossover and redundant
after it, which is why Sparkling Burst never got played and Blast Shield only
got played on a turn I was already safe.
