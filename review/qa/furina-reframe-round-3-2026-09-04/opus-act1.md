## Fight 1 — Twig Slime (S) 9/9, Twig Slime (M) 26/26, Leaf Slime (S) 12/12

**Turn 1.** Played **An Invitation** (0, "Add 1 random Common Companion card to
your hand") first, purely because it was free and it was the only card on the
screen that would show me what a Companion is. It produced **Kujou Sara —
Crowfeather Cover** (0, "Your next Attack deals 4 additional damage"). Then Sara →
**Soloist's Solicitation** on Twig Slime (S): 6+4 = 10 into 9 HP, dead. Then the
second Soloist's into Leaf Slime (S) (12 → 6), then **Aria of Recompense** (1,
"Gain 5 Encore").

*Rejected:* **Stage Presence** (1, gain 6 Block) for the third energy. Incoming
was 4 + 3 = 7 and I was at 62 HP, so 6 Block bought me 6 HP; Aria bought 5 Encore
and the chance to test what the unplayable card in my hand — Ethereal Spotlight,
refused with "CANNOT BE PLAYED: you have no Encore, and this costs 2" — actually
does. That trade (a certain 6 HP for information about the kit's own resource) is
the first real decision the fight offered and it is a good one.

I ended the turn holding 5 Encore and did **not** play Ethereal Spotlight,
reasoning that "Spotlight every Companion card" with no Companion in hand and no
Salon was 2 Encore for nothing.

**Turn 2.** The screen now printed 3 meters I had not been shown before: Encore,
and (after the first Salon card) Fanfare. **Encore had gone 5 → 2 across the turn
boundary with nothing on any screen having spent it.** See section (c).

Played **Salon Début** (1, "Add 1 random Salon Member to your Salon. Burst +5.
Elemental Skill"), which produced Surintendante Chevalmarin and — per a buff line
that the card itself does not print — *deployed* her: "A joining member performs at
once." She performed for 2 Hydro on Leaf Slime (S) and left a Hydro aura.

Then **Lynette — Enigmatic Feint** (1, "Swirl an enemy's aura onto ALL enemies.
Gain 5 Block") on Leaf Slime (S), spreading the Hydro to Twig Slime (M), then
**Chevreuse — Interdiction Fire** [Pyro] on Twig Slime (M) for the Vaporize the
screen previewed: "Pyro meets Hydro: this hit deals 1.5x damage." 26 → 15.

*Rejected:* Chevreuse straight onto Leaf Slime (S), which had the aura already and
would have Vaporized for ~10 into a 4 HP body. Spending a turn to move the aura
onto the 26 HP target instead of the 4 HP one is exactly the kind of decision the
elemental layer is supposed to create, and it is the best turn of the run.

**Where the screen and the outcome disagreed:** after the Vaporize, Twig Slime (M)
*still showed* `Hydro Aura 2`, even though the printed rule says Vaporize consumes
the aura. The screen's own long note about reactions warns that this can happen
when something re-applies the element inside the same beat, and Chevalmarin (a
Hydro member) had performed off the same card play — so the note covers it. It is
still a screen that shows the aura the rules just ate.

I also could not reconstruct the arithmetic: Twig M went 26 → 15 (11 damage) when
Chevreuse's printed 7 at 1.5× is 10.5 and Chevalmarin's perform should have added
1.5–2 on top. 11 is short of the sum and long of the multiplier alone.

**Turn 3.** Sara (0) → Chevreuse — Interdiction Fire on Twig Slime (M). Both
remaining slimes died on that one play (Sara is a Companion, so playing her also
performed Chevalmarin, which finished the 2 HP Leaf Slime). *Rejected:* Chevreuse
onto Leaf Slime — a 4 HP body does not need a 16-damage Melt-grade hit.

**Reward:** first upgraded set. Took **Macaron Break+** (1, "Gain 3 Encore. Gain 3
Block"). *Rejected:* Grand Salon+ (member numbers +2 — scales members I could not
afford to perform), Casting Call+ (capacity was not the bind; I had one member),
Sucrose+ (a second swirl when I already had Lynette). Encore was the thing I had
visibly run dry on, so I bought the fix for it.

## Fight 2 — Shrinker Beetle 40/40

**Turn 1.** Aria (5 Encore) → Soloist's (6) → Macaron Break+ (3 Encore, 3 Block),
banking 8 Encore. Then, with 0 energy left, **Ethereal Spotlight** (0 energy,
2 Encore) as an experiment: I wanted to know whether "Spotlight every Companion
card" is a lasting effect or a one-shot.

It is lasting, and the screen told me so in a buff line the card never prints:
`Guest Cast 1 — Companion cards are Spotlighted: 50% stronger printed damage and
Block, no Fanfare. Lasts until the Spotlight moves.` **Lynette's printed Block went
5 → 7 the instant I played it.** That is the moment the kit clicked.

*Rejected:* Regal Bearing (3 Block, 1 Weak). The beetle's turn-1 intent was a
debuff, not an attack, so Weak would very likely have expired unused and Block
would have absorbed nothing.

**Turn 2.** The beetle's debuff landed: `Shrink -1 — While Shrinker Beetle is
alive, your Attacks deal 30% less damage`, and Soloist's *printed number changed
from 6 to 4* to show it. Good. Played Salon Début (deployed Gentilhomme Usher, who
performed for Block), An Invitation (produced **Freminet — Pressurized Floe:
Backstroke**, 2 energy, "Deal 10 damage. Gain 6 Block"), then Freminet.

*Rejected:* Soloist's (4) + Stage Presence (6 Block) for the same 2 energy — a
strictly worse rate than 10 damage plus Block plus a member performance.

**Where the screen and the outcome disagreed — the clearest one of the run.**
Freminet printed "Deal 10 damage. Gain 6 Block." He dealt exactly 10 and my Block
went 3 → 15, a gain of 12 when the printed numbers on screen (6 from Freminet plus
Usher's 3) sum to 9. In fight 3, on a clean board with the Spotlight up and no
Shrink, the same card printed "Deal 15 damage. Gain 6 Block" and gave **9 Block**.
So Freminet's *damage* half shows the Spotlight buff in its printed number and his
*Block* half does not — while Lynette's Block half does. One card's two halves
disagree about whether the printed number is the real one.

**Turns 3–4.** Salon Début → Chevreuse → Soloist's (11 damage, matching the printed
numbers exactly this time), then Freminet (15) + Soloist's (4) to finish.
*Rejected each turn:* holding energy for Block. The beetle hit for 7 and 13 into
70+ HP; racing was correct and never felt close.

**Reward:** took **Charlotte — Framing: Freezing Point Composition+** [Cryo].
*Rejected:* Stage Lights+ and Curtain Cue+ (both key off the Spotlight, which I
already had a free source of every turn) and Duet+ (doubles the next Companion, but
my deck held only two Companions at the time and it would have been dead half the
draws). Charlotte adds a Companion, a cheap attack, a draw, and a second element —
Cryo onto Chevreuse's Pyro is Melt at 1.75×, the biggest multiplier on the board.

## Fight 7 (Boss) — Kin Priest 190/190, Kin Follower 58/58, Kin Follower 59/59

the fight is 190 HP of Priest with 300+ HP of ablative escort. That framing is

either a virtue or the absence of a decision depending on taste.

(13) → Soloist's (6) into the Priest. 190 → 171.
*Rejected:* Ring of Bursting Grenades+ for 18-to-all. 18 into the Priest plus 36
into bodies that flee when he dies is worse than 19 into the Priest — *unless* the
Followers live long enough to out-damage that, which by round 5 they nearly did.

**Round 2 — the dead hand.** An Invitation, Stage Presence, Stage Combat, Casting

*Rejected:* Casting Call (1, "Your Salon has room for 1 more Salon Member") — I

**Round 3.** The best sequencing decision of the boss: Chevreuse+ first to *apply*
Pyro, Charlotte+ second to *Melt* it, and **The Crowd Answers** third so that the

*Rejected:* Charlotte first (no aura on the Priest at that moment, so no Melt and no

**Round 4.** Two copies of Chevreuse — Interdiction Fire+ at 13 each, plus Lisa.

**Round 6.** Ring of Bursting Grenades+ at 22 killed **both** Followers in one cast

## The kit, after 7 fights

**(a) Which decisions felt like real choices, and what they traded off.**

Three kinds, and they are good ones.

1. **Where to point the reaction.** Fight 1 turn 2 is the model: Chevalmarin's
   perform put Hydro on a 4 HP slime, and I spent a whole card (Lynette's swirl) to
   move that aura onto the 26 HP one so Chevreuse's Pyro would Vaporize the target
   that mattered. Trade: a turn of tempo and 5 Block for roughly +4 damage placed
   where it counts. The Melt-vs-kill decision in fight 6 is the same shape and I
   chose the greedy side and got punished for it.
2. **Sequencing within the turn.** Boss round 3 — Pyro first, Cryo second to Melt
   it, The Crowd Answers third to be paid for the reaction — is three cards where
   each one's value depends on the two before it. Fight 3 turn 2 (deploy the Hydro
   member *before* the Cryo attack so it Freezes) is the same idea in miniature.
3. **What to buy with the third energy.** Almost every turn came down to "one more
   attack, or Encore, or Block", and because Encore is spent by Salon performances
   *and* by the Spotlight, banking it is a real cost. Fight 1 turn 1 (Aria over
   Stage Presence: 5 Encore instead of 6 HP) and fight 5 turn 2 (Regal Bearing's
   Weak over a random member's performance against a 4-hit attacker) are the two I
   thought hardest about.

The elemental layer is the strongest thing in the kit. Vaporize, Melt, Frozen,
Overloaded and Swirl all fired, all mattered, and the screen previews them
(`Reaction preview: Melt`) so the choice is informed rather than a memory test.

**(b) What felt automatic, and what never seemed worth playing.**

**The opening is fully solved by fight 4 and I ran it identically four times:**
Macaron Break+ or Aria (buy Encore) → Ethereal Spotlight (0 energy, 2 Encore) →
the biggest Companion in hand. There is no decision in it. Guest Cast "lasts until
the Spotlight moves" and nothing in a normal fight ever moves it, so the Spotlight
is a once-per-combat toll rather than a recurring choice — and the relic hands me a
fresh copy every turn, so from turn 2 on the card is simply discarded unplayed. A
free card that arrives every turn and is correct to play exactly once per fight is
a card I stopped reading.

Never worth playing: **Casting Call** (I bought it and it was a dead draw — Salon
capacity was never the constraint, because I never held more than one member);
**Stage Presence** (6 Block for the same energy as 13 damage, and I removed one at
a shop); **Salon Début** in any fight I expected to be short, since one member
performing for 2–3 at a cost of 1 Encore per performance rarely repays the energy
before the fight ends. The whole **Salon** subsystem underperformed its word count:
it is by far the longest rules block on the screen — Deploy, Evoke, front member,
three named members, Fanfare-per-10 scaling, three-quarters performance without
Encore — and across seven fights it contributed maybe 25 damage and 30 Block total.

**The Burst meter never moved.** Salon Début prints "Burst +5. Elemental Skill",
the keyword block says an Elemental Skill grants 5 and "every Elemental Reaction
grants 5", and I played that card six or seven times across fights that contained a
dozen reactions. **Every single printing of that block, in every fight, said "You
hold 0 of 70 Burst Energy."** I never saw the number change and I never received a
Burst card. Either the meter is not wired up, or it is wired to something the card's
own text does not describe. A 70-point meter that reads 0 after seven fights is
either a defect or a mechanic that does not exist in act 1.

**Fanfare** is nearly as inert. It printed on screen constantly, peaked at 15, and
the only thing it ever did was "+1 per 10 Fanfare" to member numbers I had already
decided were marginal. Its own buff text says "Cards read it and none spends it",
which is honest, but it means the meter is a number I watched for seven fights and
never once acted on. (Two cards I *declined* in shops keyed off 12 and 15 Fanfare,
so the payoff exists — it just is not in the starting kit.)

**(c) What I could not understand, or that contradicted its own printed text.**

1. **Encore vanished twice with nothing spending it.** Fight 1: I ended round 1
   holding **5**, played nothing, and round 2 opened at **2**. Fight 4: ended round 1
   at **3**, round 2 opened at **0**. Both times exactly −3. In fights 2 and 3 the
   same boundary preserved it exactly (6 → 6, 3 → 3). I could not find the
   difference. The screen's own caveat — "the game's data feed carries this meter's
   amount only: no maximum, and no rule for how it is spent" — means I cannot even
   tell whether Encore decays, whether something charged it, or whether the feed is
   lying. This is the single thing I most wanted a rule for and never got one.
2. **Freminet's Block half ignores the Spotlight in print but not in effect.** With
   Guest Cast up he prints "Deal **15** damage. Gain **6** Block" and delivers 15
   damage and **9** Block. Lynette, on the same buff, correctly prints 7 (from 5).
   So the same effect is shown on one card and hidden on another.
3. **"Spotlight Mode: 2", "Spotlight Moved: 1", "Spotlight Plays: 2"** appear and
   disappear in the meter list all fight and nothing on any card explains what a
   Spotlight *mode* is, what it means for it to have *moved*, or where the Spotlight
   is when it is not on the Guest Cast. Guest Cast's own text — "Lasts until the
   Spotlight moves" — is the only hint that the Spotlight has a default home, and
   the kit never tells you where that is or how you would move it back.
4. **Salon Début does not print that it deploys.** The card says "Add 1 random Salon
   Member to your Salon." The member performs immediately. The only place that rule
   appears is a *buff* line that materialises after the fact ("A joining member
   performs at once"). A card whose most important behaviour is printed on the
   result rather than the card is a card you learn by accident.
5. **The Slither enchantment has no text and raised a card's cost.** Soloist's
   Solicitation (Slither) printed identically to the plain copy on every screen —
   "cost 1, Deal 6 damage" — until boss round 5, when it suddenly printed **cost 2**.
   Nothing named Slither ever appeared in the keyword list. I took the enchantment
   blind at the Wood Carvings event and after a full act I still cannot tell you
   what it does; I only know it made a card worse once.
6. Minor: the Vaporize in fight 1 left the Hydro aura visibly on the target. The
   screen's reaction note explains how that can happen, but it means the aura
   display cannot be trusted as a record of what just resolved.

**(d) The card I never wanted to play, and the one I was happiest to draw.**

Never wanted: **Casting Call** — a 1-cost power that raises a ceiling I never came
near, drawn twice in the fights that mattered, played once out of desperation and
worth nothing both times. Runner-up **Stage Presence**, which I paid gold to delete.

Happiest to draw: **Chevreuse — Ring of Bursting Grenades+**. It opened four
fights, it cleared the entire elite's spawn wave in one cast after I had spent a
whole earlier turn setting the HP up for it, and in boss round 6 it killed both
