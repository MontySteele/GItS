## Fight 1 — Twig Slime (S) 8, Leaf Slime (M) 34, Leaf Slime (S) 11

**Stage when turn one opened:** one member, `Crabaletta` (front), and the Salon
log already read "**Crabaletta** hit Twig Slime (S) for 6 Hydro" before I had
played anything. So the fight does not start empty and does not start quiet — a
free 6-damage performance is banked before my first decision. That was true in
every one of the six fights. No Companion card was in my opening hand, so the
"did a Companion card played on turn one perform anybody" question is: none
available to play.

**Turn 1.** Played `Curtain Rises` on C → 6 damage, deploys Chevalmarin, who
performed 2 on C. Then `Salon Début` → Crabaletta joins, performs 6, kills C.
Then `Stage Presence` for 6 Block.
*Rejected:* `Ethereal Spotlight`. Its two tooltips shout at you — "**Buy it
before anything performs** — You open a fight with 2 Encore and this costs 2" —
but what it buys is "*Spotlight every Companion card*", and I owned no Companion
cards yet. **The loudest card on the opening screen was the one with no payoff.**
That was a real read and a real rejection, but only because I noticed the word
*Companion* was doing work the urgency text never mentions.
*Also rejected:* `Second Course` before `Salon Début`, so that Salon Début's
performance would be paid at full price (6) rather than dry (4).

**Turn 1, second half.** Attempted `Second Course` at 0 Encore. **Refused:**
"'Second Course' cannot be played right now: you have no Encore, and this costs
1." Clean, and the hand had already reprinted the card with `CANNOT BE PLAYED:
you have 1 Encore, and this costs 2` on the Spotlight a play earlier. The
affordance printing is good.

**Turn 2.** Took 0 damage (6 Block vs a 4). Played `Aria of Recompense` →
Encore 0 → **10** (Fanfare was 5, so both halves paid). Then `Ethereal
Spotlight` (−2 Encore) — and the hand immediately reprinted **Charlotte 4 Block
→ 6** and **Freminet 6 damage → 9**. Then `Curtain Cue`: its tooltip flipped
from "You have NOT moved it this turn" to "You **HAVE** moved it this turn" and
it paid 3 Encore + a draw. Then `An Invitation` → Lynette. Then `Freminet` on B
for 9 + Cryo; its performance sent Crabaletta at a **random** target — it took
the 2-HP Twig Slime instead of the Cryo-soaked B, so I lost the Frozen that
turn. Then `Charlotte`: 6 Block, and *its* performance was Chevalmarin, whose
2 Hydro landed on B's Cryo aura → `Frozen 1`, and B's intent fell 8 → 4 on the
screen.
*Rejected:* `Guest List` and `Lynette` — block and a body on the stage beat a
draw against 12 incoming.
**This was the best turn of the round and every piece of it was a decision:**
Aria before Spotlight (else Spotlight is unaffordable), Spotlight before Curtain
Cue (else Cue pays 1 instead of 3), Freminet before Charlotte (else the Cryo has
no Hydro follow-up).

**Turn 3.** `Second Course` onto a full stage → Evoked the front Crabaletta for
14 and the fight ended. That was the plan from turn 2 (fill to three, then let a
deploy Evoke), so the "obvious" lethal was two turns of setup paying off.

## Fight 2 — Shrinker Beetle 40

**Stage at turn one:** `Crabaletta`, having already hit for 6 unprompted. No
Companion card in the opening hand.

**Turn 1.** `Ethereal Spotlight` (Encore 2 → 0) → `Curtain Cue` (+3 Encore, draw)
→ `Salon Début` → `Curtain Rises` on A → `Charlotte`. 20 damage exactly as the
faces predicted, 6 Block.
*Rejected:* not buying the Spotlight and keeping 2 Encore for two full-price
performances (+4 damage). Spotlight+Cue nets +1 Encore *and* lights the deck for
the whole fight, so the "expensive" purchase is actually free if Curtain Cue is
in hand. **The Spotlight/Cue pair is the kit's cleanest piece of design.**

**Turn 2.** The beetle's debuff landed: `Shrink -1 — While Shrinker Beetle is
alive, your Attacks deal 30% less damage.` **Logging ask (4), damage arithmetic:**
`Freminet — Pers, Deploy! (Sharp 2)` printed **"Deal 7 damage"** and delivered
**7** (14 → 7). Unmodified it prints 6; Spotlighted alone it prints 9; here it
printed 7. The only arithmetic that fits all three is (6 × 1.5) + 2 = 11, then
× 0.7 = 7.7 → 7. So Spotlight multiplies the base, Sharp adds after, the enemy
debuff multiplies last — and the card face showed the fully-resolved number. The
face never lied to me once all round.
Its performance printed "**Crabaletta** hit Shrinker Beetle for 4 Hydro … (dry:
it could not pay its Encore, so it acted at three-quarters)" — 6 × 0.75 = 4, and
**not** reduced by Shrink, which matches "a performance is not an Attack".
Then `An Invitation` → Neuvillette (4 damage after both modifiers) finished it.
*Rejected:* `Stage Presence` — the beetle was at 3.

Also here: Freminet's Cryo hit the Hydro aura → `Frozen`, and then Crabaletta's
Hydro performance **re-applied a Hydro aura in the same beat**, so the end-of-turn
screen shows `Frozen 1` *and* `Hydro Aura 2` and looks as though nothing was
consumed. The Elemental Reaction tooltip warns about exactly this. Warning it in
prose does not make the screen readable.

## The kit, after 6 fights

**(a) Which decisions felt like real choices, and what they traded off.**

1. **Buy the Spotlight or keep the Encore — on the turn, fight-opening, every
   fight.** 2 Encore is the entire opening pool and the Spotlight costs all of
   it, so lighting the deck means every performance that turn is "dry" at
   three-quarters. It is a straight trade of this turn's tempo for the whole
   fight's Companion damage, and the answer genuinely flipped: **no** in fight 1
   (I owned no Companion cards), **yes** in fights 2–6. That is the kit's best
   recurring decision.
2. **Hold `Aria of Recompense` for one turn to clear Fanfare 3 — on the turn,
   fight 4.** 5 Encore now or 10 next turn. Printed threshold, printed counter,
   real cost. After the Smith it is 8 vs 16 and the decision gets bigger, not
   smaller.
3. **Spend Encore as fuel or bank it as armour — across every fight.** Encore is
   one pool for three jobs (a card's price, a performance's price, and a shield
   that sits behind Block). In fight 3 the pool ate a 12-damage hit whole; in
   fight 5 it ate 16. Every performance I bought was a point of armour I did not
   have. This is the most interesting thing in the kit and the screen states it
   in one line ("One pool, in order: a card pays, a member spends 1 or acts at
   3/4").
4. **Fill the stage to three and then deploy on purpose, to force an Evoke —
   earlier in the fight, fights 1, 4 and 5.** Two turns of setup, one 14-damage
   payoff. The card that Evokes is not the card that set it up, which is what
   makes it feel like a plan rather than a button.
5. **At the draft: Chevreuse over Poised Riposte, reward 1.** Glam'd AoE that a
   Spotlight scales and a Pyro aura multiplies. Two of the six fights ended on
   that pick.

**(b) What felt automatic, and what never seemed worth playing.**

- **`Curtain Cue` after `Ethereal Spotlight` is not a decision, it is a rule.**
  0 cost, +3 Encore and a draw versus +1 Encore. Once both are in hand the order
  is forced. Same for **`Double Time`**, which refunds its own energy whenever a
  member is on stage — and a member is *always* on stage, because every fight
  starts with one. It is a free draw wearing a cost.
- **`Stage Presence` (6 Block) and `Regal Bearing` (3 Block + 1 Weak) were dead
  weight in five of six fights.** Once Aria is online, Encore is a bigger and
  more flexible shield than any Block card in the deck, and Block does not also
  buy performances. I played Stage Presence twice all round and Regal Bearing
  never.
- **`Soloist's Solicitation` (6 damage, no rider) is filler** — I only ever
  played it to finish something.
- Late in the elite, turns became "pay in the right order" rather than "choose".

**(c) What I could not understand, or that contradicted its own printed text.**

1. **The Evoke's damage does not follow the dryness rule and I cannot derive it.**
   In fight 4 at 0 Encore, three performances printed "(dry … three-quarters)"
   and the Evoke in the same breath delivered its full 14. The tooltip says "The
   card's Encore price pays for it" — `Curtain Rises` has no Encore price. So
   either the rule has an exception nobody printed, or the sentence means
   something I never worked out.
2. **The Salon log prints the Evoke *after* the joining member's performance,**
   while the card text says a full stage Evokes the front member **first**.
3. **Electro-Charged is applied as a debuff literally named `Poison`.** The
   reaction preview and the resulting body state use two different words for one
   thing.
4. **`Guest Cast` says "Lasts until the Spotlight moves"; `Ethereal Spotlight`
   says lighting "lasts this combat."** Since playing the Spotlight is what
   "moves" it, the buff's own duration text reads as though the thing that
   creates it also ends it.
5. **The Self-Help Book enchant screen silently omitted Chevreuse.** It offered
   an Attack to enchant and listed four Attacks; my best Attack, the Glam'd
   Chevreuse, was not among them, with no line saying why. I inferred "one
   enchantment per card" and could be wrong.
6. **Generated Companion cards may or may not join the deck.** `An Invitation`
   gave me a second `Freminet — Pers, Deploy!`, and the floor-5 deck screen
   listed `Freminet — Pers, Deploy! × 2` — one of them without the `Sharp 2` I
   had paid an event for. By floor 9 the deck listed one. Either the deck screen
   counts combat-generated cards, or something was added and removed. Either way
   I could not tell which of two identically-named cards I was holding, and the
   hand's own note admits it: "where two copies show none and differ only by one,
   this page cannot say which is which."
7. **`Hexerei` appeared as a keyword on Sucrose's card with an entirely empty
   definition** in the Words block.
8. **A performing member picks its own target at random.** This is printed
   clearly and I still misjudged it twice — in fight 1 the performance I was
   relying on to trigger a Frozen went to a 2-HP slime instead. It is legible and
   it is *still* the thing that most often made my plan not happen.

**(d) The card I never wanted to play, and the one I was happiest to draw.**

- **Never wanted:** `Regal Bearing` — 3 Block and 1 Weak for a full energy, in a
  deck whose defensive resource is Encore. It has no interaction with anything
  the kit is about. `Stage Presence` is a close second.
- **Happiest to draw:** `Aria of Recompense` (and after Smith, `Aria of
  Recompense+`). It is the card that switches the kit on: it turns a dead turn
  into 10–16 Encore, which is simultaneously the fight's armour and the fuel that
  makes every member perform at full price instead of three-quarters. Twice it
  turned a 16-damage enemy turn into zero HP lost. `Chevreuse` is the card I was
  happiest to *have*; Aria is the one I was happiest to see.

**(e) Did the first turn of the first fight already present a decision?**

**Yes — but the wrong one was the loud one.** The screen opened with a member
already on stage who had already dealt 6 damage, 2 Encore, and a 0-cost
`Ethereal Spotlight` shouting "Buy it before anything performs" in bold. The
actual decision was to *refuse* the card the screen was pushing, because
"Spotlight every Companion card" is worthless when the starting deck contains no
Companion cards — a fact the urgency text never mentions. Underneath that there
was a second, better decision: which of three Deploys to spend three energy on,
and in which order, given that a Deploy pays full price only while Encore lasts.
So: a real decision on turn one, arrived at by ignoring the screen's own advice.

**One structural note the five questions do not have a slot for.** Every fight
opened with a member already deployed and one performance already spent, before I
had any input. It makes the first screen feel like the second turn of a fight I
did not play, and it means the Salon is never a thing I *start* — only a thing I
top up. In fight 4 that opening performance was not even printed.
