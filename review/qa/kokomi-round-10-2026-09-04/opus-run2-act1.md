## Fight 1 — Corpse Slug (1) 26 HP, Corpse Slug (2) 27 HP

Both wore `Ravenous 4 — When an enemy dies, Corpse Slug immediately eats it, becoming Stunned
and gaining 4 Strength.`

**Turn 1.** Hand: Slack Water, Defend ×4. Played **Slack Water on Corpse Slug (2)** (the one
whose intent read `3x2`), then one **Defend**.
*Rejected:* writing Slack Water as a Plan on the Bake-Kurage. Its Plan line is `Apply 1 Weak to
ALL enemies`, which with the relic would have been 2 damage and a Weak on each, next turn — but
this turn I'd have eaten the full `3x2` and gained nothing. Immediate Weak on the actual attacker
knocked its intent from `3x2` to `2x2` on the screen, which is the sort of confirmation I wanted
early. *Also rejected:* a second Defend — 5 Block already covered a 4-damage intent.
Slack Water read `Deal 4 damage`; the slug went 27 → 21. Six, not four. Tamakushi Casket
(`whenever you apply a debuff to an enemy, it deals 2 Hydro damage`) is the missing 2, and it is
printed on the relic, so the screen and the outcome agreed once I put two printed things together.

**Turn 2.** Frail 2 landed; Defend now printed `Gain 3 Block`. Played **Kurage's Oath as a Plan
on the Bake-Kurage** (1 energy), then **Strike ×2** into Slug (2).
*Rejected:* playing Oath now for `Deal 3 damage to ALL` instead of planning it for `Deal 7 damage
to ALL`. This is the first genuinely interesting decision the kit offered: 6 immediate damage
across two bodies versus 14 next turn, for the same 1 energy, at the cost of a turn of tempo and
of committing before I see the next intent. *Also rejected:* Defend — under Frail it was 3 Block
against 14 incoming, which is not a defence, it is a rounding error.

**Turn 3.** The Bake-Kurage block printed exactly what it did:
`Bake-Kurage: Kurage's Oath, 7 / Corpse Slug (1) lost 7 HP / Corpse Slug (2) lost 7 HP`.
Played **Slack Water on Slug (1)**, **Strike on Slug (1)**, **Defend**.
*Rejected:* killing Slug (2) (at 2 HP) with a Strike to fish the Ravenous Stun. Stunning the
survivor would have bought me a free turn, but it would also have handed a 19-HP slug +4 Strength
permanently, turning its 8 into a 12. I chose to eat one weakened 8 instead. This was the second
real decision of the run and it came from the *enemy*, not the kit.

**Turn 4.** Played **Kurage's Oath** for its now-line (`Deal 3 damage to ALL`, killing Slug (2)
at 2 HP), then **Strike** to finish Slug (1).
*Rejected:* nothing meaningful — two lethal orderings existed and both won.

Ended 47/80. Card reward: Rally / Sea-Salt Prayer / The General's Banner / **Thoma — Crimson
Ooyoroi**. Took Thoma: it was the only off-element source on offer (Pyro), and the screen's own
keyword block advertises Vaporize at 1.5× on a Hydro aura. Kokomi's own cards are all Hydro, so
without an import the entire Elemental Reaction apparatus is inert.

## Fight 2 — Sludge Spinner 39 HP

**Turn 1.** Opening hand was **Defend, Defend, Strike, Strike, Strike** — not one card from the
kit. Played three Strikes, ended.
*Rejected alternative: none.* There was no Kokomi card in hand and one enemy, so "hit it" was the
only line. **This turn presented no decision at all** and I am recording that as the finding.

**Turn 2.** Weak 1 on me. Note the previews were honest: Oath printed `Deal 2 damage to ALL`
(down from 3) and Slack Water `Deal 3 damage` (down from 4), while the **Plan** line still read 7
— which is exactly what the Plan keyword promises (`your Weak and Strength do not [count]`).
That is the single best-written piece of text in the kit.
Played **Thoma**, then **Slack Water on Sludge Spinner**, then **Defend**.
*Rejected:* holding Thoma. It lasts 2 turns and I had only one Attack in hand, so half of it was
wasted — but the discard pile said my next hand would be Strike-heavy, and the alternative was
leaving 1 energy idle. *Also rejected:* planning Oath — with 11 incoming and 8 Block available I
wanted the block.
21 → 11, i.e. **10 damage**. I could not tell from the screen what happened. Slack Water 3
(weakened) + relic 2 + Thoma's 5 Pyro = 10 with **no** Vaporize; but Weak on me reducing the Pyro
to 3 and *then* Vaporize 1.5× also = 10. The Hydro aura was gone afterwards, which the keyword
says is what a reaction does — so a reaction *probably* fired and I still could not price it.
See (c).

**Turn 3.** Played **Slack Water** — the Thoma proc finished it.
*Rejected:* Strike. Slack Water is strictly better here because the Weak proc pays the relic 2.

Ended 39/80. Card reward: took **Kujou Sara — Tengu Stormcall** (Electro, `Deal 5 damage. Next
turn, your Attacks deal 5 additional damage.`) over Undertow's flat 10. Electro is the other half
of Electro-Charged, and the +5 is a bigger swing across a hand of three Strikes.

## Fight 3 — Seapunk 44 HP

**Turn 1.** Played **Kujou Sara on Seapunk** (put up `Electro Aura 2`), then **Slack Water on
Seapunk** — a Hydro hit into an Electro aura — then **Strike**.
*Rejected:* Defend as the third card, worth ~5 HP; I took the 6 damage instead because 44 HP at
39 of mine is a race I lose by playing for the long game.
This is the best moment the kit produced. 39 → 31 is **8**, and it decomposes as Slack Water 4 +
relic 2 (for the Weak) + relic 2 again (for the Electro-Charged debuff the reaction applied),
with the aura ending as `Hydro Aura 2` because the relic's own Hydro hit re-applied it. The
screen's Elemental Reaction paragraph warns about exactly this ("the aura is consumed and
RE-APPLIED inside the same beat, so no screen ever shows it gone"). It read as a warning written
by someone who had already been confused by it, and it was correct.
**But:** the reaction's status printed as `Poison 4 (debuff) — At the start of its turn, loses 4
HP, then reduce Poison by 1`, not as Electro-Charged. Nothing on the enemy panel names the
reaction that produced it. See (c).

**Turn 2.** Played **Thoma**, **Strike**, then **Kurage's Oath** for the kill.
*Rejected:* Defend (the intent was `2x4`, small). 21 → 3 is 18: Strike 6 + 5 (Sara's buff) = 11,
plus 5 Pyro × 1.5 Vaporize = 7. **This is the clean confirmation that Vaporize is 1.5× and does
consume the aura** — the aura line disappeared in the same beat.

Ended 31/80. Took **Shinobu — Grass Ring of Sanctification** (cost 0) over Salt Line and Chain of
Command; Chain of Command wants a Companion-dense deck I did not have.

Shop had 186 gold's worth of choices. Bought **Deep Current** (50) and **Kirara — Surprise

Spoils Map** into my deck — a permanent dead draw for the rest of the act. Rest site: rested

## Fight 4 — Sewer Clam 56 HP (+ 8 Block, `Plating 8`)

**Turn 1.** Played **Shinobu** (free 4 Block), **Kirara** (8 Block + 10 damage next turn),
**Slack Water**, **Strike**.
*Rejected:* Defend as the fifth card — no energy. *Rejected:* leading with Oath's now-line; 3 to
all against a single body with 8 standing Block is zero.

**Turn 2.** Kirara's promised 10 arrived at the start of my turn and **went almost entirely into
the Plating block** — the clam went 52 → 50. That is the lesson of this fight and it applies to
the Bake-Kurage too: *everything the kit defers resolves at the start of your turn, which is
precisely when a Plating/Block enemy is at full guard.* Against this archetype the Plan mechanic
is actively worse than playing the card.
Played **Kujou Sara** (Electro into the standing Hydro aura → Electro-Charged, relic proc),
**Deep Current**, **Strike**.
*Rejected:* planning Kurage's Oath for 7-to-all — for the reason just given, 7 arriving at the
start of my turn would have been eaten by 8 Plating Block, while 7 spent inside my turn lands
after the block is already stripped. **This was the sharpest real decision of the run**, and it
is a decision the kit's own text does not help you make: nothing on the Plan keyword tells you
the carry-out happens *before* you can strip block.

**Turn 3.** Played **Thoma**, **Strike** (printed `Deal 11 damage`), **Defend**. 27 → 13.
**Turn 4.** **Strike**, **Deep Current** — dead.
*Rejected:* the Duplicator potion, held for a boss I never reached.

Ended 49/80. Took **Gorou — Inuzaka All-Round Defense** over **The Moon Overlooks the Waters**
(cost 2 power, `Plans also happen now, as you write them`). I want to flag that pick: Moon is
obviously the kit's keystone card, and I passed on it because with exactly two Plan cards in
17 it would have been 2 energy for roughly one extra Kurage's Oath a turn. A keystone I can
recognise on sight and still correctly decline is a signal about the density of Plan cards on
offer, not about the card.

## Fight 5 (Elite) — Phantasmal Gardener ×4, 29 / 30 / 31 / 26 HP

All four wore `Skittish 6 — The first time Phantasmal Gardener is hit each turn, it gains 6 Block.`

**Turn 1.** Played **Slack Water as a Plan on the Bake-Kurage**, **Gorou** into the 7-damage
gardener, **Shinobu**, **Defend**.
*Rejected:* Slack Water's now-line. Against four bodies its Plan (`Apply 1 Weak to ALL`) is four
Weaks and, through Tamakushi Casket, four × 2 damage — the only turn in the run where the Plan
line was unambiguously the better half of the card. This is the kit working.

**Turn 2.** The readout printed the whole beat honestly:
`Bake-Kurage: Slack Water, 1 / Phantasmal Gardener (1) lost 2 HP` … ×4, and all four came back
Weak. Notably **none of the four gained Skittish Block from the relic's Hydro pings**, which I
could not explain from anything printed.
Played **Kujou Sara** into gardener (3), **Strike** into gardener (1), **Kirara**.
*Rejected:* an AoE. Skittish means the first hit each turn lands clean and everything after it
eats 6 Block per body, so one big AoE per turn is right and I did not have one in hand.

**Turn 3.** Sara's buff made **Deep Current** print `Deal 11 damage to ALL enemies`. Played
**Thoma**, **Deep Current** (killed two), **Shinobu**, **Strike**.
*Rejected:* Kurage's Oath's Plan for 7-to-all — it would have resolved at the start of the *next*
turn, one turn too late to stop the 19 incoming, and Deep Current's 11-to-all now was simply
larger. **Note: 44 damage of AoE from a shop card completely outclassed the kit's own AoE Plan**,
which is a thing worth saying out loud.
One arithmetic item I could not reconcile: the second surviving gardener went 24 → 12 (12 damage)
where Deep Current 11 + a Skittish 6 Block + a 5 Pyro proc should have left it at 13 with 1 Block.
It showed 12 and no Block. One HP off; I could not close it from the screen.

**Turn 4.** **Gorou**, **Strike** — dead. *Rejected:* nothing; lethal was lethal.

Ended 45/80, took **Cleansing Wave** over **Nereid's Ascension** (`Plan: for 2 turns, the
Bake-Kurage carries out every Plan twice. Exhaust.`) — same reasoning as Moon.

## Fight 6 — Living Fog 80 HP (unfinished; the action budget ended here)

**Turn 1.** Played **Strike ×2** (each printing `Deal 9 damage` — the new Strike Dummy relic),
**Gorou**, **Shinobu**. 80 → 54.
*Rejected:* holding a Defend; 8 Block from Gorou + Shinobu already covered the 8-damage intent.

**Turn 2.** `Smoggy 1 — You can only play 1 Skill per turn` landed, which quietly disables most
of the Kokomi kit at once (Kurage's Oath, Kirara, Cleansing Wave and Shinobu are all Skills, and
*writing a Plan is playing the card*, so Smoggy caps Plans at one per turn too — nothing on
screen says that, I inferred it).
Here I found the thing I am most confident about. **Slack Water printed `Deal 7 damage`.** Its
base is 4 and it had printed 4 all run. My only new relic reads
`Strike Dummy — Cards containing "Strike" deal 3 additional damage.`
"Slack Water" does not contain "Strike". **Deep Current**, also an Attack, also a Kokomi card,
still printed `Deal 6 damage to ALL enemies` — no bonus.
I spent two of my last actions measuring rather than guessing:
- **Slack Water on Living Fog:** 54 → 45 = **9** = 7 (card, boosted) + 2 (Casket for the Weak).
  The +3 is real, not just a preview.
- **Deep Current:** 45 → 39 = **6**. No +3.
So Strike Dummy is paying out on one Kokomi card whose printed title contains no "Strike" and not
on another. Whatever the relic is matching on, it is not the printed name.

**Turn 3 (last two actions).** Played **Kurage's Oath** (my one permitted Skill) and ended turn.
*Rejected:* planning it — I would never have seen the carry-out inside the budget, so playing it
for its now-line was the only honest use.
Stopped at 42/80, Living Fog at 36/80 with a freshly summoned Gas Bomb (7 HP, Death Blow 8).

## The kit, after 6 fights

**(a) Which decisions felt like real choices, and what they traded off.**
Three, and all three were the same axis: *now versus next turn, at the same energy price.*
- Fight 1 turn 2: Kurage's Oath as `Deal 3 damage to ALL` now, or `Deal 7 damage to ALL` at the
  start of my next turn. Double damage for a turn of tempo and for committing before I see the
  next intent. That is a good card.
- Elite turn 1: Slack Water's `Apply 1 Weak` on one body versus `Apply 1 Weak to ALL` next turn,
  which against four bodies also became four relic pings. The Plan half scales with the number of
  enemies, the now half doesn't, and that is a clean, legible reason to choose.
- Fight 4 turn 2: whether to plan *at all* into a `Plating 8` enemy. This one was real and also
  the least fair, because the reason the answer is no — the carry-out lands at the start of my
  turn, before I can strip block — is nowhere on the card.
Everything else that felt like a choice came from base-game enemies (Ravenous, Skittish), not
from Kokomi.

**(b) What felt automatic, and what never seemed worth playing.**
The Bake-Kurage itself was automatic in the bad direction: I planned a card on **4 of ~20 turns**.
With two Plan cards in a 17-card deck the jellyfish is empty most turns, and the screen even says
so in the same words every time — *"Nothing is planned. The morning is empty."* I read that line
about sixteen times. A mechanic that has a poetic idle message you see four times more often than
its active one is telling you something.
Never worth playing: **Defend**, once Frail or a real intent showed up (5 Block, 3 under Frail);
the **Spoils Map** (a genuinely dead card the deck is forced to carry); and **Kurage's Oath's
now-line**, which at 3-to-all was only ever a mop-up tool — I planned it or I used it as a
finisher, never as a turn's real play.

**(c) What I could not understand, or that contradicted its own printed text.**
1. **Strike Dummy's +3 applies to Slack Water but not Deep Current** (both Kokomi Attacks,
   neither titled "Strike"), measured in both directions. This is the one I would check first.
2. **The Elemental Reaction never names itself.** Electro-Charged printed on the enemy as
   `Poison 4`. Vaporize printed as nothing at all — the aura simply vanished and the damage was
   bigger. The keyword block explains reactions in loving, near-legalistic detail (it even
   pre-warns you about an aura being consumed and re-applied inside one beat) and then the enemy
   panel refuses to say which reaction just fired. In fight 2 that cost me the ability to price a
   hit at all: 10 damage was equally consistent with "Vaporize fired" and "Vaporize didn't", and I
   still don't know which happened.
3. **Card previews are inconsistent about buffs.** Weak on me *was* folded into printed numbers
   (Oath showed 2, Slack Water 3). Sara's `Fantastic Voyage 5` was **not** folded in the first
   time — fight 3 round 2 printed `Strike — Deal 6 damage` while the buff was up, and it hit for
   11 — but **was** folded in later (fight 4 round 3 printed `Deal 11 damage`, same buff, same
   card). Same card, same buff, two different printed numbers on two occasions.
4. **The Plan's timing is undocumented where it matters.** "Carries out at the start of your next
   turn" is stated; that this is *before* you can strip enemy Block, and that a `Plating` enemy
   therefore eats your whole Plan, is not. Same for Kirara.
5. **Sara's buff is named `Fantastic Voyage`** on the status line, a name that appears on no card
   I saw. I had to infer the link from the number.
6. Minor: the relic's Hydro pings did not trigger the gardeners' `Skittish` Block, and I could not
   tell from any printed text whether that was intended.

**(d) The card I never wanted to play, and the one I was happiest to draw.**
Never wanted: **Defend** — but the honest kit answer is **Kurage's Oath's now-line**. The card is
only exciting as a Plan, so drawing it on a turn I needed damage *this* turn felt like drawing
half a card.
Happiest to draw: **Slack Water**, and not for its own text. It is the only card that reliably
lights Tamakushi Casket, so it is really "4 damage, 2 more damage, a Weak, and a Hydro aura for
1 energy", and it is the one card whose Plan line I actually wanted twice. The relic is doing an
enormous amount of the work of making Kokomi feel like a character; the cards are doing less.

**(e) Did the first turn of the first fight already present a decision?**
Yes, barely, and by luck of the draw. Turn 1 was Slack Water + four Defends against a `3x2`
intent, and the choice was Slack Water now versus Slack Water as a Plan. It is a real choice, but
it is a *soft* one — the Plan line (Weak to ALL) is nearly worthless against two enemies when one
of them is already the only threat, so the correct answer is obvious once you read both halves.
And the contrast with **fight 2 turn 1 — Defend, Defend, Strike, Strike, Strike, no Kokomi card
at all, no decision whatsoever** — is the more important data point. With two kit cards in a
nine-card starting deck, roughly one opening hand in three has nothing of the character in it.
