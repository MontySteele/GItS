1. **Night Watch — FOLLOWS; C2, C6:** “1 Weak to ALL” at Dusk versus Slack Water’s “1 Weak to ALL” next morning exchanges timing and forfeits its face-up “4 damage and 1 Weak”; against enemies preparing this turn and attacking next, unupgraded Night Watch’s Weak expires before that attack, whereas Slack Water reduces an 8-damage attack to 6, and [Sucker Punch](https://slay-the-spire.fandom.com/wiki/Silent_Cards) (Common, 1 Energy, 7 damage/1 Weak; upgraded 9/2) also supplies immediate damage that Night Watch lacks.

2. **Breakwater — FOLLOWS; C2, C3, C6:** “5 Block, plus 3 for each Plan carried out this turn” gives 5 after an empty morning or 14 after three Plans, versus 1-Energy Defend’s 5, Shrug It Off’s 8 plus draw 1, and Blur’s 5 kept, so on the empty-morning turn it loses 3 Block and 1 card against Shrug, while the three-Plan turn gains 6 Block but still loses that draw; the player chose the preceding commitments and whether to spend on this payoff, and Dusk Block cannot support an earlier same-turn Block-dependent action as Defend can.

3. **Scout Ahead — FOLLOWS; C2, C3, C6:** “Draw 1 card for each LATER Plan” costs 1 Energy now for 0 immediate cards and, first of three, 2 next morning versus Skim’s 1 Energy for 3 immediately, leaving it 3 cards behind on the writing turn and 1 behind in total after resolution; additional committed Plans and surviving the intervening enemy turn supply prices beyond its own cost, while first-versus-last ordering changes its next-morning draw from 2 to 0 by player choice.

4. **Read the Field — FOLLOWS; C2, C6, C7:** “Look at the top 3…put one into your hand” costs 1 Energy for 1 selected card and 0 Block versus [Quick Slash](https://www.reddit.com/r/slaythespire/comments/178cd0v/daily_slay_the_spire_discussion_475696_quick_slash/) (Common, 1 Energy, 8 damage and draw 1) and Skim (Uncommon, 1 Energy, draw 3), so when the desired card is already on top it loses 8 immediate damage against Quick Slash or 2 immediate cards against Skim; “Plan: Gain 10 Block” instead supplies 0 Block this turn versus Defend’s 5, and neither half increases deck size.

Read the Field makes a safe turn’s Energy compete with writing when accessing a selected card now matters, and Scout Ahead offers the narrower competition of immediate draw 1 against delayed positional draw. Breakwater rewards previous writing by 3 Block per resolved Plan, and Scout Ahead rewards additional later Plans by 1 card each, so both also pay the writer more for writing more.
