# Kokomi — blind seat, lane 2, natural act 1

## Identity

- **Run seed:** none printed. No screen I saw carried a seed.
- **Character:** the Kokomi kit — a Hydro character whose opening relic is
  **Tamakushi Casket** ("Start each combat with the Bake-Kurage. Whenever you
  apply a debuff to an enemy, it deals 2 Hydro damage to that enemy") and whose
  signature is the **Bake-Kurage**, a jellyfish that holds **Plans**.
- **Ascension:** never printed as a number. The first battle screen opened at
  **HP 64/80**, i.e. 80% of max at the start of the run, which is the only
  evidence of an ascension setting I was shown.
- **Act / boss:** act 1. The map named the boss at the top of the act:
  **Vantom**.
- **Actions accepted:** 110 of 120.
- **Termination:** the **action budget**. At 110 accepted acts I was on the
  floor-10 map with only a Monster node ahead; a fight costs ~15–20 acts, so
  there was no room to open one and I stopped rather than play into the cap
  mid-combat. No refusal streak, no stall, wall clock well inside 5400 s.
- **HP trajectory:** 64/80 (fight 1 start) → 56/80 after fight 1 → max HP cut
  to 72 by an event (Kill the Trees, −8 max) → 45/72 after fight 2 → 42/72
  after fight 3 → 28/72 after fight 4 → rested to 49/72 → 40/72 after fight 5,
  where the round ended. Never below 28.
- **Gold at the end:** 192.
- **Potions held:** 1 of 3 — **Colorless Potion**. (Explosive Ampoule was spent
  for lethal in fight 5.)
- **Relics at the end:** Tamakushi Casket; Lava Rock (Act 1 boss drops 2
  relics); Shuriken (play 3 Attacks in a turn → 1 Strength); Tiny Mailbox
  (2 random potions whenever you Rest).
- **Deck at the end (15):** Strike ×4, Defend ×4, Kurage's Oath, Slack Water,
  Sayu — Muji-Muji Daruma, Charlotte — Framing: Freezing Point Composition,
  Vanguard, Amber — Fiery Rain *(Sharp 2)*, Ripple.
- **Neow pick:** **Lava Rock** (Act 1 boss drops 2 relics). The other two rows
  were New Leaf (transform 1 card) and Precarious Shears (remove 2, lose 16
  HP); I could not see my deck from that screen, so I would have been
  transforming or removing blind, and 16 HP is a real price on a run that
  opened at 64/80 — Lava Rock was the only row with no cost and no guesswork.

## Fight 1 — Shrinker Beetle [A], 39 HP

Opened at 64/80, 3 energy, hand Strike ×3 / Defend / Kurage's Oath. Its intent
was `Strategic (DebuffStrong)`.

**Turn 1 — Plan Kurage's Oath on the Bake-Kurage; Strike, Strike.**
The whole turn was this question: Oath's face prints two lines,
`Deal 3 damage to ALL enemies` and `Plan: Deal 7 damage to ALL enemies`, and
one energy buys either. **Rejected: Strike ×3 for 18 now.** Three Strikes is
more damage this turn (18 vs 12) but the Plan is 7 damage arriving on a turn
where I still have all 3 energy, so the two-turn total is 37 against 36 and the
Plan line keeps improving the longer the fight runs. That is a real choice on
turn 1 and it was legible from the card face alone. Also **rejected: play Oath
now for the Hydro aura** — the screen's own Words block told me flat out
`NO REACTION IS REACHABLE HERE: Hydro is the only element this screen can
supply`, so the aura was worth nothing, which is a straight and honest
disclosure I appreciated.

**Turn 2 — Slack Water on A; Strike; Defend.** The Plan resolved for exactly
the 7 it printed (39 → 20 across the turn boundary, 12 of it my Strikes). The
beetle had landed **Shrink −1** on me — "you deal 30% less damage with every
hit" — and the hand re-printed itself accordingly: Strike now read
`Deal 4 damage`, not 6. That is the single best legibility thing I saw all
round; the card faces do the arithmetic for you. **Rejected: Plan Slack Water**
(`Plan: Apply 1 Weak to ALL enemies`) — with one enemy the ALL clause is worth
nothing and the Weak would have arrived *after* the attack it was meant to
blunt. Playing it now moved the intent icon 7 → 5 in front of me and set off
Tamakushi Casket for 2 more.

**Turn 3 — Strike, Strike, Defend.** 12 HP left, 13 damage incoming.
**Rejected: Strike + Defend ×2** (take 3 instead of 8). I took the 8 because
Shrink −1 is worded "while Shrinker Beetle is alive" — every extra turn it
lives is a 30% tax on everything, so buying the kill a turn earlier was worth
5 HP. That is a decision the debuff's own text creates.

**Turn 4 — Strike for lethal.** No alternative; it was turn 3's line paying
off.

Verdict: three of four turns had a real rejected alternative, and the one that
did not was a lethal set up the turn before.

## Fight 2 — Leaf Slime (S) [A] 15 HP, Leaf Slime (M) [B] 32 HP, Twig Slime (S) [C] 8 HP

**Turn 1 — Plan Kurage's Oath; Slack Water on C; Strike on B.** Three bodies
means Oath's `Plan: Deal 7 damage to ALL` is 21 damage for one energy, so the
Plan was not close. The live decision was the other two energy, and I worked
through four lines:
- Kill C outright (Slack Water + Strike into 8 HP) and take zero — **rejected**,
  because it spends 12 damage on an 8 HP body.
- Slack Water on C + Strike on B, leaving C at 2 to swing for 3 — **taken**.
  Six more damage onto the 32 HP body for 3 HP of mine, and next turn's Plan
  was going to finish C anyway.
- Defend instead of the Strike — rejected; C was Weak'd to 3 and I would have
  been blocking 3 with a 5.
That is a genuine draft-of-targets decision, made on the turn.

**Turn 2 — Strike ×3, two into A and one into B.** The Plan landed 7 on every
body exactly as printed (A 15→8, B 26→19, C dead). **Rejected: all three
Strikes into B** (B to 1, but both bodies survive and I eat 11 instead of 8),
and **rejected: Strike + Strike + Defend** (take 3, but A lives). Killing A
with two 7s, one of them 4 points of overkill, was the choice — and the third
Attack in the turn is what fired **Shuriken** for a permanent +1 Strength, so
the relic itself was arguing for the aggressive line. The hand re-printed to
`Deal 7 damage` immediately, so I could see the Strength had landed.

**Turn 3 — Strike ×2 for lethal on B.** No alternative existed: B had 13 and
two Strikes are 14. I looked for a way to land three Attacks for another
Shuriken proc and there was none, so this was a dead turn — but a dead turn
created by the previous turn's plan, not by the kit.

## Fight 3 — Fuzzy Wurm Crawler [A], 55 HP

This is the fight where the kit's second half showed up, because I had drafted
**Charlotte — Framing: Freezing Point Composition** [Cryo] out of fight 2's
reward. Up to here every screen had said the same thing —
`NO REACTION IS REACHABLE HERE: Hydro is the only element this screen can
supply` — and one draft pick deleted that line and replaced it with a
**Frozen** definition. **The decision that shaped this whole fight was made at
the draft, not on a turn.**

**Turn 1 — Slack Water on A → Charlotte on A → Strike on A, in that order.**
The order *was* the turn. Slack Water is Hydro and left `Hydro Aura 2`;
Charlotte is Cryo and printed
`Frozen — Hydro on a Cryo aura, or Cryo on a Hydro aura. Its next action deals
half damage, and until it acts the first Attack to hit it Shatters for 6
damage`; the Strike then collected the Shatter for 6 + 6 = 12. 22 damage out of
three 1-cost cards, plus the Shuriken proc for three Attacks. **Rejected: any
Defend** — the incoming was 4 and the sequencing was worth ten times a 5-block.
The whole reason this worked is that the pieces are in a specific order, which
is exactly the kind of turn a seat wants to find.

**Where the screen and the outcome disagreed.** After Charlotte the intent icon
read `the number on its icon is 1`. I then Shattered it, and Shatter's own text
says it "removes Frozen". I took **3**, not 1. Nothing lied — the printed
number was true when it was printed and stopped being true the moment I did the
obvious thing — but I had no re-drawn number to read, and the page's own
disclaimer (`this page has no second source for it and does no arithmetic on
it`) is the reason. It is a small trap and it is aimed exactly at the play the
kit most wants you to make.

**Turn 2 — Plan Kurage's Oath; Strike; Sayu — Muji-Muji Daruma.** The enemy's
intent was `Empower (Buff)` — nothing incoming, and it came back with
**Strength 7**. **Rejected: Plan Oath + Strike ×2** (14 now instead of 7).
I took Sayu instead of the second Strike because Sayu reads
`deal 6 damage to a random enemy if you are above 70% HP, otherwise gain 6
Block` and I was at 42/72 — 58% — so it was a guaranteed 12 Block over two
turns for one energy, and I could see a Strength-7 body about to swing. Both
lines kill on the same turn; one of them takes 11 less. This is the best
decision I made all round and it came off reading the HP condition against my
own bar.

**Turn 3 — Strike ×2; Defend.** It hit for 11; Defend's 5 plus Sayu's 6 is 11.
Zero damage taken, to the point. **Rejected: three Defends** — 21 block against
an 11 hit while a Strength-7 enemy heals nothing is throwing the fight away.

**Turn 4 — Strike for lethal.** Turn 3 paying off.

## Fight 4 — Nibbit (1) [A] 42 HP, Nibbit (2) [B] 45 HP

87 enemy HP against my 42/72. The hardest fight and the one where the kit's
pieces all argued with each other.

**Turn 1 — Vanguard on A; Strike; Strike; Defend.** Vanguard is
`cost 0, Apply 1 Vulnerable` and it turned 12 damage into 21 in one go —
because Vulnerable's 50% applied to **Tamakushi Casket's answering 2 as well**
(the ping landed for 3). **Rejected: Vanguard as a Plan** (`Plan: Apply 1
Vulnerable and 1 Weak`) — the Bake-Kurage's own rules page told me why:
*"A Plan is carried out before you play anything, so it lands in whatever Block
the enemy is still standing in from its own turn — you cannot strip that Block
first"*, and A's intent already carried a Defend part. Planning into a body
that is about to block is the trap the kit prints on its own tin, and it was
worth reading.

**Turn 2 — Slack Water on B → Charlotte on B (Frozen) → Plan Kurage's Oath.**
B was showing **14 damage** with Strength 2. Slack Water's Weak plus
Charlotte's Frozen took that icon to **5**, and both debuffs each rang the
Casket for 2, so the "defensive" pair still did 12 damage. **Rejected: Strike
for the Shatter** — 12 damage (6 + 6 unblockable) was on the table, but Shatter
"ends the freeze", which would have handed B its Weak-only hit back for about
10 HP while I sat at 57%. **Rejected: Defend for the last energy** — the Plan
was worth 14 (7 into each body, and A's block would have expired by then)
against a 5-block, so I planned Oath and ate the 5. That is three distinct
non-obvious calls in one turn.

**Turn 3 — Slack Water on A; Strike on A; Kurage's Oath (played, not planned).**
A had 14 and was showing 14 damage. The line was arithmetic:
6 + 6 + 3 = 15 ≥ 14, so Oath's *weak* face — the 3-to-ALL one I had spent the
whole run planning instead of playing — was the card that killed the attacker
and deleted 14 incoming damage. **Rejected: Sayu + Defend for ~11 block** —
that leaves a 14 HP body alive and swinging. A card being "the bad half of the
card" right up until the turn it is exactly lethal is good design.

**Turn 4 — Strike; Strike; Charlotte, in that order, into B's 5 Block.** No
damage was coming (B was buffing), so every energy went to damage and I put
Charlotte **last** deliberately, to leave Frozen standing so the following
turn's first Strike would Shatter. **Rejected: Charlotte first** (its 4 would
have been eaten by the block) and **rejected: any Defend** (nothing to block).
Three Attacks, so Shuriken again.

**Where the screen and the outcome disagreed — the second time.** The Shatter
never happened. B's next *action* was its Buff, and Frozen — "its next action
deals half damage" — was spent harmlessly on an action that deals no damage at
all. By my turn the Frozen line was simply gone from B's block with nothing to
show for it. I do not think that contradicts the printed text, but the text
does not warn you, and the intent that eats your freeze is visible on the
screen when you apply it. **Freezing a buffing enemy is a wasted card and the
face gives you no help seeing that.**

**Turn 5 — Strike ×2 for lethal.**

**One refusal, and it was a good one.** I typed
`play "Kurage's Oath" on "A"` and got back:
`"Kurage's Oath" does its own aiming, so it takes no 'on "A"'. The form that
resolves: play "Kurage's Oath"`. It named the working form; I retyped it and it
went through. That is the only refusal of the round.

## Fight 5 — Cubex Construct [A], 65 HP, Artifact 1

**Turn 1 — Plan Kurage's Oath; Strike; Defend.** It was buffing, so nothing was
incoming and the Defend was a genuinely dead card I played only because the
energy had nowhere better to go. **Rejected: Oath played now for 3** — the
Plan's 7 is more than twice as much and there was no block to plan into.
Noted: `Artifact 1 — Negates 1 debuff` sat on it all fight and quietly turned
Slack Water and Vanguard into worse cards, which is a fine enemy design but it
means my only two debuff cards were the wrong picks here.

**Turn 2 — Amber — Fiery Rain → Charlotte → Strike, in that order.** This was
the best turn of the round and it was entirely legible in advance, because both
element cards had grown a preview line once an aura was on the board:
- Amber: *"Reaction preview: Vaporize — The triggering hit deals 1.5x damage
  and consumes the aura. Into that Hydro aura this card's 4 lands 6."*
- Charlotte: *"Reaction preview: Frozen — Hydro meets Cryo…"*

So: my turn-1 Plan had left a Hydro aura; Amber's first of three hits Vaporized
into it for 6 and its later hits re-applied **Pyro**; Charlotte's Cryo then hit
that Pyro aura for **Melt** at 1.75×, i.e. a card that prints `Deal 4 damage`
landing 7. 52 → 38 → 31 → 25 across the turn, every number exactly what the
preview said. **Rejected: Charlotte first** (no Pyro aura yet, so 4 instead of
7, and the Vaporize would be gone). **Rejected: Slack Water instead of Strike**
— its Weak would have been eaten by Artifact. Three separate cards, three
different elements, one correct order, and the screen told me the order was
correct before I committed. That is the kit at its best.

**Turn 3 — Strike ×3, then Explosive Ampoule for lethal.** 25 HP; three Strikes
are 21 and the potion is 10, so 31 killed it a turn early and skipped an 11
damage hit from a Strength-4 body. **Rejected: hold the potion** — I was
holding it for a boss I had 10 actions of budget left to reach, and 11 HP plus
a turn is a real price.

## The kit, after 5 fights

**(a) Which decisions felt like real choices, and what they traded off.**

1. **"Play the face or write the Plan", every turn, on the turn.** Every
   character card prints two halves for the same one energy — Kurage's Oath is
   `3 to ALL` or `Plan: 7 to ALL`, Slack Water is `4 + Weak` or
   `Plan: Weak to ALL`. The trade is tempo now against a bigger number arriving
   before you spend next turn's energy, and it flips on real information: how
   many bodies are up (an ALL Plan is worth 3× against three slimes), and
   whether the target is about to block, because the Bake-Kurage's own rules
   page says a Plan "lands in whatever Block the enemy is still standing in
   from its own turn". In fight 4 turn 1 that sentence alone made me play the
   card instead of planning it. This is the kit's core decision and it is a
   good one.
2. **Element ordering inside a single turn, on the turn.** Slack Water →
   Charlotte is Frozen; Amber → Charlotte is Melt at 1.75×; Charlotte first is
   neither. Same three cards, same three energy, damage between 15 and 27
   depending purely on sequence. This is the most satisfying thing in the kit.
3. **Whether to Shatter, on the turn** (fight 4, turn 2). Shatter is 6
   unblockable but "removes Frozen", so cashing it hands the enemy back its full
   hit. Damage against damage-prevention out of one keyword — a clean trade.
4. **Sayu against my own HP bar, on the turn.** `deal 6 … if you are above 70%
   HP, otherwise gain 6 Block` means the same card is offence or defence
   depending on a number I control. At 42/72 I knew exactly which half I was
   buying.
5. **Two draft picks decided whole fights.** Taking Charlotte after fight 2
   converted the standing message `NO REACTION IS REACHABLE HERE` into a live
   Frozen; taking Amber after fight 4 opened Vaporize and Melt. Fight 5 turn 2
   was won at those two card-reward screens.

**(b) What felt automatic, and what never seemed worth playing.**

- **Defend.** Four copies, `Gain 5 Block`, and in five fights I played it as a
  real decision maybe twice. Against an enemy that is buffing it is literally
  dead (fight 5 turn 1 I played one because the energy had nowhere to go), and
  against a Strength-4 body 5 is not enough to matter. Every interesting
  defensive turn I had came from Sayu, from Frozen, or from killing the
  attacker — never from Defend.
- **Strike.** It is the filler that makes Shuriken work and the thing that
  cashes Shatter, so it is not *bad*, but no Strike was ever a decision.
- **Lethal turns.** Four of my sixteen combat turns were "play the obvious
  lethal". All four were set up one or two turns earlier, so I count them as
  plans paying off rather than dead turns — but that is a third of the round.
- **The Hydro aura on its own.** For the first two fights every screen told me
  Hydro was the only element available and no reaction was reachable, so
  Kurage's Oath and Slack Water were leaving auras that did nothing at all.
  Honest, and slightly deflating: the kit's own element is inert until a
  *different* element is drafted into it.

**(c) What I could not understand, or that contradicted its own printed text.**

- **The Frozen intent number goes stale the instant you do the right thing.**
  Fight 3, the icon read 1, I Shattered, and it hit for 3 — because Shatter
  removes Frozen and Frozen was half the reason the number was 1. Nothing lied,
  but the screen shows you a number that your own best play invalidates.
- **Frozen is silently wasted on a non-attack action.** Fight 4: "its next
  action deals half damage", the Nibbit's next action was a Buff, and the freeze
  evaporated for nothing. The face does not warn you, and the intent that will
  eat it is on screen when you apply it.
- **The aura that gets consumed and re-applied inside one beat.** The Words
  block explains this at length —
  *"where a reaction's own debuff sets off a relic that hits with the aura's own
  element, the aura is consumed and RE-APPLIED inside the same beat, so no
  screen ever shows it gone and the reaction looks as though it did not
  happen"* — and I hit it twice with Tamakushi Casket. The prose is genuinely
  good, but the fact that a rule needs a paragraph of pre-emptive apology is
  itself the finding: the Casket's Hydro ping makes the aura state unreadable
  by inspection.
- **"Play 3 Attacks in a single turn" versus writing a Plan.** I never found
  out whether planning an attack card counts toward Shuriken, because I never
  had a turn where it was worth testing. Nothing on any screen says.
- **A rendering bug, not a design one.** On fight 4 round 2 the whole
  `## The other side` block printed **twice**, every line duplicated. It did not
  recur.

**(d) The card I never wanted to play, and the one I was happiest to draw.**

- **Never wanted: Defend.** See (b) — 5 Block is below the water line by
  fight 3, and it is the card I discarded unplayed most often.
- **Happiest to draw: Amber — Fiery Rain.** `Deal 4 damage to ALL enemies 3
  times` for one energy is already the best rate in the deck, but what made it
  the draw I wanted was that it *creates* the aura Charlotte then Melts, so
  drawing it turns two other cards into better cards. Honourable mention to
  **Vanguard**, a 0-cost Vulnerable that also amplifies the relic's ping.

**(e) Did the first turn of the first fight already present a decision?**

**Yes, and a real one.** Turn 1 of fight 1 was `Plan Kurage's Oath for 7 next
turn, or three Strikes for 18 now` — 12 damage this turn against 19 across two,
decided by how long I thought a 39 HP body would live. The card face carries
both halves and both numbers; nothing had to be learned first. That is a good
opening turn.
