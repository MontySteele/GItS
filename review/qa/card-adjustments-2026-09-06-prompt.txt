# Doctrine read: six adjusted or re-seamed prototype rows against the kit charters

Each numbered card below is ONE ARM. For each arm, in order, give FOLLOWS or
REQUIRES_MODIFICATION and name the clause (C1 to C8) you ruled against, and
quote the line of the card and the arithmetic (energy, Block, damage, cards,
turns) that breaches it, in one sentence. A verdict with no clause named is
not a verdict. Read adversarially: the author is a different model family and
the owner wants the row that should not reach a tester named. Do not soften.
Name clauses, not remedies: a number, a face or a rewritten row you volunteer
is DISCARDED.

Five of the six arms are ADJUSTMENTS to rows that passed this door on
2026-09-04 or 2026-09-05, and the sixth moves an existing row into the
natural offer. For an adjusted row, the earlier verdict and its reasoning are
stated; rule on the row AS IT NOW READS, and say in your one sentence whether
the earlier reasoning still holds.

## The charter

- **C1 (owner, 2026-09-04).** "I don't want us to end up gaming the metric
  of 'can an agent beat the game' by printing overly strong cards. Canonical
  characters still die in the hands of the same agents, so a successful
  design phase should not end with 'all agents always win.'" A row that
  removes a losing line the kit is meant to keep, instead of pricing it, is
  out of charter.
- **C2 (LAW D3, binding prices).** A benefit carries a binding price. The
  counterfactual test: there must be a real turn in which the card is worse
  than a plain card of its cost. Repeatable energy or draw at Uncommon or
  below is priced by something past the card's own cost. The owner's own
  reading of one row: "'once per turn, gain 1 Energy' with no drawback is
  one free energy, Echo Form territory for a 1-cost Uncommon Power."
- **C3 (LAW D2, player-controlled leverage).** The card's value is decided
  by a choice the player makes (bank or cash a Bomb; play a Plan now or write
  it; make the stage perform), not by a number that rises while you watch.
- **C4 (Klee brief §6).** "She cannot stall, and she cannot block on demand.
  Her only plain Block is Duck and Cover, 5 for 1. Every other defence she
  has is conditional on the decision she just made." The weakness is
  load-bearing because it is what makes cooking a bet.
- **C5 (Klee brief §3 rule 7; Kokomi brief §2 rule 4).** Nothing fires by
  itself: no automatic free attack, no passive feed; every effect is a card
  the player played and paid for.
- **C6 (not strictly better).** A row may not be a strictly better version
  of a shipped pool row or of a base-game card at the same or lower rarity
  and cost. Defend is a BASIC card and every class's Common block card
  exceeds it by design; compare against Commons and Uncommons (Iron Wave,
  Shrug It Off, Flame Barrier), not against Defend. Other reference points:
  Second Wind, Berserk (Uncommon Power, 0: 1 Energy a turn, 2 Vulnerable),
  Echo Form (Rare, 3), Offering, Battle Trance, Adrenaline (Rare, 0: 2
  Energy, draw 2, Exhaust), Skim (Uncommon, 1: draw 3), Barricade,
  Calculated Gamble.
- **C7 (Kokomi identity Law 4).** In her pool a Common never increases deck
  size.
- **C8 (Furina reframe §4.1, D2).** Under the arm Fanfare is minted by a
  Salon member performing and by nothing else, 2 per trigger and 5 per
  Evoke, decaying 20% a turn. An arm-only copy of a shipped rider changes
  the threshold only, and the copy's threshold has to sit in the observed
  range (0 to 15 across the rounds so far) the way the shipped threshold sat
  in the shipped economy's range (20 to 30 reached).

## The engines

**Klee.** Her Attacks apply Pyro. A **Bomb** is a charge on an enemy that
grows by 4 at the start of each of her turns and goes off only when a card
says **Set off** (every Bomb on that enemy, each as its own Pyro hit; not an
Attack; Block absorbs it). A **Mine** also goes off when its enemy attacks
her. **Spark**: 1 at combat start, +1 per Bomb going off, spent by some cards
instead of energy; Sparks are lost after combat. Starter: 4 Strike, 4 Defend,
Ka-pow! (0, Retain, Set off, deal 4), Jumpy Dumpty (1, Innate, Bomb 8; when
it goes off, Mine 3 on all enemies). Energy 3, HP 62. The pool's other
detonators, for calibration: Fwoosh! (Common Attack, 0 energy, 1 Spark: Set
off, deal 6); Pocket Match (Common Attack, 0 energy, 1 Spark, Retain: Set
off, deal 5; upgrade 7); Sizzle (Common Attack, 1: Set off, deal 6, 6 more
if a Bomb reacted this turn); Countdown (Common Skill, 1: Set off, draw 1);
Tinder Toss (Common Attack, 0 energy, 1 Spark: twice, Set off a random enemy
and deal 4). Seat evidence on the row below, three readings: "never a
decision ... the Retain is a lie told by the card frame"; passed "because
Retain plus an escalating cost is a card that punishes the exact
hand-holding the rest of the kit rewards"; an assembled deck of Spark-priced
cards deadlocked at the act-1 elite with its only detonator priced out.

**Furina.** Under the arm the shipped Burst meter is gone; Fanfare is minted
only by performance (C8) and DRAINED to 0 by two Rares (the floor and cap are
untouched by a drain). The Salon: up to three members on a stage; a Companion
card play makes the FRONT member perform and rotate; a Deploy puts a member
on and performs it; a Deploy onto a full stage EVOKES the front member (it
performs with its Focus term three times, mints 5 Fanfare, and leaves).
Spotlight, under the arm: the player pays 2 Encore to designate the
Spotlighted Companion; Spotlighted numbers carry a base multiplier and the
riders add to it for the turn. Encore: she opens each combat with 2, gains it
from her own cards, and it decays. Energy 3, HP 78. Starter: 3 Strike-grade,
3 Defend-grade, Regal Bearing, Aria of Recompense (1: gain 5 Encore; at 3
Fanfare gain 5 more), Salon Début (1: Deploy Crabaletta), An Invitation (0,
Exhaust: add a random Common Companion card). Shipped comparison rows in the
arm's offer: Limelight (Uncommon Skill, 1, Exhaust: spend 1 Encore,
Spotlighted +25% this turn, gain 1 Energy, draw 1; upgrade draw 2); Deep
Breath (Uncommon Skill, 1, Exhaust: choose gain 1 Energy and 2 Encore, or
another mode; upgrade cost 0); Compose Herself (Common, 1: draw 2, draw 1
more at 8 Encore); Universal Revelry (arm copy, Rare Attack, 2: 9 to ALL
plus 1 per 2 Fanfare, 6 more to ALL at 8 Fanfare).

## The arms

1. **Long Fuse (adjusted)** — Klee, Common Attack, 1 energy, Retain. Set
   off. Deal 6 damage. Upgrade: 9 damage. WAS: the same card with "Costs 1
   more each turn it stays in your hand"; that row was ruled FOLLOWS on C2
   on 2026-09-04 BECAUSE "retaining it once raises its cost from 1 to 2
   energy: keeping the detonator carries a binding price". The escalation is
   now gone. Precedent to weigh: Held Tide (Kokomi, Uncommon Attack, 1,
   Retain, deal 6 or 12 after a carry-out) was WITHDRAWN on C1 because
   "Retain waits out the dead turns". Nearest neighbours: Pocket Match
   (above), Sizzle, Ka-pow!.
2. **Florid Cadenza (arm copy, adjusted twice)** — Furina, Uncommon Skill,
   0 energy, Exhaust. Draw 1 card. If you have at least 6 Fanfare, draw 2
   more. Upgrade: the bar moves to 3 Fanfare. WAS: the upgrade dropped the
   gate (draw 3 for 0 with no condition), and the copy did not Exhaust; the
   base card passed on C8 on 2026-09-04 and the upgrade was not read. The
   second review found that a bar which does not deplete makes three copies a
   loop by HOLDING the rest of the deck in hand (hand cap 10, overflow to
   discard), at bar 6 as much as at 3, so the copy is now one shot per copy.
   Read C2 against that technique and C6 against Skim and Adrenaline.
3. **Shared Billing (arm copy, upgrade adjusted twice)** — Furina, Common
   Skill, 1 energy. Apply Hydro to a random enemy. Spotlighted Companion
   cards gain 25% this turn. Gain 1 Energy. Upgrade: also gain 3 Block. WAS:
   the shipped upgrade takes the cost to 0 (a 0-cost card that gains 1
   Energy); the first adjustment made the upgrade draw 1, which the second
   review found is the same loop piece (refunds its own Energy and replaces
   itself; two copies cycle at no cost, stacking the Spotlight rider). Read
   the base card and the upgrade both, C2 against the holding technique.
4. **Rapturous Applause (arm copy)** — Furina, Rare Power, 1 energy. Your
   Attacks deal 1 additional damage per 5 Fanfare. Upgrade: 2. WAS: the
   shipped row is 1 per 10 and also grants "Fanfare +8, and cannot fall
   below 8" (a permanent floor, which under the arm satisfies every rider bar
   without a performance); the floor is removed, and the first read of this
   copy paid for it by doubling the amount to 2 per 10, which moves the
   PAYOUT. This is the C8 threshold mapping instead — the shipped meter's
   20-to-30 range maps to the arm's 0-to-15, so the bar halves and the slope
   stands, exactly as the four rider copies' 12/12/15/20 became 6/6/8/10.
5. **Unheard Confession (arm copy)** — Furina, Rare Power, 2 energy.
   Whenever your Fanfare changes amount, gain 2 Block. Upgrade: costs 1. WAS:
   the shipped row also grants the same "Fanfare +8" floor at 1 Block per
   change; the floor is removed and the payout is 2. It pays per change
   EVENT, not per point: a trigger, an Evoke and a drain are one event each.
   The turn-start decay is also an event, but the engine decays Fanfare
   BEFORE the turn-start Block clear, so decay's 2 Block is wiped before the
   player acts and buys nothing. A turn with two performances therefore
   yields 4 usable Block, and a drain the same turn 2 more.
6. **Let the People Rejoice (enters the offer, re-priced)** — Furina, Rare
   Attack, 2 energy. Drain your Fanfare. Deal 5 damage to ALL enemies, plus 2
   per Fanfare drained. Upgrade: the Prototype-stage default, costs 1. It
   passed this door as a grant-only row on 2026-09-04 at 1 per drained; it
   now takes the Rare offer slot of The Sea Is My Stage (Rare Power, 1:
   Fanfare +15 and cannot fall below 15), which leaves the arm's offer. The
   arithmetic against Universal Revelry's arm copy (2 energy, 9 to ALL plus 1
   per 2 Fanfare, 6 more at 8, meter KEPT): at 1 per drained the drain dealt
   15 to Revelry's 20 at 10 Fanfare and 20 to 22 at 15, never ahead in the
   range while also emptying the meter; at 2 per it deals 25 to 20 at 10 and
   35 to 22 at 15, and the meter it empties is what the next turn's readers
   were going to read. Rule on the base and the 1-energy upgrade against C6
   and C1.

Six verdicts, each with its clause and its breaching line. Under 700 words.
