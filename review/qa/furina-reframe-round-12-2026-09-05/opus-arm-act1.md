## Fight 1 — Nibbit [A], 42 HP

## Fight 2 — Shrinker Beetle [A], 40 HP

Opened HP 56/78, Encore 2, empty stage — **and no Deploy in hand**. Second Course
was in hand and printed `CANNOT BE PLAYED: you have 2 Encore, and this costs 3`.

**Turn 1.** Ethereal Spotlight → An Invitation → Bennett — Passion Overload →
Chevreuse → Charlotte → Stage Presence.

with no member on stage and Second Course locked out, **Encore had nothing else to
do**, so lighting the Spotlight was nearly free. It worked exactly as printed —
Chevreuse went 7 → 10, Charlotte 4/4 → 6/6, and the buff line answered the
ambiguity I had been carrying: "*Companion cards are Spotlighted … **No member is
one***." The Spotlight buffs the cards, never the salon.
An Invitation produced **Bennett — Passion Overload** (0 cost): "Your next Attack
deals 4 additional damage. **Gain 5 Burst Energy**." My Energy stayed 3/3, no buff
line for Burst Energy appeared, and no tooltip on any screen defines it. See (c).
Chevreuse dealt exactly 10 + 4 = 14 (40 → 26). Screen and outcome agreed.
*Rejected:* holding the Spotlight for a fight where I would have members. The
counter-argument I acted on: a new Ethereal Spotlight is added every turn until
lit, so deferring costs nothing, but Encore *is* the scarce thing and it was idle
right now.

**Turn 2.** Guest List → Soloist's Solicitation+ → Regal Bearing+.
The beetle's debuff landed: `Shrink -1 — While Shrinker Beetle is alive, your
Attacks deal 30% less damage`, and every card in hand **re-printed its shrunk

That is very good legibility.
**Guest List, play 1 of 1 — PLAYED.** 3 Block plus **Barbara — Let the Show
Begin♪**. Worth it only because I had a spare energy; the added Companion card is
dead the turn you get it if you have no energy left and no member to perform.
**Rolling Tide, hand 2 of 6 — HELD.** 2 energy for 2 total damage (Shrink halved

Took 0 damage (8 Block + Weak against a 7).
*Rejected:* Noelle — Breastplate. 9 Block against a 7-damage attack was overkill,

**Turn 3.** Aria of Recompense → Salon Début → Stage Presence.
Sequencing decision, and the important one of the fight: Aria **first** (Encore

Took 3 (13 attack − 6 Block, then **Encore 4 absorbed 4** before HP). This is the

**Turn 4.** Aria (Encore 5) → Barbara (9 Block + a 6 performance) → Salon Début
(a second Crabaletta, 6). Beetle 14 → 2, and 9 Block ate the 7. Took 0.
*Rejected:* Regal Bearing+ / Stage Presence — the block was already sufficient and
Barbara bought block *and* damage in one card.

**Turn 5.** Bennett — Passion Overload (0 energy) performed the front Crabaletta
for the last 2. Lethal, and free.
**Rolling Tide, hand 3 of 6 — HELD** (2 energy to do what a 0-cost Companion did).

## The kit, after 5 fights

**(a) Which decisions felt like real choices, and what they traded off.**

1. ***On the turn:* spend the opening 2 Encore on Ethereal Spotlight, or on member
   performances?** Encore is one pool doing three jobs — it pays card costs, it pays
   for performances (a member with none performs at three-quarters), and it absorbs
   damage after Block. There is no regeneration until you draw Aria of Recompense.
   So turn one of every fight asks the same sharp question, and I answered it both
   ways: in fight 1 I spent it on deploys and the Spotlight printed
   `CANNOT BE PLAYED` for the rest of the combat; in fights 2, 4 and 5 I had no
   Deploy in hand, Encore was idle, and lighting was nearly free. The heuristic
   "light it on the turns your stage is empty anyway" is discoverable, satisfying,
   and entirely readable off the screen.
2. ***On the turn:* where in the turn Aria of Recompense goes.** It gives 5, or 10
   at Fanfare ≥3, and only performances make Fanfare — so it wants to go *late*.
   But performances want Encore, so the deploys want to go *before* it, spending the
   opening pool at full strength. Fight 3 turn 1 (Salon Début → Freminet → Aria →
   Spotlight) is the whole kit working: 18 damage, a Frozen, Encore 8 and a lit
   stage from three energy, and every step forced by a printed rule.
3. ***On the turn:* Encore as fuel now, or as armour at end of turn.** Elite turn 3
   I deliberately played Aria last so the 10 Encore would eat 10 of a 23-damage hit,
   accepting two dry three-quarter performances (−4 damage) to save 7 HP. Two
   tooltips, one number, a genuinely close call.
4. ***Earlier in the fight:* whether to put the Usher on the stage at all.** The
   front member is the one every Companion card performs, so a Block member taxes
   the next Companion play from 6 damage into 3 Block. Against the 65-HP Cubex I
   took him anyway, because an empty stage makes "*performs nobody*" of half a hand,
   and the resulting Usher/Crabaletta alternation turned out to be a good rhythm.
   The best decision in the run and the one the kit is actually about.
5. ***At the draft:* Duet over a second Freminet in fight 3.** It decided both big
   elite turns two floors later. The elite's turns 2 and 4 played themselves — but
   they played themselves *because of that pick*, which is a plan paying off, not a
   dead turn.
6. ***On the turn:* ordering around the reaction previews.** "Reaction preview:
   Vaporize — this hit deals 1.5x damage" printed on the card in hand means putting
   Crabaletta's Hydro performance before Chevreuse's Pyro is a decision with a
   visible price tag. Fight 3 turn 2 was 31 damage on 3 energy for exactly this.

**(b) What felt automatic, and what never seemed worth playing.**

- **Stage Presence** (6 Block, two copies) and **Soloist's Solicitation** (6 damage)
  are the cards you play when the turn is already decided. Neither ever created a
  question; against a Buff or Sleep intent Stage Presence is pure filler.
- **Rolling Tide at 2 energy** against a single body. Four of six hands it was an
  instant no.
- **Regal Bearing+** is a good card that never posed a question.
- The kill turns of fights 1 and 4 were single obvious plays, but both were set up
  a turn or two earlier (Aria → Second Course; Crabaletta's Hydro → Chevreuse's
  Pyro). I count those as the plan landing.
- The genuinely dead turns were the **empty-stage** ones — fight 2 turns 1–2 and
  elite turns 1–2 — where half the hand printed "*No member on stage: performs
  nobody*" and the turn collapsed into "play your biggest attack". Four of nineteen
  turns. That is the kit's failure mode, and it is a draw problem: with only two
  reliable Deploys (Salon Début, Curtain Rises) plus one that is locked out at the
  start of every combat, whether the fight is interesting is decided by the opening
  hand.

**(c) What I could not understand, or that contradicted its own printed text.**

- **Burst Energy is invisible.** Bennett — Passion Overload prints "Gain 5 Burst
  Energy" and Barbara — Let the Show Begin♪ prints "Gain 4 Burst Energy". No screen
  in the run defines the term, no buff line for it ever appeared, and my Energy read
  3/3 immediately before and immediately after playing Bennett. I finished the act
  not knowing whether that clause does anything.
- **The Final Verdict** (shop, 157g): "Deal 0 damage, already including Fanfare.
  Your Fanfare falls to its baseline, and that baseline falls by 30." I could not
  work out what "baseline" means, whether the −30 is permanent, or why the damage
  reads 0. I did not buy it because I could not price it.
- **Take Your Bow** (shop, 74g): "The leftmost member of your Salon takes their
  bow." "Takes their bow" appears nowhere else in the game's vocabulary. I assume it
  means Evoke, but the screen never says so and the Evoke tooltip is not attached.
- **Reactions are previewed but never confirmed.** The *preview* on the card in hand
  ("Reaction preview: Vaporize … 1.5x") is excellent. But when the reaction resolves,
  nothing prints. In fight 1 turn 1 Chevreuse dealt 10 instead of 7 while the salon
  log showed only Crabaletta's two lines and the enemy still read `Hydro Aura 2`,
  because Crabaletta re-applied it in the same beat. The tooltip warns this exact
  case can happen, which is honest — but it means the first Vaporize of the run was
