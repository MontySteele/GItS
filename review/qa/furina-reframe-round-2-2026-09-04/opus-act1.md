## Fight 1 — Seapunk 44/44

**Turn 1** (3 energy, HP 62). Played `Overflowing Hospitality` → `Ethereal Spotlight`
→ `Freminet — Pers, Deploy!` on Seapunk.
*Rejected:* the straight beatdown `Usher the Waves` + `Regal Bearing` + `Freminet`
(11 damage, 1 Weak, 3 Block). I rejected it because `Overflowing Hospitality`'s
Hydro left an aura for Freminet's Cryo to react off — the card itself printed
*"Reaction preview: Frozen — Hydro meets Cryo: its next action deals half damage"* —
and the enemy's intent was a flat 11. Freezing it was worth more than three points
of extra face damage. Playing `Ethereal Spotlight` in between (0 energy, 2 of my 3
Encore) turned the printed "Deal 6 damage" into a printed "Deal 9 damage" before I
committed the energy, which is the clearest piece of feedback the kit gave me all run.
**Outcome matched the screen exactly:** 43 → 32 = 11, i.e. Freminet's 9 plus a
Salon member's 2, and the intent fell from 11 to 5.
One thing the screen and the outcome disagreed on: `Overflowing Hospitality` prints
no damage, and Seapunk went 44 → 43 when I played it. One point, unexplained.

**Turn 2** (3 energy, HP 57). Played `An Invitation` → `Aria of Recompense` →
`Kaeya — Frostgnaw` → `Soloist's Solicitation`.
*Rejected:* holding `Soloist's Solicitation` and playing `Stage Presence` for 6 Block
instead. The Frozen debuff printed *"the first Attack to hit it Shatters for 6
unblockable damage and removes Frozen"*, so the choice was legible and real:
**12 damage now (6 + 6 Shatter) at the price of un-freezing an 8-damage intent**,
versus 0 damage and a fully blocked turn. I took the damage. The screen was honest
about the cost — the intent snapped straight back from `1x4` to `2x4` the instant
the Shatter landed.
I also rejected re-playing `Ethereal Spotlight` (it was in hand and Ethereal, so it
was exhausting either way) because `Guest Cast 1` was still up from turn 1 and I
wanted the 2 Encore for a member act.

**Turn 3** (3 energy, HP 53). Played `Salon Début` → `Kaeya — Frostgnaw`, which killed it.
*Rejected:* nothing meaningful on the kill — Kaeya's printed 9 exactly covered the
printed 9 HP. `Salon Début` first was a free rider on spare energy, chosen partly to
watch whether `Burst +5` moved the Burst meter. It did not (see (c)).

**The turn that was not a turn.** Between turn 2 and turn 3, **4 banked Encore
vanished.** I ended turn 2 holding `Encore: 4` and one Salon member on stage. Turn 3
opened with no Encore line at all and Seapunk still on 9 HP — so the member neither
acted nor left, and four Encore went nowhere I could see.

**Reward:** took `Suffering for Art` (0 cost, lose 1 HP, gain 3 Encore) over
`Supporting Cast`, `Ebb and Flow` and `Noelle — Breastplate`. My relic hands me an
`Ethereal Spotlight` every turn that I can almost never afford; a 0-cost card that
prints 3 Encore is the thing that makes the relic live.

## The kit, after 5 fights

### (a) Which decisions felt like real choices, and what they traded off

Three, and only three, but the first is very good.

1. **Shatter now, or keep the Frozen.** Freezing an enemy halves its printed intent;
   the *first Attack* to hit it then Shatters for 6 and removes the Frozen. So every
   Frozen turn asks: cash 6 unblockable damage, or keep a halved intent. Both sides
   are printed, the numbers are on the screen before you commit, and the answer
   genuinely changes with your HP. Fight 1 turn 2 I cashed it; fight 3 turn 3 and
   fight 4 turn 1 I did not. That is the best thing in this kit.
2. **Ordering Hydro and Cryo inside one turn.** Neuvillette before Freminet is a
   Frozen; Freminet before Neuvillette is a wasted reaction and a Shatter I did not
   want. The cards tell you enough to work this out, and getting it right felt like
   playing well rather than guessing.
3. **`Ethereal Spotlight`'s two-Encore price.** 2 Encore for +50% on every Companion,
   against 2 Encore being two Salon member acts. Real, and it makes `Suffering for
   Art`'s 1 HP a live cost. But this only exists when I hold an Encore source, which
   was maybe half the turns.

The `Ravenous`/`Heist`/`Escape` decisions in fight 2 and 4 were also real, but those
are base-game enemy design, not this kit.

### (b) What felt automatic, and what never seemed worth playing

Most Block turns are automatic: I had `Stage Presence` (6), `The House Holds Its
Breath` (4), `Regal Bearing` (3+Weak), `Dinner Service`-shaped cards, and against a
single-digit intent it does not matter which two you play. Fight 3 turn 2 was a hand
of four of them and one unplayable card, and it presented literally nothing.

Never worth playing:

- **`Lynette — Enigmatic Feint` against one enemy.** "Swirl an enemy's aura onto ALL
  enemies" is a blank line when ALL is one, so she is a 5-Block card wearing a
  mechanic. She was excellent twice (fight 2, fight 5) and inert three times.
- **`Aria of Recompense`** — 1 energy for 5 Encore, when Encore appears to evaporate
  between turns (see (c)). I played it twice and both times the leftovers were gone
  the next turn.
- **`Salon Début`.** One energy for a *random* member with no readout of which one you
  got. In fight 3 it gave me a member that dealt 2 damage per Companion play. I never
  once chose to build a Salon on purpose; I played the card when I had spare energy.
- **`Dramatic Entrance`'s rider** and **`Florid Cadenza`'s** — both gated on "at least
  12 Fanfare". My Fanfare peaked at 6 across five fights and fades 20% per turn. I
  never came close, so the gate is decoration on the card.
- **`Ethereal Spotlight` itself, most turns.** The relic puts one in my hand every
  single turn, and roughly two turns in three it printed
  `CANNOT BE PLAYED: you have no Encore, and this costs 2`. A relic whose gift is
  usually a dead card in a five-card hand is a tax, not a gift.

### (c) What I could not understand, or that contradicted its own printed text

This is the long section, and it is the finding of the round.

1. **The Salon has two contradictory rulebooks on the same screen.** The card tooltip
   says: *"Members do NOT act on their own. A Companion card you play performs the
   front member."* The buff of the very same system, three lines above it, says:
   *"At the start of your turn, each Salon Member spends 1 Encore for its act."*
   These cannot both be true. Behaviour matched the card, never the buff — no member
   ever acted at the start of a turn in five fights.
2. **Banked Encore disappears, silently, and only when a member is on stage.**
   - Fight 1: ended turn 2 with `Encore: 4` and one member; turn 3 opened with no
     Encore and the enemy still on 9 HP. Four Encore, no effect.
   - Fight 2: ended turn 2 with `Encore: 3` and (supposedly) a member; turn 3 opened
     with none.
   - Fight 2, the control case: ended turn 1 with `Encore: 1` and **no** member;
     turn 2 opened with `Encore: 1` intact.
   - Fight 3: ended turn 3 with 1 Encore and a member; turn 4 opened with none, and
     the enemy's HP was unchanged, so no member act was paid for.
   So a member on stage appears to eat *all* remaining Encore at turn start and
   produce nothing. The printed rule is "spends 1 Encore for its act". Neither the
   quantity nor the act matches. And the Encore meter's own readout says
   *"the game's data feed carries this meter's amount only: no maximum, and **no rule
   for how it is spent**"* — the screen is openly telling me it cannot explain the
   resource I am being asked to budget.
3. **Burst Energy never moves.** `Overflowing Hospitality`, `Salon Début`,
   `Matinée Performance` and `Gentilhomme Usher` all print `Burst +5` **and** carry
   `Elemental Skill — Playing this card grants 5 Burst Energy`. `Bennett — Passion
   Overload` prints `Gain 5 Burst Energy` as its entire second line. I played these
   nine times across five fights. Every single tooltip afterwards read
   **"You hold 0 of 70 Burst Energy."** Not 5, not 10 — 0. There is no Burst meter in
   the status list either. As far as this seat can tell the character's Burst is
   unreachable, and a printed 70-point meter that never leaves 0 makes every
   `Burst +5` line on every card a lie the player is asked to pay for.
4. **`Overflowing Hospitality` sometimes does not add its member.** It prints
   *"Add 1 Surintendante Chevalmarin to your Salon."* Fight 1: `Salon Member 1`
   appeared. Fight 2 turn 2: no buff appeared and every Salon tooltip on the screen
   read "You have 0 on stage". The only difference I could see is that in fight 2 its
   Hydro triggered a Frozen reaction instead of landing bare. It also did 1 damage in
   fight 1 and 2 damage in fight 2, from a card that prints no damage at all.
5. **You cannot see which Salon member you have.** The buff says `Salon Member 1` and
   then recites all three members' abilities. There is no readout of *which*. I
   worked out mine was Chevalmarin by subtracting Neuvillette's 7 from a 9-point HP
   drop. A player is asked to plan around a unit whose identity is not printed.
6. **`Fanfare` says what generates it and then does not.** *"Generated by losing HP,
   and by spending, absorbing or paying out Encore."* In fight 2 turn 1 I lost 1 HP to
   `Suffering for Art` and spent 2 Encore on `Ethereal Spotlight` in the same turn, and
   Fanfare stayed at 0. Meanwhile `Salon Début` — which does none of those three
   things — took Fanfare from 4 to 6.
7. **`Guest Cast` has no printed duration.** It persisted across turns and across a
   whole combat, then vanished from the buff list in fight 4 round 4 while
   `Spotlight Mode: 2` stayed and Companion cards *kept* showing boosted numbers.
   The three meters `Spotlight Mode`, `Spotlight Moved` and `Spotlight Plays` appeared
   and disappeared in the status list all run and I never worked out what any of them
   meant.

To be fair about what *was* legible: the elemental reaction layer is genuinely well
explained. The reaction preview on the card ("Hydro meets Cryo"), the aura duration
on the enemy, the intent number changing the instant a Frozen lands, the Weak-adjusted
damage numbers printed on my own cards — all of that told me the truth every time and
I never once had to guess. The kit's problem is not its reactions. It is its own three
bespoke meters.

### (d) The card I never wanted to play, and the one I was happiest to draw

- **Never wanted:** `Aria of Recompense`. One full energy for 5 Encore, in a kit where
  Encore evaporates at the start of my next turn and where the only reliable sink
  costs 2. It is a card that asks me to invest in a currency the game will confiscate.
  (`Ethereal Spotlight`, arriving unplayable every turn from the relic, is worse — but
  I never *chose* to have it.)
- **Happiest to draw:** `Freminet — Pers, Deploy!`. One energy, printed 6, printed 9
  under Spotlight, applies Cryo, performs my front member, and — the reason it is the
  best card in the deck — it is the piece that turns any Hydro on the board into a
  halved enemy intent. Every good turn I had in five fights had Freminet in it.
  `Freminet — Pressurized Floe: Backstroke` (10/15 damage and 6 Block for 2) was
  better on rate but I only ever got it from `An Invitation`, never from my deck.

### (e) Did the first turn of the first fight already present a decision?

**Yes, and a good one.** Six cards, three energy, one 44 HP enemy telegraphing 11.
The choice was between an obvious 11-damage beatdown and a three-card line
(`Overflowing Hospitality` → `Ethereal Spotlight` → `Freminet`) that dealt the same
11 while halving the enemy's swing, banking a Salon member and turning Freminet's
printed 6 into a printed 9 in front of me before I spent the energy. I could read the
whole trade off the cards, including the reaction preview, and I had to sequence three
plays correctly to get it. That is a real opening turn.

The honest counterweight is that turn one was the *only* turn in fight 1 with that
shape, and by fight 3 I was drawing hands of four Block cards with nothing to decide.
