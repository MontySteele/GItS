prints "When it goes off, place a Mine 3 on ALL enemies" — setting it off early
bought a Mine on *both* bodies and a Spark off Pounding Surprise, which waiting
did not. That is a real trade and it was decided on the turn.

Turn 2. B at 9, A at 21 with Mine 7 and fresh Thorns 2. Played Strike ×2 into B
(kill), then Jumpy Dumpty on A (Bomb 8).
**Rejected:** Defend instead of the third card. I took the 3×3 on the chin (−9)
to put the second Bomb down a turn earlier. Also rejected: Striking A — its
printed **Thorns 2** made attacking it worse than bombing it, and the Bomb is
not an attack, so Thorns never fired. That is the clearest "the printed text
changed my play" moment of the run.

Turn 3. A at 14 with Bomb 12. Ka-pow! on A: 12 + 4 = lethal.
**Rejected:** nothing — and that is fine. This was turn 2's plan paying off, not
a dead turn.

## The kit, after 5 fights

**(a) Real choices, and what they traded off.**
The kit's core decision is genuinely good and it recurs: *set off now, or let it
grow*. Bombs grow 4 a turn for free, so holding is never idle — but setting off
early buys Sparks (Pounding Surprise), buys Jumpy Dumpty's Mine-on-ALL, and
banks damage before the enemy's HP is out of reach. I made that call on the turn
in fight 1 t1, fight 2 t2, fight 5 t2, and it traded differently each time.
Second: **Sparks are a second currency with its own clock**, and Tinder Toss /
Dig In cost Spark and not Energy, so a Spark-rich turn is a turn where the Energy
bar lies to you about what you can do. Fight 3 turn 3 — Tinder Toss + Ka-pow!
killing two bodies with all 3 Energy unspent — was the most satisfying turn of
the run *because* the resource I spent was one I had been earning in the
background for three fights.
Third, at the draft: buying Pop!, Fish-Flavored Bait and Chain Fuse in one shop
is the decision that decided fight 5 two floors before fight 5 existed. Chain
Fuse ("each Bomb on the enemy grows by 6") is only good if you drafted placers;
that read-ahead is a real draft decision.
Fourth, read off enemy text: Thorns 2 (attack it or bomb it), Ravenous 4 (kill
together or not at all), Hardened Shell 20 (do *not* grow the Bomb), Shriek 70
(aim one detonation at a threshold). Four elites/enemies, four different correct
shapes for the same kit. That is the strongest thing I can say about this kit.

**(b) Automatic, and never worth playing.**
Defend is the automatic card and there are four of them: with no Bomb interaction
on the face, "Gain 5 Block" is a turn filler, and fight 4 turn 3 was five cards
of pure filler. Strike is the never-worth-it card by fight 3 — 6 damage for 1
Energy next to Ka-pow!'s free detonation and Amber's 12 AoE. The starter pair is
also the part of the deck that most dilutes the thing the kit is *about*: the
turns that felt like nothing were always the turns I drew no detonator.

**(c) What I could not understand, or contradicted itself.**
1. **Fight 2 turn 3: I computed 26 damage and the enemy ended at 1 HP, not 2.**
   Nothing printed accounted for the extra point. My best guess is The Boot
   lifting Ka-pow!'s 4 to 5 — but Ka-pow!'s face in my hand still printed "Deal 4
   damage" while the Weak-adjusted faces elsewhere *did* re-print. So the kit
   re-prints one modifier onto card faces and not another, and I could not tell
   which numbers on a card face I was allowed to trust.
2. **Set off ordering vs. the "oldest first" rule.** The Bomb line says "oldest
   first" and then lists sizes "16 / 6". When I stacked Jumpy Dumpty's 8 and
   Bombs Away!'s 2 in the same turn I could not tell from any screen which one
   counted as older, and since only the *first* hit "takes the aura", that is
   load-bearing for reactions. I never learned the answer.
3. **A display defect, repeatedly.** On several screens — fight 4 turn 1 and
   turn 2, fight 5 turn 2 and turn 3 — the whole enemy block and the trailing
   glossary printed **twice, verbatim**, including "## The other side" twice and
   both copies of every Bomb/intent line. It was not two enemies; the letter was
   [A] on both copies. It did not block play but it made me re-read to be sure
   there was one body.
4. **The chooser contradicted itself.** The Gorge screen said "Choosing here arms
   a pick … Say `confirm` after `choose`", then printed "Confirm is not
   available." I chose two cards and `confirm` was **refused**: *"there is nothing
   waiting to be confirmed"* — the picks had already resolved. The Neow removal
   screen printed the same "Confirm is not available" and there `confirm` **was**
   required and worked. Same sentence, opposite behaviour.

**(d) Never wanted / happiest to draw.**
Never wanted: **Strike.** Happiest: **Ka-pow!** — Retain, 0 Energy, and it is the
button the whole kit builds toward; drawing it once meant having it every turn
until the moment was right, which is exactly the right card to attach Retain to.

**(e) Did the first turn of the first fight present a decision?**
Yes, and a good one. Jumpy Dumpty is Innate and Ka-pow! is a free Retain
detonator, so turn 1 of fight 1 is literally "plant it and hold, or plant it and
pop it now" — with a *printed* reason for each side (8 now + Mine 3 on ALL +
a Spark, versus 12 next turn). Two Toadpoles with different intents even gave the
target choice teeth. That is a strong opening hand for teaching a kit.
