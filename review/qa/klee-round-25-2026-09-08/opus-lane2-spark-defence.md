## Fight 1 — Fuzzy Wurm Crawler [A], HP 55/55

**Turn 1** (3 energy; enemy intent: Attack for 4).
Played **Witches' Circle** → **Fischl — Oz, at Your Side** → **Jumpy Dumpty** on A.
The order was the decision, and it was a real one. Witches' Circle reads "Whenever you play a Hexerei card, place a Bomb 3 on a random enemy"; Fischl's face reads "Hexerei." Putting the power down *first* meant the Fischl play itself paid a Bomb 3. It did: the enemy panel went to `Bomb 11 ... Bomb sizes here, oldest first: 3 / 8`, which is exactly Jumpy's 8 plus Witches' Circle's 3. Reversed, that Bomb 3 never exists.
**Rejected:** Strike + Defend + Strike, i.e. 12 damage now and 5 Block. Rejected because the intent line said 4 damage — cheap enough to eat — and two powers laid on turn 1 compound for the rest of the fight. Took 4.

**Turn 2** (enemy intent: **Empower (Buff)**, no damage).
Played **Sucrose — Wind Spirit Creation** (0) → **Dodoco Cover** → **Strike** ×2.
The intent line is what decided this turn. With no incoming damage, Block was worthless and every energy went to charge. Bombs grow 4 each per turn, so with four charges on the field a turn of waiting is worth +16 — more than any card in my hand could deal.
Sucrose moved Spark 2 → 4: the face says a Hexerei play makes 1 Spark, "1 more if it triggered an Elemental Reaction," and it had triggered one. Printed text and outcome agreed exactly.
**Rejected:** Ka-pow! for an immediate Set off of Bomb 26 into the Electro aura for Overloaded. Rejected purely on the intent line — detonating into a turn where the enemy does not attack spends the charge at its smallest.

**Turn 3** (enemy 33 HP, `Bomb 42 ... 11 / 16 / 7 / 8`; intent: Attack for 11, Strength 7).
Played **Barbara — Front Row Seat** → **Ka-pow!** on A. Kill.
Ka-pow is 0 energy, so Barbara was free to play first purely to *read* her per-Bomb Block clause. Bomb 42 against 33 HP was lethal several times over.
**Rejected:** Careful Now / more building. Rejected because lethal was on the table for zero energy.

**Judging the fight, not the turns:** turn 3's kill was obvious, but it was obvious *because* turn 1 sequenced the power before the Hexerei and turn 2 read a Buff intent and banked instead of spending. That is a plan paying off, not a dead turn. The fight had two real decisions and both were made before the turn that won it.

## Fight 2 — Nibbit [A], HP 42/42

**Turn 1** (intent: Attack for 12).
Played **Jumpy Dumpty** → **Sucrose** (0) → **Fischl** → **Careful Now**.
Two decisions here, one of them the best-designed thing I met all run.

First, **Careful Now** reads "Retain. Gain Block equal to your largest Bomb when played, up to 10." The phrase *when played* is load-bearing: it must come after the Bomb. Jumpy Dumpty first, Careful Now after, gave exactly `Block 8` off `Bomb 8`. Played in the other order it is a blank card. That ordering constraint is legible from the face and it bites.

Second, and this is the one I want on record: **Fischl and Barbara actively fight each other.** Barbara applies Hydro; Fischl's Oz "deals 5 Electro damage to a random enemy" at end of turn, which consumes that Hydro aura and turns a planned Vaporize into Electro-Charged. I could play one or the other, not both, and the screen never says so — you have to hold both faces in your head at once. I chose Fischl (a permanent engine) over Barbara (5 Block once), and paid 4 HP for it: Block 8 against a 12 hit.
**Rejected:** Barbara + Careful Now for 13 Block and a clean no-damage turn. Rejected to bank the engine; 4 HP for a 5-damage-per-turn power that lasts the fight is a price I would pay again.

**Turn 2** (intent: Attack for 6 **and** Defend).
Drew Dodoco Cover ×2, Strike ×2, Defend — **no detonator, no companion, nothing with a condition on it.**
Played Dodoco Cover ×2 (10 Block, two Bomb 4s) and one Strike.
**Rejected:** Defend over the Strike. This is the honest answer: neither mattered. 10 Block already covered a 6-damage hit, and the enemy's own Defend intent meant the Strike was likely eaten by its Block. **This turn presented no real decision.** Two Dodoco Covers were strictly correct and the third card was a coin-flip between two dead options. That is the finding.

**Turn 3** (enemy 26 HP + Block 5, `Bomb 32`).
Played **Ka-pow!** on A. Kill.
**Rejected:** nothing meaningful — 32 of Bomb against 26 HP is arithmetic, and it was set up two turns earlier.

## Fight 3 — Shrinker Beetle [A], HP 39/39

**Turn 1** (intent: DebuffStrong, no damage).
Jumpy Dumpty → Dodoco Cover → Strike. Build turn, decided entirely by the intent line saying no damage was coming.

What impressed me: my Strike's printed face immediately re-read **"Deal 4 damage"** instead of 6, and Perfect Timing later read 5 instead of 8. The screen rewrites the number on the card rather than making me apply the multiplier myself. That is genuinely good legibility and it is the opposite of the Bomb panel's behaviour (below).

Played **Sucrose** (0) → **Dodoco Cover** → **Barbara** → **Coven Errand**.
Sucrose first, deliberately: Swirl "consumes and copies the aura," so playing it before Barbara meant it fizzled on a bare enemy instead of eating the Hydro I was about to apply. Dodoco + Barbara gave `Block 10` against a 7 hit, and Barbara's Hydro set up Vaporize on a stack of Pyro Bombs.
**Rejected: Fischl.** I had the energy and it is my best power, and I did not play it, because Oz's end-of-turn Electro would have consumed Barbara's Hydro and cost me the 1.5× on a 30-plus Bomb. This is the same tension as fight 2 turn 1 and it is the most interesting recurring decision in the kit.
Coven Errand reads "place a Bomb 5. If you played a Hexerei card this turn, place it on ALL enemies instead" — with one enemy the conditional is cosmetic, but it was free to satisfy.

The face printed its own reaction maths for me: *"Reaction preview: Vaporize — The triggering hit deals 1.5x damage and consumes the aura. **Judging the fight:** three turns, no damage taken after the debuff turn, and the decision that won it was turn 2's refusal to play Fischl. The kill itself was arithmetic.

## Fight 4 — Tracker Raider [A] 21, Axe Raider [B] 20, Assassin Raider [C] 22

The first fight that was actually a puzzle: three bodies, 63 total HP, 15 incoming on turn 1, and my whole kit is single-target charge that wants several turns.

**Turn 1.**
Played **Jumpy Dumpty** on C → **Kindling** (0) → **Perfect Timing** on C → **Strike** on C.
The plan was to use Jumpy Dumpty's rider — "When it goes off, place a Mine 3 on ALL enemies" — as my only AoE, aimed at C because C's intent was the biggest single hit (10).
**Where the screen and my read disagreed:** I expected the Mine 3 to finish C on the spot. It did not — C sat at `HP 4/22` with `Mine 3` on it. The Mine's own text says it "goes off just before its enemy's hit," i.e. at C's turn, not at placement. **The screen had said this plainly and I misread it.** I burned my last energy on a Strike to finish C rather than trusting a Mine that would have killed it anyway a moment later. My error, not the kit's — but it is an error the wording invites, because everywhere else "goes off" is something *I* cause.
**Rejected:** blocking. With 15 incoming and 63 enemy HP I judged that killing the 10-damage body outright beat absorbing one turn.

**Turn 2** (Frail 2 applied: "Gain 25% less Block from cards"; A now `Mine 14`, incoming 13).
Played **Ka-pow!** on A → **Strike** on A (kill) → **Dodoco Cover** on B → **Careful Now**.
Two printed rules paid off exactly here. First, C died with a Mine on it and the panel showed the charge had **moved to A** — "if the enemy dies with it on, it moves to a survivor," which turned A's `Mine 3` into `Mine 14`. Free damage from a body I killed. Second, Frail rewrote the Block faces in place: Defend read **3**, Dodoco Cover read **3**.
I sequenced Dodoco Cover *before* Careful Now on purpose, so that Careful Now had a Bomb 4 to price off instead of an empty field: result `Block 6` = 3 (Dodoco) + 3 (Careful Now: Bomb 4 → 4, less 25% Frail). Had I reversed them, Careful Now would have read the field as empty and paid nothing.
**Rejected:** Defend instead of Careful Now. Identical 3 Block — but playing Careful Now let me *measure* the interaction, and cost nothing.

**Turn 3** (B alone, 17 HP + 5 Block, incoming 12).
Drew Coven Errand, Sucrose, Defend, Witches' Circle, Barbara. **No detonator.** Spark sitting at 5.
Played **Sucrose** (0, to dig) → drew **Fischl**, still no detonator → **Barbara** → **Fischl** → **Defend**.
Here I deliberately took the anti-synergy I had twice avoided, because this time it was the *point*: Barbara's Hydro plus Oz's Electro made **Electro-Charged**, and the resulting `Poison 3` ticks at the start of B's turn, which is the only damage I had that ignored B's Block. 6 Block against 12; took 6.
**This is the round's key turn for (f)** — see below. Barbara's clause "Whenever a Bomb goes off this turn, gain 3 Block" was live, printed, and paid **nothing**, because I held no card that could set a Bomb off.

**Turn 4.**
Drew **Fwoosh!** — "cost 1 Spark ... Set off. Deal 6 damage." B at 13 with `Bomb 12`. Played it. Kill.
Spark was at 8. The card cost 1. **The Spark price has never once been a constraint.**

## Fight 5 — Shrinker Beetle [A] 38, Fuzzy Wurm Crawler [B] 56

**Turn 1** (A: DebuffStrong; B: Attack 4).
Played **Sucrose** (0) → **Coven Errand** → **Jumpy Dumpty** on A → **Kindling** (0) → **Strike** on A.
Sucrose first so Coven Errand's "if you played a Hexerei card this turn, place it on ALL enemies instead" was satisfied — that genuinely mattered here, with two bodies, and it seeded B.
**This is where Kindling contradicted its own text.** Its face reads: *"Each Bomb on an enemy whose aura is not Pyro grows by 4. If there is none, your largest Bomb grows by 2."* Neither enemy had **any** aura. An enemy with no aura does not have a Pyro aura, so all four Bombs should have grown by 4. Instead the panel showed A at `5 / 10` and B unchanged at `5` — only the single largest Bomb grew, and by **2**. The card took the fallback branch. I confirmed this was the no-aura case specifically in the Elite (below), where with a Hydro aura present Kindling grew both Bombs by exactly 4 as printed. **A bare enemy is being treated as failing "whose aura is not Pyro."**

**Turn 2** (Shrink active; A: Attack 7).
Played **Dodoco Cover** on A → **Barbara** on A → **Strike**. `Block 10` against 7.
**Rejected:** Fischl again, for the third time, for the same Hydro-vs-Electro reason.

**Turn 3** — the best turn of the run. (A: 28 HP, `Bomb 45`, Hydro aura, Attack 13. B: 56 HP, `Bomb 13`, Strength 7, Attack 11. 24 incoming, me at 39.)
Drew **Chained Reactions, Ka-pow!, Bang Bang!, Perfect Timing, Defend**.
Played **Chained Reactions** → **Ka-pow!** on A → **Bang Bang!** on B → **Perfect Timing** on B. Both enemies dead, zero damage taken.
The whole turn was one ordering decision. Chained Reactions ("Whenever one of your Bombs goes off, place a Bomb 3 on a random enemy") had to land *before* any detonation or the chain never starts. Then Ka-pow killed A for 0 energy — which removed **Shrink**, so everything after it hit at full value — and A's three Bombs going off seeded three fresh Bomb 3s onto B. Bang Bang then set off B's now-`Bomb 30` for 5 hits, each of which seeded *another* Bomb 3, leaving B at 18 HP with a freshly rebuilt `Bomb 19`. Perfect Timing detonated that for the kill.
**Rejected:** blocking any part of the 24 incoming. Rejected because the chain was lethal on both bodies inside one turn, and I could see it was by reading the Bomb totals before committing.
This is the kit at its best, and every part of it was legible from the printed faces.

## Elite — Bygone Effigy [A], HP 127/127 (in progress at the action cap)

I chose the Elite over a Rest at floor 7, at 39/62, deliberately: every fight to that point had ended with more Block than I needed, and the Elite was the only room left that could actually pressure the defence loop this round was run to test. It did.

**Turn 1** (asleep, no damage). Jumpy Dumpty → Barbara → Strike.
`Slow` reads "Whenever you play a card, this enemy receives 10% more damage from Attacks this turn. It counts the cards played BEFORE this one." That is a clean, legible incentive to sequence attacks last, and I did — Strike third, for +20%.

**Turn 2** (Empower, no damage). Witches' Circle → Dodoco Cover → **Kindling** → Sucrose.
Kindling here grew `12 → 16` and `4 → 8`, **both by 4**, with a Hydro aura on the target. This is the control that confirms the fight-5 misfire is specifically the no-aura case.
Sucrose's Swirl on a lone enemy **re-applied and extended** its own Hydro (`Hydro Aura 1` → `Hydro Aura 2`) rather than stripping it. Useful, and not something I could have predicted from "the aura is consumed and copied onto ALL enemies."

**A reporting quirk worth naming:** the panel read `Bomb 18 ... Bomb sizes here, oldest first: 12`. The header and the list disagree because the header folds in the Vaporize multiplier against the standing Hydro aura (12 × 1.5 = 18). It is *honest* — it is telling me what a Set off would actually deal right now — but the same panel elsewhere prints raw sums, so I had to work out which number was which.

**Turn 3** — the turn this round exists to observe. (Enemy 112 HP, `Bomb 49`, Hydro aura, **Attack for 23**. Me at 39 HP.)
Hand: Careful Now, Coven Errand, Dig In, Chained Reactions, Ka-pow!. Energy 3, Spark 4.
Played **Careful Now** → **Chained Reactions** → **Ka-pow!** on A → **Dig In** → **Coven Errand**.
Result: `Block 18` (10 + 8) and the Effigy from 112 to **59**.

The ordering here is the sharpest decision in the kit and it is a genuine dilemma, not a puzzle with a free answer: **Careful Now prices its Block off my largest Bomb, and the detonation I want this turn is precisely what destroys that Bomb.** Played first it paid its full capped 10 (off `Bomb 20`); played after Ka-pow it would have read a field of freshly-seeded Bomb 3s and paid 3. So the defence card must be spent *before* the payoff it is defending, which means on the turn you cash in you are choosing between a big Block and a big detonation ordering — and Chained Reactions is what makes that survivable, because it rebuilds the field the same turn.
**Dig In** (8 Block for 1 Spark, no Energy) is what made the turn work at all: 3 energy could not buy both Careful Now and Chained Reactions and a Block card. Spending Spark instead of Energy for Block is the only reason I had 18 rather than 10.
Took 5. HP 34.

**Turn 4** (`Bomb 37`, enemy 56, Attack 23 again, me at 34).
Drew Defend ×2, Strike, Dodoco Cover, Fwoosh!. **Neither Careful Now nor Dig In in hand.**
Played Dodoco Cover → Defend → Defend = **15 Block** against 23. Took 8. HP **26/62**.
**Rejected:** Fwoosh! for a 37-Bomb detonation (43 damage, leaving the Effigy at 13). Rejected because at 34 HP against a repeating 23 I judged survival over tempo, and because I was at 119 of 120 actions and wanted the block resolved and *observed* rather than projected.
The action cap landed on that `end turn`. At the stop: me 26/62, Effigy 56/127 with `Bomb 65` on it — one detonation from dead.

## The kit, after 6 fights

### (a) Which decisions felt like real choices, and what they traded off

1. **Ordering the Block card against the detonation** (on the turn — Elite turn 3, fight 4 turn 2, fight 2 turn 1). Careful Now reads "your largest Bomb *when played*," so it must precede the Set off that is the whole point of the turn. Trade: a big Block now versus a bigger Bomb to price off later. This is the best decision in the kit and it is entirely readable off the face.
2. **Fischl versus Barbara** (at the draft *and* on the turn — fights 2, 3, 5). Oz's end-of-turn Electro eats Barbara's Hydro, so a planned Vaporize becomes an Electro-Charged. I declined to play my best power three separate times to protect a 1.5× multiplier. Trade: permanent engine versus one reaction. Nothing on either card warns you; you must hold both faces at once.
3. **Reading the intent line to decide whether to bank or spend** (on the turn — fight 1 turn 2, fight 3 turn 1, Elite turns 1–2). Bombs grow 4 each per turn, so a Buff/Debuff/Sleep intent converts directly into "spend nothing on Block, add charge." This makes enemy intent unusually load-bearing for this kit and it worked every time.
4. **Chained Reactions before anything detonates** (on the turn — fight 5 turn 3, Elite turn 3). Trade: 1 energy now for a field that rebuilds itself mid-turn. It turned fight 5 into a one-turn double kill.
5. **Powers before Hexerei plays** (on the turn — fight 1 turn 1). Witches' Circle first means the very next companion card pays a Bomb 3.
6. **Taking Dig In at the draft.** A Block card priced in Spark rather than Energy is the only reason the Elite's 23-damage turn was survivable alongside two 1-energy setup cards.

### (b) What felt automatic, and what never seemed worth playing

- **The starter Strikes and Defends.** Every turn where my hand was mostly basics (fight 2 turn 2, Elite turn 4) had no decision in it — I played the obviously-correct Block and then flipped a coin between two dead cards. I spent 75 gold to remove exactly one Strike and would have removed four more.
- **Sucrose — Wind Spirit Creation** is close to automatic rather than interesting: 0 cost, draw 1, generates Spark. I played it on sight every time. It is never *wrong*, which is another way of saying it is never a decision. (Its one genuinely interesting property — Swirl re-applying and extending a lone enemy's own aura — I found by accident in the Elite.)
- **Coven Errand's conditional** ("on ALL enemies instead") was cosmetic in 5 of 6 fights because I was usually facing one body.
- **All three potions went unused for the entire run.** A Block Potion (12 Block) was in my belt through both 23-damage Elite turns. That is partly my hoarding, but it is also a symptom: the Bomb loop makes you feel you are always two turns from solving the fight outright, so the emergency button never feels like *this* turn's answer.

### (c) What I could not understand, or that contradicted its own printed text

1. **Kindling, on a bare enemy — a genuine contradiction.** Face: *"Each Bomb on an enemy whose aura is not Pyro grows by 4. If there is none, your largest Bomb grows by 2."* In fight 5 turn 1 neither enemy had any aura and four Bombs were on the field; only the largest grew, by 2. In the Elite turn 2, with a Hydro aura present, both Bombs grew by 4 exactly as printed. An enemy with **no** aura is being treated as failing the test "whose aura is not Pyro," which is the opposite of what that sentence says.
2. **`Bomb N` header versus the sizes list.** `Bomb 18 ... sizes, oldest first: 12` — the header silently folds in a pending Vaporize multiplier while the list is raw. Honest but easy to misread, and the two numbers are adjacent on the same line.
3. **Mine timing.** "A Bomb that also goes off just before its enemy's hit" is accurate, but everywhere else in the kit "goes off" is something the player causes with a Set off card. I read a Mine 3 as immediate damage and wasted a Strike finishing a body the Mine would have killed. My misread, but the verb is overloaded.
4. **Witches' Circle displays as `Witches' Circle 3 (buff)`** where the 3 is the Bomb size it places, not a stack count. `Chained Reactions 3` reads the same way. Every other numbered buff on the panel is a magnitude of itself.
5. The **Fischl/Barbara aura conflict** (a), which nothing on either card hints at.

### (d) The card I never wanted to play, and the one I was happiest to draw

- **Never wanted: Strike.** 6 damage, no interaction with Bombs, Sparks, auras, or any condition in the kit. In a deck whose every interesting card asks "what is on the field," Strike asks nothing. Defend is nearly as bad but at least answers an intent line.
- **Happiest to draw: Chained Reactions.** Drawing it in fight 5 turn 3 turned a two-body, 24-incoming turn into a zero-damage double kill, and in the Elite it is the card that resolves the (a)(1) dilemma by rebuilding the field the same turn I spend it. **Honourable mention: Dig In**, which was the difference between 10 and 18 Block on the only turn this run that genuinely hurt.

### (e) Did the first turn of the first fight already present a decision?

**Yes, and a good one.** Three 1-cost cards for exactly 3 energy, so the *choice was the order, not the selection*: Witches' Circle before Fischl means the Fischl play itself pays a Bomb 3, and I could verify it landed (`Bomb sizes: 3 / 8`). There was also a real reject — Strike + Defend for immediate damage and Block — that the intent line ("Attack for 4") let me price and dismiss. A first turn where sequencing matters and the screen lets you check that it mattered is a good opening.

### (f) Turns where I needed Block and did not get it — which of the three was true

Four turns qualify. **On none of them was the Spark the missing piece, and on none of them was the Bomb missing.**

| Turn | Needed | Got | Which was true |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fight 2, turn 1 | 12 | 8 | **None of the three.** Careful Now's condition was *true* and paid in full — the Bomb was simply only 8 that early. I also chose to spend the energy on Fischl rather than Barbara. Cause: energy and a young Bomb, not a failed condition. |
| Fight 4, turn 3 | 12 | 6 | **The Block card's printed condition was false.** Barbara's "Whenever a Bomb goes off this turn, gain 3 Block" was live and paid **nothing** — because I held no Set off card. The **Bomb was present** (`Bomb 8` on B) and **Spark was at 5**; what was missing was a *detonator*. Frail also cut the flat halves 5→3. |
| Elite, turn 3 | 23 | 18 | **None of the three** — this turn worked. Careful Now paid its full capped 10, Dig In paid 8 off 1 Spark. Took 5 by choice, having spent the rest on the detonation. |
| Elite, turn 4 | 23 | 15 | **None of the three.** `Bomb 37` was on the field and Spark was at 7 — both defence conditions would have paid in full. **Careful Now and Dig In were simply not in the drawn hand.** The limiter was draw, not resource. |

**The pattern:** the defence cards' *conditions* failed exactly once (fight 4 turn 3), and when they did, the missing ingredient was neither Spark nor Bomb but a **Set off card** — Barbara prices in "a Bomb going off," which is a third resource the card does not name and which my deck could only supply from 3 of 23 cards early on. Careful Now, by contrast, prices in the Bomb itself and therefore essentially never failed: a Bomb is nearly always on the field once Jumpy Dumpty is Innate.

**Did the resource the defence cards price in ever reach zero on a turn I needed it? No — not once, and not close.** Spark ran 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5 → 6 → 7 → 8 across the run and I never failed to pay a Spark price. It **peaked at 8 while I was taking damage**. My only Spark sinks all run were Fwoosh! (1), Bang Bang! (2) and Dig In (1), acquired at floors 3, 5 and 7 respectively; before that Spark was pure accumulating surplus with **no sink whatsoever**. Pounding Surprise adds 1 per Bomb going off on top, so the big detonation turns — exactly the turns you might want to convert Spark into Block — are the turns Spark *grows* fastest.

That is the clearest finding of this round: **Spark is not a constraint on Klee's defence; it is a currency with almost nothing to buy.** Dig In was the single most valuable card I picked up precisely because it is a sink that converts the surplus into the thing that was actually scarce — Block on a turn when Energy was already committed to setup. The scarce resources in this kit are **Energy** and **detonator draw**, not Spark.
