## Fight 1 — Leaf Slime (S) 15, Leaf Slime (M) 35, Twig Slime (S) 8

Opening screen already showed them at 12/32/5: Mercury Hourglass fires before the
first hand is read. HP 64/80.

**Turn 1.** Hand: Strike ×3, Slack Water, Defend. Played Strike on the Twig Slime
[C] (5 HP, killing the only attacker), then Strike ×2 on Leaf Slime (S) [A] for
exactly 12.

*Rejected:* writing Slack Water's Plan on the Bake-Kurage. Its Plan line is
"Apply 1 Weak to ALL enemies", and with the Casket that would have been 1 Weak +
2 Hydro on each of three bodies at the start of turn 2 — but only the Twig Slime
was attacking, and killing both small bodies outright removed a 4-damage hit and
one of the three Status cards being handed to me. The Plan's payoff arrives a
turn after the bodies that made it worth writing are dead. **This was a real
decision and the kit made it: three energy bought either two corpses now or a
board-wide debuff later.** I chose corpses.

**Turn 2.** Hand: Strike ×2, Defend ×2, Kurage's Oath. Wrote **Kurage's Oath on
the Bake-Kurage** (Plan: 7 damage to ALL, versus 3 now), then Strike ×2 on the
Leaf Slime (M).

*Rejected:* playing Kurage's Oath for its 3 now, and Strike + Defend. The slime
intended 8 damage and I was at 64/80, so a turn of delay cost 8 HP to buy 4 extra
damage plus a free turn-3 opener. Against a 29-HP body with a 3/turn relic behind
me the arithmetic favoured the Plan; against a hard hitter it would not have.

**Turn 3.** Carry-out printed: `Bake-Kurage: Kurage's Oath, 7 — the 7 is damage.`
plus the Hourglass 3 → slime at 7. Two Strikes finished it.

*Rejected:* nothing. This turn had no decision, and that is the Plan from turn 2
paying off, not a dead turn — the 7 arrived exactly as written and the only
question left was which two of three identical Strikes to spend.

**Card reward.** Offered Moon's Reflection, **Undertow** (taken), Song of Pearls,

## Fight 2 — Nibbit 42

**Turn 1.** Played **Slack Water on the Nibbit** (4 damage, 1 Weak → the Casket
added 2 Hydro; 39→33 and its intent dropped from 17 to 9 on the same screen),
then Strike ×2.

*Rejected:* writing Slack Water as a Plan. Its Plan is "Weak to ALL", and with a
single enemy "ALL" is one body — so the Plan line was strictly worse than the
printed line here, and the card resolved its own dual mode for me. Also rejected
Defend over the second Strike: with 42 HP of enemy and 9 damage a turn coming in,
one turn saved was worth more than 5 Block.

**Turn 2.** Nibbit at 18, intent 6 damage **and** a Block. Played Strike (6),
Kurage's Oath **for its printed 3 now, not as a Plan**, then Defend.

*Rejected:* writing Kurage's Oath for 7. The enemy was about to gain Block on its
own turn, so a 7 landing next turn would eat the Block while a 3 landing now
would not. **This is the sharpest decision the Plan mechanic gave me all act: the
same card is worth 3 or 7 depending on whether the enemy's next intent is
defensive.** I also rejected a third attack over Defend because the Nibbit's
6+Block turn was cheap to eat down to 1.

**Turn 3.** Nibbit at 9 with 2 Block. Played **Slack Water** first, on purpose:
4 damage ate the 2 Block and 2 HP, then the Weak fired the Casket for 2 more →
9+2 Block down to 5. Then Undertow, whose face had switched to 10 because the
body was now debuffed, killed it.

*Rejected:* Undertow first (would have read 7, not 10) and plain Strike ×2. The
ordering was the decision, and the screen told me it mattered by printing the
conditional number on Undertow's own face.

## Fight 3 — Byrdonis (Elite) 83

Territorial: +1 Strength at the end of each of its turns. Opening intent 17.

**Turn 1.** Slack Water on Byrdonis (6 damage, Weak → intent 17→12), Undertow
(10, because Weak had made it debuffed), Defend. Took 7.

*Rejected:* **Toric Toughness** (2 energy, 5 Block now and 5 at the start of the
next two turns — 15 Block total). Undertow + Defend was 10 damage and 5 Block for
the same two energy, and against a body that gains Strength every turn the fight
had to be short. Also rejected writing anything on the Bake-Kurage: I held no
card whose Plan line beat its printed line against one big attacker.

**Turn 2.** Drew Defend ×2, Strike ×3 — a hand of nothing but starters. Played
three Strikes for 18. Took 12.

*Rejected:* Strike ×2 + Defend. **This turn contained no kit decision at all.**
Five starter cards, no Plan line anywhere in hand, and the only question was
"race or block", which the Strength scaling had already answered. Worth saying
plainly: the deck was still two thirds base Strike/Defend, and on the draws where
that showed up the Bake-Kurage sat there with nothing to write on it.

**Turn 3.** Byrdonis at 40, intent 19, me at 47. Used **Heart of Iron** (7
Plating), played **Shinobu — Sanctifying Ring**, then Strike ×2.

*Rejected:* playing **The Moon Overlooks the Waters+**, which was in hand. It is
my best card and I left it unplayed, because it does nothing on a turn where I
hold no Plan card, and I judged the fight had two turns left. Also rejected
saving the potion — with 19 incoming and 21 after that, 7 Plating a turn was
worth more than a later, tidier use. Ended the turn on 12 Block (Plating 7 +
Shinobu 5) and took 7 of the 19.

**Turn 4.** This is the turn where the elemental text turned into a mechanism.
Shinobu had left an **Electro Aura** on Byrdonis, and both my Hydro cards now
printed *"Reaction preview: Electro-Charged — Hydro meets Electro: the reacted
enemy loses 4 HP at the start of its turn, 1 less each turn."* Played **Slack
Water** into it: 20 HP → 12, i.e. 8 from a card that reads 4. The screen let me
account for every point — 4 card damage, +2 Casket for the Weak, +2 Casket for
the **Poison** the reaction applied (Poison is a debuff, so the Casket fired
twice on one card). Then Undertow (10) and Defend; Shinobu's end-of-turn 5 Electro
killed it before it swung.

*Rejected:* Undertow before Slack Water. Slack Water first is what set up
Undertow's debuffed face **and** consumed the aura for the reaction; that
ordering had been learned in fight 2 and paid here.

The screen also did exactly what its own long warning said it would: after the
reaction consumed the Electro aura, Byrdonis showed **Hydro Aura 2**, because the
Casket's Hydro hit re-applied an aura inside the same beat. The paragraph
("THAT LAST RULE CAN HIDE THE FIRST") predicted a confusion I would otherwise
have written down as a bug. It is a lot of words, but it earned them.

## Interlude — the pick that changed the run

At "The Future of Potions?" I traded a Lucky Tonic for an Upgraded Rare Power and
was shown three, all of them Bake-Kurage powers:

- **The Clouds Like Waves Rippling+** — 3 Block whenever you apply a debuff.
- **Nereid's Ascension+** — "At the start of your turn, the Bake-Kurage carries
  out every Plan twice."
- **The Moon Overlooks the Waters+** — "Plans also happen now, as you write them."

I took **The Moon Overlooks the Waters+**. Nereid's and Moon both double a Plan;
Moon delivers half of the doubling immediately, which is the exact drawback I had
just spent an elite refusing to pay. Nereid's was the near-miss. This is the pick
that made the second half of the act a different game, and it is worth flagging
that **the card which fixes the kit's central cost is a rare power** — before I
drew it, "write a Plan" and "eat a turn of tempo" were the same sentence.

At Wood Carvings I transformed a Defend into **Toric Toughness**, and that
5th added card tripped Book of Five Rings for 20 HP.

## The kit, after 5 fights

**(a) Which decisions felt like real choices, and what they traded off.**

- **"Play it, or write it?" — on the turn, and it is the kit's whole idea.**
  Kurage's Oath is 3 to ALL now or 7 to ALL next turn, and I made that call four
  separate times and answered it differently each time: *write it* against the
  Fuzzy Wurm Crawler (4 damage of intent — delay is nearly free), *play it* against
  the Nibbit (it was about to gain Block, so damage landing next turn would be
  eaten), *play it* on the last turn of fight 5 (there is no next turn when the
  board is lethal), *write it* in fight 1 (29 HP of enemy, 8 of intent). One card,
  four readings, all of them from the printed screen. That is the best thing in
  the kit.
- **Which Plan, when two compete — on the turn.** Fight 5 turn 1: Riptide's Plan
  (13 to ALL) versus Flank's (8 to each enemy that intends to attack) versus
  playing either now. The intents on the screen decided it.
- **Ordering inside a turn — on the turn.** Slack Water before Undertow, because
  Slack Water's Weak flips Undertow's printed face from 7 to 10 and its Hydro
  consumes an Electro aura for the reaction. The screen prints both numbers, so
  this is legible rather than lore.
- **The rare power — at the draft.** Moon+ versus Nereid's Ascension+ was the
  single largest decision of the act, and it was made on a reward screen.
- **The elite's shape — at the draft, and it was made against the kit.** Byrdonis
  was won with Strikes, a potion and a companion card, and the Plan machinery was
  never used. The decision "do I even engage with the Bake-Kurage in this fight"
  is real, and the honest answer against one big scaling attacker was no.

**(b) What felt automatic, and what never seemed worth playing.**

- **Moon+ removes the decision it is built on.** Before it, "write or play" was a
  live question every turn. After it, writing is always at least as good as
  playing — you get the printed effect's replacement *now* and the Plan *again*
  next turn — so the interesting question I praised in (a) stops being asked. It
  is the strongest card in my deck and the one that flattened my turns.
- **Ripple after Moon+ is not a card, it is a button.** 0 cost, hands back 1
  energy immediately and 1 again next turn. There was never a turn where writing
  it was wrong.
- **Every turn that dealt me five starter cards.** Elite turn 2 (Strike ×3,
  Defend ×2) had no kit content in it whatsoever. That is a draft consequence, not
  a kit defect, but it happened on the most important turn of the act.
- **Never worth playing:** three of the seven Companion cards I was shown keyed
  off resources the kit had not given me — Freminet — Shattering Pressure needs
  Frozen enemies, Heizou — Heartstopper Strike needs Swirls, Gorou — Crystal
  Collapse and Chain of Command need *other* Companion cards. **A Companion card
  that only pays off if you already have Companion cards is a dead first offer,
  and I was offered that shape three times.**

**(c) What I could not understand, or that contradicted its own text.**

- Nothing contradicted itself. The one place I expected a bug — an Elemental
  Reaction firing and the aura still showing on the body afterwards — is
  pre-explained at length by the Elemental Reaction paragraph, and when it
  happened (fight 3 turn 4, fight 4 turn 2) the numbers reconciled exactly.
- What I could **not** read off any screen: **how many Plans the Bake-Kurage can
  hold.** The buff prints `Plan 1` and the box says "Play a Plan card on it: it
  carries out the Plan at the start of your next turn" — singular, both times. I
  wrote two in fight 5 turn 1 purely on a guess, and it worked (`Plan` stacked and
  both carried out in order). If there is a cap, no screen says so; if there is
  not, the singular wording actively discouraged me from finding that out for four
  fights.
- Minor: the Elemental Reaction glossary asserted "NO REACTION IS REACHABLE HERE:
  Hydro is the only element this screen can supply" on a *deck-transform* screen
  where I already owned an Electro card. It says "this screen", so it is defensible,
  but it read as stale.
- The Plan glossary says "A carry-out is not a hit: no when-hit power fires",
  while the Bake-Kurage box says "Every planned HIT is the jellyfish's, and it is
  a Hydro hit". I could not tell from the text alone whether the Casket would fire
  inside a carry-out; it does, and the carry-out log told me so *after* the fact
  rather than the card telling me before.

**(d) The card I never wanted to play, and the one I was happiest to draw.**

- **Never wanted:** plain **Defend**. Five Block, no Plan line, nothing to write
  on the jellyfish, and after Frail landed in fight 5 it printed 3. It is the card
  that turns a Bake-Kurage turn into a base-game turn, and I paid 75 gold to
  delete one.
- **Happiest to draw: Riptide.** "Deal 9 damage to ALL enemies. Plan: Deal 13
  damage to ALL enemies." Drawing it means the next two turns are already decided
  in a good way, and its carry-out line printing `Riptide, 13` under each body is
  the most satisfying thing the log does. Honourable mention to **Slack Water**,
  which is a 1-cost card that quietly does four things (damage, Weak, a Casket
  trigger, and an aura for the reaction) and rewarded me for playing it first.

**(e) Did the first turn of the first fight already present a decision?**

**Yes, and a good one.** Three energy, three enemies, one of them attacking. The
choice was: kill the Twig Slime and the small Leaf Slime outright with three
Strikes, or spend one energy writing Slack Water's "Weak to ALL" on the
Bake-Kurage and kill only one body. The kit's central question — *is a bigger
effect one turn later worth a worse board now?* — was on the screen before I had
played a card, and it was answerable from the printed intents. I chose the
corpses, and I would make the same call again, but I had to think about it.
