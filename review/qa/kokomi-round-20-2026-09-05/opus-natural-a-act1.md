## Fight 1 — Nibbit [A], 43 HP

**Plans**

| Turn | Plans written | Carried out at the start of the next turn |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Kurage's Oath+ | — |
| 2 | — | Kurage's Oath+, 10 damage → Nibbit lost 10 |

**Turn 1** (3 energy; hand Defend ×2, Kurage's Oath+, Strike+, Strike; Nibbit
telegraphing Attack 12). Played **Kurage's Oath+ onto the Bake-Kurage**, then
**Strike+** and **Strike** into Nibbit. 15 now, 10 banked.
*Rejected:* playing Kurage's Oath+ for its own line, "Deal 3 damage to ALL enemies",
instead of its Plan line, "Deal 10 damage to ALL enemies". Same energy, same card,
3 versus 10 — the only thing I was buying with the 7 was a turn of delay, and against
a 43 HP body telegraphing 12 I could afford one turn. *Also rejected:* spending the
third energy on a Defend for 5, which against a 12 would have saved 5 HP and cost me
6 damage.

**Turn 2.** The carry-out landed 10, Nibbit at 18, and it now telegraphed Attack 6
*and* Defend. Hand had Slack Water, Strike ×2, Defend ×2. Played **Slack Water**
(4 + the Casket's 2 for the Weak = 6), then both **Strikes** = exactly 18. Dead.
*Rejected:* writing another Plan. Exact lethal was on the board this turn, and its
Defend intent meant waiting would cost me the kill. The turn itself was arithmetic —
but the arithmetic only reached 18 because of the Plan I wrote on turn 1, so I score
it as that plan paying off.

Won on turn 2 at 52/80.

## The kit, after 4 fights

**(a) Which decisions felt like real choices, and where each was made.**

The kit's one real question is *"do I spend this card now, or hand it to the
jellyfish for roughly 1.5× next turn?"* and it is a good question because the answer
genuinely changes turn to turn. Concretely:

- **On the turn — the enemy's telegraph prices the delay.** Fight 3 turn 1: War
  Council+ is 2 damage now or 10 next turn, and the Crawler was hitting for 4, so
  patience was nearly free. Elite turn 2: the same card, the same maths, and the
  Crawler's 4 had become a 4×4 — patience cost me 11 HP. Same card, opposite answer,
  and the intent line is what tells you which. That is the kit working.
- **On the turn — Weak's decay clock.** A Weak applied into a turn the enemy spends
  buffing or handing you status cards is thrown away, because one stack falls off at
  the end of *its* turn. So "play War Council or plan it" is not just damage-versus-
  delay, it is also *which* enemy turn you want the Weak attached to. That is a
  second axis on one card and I liked it.
- **Earlier in the fight.** Fight 2 turn 1: splitting my two Strikes across a 12 and
  an 11 so that next turn's 7-to-all killed both. The turn-2 screen looked like a
  free kill; it was a turn-1 decision resolving.
- **At the draft.** The Sayu / Song of Pearls / Treatise pick decided the run. Two of
  those three read off the Bake-Kurage ("once per turn, when the Bake-Kurage carries
  out a Plan…"), which is exactly the engine I was already operating, and I bought a
  vanilla 10-damage attack instead. I had written a Plan on most turns; I should have
  been able to see that. The pick was legible enough — I just picked badly, which
  means the card reward was doing its job.

**(b) What felt automatic, and what never seemed worth playing.**

- **Defend is the automatic card and the bad one.** Five Block, four copies, against
  turns that were doing 12 and then 16. It was never a *choice* — it was where the
  third energy went when there was nothing else, and it never once changed an outcome
  except when a potion tripled it.
- **Strike is automatic filler.** With 3 energy and a 5-card hand, most turns had
  exactly one interesting card and two Strikes.
- **Kill turns.** Four of my thirteen combat turns were "the number on the board is
  smaller than the number in my hand". Three of those four were the payoff of a Plan
  written one or two turns earlier and I would not call them dead. Fight 2 turn 3 —
  one Strike into a 5 HP body — was genuinely dead.
- **Never worth playing: Slack Water's Plan line.** "Apply 1 Weak to ALL enemies", no
  damage, one energy, delayed a turn. War Council's Plan line is the same Weak plus
  5–8 damage. Once War Council was in the deck, Slack Water was an attack card that
  happened to print a Plan I would never write.

**(c) What I could not understand, or that contradicted its own printed text.**

1. **War Council contradicts itself on its own face.** It prints
   *"Hydro on the carry-out — **Its own hit applies no aura**; the Bake-Kurage carries
   out the Plan as a Hydro hit, which does"* and, three lines below, the generic
   *"Applies Hydro — No aura: applies Hydro for 2 turns."* On elite turn 6 I played
   War Council **directly** (not as a Plan) and every one of the four Wrigglers came
   out of that turn wearing **Hydro Aura 2**. Sayu also resolved that turn and Swirl
   can copy an aura across all bodies, and the Casket had just dealt "2 Hydro damage"
   to each — so I cannot tell from the screen *which* of the three put it there. What
   I can say is that the two aura lines on that one card face disagree with each
   other, and a reader has no way to resolve them.
2. **Relatedly, the Elemental Reaction glossary says "a potion, a relic or an enemy
   applies none unless its own face says so"**, and the Tamakushi Casket's face says
   it deals *2 Hydro damage*. Does the Casket's Hydro leave an aura? Every screen I
   saw is consistent with either answer and none of them settles it. Since the Casket
   fires on almost every card I play, this is the single fact I most wanted and never
   got.
3. **"Infested 4 — Upon dying, summons… something."** I could not price the elite's
   death rattle at all, and it is what killed me: a 63 HP body became 77 HP of fresh
   bodies. The **4** on the buff is unexplained on the face; it turned out to be the
   count of what spawns. Everything else in this kit's text is unusually precise, so
   the one deliberate blank stood out as the one thing I could not plan around — and
   "plan around it" is the kit's whole verb.
4. **Whether Block absorbs Infection's end-of-turn damage.** It does — elite turn 5,
   23 Block, two Infections, zero HP lost — but nothing printed says so, and I had to
   guess at it on the turn that decided whether I lived.
5. **The map's deck list shows Stone Cracker's temporary upgrades as permanent.**
   After fight 1 the deck read "Kurage's Oath+ … Strike+", and those `+` marks were
   Stone Cracker's *for the rest of combat* upgrades, not upgrades I owned. The page
   does footnote that it is showing the deck "as it stood in the last fight", but the
   `+` is doing load-bearing work in a list whose whole job is to tell me what I own.

**What was unusually clear**, and worth keeping: the carry-out log. Printing
*"Bake-Kurage: War Council, 5 — the 5 is damage. **Inside the same beat: Tamakushi
Casket 2 on Fuzzy Wurm Crawler.** → Fuzzy Wurm Crawler lost 10 HP"* — the clause's
own number, the rider it set off, and the total, separated — meant I never once had
to guess where a number came from. The "the 5 is cards drawn" variant for a non-damage
Plan is the same idea and just as good. And **multiple Plans in one turn** works,
is numbered in resolution order on the screen, and needed no explanation once seen.

**(d) The card I never wanted to play, and the one I was happiest to draw.**

Never wanted: **Defend**. Happiest: **War Council**. One energy, it hits every body
on the field through the Casket, it weakens every attacker, and its Plan line was the
best damage-per-energy in my deck. It is the only card that was a good play in both
modes, which is exactly what a card in a "now or next turn" kit should be.

**(e) Did the first turn of the first fight already present a decision?**

**Yes, and it was the kit's central one, unmissably.** The opening hand held
Kurage's Oath+ reading *"Deal 3 damage to ALL enemies. Plan: Deal 10 damage to ALL
enemies"*, against a single 43 HP Nibbit telegraphing Attack 12. Three now, or ten
next turn and eat the 12. I banked it, it killed on turn 2, and I understood what the
character does before I had ended a single turn. No tutorial fight I have played in
this genre gets its own question onto turn one that cleanly.
