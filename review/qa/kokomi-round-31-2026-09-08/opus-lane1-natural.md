## Fight 1 — Shrinker Beetle 38/38 (floor 2)

Opening hand: Kurage's Oath, Defend, Opening Gambit, Defend, Slack Water. 3 energy.

**Turn 1.** Wrote **Opening Gambit** on the Bake-Kurage, then **War**— no: then
**Kurage's Oath** on the Bake-Kurage, then played **Slack Water** face-up on the beetle.
The rejected alternative was the obvious one: play Opening Gambit and Kurage's Oath
face-up for 5 + 3 = 8 damage now. I rejected it on the printed text — Opening Gambit's
Plan reads "The next Plan carried out with this one deals double damage", and the
Bake-Kurage panel says plans "carry out in the order written", so writing Gambit first
and Oath second promised Vulnerable-then-doubled-AoE next turn instead of 8 now.
Slack Water I played face-up rather than as its Dusk Plan because there was one enemy
and its Dusk line only widens Weak to ALL — nothing to widen to. Result printed:
38 → 32, i.e. 4 from the card and **2 from Tamakushi Casket answering the Weak**.
This was a real decision made on the turn, and the trade was tempo for rate.

**Turn 2.** The two plans carried out before I could act: 32 → 8, and the beetle now
wore Vulnerable 1 and a Hydro aura. I also wore **Shrink -1** ("While Shrinker Beetle
is alive, you deal 30% less damage"). The interesting thing is that the screen showed
me the shrink by *rewriting the card faces*: Strike printed "Deal 4 damage" instead of
6. I played **Strike, Strike** and the fight ended. No alternative rejected — this was
the turn-1 plan paying off, and the only question was arithmetic.

Fight cleared in 2 turns, 0 damage taken.

## Fight 2 — Nibbit 43/43 (floor 3)

Opening hand: War Council, Defend, Strike, Strike, Kurage's Oath. 3 energy.
Nibbit intended Attack 12.

**Turn 1.** Played **War Council** face-up (Weak to ALL) and wrote **both Strikes** as
Plans. Rejected: playing both Strikes face-up for 12 now. The Plan line pays 8 where
the face pays 6 — a third more per card — and Nibbit was not going to block, so I took
the rate. War Council face-up rather than planned because its Weak had to land *before*
the 12-damage swing, not after it. It did: I took 9, not 12.

**Turn 2.** Plans carried out for 18 total (43 → 25; 16 from the two Strike plans and 2
from the Casket answering War Council's Weak). Now the screen said Nibbit intended
"Attack for 6 damage **and also** Defensive (Defend)". The Bake-Kurage panel's line
decided this turn: *"A Plan is carried out before you play anything, so it lands in
whatever Block the enemy is still standing in from its own turn — you cannot strip that
Block first."* So I rejected planning entirely and played **Slack Water, Strike, Strike**
face-up: 18 damage into a body that had not blocked yet. This was the best decision of
the run and the screen handed me the reason for it in plain words.

**Turn 3.** Nibbit at 7 HP behind 5 Block, intent Buff. Played **Strike, Strike** and it
died on the second. No rejected alternative — a dead turn, but it is the previous turn's
read cashing in.

## Fight 4 — Shrinker Beetle 38/38 + Fuzzy Wurm Crawler 55/55 (floor 6)

Opening hand: Opening Gambit, Defend, Strike, War Council, Strike.

**Turn 1.** Wrote **Opening Gambit**, wrote **War Council**, played **Strike** face-up on
the beetle. Rejected: killing the beetle first with face-up damage. The reason is the
one genuinely clever line the kit prints — the Bake-Kurage panel says *"Every planned
HIT is the jellyfish's, so Shrink or Weak on you never cuts it, before or after you
write it"* — so against a Shrinker Beetle, writing is strictly better than playing, and
I wanted the two plans banked *before* Shrink landed. The Strike went face-up because
Shrink had not applied yet, so it still hit for a full 6.

**Turn 2.** Plans carried out: beetle 32 → 11, Wurm 55 → 34, both Vulnerable 1 and Weak
1. Shrink -1 was on me and the hand rewrote itself — Flank printed "Deal 5 damage"
instead of 8. I played **Flank, Flank** into the beetle (5 × 1.5 Vulnerable each) and it
died; Shrink lifted with it and the third card's face went back up to 6. Rejected:
spending the turn on the 55-HP Wurm and letting the beetle keep shrinking me. Killing
the debuffer was the decision and the screen made the payoff visible immediately by
reprinting the card faces.
With the last energy I played **Strike** face-up on the Wurm rather than writing its
Plan, because the Wurm's Vulnerable was at 1 and the Plan keyword says "the enemy's
Vulnerable counts next turn" — by the carry-out it would be gone.

**Turn 3.** Wurm 25 HP, Strength 7, intent icon "11". Played **Slack Water** (Weak +
Casket ping), **Flank**, **Strike** — 20 damage, Wurm to 5. Rejected: Defend + Second

disagreed in my favour and the page said so in advance:** the icon read 11, I had
applied Weak, and I took 8. The page's own note is honest about it — "the feed carries
no base, no modifier list and no breakdown" — but it means the number on an intent is
not the number you will take, and I had to learn that by subtraction.

**Turn 4.** Wurm at 5. Played **Strike**. Dead. No decision.

## The kit, after 6 fights

**(a) Which decisions felt like real choices, and what they traded off.**

The kit's whole decision surface is one question asked once per card: *face-up now, or
written on the jellyfish for next turn?* It is a good question because the answer moves,
and four different printed rules move it:

1. **Rate vs tempo** (on the turn). Plan lines pay more — Strike 6 → 8, Kurage's Oath 3
   → 7, War Council 0 damage → 5 — so writing is right whenever the fight has another
   turn in it. Fight 2 turn 1 and fight 5 turn 2.
2. **Block timing** (on the turn, and the sharpest of them). *"A Plan is carried out
   before you play anything, so it lands in whatever Block the enemy is still standing
   in."* The moment an enemy telegraphs Defend, writing becomes wrong. Fight 2 turn 2.
3. **Who owns the hit** (on the turn, but it reads as a draft property). *"Every planned
   HIT is the jellyfish's, so Shrink or Weak on you never cuts it."* Against the
   Shrinker Beetle, writing is immune and playing is 30% worse — fight 4 turn 1. This is
   the best rule in the kit: a debuff on me that my main line simply routes around.
4. **When the debuff has to land** (on the turn). Weak has to be on the body *before* it
   swings, so Slack Water's **Dusk** Plan and War Council face-up beat their opposites
   exactly when an attack is telegraphed, and are wasted when the intent is a Status card
   or a Buff. Fight 5 turn 1; fight 6 turn 1, where I ended a turn with unspent energy
   rather than throw a Weak away.

Two decisions were made **at the draft** and decided whole fights: taking Opening
Gambit at Neow (its doubler is what made fight 3 and fight 5 turn 3 into one-sided
turns) and taking Shell Guard at fight 4, which converts the Casket's debuff pings into
Block and is the only reason turn 6 of the elite existed.

One was made **earlier in the fight**: fight 5 turn 2's three written Strikes came to
exactly the Jaxfruit's remaining 24, which is the plan-count arithmetic actually paying.

**(b) What felt automatic, and what never seemed worth playing.**

Automatic: every lethal turn, which is fine, and every turn where the hand was four
attacks and a Defend — with one enemy, no Block on it and no debuff to time, "write
everything" or "play everything" is a coin-flip the numbers settle without me.
**Defend never seemed worth playing.** 5 Block is a rounding error against a 4×4 elite
and a 20-damage Wriggler pair, and Frail cuts it to 3; I played it three times all run
and each time it was because I had a spare energy and no target. **Rally** (from a
transform) was unplayable in practice — it discounts a Companion card and my natural
draft contained no Companion at all, which makes the whole Companion sub-theme
(General's Banner, Gorou, Rally) dead weight in a deck that never drafts one.
**Flank's Plan line** is close to a trap: written on a turn where nothing intends to
attack it does literally nothing, and there is no on-screen warning beyond the card's
own clause.

**(c) What I could not understand, or that contradicted its own printed text.**

- **Intent numbers do not survive contact with Weak, and nothing on the screen updates
  them.** The Fuzzy Wurm's icon read 11 and hit for 8. The Flyconid's read 16 and hit for
  12. The page is honest that it does no arithmetic on the figure, but the consequence is
  that the one number I need to plan block against is the one number I cannot trust.
- **Vulnerable on me did nothing I could see.** Fight 5 turn 3, wearing Vulnerable 2
  against a 16 icon with Weak applied, I took 12 — the Weak but not the Vulnerable. The
  keyword says "Receive 50% more damage from any attack or card hit." Either it does not
  apply here or the icon already contained it; nothing printed says which.
- **Weak 2 did not reduce a 6.** Elite turn 7: Wriggler B carried Weak 2, its icon read
  6, and I lost exactly 6 HP. By the keyword it should have been 4.
- **The enemy list printed twice** on multi-enemy screens (fight 3, fight 5), including
  every explanatory footnote, which is a legibility defect rather than a rules one.
- **Card choosers need `confirm` after `choose` and do not say so on every screen.**
  The elite's "Choose a card to make free" chooser refused five commands in a row before
  I worked it out (see the declaration below). Some chooser screens print the
  "Say `confirm` after `choose`" note; the potion one did not.
- I never once saw an **Elemental Reaction**. Every card in a natural Kokomi draft is
  Hydro, and the screen said so plainly and repeatedly: "NO REACTION IS REACHABLE HERE:
  Hydro is the only element this screen can supply." Six reactions are documented in
  full on the reward screens and none of them is reachable without drafting an
  off-colour Companion. That is a large, loudly-printed system that a natural draft
  cannot touch, and reading it on every card reward while never being able to use it
  made the text feel longer than the kit.

**(d) The card I never wanted to play, and the one I was happiest to draw.**

Never wanted: **Defend**. It is the card I removed at the shop and the card I would have
removed three more times. Happiest to draw: **War Council**, and specifically
War Council *next to Shell Guard* — one energy that widens a debuff to every body,
converts each of those debuffs into a Casket strike, and converts each strike into 3
Block. It is the only card whose value visibly changed with the board.

**(e) Did the first turn of the first fight already present a decision?**

**Yes, and a good one.** Opening hand held three cards with Plan lines against a single
38-HP enemy telegraphing a debuff. The choice was 8 damage now (Gambit + Oath face-up)
against Vulnerable-then-doubled-AoE next turn, and the ordering rule — plans carry out
in the order written, so Gambit must be written first for its doubler to reach Oath —
was printed on the Bake-Kurage panel where I could act on it. I made that decision
before I understood the kit at all, purely off the printed text, and it was right.
