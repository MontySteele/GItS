**Turn 3.** Nibbit 14 + 5 Block, intent Empower (no damage). Played **Soloist's

a Buff and Block would have expired unused.

**Judgement:** the fight's real decision was turn 2's ordering — Aria before
Spotlight before Second Course — and it was a genuine one, because Spotlight and
Second Course draw on the same pool and Aria's bonus half is gated on a
different meter. Turn 1 and turn 3 were bookkeeping. Zero HP lost.
**Companion cards played: 1** (Freminet). **Turns with an empty stage: 1**
(turn 1 in full; the stage was also empty for the first two plays of turn 2).

## Fight 2 — Shrinker Beetle, 39 HP

**Turn 1.** Intent was DebuffStrong — no damage — so this was a free damage turn.
Played **Bennett — Passion Overload** *first* (0 cost, +4 to my next Attack),
then **Curtain Rises** (6 + 4 = 10, and it deployed the Usher), then **Soloist**
(6). 39 → 23.

*Rejected:* playing Curtain Rises before Bennett, so that Bennett would have had
a member to perform. I rejected it deliberately: the Usher performs for 3 Block,
Block was worthless against a debuff-only intent, and the performance would have
eaten 1 of my 2 Encore. That is a real trade the screen let me price.

*Also rejected:* spending the leftover energy on Lynette for 8 Block — same
reason, and her Swirl would have eaten the Pyro aura Bennett had just applied,
which I wanted to keep for a Melt.

**Turn 2.** The Beetle's debuff landed: `Shrink -1 — While Shrinker Beetle is
alive, your Attacks deal 30% less damage.` Soloist's printed number changed from
6 to 4 on the card face, which is good: the sheet re-prints the true number
rather than making me do the arithmetic. **And the Salon quietly answered it** —
the glossary says "A performance is not an Attack", so member damage was
untouched by Shrink. That is the cleanest thing the kit did all round: an enemy
debuff that the character has a structural answer to.

Played **Guest List** (3 Block + a random Common Companion → Chevreuse —
Interdiction Fire), then **Chevreuse** (4 Pyro + the Usher performing for 3
Block), then **Soloist** (4). 23 → 15.

**Turn 3.** Beetle 15, telegraphing 13. Played **Aria** (Encore 0 → 5, only the
base half: Fanfare was 2, under the 3 the card asks for), **Salon Début** —
Crabaletta's Hydro deploy-performance hit the Pyro aura for **Vaporize, 6 → 9**
(15 → 6) — then **An Invitation** (free, fished Lynette), then **Ethereal
Spotlight** (now affordable at Encore 4) and **Freminet**, whose 4 became 6 under
the Spotlight for exactly lethal.

*Rejected:* the safe line, Regal Bearing (3 Block + Weak) for a 13-damage hit,
holding the Beetle at 6 HP. I rejected it because Shrink keeps taxing me for
every extra turn the Beetle lives, so the exact-lethal gamble was worth ~10 HP
of downside.

**Judgement:** three turns, three genuine decisions, and two of them were about
*sequencing within the turn* rather than which card to play. The Vaporize was
telegraphed by a printed `Reaction preview` line before I committed, which is
what made it plannable rather than lucky. Cost: 1 HP.
**Companion cards played: 3** (Bennett, Chevreuse — Interdiction Fire, Freminet).
**Turns with an empty stage: 0 full turns**, though the stage was empty for
Bennett's play on turn 1, which is why that play performed nobody.

**Turn 1.** 12 incoming across two attackers, no AoE in hand. Played **Aria**

9, **Stage Presence** for 6 Block.

*Rejected:* Soloist for 6 more damage in place of Stage Presence. Against four

reach it either way. **This was the decision the fight turned on**, and it was

that would not exist unless I later drew one specific card.

**Turn 2.** All four had taken Strength 2. Played **Salon Début** (Crabaletta

under the Spotlight), **Curtain Rises** (6 + 6 = ~10 and deploys the Usher),

died; Block 9 against 16 incoming, so 7 HP.

*Where the screen and the outcome disagreed:* I targeted Lynette's Swirl at
Wriggler (1), which was wearing **Cryo**. The next screen showed **Hydro** auras
on two Wrigglers and Cryo nowhere at all. I could not reconstruct from the screen
whether my Swirl consumed the Cryo, copied it and was then overwritten by
Crabaletta's Hydro, or did nothing. See (c).

**Turn 3.** Drew **Chevreuse — Ring of Bursting Grenades**, and the card face
read **"Deal 15 damage to ALL enemies"** — the base 10, re-printed with the

Crabaletta's Hydro. One card took 14/18 and 17/21 off the board and left the
third on 5; **Soloist** finished it.

*Rejected:* Rolling Tide, again, at the exact moment its "all enemies" text
looked relevant. 2 damage twice into four bodies is 4 per target and kills none
of them.

**Judgement:** this fight is the argument *for* the kit. The turn that won it was
turn 1 — spending Encore on a buff whose payoff was two turns and one draw away —
and the turn that looked decisive (turn 3) was just that plan cashing out. The
fight also handed me the plan's ingredients without telling me: Crabaletta's
Hydro performances were what made the Pyro AoE a Vaporize. Cost: 8 HP.
**Companion cards played: 4** (Freminet, Bennett, Lynette — Enigmatic Feint,
Chevreuse — Ring of Bursting Grenades). **Turns with an empty stage: 1**
(all of turn 1).

## Fight 5 — ELITE: Bygone Effigy, 127 HP

damage from Attacks this turn`, and it later re-printed as
`Slow 30 (Receives 30% more damage)`. That is a card-count ramp, and it changed
how I played every turn of this fight: cheap and free cards *first*, the big
Attack *last*. Good, legible design; it turned "what do I play" into "in what
order".

**Turn 1** (Effigy asleep — a free turn). **Ethereal Spotlight** (Encore 2 → 0),
**Gorou — Juuga** (1 energy for three turns of end-of-turn Geo, and it exhausts,

127 → 103.

*Rejected:* Curtain Rises to deploy the Usher for Block. The Effigy was asleep,

tick for every turn it is delayed.

**Turn 2.** Played the free and cheap cards first to stack Slow: **An
Invitation**, **Salon Début** (Crabaletta on stage, performing), **Lynette —
Bogglecat Box** (draw 2, performs Crabaletta again), then **Bennett** (performs
Crabaletta a third time, and — the part I nearly missed — *applies Pyro to my
next Attack*), then **Soloist** into a Hydro aura for a **Vaporize on top of
Slow 50**. 103 → 64. A 6-damage common did about 21.

**Turn 3.** Effigy at 64 with Strength 10, telegraphing 23; I had 0 Encore and

halved it again, 9 Block covered the remainder: **the 23-damage hit did zero**.

That turn is the best the kit looked all round. Three cards, three different
systems (a debuff, a block, an elemental reaction routed through a member),
and the reason it worked was that Crabaletta had been left on stage two turns
earlier.

**Turn 4.** **Aria** — Fanfare was 6, so the conditional half fired: Encore
0 → 10. Then **Second Course**, the second time it was affordable all run.

> **Second Course, as asked (second reading).** Encore **10 before, 5 after**.
> The two performances dealt **6 and 6** — Effigy 44 → 32. Flat this time,
> because Fanfare sat at 6, below the 10 that buys a member +1.

Then **Lynette — Enigmatic Feint** (7 Block + a third performance), **Fire
Potion** (20, into Slow 30), **Explosive Ampoule** (10) for the kill.

*Rejected:* Rapturous Applause on turns 3 and 4. Fanfare would have gone to 13–14
and bought +1 per Attack, but a 1-energy power in a fight I could see ending was

## The four targeted cards

- **Curtain Rises** — *played, three times* (fights 2, 3, 6). This is the card
  that fixes the kit's worst turn: 1 energy, 6 damage, and it puts a body on the
  stage so that every Companion card in hand stops printing "performs nobody".
  I reached for it every time I drew it. It is also the only reason fight 2 had
  a member out on turn 1.
- **Second Course** — *played twice* (fight 1 turn 2; elite turn 4), and
  **locked out three separate times** at Encore 2 when its price is 3. Both
  readings above. When it lands it is excellent — a deploy plus a free extra
  performance is the biggest single burst the kit showed me — but it is
  strictly an *after-Aria* card, and it never once was castable off the fight's
  opening Encore of 2. Its refusal text is also wrong (see (c)).
- **Rolling Tide** — **never played. Drawn in four of six fights and rejected
  every time.** 2 energy for "2 damage to ALL enemies twice" is 4 damage per
  body, the worst rate in the deck; a plain 1-cost Soloist beats it on a single
  target and the drafted Chevreuse beats it on a crowd by a factor of four. Its
  rider, "The front Salon member performs", is the same rider Lynette and
  Bennett carry for 1 and 0 energy. I never found a board where it was the
  right card, including the four-Wriggler fight it looks designed for.
- **Guest List** — *played once* (fight 2 turn 2, where it produced Chevreuse —
  Interdiction Fire and turned a dead turn into a real one); held twice as
  filler. It is fine and slightly dull: 3 Block is a rounding error, so the card
  is really "1 energy, tutor a random Companion", and its value is entirely the
  variance of what it hands you.

## The kit, after 6 fights (5 completed, 1 cut off)

**(a) Which decisions felt like real choices, and what they traded off.**

- **On the turn, and the best of them: ordering, not selection.** Against the
  elite's `Slow` the question stopped being *which* cards and became *in what
  sequence* — free cards first to ramp the multiplier, the Attack last. Against
  the Shrinker Beetle the question was whether to spend Bennett's free +4 before
  or after deploying the Usher, i.e. whether 3 Block was worth 1 Encore on a
  turn the enemy did not attack. Elite turn 3 was the same shape: Regal Bearing,
  then Block, then Freminet, so that Weak and Frozen and Block stacked in the
  order that made a 23-damage hit land for nothing.
- **Earlier in the fight: whether to light the Spotlight.** This is the kit's
  best decision and it is a genuinely hard one, because **the Spotlight and the
  members and Second Course all draw on the same Encore pool**, and Encore is
  also the pool that absorbs damage. Paying 2 Encore on turn 1 for a
  combat-long +50% means performing at three-quarters and taking hits to the
  face until Aria shows up. In fight 3 that gamble is the entire fight; in
  fight 6's turn 3 it is why I lost 12 HP.
- **At the draft: whether to take the Companion.** Every card reward offered a
  Companion in the fourth slot and I took it every time, and the two fights that
  ended in one card (fights 3 and 4) ended that way because of a reward screen
  two floors earlier. That is a real draft decision, but I should say plainly
  that I never once felt the pull of the other three options.
- **Aria's timing** is a small recurring one: its second half is gated on
  Fanfare ≥ 3, and Fanfare only exists if a member has already performed, so
  playing Aria a beat too early costs 5 Encore. It fired 3 times of 4.

**(b) What felt automatic, and what never seemed worth playing.**

- **Automatic:** Aria of Recompense whenever Encore is low, which is most turns —
  it is never wrong. Ethereal Spotlight the moment you can pay for it, once you
  own more than one Companion card. Both Stage Presences, which are just Block.
  Chevreuse's AoE whenever two or more enemies stand.
- **The opening turn of most fights is automatic in the worst way.** The stage
  starts empty every fight (the relic says so: *"Every fight starts unlit"*), so
  on turn 1 the Companion cards — the cards the whole character is built from —
  print *"No member on stage: performs nobody… this card is only its own line."*
  In **5 of the 6 fights the stage was empty for the entire first turn**, and in
  fight 4 there was literally no legal play except Block. The kit's first turn
  is regularly its dullest.
- **Never worth playing:** **Rolling Tide** (see above). **Rapturous Applause**
  is close behind: it prints Fanfare +8, but the threshold that matters is 10,
  so a lone Applause on a fresh fight buys nothing until a member performs
  twice. I played it once, on the first turn of the run, and declined it three
  times afterwards. **An Invitation** at 0 cost is fine; **Guest List** at 1 is
  the same card with 3 Block stapled on and I would rather have the free one.

**(c) What I could not understand, or that contradicted its own printed text.**

1. **Rapturous Applause contradicts the Fanfare tooltip, flatly.** The card says
   *"Fanfare +8."* The Fanfare buff on the same screen says *"Only a member
   performing makes it: 2 when it stays and 5 on an Evoke."* Both cannot be
   true. The card is the one that told the truth.
2. **Two meters appeared and were never explained.** After that first Applause
   the sidebar grew `Fanfare Cap Bonus: 8` and `Fanfare Floor: 8`, each annotated
   *"the game's data feed carries this meter's amount only: no maximum, and no
   rule for how it is spent."* They never appeared again in any later fight. I
   still do not know what a Fanfare Floor does, though I suspect it is why my
   Fanfare stopped decaying at 8 in fight 1.
3. **Second Course's refusal text is wrong, three times over.** With **energy
   3/3** and the card costing **1**, the face read
   `CANNOT BE PLAYED: you do not have enough energy`. The actual shortfall was
   **Encore** (2 held, 3 required). Ethereal Spotlight in the same position
   printed the correct thing — `you have 1 Encore, and this costs 2` — so the
   right message exists and Second Course is not using it. On my first fight I
   spent a genuine beat re-reading my energy bar trying to work out what I had
   miscounted.
4. **I could never tell who the front member was.** The stage buff always names
   one — *"A Companion card you play performs the Usher"* — but the Companion
   glossary says a play *"performs the front member, then sends it to the back"*,
   and after doing exactly that in fight 3 the line still named the Usher. With
   two members up I was guessing which one my next Companion card would fire.
5. **A Swirl I could not audit.** Fight 3, turn 2: I aimed Lynette's Swirl at a
   Wriggler wearing **Cryo**. The next screen showed **Hydro** on two Wrigglers
   and Cryo on none. Consumed and overwritten? Copied and then overwritten?
   Nothing at all? The screen's own Elemental Reaction note warns that exactly
   this can be invisible, which is honest but does not help me decide whether to
   play the card again.
6. **The Spotlight's reach into Gorou is unclear.** Companion cards get *"50%
   stronger printed damage"*, and Gorou is a Companion. His card printed 6 Geo;
   after playing him the buff still read *"deal 6 Geo damage"*, and the numbers
   looked like 6. Either the boost does not reach a delayed effect, or it does
   and nothing on screen says so.
7. **"Burst +5. Elemental Skill — Playing this card grants 5 Burst Energy"**
   appears on the Gentilhomme Usher card in two reward screens. **No Burst meter
   appeared on any battle screen in this run**, so I had no way to price it and
   skipped the card both times partly for that reason.
8. **"Applause Line — Deal 3 damage, already including Fanfare"** (shop, 50
   gold). I do not know what that sentence means, and at 50 gold for 3 damage I
   was not going to find out.

**(d) The card I never wanted, and the one I was happiest to draw.**

- **Never wanted: Rolling Tide.** Four fights, four draws, four declines,
  including on the two boards it is presumably for.
- **Happiest to draw: Chevreuse — Ring of Bursting Grenades**, specifically
  *after* the Spotlight was lit and *after* Crabaletta had left Hydro on things.
  Seeing the card face re-print itself as **"Deal 15 damage to ALL enemies"**
  with a `Reaction preview: Vaporize` underneath it, and knowing that was ~22 on
  two bodies, was the one moment this round where a card felt like the payoff of
  a plan rather than a number. Runner-up: **Aria of Recompense**, which is not
  exciting but is the card that unlocks everything else.

**(e) Did the first turn of the first fight already present a decision?**

**Essentially no.** Hand: Ethereal Spotlight, Rolling Tide, Lynette — Enigmatic
Feint, Stage Presence ×2, Rapturous Applause, against one Nibbit telegraphing 12.
Three energy. Rolling Tide was 4 damage for two of them. Lynette printed
*"performs nobody"* and Swirl had no aura to move. The Spotlight wanted 2 Encore
to buff exactly one Companion card in hand. What was left — Applause plus both
Stage Presences — happens to be **exactly 12 Block against exactly 12 damage**,
which reads less like a choice than like a solution. The only fork I can honestly
claim is swapping a Stage Presence for Lynette, which trades 1 HP for 2 Fanfare.
I dealt zero damage on turn 1 and did not mind, because there was nothing to
decide. **The kit did not show me a decision until turn 2**, when Aria, the
Spotlight and Second Course all wanted the same Encore.
