The run is over. In a short paragraph each, and in plain
language:

1. How do you think this character works?
2. Which tension came up again and again?
3. Which cards defined the run?
4. Where did play start to feel repetitive?
5. What would you avoid drafting next time, and why?

None of this is a judgement of whether the game is fun or good that anyone will treat as approval. It is one model's account of one run, recorded for iteration.
