## The kit, after 8 fights

**(a) Which decisions felt like real choices, and what they traded off.**

Three axes carried nearly every interesting turn, and all three are the Bake-Kurage's:

1. **Face-up now vs written on the Kurage.** Every Plan card is two cards, and the gap between them is large enough to matter — Kurage's Oath+ is 3-to-all played and 10-to-all written, Feint is 6 and 10, Coral Bulwark+ is 9 Block and 11 Block + 2 Weak. The choice is genuinely open because the Plan line costs a turn of tempo, so the answer flips on the enemy's intent: block written is block that arrives *when the swing does*, and damage written is damage that arrives *before* I spend energy. Fight 4 turn 1 is the cleanest instance — `Slippery 1` inverted the ranking of Kurage's Oath+'s two halves outright, and the printed text was enough to see it.
2. **Which Plans, and in what order.** Multiple Plans stack on the Kurage and resolve top-down, so writing Exposed Flank+ *above* Kurage's Oath+ turns 10 damage into 15. That is a sequencing decision made a full turn before the payoff, which is unusual and good.
3. **Whether the Kurage carries out anything at all this turn**, because Sango Isshin reads off it. That single condition retroactively makes turn N's Plan-writing an offensive decision about turn N+1.

Underneath, the ordinary tension was damage-now against HP: I paid 5 HP for 7 damage in fight 1, 5 HP for 9 damage on the elite, 5 HP for 16 damage against the boss, and I would defend all three.

**(b) What felt automatic, and what never seemed worth playing.**

- **Strike and Defend.** Every turn where my hand was basics was a turn with nothing to decide. Fight 1 turn 4, fight 2 turn 3, fight 3 turn 1, fight 5 turn 4 and the elite's last turn were all "play the only lethal/only block and end". That is 5 of roughly 30 combat turns with literally no rejected alternative to write down.
- **Lynette — Bogglecat Box (draw 2).** With a flat 3 energy and a deck of one-costs I was energy-capped, never card-capped. Drawing two cards I cannot pay for is a null play; I declined it twice for exactly that reason and I regret buying it.
- **Kirara's random target.** 11 Block and 10 damage for one energy is the best rate in my deck, but in the boss fight its 10 went into a Minion that was going to evict itself. I had no way to aim it and no way to price it.
- **The Moon, A Ship O'er the Seas** (Mend 3, capped at the HP you entered the fight with) reads as a card that cannot ever gain me HP across a run. I skipped it twice without hesitation.

**(c) What I could not understand, or that contradicted its own printed text.**

1. **"The Moon Overlooks the Waters — Plans also happen when played" is wrong, or at least reads backwards.** I read it as "play the card face-up and its Plan line fires too", which is the only reading in which *played* is doing work — the other branch is called *writing*, not playing. Acting on that read cost me a Coral Bulwark+ and a Sango Isshin on the same turn. The Bake-Kurage panel's own gloss is correct and unambiguous — **"Plans also happen NOW as you write them"** — so the fix is to put the panel's sentence on the card.
2. **Kurage's Oath and Sango Isshin apply a Hydro aura but do not print `[Hydro]`.** Slack Water, Undertow and Feint all carry a visible `[Hydro]` tag with the *Applies Hydro* gloss. After the Kurage carried out Kurage's Oath+ in fight 1 the target was wearing `Hydro Aura 1` from a card whose face says nothing about an element. In a kit whose whole reaction system keys off which element is clinging to a body, an untagged aura source is a real hole.
3. **Potion damage silently sits outside Vulnerable and Slow.** Explosive Ampoule dealt exactly 10 into a body at Vulnerable 3 + Slow 20 while my cards were being multiplied by both. Nothing printed says why.
4. **Strength appears not to reach the Plan line, while Vulnerable does.** With *Vajra* giving +1 Strength, Kurage's Oath+ printed "Deal 4 damage to ALL" (3+1) but its Plan still printed 10, not 11. The Plan glossary explicitly adjudicates two modifiers — "Vulnerable counts; your Weak does not" — and is silent on Strength. It should say. (Relatedly, my *own* Weak did reduce Sango Isshin's 20-to-all to 15, which is consistent with Sango being a played Attack rather than a Plan, but it took me a subtraction to be sure.)
5. **"never a Minion" is not quite what happens.** Feint's single-target Plan correctly skipped both front-standing Kin Followers and struck the Priest behind them. But in fight 7 the *Exposed Flank+* Plan applied Vulnerable to `Eye With Teeth`, a body tagged `Minion 1`, and the relic tick killed it. So ALL-enemies Plans do include Minions while targeted Plans exclude them. That may well be intended; the glossary line reads as a flat prohibition and does not say so.
6. **Vulnerable is never defined anywhere on any screen I saw**, though Plan, Block, Mend, Exhaust, Companion, Slow, Slippery, Illusion, Minion, Frail, Weak and all seven Elemental Reactions are. I bought *Exposed Flank+* on a genre assumption.
7. **The Sapphire Seed event offered "Plant and Nourish — Enchant a card with Sown"** with no gloss for `Sown` on the screen and no way to ask. I took the legible option (Heal 9 + upgrade) purely because the other one was unreadable. An event choice a player cannot evaluate is not a choice.
8. **Elemental Reactions never fired once in eight fights.** Seven of them are glossed on every combat screen — Melt, Vaporize, Overloaded, Superconduct, Electro-Charged, Frozen — and they occupied more vertical space than my hand did. Every element I could apply was Hydro, so every hit either applied or refreshed a Hydro aura and nothing ever reacted. The one Electro card offered (*Shinobu — Thundergrust*) and the one Cryo card (*Ayaka*) came at reward screens where I had better options. **The reaction system was 100% glossary and 0% gameplay for this entire act.**

**(d) The card I never wanted to play, and the one I was happiest to draw.**

- **Never wanted:** *Defend*. Five block, no rider, no Plan line, in a deck where Coral Bulwark+ writes 11 block and 2 Weak for the same energy. Four of them in an 22-card deck is four draws that do nothing interesting, and `Frail` cut them to 3.
- **Happiest to draw:** **Sango Isshin+**. Twenty damage to ALL enemies for one energy, gated behind a condition my deck satisfies as a matter of course, is a card that makes the previous turn's Plan-writing feel like it paid twice. The turn it and Kurage's Oath+ arrived together (boss round 6) is the best turn I played all act: I killed a 20-HP Follower and took the Priest from 123 to 67 while taking zero damage. Runner-up: **Kurage's Oath+**, which was the answer to `Slippery`, the answer to the Inklets, and the AoE spine of every fight.

**(e) Did the first turn of the first fight already present a decision?**

**Yes, but a thin one.** The hand was Strike, Strike, Defend, Defend, Slack Water against a 9-HP attacker and two status-givers, and the only live question was which body took Slack Water's 6 — a real choice with a real reason (put the Weak and the Hydro aura on a survivor, not on the thing two Strikes were already killing), but one worth about 4 HP over the fight. **The Bake-Kurage was on the field, printed, and glossed at turn 1 — "Nothing is planned. The morning is empty." — but I had no reason to use it**, because both Plan-worthy uses were dominated: writing Slack Water for Weak-to-ALL had nothing to bite (two of three enemies intended `StatusCard`), and I had no second Plan card. **The kit's actual first decision was turn 2**, when Kurage's Oath arrived and the 7-to-ALL Plan bought a free kill for 3 HP. One turn of delay is not much, but the answer to the literal question is that turn 1 presented a targeting decision, not a Kokomi decision.
