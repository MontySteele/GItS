## Fight 1 — Toadpole (1) [A] 25 HP, Toadpole (2) [B] 21 HP

Opened at 62/78, Encore 2, Fanfare 0, Dexterity 1, 3 energy.

**Turn 1.** Played **Salon Début** → Crabaletta joined and hit A for 6 Hydro (Encore 2→1);
then **Charlotte — First-Person Shutter** (5 Block, 4 next turn) which performed Crabaletta
again for 6 (Encore 1→0); then **Guest List** (4 Block, added Sayu — Yoohoo Art to hand).
*Rejected:* Stage Presence for the third energy — Guest List's block is 3 less but it hands
you a Companion card, and a Companion card is the only thing that makes a member act.
*Rejected:* Second Course, because at 2 Encore its own cost plus the deploy performance plus
the extra performance is 3 Encore and I could see it going dry.

The mistake that taught me the most is one I did not see until it was too late.
**Ethereal Spotlight prints "Costs 2 Encore" and I start each fight with exactly 2.**
After Salon Début it read back at me:

> CANNOT BE PLAYED: you have 1 Encore, and this costs 2

So the Spotlight is a *first-action-of-the-fight-or-never* card, and nothing on turn 1's
screen says so. That is a genuine decision — and I got it wrong blind.

**Turn 2** (Encore 0, Fanfare 3, A wearing Hydro and now Thorns 2, B at 21).
Played **Aria of Recompense** — "Gain 5 Encore. If you have at least 3 Fanfare, gain 5 more"
— and at exactly 3 Fanfare it paid **10**. That was the turn's real decision: Fanfare fades
20% a turn, so 3 → 2.4 next turn, i.e. the doubled Aria was available *this turn only*.
*Rejected:* holding Aria and playing Soloist ×2 + Curtain Rises to kill A outright (18 into
a 13 HP body) — a clean kill, but it would have thrown away the only doubled Aria I would
ever get, and 10 Encore also absorbs 10 damage.
Then **Ethereal Spotlight** (Encore 10→8, "Guest Cast" up), **Curtain Rises** on A
(6, A 25→19; Chevalmarin joined and hit **B** for 2 — its own target, not my card's),
**Soloist's Solicitation** on A (A 13→7... 7 after the round's damage; Thorns 2 twice ate my
block).

**Turn 3** (A 7 HP with Thorns, B 21). **Freminet — Pers, Deploy!** on B: printed
"Deal 9 damage" (base 6 lifted to 9 by the Spotlight), Cryo onto B's Hydro → **Frozen**,
B's 3×3 dropped to 1×3. Freminet also performed Crabaletta, which picked A and killed it
at three-quarters ("dry: it could not pay its Encore"). *Rejected:* Freminet on A — it would
have killed A but wasted 8 damage and thrown away the Frozen.
Then **An Invitation** (0) → Lynette — Enigmatic Feint; played **Lynette** (8 Block,
performed Chevalmarin for 1) and **Stage Presence** (7 Block) with the last energy.
There was no real decision left in the back half of that turn: B was frozen down to 3
damage and I had nothing but block cards. I say so plainly — that half-turn was filler.

**Turn 4.** Sayu — Yoohoo Art now printed "Deal **12** damage to a random enemy" (8 lifted
by the Spotlight). B was at 9 and was the only enemy. One card, fight over.
That is not a dead turn: it is the Spotlight I lit on turn 2 paying off two turns later.

Reward: took Matinée Performance over Poised Riposte / Courtroom Drama / Gorou.

## Fight 2 — Corpse Slug (1) [A] 25 HP, Corpse Slug (2) [B] 27 HP, both Ravenous 4

Opened 58/78. Ravenous reads "When an enemy dies, Corpse Slug immediately eats it, becoming
Stunned and gaining 4 Strength" — so the whole fight is shaped by one draft-level question:
*can I kill both bodies in the same beat?* I had bought Explosive Ampoule (10 to ALL) at the
shop, so the answer was yes, and that decided every turn afterwards.

**Turn 1.** **Ethereal Spotlight first this time** (Encore 2→0) — the lesson from fight 1.
*Rejected:* holding the 2 Encore as a shield and playing the Spotlight never; with two
Companion-card generators (Guest List, An Invitation) in the deck the lit multiplier is worth
more than 2 points of absorption over a long fight.
Then **Guest List** (4 Block → Rosaria — Ravaging Confession), **Curtain Rises** on A
(6; Chevalmarin joined and hit **B** for 1, dry), **Rosaria** on B — printed 13 (9 lifted),
Cryo onto the Hydro Chevalmarin had just left on B → **Frozen**, so B's 8 became 4.
*Rejected:* Rosaria on A, which was the body I had already damaged. I switched because
Chevalmarin's random target had put the aura on B, and the reaction was worth more than
focusing. That is the Salon's randomness *creating* a decision rather than removing one.

**Turn 2.** Second Course sat in hand reading
> CANNOT BE PLAYED: you have no Encore, and this costs 1

(Encore before: 0.) **Freminet** on A (9, Cryo on A's Hydro → Frozen, 8 → 3),
**Soloist** on B, **Regal Bearing** (4 Block, 1 Weak) on A.
The choice here was *not* to kill: I deliberately left A at 9 and B at 6, both under 10,
rather than killing one and handing the survivor +4 Strength.

**Turn 3.** **Explosive Ampoule**, 10 to ALL, both die in the same instant, Ravenous never
fires. One action, and the fight was decided at the shop two rooms earlier.

Reward: took Curtain Cue over Undercurrent / Limelight / Eula.

113 HP, ~15 incoming on turn 1 rising to ~31, against my 52. This fight is where the kit
either has an answer or does not.

**Skittish 6** — "The first time Phantasmal Gardener is hit each turn, it gains 6 Block" —
punishes exactly the thing my deck does (many 6-damage cards). But the Salon's own text says
a performance **is not a hit**, and that turned out to be literally true and to be the whole
answer to the fight. It is the one place where the kit's fiddliest rule earned its keep.

**Turn 1.** **Curtain Rises** on B (6; Chevalmarin joined, performed) + **Stage Presence ×2**
(14 Block). *Rejected:* Salon Début instead of Curtain Rises — Curtain Rises does the same

**Turn 2** (Encore 0; Spotlight read back "CANNOT BE PLAYED: you have no Encore").
**Curtain Cue** (0) → 1 Encore. **An Invitation** (0) → Gorou — Inuzaka All-Round Defense.
**Gorou** on D (8 + Block equal to half the damage), **Curtain Rises** on B (a second

Gorou is Geo and D was wearing Hydro; a Crystallize is the only explanation, and no line on

**Turn 5** (24/78 after this turn — the low point). Hand was two Soloists, two Curtain Rises,

## The kit, after 4 fights

**(a) Which decisions felt like real choices, and what they traded off.**

1. **Spend the opening Encore on the Spotlight, or keep it?** *On the turn, and only the
   first turn.* Ethereal Spotlight costs 2 Encore and you start with exactly 2, so it is
   playable as your first action of a fight and — barring Aria or Curtain Cue — never again.
   The trade is a combat-long +50% on every Companion card against two points of absorption
   and one member performance at full rather than three-quarters. It is a sharp, high-stakes,
   completely un-signposted choice, and blind I got it wrong in fight 1 and right in every
   fight after.
2. **When to fire Aria of Recompense.** *On the turn.* "Gain 5 Encore. If you have at least
   3 Fanfare, gain 5 more" against a Fanfare that fades 20% per turn: in fight 1 the doubled
   version existed on exactly one turn. Real, and legible from two numbers on the screen.
3. **Where to aim, given that the Salon will not aim with you.** *On the turn.* Twice a
   member's random target rewrote my plan for the better — fight 2 turn 1, where Chevalmarin
   dropped Hydro on B and I moved Rosaria off A to take the Frozen; and elite turn 6, where
   aiming Gorou at the *healthy* body (because its performance might roll onto the 1 HP one)
   killed two Gardeners with one card.
4. **Kill both Corpse Slugs in the same beat.** *At the shop, two rooms earlier.* Ravenous
   made "don't kill anything yet" the correct play for a whole turn, which is a good, unusual
   shape, and the decision that won it was buying an AoE potion before I knew what I'd meet.
5. **Hits versus performances against Skittish.** *Across the elite.* "A performance is not
   an Attack and not a hit" is a fussy line of rules text that turned out to be the entire
   solution to a four-body elite. That is the kit at its best.

**(b) What felt automatic, and what never seemed worth playing.**

- **Stage Presence** is a button, not a decision. Every time it was in hand it was either the
  obvious last energy or obviously worse than anything else, and it never interacted with
  anything the character does.
- **Soloist's Solicitation** likewise — 6 damage, no rider, no element, no performance. In
  the elite it was actively bad: 6 damage into a 6-block Skittish trigger is zero.
- **Matinée Performance** was the one card I regret drafting. Its "2 damage once per Salon
  Member" rider is the kit's own scaling and it is the exact shape Skittish deletes.
- **Second Course** was unplayable four of the five times I saw it — it needs a live Encore
  pool, and the Encore pool is empty in most mid-fight turns.
- The back half of fight 1 turn 3 and the whole of elite turn 7 were pure filler: a hand of
  block cards, no target worth blocking against, and nothing to decide.

**(c) What I could not understand, or that contradicted its own printed text.**

1. **Dexterity did nothing.** Oddly Smooth Stone gives "1 Dexterity"; the keyword reads
   "Adds its amount to every Block the wearer gains. It does not decay." Every measurement
   said otherwise. Fight 1 turn 1: Charlotte (printed 5) + Guest List (printed 4) → Block
   **9**, not 11. Fight 1 turn 3: Lynette printed 8 Block, Block went 0 → **8**; Stage
   Presence printed 7, Block went 8 → **15**. Not one point of Dexterity ever landed. This
   also poisoned a draft: I refused Gorou — General's War Banner and would have refused a
   Dexterity Potion, on the basis of a relic that seems not to work.
2. **The Evoke never prints.** It fired twice (elite turn 5 and turn 7) and both times
   "What your Salon did this turn" listed the ordinary performances and said nothing about
   the Evoke. I only knew it had happened because Encore jumped by 3 and every enemy
   suddenly wore Hydro Aura 2. For a mechanic with a whole keyword box — "The member performs
   and leaves. Its Fanfare bonus counts 3 times and it prints 5 Fanfare" — being silent in
   its own log is the biggest legibility hole I found.
3. **Elemental reactions are inconsistently announced.** Frozen and Swirl printed named
   previews and named debuffs. But Gorou's Geo into a Hydro aura gave me 5 Block over what
   the card promised (9 instead of 4) with no line anywhere, and Kujou Sara's Electro plus
   Freminet's Cryo left the Sludge Spinner **Vulnerable 1** with nothing on any screen saying
   a reaction had fired. I had to reverse-engineer both from a number that moved.
4. **The Spotlight's cost is invisible until it is too late.** Nothing on turn 1 says
   "this is your only window." The card only explains itself once it is already refused:
   *"CANNOT BE PLAYED: you have 1 Encore, and this costs 2."* The refusal is excellent; it
   just arrives one action after the decision.
5. Minor, and to the kit's credit: the Spotlight **rewrites the printed number on the card
   face** (Sayu 8 → 12, Freminet 6 → 9, Charlotte 5/4 → 7/6). I briefly recorded that as a
   bug — "Freminet printed 9 and dealt 9" — before realising the 9 *was* the lifted number.
   That is the right way to do it and it saved me from arithmetic all round. Sharp 2 is added
   *after* the multiplier (6 ×1.5 + 2 = 11), which the card face also showed correctly.
6. **Turn-1 Companion cards with an empty stage.** Fight 4 turn 1 I played three Companion
   cards and every one of them performed nobody. The rider warns you, but the deck has one
   deploy card per five Companion cards, so this will happen constantly. The Salon is a
   payoff engine in a deck that draws its payoffs before its enablers.

**(d) The card I never wanted to play, and the one I was happiest to draw.**

- **Never wanted: Second Course.** Not because it is weak — when it finally landed it ended
  the elite by itself — but because "Spend 1 Encore" on top of a deploy that also spends
  Encore means it reads *CANNOT BE PLAYED* on most turns it appears. A card that is dead four
  draws out of five and then wins a fight is a card I never wanted in my opening hand.
- **Happiest to draw: Aria of Recompense.** It is the only card that turns the fight back on.
  At 0 Encore the Spotlight, Second Course, and every member's full-strength performance are
  all switched off; Aria unlocks all three at once, and at 3 Fanfare it doubles. Honourable
  mention to **Curtain Cue** at cost 0 for being the small, reliable version of the same job.

**(e) Did the first turn of the first fight already present a decision?**

**Yes — and a hard one.** With 3 energy, 2 Encore, and no member on stage, the turn asks
whether to spend the entire Encore pool on Ethereal Spotlight before anything else, or to
deploy and keep the pool. I chose to deploy, and by my second card the Spotlight was locked
out for the rest of the fight. The decision was real, it was consequential, and the screen
gave me no warning that it was a one-way door — which is the finding, not the loss.

## Non-blindness declaration

  `GITS_LANE=1 python -m understudy.blindplay observe` and
  `GITS_LANE=1 python -m understudy.blindplay act "<command>"`. Lane 1 only; lane 2 never
  touched. No `harness state`, no `scenario`, no `staged_turn`, no `soak`, no other
  understudy subcommand.
- Other Bash calls, all of them scratch or plumbing:
  - `mkdir -p ...scratchpad\lane1` (a scratch directory I ended up not writing to);
  - `mkdir -p C:\Users\Monty\Documents\GitHub\GItS\review\qa\furina-reframe-round-14-2026-09-05`
    for this record;
  - `observe` piped through `sed -n '<ranges>p'`, `grep -v '^    \*'` and `grep -i "gold"`,
    to re-read one block of a screen without reprinting the whole thing. Those pipes only
    ever filtered `observe` output — nothing else was read.
  - One compound call ran `observe | sed ...` followed by `act 'end turn'` in the same shell
    line; no two `act` calls were ever chained.
- Tools used: **Bash** (as above) and **Write** (once, for this file).
- One refused command, elite turn 7: `play "Stage Presence"` →
  *"'Stage Presence' cannot be played right now: you do not have enough energy."* My error.
  No stall, no `TOOL-BLOCKED`, no three-refusal or six-screen stop.
- **Repo files read: none.**
