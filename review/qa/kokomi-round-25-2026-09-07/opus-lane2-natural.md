## Fight 1 — Leaf Slime (S) 12, Twig Slime (M) 26, Twig Slime (S) 9

**Turn 1** (3 energy; hand Defend, Strike ×3, Kurage's Oath). Played Kurage's
Oath *now* (3 to ALL), then Strike into Twig Slime (S) for the kill, then
Strike into Leaf Slime. **Rejected:** writing Oath as a Plan for 7 to ALL next
turn. The Plan is more than twice the damage, but 7 killed nothing on this
board either, and playing it now let the two Strikes finish a body and take a
4-damage attack off the table. The choice was real and it was arithmetic I
could do off the printed faces.

**Turn 2.** Slack Water on the Twig Slime (M) — "Deal 4 damage. Apply 1 Weak" —
then Strike to kill the Leaf Slime, then Defend. **Rejected:** writing Slack
Water's Plan ("Apply 1 Weak to ALL enemies"). Weak had to land *this* turn to
shave the 11 incoming; a Plan lands a turn late. This is the first instance of
a pattern that held all round: **Slack Water's two halves are a genuine
now-or-later fork, and the enemy's intent decides it.**

**Turn 3.** Slack Water, Strike, Strike into the last body — lethal. No
alternative worth rejecting; it was a lethal check.

**Judged across the fight:** two of three turns had a real decision, both of
them the same one (immediate half vs Plan half), settled by the intent line.
The draft did not shape it — this was the starter deck.

**A number I had to work out rather than read:** the Twig Slime took 6 from
Slack Water, not 4. Tamakushi Casket ("whenever you apply a debuff, it deals 2
Hydro damage") fires on the Weak. The relic says so; no card face adds it in.

## Fight 2 — Fuzzy Wurm Crawler 57

**Turn 1.** Slack Water on it (Weak + Hydro aura), then Thundergrust (Electro
into the Hydro aura), then wrote **Flank** as a Plan. **Rejected:** playing
Flank now for 8. The Plan is the same 8 but lands at the start of my next turn,
which effectively banks a card's worth of energy — against a 57 HP single
target with nothing to race, that is free.

The outcome was **16 damage, not the 14 I had counted**: Slack Water 4 + casket
2, Thundergrust 8, and then a *second* casket 2 because Electro-Charged applies
Poison, and Poison is a debuff. The enemy panel afterwards still read **Hydro
Aura 2** even though an Electro hit had just consumed it — exactly the case the
Elemental Reaction box warns about in capitals ("the aura is consumed and
RE-APPLIED inside the same beat… no screen ever shows it gone"). **This is the
one place where the screen and the outcome disagree by construction, and the
rules box is the only thing that saves it.** I believe I only read it correctly
because I had that paragraph in front of me; a player without it would conclude
the reaction did not happen.

**Turn 2.** The Plan carried out (Flank 8 + Poison 4), which turned **Feint**
from "Deal 5" into "Deal 10 damage instead" — the fight's best moment, because
the payoff was set up two turns earlier and I could see the condition satisfied
on the face. Feint + Strike + Strike. **No rejected alternative:** nothing in
hand was Plan-capable, and the enemy was buffing, so it was pure damage. A dead
turn by the letter, but it was the *payoff* turn of the Turn-1 plan, so I count
it as the plan paying off and not as an absent decision.

**Turn 3.** 4 HP left, one Strike. Lethal check.

## Fight 4 — Shrinker Beetle 40, Fuzzy Wurm Crawler 56

**Turn 1.** Wrote Kurage's Oath as a Plan (7 to ALL beats 3 to ALL, and only 4
damage was coming in), Strike into the Beetle, Defend. Real choice, made on the
intent line.

**Turn 2.** The Beetle's **Shrink -1** was on me: "you deal 30% less damage with
every hit." **Here the screen and the outcome disagreed.** My Strike's face was
rewritten to "Deal 4 damage" — the shrunk number, printed. **Feint's face still
read "Deal 5 damage… deal 10 damage instead"** — and Feint then dealt **7**.
The 27→16 arithmetic only works if Feint was shrunk to 7 while printing 10.
Two cards in the same hand, one of them told me the truth and the other did
not.

Played Feint, Strike, and wrote **Feigned Retreat** as a Plan. **Rejected:**
Feigned Retreat now for 4 Block; the Plan is 4 Block *and* 6 damage for the
same energy, and I was only taking 7.

**Turn 3.** The planned Feigned Retreat dealt its **full 6** while Shrink was
still on me. So a carry-out is not shrunk, but a played card is. Nothing on
either face says that; I inferred it from HP.

Killed the Beetle (Thundergrust into the Hydro aura, then Strike), which lifted
Shrink mid-turn, then wrote Flank as a Plan with the freed energy. **Rejected:**
Flank now for the same 8 — banking it was strictly better because nothing else
in hand wanted the next turn's energy.

**Turn 4 — the round's showcase turn.** Hand: Opening Gambit, Slack Water,
Strike ×2, Feigned Retreat. I wrote **two** Plans — Opening Gambit first
("Apply 1 Vulnerable to ALL enemies. The next Plan carried out with this one
deals double damage"), then Feigned Retreat as the doubled one — and spent the
third energy on Slack Water *now*, because its Weak had to land before an
11-damage hit, not after it. The next turn's carry-out took the Crawler from 35
to 14 in one beat: casket 2 on the Vulnerable ×1.5, then 6 doubled to 12 and
×1.5 for Vulnerable = 18. That is the single most satisfying thing the kit did
all round, and every number in it was printed somewhere on the screen.

**Turn 5.** Thundergrust's face had rewritten itself to "Deal 12 damage" (8 ×
1.5 for Vulnerable). Lethal. Here the face *did* fold the multiplier in — the
opposite of what Feint did under Shrink.

## Fight 7 — Elite: Bygone Effigy 127

The elite prints **Slow 0** — "Whenever you play a card, this enemy receives
10% more damage from Attacks this turn. It counts the cards played BEFORE this
one." That is a whole-turn ordering puzzle, and it is legible: play cheap first,
biggest last.

**Turn 1** (it was asleep). Strike (×1.0) → Deep Current (×1.1) → Flank (×1.2),
ascending. 21 damage. **Rejected:** writing Flank as a Plan — a carry-out
happens at the start of the turn with zero cards played, so **the Slow
multiplier is worth nothing to a Plan.** That is a real interaction the kit
creates and neither card says it; I had to derive it from "A Plan is carried out
before you play anything."

**Turn 2** (it buffed). Slack Water (Weak, casket) → Thundergrust
(Electro-Charged) → Undertow, which reads "Deal 7 damage. If the enemy has a
debuff, deal 10 instead" and so wanted to come last, after the Weak and the
Poison. 32 damage. Ordering was the entire decision and it was a good one.

**Turn 3.** 23 incoming. Wrote Opening Gambit then Feigned Retreat as Plans
(the doubling pair) and played Coral Bulwark now for 6 Block. **Rejected:**
writing Coral Bulwark too, for 8 Block one turn later — I wanted Block against
the 23 that was landing *this* turn.

**Turn 4.** The carry-out landed 24 and Vulnerable. Feint (a Plan had carried
out → 10, ×1.5 → 15) then Clorinde. **Clorinde killed it from 35 HP**: 22 base
+ 6 for the aura, ×1.5 Vulnerable, and the Slow multiplier for being the second
card. Elite dead without ever taking its 23 twice.

## Fight 8 — Fogmog 74 + Eye with Teeth 6 (Minion, Illusion)

**Turn 1.** Deep Current now, plus Plans on Flank and Tide Wall. **This is where
a Plan whiffed and I want it recorded plainly.** Flank's Plan is "Deal 8 damage
to each enemy that intends to attack." I wrote it while Fogmog's intent was
**Summon**. At the carry-out, Fogmog's intent line read *Attack for 8* — and the
Plan dealt **zero**. 74 → 68 is Deep Current's 6 and nothing else. So the
condition is evaluated against the intent standing when the Plan was written,
not the intent standing when it resolves, and **no line on the card or in the
Bake-Kurage box says which.** The summoned Eye is a Minion, and the box does say
a single-target Plan never hits a Minion, so there was no target either way.
This is the one Plan I wrote all round that I could not have priced correctly
from the screen.

**Turn 2.** Thundergrust (Electro-Charged) → Undertow (10, because the Poison
was now on) → Opening Gambit written as a Plan. **Rejected:** Flank now for 8;
Vulnerable on a 68 HP body is worth more than 8 once you are dealing 30+ a turn.

**Turn 3.** Slack Water (Weak, ×1.5) → Feint (10 ×1.5 = 15) → Strike (×1.5).
33 damage and the incoming 15 came down to 11. Sequencing again: Weak first so
it applies to the hit, Feint after the Plan check.

**Turn 4.** Fogmog at 5. Strike. The leader died, the Minion abandoned combat —
which the Minion keyword had told me it would.

## Fight 9 — ACT 1 BOSS: Kin Priest 190 + Kin Follower 58 + Kin Follower 59

I came in at 64/80 with **5 energy** on turn 1 (Venerable Tea Set).

**Turn 1.** Undertow into the Priest to put a Hydro aura on it, *then* Clorinde
(22 + 6 for the aura) — 35 off a 190 HP boss. Plus Read the Field written as a
Plan (10 Block) and Defend now. **Rejected:** Clorinde first. The +6 only exists
if something already put an aura on the body, so a Hydro card has to precede it;
that ordering is the card's real text and it is legible.

**The single biggest thing the screen did not tell me:** after Clorinde
resolved, a buff appeared on *me* — **"Night Vigil 6 — Your Attacks against
enemies holding an elemental aura deal 6 additional damage."** Clorinde's face
prints that sentence as though it were a rider on the card's own hit. It is
actually a **combat-long buff on the player**, and every attack I made for the
rest of the fight got +6 against an aura'd body. I only learned that by seeing
the buff line on the next screen. That is a large, fight-defining effect hiding
behind a sentence that reads like flavour on an attack.

**Turn 2.** Feint (16 with the aura bonus) and Flank (14) into the Priest, then
Slack Water written as a Plan ("1 Weak to ALL"). **Rejected:** playing Slack
Water for 12 more damage now. Weak on all three bodies is worth more than 12
across a fight this long, and — this is the part I had to think about —
**writing at least one Plan every turn is what keeps Feint at 10 instead of 5.**
Feint quietly makes "write something" the default, which is a design pressure
worth knowing about.

**Turn 3.** The Priest put **Weak on me**; every attack face in hand rewrote
itself downward (Flank 8 → 6, Strike 6 → 4, Opening Gambit 5 → 3). Incoming was
only 12. So I wrote **every Plan-capable card in hand** — Opening Gambit, then
Feigned Retreat (the doubled one), then Flank — and ended the turn with no Block
and no attack. **Rejected:** Defending. The reasoning was explicit and it is the
best argument for the mechanic in the whole round: *my cards are bad this turn
and cheap next turn, so move my damage forward in time.* The carry-out then
took the Priest 120 → 85 and the followers 56 → 41 and 57 → 54 in one beat.

**Turn 4.** Chevreuse — Ring of Bursting Grenades printed its own preview:
*"Reaction preview: Vaporize — The triggering hit deals 1.5x damage and consumes
the aura. Into that Hydro aura this card's 10 lands 22."* All three bodies were
wearing Hydro and Vulnerable. I played **Strike first** (because Vaporize
consumes the auras and I would lose the +6 afterwards), then Chevreuse: the
followers went 41 → 5 and 54 → 18, the Priest 85 → 30. Best-played turn of the
run, and the preview line is why I could plan it.

**Turn 5.** Feint (Hydro, re-auras) → Thundergrust (Electro-Charged, +6, +5 for
being below half HP) killed the Priest at 30, and both followers abandoned. My
third card was refused — *"you are not in a battle"* — because the fight had
already ended; a clean, informative refusal.

**Boss cleared at 29/80.** Ten turns of decisions, only two of which were
formalities.

## The kit, after 11 fights

**(a) Which decisions felt like real choices, and what they traded off.**

1. **Now-half vs Plan-half, decided on the enemy's intent line** — *made on the
   turn*, and it is the kit's spine. Slack Water's Weak has to land before the
   hit it is shrinking (Fight 1 turn 2, Fight 5 turn 3), but Kurage's Oath's
   3-to-ALL wants to be 7-to-ALL whenever nothing is coming in (Fight 4 turn 1,
   Fight 8 turn 1). Same card, opposite answers, and the intent line is enough
   to tell them apart. This is a good mechanic.
2. **Whether to bank energy** — *made on the turn*. A Plan written today is a
   card's worth of effect that costs no energy tomorrow. On turns where the
   enemy is buffing or asleep, writing is close to free; on turns where I am
   about to eat 20, writing is a loan against my HP. Fight 9 turn 3 (write
   everything, take 12) and Fight 6 turn 3 (write one, block with the rest) are
   the same decision answered opposite ways, and I could argue both.
3. **Ordering within the turn** — *made on the turn*, and it recurs constantly:
   Undertow last (after a debuff exists), Strike before Chevreuse (Vaporize
   eats the aura the +6 needs), a Hydro card before Clorinde, ascending damage
   under Slow. The kit rewards reading the whole hand before the first click
   more than any StS character I have played through this bridge.
4. **The Opening Gambit → Feigned Retreat doubling pair** — *made at the
   draft*, cashed on the turn. Once both were in the deck, every turn with two
   spare energy had a strong line, and the fights where I drew them (4, 7, 9)
   are the fights I won cleanly.
5. **Clorinde vs Hard To Kill** — *made at the draft, unmade by the board*.
   Fight 11's cap turned my best card into my worst, and the counterplay (many
   small hits) was already in the deck. That is the draft paying off in reverse
   and it was the most interesting single screen of the round.

**(b) What felt automatic, and what never seemed worth playing.**

- **Strike and Defend.** Four and four, and they do nothing with the jellyfish.
  Roughly one turn in three in act 1 dealt me a hand with no Plan-capable card
  (Fight 3 turn 2 was 3 Defends and 2 Strikes) and those turns had no decision
  at all. **The starter ten is where the kit's decision density goes to die.**
- **Tide Wall.** "Plan: Gain 3 Block for each Plan carried out with it." I drew
  it three times and wrote it once, for 6 Block. To beat a plain Defend it needs
  two other Plans on the same turn, which means it is only good on the turns
  that are already good.
- **Read the Field's look-at-two rider.** I never once used the filter — I wrote
  it as a Plan for the 10 Block every time. The rider is invisible to the
  decision.
- The **lethal-check turns** (Fight 1 t3, Fight 2 t3, Fight 3 t3, Fight 8 t4)
  were automatic, but each was the payoff of a Plan written one or two turns
  earlier, so I would not call them empty.

**(c) What I could not understand, or that contradicted its own printed text.**

1. **Nereid's Ascension: card says "your first Plan twice", the buff on my panel
   says "every Plan twice".** Direct contradiction, same run, two screens apart.
2. **Feint's face lied under Shrink.** Strike's face rewrote itself from 6 to 4
   under Shrink -1; Feint's kept printing 5/10 and dealt 7. Faces fold in
   Vulnerable, Weak-on-me and Tangled correctly elsewhere, so this reads as a
   miss on one card rather than a rule.
3. **Whether a carry-out is affected by debuffs on me.** Feigned Retreat's Plan
   dealt its full 6 while Shrink was on me. "A Plan carries the numbers you
   wrote" suggests that is intended; "your Strength folds in as you write it"
   suggests the write-time state matters. I still cannot state the rule.
4. **Flank's Plan condition timing** (Fight 8): written while the enemy intended
   to Summon, resolved while it intended to Attack, dealt nothing. Nothing says
   which intent it reads.
5. **Clorinde's second sentence is secretly a combat-long buff on me** (Night
   Vigil 6). Reading the card, I expected the +6 to apply to that hit.
6. **The re-applied aura.** The rules box explains it in capitals, which is the
   only reason I got it right — but it is explaining away a case where the
   screen shows the opposite of what happened.
7. **The casket's 2 damage takes the Vulnerable multiplier** (it landed as 3 per
   body in Fight 6). Sensible, unstated.
8. **The claimed-but-not-gained gold under Ectoplasm.**

**(d) The card I never wanted to play, and the one I was happiest to draw.**

- **Never wanted:** **Defend.** Not because 5 Block is bad, but because drawing
  it is the kit telling me this turn has no jellyfish in it. Of the printed-kit
  cards, **Tide Wall** — I could not find a turn where it beat the Defend it
  was competing with.
- **Happiest to draw:** **Feint.** "Deal 5 damage. If a Plan was carried out
  this turn, deal 10 damage instead" is the card that makes writing a Plan feel
