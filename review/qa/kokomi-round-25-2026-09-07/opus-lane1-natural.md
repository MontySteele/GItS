## Fight 1 — Shrinker Beetle, 40 HP

**Turn 1.** Hand was 4× Defend and 1× Strike. Enemy intent read
*"Strategic (DebuffStrong)"* — a debuff, no damage number. I played Strike and ended
with 2 energy unspent. **Rejected:** playing Defends, because the printed intent
carried no damage and Block "prevents damage" — there was nothing for it to prevent.
**This turn presented no decision.** The hand was five cards, four identical, and the
only live line was "play the one attack". I record that as a null turn.

Before that I spent one refused command learning the interface:
`play "Strike" on "Bake-Kurage"` → *"'Strike' cannot be planned on Bake-Kurage, so
aiming it there would spend the action and do nothing."* Clean, useful refusal.

**Turn 2.** Drew Slack Water — the first card with a Plan line. *"Deal 2 damage.
Apply 1 Weak. Plan: Apply 1 Weak to ALL enemies."* Intent was Attack 7.
I played it **immediately** and added 2 Strikes. **Rejected the Plan half:** the
Weak had to land before the 7-damage hit, and the Plan resolves at the start of my
*next* turn — one turn too late. With one enemy, "ALL" bought nothing.
This was the first real decision of the run and it was legible entirely off the
printed text.

Note the screen was honest about my own debuff: Strike read *"Deal 4 damage"* while
Shrink -1 was on me, not 6. The face folded the modifier in.

**Turn 3.** Enemy 22 HP. Hand had Kurage's Oath (*"Deal 3 damage to ALL. Plan: Deal
7 damage to ALL"*). I **planned** it and played 2 Strikes. **Rejected:** 3 Strikes
for 12 now. Both lines kill on turn 4, but 7 planned versus 3 immediate is a 2.3×
gap on the same card and I did not need the damage this turn. This is the decision
the kit is built on and it read clearly.

**Turn 4.** The plan carried out for 7 (22 → 8 after my Strikes, then 7 → 1... the
carry-out log showed the beetle at 7 after the plan). Slack Water + Strike finished
it. Obvious lethal, but it was the payoff of the turn-3 plan, not a dead turn.

**Verdict:** the fight's decisions were turn 2 (immediate over Plan) and turn 3
(Plan over immediate) — the same card class answered opposite ways two turns apart,
which is a good sign.

## Fight 2 — Fuzzy Wurm Crawler, 55 HP

**Turn 1.** Hand: Tide Chart and 4 vanilla cards. No Plan-capable card, so the
`play ... on "Bake-Kurage"` line was absent from "What you can say" entirely — a nice
touch, the interface tells you when the mechanic is off. Played 3 Strikes.
**No decision.** Tide Chart was dead in hand (0 Plans written ⇒ 0 cards drawn).

**Turn 2.** Enemy was buffing (no damage). I tested
`play "Feint" on "Bake-Kurage"` — **and it was accepted**, even though Feint prints
**no Plan line at all**. Nothing on the card face told me it was Plan-capable, and
nothing told me what its Plan would do. I ended the turn genuinely not knowing what
I had bought.

**Turn 3.** The carry-out log answered it:
`Bake-Kurage: Feint, 12 — the 12 is damage.` Feint reads *"Deal 5 damage. If a Plan
was carried out this turn, deal 10 damage instead"* with Sharp 2 — so planned, it
satisfied **its own condition** and resolved at 10+2. A 1-energy card that turns
into 12 by being planned, with no printed Plan line saying so. **This is the single
biggest legibility gap I hit.** It is also very strong.

Enemy at 19, intent 11, Strength 7. I played Slack Water (Weak, cutting 11→8) and a
Strike, then **planned Kurage's Oath for exact lethal** (12 dealt, 7 left, plan does
7). **Rejected:** Oath immediately for 3 — that leaves the enemy at 4 alive.
The plan killed it at the start of turn 4 before it acted; the fight-over screen
printed the carry-out, which I appreciated ("a Plan whose kill ends a fight never
reaches a battle screen").

**Verdict:** one real decision (plan-for-exact-lethal), one accidental discovery.

## Fight 3 — Nibbit, 42 HP

**Turn 1.** 42 HP hitting for 12; I was at 45/87. Hand: 2 Defends, Feint, Treatise,
Kurage's Oath. I **wrote two Plans** — Oath first, then Feint — and spent the last
energy on a Defend. **Rejected:** Treatise (the engine would have cost me 5 HP I
could not spare at 45), and rejected playing Feint immediately for 7 when planned it
is 12.

**Turn 2.** Carry-out: Oath 7, then Feint 12 — 19 damage in one beat, exactly as
written. 42 → 23. Nibbit then showed *Attack 6 **and also** Defensive (Defend)*.
That changed the whole turn: the Plan rule printed on every combat screen says
*"A Plan is carried out before you play anything, so it lands in whatever Block the
enemy is still standing in from its own turn — you cannot strip that Block first."*
So I **stopped inspecting for Plans** and played 2 Strikes + Coral Bulwark
immediately, hitting before the block existed. **Rejected:** planning Coral Bulwark,
whose 8 Block would arrive after the turn I needed it.

**Turn 3.** Enemy 11 HP behind 5 Block. Slack Water (its Weak set off the Casket,
chewing the last of the block) + 2 Strikes = lethal. Payoff turn.

**Verdict:** the best fight of the run for decisions. The enemy's Block intent
punished planning, and the screen had already told me why.

## Fight 4 (Elite) — Phrog Parasite 64 HP, then 4 Wrigglers (20/19/18/21)

**Turn 1.** Intent was *"give you 3 Status cards"* — no damage. I played Kirara,
planned Kurage's Oath, and Struck. **Reasoning:** my hand was clean now and about to
be polluted, so converting clean cards into next-turn damage hedged against a clogged
hand. **Rejected:** 3 Strikes for 18 now, which banks nothing.

The 3 Status cards never appeared in my hand that turn; the pile counts went from 17
to 20 cards. Nothing on the screen said where they went. They surfaced two turns
later as **Infection** (*"Unplayable. At the end of your turn, if this is in your
Hand, take 3 damage"*).

**Turn 2.** Intent 4×4. Slack Water **immediately** for the Weak (multi-hit intents
are exactly where Weak pays), + Strike + Defend. **Rejected the Plan half:** the
barrage was this turn.

**Turn 3.** Intent was Status cards again — no damage. So block was dead and I
**planned Coral Bulwark** (8 Block + Weak arriving when it would matter), played Tide
Chart, Struck. This is the cleanest "the immediate half is worthless this turn, so
take the Plan half" case in the run.

**Turn 4.** Carry-out printed beautifully:
`Coral Bulwark, 8 — the 8 is Block. Inside the same beat: Tamakushi Casket 2 on Phrog
Parasite.` Two Plans had carried out, so **Feint's condition was live** and it hit
for 12; Slack Water + Kirara finished the block wall. Took 0 damage.

**Turn 5.** The Parasite died to Kirara's delayed 10 and **Infested 4 summoned four
Wrigglers totalling 78 HP** — a second phase bigger than the first. All Stunned. Free
turn: 3 Strikes for an exact 18 kill on the 18-HP body. **Rejected:** spreading
damage, since I had no AoE in hand.

**Turn 6.** Hand was 2 Defends, 2 Infections, Coral Bulwark — **no attacks at all**.
I planned Coral Bulwark (its planned Weak → Casket 2 was the *only* damage I could
reach that turn) and played the Defends. A turn where the kit's Plan line was
literally my only offence.

**Turn 7.** Three bodies, 2 Defends dead in hand. **Planned Kurage's Oath for 21
across three bodies** (vs 9 immediate) + Tide Chart + 2 Strikes into the body the
plan would finish. **Turn 8:** the plan killed Wriggler (2) exactly as designed.
Feint (condition live again) killed Wriggler (1). **Turn 9:** Strike finished the
last.

**Verdict:** long, and the Plan line carried it. Two turns had no decision
(the stunned turn, the no-attack turn) but both were the *consequence* of earlier
choices rather than empty turns.

compound over a long fight; played Treatise, planned Kurage's Oath, played Coral
Bulwark for 7 Block. **Rejected:** 2 Strikes for 12 now — against 81 HP with an

**Tide Wall planned alone gave 4 where playing it gives 5** — its Plan half is a
downgrade at one Plan. Notably **Tide Chart appears in the carry-out list but did not
count** toward "each Plan carried out with it". The card's wording and the carry-out
log disagree about what counts as a Plan.

Then the engine paid: two carry-outs meant **Feint hit for 14** (10 + Sharp 2 +
Strength 2). Feint + Slack Water + Strike = 30 damage in one turn. 62 → 32.

**Turn 4.** Enemy 32, intent 21. I took Feint + Strike + Coral Bulwark (block),
leaving it at 13 rather than going all-in to 4. **Rejected:** 28 damage for 7 more HP
lost — I judged a 13-HP enemy a near-certain kill next turn and it was.
**Turn 5.** 2 Strikes at 10 each. Dead.

**Verdict:** the fight where the kit's engine visibly assembled — Plans feeding
Feint, Treatise feeding draw, Ritual feeding Strikes.

## The kit, after 8 fights

**(a) Which decisions felt like real choices, and what they traded off.**

The kit's central decision — *immediate half or Plan half* — is a real, recurring,
non-obvious choice, and I answered it both ways many times for reasons I could state
from printed text. The things that actually decided it, in order of how often they
came up:

- **On the turn, from the enemy's intent.** A damage intent this turn forces the
  immediate half of any debuff card, because a Plan resolves one turn late (Slack
  Water, fight 1 turn 2; elite 1 turn 2; boss turn 2). A **no-damage intent** (Buff,
  StatusCard, Debuff) makes the immediate half of every block card worthless and
  hands the Plan half the turn for free (elite 1 turn 3; boss turns 5 and 11).
  This is the cleanest, most legible axis in the kit.
- **On the turn, from a printed threshold.** Boss turn 4 — Plow 150 — was the single
  best decision of the run: four Plan-capable cards in hand, zero written, because
  15 immediate damage bought a cancelled 22-damage attack. Trade: all banked value
  for one enemy turn.
- **On the turn, from an enemy Block intent.** The combat screen's own rule — a Plan
  lands in whatever Block the enemy is standing in — made fight 3 turn 2 and fight 6
  turn 4 into "hit now, before the block exists". A rule I read on the screen and
  then used twice is a good rule.
- **Earlier in the fight, from ordering.** Writing Exposed Flank *before* Kurage's
  Oath or Riptide made the damage Plan 43-46% bigger (7→10, 13→19). Nothing on the
  cards says this; the carry-out log taught it. Once learned it is a genuine skill
  and I used it three times.
- **At the draft.** Taking Tide Chart, Tide Wall and Second Wave shaped which turns
  had multi-Plan payoffs at all. Fight 7's whole solution (planned Slack Water
  stripping three Slipperies via the Casket) existed because of a relic + a card, and
  that was decided at Neow and in fight 1's reward.

**(b) What felt automatic, and what never seemed worth playing.**

- **Strike and Defend turns.** Fight 1 turn 1 (four Defends and a Strike against a
  no-damage intent), fight 2 turn 1, fight 6 turn 1. When the hand has no Plan-capable
  card, the bridge correctly drops the Bake-Kurage line from "What you can say" and
  the turn has no shape at all. Roughly a quarter of my turns were like this.
- **Tide Wall never seemed worth playing.** Its Plan half is *worse* than its
  immediate half (4 vs 5) unless two or more real Plans carry out with it, and in
  eight fights that condition was never both true and useful — the turns where I had
  2+ Plans to write were also turns where I wanted damage Plans, not block. It is the
  card I would cut.
- **Tide Chart was near-dead.** It draws 1 per Plan, and in practice I never had more
  than 2 Plans up, so it was a 0-cost draw-1-next-turn — and on the turns I had no
  Plans (which are common) it draws nothing at all. It also does not count toward
  Tide Wall's own scaling clause, which makes the two cards look like a combo that
  is not one.
- **Ringing turns.** Not automatic so much as *amputated* — one card from a six-card
  hand with 3 energy. The decision collapses to "which single card", which was
  Kirara three times out of three.

**(c) What I could not understand, or that contradicted its own printed text.**

1. **Feint has no Plan line and can be planned.** Nothing on the face says so; I
   found it by testing. Worse, planned it satisfies **its own** "if a Plan was
   carried out this turn" condition and resolves at the 10 branch. A 1-energy card
   that reads 5 and delivers 12 when you do the undocumented thing with it.
2. **Coral Bulwark's Plan half printed 8 and delivered 6** under Frail, and I made
   the decision on the 8. The carry-out log flagged it (*"the clause asked for 8"*),
   which is the right disclosure, but the disclosure comes *after* the choice.
3. **Sharp is not folded into the card face, but Shrink, Frail and Dexterity are.**
   Feint printed "Deal 5 damage" all run while carrying Sharp 2; Strike printed
   "Deal 4" under Shrink and "Deal 10" under Strength 4; Coral Bulwark printed 7 with
   Dexterity and 5 with Frail. Two different conventions on the same screen.
4. **Tide Wall's "each Plan carried out with it"** does not count Tide Chart, which
   the carry-out log lists as a carry-out. The log and the card disagree about what a
   Plan is.
5. **Reward screens print relic names with no rules text.** I picked "Mazaleth's
   Gift" believing it was one of the two relics on offer; it was a **potion**. The
   screen gave me no type and no text. The treasure-chest screen *does* print relic
   text, so this is inconsistent between screens.
6. **The shop sold me a potion I could not receive.** I bought **Powdered Demise**
   for 77 gold with 3/3 potion slots full. No warning, no refusal, and the potion was
   never in my belt — the boss screen listed only Dexterity Potion, Liquid Bronze and
   Poison Potion. 77 gold destroyed silently. I would call this a defect, not a
   legibility issue.
7. **Status cards vanish on delivery.** *"give you 3 Status cards"* resolved with
   nothing in my hand and the pile counts silently +3. Where they went was never
   printed.
8. **The Elemental Reaction block is a large rules panel that was never once
   reachable.** Every combat screen printed six paragraphs ending *"NO REACTION IS
   REACHABLE HERE: Hydro is the only element this screen can supply."* It was the
   longest text on the screen for the whole run and it never applied. Two Anemo and
   one Pyro card were offered in drafts and I could not evaluate them at all, because
   the reaction values are only defined "on the first screen that reaches a second
   element" — so a blind drafter cannot price them.
9. **Kirara's damage numbers I could never reconcile.** "Deal 10 damage to a random
   enemy" produced 12, 7 and 15 on different turns. Strength and Vulnerable presumably
   explain it, but the card is a Skill and I could not predict it.

**(d) The card I never wanted to play, and the one I was happiest to draw.**

- **Never wanted: Tide Wall.** Its Plan half is a downgrade at one Plan and I never
  reached the turn shape that fixes it. Runner-up: Tide Chart, for the same reason
  one step removed.
- **Happiest to draw: Feint.** Every time it appeared I first checked the carry-out
  log to see whether a Plan had resolved this turn, and that check *is* the kit — the
  card makes you look at what the jellyfish did before you look at your own hand. It
  hit for 7, 12, 14 and 18 in this run depending entirely on what I had set up.
  **Exposed Flank** is a close second for the same reason in reverse: it makes the
  *order* you write Plans matter.

**(e) Did the first turn of the first fight already present a decision?**

**No.** Hand was four identical Defends and one Strike against a printed
"Strategic (DebuffStrong)" intent with no damage number. Block prevents damage and
there was no damage; there was exactly one card worth playing and 2 energy left over.
The Bake-Kurage was on the field with its whole rules panel printed, and **nothing in
my opening hand could interact with it** — the `on "Bake-Kurage"` line was not even
offered. The kit's mechanic did not appear until turn 2 of fight 1, and the first
genuine immediate-vs-Plan choice was turn 2. I would call the opening hand the
weakest first impression in an otherwise strong kit.

## Debrief

> On the turns where you wrote two or more Plans, did you inspect the hand and choose
> to prepare everything, or did you recognise an off-turn and stop inspecting? Name a
> turn for each answer that applies, with the hand.

**Both happened, and the thing that decided which was almost always the enemy's
printed intent.**

**I inspected and chose to prepare everything** on turns where the enemy's intent
carried no damage, because on those turns the immediate half of every card in hand is

The honest summary: **when I wrote every Plan-capable card, the turn was usually free
rather than chosen** — I was writing them because the enemy was not attacking and the
energy had nowhere better to go. The *chosen* multi-writes were rarer and depended on
having two Plan cards whose carry-outs interact (Exposed Flank into a damage Plan;
Second Wave into anything). Where the kit is at its best is not "write everything" —
it is "write these two, in this order".
