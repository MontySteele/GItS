## Findings, ranked by sharpness

**1. Every block card prints its Unmovable-doubled value; only the first one
played is actually doubled.**

Hand, verbatim, with `Unmovable 1 (buff) — The first time you gain Block from a
card each turn, double the amount gained.` up:

> `**Defend** — cost 1, skill` / `Gain 10 Block.`
> `**Cleansing Wave** — cost 1, skill` / `Gain 10 Block. Remove one of your debuffs. Plan: Gain 10 Block.`

Played in that order, the Block meter read `Block 10` then `Block 15`. The second
card printed 10 and delivered 5. This is the same display machinery both previous
seats praised for baking Weak and upgrades into the face — it bakes in a
conditional it cannot honour, and it does it on *every* copy in hand
simultaneously. On turn 5 I planned a 20-block turn off two printed 10s and got
15, at 17 HP.

**2. A card's printed face silently drops Fantastic Voyage when Weak is also on
you. The two modifiers do not compose.**

With `Weak 1 (debuff)` and `Fantastic Voyage 5 (buff) — Your Attacks deal 5 more
damage this turn.` both on my status line, Slack Water printed:

> `Deal 3 damage. Apply 1 Weak.`

The Slumbering Beetle went `HP 84/86` → `HP 76/86`. That is 6 damage plus the
Casket's 2; 6 is (4 + 5) × 0.75. The face showed base × Weak and dropped the
buff. The previous seat's act-1 record has the opposite case, with no Weak
present: *"Strike read `Deal 11 damage`"*. So the face is honest about Weak
alone, honest about Fantastic Voyage alone, and wrong about both together — and
it is wrong in the direction that makes you not attack. I passed on a Strike that
turn because the screen said 4; it would have been 8.

**3. A Plan is reduced by a Weak you acquire *after* writing and paying for it,
and the card's Plan line never shows a reduced number.**

Written on a turn when I had no debuff, from faces reading
`Plan: Deal 12 damage` and `Plan: Deal 5 damage and apply 1 Weak to ALL enemies`.
The enemy's `Strategic (Debuff)` intent then put `Weak 1` on me at the end of that
turn. The morning printed:

```
- The Bake-Kurage carried these out at the start of this turn, front first:
  - Bake-Kurage: Ambush, 9
  - Bake-Kurage: War Council, 3
  - Bake-Kurage: War Council, 3
```

12 → 9 and 5 → 3, both exactly ×0.75. This **reverses the previous seat's
headline discovery**, quoted from `opus-act2.md`: *"while you are Weak, banking
is strictly better than swinging, because the Plan resolves next morning when the
Weak has expired."* That was true against a boss that Weakened during its turn;
it is false against any enemy that Weakens at the end of yours, which is what a
`Strategic (Debuff)` intent does. The rule is that Plan damage is computed at
resolution against your current debuffs — and since debuffs arrive *between*
writing and resolving, **the number you paid for is never the number you get, and
no screen shows the real one until it has already happened.** Card attack faces
reprint when Weak lands; Plan lines never do.

**4. With The Moon Overlooks the Waters installed, a Plan that fires on play is
logged under a heading that says it happened at the start of the turn.**

Verbatim, from a turn where the morning had been empty and I played War Council
mid-turn:

```
- The Bake-Kurage carried these out at the start of this turn, front first:
  - Bake-Kurage: War Council, 5
- Planned, and carried out at the start of your next turn in this order (1):
  1. **War Council**
- Plans also happen NOW as you write them.
```

Nothing was carried out at the start of that turn. The same card is
simultaneously reported as already-resolved-this-morning and still-queued, and
the log gives no way to tell an on-play firing from a morning firing. The panel's
own last line is the only thing that explains it, and it explains the rule rather
than the entry.

**5. The Bake-Kurage takes at least three Plans, not two.**

> `Plan 3 (buff) — Carries out 3 Plans at the start of your next turn, in order.`

Both previous seats wrote two and recorded `Plan 2` as though it were the pet's
capacity; the act-1 record explicitly calls it "a real budget rather than a
toggle" on the strength of two. There is no printed cap anywhere, and the only
way to learn the number is to keep spending energy until something refuses.

**6. Breakthrough applies a Hydro aura to every enemy, and its face says nothing
about any element.**

> `**Breakthrough** — cost 1, attack` / `Lose 1 HP. Deal 9 damage to ALL enemies.`

No `[Hydro]` tag, no `*Applies Hydro*` keyword box — unlike Slack Water, Feint,
Deep Current and Undertow, which all carry both. After I played it, all three
enemies printed `Hydro Aura 2`. This mattered immediately: the Kujou Sara I
played next triggered an Electro-Charged reaction I had no way to predict from
the screen. An off-class card taken from an event silently carries the
character's element while printing none of the character's keywords.

**7. `Kokomi Burst` is now on its third seat, its third different behaviour, and
still has no rule.**

> `Kokomi Burst: 5/20 — the game's data feed carries this meter's amount and its maximum, and no rule for how it is spent`

It was **absent from rounds 1 and 2**, appeared **mid-turn in round 3**, and then
sat at **exactly 5/20 for seven consecutive rounds** through four enemy deaths,
two Elemental Reactions, eleven Plans and a fight I lost. The act-1 seat saw it
climb to 15/20; the act-2 seat saw it read 25/20, above its own maximum. Three
seats, three readings, no card, relic or keyword box mentioning it. The
contrast the previous seat drew is now stronger, not weaker: `Charge` arrived
with a complete keyword box — what it is, how it grows, that it has no maximum,
how cards spend it — printed on the very card that grants it. Burst has none of
that, and this round it did not even move.

**8. Skipping a card reward does not consume it.**

The screen prints `You may skip this.` I skipped, and the reward list did not
shrink — it stayed at `Add a card to your deck. (1)` / `(2)`. I re-opened the same
entry and **the same three cards came back, unrerolled**. So the skip is
reversible until you `proceed`, and nothing says so. Both previous records treat
a skip as a decision made; it is not one.

**9. An event whose whole content is "add cards to your deck" cannot be
declined, but each of its cards can.**

`Colorful Philosophers` offers three options, all of them "Obtain 3 X cards", and
`skip` is refused: `there is nothing here to skip`. There is no fourth option and
no gold, HP or relic alternative. The three cards then arrive as three ordinary
skippable reward screens — so the mandatory page is the one that looks like a
choice, and the optional pages are the ones that look like consequences.

**10. Two things entered my deck and my relic list this round without any screen
ever printing what they were.**

`This or That?` offered `Add Clumsy to your Deck. Obtain a random Relic.` I took
it; the resulting page printed the single word `Proceed`. **The relic was never

**11. The death screen prints one line and a floor number.**

In full:

> `the run is over; there is nothing left to play`
> `The run ended on floor 24.`

No cause of death, no final HP, no enemy named, no deck, no gold, no relics, no
score, no summary of the run. A player who looks away for one call cannot learn
from this screen what killed them. Separately: **`floor 24` is the only floor
number any screen printed across all three seats' records** — every map screen
speaks only in `N floors ahead`, so this is the first and last time the run's
position was stated as an absolute.

**12. Enemy powers are printed in the player's second person.**

> `Plating 15 (buff) — At the end of your turn, gain 15 Block. Plating is reduced by 1 at the start of your turn.`

That line is on the **Slumbering Beetle**. "Your turn" means the Beetle's turn.
The keyword text is written for the player and reused verbatim on an enemy, so
the sentence says the opposite of what it means. I had to work out from the Block
number moving between rounds which turn it referred to.

**13. `Slumber` counts a slept turn as a turn taken.**

> `Slumber 2 (buff) — Awakens upon taking turns or losing HP 2 times.`
> `Intent: Sleeping (Sleep) — This enemy is doing nothing this turn.`

On a round where the Beetle's intent was `Sleeping` and it lost no HP (my 10
points of AoE were entirely absorbed by its Block 15), `Slumber 2` became
`Slumber 1`. So "doing nothing this turn" is "taking a turn". A player reading
those two lines together would reasonably plan to keep it asleep by not damaging
it, and that plan does not exist.

**14. Undertow's face is not Weak-adjusted while every other attack's is.**

In one hand, with `Weak 1` on me: `Strike` printed `Deal 4 damage` (base 6),
`Rosaria — Ravaging Confession` printed `Deal 6 damage` (base 9), and
`Undertow (proto)` printed `Deal 7 damage. If the enemy has a debuff, deal 10
instead.` — its base numbers, unreduced. I did not play it, so I cannot say what
it would have dealt; I am recording the display inconsistency only, since the two
neighbours on the same screen were both adjusted and the card with the
conditional was not.

**15. The aura *is* consumed — the Casket immediately puts it back, which is why
it never looks consumed.**

The previous seat recorded as a defect that "the aura is not consumed when its own
text says it is", twice, once at a higher number than before. I think I can now
explain it rather than just confirm it. Sara's Electro hit Rock's `Hydro Aura 2`,
the reaction demonstrably happened (`Poison 4` appeared), and the aura still read
`Hydro Aura 2` afterwards. But the reaction applies a debuff, and
`Tamakushi Casket` says *"Whenever you apply a debuff to an enemy, it deals 2
**Hydro** damage to that enemy"* — and a Hydro hit refreshes a Hydro aura to its
full 2 turns. The same thing happened on the Beetle when War Council's Weak fired
the Casket: `Hydro Aura 1` became `Hydro Aura 2`. So the consumed state exists for
less than one screen refresh and no page ever shows it. That is still a defect —
it makes the aura keyword's central sentence unfalsifiable from the screen — but
it is a different one from "the rule does not fire".

## What the previous records got wrong or right, checked against my screens

**Got wrong:**
