(4) THE PULSE FLOOR §5.0(3) WAS DRAFTED AGAINST DOES NOT EXIST AT THIS CELL.
    Pooled: `kurage_pulse` mean 0.254 per sampled turn, max 1 — the
    once-per-turn bound holds — and 79,717 of 106,861 sampled turns (74.6%)
    carry NO pulse.

    Read off the code, not inferred: every use of `C.KURAGE_ALWAYS_ON` in the
    tree is conjoined with `C.KURAGE_MEMORY` (`loader.py:939`,
    `combat.py:1391`, `effects.py:3755`), and the constant's own comment says
    "READ ONLY WHEN KURAGE_MEMORY IS ON" (`constants.py:700`).
    `KURAGE_MEMORY = False` is the shipped quarantine (`:695`). So the
    always-on base kit is OFF at this cell, the jellyfish is the v0.4
    card-summoned one at `KURAGE_DURATION = 1` (`constants.py:520`), and it
    stands for a single turn per play of `bake_kurage`.

    §5.0(3) read `KURAGE_ALWAYS_ON = True` and `KURAGE_MEMORY = False` on
    2026-08-30 and concluded the pulse was "present on essentially every
    sampled player turn and is the floor of the distribution". The
    conditional between the two constants was missed. Recorded as an erratum
    in §9; §5.0 itself is unedited (R101b).
