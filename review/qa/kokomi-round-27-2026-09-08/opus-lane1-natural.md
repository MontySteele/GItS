## Fight 1 — Shrinker Beetle (38 HP)

Opening hand: Defend ×2, Kurage's Oath, Slack Water, Strike. 3 energy.

**Turn 1.** The Beetle's intent was `Strategic (DebuffStrong)` — no damage
coming — so both Defends were dead cards and the turn was purely "how much
damage, and when". I wrote **Kurage's Oath** onto the Bake-Kurage (its Plan line
reads 7 to ALL against 3 to ALL played now), then **Slack Water** and **Strike**
into the body. 12 HP came off (4 + 2 Casket + 6), exactly as the faces read.
*Rejected:* playing Oath now for 3 — writing it bought +4 for the same energy,
and with no incoming damage there was no reason to hold tempo. Also rejected:
either Defend, for the reason above.

**Turn 2.** The Plan carried out for 7 — and this is the turn the kit taught me
its best rule. The Beetle's debuff had landed: `Shrink -1 — you deal 30% less
damage`. My Strikes had **re-printed as "Deal 4 damage"** instead of 6, but the
Plan still paid its full **7**, because "A Plan carries the numbers you wrote; a
debuff on you afterwards does not change it." That is a real, legible, decision-
shaped rule, and I only learned it by having written the Plan a turn early.
I then played 3 Strikes (12) leaving the Beetle at 7 and ate its 7.
*Rejected:* 2 Strikes + a Defend, which takes 2 instead of 7 but leaves the
Beetle at 11 — and with 4 Strikes in a 10-card deck I judged I was more likely
to draw the two hits I needed against 7 than the three I would need against 11.
That was a real arithmetic choice with a real trade (5 HP against a draw
probability), made **on the turn**.

**Turn 3.** Beetle at 7 with a 13-damage swing telegraphed. Strike (4) +
Slack Water (2 + 2 Casket) = 8, lethal exactly. No alternative worth naming:
this is the plan from turn 2 paying off, not a dead turn.

## Fight 2 — Fuzzy Wurm Crawler (55 HP)

**Turn 1.** It intended 4 damage — nearly nothing — so the whole turn was a
setup question. I wrote **Exposed Flank** as a Plan (2 Vulnerable to ALL, landing
*before* I play anything next turn), Struck for 6, and Defended.
*Rejected:* playing Exposed Flank now for 1 Vulnerable and Striking into it for
9. That is 11 damage this turn against 6; but Vulnerable decays at the end of
the enemy's turn, so playing it now spends the multiplier on one Strike, and
writing it spends the multiplier on a whole 3-energy turn. This is the single
most interesting decision the kit offered me all round: **the Plan converts
tempo into a multiplier window.** Made on the turn, off a card taken at the
draft.

**Turn 2.** The Plan carried out and the screen printed the whole beat honestly:
`Exposed Flank, 2 — the 2 is Vulnerable. Inside the same beat: Tamakushi Casket
3` — the relic's 2 damage had itself been scaled by the Vulnerable it had just
applied. With Vulnerable up I emptied the hand: Strike, Strike, Slack Water =
27 into a 46-HP body. *Rejected:* writing Kurage's Oath as a second Plan; it
would have delivered ~10 next turn against 9 now, and the enemy was buffing
itself (it came out of the turn with Strength 7), so racing beat scheduling.

**Turn 3.** 19 HP left, Vulnerable 1 still up, 3 Strikes at 9 each. Lethal. The
decision was made on turn 1; this turn just cashed it.

**Turn 1.** Strike Dummy had just landed and the Strikes re-printed as **9**,
which is the second time this round a face changed under me correctly and
legibly. Three Strikes (27) killed the 21-HP Assassin — the only body
telegraphing damage. *Rejected:* Slack Water + 2 Strikes, which also kills it
(24 ≥ 21) but spends the Weak/Hydro on a corpse.

**Turn 2.** Frail 2 landed and my Defend re-printed as **3 Block**. Riptide+
(12 to ALL, 3 of it eaten by the Crossbow Raider's Block) then Gorou+ for the
kill on the 11-HP remainder. *Rejected:* Chevreuse first — same energy, less
damage into the blocked body.

**Here is the disagreement I could not resolve.** Gorou+ prints
"Gain Block equal to half the damage dealt" and the words panel promises
`Crystallize — Geo on any aura. The aura is consumed and you gain 4 Block.`
Riptide+ had just applied Hydro to the Crossbow Raider. Gorou+ into it should
have been 5 (half of 11) + 4 (Crystallize) = 9 Block, or 6 after Frail's 25%.
The screen showed **Block 4**. In the elite fight two floors later the identical
sequence (Riptide/Oath Hydro, then Gorou+ into it, no Frail) showed **Block 9**
and Crystallize plainly fired. So either the target dying inside the hit
cancels the Crystallize, or Frail is being applied twice, or Crystallize is
Frail-scaled and the halves round down twice. **The card face and the reaction
panel do not say which, and no line on the screen reported the reaction one way
or the other.** That is the one place this round where I could not reconcile the
printed rules with the number I got.

**Turn 3.** Strike + Ambush into a 10-HP body. Obvious lethal, set up two turns

## The kit, after 9 fights

**(a) Which decisions felt like real choices, and what they traded off.**

1. **Write the Plan or play the card now** — every Plan card, every turn. This
   is the kit's actual decision and it is a good one, because the two halves are
   different quantities (Oath 3-now vs 7-written; Ambush 5 vs 12; Exposed Flank+
   2 Vulnerable to one vs 3 to ALL) and the written half lands *before* you act,
   which converts a turn of tempo into a multiplier on a whole later turn. Made
   **on the turn**. Fight 2 turn 1 and boss turn 1 are the two cleanest examples.
2. **Whether the Vulnerable window is worth a turn.** Exposed Flank+ written as
   a Plan is not damage — it is permission to spend the next turn at ×1.5. I took
   that trade at the boss (Riptide+ landed 81 damage in one card because of it)
   and declined it at the elite. Both times it was a live judgement with a real
   cost. **On the turn, off a draft pick.**
3. **Which body to kill, given exact-lethal arithmetic.** Fight 4's Slippery
   stacks and the elite's four Wrigglers both turned into "match my printed
   numbers to their printed HP without waste". Fight 4 in particular inverted my
   card quality — Riptide+ was the worst card in my hand and two 6-damage Strikes
   were the best. That is excellent design.
4. **Boss turn 7: die on purpose.** Trading a one-shot relic for 37 HP and a
   Strike, instead of blocking exactly lethal and stalling. **On the turn.**
5. **At the draft:** Riptide+ over Ambush over Night Watch; Nereid's Ascension
   over three fair cards; Strike Dummy at the shop, which retroactively made four
   starter cards good and changed how every subsequent turn played. Fight 5's
   whole shape was decided at that shop counter.

**(b) What felt automatic, and what never seemed worth playing.**

- **Any turn with one enemy standing and no Plan card in hand** is automatic: hit
  it with the biggest number. Fights 1, 2 and 9 each ended on such a turn, and
  fight 3's last three turns were nothing else. Roughly a third of my turns had
  no rejected alternative I could name honestly.
- **Defend** is a non-decision. It exists to be the card you play when the third
  energy has nowhere better to go, and once Frail re-printed it as 3 Block it was
  barely that.
- **Breakwater never earned its slot.** See the Debrief — three times in hand,
  twice played, and I cannot point to a turn it changed.
- **Toric Toughness** is fine but sleepy: 2 energy for block that arrives on
  turns whose shape I cannot yet see.
- **Sango Isshin's headline clause never fired.** I drew it three times and every
  time the "if the Bake-Kurage carried out a Plan this turn" condition was off,
  so it was an 8-damage 2-cost card. That is a card that needs the turn before it
  to have been spent on its behalf, and nothing on the card tells you that at the
  moment you would have needed to write the Plan.

**(c) What I could not understand, or that contradicted its own printed text.**

1. **Flex Potion's Strength outlived its text by a full turn.** The buff read
   "until the end of this turn"; on the next turn Gorou+ still printed 16 (11+5)
   and Chevreuse's preview still computed off 15. Gone the turn after. Recorded
   in fight 7 turn 3.
2. **Gorou+'s Crystallize did not visibly fire in fight 5 turn 2** (Block 4 where
   the same sequence gave Block 9 in the elite fight). I could not tell from any
   printed line whether the reaction happened, whether Frail scaled it, or
   whether the target dying inside the hit ate it. The Elemental Reaction panel
   even warns that a reaction can be invisible — which means "I saw no reaction
   line" is not evidence either way, and I had no way to settle it.
3. **The act-2 Ancient room ("Tezcatara") printed no options at all** — a
   heading, then the "this room prints no Proceed and this page has no verb that
   leaves one" note, and nothing between them. I typed `choose 1` on a guess and
   it silently worked, handing me **Very Hot Cocoa** (+4 energy every combat, the
   single biggest item of the run). Had I trusted the screen I would have called
   it a stuck room and stopped the round. That is the most serious defect I hit.
4. **Very Hot Cocoa prints "an additional 4Energy"** — missing space.
5. Minor: the reward screen's only signal that the Thieving Hopper had *stolen*
   Chevreuse out of my deck was Chevreuse's text appearing in the words panel of
   a fight it wasn't in.

**(d) The card I never wanted to play, and the one I was happiest to draw.**

- **Never wanted: Breakwater.** One energy for 5 Block at dusk, plus 3 per Plan
  carried out *this* turn — but Plans carry out at the *start* of the turn, so
  the bonus only exists on turns where I had already spent the previous turn
  writing Plans, and on those turns I wanted my energy on the payoff, not on
  block. Twice I played it purely because the card was going to be discarded
