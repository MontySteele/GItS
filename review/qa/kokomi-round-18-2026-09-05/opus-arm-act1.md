This was the sharpest small decision of the fight and it was a *sequencing* one: Slack Water's Weak

Undertow's face from 7 into 10 — "Deal 7 damage, already including 3 if the enemy has a debuff."
Two cards, 2 energy, dead. **Rejected:** Strike + Strike + Undertow, which also kills (19 > 15) but
spends all three energy and teaches me nothing. Judged across the fight: turns 1 and 2 were the
plan, turn 3 was the plan paying off plus one genuine ordering choice.

**Companion cards played: 0** (I owned none yet).
**Companion offered in the reward: Charlotte — Framing: Freezing Point Composition** [Cryo], 1 cost,
"Deal 4 damage. Draw 1 card." **Taken.** I took it over **Exposed Flank** ("Apply 1 Vulnerable.

I took Charlotte because my deck was mono-Hydro and every combat screen was printing six lines of
Elemental Reaction rules ending in "**NO REACTION IS REACHABLE HERE**". A rule I can never trigger
is a rule I cannot report on. This turned out to be the right call for the round and probably for
the run: Frozen mattered in three of the next four fights.

## Fight 2 — Twig Slime (S) [A] 7/7, Twig Slime (M) [B] 27/27, Leaf Slime (S) [C] 15/15

**Turn 1.** Charlotte on **Twig Slime (M)** first, then War Council → Bake-Kurage, then Strike into
the 7 HP slime. Two decisions here, both real:

1. War Council now (Weak to all three, so 3 casket triggers = 6 damage spread and their attacks
   shrink) **versus** War Council planned ("Plan: Deal 5 damage and apply 1 Weak to ALL enemies",
   which is 5 + 2 casket = 7 to each = 21). Incoming was only 4 that turn, so I bought the 21.
2. Which body Charlotte should mark. She applies Cryo; the jellyfish's carry-out is always a Hydro

The reward for reading was immediate and legible: after Charlotte resolved, Slack Water in my hand
grew a new line — *"Reaction preview: Frozen — Hydro meets Cryo: its next action deals half damage,
and the first Attack to hit it Shatters for 6 damage."* That preview is the single best piece of
text in this kit. It turned an inference into a printed fact.

**Where the screen and the outcome disagreed.** I predicted 7 to each body (5 + 2 casket). Leaf
Slime lost exactly 7. Twig Slime (M) lost **9**. The carry-out block lists **three** casket hits,
but the numbers need **four**: the Plan's Hydro hit met Charlotte's Cryo, made Frozen, Frozen is a
debuff, and the casket fired again. The glossary had warned me about precisely this —

consumed and RE-APPLIED inside the same beat, so no screen ever shows it gone" — and sure enough
the enemy afterwards showed **Frozen 1 and Hydro Aura 1** side by side, as if nothing had been
consumed. The *rule* was documented. The *arithmetic* was not: the beat listed three relic hits and
delivered four, and I only found the fourth by subtracting.

**Turn 2.** Twig M at 14 with Frozen, Leaf at 8. Played Well Laid (cost 0) into the frozen body
first, deliberately, because Well Laid is an **attack** and Frozen's second clause is "the first
**Attack** to hit it Shatters for 6 unblockable damage" — 5 + 6 = 11, taking it to 3. Then
Kurage's Oath (3 to ALL) finished Twig M and took Leaf to 5, then Pincer (3 twice) killed Leaf.
Two energy for a three-body board. **Rejected:** leading with Kurage's Oath, which is a *skill* and
would have wasted the Shatter; and Nereid's Ascension, which is a power in a fight that was ending.
The whole turn was set up by turn 1's Charlotte pick — that is the draft-and-earlier-turn decision
paying off, not a free turn.

**Companion cards played: 1** (Charlotte).
**Companion offered: Kujou Sara — Crowfeather Cover** (0 cost, "Your next Attack this turn deals 4
additional damage and applies Electro"). **Declined** — I took **Battle Plan** ("Draw 1 card. Plan:
Gain 1 Energy and draw 2 cards"). Sara is genuinely tempting (Electro on a Hydro deck is a second

## Fight 4 — Shrinker Beetle [A] 40/40, Fuzzy Wurm Crawler [B] 55/55

Entered at 31/80. This is the fight where I nearly died, and it is the most informative one.

| Turn | Plans written | Carry-outs at the start of the next turn |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pincer | `Bake-Kurage: Pincer, 3` → "Shrinker Beetle lost 9 HP" |
| 2 | none | — |
| 3 | Kurage's Oath | `Bake-Kurage: Kurage's Oath, 7 — the 7 is damage.` → "Fuzzy Wurm Crawler lost 7 HP" |
| 4 | none | — |
| 5 | none | fight ended |

**Turn 1.** Deep Current (6 to all), Pincer → Bake-Kurage, Well Laid (2), Defend.
**Rejected:** holding Defend for damage — at 31 HP against a 4-damage opener it looked cheap, and
it was. Predicted 9 from the Plan; printed 9.

**Turn 2.** The Shrinker Beetle's debuff landed: **Shrink −1 — "While Shrinker Beetle is alive,
your Attacks deal 30% less damage."** Every face in my hand rewrote itself — Strike printed
"Deal **4** damage", Charlotte "Deal **2** damage". That is excellent: the debuff is legible on the
cards, not just in a status line. The whole turn was then forced in one direction — kill the beetle
— and the only real choice was the order: Charlotte first (2 + 2 casket = 4, and Frozen), then
Strike (4 + the Shatter 6 = 10). 23 → 9 in two cards. **Rejected:** Defend against 7 incoming;
racing was correct because every turn the beetle lived cost me 30% of everything.

**Turn 3 — the turn that decided the fight.** I was at 24 HP. Printed intents: Shrinker Beetle 13,
Fuzzy Wurm Crawler 11. That is 24. Exactly lethal.

The line was War Council (Weak to all — 2 casket each, beetle 5 → 3, and both attacks shrink 25%),
then Pincer into the beetle to kill it and clear Shrink **before** spending anything else, then
Kurage's Oath → Bake-Kurage (7 to all next morning, at full strength because Shrink was gone),
then the Poison Potion on the Crawler. **Rejected:** Kurage's Oath now for 3 (I would have been
paying Shrink on it), and holding the potion for a boss I could see was six floors away and was not
going to reach at 24 HP.

Two things I learned only by doing it. First, **applying Poison from a potion sets off Tamakushi
Casket** — the Crawler went 47 → 45 the instant the potion resolved. The relic says "whenever you
apply a debuff", and a potion counts; the Vulnerable gloss on other screens says "A potion's does
not", so I had expected potions to be carved out. Second, killing the beetle mid-turn restored my
card faces immediately, so the ordering was worth roughly 30% of the turn.

**Turn 4.** 16 HP, Crawler at 32 with Strength 7 and Poison 5. Slack Water (4 + 2 casket, and Weak
to cut its 11 to 8), then Undertow at the now-debuffed body for **10**, then Flank for 8: 32 → 8.
**Rejected:** planning Riptide for 13 next morning — correct in a healthy fight, suicide in this
one. **Turn 5:** Flank finished it.

**A face that lied by omission:** on turn 4 Undertow printed "Deal 7 damage" in my hand while the
only enemy alive was carrying both Weak and Poison. It dealt 10 when aimed. The card's own note
covers it — "The face shows whichever applies to the enemy you are aiming at" — but on a
single-enemy screen there is nothing to aim at yet and the default reading is simply wrong. I
double-checked my arithmetic three times before playing it.

**Companion cards played: 1** (Charlotte).
**Companion offered: Kujou Sara — Crowfeather Cover** again. **Declined** — took **Ambush** ("Deal 5
damage. Plan: Deal 12 damage"), the best damage-per-energy Plan I had been shown.

spend Flank to finish a 5 HP body I had planned to kill for free.

**Companion cards played: 1** (Charlotte).
**Companion offered: Barbara — Let the Show Begin♪** [Hydro], 1 cost, "Gain 6 Block. Apply Hydro."
**Taken.** At 12/80 with Defends printing 3 under Frail, a flat 6 Block that also lays the aura my

## The five targeted cards

here is how each actually behaved:

- **Pincer** — played the most, in four of five fights, both faces. 6 now / 9 planned for 1 energy

- **Nereid's Ascension** — played once, and it won that fight by itself. But I *held* it in three
  later hands and never played it: it costs 2, does nothing the turn it lands, and I was under 25 HP

  when I kept drawing it.
- **Tide Chart** — played once, drew exactly the 1 card it promised. Held and never played three

  nothing. I never found the turn it was built for.
- **Well Laid** — played three times. At base it prints "Deal 2" and is not worth the action; at 5

  Shatter in fight 5. Its "already including 3 for each Plan carried out this morning" wording is
  the same honest style as Undertow's and I never misread it — the number on the face was always
  right for the morning I was in.

**Never wanted to play, of the five:** Tide Chart. **Happiest to draw, of the five:** Pincer.

## The kit, after 5 fights

**(a) Which decisions felt like real choices, and what they traded off.**

- **At the draft (Neow):** bundle 2 over bundle 1 — three Plan riders traded for AoE plus
  "Apply 1 Weak to ALL enemies", which reads directly off the starting relic. War Council opened two
  of five fights; bundle 1 would have given me a different round.
- **At the draft (fight 1 reward):** Charlotte over Exposed Flank — the strongest raw amplifier in
  the offer traded for the deck's only second element. It paid three times (fights 2, 3, 5) and once
  it was the difference between taking 10 and taking 5 at 17 HP.
- **Earlier in the fight (fight 3, turn 1):** Nereid's Ascension and a Battle Plan written to the
  jellyfish, against 12 unblocked damage, instead of 9 damage now. The whole fight was that bet
  resolving; the kill happened at the start of my turn with no card played, and that is the plan
  paying off, not a dead turn.
- **On the turn (fight 3, turn 2):** bank two Pincers behind an enemy that had *printed* its
  intent to Block, or spend the 5 energy now. The Block was real ("and 5 more absorbed by Block")
  and the doubled Plans went through it anyway.
- **On the turn (fight 4, turn 3), at 24 HP against exactly 24 printed damage:** the *order*
  War Council → Pincer → Kurage's Oath, chosen so the Shrinker Beetle died before I spent my best
  cards, because Shrink was taxing every attack 30% while it lived. Killing a body mid-turn changed
  the value of every card after it. This was the best turn of the round.
- **On the turn (fight 5, turn 3), at 17 HP against 18:** Freeze the Brute for mitigation (its
  intent halved 10 → 5) instead of Defend (3 Block under Frail). Damage prevention through a
  reaction rather than through a Block card.
- **On the turn (fight 1, turn 3):** Slack Water before Undertow, so Undertow's face turned from 7
  into 10 and the casket paid 2 on the way. Small, but it is a decision the deck manufactures every
  time it holds a debuff card and a conditional attack.

The common shape: **the interesting decisions are about ordering and about tempo, not about
targeting.** Which card goes to the jellyfish, and in what order the cards on the board resolve.

**(b) What felt automatic, and what never seemed worth playing.**

- **Fight 1, turn 1 was near-automatic** beyond the one Pincer question: three 1-cost cards, one
  enemy, nothing that traded against anything.
- **Defend never earned its slot.** 5 Block, 3 under Frail, in a deck whose answers to damage are
  Weak, Frozen and killing things faster. I played it twice in five fights and both times it was
  the leftover energy.
- **Strike is filler.** Its only real use by the end was as a cheap **Attack** to spend a Shatter on.
- **Tide Chart with no other Plan written does nothing**, and I never found a turn where writing a
  Plan *plus* Tide Chart beat writing a Plan plus a 1-cost card.
- **Well Laid at base (2 damage)** is not worth an action; it is a good card only on a morning where
  the jellyfish already carried something out.
- **Fight 4, turn 2 was forced, not chosen:** Shrink rewrote every face and there was exactly one
  correct thing to do (kill the beetle). Legible, but not a decision.

**(c) What I could not understand, or that contradicted its own printed text.**

1. **Frozen.** "Its next action deals 50% less damage. The first Attack to hit it Shatters for 6
   unblockable damage and removes Frozen." Those read as two independent riders. In fight 5 the
   Brute took its halved action and Frozen was gone the next turn with no Attack having Shattered
   it; I played a card expecting 8 damage and got 2. Whatever the real rule is — the halved action
   consumes it, or it has a one-turn clock — the face does not say it. **This is the one place in
   the round where I acted on the printed text and the printed text was wrong.**
2. **The carry-out block under-reports the casket.** Fight 2 printed "Inside the same beat:
   Tamakushi Casket 2, Tamakushi Casket 2, Tamakushi Casket 2" — three entries — but the HP losses
   (1 / 9 / 7) need four. The fourth fired off the Frozen the Plan's own Hydro hit created. The
   glossary warns that this case hides itself; the itemised list still ought to itemise it.
3. **Undertow's resting face.** It printed "Deal 7 damage" while the only living enemy carried Weak
   and Poison, and dealt 10 when aimed. The card explains why, but on a one-enemy screen the number
   you read is not the number you get, and I was doing lethal arithmetic off it at 16 HP.
4. **Does a Plan carry-out count as "your Attack"?** The Plan gloss says "your Weak and Strength do
   not [count]" and says nothing about Shrink, and I never had a clean read on whether the
   jellyfish's hits were being taxed 30% in fight 4. That gloss is otherwise the most careful text
   on the screen; the omission stood out.
5. **Two tool refusals, both recoverable, both worth writing down:**
   - `play "Strike" on "A"` was refused with *"'Strike' cannot be planned on Bake-Kurage, so aiming
     it there would spend the action and do nothing. The form that resolves: play \"Strike\""* —
     the letter handle `A` resolved to the **jellyfish**, not to Fuzzy Wurm Crawler [A], even though
     the screen says "Either handle aims a card — `on \"B\"` is the same body as the full name
     beside it." I used full names for the rest of the round and never hit it again.
   - `rest` at the floor-10 rest site was refused with *"Rest site room is not open"* while the
     screen was printing **Rest** as an option and listing `rest` as a thing I could say.
     `choose "Rest"` worked immediately.
   Neither was three-in-a-row and neither stopped the round.

**(d) The card I never wanted to play, and the one I was happiest to draw.**

Never wanted: **Defend**. Five fights, four copies, two plays, and both of those were leftover
energy rather than a decision. A 5-Block card is not part of this kit's answer to damage.

Happiest to draw: **Kurage's Oath** — "Deal 3 damage to ALL enemies. Plan: Deal 7 damage to ALL
enemies" for 1 energy is the best rate I was shown all round, it opened two fights, and it is the
card that most cleanly poses the kit's central question (now, or bigger next morning?). The single
best *draw* of the round was **Nereid's Ascension** in fight 3, but I would not want it in most of
the hands I actually had.

**(e) Did the first turn of the first fight already present a decision?**

**Yes — one, and only one.** Pincer's face read "Deal 3 damage twice. **Plan:** Deal 3 damage three
times", so with 3 energy against a single 55 HP body intending 4 damage I had to price a turn of
delay against +3 damage. That is a genuine question and the screen gave me everything I needed to
answer it. Everything *else* on that turn was automatic: two more 1-cost cards, one target, no
ordering that mattered, and a Defend that was obviously wrong. So: a decision, yes, but a thin one —
