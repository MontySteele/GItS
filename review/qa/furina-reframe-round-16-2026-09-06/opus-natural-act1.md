## Fight 1 — Nibbit [A], 43 HP (floor 1)

**Round 1.** The screen opened with the stage already holding one Crabaletta and an arrival
performance already printed ("Crabaletta hit Nibbit for 6 Hydro"), Encore 2, Fanfare 2.

Played **Salon Début** → **Lynette — Enigmatic Feint** → **Stage Presence**. Rejected:
**Ethereal Spotlight**, which the screen pushed hard — *"You open a fight with 2 Encore and
this costs 2. Any performance spends one, so it is this turn's first action or not this
fight."* I rejected it because my hand held exactly one Companion card (Lynette, 5 Block),
so a 50% boost bought me two points of Block for my whole Encore pool. The trade was 12
damage + 11 Block now against a combat-long buff on cards I did not have. Also rejected
**Shared Billing**, which costs 1 and refunds 1 and, with the Spotlight unlit and only one
enemy already wearing Hydro, does *literally nothing* — a free blank.

Outcome: 12 damage, 11 Block against a 12 intent. Took 1.

**Round 2.** Played **An Invitation** (free, dig) → drew **Lisa — Violet Arc**. Then
**Chevreuse — Interdiction Fire** on Nibbit for 15 (7 → Vaporize into the Hydro aura) plus a
6 performance. Rejected: playing **Lisa** first. That was the turn's real decision — both
cards want the same Hydro aura and only one can have it. Lisa's Electro-Charged is a DoT and
a draw; Chevreuse's Vaporize is 1.5× immediately. I took the burst, then played Lisa into
the *bare* body so her Electro applied fresh, then **Soloist's Solicitation** to leave Nibbit
on 1 HP so the 4-point tick killed it at the start of its turn, before it attacked.

Rejected on the last energy: **Aria of Recompense** (10 Encore). Correct to reject — the
fight was over.

**Judgement:** two turns, two real decisions, one of them (Vaporize vs Electro-Charged into
a single shared aura) genuinely good. The lethal was set up a turn earlier by holding
Soloist's for the poison tick, so it counts as a plan paying off.

Screen-vs-outcome: after Chevreuse consumed the Hydro aura, the enemy's aura read **Hydro 2**
again on the next observe — Crabaletta's Hydro performance re-applied it inside the same
beat. The Elemental Reaction glossary warns about exactly this, and it is the only reason I
could read the board.

## Fight 2 — Fuzzy Wurm Crawler [A], 56 HP (floor 2)

**Round 1.** Stage: one Crabaletta, arrival performance printed (6 Hydro). Encore 2.

Played **Surintendante Chevalmarin** (Encore 2→5, the member joins and performs 2) → then
**Ethereal Spotlight**, which the same warning text said was impossible after a performance.
**It was not impossible.** Chevalmarin's +3 Encore paid for it. This is the fight where the
kit clicked. Then **Chevreuse — Interdiction Fire**, whose face had rewritten itself from
"Deal 7" to "Deal 10", for **15** into the Hydro aura, plus a 6 performance. Then
**Salon Début** to put a third body up.

Rejected: doing this in the order the card told me (Spotlight first, then everything dry at
three-quarters). Rejecting the card's own advice was the best play of the fight.

**Round 2.** Enemy was buffing (no damage incoming), so Block was worthless. Played
**Shared Billing** (net-free energy), **Aria of Recompense** → **10 Encore**, then
**Soloist's Solicitation** ×2 for 12. Rejected: **Lynette** (8 Block and a 2-damage
Chevalmarin performance) — 12 damage beat 8 Block into an Empower intent. I noted at the
time that Lynette would also have rotated the 2-damage Chevalmarin off the front, and took
the damage anyway; that cost me on the next turn's front-member choice, which is a real
trade the screen let me see.

**Round 3.** Enemy 9 HP, Strength 7, swinging 11. Played **Second Course** into a **FULL**
stage. That is Evoke #1 (below). The fight ended on it.

**Judgement:** three turns, and the decisive choice was made on the turn (order of
Chevalmarin → Spotlight → Chevreuse), not at the draft. Best fight of the round.

## Fight 3 — Leaf Slime (S) [A] 14 · Twig Slime (M) [B] 27 · Twig Slime (S) [C] 11 (floor 3)

**Round 2.** Played **Aria** (10 Encore) → **Change the Bill** → **Chevreuse** on A.

**Judgement:** the best fight of the round for decisions, and every one of them was on the

## The kit, after 6 fights

**(a) Real choices, and what they traded.**

- **At the draft, Neow:** bundle 1 vs bundle 2 — a body for an empty stage versus performers
  with nobody to perform. The Companion glossary made this legible before I had seen a card,
  which is good writing.
- **On the turn, every fight's first turn:** spend the opening 2 Encore on Ethereal
  Spotlight (a combat-long 50% on Companion cards) or on two full-price performances. I
  answered it differently in fights 1, 4 and 5 and I think all three were right for their
  board. **This is the kit's best recurring decision** and it is entirely on the turn.
- **On the turn:** the order Chevalmarin → Spotlight → Companion card. Chevalmarin's +3
  Encore makes the "impossible" line possible. Working that out was the most satisfying
  minute of the round.
- **On the turn, rotation:** Change the Bill rotates *then* performs, so playing it before or
  after a Companion card changes which member does the work (6 vs 2). I made this call three
  times and it swung a turn each time.
- **Earlier in the fight:** deploy onto a **full** stage to convert the front member into an
  Evoke, instead of adding a body. Twice, both lethal.
- **Earlier in the fight, elite round 3:** bank 10 Encore as a damage sponge instead of
  pushing 8 more damage. Encore absorbing a whole enemy turn for zero HP is the most
  distinctive thing the kit does.
- **On the turn, fight 1 round 2:** Vaporize or Electro-Charge — two cards competing for one
  aura, only one can have it.

**(b) Automatic, and never worth playing.**

- **The arrival performance.** Every one of the six fights opened identically: one Crabaletta
  on stage, one free 6 Hydro already dealt, Fanfare 2, Encore 2. It is not a decision and it
  never varied.
- **Soloist's Solicitation.** "Deal 6." With one enemy up there is no target choice and no
  interaction with Encore, Fanfare, the Spotlight or the Salon. It was in most hands and I
  never thought about it.
- **Stage Presence.** Gain 6 Block. Same.
- **Shared Billing** is close to a blank: it costs 1 and refunds 1, and unless the Spotlight
  is lit and a Companion card follows it *this turn*, its whole effect is refreshing a Hydro
  aura my own members refresh anyway. I played it three times purely because it was free.
- **Regal Bearing.** Three block and one Weak for a full energy. It was in my hand in four
  separate fights and **I never once played it.**

**(c) What I could not understand, or what contradicted itself.**

1. **Ethereal Spotlight lectures you with a false rule.** *"You open a fight with 2 Encore and
   this costs 2. Any performance spends one, so it is this turn's first action or not this
   fight."* Two things are wrong. The arrival performance fires **before** my first action and
   **does not spend Encore** (Encore read 2 on every round-1 screen, after a performance had
   already printed); and any Encore-granting card reopens the window — I lit it after a
   performance in fights 2, 3 and 6. A card that tells you a constraint the game does not
   enforce is worse than a card that says nothing.
2. **Chevreuse's reaction preview never updates.** With the Spotlight lit the title read
   "Deal 10 damage" (and later "Deal 13 damage") while the preview underneath still read
   *"Into that Hydro aura this card's 7 lands 10"* / *"this card's 9 lands 13"*. The hits
   actually landed 15, 19 and ~29. The preview is quoting the printed base through every
   multiplier that matters.
3. **Electro-Charged prints on the body as "Poison N."** The card, the reaction preview and
   the glossary all say Electro-Charged; the debuff on the enemy says Poison, with the right
   numbers. I had to infer they were the same thing.
4. **"Hexerei"** is printed on Razor — Claw and Thunder and is defined nowhere on any screen I
   saw.
5. **Surintendante Chevalmarin's printed Encore went 3 → 6** between two fights with no
   on-screen cause. Fanfare was 9 at the time, but the Fanfare clause is explicitly about
   *member* numbers, not card faces.
6. **Leading Role's discount displays on every spotlighted card in hand at once.** In elite
   round 3, Chevreuse+ *and* Lynette both showed cost 0. Only one can get it. The tooltip is
   honest ("this turn's board"), but the hand is not.
7. Minor: Byrdonis's Strength 1 did not appear on its 4×3 multi-hit (12 landed, not 15).

**(d) The card I never wanted, and the one I was happiest to draw.**

Never wanted: **Regal Bearing** — four fights in hand, zero plays. Happiest to draw:
**Aria of Recompense**. It is the difference between a turn where every performance prints
*"(dry: it could not pay its Encore)"* and a turn where nothing gets through to my HP at all.
Honourable mention to **Change the Bill**, which is the only card that lets me aim the engine
at all.

**(e) Did the first turn of the first fight present a decision?**

Yes, but a thin one, and it answered itself the wrong way. The screen posed exactly one
question — all 2 Encore into Ethereal Spotlight, or into performances — and pinned a warning
to the bottom of the board insisting it was now-or-never. I could not take it seriously,
because my hand held one Companion card whose entire spotlightable content was 5 Block, and
because a performance had *already happened* without spending the Encore the warning was
about. What was left was ordinary: three cards, 12 damage and 11 Block against a 12 intent.
The kit's real first-turn decision did not appear until fight 2, when Chevalmarin was in the
opening hand.

## Extra logging asks

**(1) Every Evoke.** I caused exactly **two**, and **the bridge printed nothing about either
one.**

- *Evoke 1* — fight 2, round 3. **Encore held before: 10.** Played **Second Course** into a
  stage the card itself flagged as *"Your stage is FULL: the next deploy Evokes the front
  member first."* Front was Chevalmarin, whose Evoke text is "applying Hydro to ALL enemies
  and granting 3 Encore". The next screen was the reward screen. No Evoke line, no Fanfare
  line, no "5 Fanfare" print — nothing.
- *Evoke 2* — fight 4, round 4. **Encore held before: 7.** Played **Surintendante
  Chevalmarin** into a full stage on purpose, to Evoke the front **Crabaletta** (printed
  Evoke: 14 Hydro). Fogmog was on 6. The next screen was the reward screen. Again nothing
  printed.

So: two Evokes, both correct as far as the corpse count goes, and **zero Evoke text ever
reached me**. Both times the Evoke was lethal and the fight-end swallowed the print. I never
once saw the "Its Fanfare bonus counts 3 times and it prints 5 Fanfare" clause happen on a
screen. Deploy cards my deck held: **three** — Salon Début, Second Course, and Surintendante
Chevalmarin (its "Add 1 … to your Salon" behaves as a Deploy: it performed on joining every
time, and it Evoked a full stage).

**(2) Enemy powers that say "when hit" / "the first time it is hit".** **None appeared.**
Across six fights the enemy powers I saw were Hydro Aura, Territorial (end of its turn, +1
Strength), Strength, Illusion (revives on death), Minion, Poison/Electro-Charged and
Vulnerable. Not one of them was a when-hit trigger, so I could not test the Salon's promise
that *"A performance is not an Attack and not a hit: Vulnerable moves it, but no Shatter and
no when-hit power fires."* The half of that clause I **could** test held: **Vulnerable does
move a performance** — with Vulnerable 3 on Byrdonis, Crabaletta's 7 landed **10**.

**(3) Turns I wore Weak.** **None, in six fights.** No enemy applied Weak to me. Regal
Bearing (which applies Weak to *them*) sat unplayed in my hand in four fights, and Courtroom
Drama's Weak landed on a body that died in the same beat, so I never saw it printed either.
Flyconid's Debuff intent arrived on exactly the turn my budget ran out.

**(4) First turn of every fight — what the stage held, and whether the arrival performance
printed.** It printed every single time, identically:

| Fight | Stage at round 1 | Arrival performance | Encore after it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Nibbit | 1 × Crabaletta | printed — 6 Hydro on Nibbit | 2 |
| 2 Fuzzy Wurm Crawler | 1 × Crabaletta | printed — 6 Hydro | 2 |
| 3 Slimes | 1 × Crabaletta | printed — 6 Hydro on Twig Slime (M) | 2 |
| 4 Fogmog | 1 × Crabaletta | printed — 6 Hydro | 2 |
| 5 Byrdonis (elite) | 1 × Crabaletta | printed — 6 Hydro | 2 |
| 6 Jaxfruit + Flyconid | 1 × Crabaletta | printed — 6 Hydro on Jaxfruit | 2 |

Six for six: one Crabaletta, Fanfare 2, Encore 2, and a free 6 damage already on the board
before I could act. **The arrival performance never charged Encore**, which is the fact that
falsifies the Ethereal Spotlight warning quoted in (c1) — and, separately, it means the
opening of every fight is byte-identical no matter what my deck looks like.
