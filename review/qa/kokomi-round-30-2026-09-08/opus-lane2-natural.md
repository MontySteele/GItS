## Fight 1 — Fuzzy Wurm Crawler [A], HP 57/57

**Turn 1** (hand: Defend ×3, Strike, Kurage's Oath; enemy intent Attack 4).
Played Kurage's Oath **on the Bake-Kurage** (Plan: 7 to ALL next turn), then
Strike, then Defend. *Rejected:* playing Kurage's Oath now for its printed
"Deal 3 damage to ALL enemies" — the Plan line prints 7 for the same 1 energy
and the enemy's intent was only 4, so there was nothing to rush. That is a real
first-turn decision and it is made entirely off two numbers printed on the same
card face. The third energy went to Defend rather than nothing, which is the
weakest part of the turn: with a 4-damage intent, 5 Block was near-irrelevant.

**Turn 2** (Strike ×3, Defend, Slack Water; enemy 44 HP, intent Empower).
Played Slack Water on A, then two Strikes. *Rejected:* writing Slack Water's
**Dusk Plan** ("Apply 1 Weak to ALL enemies") on the jellyfish. Dusk resolves at
this turn's end, before enemies act, so the Weak would have landed at the same
moment either way — but with a single enemy the played side additionally deals
its 4 damage, so the Plan side was strictly worse. The decision was real and it
was decided by one clause: *Dusk: the Bake-Kurage carries this Plan out at the
end of this turn, before enemies act.* Damage came out 44 → 26, i.e. 18 = 4
(Slack Water) + 2 (Casket, off the Weak) + 6 + 6. The Casket's 2 was **not**
printed anywhere on the played-card path; I reconstructed it from the HP drop.

**Turn 3** (Strike ×4, Kurage's Oath; enemy 26 HP, Strength 7, intent Attack 11).
Three Strikes, 18 damage, enemy to 8. *Rejected:* Kurage's Oath as a Plan again —
7 next turn is less than the 6 a Strike does now, and the enemy had just gained
Strength 7, so the fight wanted to end. This is the Plan trade reading the other
way, which is the first sign the choice is actually two-sided.

**Turn 4** (Defend ×4, Slack Water; enemy 8 HP, intent Attack 11).
Slack Water (4 + 2 Casket = 6, enemy to 2) then two Defends. *Rejected:* three
Defends. The Weak from Slack Water plus 10 Block was exactly enough to eat the
attack — HP stayed 64 — so the "kill slower but take nothing" line was the right
one and it was a genuine arithmetic decision, not an automatic one.

**Turn 5:** one Strike killed it. No alternative worth naming — that is the
turn-4 plan paying off, not a dead turn.

## Fight 2 — Shrinker Beetle [A], HP 40/40

**Turn 1** (Strike, Defend ×4; intent DebuffStrong).
Played Strike, ended. **This turn presented no decision at all:** four Defends
against a debuff intent are worth nothing, and there was exactly one attack in
hand. Worth saying plainly: the hand, not the kit, was the problem — no Plan
card was in hand, and the screen's `What you can say` block correctly dropped the
`play "<card>" on "Bake-Kurage"` line, which is a nice piece of honesty.

**Turn 2.** The Beetle applied **Shrink -1** ("While Shrinker Beetle is alive,
you deal 30% less damage"). This is where the kit's best printed idea showed up:
Strike's face changed from "Deal 6 damage" to **"Deal 4 damage"**, Kurage's Oath's
front half changed from "Deal 3 damage to ALL" to **"Deal 2 damage to ALL"** —
and its **"Plan: Deal 7 damage to ALL enemies" did not move**. The Plan rule
text says exactly why: *"Every planned HIT is the jellyfish's, so Shrink or Weak
on you never cuts it."* So I wrote Kurage's Oath as a Plan, played Slack Water on
A, and Struck. *Rejected:* Oath played now, which Shrink had cut to 2. The
decision was made on the turn, but it was legible only because the sheet
re-prints the shrunken numbers and leaves the Plan number alone. That is the
single best legibility moment in the whole run.

**Turn 3** (Heizou, Strike ×3, Defend; enemy 19 HP wearing Hydro Aura 1, intent
Attack 13). Heizou's face now carried an extra line it had never printed before:
*"Reaction preview: Swirl — Anemo meets an aura: the aura is consumed and copied
onto ALL enemies."* So the screen told me the reaction was live before I spent
the card. I played it. **What reacted this turn** then listed
"**Swirl** on **Shrinker Beetle**, off Shikanoin Heizou — Heartstopper Strike."

Here the screen and the outcome disagreed. Heizou's face printed **"Deal 6
damage"** while, in the same hand and under the same Shrink, Strike printed the
cut number 4. The actual HP loss was **19 → 15, i.e. 4** — so Shrink *did* cut
Heizou, and Heizou's face was the only card in the hand not showing its cut
number. Two further things I had to reconstruct: the "+4 additional damage for
each Swirl this turn" did **not** apply to the hit that caused the Swirl (4 dealt,
not 8), and with a single enemy the Swirl "consumed and copied onto ALL enemies"
resolved to *re-applying Hydro to the same body at duration 2* — the aura line
went from "Hydro Aura 1" to "Hydro Aura 2". Nothing said that would happen; I
read it off the aura counter.

Then I used **Flex Potion** (5 Strength) and Struck twice. The card face
immediately re-printed as **"Deal 7 damage"** — that is (6 + 5) × 0.7 = 7.7
floored — which told me, before I committed, that Strength lands *before* Shrink
multiplies, and that two Strikes would be 14 against 15 HP. It came out at
exactly 1 HP left. Rejected: Strike + Defend, which would have left it at 8 and
cost me a second 13-damage hit; taking one hit at 13 beat taking 8 and then 13.
The screen paid for this decision precisely — the printed 7 was the number.

**Turn 4:** one Strike for the kill. Turn-3's plan paying off.

## Fight 4 (Elite) — Bygone Effigy [A], HP 127/127 — the fight that killed the run

The Effigy carries **Slow 0**: *"Whenever you play a card, this enemy receives
10% more damage from Attacks this turn. It counts the cards played BEFORE this
one."* That single line turned every turn of this fight into an ordering puzzle,
and it is the best decision generator I met all run — it is also not part of the
kit, it is the enemy.

**Turn 1** (intent Sleeping — nothing incoming). Wrote Kurage's Oath as a Plan
first (so it would count as a played card for Slow), then Strike, Strike. Damage
came out 127 → 114 = 13 = 6 + 7, i.e. floor(6 × 1.1) + floor(6 × 1.2): the
counter's arithmetic is reconstructable but the per-card figure is never printed —
Strike's face still reads "Deal 6 damage" no matter what Slow says. *Rejected:*
playing Oath for its 3-to-ALL, and rejected leading with a Strike (which would
have wasted the multiplier on the cheapest card). Both rejections were on the
turn; both were arithmetic I did by hand.

**Turn 2** (Effigy 107, intent Empower — nothing incoming; hand almost all
block). Played Slack Water for 4 + 2 Casket, then wrote **Coral Bulwark** and
**Tide Wall** as Plans. This is the multi-Plan turn. The screen then printed:

> Planned, and carried out at the start of your next turn in this order (2):
> 1. **Coral Bulwark**
> 2. **Tide Wall**

*Rejected:* playing both block cards now (6 + 4 = 10 Block, which would have been
wasted against an Empower turn) — writing them instead paid **8 + 6 = 14** into
the turn where the attack actually lands. This is the kit's central trade and it
was legible: the queue is printed, in order, with a count.

**Turn 3.** The carry-out block printed:

> - Bake-Kurage: Coral Bulwark, 8 — the 8 is Block. Inside the same beat:
>   Tamakushi Casket 2 on Bygone Effigy.
>     - Bygone Effigy lost 2 HP
> - Bake-Kurage: Tide Wall, 6 — the 6 is Block.
>     - no enemy lost HP

So **Tide Wall paid 6 = 3 × 2 Plans**, counting itself and Coral Bulwark, and the
Casket's answering 2 was attributed *by name, inside the beat*. That is the one
place in the run where a number I would otherwise have had to reconstruct was
handed to me. Block showed 14 at the top of the turn; the intent read 17 (the
Weak from Coral Bulwark's Plan already folded in); I took 3.
Then: wrote Kurage's Oath as a Plan again, Strike, Strike (13). *Rejected:*
Defending — I had 14 Block already, so the free turn went to damage.

**Turn 4** (Effigy 99 → intent **Attack 23**, Strength 10, my Block 0).
Used **Ship in a Bottle** (10 now, 10 next turn), then Coral Bulwark played (6),
Defend (5), Strike last (7, riding Slow 30). 21 Block against 23. *Rejected:*
writing the block cards as Plans — and this is the sharpest thing I learned about
the kit: **planned Block arrives at the start of your next turn, so it protects
the turn after the one you write it in.** Writing block while an attack is
already announced is a turn of free damage taken. The jellyfish panel says the
carry-out timing plainly, but it never says "and therefore your Plan-block is
always one turn late"; I had to work that out from the round-2/round-3 pair.

**Turn 5** (Block 10 from the potion's second half; Effigy 72, intent 23).
Coral Bulwark (6), Defend (5), Slack Water last (4 + 2 Casket, and **1 Weak**).
21 Block versus a Weakened attack → **took 0**. *Rejected:* a third block card.
The Weak-plus-block combination was the best turn I played and it is exactly the
kit's own loop: a debuff card that is also 2 free Casket damage and a 25% cut.

**Turn 6** (Effigy 66, my HP 52). Spent **Block Potion** (12) and dumped Strike,
Heizou (Swirl fired again — printed by name in *What reacted this turn*), Strike:
66 → 47, i.e. 19 = 6 + 6 + 7 under Slow. Took 11. *Rejected:* two Defends and one
Strike; I judged the race, not the turn — the Effigy's Strength-10 attack was
going to outlast my block, so the correct line was to shorten the fight. In
hindsight this was the losing decision, and I want it on the record as mine.

**Turn 7** (all-block hand; Effigy 47, my HP 41). Wrote Kurage's Oath as a Plan
(7 for next turn), played Coral Bulwark (6) and Defend (5), took 12. *Rejected:*
Coral Bulwark + Defend + Defend for 16 Block and zero damage — that line takes 7
instead of 12 but adds a whole extra enemy turn to a fight I was already losing
on tempo.

**Turn 8** (Effigy 40, my HP 29, intent 23). Slack Water first (Weak, 6), then
two Strikes: 19 damage, 40 → 21. Took 17 (Weakened) → 12 HP. *Rejected:* the
half-block line (Slack Water + Defend + Strike), which leaves me at 17 HP against
a 27-HP enemy — strictly a slower loss.

**Turn 9 — the death.** Effigy at 21, me at 12, intent 23, and 3 energy against a
hand of Defend, Heizou, Strike ×3. Every line loses and the screen let me prove
it before I moved: max damage is three Strikes at Slow = 6 + 6 + 7 = **19**, two
short of 21; max block is two Defends = **10**, three short of 23. I spent
**Clarity Extract** to draw one more card hoping for Slack Water (whose Weak
would have cut 23 to 17 against 11 Block and left me alive at 6) — I drew a
Defend. So I played three Strikes, left the Effigy on **2 HP** exactly as
computed, ended the turn, and died. The next `observe` returned
`TOOL-BLOCKED: game_over`.

I want to be exact about what this says and does not say: the kit did not lie to
me on that turn. I could compute both losing numbers off printed faces two
minutes before dying. What killed the run was that a 13-card deck with 4 Strikes
of 6 has no ceiling, and the elite's 127 HP plus Strength 10 outruns it — that is
a draft/scaling verdict, not a legibility one.

## The kit, after 4 fights

**(a) Which decisions felt like real choices, and what they traded off.**

1. *Play the card now, or write it as a Plan* — on the turn, every turn a Plan
   card was in hand. Kurage's Oath is the clean case: **3 to ALL now versus 7 to
   ALL next turn** for the same 1 energy. It is a genuine tempo-versus-rate trade
   and it reads both ways: I planned it in fights 1, 2 and the elite, and refused
   to plan it in fight 1 turn 3 and fight 3 turn 3 because the fight was ending.
2. *Plan under a debuff* — on the turn, fight 2. When Shrink -1 landed, the played
   halves of my cards visibly shrank on their faces and the Plan halves did not.
   Choosing the jellyfish there is a decision the screen hands you.
3. *Block now versus block later* — on the turn, the elite. Planned Block arrives
   at the start of your **next** turn, so writing block while an attack is already
   announced eats a full hit. Getting this wrong once cost me ~12 HP.
4. *Stacking Plans for Tide Wall* — earlier in the fight (elite turn 2, paid turn
   3). Tide Wall pays 3 per Plan carried out with it, so its value is set by a
   decision made a turn before it resolves. It paid 6 with one companion Plan;
   the interesting version (three or four Plans) I never got to build.
5. *Card ordering under the Effigy's Slow* — on the turn. Not the kit's, the
   enemy's, but it was the densest decision of the run and it interacted with the
   kit well: writing a Plan is also a card played, so it feeds the multiplier for
   the attacks that follow it.
6. *At the draft* — taking Heizou was the only decision that changed what the kit
   could do at all: it is the sole non-Hydro card I ever held, and Swirl was the
   only reaction I saw fire. Every Hydro card meeting a Hydro aura merely
   refreshes it, and the screen says so in as many words:
   *"NO REACTION IS REACHABLE HERE: Hydro is the only element this screen can
   supply."*

**(b) What felt automatic, and what never seemed worth playing.**

Defend was automatic in the worst sense: 5 Block with no rider, and in fight 2's
first turn I held four of them against a debuff intent and had literally nothing
to decide. Strike was automatic but honestly so. Playing a Plan card *now* when
its front half is a strictly-worse copy of its Plan half (Kurage's Oath at 3 vs 7)
was almost never right outside a lethal turn, which makes about half of that card's
face decoration. Slack Water's **Dusk Plan** never once beat playing Slack Water:
Dusk resolves before enemies act anyway, so the Plan side only ever cost me the 4
damage and the Casket trigger — I never found the board (multiple enemies, no
target worth hitting) where it would win. Tide Wall played from hand for 4 Block
is a card I would cut; only its Plan side is interesting.

**(c) What I could not understand, or that contradicted its own printed text.**

- **Heizou's face does not obey Shrink.** Under Shrink -1 every other card in the
  hand re-printed its reduced number (Strike 6 → 4, Kurage's Oath 3 → 2) while
  Heizou kept printing "Deal 6 damage" and then dealt **4**. Same screen, two
  conventions. This is the one flat screen-versus-outcome disagreement I found.
- **"Deals 4 additional damage for each Swirl this turn"** — the Swirl caused by
  Heizou's own hit does not pay Heizou. Nothing on the card says the count is
  taken before the hit. With one Heizou in the deck, that clause never once
  produced a number, so I still do not know what it is worth.
- **Swirl on a lone enemy.** "The enemy's aura is consumed and copied onto ALL
  enemies" resolved, against a single body, as *the aura coming back on the same
  body at a longer duration* (1 → 2). Consumed and re-applied to itself is a
  strange reading of "consumed", and I only worked it out by watching a counter.
- **The Casket's 2 damage is invisible on the played-card path.** On a Plan
  carry-out the screen names it ("Inside the same beat: Tamakushi Casket 2 on
  Bygone Effigy"). When I play the debuff card from my hand, nothing is printed —
  I inferred the 2 from HP arithmetic every single time.
- **"Slow 0" versus "Slow 30".** The badge shows 0 at the start of my turn and
  30 after three cards, but no card face ever shows the boosted number, so the
  multiplier is arithmetic I do entirely by hand on every attack.
- Minor: the Neow "Large Capsule" row printed its own disclaimer that the feed
  carried no face for the card it promises. I avoided it partly for that reason.

**(d) The card I never wanted to play, and the one I was happiest to draw.**

Never wanted: **Defend**. Four of them in a thirteen-card deck, no rider, and in
this kit they compete with Coral Bulwark (6 Block, or 8 + Weak + 2 Casket damage
planned) and lose badly. Happiest to draw: **Coral Bulwark**, and by a distance —
it is the only card I held that was strong played *and* strong written, so drawing
it never cost me a decision, it gave me one.

**(e) Did the first turn of the first fight already present a decision?**

Yes, and a clean one. Fight 1 turn 1 I held Kurage's Oath against a 4-damage
intent, and both halves of the trade were printed on the one face: "Deal 3 damage
to ALL enemies" against "Plan: Deal 7 damage to ALL enemies", same 1 energy. I
planned it because a 4-damage intent means nothing needs to die today. The rest of
that turn (Strike, then a near-pointless Defend) was filler, so the honest version
is: the first turn presented **one** decision, made on the kit's own mechanic,
surrounded by two cards that decided nothing.

## Debrief

*Answering the coordinator's question as put.*

**Numbers I had to reconstruct from an outcome because no line stated them:**

1. **The Tamakushi Casket's 2 Hydro damage, on every card I played from hand.**
   Screen: the battle screen, after `play "Slack Water" on "A"`. There is no
   line — no damage log, no "Casket 2" note, nothing in *What reacted this turn*
   (which stayed "Nothing reacted this turn"). What I did with it: subtracted.
   Fight 1 turn 2, 44 → 26 across Slack Water + 2 Strikes told me Slack Water's
   beat was 6, not 4. I carried that reconstructed 6 as a planning number for the
   rest of the run. Note the asymmetry: on the **Plan** path the same 2 *is*
   printed — "Inside the same beat: Tamakushi Casket 2 on Bygone Effigy" — so the
   information exists and only the played-card path withholds it.
2. **Every Slow-multiplied attack number.** Screen: the elite battle screen.
   Strike's face reads "Deal 6 damage" whether Slow is 0 or 30; the Slow badge
   reads "(Receives 30% more damage)" but no card face moves. What I did: computed
   floor(6 × 1.1) = 6 and floor(6 × 1.2) = 7 by hand, confirmed it against
   127 → 114 on turn 1, and then used that rounding rule to plan the fatal turn 9
   (three Strikes = 19 against 21 HP). The rounding rule itself — floor, not round
   — is nowhere on any screen; I derived it from a 13.
3. **Heizou's actual damage under Shrink.** Screen: fight 2 turn 3 hand. The line
   was there but wrong for that card ("Deal 6 damage" while Strike printed 4).
   What I did: read 19 → 15 and concluded Shrink applies to Heizou too and its
   face is the odd one out. I then stopped trusting Heizou's printed number.
4. **What Swirl did to a single enemy.** Screen: fight 2 turn 3. *What reacted
   this turn* named the Swirl but printed no effect. What I did: read the aura
   counter going 1 → 2 and concluded the aura had been re-applied to the same
   body, longer.
5. **Whether the "+4 per Swirl" clause paid.** No line anywhere states the Swirl
   count or the resulting bonus. What I did: 6 base, 4 dealt under Shrink →
   nothing was added → concluded the causing hit does not count itself.
6. **Where Strength sits relative to Shrink.** Screen: fight 2, after Flex Potion.
   Here the screen actually *did* the work — Strike re-printed as "Deal 7 damage",
   which is floor((6+5) × 0.7) — so I did not have to reconstruct it, and I could
   see before committing that two Strikes made 14 against 15 HP. This is the model
   the other numbers should follow.

**Lines that stated something I would otherwise have had to reconstruct:**

7. **Which body a Plan would land on.** Stated, on the jellyfish panel, every
   turn: *"A Plan with one target hits the front enemy and never a Minion — unless
   every enemy is a Minion, when it takes the front one anyway. A Plan whose card
   says ALL hits every living enemy, Minions included."* I never had to test it —
   though I also never had a multi-body fight, so the rule went unexercised. It
   removed a whole class of doubt from writing Plans blind.
8. **When a Dusk Plan resolved.** Stated: *"Dusk: the Bake-Kurage carries this
   Plan out at the end of this turn, before enemies act."* What I did with it: it
   is exactly why I never wrote Slack Water as a Dusk Plan — knowing the Weak
   lands before the enemy acts either way, the played side wins on the extra 4
   damage. A stated line that closed a decision rather than opening one.
9. **When an ordinary Plan resolved, and in what order.** Stated twice over: the
   forward view — *"Planned, and carried out at the start of your next turn in
   this order (2): 1. Coral Bulwark 2. Tide Wall"* — and the backward view, the
   carry-out block naming each Plan, its figure, and what the figure *is*
   ("Coral Bulwark, 8 — the 8 is Block", "Kurage's Oath, 7 — the 7 is damage").
   The "the N is Block / the N is damage" gloss is the single most useful clause
   on the screen: without it the bare number on a Plan line is ambiguous.
10. **Which reactions happened in a beat.** Stated by name in *What reacted this
    turn*: "**Swirl** on **Bygone Effigy**, off Shikanoin Heizou — Heartstopper
    Strike", and the negative case is stated too ("Nothing reacted this turn"),
    which is what let me be sure the Casket's Hydro was not producing reactions.
    Better still, the *forward* version: Heizou's face grew a **"Reaction preview:
    Swirl"** line only on the turns an aura was actually standing, so I knew
