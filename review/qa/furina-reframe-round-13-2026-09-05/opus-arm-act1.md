## Fight 1 — Sludge Spinner [A] 37 HP

Stage empty at the start; **1 turn with no member on stage** (turn 1).
Companion cards played: **2** (Chevreuse — Interdiction Fire, Dahlia —
Sacramental Shower).

**Turn 1.** Played Chevreuse — Interdiction Fire (7, applies Pyro) → Soloist's
Solicitation (6) → Regal Bearing+ (5 Block, 1 Weak). Enemy 37 → 24; its
telegraphed 8 fell to 6 under Weak and 5 Block ate all but 1.
*Rejected:* Rolling Tide — "Deal 2 damage to ALL enemies twice" is 4 into a
single body, and its second line, "The front Salon member performs," was dead
because Chevreuse's own text told me plainly: *"No member on stage: performs
nobody. Deploy a member first, or this card is only its own line."* That
sentence is the best-written thing on the screen. Also rejected Ethereal
Spotlight: it costs 2 Encore, which was my whole pool, and I had not yet learned
where Encore comes from.
*Rolling Tide line: held. Not played, no stage, worst damage rate in hand.*

**Turn 2.** Aria of Recompense (+5 Encore, 1 → 6) → Salon Début (deploy
Crabaletta; she performed at once) → Soloist's Solicitation+ (6). Enemy 24 → 12.
*Rejected:* Stage Presence / Guest List. The screen had just taught me that
Encore "after Block it absorbs damage before HP" — I had watched Encore go 2 → 1
absorbing the 1 damage past my Block. With 6 Encore banked, a 6-Block card was
redundant armour, so I spent the energy on damage instead. That is a real
trade the kit put in front of me and I could read it off the screen.
*Rolling Tide: not in hand.*

**The Vaporize that did not happen.** The salon log read: *"Crabaletta hit
Sludge Spinner for 6 Hydro, and left no aura on it."* The enemy was wearing
Pyro Aura 2, and Crabaletta was at full strength (Encore paid). Vaporize is
printed as *"this hit deals 1.5x damage and consumes the aura"* — so 9. The HP
bar moved 24 → 18. **Six.** The aura was consumed; the multiplier was not
applied. See (c).

Also on that same screen, while Salon Début (a Hydro member) sat in my hand, the
Elemental Reaction block asserted: *"NO REACTION IS REACHABLE HERE: Pyro is the
only element this screen can supply."* It was wrong in the most direct way
available — the Hydro that broke the claim was a card in the hand printed
underneath it.

**Turn 3.** An Invitation (free, fetched Dahlia — Sacramental Shower) → Dahlia
(6 damage + performs Crabaletta) → Curtain Rises for the kill.
*Rejected:* Salon Début and Lynette, which also perform Crabaletta. This was the
turn's actual decision and it was a good one: **three cards in hand all
performed the same member, and the choice was which rider I wanted stapled to
the perform** — 6 card damage (Dahlia), 5 Block and a Swirl (Lynette), or a
second body on stage (Salon Début). I took the damage because it was lethal-adjacent.
Second Course printed `CANNOT BE PLAYED: you have no Encore, and this costs 3`.
*Rolling Tide: not in hand.*

**Verdict on the fight.** Crabaletta's dry perform read
*"4 Hydro … (dry: it could not pay its Encore, so it acted at three-quarters)"* —
6 × 0.75 = 4.5 → 4. That parenthetical is excellent; it told me exactly why the

TAKEN.** A 0-cost Companion is a free perform every time it is drawn; I passed

## Fight 2 — Corpse Slug (1) [A] 25 HP, Corpse Slug (2) [B] 27 HP

**Turn 1.** Ethereal Spotlight (0 energy, 2 Encore) → An Invitation (fetched
Sayu) → Chevreuse (now printing **10**, up from 7) on A → Sayu (6 Block now,
6 more next turn) → Guest List (3 Block, fetched Freminet — Pers, Deploy!).
*Rejected:* keeping the 2 Encore. The trade was legible and I made it
deliberately — 2 Encore (2 points of absorb, or two performs at full instead of
three-quarters) against +50% on every Companion card for the rest of the fight,
in a deck where I had already counted six Companion cards. I would make it again.
*Rolling Tide: not in hand.*

**Turn 2.** Salon Début (deploy Crabaletta, performs) → Kujou Sara (0 cost;
performs Crabaletta a second time, and arms +4 on my next Attack) → Soloist's
Solicitation on **B** → Stage Presence.
**The real decision of this fight was made here and it was made off printed
text.** A was at 5 HP and B at 17. Killing A was the tempo play — B eats it and
is Stunned, skipping a turn. I killed *B-ward* instead, because Ravenous also
grants +4 Strength, and B's move was `3 damage 2 times`: +4 Strength on a
two-hit move is +8 a turn for the rest of the fight, while the stun buys one
turn once. So I deliberately kept the nearly-dead body alive and beat on the
healthy one, so that when the eating happened it would be the 5-HP slug that
got the buff. That is a genuine, non-obvious choice, and every input to it was
printed.
*Rolling Tide: not in hand.*

**Turn 3.** Soloist's Solicitation+ (9) on B → Curtain Rises (6, deploys
Chevalmarin) on B → **Rolling Tide** for the finish. Both slugs died in the same
beat.
*Rolling Tide line: **played**, and it was the right card: two bodies, "2 damage
to ALL enemies twice" reaches both, and by then Chevalmarin was on stage so its
performer clause was live.*
*Rejected:* Aria of Recompense (+10 Encore at Fanfare 3) — the biggest engine
number I had seen, declined because the arithmetic said the fight ended this
turn and Encore does not carry between fights.

**Card reward — Companion offered: Kaeya — Frostgnaw, printed
"Deal 8 damage. Gain 3 Block." TAKEN.**

## The kit, after 5 fights

### (a) What felt like a real choice

1. **Reaction ordering, on the turn.** Fight 4 turn 2: Chevreuse to lay Pyro,
   *then* Kaeya to Melt it, 1.75×. Same two cards, +9 damage for getting the
   order right. This is the best decision in the kit — hidden, repeatable, and
   discoverable from the "Reaction preview" line. Fight 5 turn 3 was the same
   idea with Vaporize and a kill on the end of it.
2. **Whether to burn the starting Encore on Ethereal Spotlight, at the top of
   the fight.** Encore is armour *and* it is the fuel that keeps members off
   three-quarters *and* it is the cost of the Spotlight. Three uses, one pool,
   and the screen says so in one line. I paid it in fights 2, 3, 4 and 5 and
   lived on three-quarters performs all act as a result. That is a real,
   uncomfortable trade and I never felt sure I was right.
3. **Which rider to buy with a perform, on the turn.** Once a member is on
   stage, three or four cards all perform it, and the pick is the rider:
   damage (Dahlia), Block + Swirl (Lynette), draw (Bogglecat), +4 and an element
   (Kujou Sara). Fight 1 turn 3 and fight 4 turn 3 were both decided here.
4. **Kill order against Ravenous, in the fight.** Fight 2: deliberately keeping
   the 5-HP slug alive so the +4 Strength would land on the body I could kill
   next turn. Entirely legible off the enemy's own buff text.
5. **At the draft.** Taking Kujou Sara over Lasting Impression made every later
   turn have a free perform in it; taking Florid Cadenza over Lynette —
   Bogglecat Box was the one draft pick I am still unsure about. Taking Duet
   turned two later turns into 24-damage turns.

### (b) What felt automatic, and what was never worth playing

- **Stage Presence** and **Guest List** are filler. Once Encore is up, Block
  cards are redundant with a pool that already absorbs after Block, and I played
  them mostly because I had a spare energy.
- **Rolling Tide is the weakest of the four targeted adds by a distance.** Of
  the six hands that held it I played it three times and every one of those was
  a specific board state (two-plus bodies whose Block or Skittish had already
  been spent). Against one enemy it is 4 damage. Against Plating 8 it is 0.
  Against four Skittish bodies it is *worse than zero* — it hands the board 24
  Block. It is a card that is either a trap or a mop-up, never a plan.
- **Second Course was never once playable.** It costs 3 Encore, and Encore was
  at 0 or 2 every single time the card appeared. It printed
  `CANNOT BE PLAYED: you have no Encore, and this costs 3` in fights 1, 3, 4 and
  5. I never learned what it does beyond its text. Of the four targeted adds it
  is the one that contributed literally nothing.
- **Chevalmarin is not worth deploying.** She performs for 2 (3 at Fanfare 10+),
  which is 1 when dry. Crabaletta performs for 6. Curtain Rises is a fine card
  because of its 6 printed damage, not because of the member it fields, and I
  ended the elite with two Chevalmarins clogging a three-slot stage.
- **I never once saw an Evoke.** The stage holds 3, I had at most 3 members, and
  no card in my deck deliberately overfilled it. Evoke is defined on four
  separate screens — "counts 3 times and it prints 5 Fanfare", Crabaletta's
  "Evokes for 14 Hydro damage" — and it is, from where I sat, entirely
  theoretical.
- **Fanfare is nearly inert.** The screen says so itself, every single turn:
  *"Fanfare: 7 — cards read it and none spends it."* Across five fights it did
  two things: Aria's +5 bonus, and +1 on a member's number at 10. I hit 17 and
  felt nothing.

### (c) What I could not understand, or that contradicted its own text

1. **Two cards in this run share a name with a completely different card.**
   - *Kaeya — Frostgnaw*: the card-reward copy read **"Deal 8 damage. Gain 3
     Block."** The copy An Invitation fetched into my hand under the Spotlight
     read **"Deal 9 damage"** with **no Block clause at all**, while the deck
     copy on the same Spotlight read **"Deal 12 damage. Gain 4 Block"**
     (= 8 × 1.5 and 3 × 1.5). So the fetched "Kaeya — Frostgnaw" is a 6-damage,
     no-Block card wearing the same title.
   - *Dahlia — Sacramental Shower*: the copy in play is a cost-1 **attack**,
     "Deal 6 damage", applies Hydro. The copy offered as a fight-4 card reward
     is a cost-1 **skill**, "The next time an enemy attacks you, deal 9 Hydro
     damage to it first."
   These are not upgrades and are not marked `(upgraded)`. From the seat they
   are simply two different cards with one name, and the tool's own
   disambiguation rules — which promise that same-named things are separated by
   `(1)`/`(2)` or `(upgraded)` — cannot help, because the collision is across
   screens.
2. **A Vaporize that consumed the aura and did not multiply.** Fight 1, turn 2:
   Crabaletta, at full strength, into a Pyro aura. The log said
   *"hit for 6 Hydro, and left no aura on it"* — aura consumed — and the HP bar
   moved exactly 6. Vaporize prints 1.5×, so 9. I checked the numbers three
   ways and could not make 6 into a multiplied hit. In fight 2 the *same* card
   into the *same* aura while dry hit for 6 off a 4.5 base, which *is* 1.5×. I
   cannot reconcile those two.
3. **"NO REACTION IS REACHABLE HERE: Pyro is the only element this screen can
   supply"** — printed on a screen where Salon Début, a Hydro member, was in my
   hand, and which I broke two plays later.
4. **Kujou Sara's Electro silently eats the aura you are setting up.** Fight 5
   turn 5: I armed Sara (+4 damage, "applies Electro"), then fired a Duplicated
   Chevreuse at a Hydro-aura body expecting Vaporize twice. I got 24 raw instead
   of 31, and the body came out wearing Pyro. The only story that fits is that
   Sara's Electro consumed the Hydro first, for an Electro-Charged that no screen
   ever mentioned. The Elemental Reaction keyword text *warns* that reactions can
   be hidden — but it warns about a relic, and knowing the warning exists did not
   let me predict this. **Two riders on one play, each with its own element, and
   no printed rule for which one lands.**
5. **Skittish and performances.** Member performances appear not to trigger
   *"the first time Phantasmal Gardener is hit each turn, it gains 6 Block."*
   Chevalmarin hit for 2 and the HP moved 2. Nothing says this. The Salon
   keyword does say *"A performance is not an Attack: Vulnerable moves it,
   Shatter and on-Attack triggers do not"* — which, on a careful reading, is the
   answer. But it takes a careful reading, and the enemy buff says "hit", not
   "attacked".
6. **Sayu — Muji-Muji Daruma printed "Gain 6 Block" under a live Spotlight and
   delivered 6.** Probably a 4-Block base scaled to 6; I could not tell from the
   seat, and I flag it only because the Kaeya case above makes fetched cards
   suspect.

### (d) The card I never wanted, and the one I was happiest to draw

- **Never wanted: Second Course.** Not because it is weak — because I never once
  got to find out. Four appearances, four `CANNOT BE PLAYED`. Runner-up:
  **Rolling Tide**, which I *could* always play and usually should not.
- **Happiest to draw: Kaeya — Frostgnaw.** 12 damage and 4 Block on one 1-cost
  card under the Spotlight, it performs a member for free, it applies Cryo, and
  it is half of the Melt combo that produced the run's biggest turn. Honourable
  mention to **Kujou Sara — Crowfeather Cover**: a 0-cost card that performs a
  member is the closest the kit gets to free money, and twice it turned a 6 into
  exact lethal.

### (e) Did the first turn of the first fight present a decision?

**Yes, but a thin one, and not the one the kit wants.** The hand held four
1-cost cards and 3 energy, so the choice was "which three of these four", which
is a real choice but a generic one — it would be the same choice in any deck.
The Furina-specific decisions were all shut off: the stage was empty, so
Chevreuse's performer clause was dead (and said so), Rolling Tide's performer
clause was dead, and Ethereal Spotlight wanted 2 Encore I did not yet understand.
**The kit's first genuinely Furina turn was fight 1 turn 2**, when Aria and
Salon Début arrived together and I had to decide whether to build the pool or
spend it. Across five fights, **every single fight opened with an empty stage
and at least one turn where the Companion cards in hand were "only their own
line."** That is the most consistent structural thing I observed, and it means
the kit's opening turn is reliably its dullest.
