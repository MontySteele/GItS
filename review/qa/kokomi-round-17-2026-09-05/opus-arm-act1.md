## Fight 1 — Fuzzy Wurm Crawler, 56 HP

**Turn 1** (3 energy; it intends 4 damage). Played **Battle Plan** onto the
Bake-Kurage, then **Kurage's Oath** onto the Bake-Kurage, then **Strike**.
*Rejected:* playing both Plan cards for their now-lines (Battle Plan "Draw 1",
Oath "Deal 3 to ALL") for 9 damage this turn instead of 6. Against a 4-damage
enemy the whole fight was slack, so the investment line is free; the specific
thing I wanted to learn was whether the jellyfish holds more than one Plan, and
it does — the buff line read **"Plan 2 — Carries out 2 Plans at the start of
your next turn, in order"** and the Bake-Kurage block listed them numbered 1 and
2. That is a real decision at the draft-of-the-turn level and the screen
answered it without my having to guess.

**Turn 2** (4 energy — Battle Plan's Plan paid +1). The morning printed
`Battle Plan, 1 — the 1 is Energy` and `Kurage's Oath, 7 — the 7 is damage`,
and the enemy had lost exactly 7. Played **Nereid's Ascension** (2), **Tide
Chart** (0), **Slack Water** onto the Bake-Kurage (1), **Strike** (1).
*Rejected:* Strike ×2 + Oath for ~15 damage now. I took the power instead
because the fight had no clock on it and "the Bake-Kurage carries out every Plan
twice" is the kind of line you only get to pay for once. This is the fight's
real decision and it was made here, not on a later turn.

**Turn 3** (3 energy). Enemy now Strength 7, intent 8. Played **The Moon
Overlooks the Waters** (2) — *"Plans also happen now, as you write them"* —
then **Slack Water** onto the jellyfish, then **Well Laid** (0).
*Rejected:* two Defends for 10 Block against an 8-damage hit. I had 60 HP and
wanted to see the two powers interact, and what I saw is the fight's sharpest
finding — see (c) below: the now-copy resolved **once**, not twice.

**Turn 4** (3 energy). **Battle Plan** onto the jellyfish — with Moon out it
refunded its own cost immediately (+1 Energy, draw 2) and still stood as a
written Plan — then **Strike**, **Strike**, **Strike**, **Tide Chart**.
*Rejected:* holding Battle Plan to plan a damage card instead. With Moon up, a
0-net-cost card that draws 2 now and pays +2 Energy / 4 cards next morning is
strictly the better line; there was nothing to weigh.

**Turn 5.** Morning paid +2 Energy. **Well Laid** killed it from 4.

**Judged across the fight:** two decisions, both real, both made early — plan-
stack vs. tempo on turn 1, and buy the engine vs. race on turn 2. Turns 4 and 5
were the plan paying off, not dead turns. Companion cards played: **0**.

## Fight 2 — Shrinker Beetle, 38 HP

**Turn 1.** **Nereid's Ascension** (2), **Strike** (1).
*Rejected:* Strike + Defend + hold the power for a bigger fight. Wrong call in
hindsight but a defensible one — see turn 2, where the beetle's debuff made the
power the only good thing I had done.

**Turn 2.** The beetle applied `Shrink -1 — While Shrinker Beetle is alive,
your Attacks deal 30% less damage`, and **every card in my hand repriced itself
on the screen**: Strike printed "Deal 4 damage", Kurage's Oath "Deal 2 damage to
ALL" — but Oath's **Plan line still printed 7**. That is the fight's decision
and the screen handed it to me: route damage through the jellyfish, because a
Plan is the jellyfish's hit and my debuff does not ride on it. Played **Kujou
Sara — Crowfeather Cover** (0) then **Strike** — 7 HP lost, so the +4 was added
before Shrink took its 30% — putting `Electro Aura 2` on a body that had been
wearing Hydro all game. Then **Battle Plan** onto the jellyfish and a second
**Strike**. *Rejected:* Defend. At 44 HP against a 7-damage hit I wanted the
Electro down while the aura window was open.

**Turn 3.** Every Hydro card in hand now printed a line I had not seen before:
*"Reaction preview: Electro-Charged — Hydro meets Electro: the reacted enemy
loses 4 HP at the start of its turn, 1 less each turn."* Played **The Moon
Overlooks the Waters** (2), then **Kurage's Oath** onto the jellyfish (1). The
carry-out printed `Kurage's Oath, 7 — the 7 is damage. Inside the same beat:
Tamakushi Casket 2` and the beetle lost 9 — and came out the far side wearing
`Poison 4` **and a fresh Hydro Aura 2**. This is precisely the case the screen's
own Elemental Reaction paragraph warns about ("the aura is consumed and
RE-APPLIED inside the same beat, so no screen ever shows it gone and the
reaction looks as though it did not happen"), and the warning was correct.
*Rejected:* the same 3 energy as Strike + Slack Water + Defend for ~10. The Moon
line did 9 now and set 14 for the morning.

Then **Defend**, **Defend** — *rejected:* Slack Water for 2 more damage and a
Weak. The beetle was dying to the morning Plan regardless, so the only live
question was how much of its 13 I ate, and 10 Block answered it.

**Turn 4.** The doubled Oath killed it before a battle screen printed.

**Judged across the fight:** the decision was turn 2's, and it was a good one —
the enemy's own debuff rewrote which of my two damage routes was live, and the
card faces told me so without my having to test it. Companion cards played: **1**
(Kujou Sara).

Trading 6 damage for ~8 HP at 34/80 was not close.

**Turn 3.** It blocked 5 at 3 HP with Poison 3 on it. **Strike** anyway.

saved an action and almost certainly worked; I paid one action not to find out
what an Empower does at 3 HP. Not a decision the kit presented — a decision the
budget presented.

Companion cards played: **1** (Kujou Sara).

*Rejected:* Sara + Flank straight for 12 on the Flyconid. The doubled Plan was

**Turn 2.** **Kurage's Oath** onto the jellyfish, **Strike**, **Strike** into

**Turn 6.** 3 HP, `8×3` with Strength 5 = 24 incoming, one Defend (7) in hand,

## The kit, after 5 fights

**(a) Which decisions felt like real choices, and what they traded off.**

1. **Fight 1, turn 1 — write two Plans or spend the turn.** Traded 3 damage now
   for +1 Energy, 2 cards and 4 extra damage in the morning. Made *on the turn*,
   and it is the kit's signature question: the Bake-Kurage turns every card into
   a now/later fork with a printed price on both sides.
2. **Fight 2, turn 2 — route damage around Shrink.** The debuff repriced my
   attacks live on the card faces while leaving the Plan lines untouched, so
   "play it" and "plan it" stopped being the same card. Made *on the turn*, but
   only findable because the screen prints both numbers.
3. **Fight 4, turn 2 — kill Jaxfruit now for exactly 15, or plan a doubled
   14-to-all and let it swing into Block I already own.** Traded certainty for
   11 extra damage into the second enemy. Made *on the turn*, enabled *earlier
   in the fight* by the potion and by Ascension being down.
4. **Elite, turn 5 — assemble 1 damage out of a lethal 21.** Weak (−6),
   Dexterity (+2 per Defend), two Defends, and Riptide with the energy left
   over. Made *on the turn*, but only reachable because Slack Water and Ripple
   were drafted and the Dexterity Potion was still in the belt.
5. **Fights 3 and 4 — Kujou Sara.** Both fights ended on an exact-lethal Sara +
   Strike. That decision was made *at the draft*, on floor 1, and it is the
   cleanest example in the round of a draft pick paying off two fights later.

**(b) What felt automatic, and what never seemed worth playing.**

Automatic: **Defend** (there is one number and no question in it), **Strike**
(same), and every turn where a doubled Plan had already decided the fight before
I sat down — fight 1 turns 4–5, fight 2 turn 4, fight 4 turn 4. Those are the
plan paying off rather than dead turns, but the count is high: roughly a third
of my turns had nothing in them.

Never worth playing: **Stolen Chapter's Plan line** (a doubled draw-4 is 8 cards
against 3 energy — it never once looked right), and **Tide Chart** in any form —
0 cost, and in three plays I could not detect it doing what it said. **Well
Laid** at 2 base is a card you play because it costs nothing, not because you
wanted it.

**(c) What I could not understand, or that contradicted its own printed text.**

1. **Well Laid — the printed number already contains the bonus, but the card
   still prints the bonus clause as if it were pending.** It reads "Deal 5
   damage. Deals 3 more for each Plan the Bake-Kurage carried out this morning"
   on a morning with carry-outs, and then deals **5**. I scored that as a
   defect and carried the wrong belief for a whole fight before I saw the card
   print "Deal 2 damage" on a clean morning and understood: base is 2, the
   printed head is live, and the clause below it is describing the arithmetic
   that has *already happened*. Every other card on this screen prints a number
   that is still to be applied. This one prints a result wearing a promise.
2. **Nereid's Ascension and The Moon Overlooks the Waters do not compose, and
   nothing says so.** Ascension: "The Bake-Kurage carries out every Plan
   **twice**." Moon: "Plans also happen **now**, as you write them." With both
   on the table I wrote Slack Water and the screen printed exactly **one**
   immediate carry-out under "carried out THIS TURN, the moment each was
   written", and the Plan counter read `Plan 1`. The morning copy doubles; the
   now copy does not. That may well be intended, but "every Plan twice" is not a
   sentence that admits an exception, and I only learned it by spending 2 energy
   to test it.
3. **A Weak applied through a Plan did not appear in the enemy's power list.**
   Fight 1, round 3: two Slack Water carry-outs had landed, the Fuzzy Wurm
   Crawler's intent had dropped from 11 (4 + Strength 7) to **8**, which is
   11 × 0.75 — so Weak was demonstrably on it — and its power list showed
   `Hydro Aura`, `Strength 7`, and no Weak line at all. `Weak 3` only printed
   after a third application. For two turns the only evidence that my debuff had
   worked was arithmetic on the intent number.
4. **The refusal that cost me the elite.** `play "Riptide" on "Byrdonis"` →
   `error Card 'Riptide' cannot be played on 'Byrdonis'`, and **no working forms
   were listed back**, contrary to what the brief promises for an unusable name.
   The card is an ALL-enemies attack and the correct form is the bare
   `play "Riptide"`, which I did not discover for four rounds. It ate 2 of 3
   energy on the opening turn of an 84 HP elite that gains Strength every round.
5. **Tide Chart.** "Next turn, after the Bake-Kurage carries out its Plans, draw
   1 card for each." Two carry-outs pending; one extra card arrived. I cannot
   tell whether "each" counts written Plans or carry-outs, or whether it fired
   at all, and no screen ever narrated it the way the Bake-Kurage block narrates
   Plans. Everything else about this jellyfish is narrated in loving detail;
   this one card is silent.
6. **What "this morning" counts.** Well Laid gained +3, not +6, on a morning
   whose block listed two carry-outs of one doubled Plan. So Well Laid counts
   *written Plans* and Ascension's doubling does not feed it — while the same
   screen prints the two carry-outs as two entries. Two different senses of
   "Plan" on one page.

Praise where it is due, because it is the same subsystem: the **Elemental
Reaction** paragraph predicted, in advance and in writing, the exact confusion I
was about to have — that the relic re-applies the aura inside the reaction's own
beat so the reaction looks as though it never happened — and then that happened,
twice, and I recognised it instead of filing a false defect. And the **"Reaction
preview: Electro-Charged"** line that appears on Hydro cards the moment a
foreign aura is up is the single most useful thing on the combat screen.

**(d) The card I never wanted to play, and the one I was happiest to draw.**

Never wanted: **Tide Chart**. Free, unverifiable, and it made me spend an action
each time to learn nothing. **Stolen Chapter** is a close second — I played it
four times and every one was "I have a spare energy and no better idea".

Happiest to draw: **Battle Plan**, by a distance. With The Moon on the table it
costs nothing at all — write it, it resolves immediately, refunds its own
energy, draws 2, and *still stands* as a written Plan that pays +2 Energy and 4
cards the next morning. Runner-up: **Kujou Sara**, which ended two fights on
exact lethal, and which is the only card in twenty that makes the reaction
system exist.

**(e) Did the first turn of the first fight already present a decision?**

**Yes** — and a good one. Five cards, three energy, a 4-damage enemy, and two of
the five carrying a "Plan:" line with a strictly better number on it than the
card's own text. The question "how much of turn 1 do I spend on turn 2" was
readable off the faces before I played anything, and the answer was not obvious:
Kurage's Oath planned is +4 damage but costs me the tempo, Battle Plan planned
is +1 energy and +1 card. I could not tell from the screen whether the jellyfish
would hold both, which made it a decision with real uncertainty in it, and the
game answered clearly the moment I committed (`Plan 2 — Carries out 2 Plans at
the start of your next turn, in order`). That is the first turn of the first
fight doing its job.
