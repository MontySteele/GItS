# Doctrine read: Kokomi pool pass five, the phase of the Dusk lines, against the kit charters

Each numbered arm below is ONE change. For each, in order, give FOLLOWS or
REQUIRES_MODIFICATION and name the clause (C1 to C7) you ruled against, and
quote the line and the arithmetic (energy, Block, damage, cards, turns) that
breaches it, in one sentence. A verdict with no clause named is not a
verdict. Read adversarially: the author is a different model family and the
owner wants the row that should not reach a tester named. Do not soften.
Name clauses, not remedies: a number, a face or a rewritten row you volunteer
is DISCARDED.

Why this read exists. Pool pass five (EB-685, 2026-09-08) changed WHEN two
Dusk clauses look, retired Night Watch, and reworded Scout Ahead. It was
built and played (rounds 29 to 32) WITHOUT this read; the owner ruled on
2026-09-08 (R267) that it goes to the door now, after the fact. Two of its
four changes have since been REVERSED by that ruling: Slack Water's Plan is
a morning Plan again (the brief names the delayed Weak as the turn-one
decision, and a starter card moves only by ruling), and Scout Ahead's face
went back with its position clause. Arms 2 and 4 below are therefore read
for the record: say whether the pass's version breached a clause, and
separately whether the restored version does.

## The charter

- **C1 (owner, 2026-09-04).** "I don't want us to end up gaming the metric
  of 'can an agent beat the game' by printing overly strong cards." A row
  that removes a losing line the kit is meant to keep, instead of pricing
  it, is out of charter.
- **C2 (LAW D3, binding prices).** A benefit carries a binding price. There
  must be a real turn in which the card is worse than a plain card of its
  cost. Repeatable energy or draw at Uncommon or below is priced by
  something past the card's own cost.
- **C3 (LAW D2, player-controlled leverage).** The card's value is decided
  by a choice the player makes (play now or write; which order; which
  half), not by a number that rises while you watch.
- **C5 (Kokomi brief section 2 rule 4).** Nothing fires by itself; every
  effect is a card the player played and paid for.
- **C6 (not strictly better).** A row may not be a strictly better version
  of a pool row or a base-game card at the same or lower rarity and cost.
  Reference points: Skim (Uncommon, 1: draw 3), Seeing Red, Blur (Uncommon,
  1: 5 Block kept), Flame Barrier, Iron Wave, Shrug It Off, Pommel Strike,
  Whirlwind, Bludgeon, Sword Boomerang, Echo Form (Rare, 3).
- **C7 (Kokomi identity Law 4).** In her pool a Common never increases deck
  size.

## The engine (as it stands today, 2026-09-08)

The Bake-Kurage sits on her side all combat. A card with a Plan line may be
played ON it: cost paid now, the card to the discard, the Plan written to a
queue. At the START of her next turn (after the enemy acts, after her draw)
every queued Plan is carried out in the order written. A Dusk Plan is
carried out at the END of this turn, before enemies act. No cap (R266).
Riders: Opening Gambit (the next Plan carried out with it deals double),
Second Wave (the next is carried out twice), Scout Ahead (draw 1 per LATER
Plan carried out with it). Readers: Well Laid, Sango Isshin, Treatise, Song
of Pearls, Feint (5, or 10 after a carry-out; Plan 10). Nereid's Ascension
(Rare Power, 2): the FIRST Plan carried out each turn is carried out twice.
Starter: four Strike, four Defend, Kurage's Oath (3 to ALL; Plan 7 to
ALL), Slack Water (4 and 1 Weak; Plan 1 Weak to ALL, morning). Energy 3,
HP 80. The relic Tamakushi Casket strikes an enemy for 2 whenever you apply
a debuff to it.

## The arms

1. **Breakwater (the count moves to the queue at Dusk)** -- Common Skill,
   1. Dusk Plan: Gain 5 Block, plus 3 for each Plan the Bake-Kurage is
   HOLDING (written this turn and still waiting for the morning). Upgrade
   base 7. Was (pass four): plus 3 for each Plan carried out this turn,
   which counted a morning already drained and paid 5 on every play. Now
   the wall scales with what was written today: three Plans written and
   Breakwater last, 14 Block at end of turn for 1 Energy. Read C2 and C6
   against Defend and Shrug It Off, and C3 for whether "write more, block
   more" is a choice or a bonus for the play the kit already wants.
2. **Slack Water (the pass's version, since reversed)** -- Starter Attack,
   1. Deal 4 damage. Apply 1 Weak. DUSK Plan: Apply 1 Weak to ALL enemies.
   The written Weak lands this turn before the swing instead of the next
   morning. The restored version (today): the same face with a MORNING
   Plan, the Weak arriving the turn after. Rule on both: did moving the
   starter's only written Weak to Dusk remove the "protect now versus
   prepare later" losing line the brief keeps (C1), and does the restored
   morning Weak carry a binding price (C2) on turn one against an 8 intent.
3. **Night Watch RETIRED** -- the multi-body Weak before the swing was
   Slack Water's Dusk half after arm 2, so the pool did not need it twice.
   With arm 2 reversed, the pool now holds NO written Weak that lands
   before the enemy acts. Rule on whether that gap breaches any clause
   (it is a pool question and not a starter one), or whether it is simply
   a hole a later pass may fill.
4. **Scout Ahead (face wording, since reversed)** -- the pass's face:
   "Plan: Draw 1 card for each Plan carried out this turn, this one
   included, in any order." The restored face: "Plan: Draw 1 card for each
   later Plan carried out with this one." Rule on the restored rule only:
   a Common, 1 Energy (upgrade 0), draw 1 face-up, or written first of
   three draws 2 the next morning, last draws 0. C2 (a repeatable draw at
   Common priced by position and by the Energy spent on the other Plans),
   C3 (the order is the player's), C6 against Skim.

Four verdicts, each with its clause and its breaching line, then two
sentences: with Slack Water back on the morning and Night Watch gone, is
the kit's "protect now" line priced or absent. Under 600 words.
