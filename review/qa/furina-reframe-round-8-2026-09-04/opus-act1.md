## Fight 1 — Nibbit (44 HP)

Opening hand: Ethereal Spotlight, Stage Presence, Soloist's Solicitation ×2,
Lynette — Enigmatic Feint, Chevreuse — Interdiction Fire. 3 energy. Nibbit
intended Attack 12.

**Turn 1.** Chevreuse — Interdiction Fire on Nibbit (7, applies Pyro), then
Soloist's Solicitation ×2 (6 each). **Rejected:** the block line — Stage Presence
6 + Lynette 5 = 11 Block for two of my three energy, which would have covered
the 12 almost exactly but stretched a 44-HP fight to six or seven turns at
6 damage a turn. Against a lone 12-damage attacker the race is plainly better
arithmetic, so the "decision" was really only a check that I had read the two
numbers. **Also rejected:** Ethereal Spotlight, which was not a choice — it
printed **"CANNOT BE PLAYED: you have no Encore, and this costs 2"**, and
nothing in my opening hand made Encore. A 0-cost card that the relic forces into
my hand every turn and that I cannot play on turn 1 of the first fight is a
strange first impression.

I ended the turn holding the Spotlight and it Exhausted itself (Ethereal), which
is the correct printed behaviour but meant the relic's card was pure noise on
that turn.

Took 12. HP 62 → 50.

**Turn 2.** This was the first turn that taught me the kit. New hand: An
Invitation, Salon Début, Stage Presence, Regal Bearing, Aria of Recompense.
Played **An Invitation** (0, added Fischl — Nightrider [Electro] to hand) →
**Aria of Recompense** (Encore 0 → 5) → **Ethereal Spotlight** (0 energy, 2
Encore; Companion cards +50%) → **Salon Début** (Crabaletta deployed and
performed: 9 damage, Nibbit 25 → 16) → **Fischl — Nightrider** on Nibbit (16 →
3, and left Poison 4 behind).

**Rejected:** Stage Presence and Regal Bearing. Nibbit had dropped to Attack 6 +
Defend; 6 Block for one of three energy was worth less than a chain that ended
the fight. The real decision was **Aria first or damage first** — Aria costs a
whole energy and does nothing visible, and the payoff is that it unlocks a
0-cost card and pays for a member's performance. Committing to it on a turn
where I could instead have thrown 18 raw damage is the trade this kit keeps
asking, and it is a good one.

**Where the screen and the outcome disagreed.** The Elemental Reaction block on
turn 1 said, in capitals, **"NO REACTION IS REACHABLE HERE: Pyro is the only
element this screen can supply"** — on a screen where Lynette — Enigmatic Feint
was in my hand printing *"Reaction preview: Swirl — Anemo meets an aura"*. The
warning is computed from something narrower than what my hand could do.

Fight over on turn 2 (Poison finished it). **Reward:** 17 Gold, Block Potion, and
a card reward where **all four options carried (Glam)** — Silken Tress had
worked exactly as printed. I took **Lynette — Magic Trick: Astonishing Shift**
(Swirl + 4 to ALL, Replay 1) over The House Holds Its Breath (8 Block across the
Replay), Casting Call (+2 Salon room with no Deploys to fill it) and Leading
Role (a discount on Spotlighted cards when I could Spotlight once per fight at
best). A Replayed AoE that is *also* a Companion card looked like it would do
three jobs; it did.

## Fight 2 — Shrinker Beetle (39 HP)

**Turn 1.** Salon Début (Crabaletta deploys and performs) → Lynette — Magic
Trick (Swirl + 4 AoE, Replayed) → Soloist's Solicitation. **Rejected:** every
Block card in hand — the Beetle's intent was `Strategic (DebuffStrong)`, no
damage number at all, so blocking was strictly wasted energy. That is a
legitimate read the screen gave me cleanly.

**This is where the Replay stopped adding up.** 39 → 23 is 16 damage. My count of
what the printed text promised was 20: Crabaletta on the Deploy (4, at
three-quarters with no Encore), then Lynette's own 4 plus a Crabaletta
performance (4), then the Replay's 4 plus *another* performance (4). And the
Fanfare counter read **4**, not 6 — and Fanfare is printed as "2 when it stays"
per performance, so the screen was telling me **two** performances happened, not
three. I read this as: **a Glam Replay repeats the card's own text but does not
perform a member a second time.** Nothing on the Companion keyword, the Glam
keyword or the Replay keyword says so. The keyword says flatly *"A Companion
card you play performs the front member."*

**Turn 2.** Shrink -1 landed (*"While Shrinker Beetle is alive, your Attacks
deal 30% less damage"*) and — this part was excellent — **every attack in my
hand reprinted its number**: Soloist's 6 → 4, Chevreuse 7 → 4. I never had to do
that arithmetic myself.

Aria of Recompense (Encore 0 → 10, because Fanfare 3 met its own "at least 3
Fanfare" clause) → Ethereal Spotlight (Encore 10 → 8) → Chevreuse — Interdiction
Fire on the Beetle: 17 → 1. **Rejected:** the plain line, Chevreuse + Soloist +
Stage Presence, which was 10 damage and left the Beetle alive at 7. Spending two
of three energy on cards that deal no damage (Aria, and the Spotlight it pays
for) to make the third card hit for triple is the most interesting decision the
kit offered me all act, and I could work it out entirely from printed text:
4 printed × 1.5 (Spotlight) × 1.5 (Vaporize, which the card previewed by name).

Finished with Lynette — Enigmatic Feint. **No rejected alternative:** the Beetle
was at 1 HP and four different cards killed it. I picked the one that would
teach me something (whether a Companion card's Block is Spotlighted — it was:
5 → 7).

Zero damage taken. **Reward:** 17 Gold, and I took **High Tide** (15 damage for
1 energy, "already including Fanfare") over Dahlia — Sacramental Shower, Stage
Lights and Take It From the Top. 15 for 1 was more than double anything else my
deck could do.

## Fight 3 — Leaf Slime (S) 14, Twig Slime (M) 28, Twig Slime (S) 8

**Turn 1.** An Invitation (0; gave Bennett — Passion Overload) → Chevreuse on
Twig Slime (M) (7, Pyro aura on it) → Lynette — Magic Trick targeting Twig Slime
(M) (Swirl: the Pyro was consumed and **copied onto all three**, plus 4 to ALL,
Replayed for 8 total) → Bennett — Passion Overload (0; next Attack +4) → High
Tide on Twig Slime (M) (Hydro into Pyro = Vaporize).

Twig Slime (S) died to the AoE; Twig Slime (M) died to High Tide; Leaf Slime
ended the turn at 6 with a Pyro aura it had never been hit by.

**Rejected:** targeting Lynette at the Leaf Slime, and holding Chevreuse for a
later Vaporize. The whole turn was one real decision — **spend a card to put an
aura on one body, then spend a second card to spread it** — and it is the best
thing in this kit. The Swirl→spread→Vaporize sequence was legible from three
printed keyword blocks and it did exactly what those blocks said.

**Turn 2.** Soloist's Solicitation killed the Leaf Slime. **No rejected
alternative:** 6 HP, one enemy, six cards in hand and any of three of them ended
it. A pure clean-up turn.

**Where the screen went quiet.** Bennett granted 5 Burst Energy and High Tide's
Elemental Skill granted 5 more. **No screen printed a Burst Energy counter, then
or ever.** I gained at least 10 of a resource across the act and never once saw
it, never saw what spends it, and never saw a card that mentioned it other than
as something granted.

**Reward:** 11 Gold; took **Chiori — Fluttering Hasode** (1 energy, 6 Geo at the
end of each of 3 turns, ignoring Block — 18 damage for one energy, plus a third
element) over Crescendo, Compose Herself and Casting Call.

## Fight 4 — Fogmog (74 HP), later + Eye with Teeth (6 HP)

**Turn 1.** Fogmog intended `Summon`, no damage. Aria of Recompense (Encore 5) →
Ethereal Spotlight (Companion cards +50%) → **Chiori — Fluttering Hasode** →
An Invitation (0; gave Diona — Icy Paws) → Diona — Icy Paws (7 Block, Cryo on
Fogmog).

**Rejected:** Stage Presence, and Lynette — Enigmatic Feint over Diona. Diona
won on the tiebreak of also leaving an aura for Chiori's Geo to react with.

**The clearest contradiction of the act.** I played the Spotlight *before*
Chiori precisely so her damage would be 50% stronger. The buff Chiori left
behind printed **"Tamoto 3 — At the end of your turn, deal 6 Geo damage"** —
still 6. On the same screen, Lynette — Enigmatic Feint printed Block 7 (up from
5) and Diona printed Block 7, so the Spotlight was live and was visibly
modifying other Companion cards. Ethereal Spotlight says *"Spotlight every
Companion card. Their printed damage and Block are 50% stronger."* Chiori is a
Companion card, 6 Geo is her printed damage, and it was not stronger. Whatever
the rule is — that the Spotlight modifies the card's own resolution and not a
power it creates — no screen states it, and I paid an entire card and two Encore
on a wrong reading of the plainest sentence in the kit.

**Turn 2.** Fogmog 68, attacking 8 and buffing; Eye with Teeth summoned (revives
on death, so killing it is pointless). High Tide on Fogmog (15) → Lynette — Magic
Trick (6 to ALL now, Spotlighted from 4, Replayed) → Soloist's Solicitation.
Fogmog 68 → 29, the Eye dead-and-returning. **Rejected:** Stage Presence and
Regal Bearing — I had Encore 3 sitting on the sheet and Encore *is* the block, so
buying more of a resource I already held was the worse buy. It played out
exactly right: 8 incoming, Encore 3 absorbed it, and I lost **1** HP.

**Turn 3.** Fogmog 29, hitting for 15 with Strength 1. Salon Début (Crabaletta
performs 4 Hydro on Fogmog, leaving a Hydro aura) → Chevreuse — Interdiction
Fire on Fogmog (10 printed after the Spotlight, ×1.5 Vaporize = 15, then
performing Crabaletta again for 4) → Soloist's Solicitation (6). 29 → 6 → 0.

**Rejected:** the safe line, Chevreuse + Soloist + Stage Presence, which left
Fogmog at 13 and me eating 15. I chose the kill because I could *count* it — the
Vaporize preview named its multiplier and the member's three-quarters rate was
printed on the Salon block. It came to exactly 29 and it was exactly right. This
was the best turn of the round: three cards, an ordering constraint (the Hydro
had to land before the Pyro), and an answer I could verify before committing.

**Reward:** 14 Gold; took **Courtroom Drama** (first Elemental Reaction each
turn applies Vulnerable and Weak) over Leading Role, Quick Change and a second
Lynette — Enigmatic Feint, on the basis that I was triggering a reaction nearly
every turn. I drew it twice more and **never played it** — see (b).

*Between fights:* the Jungle Maze event offered 163 Gold for 18 HP or 55 Gold
for nothing. I took **Join Forces** (55): I had seen no healing card, no healing
relic and no healing event all act, and 18 HP off 49 with two Elites on the
board was a price I could not pay back. Then the Room Full of Cheese: I took
**Gorge** (choose 2 of 8 Commons) over 14 HP for The Chosen Cheese, and picked
**Shared Billing** (applies Hydro, refunds its own energy — a free aura for
Chevreuse to Vaporize) and **Mademoiselle Crabaletta** (a second way to get a
member on stage, since every Companion card I owned had been performing nobody).

## Fight 5 — Brute Raider 31, Tracker Raider 21, Axe Raider 20

**Turn 1.** Chiori — Fluttering Hasode → Aria of Recompense → Lynette — Magic
Trick (8 to all across the Replay). **Rejected:** Chevreuse — Interdiction Fire,
which is 7 to one body against 24 spread across three. Also rejected: leading
Chevreuse to put Pyro down for Lynette to Swirl, which would have been prettier

**Turn 2.** High Tide killed the Tracker Raider outright (13 HP, the one
intending eight separate hits) → Soloist's on the Brute → Stage Presence.
**Rejected:** Courtroom Drama. This is the moment I decided the card was a trap
in practice — one energy for a rider on reactions, in a fight with maybe two
reactions left, against removing the enemy that was about to hit me eight times.
I never found a turn where the answer came out differently.

**Turn 3.** The best-reasoned turn of the act. Brute 11 (attacking 10 with
Strength 3), Axe 6 behind 5 Block (attacking 12) — 22 incoming onto 41 HP.
An Invitation (0; gave Charlotte — Framing: Freezing Point Composition) →
Shared Billing (free; Hydro landed on the Brute) → **Charlotte on the Brute**
(Cryo into Hydro = Frozen: *"its next action deals 50% less damage. The first
Attack to hit it Shatters for 6 unblockable damage"* — the Brute's 10 became 5)
→ Salon Début (Crabaletta performed onto the Brute, 11 → 3) → Soloist's killed
the Brute through the Shatter.

I then **ended the turn on 0 energy with the Axe Raider alive at 6 behind 5
Block**, deliberately: Chiori's Tamoto tick deals 6 **ignoring Block** to a
random enemy, and with only one enemy left "random" is not random. It killed the
Axe Raider. **Rejected:** playing Lynette — Enigmatic Feint for 3 Block (Frail
had cut it), and using the Block Potion. Both would have been wasted on a turn
that ended the fight. Working out that the ignores-Block tick was my only clean
answer to a 5-Block body was the most satisfying thing I did in the run, and
every piece of it was printed.

**Reward:** 14 Gold, Speed Potion; took **Full Ensemble** (2 energy, adds all
three named members at once) over Slip Backstage, Melody of Many Waters and
Charlotte — First-Person Shutter, because the recurring failure of this deck was
that **every Companion card I played performed nobody**.

**Turn 6.** Fanfare 18 made High Tide print **24** damage, up from 15, with the

## The kit, after 6 fights

**(a) Which decisions felt like real choices, and what they traded off.**

Three shapes, and all three are good.

1. **Spend a turn's energy on nothing, to make one card hit three times as
   hard.** Aria of Recompense → Ethereal Spotlight → Chevreuse is two dead cards
   buying one live one, and against the Shrinker Beetle it turned a printed 4
   into 9 taken. The trade is legible because every multiplier is named on a
   card: ×1.5 Spotlight, ×1.5 Vaporize.
2. **Order of elements within a turn.** Fight 4, turn 3 only works if the Hydro
   from a member's performance lands before Chevreuse's Pyro. Fight 3 only works
   if Chevreuse's Pyro lands before Lynette's Swirl spreads it. Getting these
   backwards costs the whole turn, the screens give you everything you need to
   get them right, and the reaction previews update live in hand as auras change.
   This is the best-taught mechanic in the kit.
3. **Encore as a third resource that is simultaneously armour.** Choosing not to
   play a Block card because I was already holding 3 Encore, and then losing
   exactly 1 HP to an 8-damage hit, felt like the kit rewarding me for reading
   one sentence carefully.

The stage adds a fourth: **spend now or build the stage**. I said no to
Mademoiselle Crabaletta twice on explicit arithmetic (a member is worth ~4 a
turn at three-quarters), and then Full Ensemble paid off enormously. That is a
real tension.

**(b) What felt automatic, and what never seemed worth playing.**

*Automatic:* **Soloist's Solicitation.** "Deal 6 damage" for 1 energy is the
card I played when the turn was already decided; it never once changed a plan.
Every clean-up turn (fight 1 turn 2's Poison tick, fight 3 turn 2, fight 2's
1-HP finish) presented **no rejected alternative at all** — the enemy was under
6 HP and three cards in hand all killed it.

*Never worth playing:* **Courtroom Drama.** I drew it three times across two
fights and rejected it every time; one energy for a rider on the *first*
reaction each turn is always outbid by the card that removes an attacker.
**Regal Bearing** (3 Block, 1 Weak) was never the right 1 energy either.
And **Ethereal Spotlight** is worth playing but is dead in hand more often than
not: the relic feeds it to me every single turn, and on any turn where I have
not spent an energy on Aria, it prints "CANNOT BE PLAYED" and Exhausts itself.
That is a card-shaped object in my hand every turn of every fight that I can act
on maybe twice a combat.

**(c) What I could not understand, or that contradicted its own printed text.**

Four things, in descending order of how much they cost me.

1. **The Spotlight does not reach Chiori's damage.** Ethereal Spotlight:
   *"Spotlight every Companion card. Their printed damage and Block are 50%
   stronger."* Chiori — Fluttering Hasode is a Companion card whose printed
   damage is 6 Geo. Spotlighted, she still made a Tamoto buff reading **6**, on
   a screen where two other Companion cards had visibly gone 5 → 7. I sequenced
   a whole turn around the boost and got none of it.
2. **A Glam Replay does not perform a member.** The Companion keyword says
   flatly that a Companion card you play performs the front member; Replay says
   it plays the card an additional time. Twice (fight 2 turn 1, fight 6 turn 3) I
   counted 16 damage where the text promised 20, with the Fanfare counter —
   2 per performance — agreeing with 16. If Replay is meant to skip the
   performance, nothing anywhere says so, and Fanfare is the only instrument
   that let me detect it.
3. **Burst Energy does not exist on any screen.** High Tide, Mademoiselle
   Crabaletta, Full Ensemble, Melody of Many Waters and Bennett — Passion
   Overload all print "Burst +5" or "grants 5 Burst Energy". I granted myself at
   least 20 across the act. No status line, no counter, no card that spends it.
   I have no idea whether I was three points from something or nowhere near.
4. **"NO REACTION IS REACHABLE HERE"** printed in capitals on a screen where my
   hand held an Anemo card with a live Swirl preview. The claim is computed from
   less than the player can actually do, and it is stated with more confidence
   than anything else on the page.

Two smaller ones. The word **Companion** made me expect that playing
Chevreuse — Interdiction Fire would put Chevreuse on the stage; it does not —
Companion cards *use* the stage, only Deploy/Add cards fill it, and for the first
two fights every Companion card I played "performed nobody" while I waited to
understand why. And **the map's deck list** printed Ethereal Spotlight ×6 and
Infection ×6 as deck contents; the page does warn it is showing the last fight's
combat deck, but that warning is doing a lot of work.

**(d) The card I never wanted to play, and the one I was happiest to draw.**

*Never wanted:* **Regal Bearing** — 3 Block and 1 Weak for a full energy, in a
deck where Stage Presence gives 6 Block for the same. I played it zero times in
six fights. Runner-up is Courtroom Drama, which I owned deliberately and never
cast.

*Happiest to draw:* **Lynette — Magic Trick: Astonishing Shift**. It is AoE, it
is a Swirl that spreads an aura I planted, it is a Companion card that performs a
member, and under Glam it does the damage half twice. It won fight 3 outright,
cleared two Wrigglers in the elite, and it is the only card that made me change
my whole turn to set up for it. Honourable mention to **High Tide**, purely for
printing "You hold 18 Fanfare: +9 damage, already counted in the number above" —
the number on the card was always the number that happened.

**(e) Did the first turn of the first fight already present a decision?**

**Barely, and not a good one.** The choice on offer was race or block, against a
single enemy with one attack number, and the arithmetic settles it in one line —
6 Block for an energy that could be 6 damage, against 44 HP that needs three
turns either way. Worse, the two cards that make this kit interesting were both
inert: Ethereal Spotlight printed **"CANNOT BE PLAYED: you have no Encore"**, and
Lynette — Enigmatic Feint's Swirl had no aura to move and no member to perform.
The first turn that presented a real decision was **turn 2 of fight 1**, when An
Invitation, Aria and Salon Début arrived together and I had to choose whether to
spend an energy on a resource instead of on damage. That is one turn later than
it should be, and the gap is caused by the opening hand containing the kit's two
conditional cards with neither condition met.

## Non-blindness declaration

Commands run outside the two allowed ones (`blindplay observe` and
`blindplay act`), all via the Bash tool:

- `mkdir -p .../scratchpad/lane2` and one `echo` appending a line to
  `notes.txt` in that scratchpad directory (one call, at the very start).
- `mkdir -p review/qa/furina-reframe-round-8-2026-09-04` immediately before
  writing this file, plus the `echo ok` that call ended with.
- Many `observe` invocations were piped through `sed -n '<ranges>'` to re-read
  one block of the screen (hand only, enemies only, header only) without
  reprinting the whole page. `sed` only ever narrowed output that `observe` had
  already printed to me; it never added anything.

Tools used: **Bash** (for the two allowed commands and the scratch/`sed` calls
above) and **Write** (once, for this file).

No `harness state`, no `scenario`, no `staged_turn`, no `soak`, no other
understudy command.

**Repo files read: none.**
