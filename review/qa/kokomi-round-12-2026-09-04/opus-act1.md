## Fight 1 — Seapunk 45/45

**Turn 1** (HP 64, 3 energy). Played **Kurage's Oath+ on "Bake-Kurage"** (writes
the Plan), then **Slack Water**, then **Strike (1)**. 45 → 33.

*Rejected:* playing Kurage's Oath+ now for its 3 AoE. The face prints "Deal 3
damage to ALL enemies. Plan: Deal 10 damage to ALL enemies", so against one
enemy the choice is a legible 3-now against 10-next-turn, and the only thing on
the other side of the ledger is that the 10 arrives after the enemy has acted
once. At 64 HP against an 11-damage intent I could afford to buy the 10. Also
rejected Defend: Slack Water's Weak dropped the printed intent from 11 to 8, so
the attack card was the block card.

Numbers checked out: Slack Water 4 + 2 (Tamakushi Casket firing on the Weak) +
Strike 6 = 12.

**Turn 2** (HP 56). The Plan carried out for 10 at the start of the turn,
33 → 23. Hand came up Defend ×3, Strike ×2 — **no Plan card, so this turn
presented no decision at all**. Played Strike, Strike, Defend; 23 → 11.
*Rejected:* three Defends, which is a slower version of nothing.

One disagreement between screen and outcome: the aura keyword says "a Hydro hit
refreshes its duration", and the Bake-Kurage's carry-out is printed as a Hydro
hit, but Seapunk's Hydro Aura read **2** before the carry-out and **1** after
it. Either the refresh did not happen or the decay is ordered after it; the
screen cannot tell me which.

**Turn 3** (HP 53). Strike, Strike — 11 → dead. No decision.

Reward: 11 gold; took **Undertow** ("Deal 7 damage. If the enemy has a debuff,
deal 10 instead") over Deep Current (6 to ALL), Lisa (Electro + Vulnerable over
3 turns, Exhaust) and Salt Line (8 Block, Exhaust). Reasoning I could form off
the printed faces alone: my relic pings on every debuff I apply, so "the enemy
has a debuff" is a condition I turn on for free, and my only single-target
damage was Strike.

## Fight 2 — Corpse Slug 25/25 + Corpse Slug 26/26

Both printed `Ravenous 4 — When an enemy dies, Corpse Slug immediately eats it,
becoming Stunned and gaining 4 Strength.`

**Turn 1** (HP 59). Planned **Kurage's Oath+** on the Bake-Kurage, then
Strike ×2 into Corpse Slug (1). 25 → 13. Took 8 and a Frail 2.

*Rejected:* spreading the Strikes one to each slug. Ravenous makes the kill
order matter — killing one hands the other +4 Strength — so I wanted one body
low enough to die on a turn where the second could die in the same turn, and
the planned 10-to-ALL was the tool that would put them both in range at once.

**Turn 2** (HP 51). The Plan carried out 10 to ALL: slugs at **3** and **16**.

This was the best turn of the act and the only one where I did arithmetic in
advance. Three energy, and the line I found off the printed faces was:
**Strike into Corpse Slug (1)** (3 HP, dies) → Ravenous fires on Corpse Slug (2)
so it is *Stunned*, i.e. its 3×2 never lands → **Slack Water on Corpse Slug (2)**
(4 + 2 relic = 6, 16 → 10, and the Weak it applies is what turns on the next
card) → **Undertow on Corpse Slug (2)** which now reads 10 instead of 7. 10 − 10
= dead. Fight over on the exact number.

*Rejected:* the mirror order — Slack Water and Undertow into slug 2 first, then
Strike the 3 HP slug. Same total, but it fails safe in the wrong direction: if
Undertow had turned out to read 7 (i.e. if the Hydro *aura* did not count as a
debuff, which the screen never resolves) slug 2 lives at 3 and hits me, whereas
killing slug 1 first buys the Stun whether or not the rest lands. The
Frail/Weak-adjusted Defend, printed at **3 Block** instead of 5, was worth
nothing and I did not consider it.

Reward: 14 gold, Vulnerable Potion; took **The Moon Overlooks the Waters**
(cost 2, power, "Plans also happen now, as you write them") over Exposed Flank,
Chain of Command (a Companion payoff with no Companions in the deck yet) and
Shinobu. Bought purely on the printed sentence: it converts every Plan card
from *10 next turn* into *10 now and 10 next turn*.

numbers on one card, both correct, both correctly different. That is good.

**Then the sharpest disagreement of the act.** Under the same Weak, my hand

- `Undertow — Deal 7 damage. If the enemy has a debuff, deal 10 instead.`

I played Undertow and the enemy went **23 → 18**. It dealt **5**, not 7. Strike's

**Turn 3** (HP 40). Yae Miko (my one Skill), Undertow, Strike. 36 → 13 after the

**Turn 4** (HP 36). Enemy at 13 wearing an Electro Aura, and Slack Water's face

> *Reaction preview: Electro-Charged* — Hydro meets Electro: the reacted enemy
> loses 4 HP at the start of its turn, 1 less each turn.

That preview is the single best piece of legibility in the kit — the card tells

Elemental Reaction glossary warns about, where the reaction consumes the aura and
the relic's own Hydro hit re-applies it inside the same beat so no screen ever
shows it gone. The glossary predicted its own confusing case correctly, which is
rare and worth saying.

One naming crack: the glossary calls it **Electro-Charged**, the body wears it as
**`Poison 4 (debuff)`**. Nothing on screen connects the two names; I only knew
because I watched the HP.

Reward: 20 gold; took **War Council** ("Apply 1 Weak to ALL enemies. Plan: Deal

## The kit, after 7 fights

**(a) Which decisions felt like real choices, and what they traded off.**

Three shapes, and all three are the same trade seen from different angles.

1. **Now versus next turn.** Every kit card prints two numbers — its own, and
   its Plan — and playing it on the Bake-Kurage buys the bigger one at the price
   of a turn. Fight 4 turn 2 is the pure case: 10-to-ALL now plus 10-to-ALL
   queued, bought by eating 12 damage at 30 HP. The trade is legible because
   both numbers are on the card face at once, and it moves: fight 7 turn 3
   inverted it, and the same face told me so.
2. **The engine turn.** The Moon Overlooks the Waters costs a whole turn (2 of
   3 energy for zero damage) and doubles every Plan afterwards. Whether that
   turn is affordable depends on how long the fight in front of me is, and I got
   it right in fight 3 (39 HP body) and it was dead weight in fight 7 turn 2
   (no Plan card in hand). That is a real decision precisely because it can be
   wrong.
3. **Constraint hands.** Smoggy ("only 1 Skill per turn") in fight 5 was the
   best turn of the act, because The Moon is a *Power* and slips the cap while
   Kurage's Oath+ spends the one Skill. That is a decision made entirely out of
   printed text, and it rewarded reading rather than knowing.

Underneath all of it, the debuff→relic→reaction chain gave a fourth, quieter
choice: which body to put a debuff on, because Tamakushi Casket pings it,
Undertow doubles against it, and the Sakura's Electro turns my own Hydro into
Electro-Charged. Fight 2 turn 2 and fight 7 turn 2 were both won on that chain.

**(b) What felt automatic, and what never seemed worth playing.**

The Strikes and Defends. Roughly a third of my turns were pure vanilla — fight 1
turn 2 and turn 3, fight 3 turn 3, fight 6 turn 4 — because the ten-card
starting deck is eight base cards and two kit cards, so a five-card hand often
contains no Plan card at all. **When the Bake-Kurage has nothing written on it,
this character is Ironclad with worse Strikes.** That is the biggest structural
complaint I have: the kit's whole identity sits on two starter cards, and the
draw decides whether you get to play the character this turn.

Defend specifically never felt worth playing except as the second half of an
already-lost turn. 5 Block (3 under Frail) against 11-to-17 incoming is not a
decision, it is a rounding error, and I played it 5 times all act.

The Moon in the back half of a fight is a dead card, and I want to flag that
it *looks* the same in hand at turn 1 and turn 5 — nothing on its face says
"this is worth nothing with an empty hand of Plans."

**(c) What I could not understand, or that contradicted its own printed text.**

- **Undertow's face is not adjusted for Weak.** Under Weak 1, Strike printed
  "Deal 4 damage" (correct) while Undertow printed "Deal 7 damage" and dealt
  **5**. Two cards, same screen, same debuff, one honest number and one stale
  one. I made a play off the stale one.
- **A Plan lands after the enemy's Block goes up.** Punch Construct's turn-1
  intent was Defend; my queued Kurage's Oath+ resolved at the start of my turn 2
  into fresh Block and moved its HP by **zero** (35 → 35), while still applying
  its Hydro aura so it *looked* like it had worked. Nothing on the Plan keyword,
  the card, or the carry-out panel warns that the Plan you buy on the turn an
  enemy is blocking is the Plan you throw away. The Bake-Kurage panel prints
  "Sludge Spinner lost 10 HP" when a Plan connects — here it had nothing to
  print and I had to subtract to notice.
- **Vigor prints on a Skill.** Akabeko gives "Vigor 8 — Your next **Attack**
  deals 8 additional damage", and Kurage's Oath+ — a **skill** — printed
  "Deal 11 damage to ALL enemies" while Vigor was up. Either the face is lying
  or Vigor is not Attack-only. I never spent Vigor there, so I do not know which,
  and I would rather say that than guess.
- **"Hydro refreshes its duration" versus what I watched.** Fight 1: aura read
  2, the Bake-Kurage's Hydro carry-out landed, aura read 1.
- **Electro-Charged is worn as `Poison N`.** The glossary defines
  Electro-Charged; the body prints "Poison 4 (debuff) — At the start of its turn,
  loses 4 HP, then reduce Poison by 1." Same effect, two names, no link.
- **Undefined words offered as purchases.** The floor-2 event sold "Enchant a
  card with **Steady**" for 55 and 99 gold and never says what Steady does.
  Akabeko sold Vigor with no definition until it was already on me.
- **The Plan keyword's own sentence is the least readable text in the kit:**
  "On the Bake-Kurage, paid now; next turn: front non-Minion, or ALL, Minions
  too." I read it perhaps fifteen times across the act and I still could not
  restate the targeting clause. Everything I actually learned about Plans I
  learned from the Bake-Kurage panel, which is written in plain sentences and is
  excellent.
- **I never once had two Plans queued.** The panel says "carried out ... in this
  order (1)" and clearly expects a stack, and I never got there in 7 fights —
  never had two Plan cards and spare energy in the same hand. So the ordering
  rules the panel is built for went untested.

**(d) The card I never wanted to play, and the one I was happiest to draw.**

Never wanted: **Defend.** 5 Block is not an answer to anything act 1 pointed at
me, and the two turns I spent double-Defending (fight 4 turn 1) bought 10 HP.

Happiest to draw: **Kurage's Oath+.** It is the only card in the deck whose face
poses a question — 3 now or 10 later, and against how many bodies — and it
answers differently in fight 4 turn 2 and fight 7 turn 3. **The Moon Overlooks
the Waters** was the most exciting card to *own*, but Oath+ is the one that made
turns.

**(e) Did the first turn of the first fight already present a decision?**

**Yes, and a good one.** Turn 1 of fight 1, against a single Seapunk, the hand
held Kurage's Oath+ printing "Deal 3 damage to ALL enemies. Plan: Deal 10 damage
to ALL enemies" and Slack Water printing "Deal 4 damage. Apply 1 Weak. Plan:
Apply 1 Weak to ALL enemies." That is two cards each offering a now-line and a
later-line, on a board where the Plan's ALL clause is worth nothing extra, so the
whole question is tempo — and the enemy's printed 11-damage intent is the price
tag. I chose to plan the Oath and play the Slack Water, and I could say why
before I saw the result. Very few characters put a real decision on the first
turn of the first fight; this one does, off card faces alone, with no prior
knowledge.
