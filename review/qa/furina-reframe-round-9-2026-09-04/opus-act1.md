## Fight 1 — Nibbit, HP 43/43

**Turn 1** (3 energy, Nibbit telegraphing Attack 12).
Played **Salon Début** → **Regal Bearing** → **Stage Presence**.
Salon Début first because the deploy is the only thing in the hand that compounds; the screen
warned me what it would cost me: *"a member with no Encore to spend performs at three-quarters"*,
and sure enough the log read **"Crabaletta hit Nibbit for 4 Hydro … (dry: it could not pay its
Encore, so it acted at three-quarters)"** — 4 instead of 6.
Then Regal Bearing, and I watched the enemy's printed intent fall from **12 to 9** the instant the
Weak landed, which let me buy exactly enough Block with one Stage Presence (3 + 6 = 9) instead of
two.
*Rejected:* Stage Presence ×2 + Soloist's — 12 Block and 6 damage. Identical damage taken (zero),
but it puts nobody on the stage, and the deploy is the one line that pays again next turn.
**Outcome matched the screen:** 0 damage taken.

**Turn 2** (3 energy, Nibbit at 39, telegraphing "Attack 6, a part it MAY perform" + Defend).
Played **An Invitation** (0) → **Aria of Recompense** → **Ethereal Spotlight** (0 energy, 2 Encore)
→ **Freminet — Pers, Deploy!** → **Neuvillette — O Tears, I Shall Repay**.
This was the turn the kit clicked. An Invitation is free so it is never a decision; Aria was, and I
took it over a second attack because **Ethereal Spotlight is unplayable at 0 Encore** and it buys a
combat-long +50% on every Companion card. Freminet's Cryo met the Hydro aura Crabaletta had left
and the card printed the answer before I committed: *"Reaction preview: Frozen — Hydro meets Cryo:
its next action deals half damage, and the first Attack to hit it Shatters for 6 damage."*
Nibbit went 39 → 24 (Freminet 9 spotlighted + Crabaletta 6) and its intent halved from 6 to 3.
Neuvillette then took it 24 → 5: 7 + Shatter 6 + Crabaletta 6 = 19, exactly as printed.
*Rejected:* a second **Salon Début** to build the stage. With Fanfare still at 3 and one member
already performing twice per turn off Companion plays, a second body was worth less than the
Spotlight that multiplies every Companion play for the rest of the fight.

**Turn 3** (3 energy, Nibbit at 5). Played **Soloist's Solicitation**, then **Aria of Recompense**.
**Here the screen and the outcome disagreed.** Soloist's prints *Deal 6 damage*; Nibbit was at 5;
it survived at **4**. The only reading that fits is that Nibbit had Block from the Defend half of
its previous multi-part telegraph and absorbed 5 of the 6 — **but no enemy Block value is printed
anywhere on the combat page.** I could not have predicted the non-kill from what I was shown.
*Rejected:* Stage Presence ×2 for 12 Block — it telegraphed Empower, so I bought Encore instead,
and Aria's **"If you have at least 3 Fanfare, gain 5 more"** fired off Fanfare 4 for 10 Encore.
This was also the turn with **no attack and no Companion in hand while a member stood on stage** —
Crabaletta was on the board and there was literally no legal way to make her act. Not a decision:
a dead turn by construction.

**Turn 4.** Freminet (printed 9) killed it. No decision.

Reward: 15 gold; took **Charlotte — Snappy Silhouette** over Take Your Bow / Compose Herself /
Lasting Impression, because it is a Companion (so it performs a member) *and* applies 2 Vulnerable
*and* draws — three jobs on a 1-cost.

## Fight 2 — Shrinker Beetle, HP 40/40

**Turn 1** (3 energy, telegraphing DebuffStrong — no damage incoming, so a pure damage turn).
Played **Salon Début** → **Freminet** → **Soloist's Solicitation**, deliberately in that order:
deploy first so the Companion play performs her, Cryo second so it lands on the Hydro aura she just
applied, Attack last so it eats the Shatter. 40 → 14, 26 damage off three 1-cost cards.
*Rejected:* Charlotte — First-Person Shutter (4 Block now, 4 next turn). Half its Block would have
been spent on a turn the enemy telegraphed a debuff rather than an attack.

**Turn 2** (3 energy, Beetle at 14, now attacking for 7, and I was wearing
**Shrink -1 — "While Shrinker Beetle is alive, your Attacks deal 30% less damage"**).
Played **An Invitation** (0) → **Aria of Recompense** → **Ethereal Spotlight** (0 energy, 2 Encore)
→ **Charlotte — Snappy Silhouette** → **Bennett — Passion Overload** (0).
The interesting part: with Shrink up, my *Attacks* were taxed but Crabaletta's performances were
the bulk of my damage. Snappy applied Vulnerable 2 and the member performance that followed hit for
**9 off a printed 6** — exactly ×1.5. Bennett's free performance finished it.
*Rejected:* Stage Presence ×2 (12 Block) against a 7-damage attack. I had 8 Encore banked and the
page says Encore *"absorbs damage before HP"*, so the Block would have been paying twice for the
same 7 points.

Reward: 18 gold, Dexterity Potion; took **Charlotte — Framing: Freezing Point Composition** (Cryo
Companion attack that draws) over Director's Cut / Lasting Impression / Stage Lights.

## The kit, after 6 fights

**(a) Which decisions felt like real choices, and what they traded off.**

Four kinds, and they are genuinely different from each other:

1. **Order within a turn.** This is the kit's best decision and it recurred every single fight.
   Deploy before Companion plays or the Companions perform nobody; element before the Attack that
   eats the Shatter; Weak into an Artifact before the Vulnerable you actually want; every 0-cost
   card before the swing while Slow ticks up. The elite turn 1 was six cards where *only* the order
   mattered, and it was legible: I could watch Slow climb 10 → 50 on the enemy block as I played.
2. **Encore as a shield or as fuel.** One pool doing two jobs is a real tension. Aria's 10 Encore is
   a 10-point shield *or* it is Ethereal Spotlight (2) plus four full-strength member performances.
   I paid the 2 for the Spotlight in three separate fights and never regretted it, but the choice
   was live each time, and the "dry, acted at three-quarters" line in the salon log makes the cost
   of running the pool empty visible rather than theoretical.
3. **Freeze the attacker instead of buying Block.** Twice — fight 3 turn 2 and the elite — a Cryo
   card halved a telegraphed attack *and* dealt damage for the same energy a Defend would have
   spent. That reframes a whole card type and it is the most satisfying thing here.
4. **Which reaction to set up.** Superconduct for +2 Vulnerable before the burst, Melt for 1.75×,
   Swirl to spread an aura before an AoE. The *"Reaction preview:"* line on the card in hand is the
   single best piece of interface in this kit — it tells you the consequence before you commit, by
   name, on the card, and it was right every time.

**(b) What felt automatic, and what never seemed worth playing.**

- **An Invitation** (0 cost, add a Companion, exhaust) is never a decision. I played it on sight
  five times and would again. Free is not a choice.
- **Stage Presence** (6 Block, vanilla) I played **once in six fights**, on the very first turn.
  After the Spotlight was up, blocking was almost always the worst use of an energy: a Companion
  card does its own job *and* performs a member, and Encore soaks chip damage for free.
- **Regal Bearing** was dead weight for five fights and then had exactly one brilliant turn (feeding
  its Weak to the Cubex Construct's Artifact). That is a fine shape for a card, but it means four
  fights of holding a card I never wanted.
- **Casting Call** — 26 gold and a slot in my deck for a Salon capacity I never once approached. I
  rejected it in three separate turns. With one Deploy in the deck it is unplayable-in-practice.
- **Turn 3 of fight 1 was fully automatic in the bad way**: no attack, no Companion, a member
  standing idle on stage that the rules explicitly forbid from acting on its own
  ("Members do NOT act on their own"), and nothing to do but bank Encore.

**(c) What I could not understand, or that contradicted its own printed text.**

1. **Enemy Block is never printed.** Fight 1 turn 3: Nibbit at 5 HP, I played a card printing
   *Deal 6 damage*, and it lived at 4. Nothing on the combat page showed the Block that ate the
   other 5. That is the only outright unpredictable outcome of the run.
2. **"If you moved the Spotlight this turn"** gates *Director's Cut* and *Take It From the Top*, and
   **nothing ever defines what moving the Spotlight is.** Ethereal Spotlight's own buff says
   "Lasts until the Spotlight moves," which restates the phrase instead of explaining it. I passed
   on both cards purely because I could not tell whether I could turn the condition on.
3. **"Burst Energy"** (Bennett — Passion Overload: *"Gain 5 Burst Energy"*) is never defined
   anywhere on any screen. Nor is **"Guest Stars"**, which appears inside Blocking Notes' scaling
   clause ("+2 Block per Companion card you have played this turn, including Guest Stars").
4. **"Take Your Bow — The leftmost member of your Salon takes their bow"** was offered as a card
   reward with no keyword, no number, and no glossary line. I could guess it means Evoke, but I
   declined a 0-cost card because I could not read it.
5. **A member performance is an Attack for Vulnerable but not for Frozen.** Vulnerable 2 turned
   Crabaletta's printed 6 into exactly 9. But twice — fight 1 turn 2, and the elite turn 3 —
   **Frozen survived a Crabaletta performance** that landed after it was applied, so the *"first
   Attack to hit it Shatters for 6"* clause did not count her hit. Both readings cannot be right.
6. **"Deal 6 damage, already including Salon"** (House Call, in the shop) — I could not work out
   what that means and did not buy it.
7. **The map's deck list is not my deck.** It is the last *fight's* deck, so it lists combat-only
   cards (Ethereal Spotlight ×3, Neuvillette, Bennett, Shinobu, a Slimed) mixed with real ones. The
   page does disclose this in italics, but it means the one screen that is supposed to tell you what
   you own does not.
8. **Enemy numbering silently reshuffles when one dies** (see fight 6). The page documents this
   hazard for cards in hand and not for enemies.

**(d) The card I never wanted to play, and the one I was happiest to draw.**

- **Never wanted: Casting Call.** A 1-cost Power that grants a fourth stage slot in a deck with one
  Deploy card. I paid gold for it and then declined to play it three times.
  (Runner-up: **Stage Presence** — a vanilla 6 Block in a kit whose whole argument is that you
  should be doing something more interesting with the energy.)
- **Happiest to draw: Charlotte — Framing: Freezing Point Composition.** 1 cost, 4 damage, draws a
  card, is a Companion so it performs a member for free, and its Cryo is the Frozen/Superconduct/Melt
  hinge — it halved a 23-damage elite swing, it converted an Electro aura into 2 free Vulnerable,
  and it never once cost me tempo because it replaces itself.
  (Runner-up: **Chevreuse — Interdiction Fire** — 58 damage in the two cards it anchored.)

**(e) Did the first turn of the first fight already present a decision?**

**Yes, and a good one.** Three energy, one Deploy, two Defends, one Weak, one Strike, against a
telegraphed 12. The obvious line (Stage Presence ×2 + Soloist's) and the line I took (Salon Début +
Regal Bearing + Stage Presence) both take exactly zero damage, so they are not separated by
survival — they are separated by whether you spend the turn setting up an engine at the cost of
4 damage instead of 6. And Regal Bearing's Weak visibly moved the printed intent from 12 to 9,
which let one Defend cover what would otherwise have needed two. That is a genuine, readable,
non-obvious turn-one choice. The caveat is that the deploy's payoff came with a printed penalty I
could not avoid — Crabaletta performed "dry" at three-quarters because Encore starts at 0 — so the
kit's opening move is, by construction, its own weakest version.

## Non-blindness declaration

Commands outside the two allowed ones (`observe` / `act`), all via the Bash tool:

- `mkdir -p .../scratchpad/lane1 && echo "acts=0 start" > .../notes.md` — created a scratch notes
  file once at the start. I never actually appended to it afterwards; I kept the action count from
  the bridge's own `actions: N of 120` line instead.
- Piped `observe` output through `sed -n '<ranges>'` on most turns to re-read one block (hand,
  enemy block, salon log, or header) instead of reprinting the whole page.
- `grep -A4 'Nibbit'` on one `observe`, once, to re-read the enemy block after applying Weak.
- `head -5` on one `observe`, once.
- `echo ---` / `echo ---HAND---` as separators between two piped `observe` calls.

**One self-inflicted artifact, declared because it briefly misled me:** in fight 1 turn 2 I used
`sed -n '1,20p;/What your Salon/,/^## Your hand/p'`, whose ranges overlapped on lines 18–20, so the
salon log's header and its single Crabaletta line printed **twice** and I momentarily read it as two
performances. The damage arithmetic (39 → 24 = 15 = Freminet 9 + Crabaletta 6) corrected it. This is
the known overlapping-`sed` trap, it was my error, and it is not a page defect. Later duplicate
Crabaletta lines, read with non-overlapping ranges, were genuine — two performances in one turn.

Two commands were refused (non-consecutive, so no three-refusal stop):

1. `play "Soloist's Solicitation"` in fight 3 turn 1 — *"cannot be played right now: you do not have
   enough energy."* My miscount, correctly refused, and the refusal listed the working forms.
2. `confirm` after picking **The Guest List** at the Brain Leech event — *"there is nothing waiting
   to be confirmed."* Worth noting as a small inconsistency: **both** that screen and the Neow
   removal screen reported `ok Toggling card selection`, but the removal screen then required
   `confirm` while the event's card pick had already resolved. Same acknowledgement text, two
   different contracts.

No `harness state`, `scenario`, `staged_turn`, `soak`, or any other understudy subcommand was run.
The Write tool was used exactly once, for this file.

**Repo files read: none.**
