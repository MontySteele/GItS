1. **Booby Trap — FOLLOWS (C2, C3, C5, C6):** “0 Energy, 1 Spark: Mine 5” versus Pop!’s “0 Energy: Bomb 5” spends the entire turn-one bank for equal charge size; against a lone nonattacking enemy when immediate Set off is intended, the Mine adds no damage and leaves one fewer Spark for Dig In’s “1 Spark: 8 Block,” so holding that Spark and choosing placement timing remain real choices even without target selection.

2. **Tinder Toss — REQUIRES_MODIFICATION (C6):** “1 Spark: Set off ALL, then 3 damage to ALL” versus Fireworks Show’s “2 Sparks: Set off ALL” gives the Common the entire Uncommon effect for one fewer Spark plus 3 damage per enemy; on a turn with two bombed enemies and 2 Sparks, Tinder Toss produces the same detonations, 6 additional damage, and one additional Spark remaining.

3. **Grounded — FOLLOWS (C2, C6):** “1 Energy: at the start of your turn, if you played no Set off card last turn, gain 4 Block and 1 Spark” versus Metallicize’s “1 Energy: 3 Block at end of turn” pays nothing on the turn Grounded is played and nothing on the turn after a Set off card, while Metallicize supplies 3 Block on each such turn, establishing an actual defensive loss despite Grounded’s stronger qualifying payout.

4. **Return to Sender — FOLLOWS (C2, C6):** “1 Energy, 2 Sparks: 8 Block,” with absorbed damage converted into at most Bomb 8, versus Dodoco Cover’s “1 Energy: Bomb 4, 5 Block” gains 3 Block and at most 4 Bomb for 2 Sparks when attacked, but on a nonattacking turn produces zero Bomb against Dodoco Cover’s 4, and on turn one with the starting 1 Spark cannot answer an elite’s attack at all without additional income.

The supplied pool contains no Common that is strictly better than Pop!: Booby Trap’s additional 1-Spark price binds. A Cook deck holding Grounded chooses Set off when detonating its accumulated Bombs kills an enemy before an otherwise lethal attack that its Mines and end-of-turn Splash cannot prevent, accepting the loss of next turn’s 4 Block and 1 Spark.
